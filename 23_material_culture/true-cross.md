# True Cross

The traditional relic of the cross of Christ's crucifixion; per Christian tradition discovered by [[helena-empress|Empress Helena]] at the site of [[church-of-the-holy-sepulchre-jerusalem|the Holy Sepulchre]] ~326 CE; fragments dispersed across many medieval European reliquaries.
