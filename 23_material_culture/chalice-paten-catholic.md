# Chalice and Paten — Eucharistic Vessels

The two principal vessels of the Roman + Eastern Catholic + Anglican Eucharistic liturgy: the **chalice** (cup) holding the consecrated wine, and the **paten** (small plate or disk) holding the consecrated bread/host. Both have continuous use from the earliest Christian centuries (chalice attested in 1 Cor 11; paten formalized in late antiquity). Standard requirements: gold/silver-plated metal; consecrated for liturgical use by the bishop; reserved for the Eucharist alone. The chalice-and-paten pairing is structurally parallel to other paired ritual-vessels across traditions (cf. Vajrayāna bell-and-dorje).
