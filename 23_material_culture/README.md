# 23 — Material Culture, Relics & Ritual Objects

**Lens slot 23** of the 26-lens ontology. New 2026-05-18. Holds nodes of type `relic`.

## What lives here

Specific named physical artifacts with religious significance — relics, ritual objects, sacred artifacts. The *thing itself*, with provenance, history, location, claimed authenticity (often contested).

**Examples:**
- **Christian relics:** the True Cross, the Holy Lance / Spear of Longinus / Spear of Destiny, the Crown of Thorns, the Shroud of Turin, the Sudarium of Oviedo, the Veil of Veronica, the Volto Santo of Lucca, reliquaries (the Reliquary of the Three Kings at Cologne).
- **Jewish relics / objects:** the Ark of the Covenant (the claimed physical object — distinct from the *symbol* of the Ark which lives in `09_symbols/`), the Tablets of the Law, the Menorah of the Second Temple, the Urim and Thummim.
- **Islamic relics / objects:** the Black Stone of the Kaaba, the Footprint of the Prophet (various), the Mantle of the Prophet (Topkapı), the Hair of the Prophet (various reliquaries), Qadam Rasul shrines.
- **Hindu / Buddhist relics:** Buddha relics (sariras) — Tooth Relic of Kandy, Buddha tooth relic temple Singapore, relics at Sanchi Stupa, the Begging Bowl of the Buddha, the Footprint of the Buddha. Hindu: claimed relics rare (theology differs), but specific iconic *murti* (the Padmanabhaswamy Vishnu, the Nataraja of Chidambaram).
- **Ethiopian Christian:** the Tabot (Ark replica — central to every Tewahedo church; class of object distinct from the claimed Original Ark).
- **Legendary / disputed:** the Holy Grail (as claimed object, separately from the Grail-symbol and the Grail-quest motif), the Stone of Scone, the Spear of Destiny (legendary attribution variants), the Akashic Records (not material, exclude).
- **Mesoamerican:** specific identified ritual objects from Templo Mayor, the Aztec sun stone (Piedra del Sol), specific Maya stelae.
- **Ritual objects (class-level):** prayer beads (rosary / mala / misbaha / komboskini — class node), reliquaries (class), monstrances (class), Torah scrolls (class — specific notable Torah scrolls can also be nodes), specific antimensia.
- **Liturgical / contested origins:** Spear of Destiny in occult / Nazi-era circulation (T4 territory with explicit flagging), Ark of the Covenant in Hancock-style speculative literature (T4 with flagging).

## Why separate from Symbols

- **Symbols (`09_symbols/`)** = *abstract iconographic units*. The cross AS sign (the visual / semantic form). The ankh AS sign. The ark AS symbol.
- **Material Culture (`23_material_culture/`)** = *specific physical instances*. The True Cross (claimed physical artifact). The Ark of the Covenant (claimed physical object). The specific reliquary.

Cross-edges link instance to symbol: `true-cross-relic —is-instance-of-symbol→ cross-symbol`.

## Why separate from Rituals

Rituals = acts; ritual objects = the props used in or generated by those acts. The Eucharist (ritual) uses the chalice (ritual object); the Torah-reading (ritual) uses the Torah scroll (object); the Hajj (ritual) involves circumambulation of the Kaaba (sacred site) and (for many pilgrims) kissing the Black Stone (relic).

## What does NOT live here

- **Generic symbols** → `09_symbols/`
- **Sacred sites / built structures** → `20_sacred_architecture/`
- **Texts as documents** → `02_documents/` (but specific physical manuscripts of great significance — Codex Sinaiticus, the Dead Sea Scrolls as physical objects, the Nag Hammadi codices as physical objects — can have nodes here too, cross-linked to the document)

## Slug convention

Suffix with `-relic` when the term overlaps with a symbol or place: `true-cross-relic`, `ark-of-the-covenant-relic`, `holy-lance-relic`, `mantle-of-the-prophet-relic`.

Class-level objects (rosary, reliquary, monstrance): unsuffixed, `rosary-prayer-beads`, `reliquary-class`, `monstrance-eucharistic`.

When a relic and the symbol it embodies should both be nodes: `cross-symbol` (in `09_symbols/`) + `true-cross-relic` (here). Cross-edged.
