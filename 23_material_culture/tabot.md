# Tabot

Ethiopian Tewahedo replica / continuation of the Ark of the Covenant; housed wrapped in cloth on the central altar of every [[tradition-ethiopian-tewahedo|Tewahedo]] church. The tradition holds that the original Ark resides at the Church of Our Lady Mary of Zion in Aksum.
