# Ghaṇṭā + Vajra — The Bell-and-Dorje Pair

The paired ritual implements held by a Vajrayāna practitioner during tantric liturgy: the **bell (ghaṇṭā)** in the left hand, the **vajra (dorje)** in the right. Iconographically a unified pair — the bell carries the *wisdom* (prajñā / female) aspect, the vajra carries the *method* (upāya / male) aspect; the two held simultaneously embody the union-of-wisdom-and-method that is the Vajrayāna soteriological goal. The bell's hollow body is decorated with the wisdom-syllable iconography; its handle is itself a half-vajra. Standard equipment for sādhana, abhiṣeka, and pūjā across all four Tibetan schools and Japanese Shingon.
