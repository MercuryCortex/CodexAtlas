# Classical Tibetan

The literary + liturgical language of Tibetan Buddhism from the early imperial period (7th c. CE, when the Tibetan script was created on Indic-script models under Songtsen Gampo) to the present. The vehicle of the Kangyur + Tengyur canon, the rNying-ma + bKa'-brgyud + Sa-skya + dGe-lugs textual traditions. Continues to function as the pan-Tibetan-Buddhist liturgical lingua-sacra (Mongolia, Bhutan, the diaspora) the way Latin functioned for Catholic Europe.
