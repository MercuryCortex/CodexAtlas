# Classical Mandaic

Eastern Late Aramaic language used by the Mandaean (gnostic-baptismal) religious community of lower Mesopotamia. Sister-register of [[language-babylonian-aramaic|Babylonian Aramaic]]; the principal liturgical and scriptural language of [ginza-rabba|Ginza Rabba], [mandaean-book-of-john|Mandaean Book of John], and the entire Mandaean canon.
