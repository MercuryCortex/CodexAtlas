# Ardha-Māgadhī Prakrit

The Middle Indo-Aryan Prakrit dialect in which the **Jain Agamas** (the canonical scriptures of the Śvetāmbara Jain tradition) are composed — the *Ācārāṅga*, *Sūtrakṛtāṅga*, *Sthānāṅga*, etc. The name ("half-Magadhi") reflects a position between the eastern Magadhi Prakrit + central-Indian Prakrits. The Jain canonical use of Ardha-Māgadhī parallels the Buddhist use of [language-pali|Pali] — both Middle-Indo-Aryan vernacular alternatives to Sanskrit, both attached to the heterodox śramaṇa traditions.
