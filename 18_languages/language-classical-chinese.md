# Classical Chinese (Literary Sinitic)

The pan-East Asian literary + administrative language from the Spring and Autumn period (c. -500) to the early 20th century — used as a written lingua franca across China, Korea, Japan, and Vietnam (the Sinographic cultural sphere). The vehicle of the Confucian canon, the Daoist + Buddhist canon (Chinese translations), the dynastic histories, and the administrative apparatus of every East Asian centralized state until the modern vernacularization movements.
