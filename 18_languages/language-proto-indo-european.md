# Proto-Indo-European (PIE)

The reconstructed common ancestor of the [[language-indo-european-family|Indo-European language family]]. PIE is not directly attested — it has been reconstructed by ~200 years of comparative philological work from the systematic correspondences across its daughter languages (Vedic Sanskrit, Old Avestan, Mycenaean Greek, Hittite, Latin, Gothic, Old Irish, Old Church Slavonic, etc.).

## What is reconstructed

PIE reconstruction yields:

- A phonological inventory (3 laryngeals, voiceless/voiced/voiced-aspirated stop series, accent + ablaut patterns)
- A morphological system (8 cases, 3 numbers, complex verbal aspect, primary/secondary endings)
- A core lexicon (numbers, kinship, body parts, agricultural + pastoral vocabulary, basic verbs)
- A small but securely-reconstructable *poetic-ritual* inheritance (Watkins 1995): inherited formulae for "imperishable fame" (*kléos áphthiton* / *śrávas ákṣitam*), the chaoskampf serpent-slaying line (*gʷhen- h₃gʷhi-*), the "wide cattle-pen" + "swift horses" metaphors
- Theonymic etyma: *Dyḗus ph₂tḗr* "Sky-Father" → Sanskrit *Dyaus Pitar*, Greek *Zeus patēr*, Latin *Iuppiter*, Old Norse *Týr*, Hittite *šiu-*

## Where (homeland)

The dominant scholarly position locates PIE in the **Pontic-Caspian steppe** (modern Ukraine / southern Russia) c. -4500 to -2500, associated with the **Yamnaya culture**. This *Kurgan hypothesis* (Marija Gimbutas + Mallory + Anthony) has been substantially strengthened by post-2015 ancient-DNA evidence showing massive migration from steppe populations into both Europe and South Asia in the third millennium BCE (Haak et al. 2015, Allentoft et al. 2015, Narasimhan et al. 2019).

An older competing hypothesis (Colin Renfrew's *Archaeology and Language*, 1987) located PIE earlier and further south in Anatolia, associated with the Neolithic farming expansion. This is now a minority position, though it retains some defenders for specific sub-questions.

## Why this lens matters for the Atlas

PIE is the root of the single largest cross-tradition transmission vector in world religious history. Three concrete payoffs for the graph:

1. **The *Dyaus / Zeus / Jupiter / Týr* sky-father cognacy** is one of the cleanest documented examples of the `cognate` edge type — the same word + same theological position preserved across IE branches with no later contact. See the deity nodes in `03_deities/` for the individual instances.
2. **The *deva / daēva* polemic inversion** between Indo-Aryan and Iranian (see [[asura-deva-inversion]] and [[sanskrit-vedic]] / [[language-old-avestan]]) is a *late-Indo-Iranian* development — but it is only legible *as* an inversion because PIE reconstruction tells us the underlying term was originally positive.
3. **The chaoskampf serpent-slaying formula** that Watkins 1995 reconstructs from Vedic Indra-Vṛtra + Hittite Illuyanka + Greek Python + Norse Jörmungandr + Iranian Aži Dahāka is *inherited poetic vocabulary* from the proto-period — a stunning case of the comparative method working at the level of literature.
