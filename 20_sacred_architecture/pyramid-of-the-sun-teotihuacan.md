# Pyramid of the Sun

## What it is

The **Pyramid of the Sun** at Teotihuacan is the largest building of pre-Columbian Mesoamerica and the **third-largest pyramid in the world by volume** (~1.2 million m³, behind only Khufu and the Great Pyramid of Cholula). Built between c. 100 and 250 CE — substantially complete by ~200 CE — it dominates the centre of the planned-grid city of Teotihuacan, the **largest pre-Columbian urban centre in the Americas** at its peak (population estimates ~100,000–200,000 by 500 CE).

The pyramid is at the centre of the city's central north-south axis (the misleadingly-named **Avenue of the Dead**, *Calzada de los Muertos* — an Aztec name; the Teotihuacanos called it something we don't know), flanked north by the smaller [[pyramid-of-the-moon-teotihuacan|Pyramid of the Moon]] and south by the [[temple-of-the-feathered-serpent-teotihuacan|Temple of the Feathered Serpent]] in the Ciudadela. These three monuments form the architectural-religious centre of the city.

The actual identity of the people who built it is unknown — Teotihuacan **collapsed catastrophically c. 550 CE** with sacred precincts ritually burned and the elite buildings systematically razed. When the **Aztec/Mexica** rose to power 800 years later they considered the ruins of Teotihuacan to be the **place of the gods' creation** — they named it *Teōtīhuacān* ("place where the gods were made" or "place where one becomes a god"), and the Aztec emperors made regular pilgrimages to it.

## History

### Construction phases

Sugiyama and colleagues (2010s tunnel excavations) have established:
- **Phase 1 (c. 100 CE):** initial pyramid platform, smaller than the visible structure.
- **Phase 2 (c. 150–250 CE):** the major monumental enlargement to essentially its current form.
- **Phase 3 (c. 250–350 CE):** addition of the *adosada* (front platform extension on the west face — the platform you see in photographs at the base of the staircase).

The whole structure was originally **stuccoed and painted in vivid colours** — red predominant, with murals on the upper terraces (only fragmentary murals from elsewhere in the city give us evidence of the colour palette). The bare brown stone visible today is post-collapse erosion.

### The 1905–1910 Batres restoration

[[leopoldo-batres]]'s state-sponsored 1905–1910 excavation/restoration in advance of Mexico's centennial celebration of 1910 caused **significant damage**. Batres stripped the outer protective stone layer (interpreting it as later debris), added a non-original fifth terrace (the pyramid has four real terraces + a small summit platform — the visible five-terraced silhouette is partly Batres's invention), and built the modern staircase. Subsequent excavations have corrected the worst errors but the structure remains a "restored ruin" rather than an untouched original.

### The cave discovery (1971)

In 1971 a team installing modern lighting in the pyramid's interior tunnel system discovered that the previously-known interior tunnel led to a **previously-unknown natural lava-tube cave system extending ~100 m east from a point directly beneath the centre of the pyramid**. The cave terminates in a **four-petaled chamber** (Doris Heyden 1975) — a natural-and-modified cavity at the symbolic centre of the Mesoamerican universe.

This redefined the pyramid's religious meaning. Mesoamerican cosmology held that **caves are openings to the underworld and to the place-of-emergence of the human race** (the cosmogonic cave of Chicomoztoc in later Aztec myth). The Pyramid of the Sun is **built directly over a natural sacred cave** — the pyramid IS the cosmic mountain WITH ITS WORLD-CAVE inside it. The architecture realizes the entire Mesoamerican cosmological structure in one monument.

### The Aztec adoption

The Aztecs called the city ***Teotihuacan*** — "place where gods were born / made". The [[phase-8-002-historia-de-los-mexicanos-por-sus-pinturas|*Historia de los Mexicanos por sus Pinturas*]] (~1540) records the Aztec belief that **the present cosmic age (the Fifth Sun) was created at Teotihuacan** through the self-sacrifice of the gods, who then assembled at the pyramids and threw themselves into a great fire to become the sun and the moon. The Pyramid of the Sun is the place where **Nanahuatzin** (the humble god) became the Sun; the Pyramid of the Moon is where the proud **Tecciztecatl** became the Moon.

The Aztec narrative postdates Teotihuacan's collapse by 800 years and almost certainly is a later interpretation, but it preserves the religious importance assigned to the ruins.

## Architectural form

### Dimensions

- **Height:** ~65 m (originally; post-Batres reconstruction).
- **Base:** 220 m × 230 m (essentially square within a few metres).
- **Volume:** ~1.2 million m³ of earth and stone fill, faced with cut volcanic stone and stucco.
- **Four terraces** (originally; the visible fifth terrace is Batres's restoration error).
- **West-facing single staircase** — the staircase descends in a single steep run, originally faced with carved talud-tablero (the diagnostic Teotihuacan architectural style: alternating slope + vertical panel).
- **Summit platform**: a small temple shrine almost certainly originally stood here; nothing of it survives.

### The cave beneath

The natural lava-tube cave runs **100 m east from below the pyramid's base** at an angle, terminating in a **four-petalled chamber** (four small alcoves around a central chamber). The cave was modified by the Teotihuacanos: walls cut, drainage worked, the entrance closed off and likely re-sealed multiple times. Modern access is by a stairway down from the centre of the pyramid's adosada platform.

### Alignment

The pyramid is **oriented 15.5° east of true north** — the same orientation as the entire Teotihuacan urban grid. This orientation is **deliberate and astronomically calibrated** (Aveni 1980):
- On the days the sun passes **directly overhead at Teotihuacan's latitude** (the *zenith passages*: May 18 and July 25), the sun sets behind the pyramid's main axis. These dates bracket the **rainy season onset**.
- On April 29 and August 12 the setting sun aligns with the western horizon as observed from the Pyramid's summit looking due-west (across the city's main axis).

The Teotihuacanos almost certainly used the pyramid for solar / zenith-passage observation, marking the agricultural year and the cosmic order.

## Theological / cosmological function

### Cosmic mountain over the world-cave

The Pyramid of the Sun is the **purest Mesoamerican statement of the cosmic-mountain-over-the-sacred-cave architectural archetype**. The pyramid (the artificial mountain that *is* the cosmos's mountain) sits **directly above** a natural cave (which *is* the cosmos's underworld and place-of-origin). Vertical: cosmic mountain → sacred cave → underworld. The architecture *is* the Mesoamerican cosmos in three dimensions.

Mircea Eliade's [[axis-mundi]] framework finds one of its clearest single architectural examples here.

### Possibly: cosmogenesis enactment

If the Aztec interpretation reflects an older Teotihuacan tradition (debated), then the pyramid was the site of the **architectural enactment of cosmogenesis** — the place where the gods sacrificed themselves to create the current cosmic age. The pyramid then is not just a temple but the **cosmographic centre of the world**: the precise spot where time and the universe began.

### Mortuary uses

Sugiyama's recent excavations (2008–present) at the Pyramid of the Moon and Feathered Serpent have revealed **massive sacrificial burials** inside the construction phases — humans + sacrificed animals (eagles, jaguars, pumas, wolves) deposited as foundational dedications. Equivalent deposits at the Sun pyramid are suspected but not yet confirmed (the structure is structurally compromised and tunnel-excavation has been limited).

## MASSIVE WIN — the largest pre-Columbian American monument as cosmic-mountain-over-cave

**The cleanest Mesoamerican example of the artificial-mountain-over-natural-portal pattern:** Pyramid of the Sun (artificial mountain) directly above a natural cave system (natural underworld portal). This **architectural inversion + completion of natural sacred geography** is the Mesoamerican analogue to:

- **Egyptian:** The [[great-sphinx-of-giza|Sphinx]] + Khufu's pyramid built **over earlier rock-cut tombs and (per Lehner) some natural depressions** — though far less natural-cave-direct than at Teotihuacan.
- **Greek:** The [[parthenon-athens|Parthenon]] built on the Acropolis rock **above the sacred caves of Pan and Apollo** on its north face — same vertical: built temple on cliff above sacred cave.
- **Roman:** [[mithraea]] (Mithraic temples) built below ground or in natural caves as deliberate cave-architecture; the inversion of Teotihuacan's pyramid-above-cave: cave-IS-temple.
- **Indic:** [[ajanta-caves|Ajanta]], [[ellora-caves|Ellora]], [[elephanta-caves]] — cave-temple programmes excavated into living mountain. The cosmic mountain WITH a cut-into temple-cave — same vertical relationship pyramid:cave as Teotihuacan but inverted (mountain is natural, cave is built).

The Teotihuacan vertical reading — **artificial mountain ON natural cave** — appears to be the strongest single architectural realisation of the [[axis-mundi]] in any tradition. The pyramid does not just **symbolise** the world axis; it **physically constructs** the upper axis on top of the lower one already given by geology.

**Cross-civilization parallel:** Stepped monumental pyramids in 4–5 mutually-uncontacting civilizations (Egyptian Khufu, Mesopotamian ziggurat-of-Ur, Buddhist Borobudur, Maya Tikal/Palenque, Teotihuacan/Aztec central-Mexican family). Five emergences of one architectural type-class. Convergent religious-architectural reasoning at maximum civilizational scale.

**Mesoamerican transmission:** Teotihuacan ↔ Maya area extensive contact in the Early Classic (lowland Maya cities like Tikal show Teotihuacan iconography + a possible Teotihuacan-installed dynasty under [[sihyaj-kahk]] in 378 CE). The Pyramid of the Sun is the central-Mexican typology that propagated the Mesoamerican monumental pyramid form outward.
