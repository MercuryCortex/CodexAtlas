# Ain Dara Temple Syria

Iron Age (~10th–8th c. BCE) Syrian temple at Tell Ain Dara, northern Syria; the **closest known architectural parallel to [[solomons-temple-first|Solomon's First Temple]]** (tripartite plan, cherub iconography, similar dimensions). Heavily damaged in airstrikes 2018.
