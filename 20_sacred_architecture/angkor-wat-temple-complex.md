# Angkor Wat Temple Complex

Largest religious building in the world by area; Khmer temple-mountain at Angkor (Cambodia), ~1120 CE; built under Suryavarman II as Vaishnavite Hindu temple, converted to Buddhist use; five-towered architectural realisation of [[mount-meru]].
