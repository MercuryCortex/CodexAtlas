# Suleymaniye Mosque

Imperial Ottoman mosque commissioned by Suleiman the Magnificent and designed by [[mimar-sinan]] in 1557. Principal example of the classical Ottoman mosque form in dialogue with [[hagia-sophia]]'s dome-and-half-dome system.
