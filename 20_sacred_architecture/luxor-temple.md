# Luxor Temple

Ancient Egyptian temple complex at Luxor (Thebes); built ~1400 BCE under [[amenhotep-iii]] and expanded by [[ramesses-ii]]; connected to [[karnak-temple-complex|Karnak]] by the Avenue of Sphinxes (~3 km); principal site of the annual Opet Festival.
