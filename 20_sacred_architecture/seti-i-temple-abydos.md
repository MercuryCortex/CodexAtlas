# Seti I Temple at Abydos

The mortuary temple of Pharaoh Seti I (19th Dynasty, c. -1290 to -1279) at [[place-abydos]] — Egypt's principal Osirian cult-centre. Preserves the famous **Abydos King List** (a sequential list of legitimate pharaohs back to Menes, key source for pharaonic chronology) and the **Osirion**, the cenotaph-like Osiris temple behind the mortuary temple proper. One of the best-preserved New Kingdom temple complexes.
