# Etemenanki

"House of the Foundation of Heaven and Earth" — the great seven-tiered ziggurat at Babylon, ~91 m tall, rebuilt by [[nebuchadnezzar-ii|Nebuchadnezzar II]] (~600 BCE); probable historical referent of the Genesis [[tower-of-babel]] narrative.
