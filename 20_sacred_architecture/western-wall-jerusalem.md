# Western Wall Jerusalem

The surviving western retaining-wall of [[herod-the-great|Herod's]] expanded Temple-Mount platform (1st c. BCE) — NOT a wall of [[solomons-temple-second|the Temple itself]], but of the enclosure terrace Herod built around it. The most important pilgrimage-and-prayer site in Judaism since 70 CE.
