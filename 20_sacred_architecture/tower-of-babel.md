# Tower Of Babel

The architectural myth of Genesis 11:1–9 — the tower with its top in the heavens, divinely punished by language-confusion. Hebrew polemic inversion of the Mesopotamian [[ziggurat-of-ur|ziggurat]] / [[etemenanki|Etemenanki]] as [[theme-ziggurat-as-stairway-to-heaven|gate-of-the-gods]]. Probably refers to Etemenanki specifically.
