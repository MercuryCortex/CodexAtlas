# Kinich Ahau

Kinich Ahau (Classic Maya *K'inich Ajaw*, "Sun-faced Lord"; "God G" in the Schellhas catalogue) is the **Maya sun god** and one of the principal deities of the Classic and Postclassic Maya. He is recognized by the *k'in* (sun/day) sign on his body, his squared "cruller" eyes, a Roman-nosed profile, and a single great filed incisor. By day he crosses the sky as the radiant sun; by night, descending into **Xibalba** (the underworld), he becomes the fearsome **Jaguar Sun** (the "Jaguar God of the Underworld"). His importance is bound up with **kingship**: the very title *k'inich* prefixes the names of countless Maya kings (K'inich Janaab Pakal of Palenque, K'inich Yax K'uk' Mo' of Copán), marking the ruler as a solar lord and the sun god's earthly embodiment. He is closely tied to — and often regarded as the solar manifestation of — the supreme creator **[[itzamna]]**. His presence had been absent from the vault despite being one of the two or three most central Maya gods.
