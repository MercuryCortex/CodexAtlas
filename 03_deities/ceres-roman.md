# Ceres

## Identity
Roman grain-goddess and patroness of the **plebeian order**. Her Aventine temple (493 BCE) served as the **plebeian archive** for senatorial decrees affecting the plebs — making Ceres the principal cult-protectress of the Roman lower-class order against patrician arbitrariness. The English word **cereal** descends from her. After Hellenization she fully absorbed [[demeter]]'s mythology, including the search for Proserpina/Persephone in the underworld.
