# Laz

Consort of [[nergal]] at his cult-centre **Kutha** (modern Tell Ibrahim) — the most important Akkadian-period underworld-cult site in lower Mesopotamia. Laz is the local-cult consort, distinct from the Sumerian tradition that paired Nergal with [[ereshkigal]] (the underworld-descent narrative). The two traditions were reconciled in different periods: in some Old Babylonian sources Laz and Ereshkigal are alternate names, in others Laz is the senior wife and Ereshkigal is acquired through the Descent narrative.
