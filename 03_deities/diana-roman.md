# Diana

## Identity
Roman goddess of **the hunt, wilderness, the moon, and childbirth**. Two principal cults: **Diana Aventinensis** (Aventine Hill, Rome — historically the patron of slaves and the lower classes, with August 13 the slaves' festival day) and **Diana Nemorensis** (Lake Nemi, Aricia — the sacred grove with the priesthood of the *Rex Nemorensis*, the runaway slave who became priest by killing the previous incumbent, the lurid case study that framed Frazer's *Golden Bough* 1890). **Diana of Ephesus** (a different cult-figure, Anatolian-substrate, with the famous many-breasted statue) is the explicit antagonist of Paul's mission in Acts 19:23–41.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[artemis]]** — *ancient-identification*: Roman cognate.
- **[[hekate]]** — *ancient-identification*: chthonic aspect (Diana Trivia).
- **[[mary-theotokos]]** — *polemic-against* via Acts 19; Diana of Ephesus → Marian Theotokos at the Council of Ephesus 431 (the very city — the Marian replacement of the previous principal goddess is structurally one of the cleanest Christian-takeover-of-pagan-cult-site cases).
