# The World Soul

The Third Hypostasis in Plotinian metaphysics — produced by Nous, the World Soul is the principle that animates the cosmos and from which individual souls derive. The soul has both an upward face (toward Nous) and a downward face (toward the production of nature); Plotinus' celebrated thesis is that human souls retain a part that has *not* descended — a permanent connection to Nous that grounds the philosophical ascent.
