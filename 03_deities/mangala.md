# Mangala

Mangala (*Maṅgala*; also **Angaraka**, "the burning coal," and **Kuja/Bhauma**, "earth-born") is the **Navagraha deity of the planet Mars** — red-bodied, fiery, four-armed, riding a ram. He is a **malefic**: the planet of war, raw energy, aggression, courage, and conflict, governing land, property, and the spilling of blood. In one well-known myth he is **born of the earth** (Bhumi/**[[prithvi]]**) from a drop of Shiva's sweat or blood, hence *Bhauma* and *Kuja*. His astrological weight in living Hindu practice is enormous: a person born under an afflicted Mars is a *Mangala-dosha* ("manglik"), a condition taken very seriously in marriage-matching, requiring remedial ritual. With **[[shani]]** (Saturn), **[[brihaspati]]** (Jupiter), **[[shukra]]** (Venus), Surya, Chandra, Budha, and the shadow-planets Rahu and Ketu, Mangala completes the **Navagraha** — the nine planetary governors of karma and time. He gives his name to Tuesday (*Maṅgalavāra*).
