# Hypnos

Hypnos (Greek *Hýpnos*, "Sleep") is the personification of sleep, son of **[[nyx-primordial]]** (Night) and twin of **[[thanatos-greek]]** (Death). In the famous *Dios apate* of *Iliad* 14, Hera bribes Hypnos with the Charis Pasithea as wife to lull Zeus to sleep so that Poseidon can aid the Greeks — and Hypnos recalls how a previous such favour nearly got him hurled from heaven by an enraged Zeus. He dwells in the misty far west with his brother and is the father of the dream-gods (the Oneiroi, chief of them Morpheus). His gentle, restorative aspect made him a figure of healing sleep at incubation sanctuaries. The Romans call him **Somnus**.
