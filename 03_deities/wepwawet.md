# Wepwawet

Wepwawet (Egyptian *Wp-wȝwt*, "Opener of the Ways") is the jackal- or wolf-headed god of Asyut (Greek *Lykopolis*, "wolf-city"), a deity of both **war** and the **funerary** sphere whose single function unites them: he *opens the way*. As a war-god his standard (the *shedshed*) is carried before the king and the army into battle, clearing the path to victory; as a funerary god he opens the roads of the underworld before the deceased and leads the processions of Osiris at Abydos. One of the oldest Egyptian deities (his standard appears on the Predynastic Narmer Palette), he is closely paired with — and often confused with — **[[anubis]]**, the other great canid of the dead; the rule of thumb is that Wepwawet *leads and opens* while Anubis *embalms and guards*. He frequently precedes the king in jubilee (*sed*-festival) iconography as the divine pathfinder of royal renewal.
