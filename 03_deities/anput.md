# Anput

Anput is the female counterpart and consort of [[anubis]] in Egyptian religion — a deity whose identity is largely defined by her relationship to Anubis rather than by independent mythological function. She is the goddess of the seventeenth nome (administrative province) of Upper Egypt and shares Anubis's domain over embalming and funerary rites. The name *Anput* is the feminine grammatical form of *Anpu* (Anubis), and she is depicted as a jackal-headed woman mirroring Anubis's iconography. Her daughter is Kebechet, the serpent-goddess of purification, who assists Anubis in the embalming process. Anput appears in some traditions as the protective spirit of the funerary bier.
