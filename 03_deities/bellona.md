# Bellona

Bellona (from Old Latin *Duellona*, from *duellum/bellum*, "war") is the Roman **goddess of war** — the divine personification of martial fury and the companion of **[[mars-roman]]** (named variously as his sister, wife, or charioteer). Her temple in the **Campus Martius**, vowed by Appius Claudius Caecus in 296 BCE, stood pointedly *outside* the sacred boundary (*pomerium*): there the Senate met to receive returning generals and foreign ambassadors who could not be admitted into the city, and beside it stood the *columna bellica*, the symbolic war-column over which the fetial priest hurled a spear to declare war. From the first century BCE her cult fused with that of the Anatolian mother-goddess **Ma**, served by the **bellonarii**, ecstatic priests who gashed their own arms and thighs in frenzied processions — a strain of orgiastic eastern religion alongside the staid civic war-goddess. The Greeks identified her with **Enyo**.
