# Kubaba

Kubaba (Hieroglyphic Luwian *Kubaba*) is the great tutelary goddess of **Carchemish**, the Syro-Hittite city on the Euphrates, and the supreme female deity of the Iron Age **Neo-Hittite** ("Syro-Hittite") states that succeeded the Hittite Empire. Enthroned, wearing a mural crown and holding a mirror and pomegranate, she is the divine queen of her city and a guarantor of sovereignty. Her chief historical importance is genealogical: Kubaba is widely recognized as the Anatolian **ancestress of the Phrygian Great Mother**, *Matar Kubileya* — i.e. of **[[cybele]]** — the name and the enthroned-great-goddess type passing westward from Carchemish into Phrygia and from there into the Greek and Roman worlds (Roller 1999). This makes her one of the most consequential "upstream" deities in the Atlas: the historical root of the Magna Mater whose mystery cult reached Rome itself.
