# Abathur

A Mandaean liminal figure positioned at the boundary between the World of Light and the lower realms. As Abathur Muzania ("Abathur of the Scales") he weighs the souls of the dead. In some Mandaean cosmogonies he is the father of Ptahil — placing Abathur one step above the demiurgic figure in the Mandaean hierarchy. The weighing-of-souls motif parallels Egyptian Ma'at-weighing-of-the-heart imagery, with a likely shared late-antique Mediterranean substrate.
