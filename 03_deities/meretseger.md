# Meretseger

Meretseger ("She Who Loves Silence") is the Egyptian **cobra-goddess of the Theban necropolis** — the personified **pyramid-shaped peak (al-Qurn)** that towers over the Valley of the Kings, also called *Dehenet-Imentet*, "the Peak of the West." She is above all the goddess of the **workmen of Deir el-Medina**, the artisans who cut and decorated the royal tombs and who lived in her shadow. Their votive stelae record her as a deity of striking **moral immediacy**: she strikes the sinner who swears falsely or transgresses with **blindness or venomous sickness**, yet, when the wrongdoer confesses and repents, she **heals them in mercy** — one workman, Neferabu, left a stela thanking her for restoring his sight after such a chastening. As "she who loves silence" she embodies the hush of the desert necropolis; her cult, intensely local, faded once Deir el-Medina was abandoned at the end of the New Kingdom.
