# Eileithyia

Greek goddess of childbirth and midwifery; daughter of Zeus and Hera in Hesiod's *Theogony* 922. Attested from Mycenaean Linear B as *e-re-u-ti-ja* (Knossos KN Gg 705) — one of the earliest-attested Greek deities. Frequently identified with Egyptian [[nekhbet]] in Greco-Egyptian syncretic contexts (the city later called Eileithyiaspolis by the Greeks was the cult-centre of Nekhbet).
