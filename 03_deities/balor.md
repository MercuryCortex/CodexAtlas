# Balor

Balor (Irish *Balar*) is the **Fomorian** king-champion of Irish myth — the embodiment of the destructive, chaotic powers that the **Tuatha Dé Danann** (the gods of Ireland) must overcome. His terrible attribute is a single **venomous eye** whose gaze slays whole armies; so heavy and baleful is its lid that it takes several men with hooks to raise it on the battlefield (it had been poisoned, the tale says, by the fumes of his father's druids' brew). A prophecy warns that Balor will be killed by his own **grandson**, so he imprisons his daughter Eithne in a tower — but the god Cian reaches her, and their son is **[[lugh]]**. At the climactic **Second Battle of Mag Tuired** (*Cath Maige Tuired*), as Balor's lethal eye is being pried open, the young Lugh drives a sling-stone clean through it and out the back of his skull, so that the death-gaze falls upon Balor's own Fomorian host. Balor is the Irish archetype of the monstrous-grandfather slain by the bright young god — a Celtic cousin of the Greek Kronos-prophecy pattern.
