# Neptune

## Identity
Roman god of the sea — though originally an Italic fresh-water deity (springs, lakes, rivers) before Hellenization absorbed him into the role of Poseidon. The **Neptunalia** (July 23) was a high-summer festival when fresh water mattered most — the original cult function remained legible even after the Greek-sea-god overlay.
