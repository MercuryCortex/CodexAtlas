# Manannán mac Lir

## Identity
Celtic sea-god — son of **Lir** ("Sea") — and lord of the otherworldly islands of Tír na nÓg, Mag Mell, and Emain Ablach. Patron of the Isle of Man (the toponym Mann/Manaw is derived from his name; *Manainn* "Man" / *Manawydan* are linguistic reflexes). He ferries souls to the otherworld, gives heroes magical equipment (the sword **Fragarach**, the silver branch, the wave-cloak), and reveals the otherworld geography to mortal voyagers in the *immrama* (sea-voyage tales).
