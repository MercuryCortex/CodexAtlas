# Enki / Ea

God of the fresh subterranean waters (Abzu/Apsû), wisdom, craft, and magic. The trickster-savior of Mesopotamian myth — repeatedly thwarts other gods' plans to destroy humanity. In [[phase-1-006-atrahasis]] and the parallel Gilgamesh flood, it is Enki/Ea who warns the chosen man to build the boat *against* Enlil's decree. Father of Marduk. Cult center: Eridu (Sumer's mythologically oldest city), the E-abzu temple.
