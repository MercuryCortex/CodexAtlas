# Rhea

## Identity
Titan daughter of [[uranus-greek]] and [[gaia]]; consort-sister of [[kronos]]; **mother of the six chief Olympians** — Zeus, Hera, Poseidon, Hades, Demeter, Hestia. Hesiod's *Theogony* 453–506 narrates Rhea's deception of Kronos when, in fulfillment of the oracle that he would be overthrown by his son, Kronos swallows each of their children at birth. Rhea hides the infant Zeus in the Idaean cave on Crete (or Ida in Phrygia per the Anatolian tradition) and presents Kronos with a stone wrapped in swaddling clothes — the **omphalos stone** later venerated at Delphi.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[cybele]] / Magna Mater** — *ancient-identification*: Phrygian / Anatolian Mother-Goddess fused with Rhea from Pindar onward.
- **[[gaia]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: earth-mother typology.
