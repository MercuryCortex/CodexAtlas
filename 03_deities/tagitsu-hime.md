# Tagitsu-hime

Tagitsu-hime (also **Takitsuhime**) is the middle of the three **Munakata goddesses** — her name evoking the *surging, rushing* tidal water, as her sister **[[tagori-hime]]**'s evokes the misting sea. Born with **[[ichikishima-hime]]** and Tagori-hime from the sword of **[[susanoo]]** in the *ukehi* pledge with **[[amaterasu]]** (*Kojiki*, *Nihon Shoki*), she is enshrined at **Nakatsu-miya** on the island of **Ōshima**, the middle of the three Munakata shrine-sites strung out across the Genkai Sea toward Korea. The three goddesses together form a single maritime cult — the *Michinushi no Muchi*, "Lords of the Way" — to whom the early Yamato state made offerings for safe passage and success on the continental sea-route, a devotion materially preserved in the ritual deposits of Okinoshima.
