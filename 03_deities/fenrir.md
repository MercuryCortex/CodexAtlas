# Fenrir

## Identity
The monstrous wolf-son of [[loki]] and Angrboða, brother to [[jormungandr]] and [[hel]]. **The apocalyptic wolf** — at Ragnarök Fenrir breaks his binding, runs across the world with his lower jaw on the earth and upper jaw scraping heaven, and **swallows [[odin]] whole** before being torn open by Odin's son Víðarr. The death of Odin at Fenrir's jaws is the structural climax of Norse eschatology.

## Mythological role
**The binding**: the gods, recognizing the wolf-cub's monstrous growth-rate, attempt to bind him three times. The first two chains he breaks easily; only the dwarf-forged **Gleipnir** — fashioned from six paradoxical materials (the sound of a cat's footstep, the beard of a woman, the roots of a mountain, the sinews of a bear, the breath of a fish, the spittle of a bird) — holds. Fenrir submits to the third chain only on condition that a god places a hand in his mouth as surety. **Týr** (already in the vault) volunteers and loses his hand when the binding holds — etiological of Týr's iconographic one-handedness.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[jormungandr]]** — sibling apocalyptic monster (the Midgard Serpent kills Thor at Ragnarök).
- **[[hel]]** — sister, ruler of the dead.
- **Leviathan / [[leviathan]]** — *parallel-motif*: cosmic monster bound until the eschaton, released at world's end.
