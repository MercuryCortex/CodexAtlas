# Nuska

Nuska (Sumerian/Akkadian *Nuska/Nusku*) is the god of **light and fire** — specifically the divine **lamp** that pierces the dark — and the *sukkal* (vizier) and son of **[[enlil]]** at Nippur, in which role he carries and announces the chief god's commands. His emblem is the lampstand. Because fire both illuminates and purifies, Nuska is a central deity of Mesopotamian apotropaic ritual: in the great anti-witchcraft series **Maqlû** ("Burning"), it is Nuska's flame that consumes the figurines of the warlocks and witches and turns their evil back upon them. At Harran he is closely associated with the moon-god Sin. He overlaps the smelting-and-purification fire-god Gibil/Girra, the two functioning as the benevolent, ritually-controlled face of fire.
