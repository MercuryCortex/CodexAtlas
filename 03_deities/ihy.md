# Ihy

Egyptian child-god — the divine son of Hathor + Horus of Edfu, principal at Dendera. Iconographically a naked child shaking the sistrum (the percussion instrument associated with Hathor's cult). Member of the Dendera-Edfu divine family-triad (Hathor + Horus + Ihy).
