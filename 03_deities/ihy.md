# Ihy

Ihy is the Egyptian **child-god of music and jubilation** — the divine son of **[[hathor]]** (with **[[horus]]** of Edfu), and the third member of the great temple-triad of **Dendera**, Hathor's chief cult-centre. His name is often read as "sistrum-player," and that is his essence: he is depicted as a naked child with the sidelock of youth, a finger to his lips, holding the **sistrum** and the **menat**-necklace — the rattling percussion instruments whose sound, in Egyptian belief, soothed the gods, drove off hostility, and embodied the joy and erotic vitality of Hathor's worship. As a child-deity he also carries the theme of perpetual rebirth and renewal. In the annual festival in which Hathor of Dendera travelled upriver to unite with Horus of Edfu, Ihy is the offspring of their sacred marriage — the personified gladness of the divine reunion.
