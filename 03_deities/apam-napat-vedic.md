# Apām Napāt

Vedic + Avestan Indo-Iranian deity — "Grandson of the Waters" — the hidden celestial fire concealed in the cosmic waters. One of the cleanest cases of Indo-Iranian deity-continuity: same name + same theology in both Rig Veda (RV 2.35, the *Apām Napāt* hymn) and the Younger Avesta (Yašt 19, the *Zamyād Yašt* where Apąm Napāt is associated with the [[khvarenah|Khvarenah]] / cosmic glory). Cognate constructions appear in Roman religion (Neptūnus) and the Celtic *Nechtán*.
