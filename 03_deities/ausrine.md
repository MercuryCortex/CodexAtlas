# Aušrinė

Aušrinė is the Lithuanian goddess of the **morning star** — the planet Venus shining before sunrise — and of the **dawn** itself, the bringer and kindler of the day's first light. In the rich Lithuanian folk-song tradition (the *dainos*) she belongs to the celestial family of the sun-goddess **[[saule|Saulė]]**, as her radiant **daughter** (or her attendant maiden), whose task is to **light the fire and make ready the way** before Saulė rises and crosses the sky; her sister **Vakarinė** is the evening star (Venus at dusk), who makes the sun's bed at day's end. Her name comes from the same Indo-European root for "dawn" (*aus-*) that gives the Greek **[[eos-greek|Eos]]**, the Roman Aurora, and the Vedic Uṣas — and she is indeed the Baltic reflex of that ancient and beautiful **Indo-European dawn-goddess**, preserved with unusual vividness in Lithuanian song long after her cousins had faded into literature. Aušrinė embodies the daily miracle of first light and the Baltic poetry of the sky.
