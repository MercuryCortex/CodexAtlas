# Auðumla

Auðumla (Audhumla) is the **primeval cow** of Norse cosmogony — one of the first beings to take shape in the creation, and the nourisher from whom both the giants and (indirectly) the gods descend. Snorri's *Gylfaginning* tells that as the rime and ice of the great void **Ginnungagap** began to melt in the meeting of fire and frost, two beings emerged: the frost-giant **[[ymir]]**, and the cow **Auðumla**. From her udder flowed **four rivers of milk** that fed Ymir; and Auðumla herself fed by **licking the salty rime-stones** — and as she licked, over three days she freed from the ice a man, **Búri**, the grandfather of **Odin** and his brothers. So the entire lineage of the gods is, at its root, **licked into being by a cow**. Auðumla is the Norse embodiment of primal nourishment at the world's beginning — a striking parallel to the world-nourishing cosmogonic cow found elsewhere (e.g. the Hindu **[[kamadhenu|Kamadhenu]]**).
