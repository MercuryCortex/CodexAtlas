# Metis

Metis (*Mētis*, "cunning intelligence") is the Titaness who personifies the resourceful, practical wisdom the Greeks called *mētis* — the cleverness of the strategist, craftsman, and trickster. An Oceanid daughter of Oceanus and Tethys, she is the **first consort of [[zeus]]** and the one who gives him the emetic that forces Cronus to disgorge his swallowed children. But a prophecy warns Zeus that Metis will bear a child mightier than its father — so Zeus **swallows the pregnant Metis whole**, absorbing her cunning into his own sovereignty (and ensuring no usurper). From within him she counsels him still; and in time her daughter **[[athena]]** is born, fully armed, from Zeus's split head — inheriting her mother's *mētis*. Metis thus encodes a deep Greek idea: that legitimate rule must internalize wisdom, and that the cleverness which could overthrow power is, when absorbed, what stabilizes it.
