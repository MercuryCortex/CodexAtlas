# Amphitrite

Amphitrite (Greek *Amphitrítē*) is the queen of the sea — a Nereid (daughter of Nereus and Doris) or Oceanid who becomes the consort of **[[poseidon]]** and the personification of the sea's calm surface. In the myth, Poseidon courts her but she flees to the depths near Atlas; a dolphin finds and persuades her, for which Poseidon sets the dolphin among the stars (Eratosthenes, *Catasterismi*). With Poseidon she is mother of **[[triton]]** the merman-herald (and of Rhode and Benthesikyme). In Greek art she rides beside Poseidon in his sea-chariot, crowned with crab-claws and attended by Nereids; though seldom worshipped independently, she is the standard female personification of *thalassa* (the sea). The Romans loosely equate her with Salacia.
