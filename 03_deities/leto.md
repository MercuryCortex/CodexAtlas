# Leto

Leto (Greek *Lētṓ*; Roman *Latona*) is the Titaness daughter of Coeus and Phoebe and, by **[[zeus]]**, the mother of the twin Olympians **[[apollo]]** and **[[artemis]]**. Her central myth is the persecution of her pregnancy by the jealous **[[hera]]**: forbidden to give birth on any solid land, the wandering Leto finally bears her children on the floating island of **Delos** (which she anchors to the sea-floor in gratitude), clinging to a palm tree — an event narrated in the *Homeric Hymn to Apollo* and foundational to Delos's standing as a pan-Hellenic sanctuary. The Lycian peasants who deny the thirsty Leto water are turned to frogs (Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 6). Though rarely worshipped apart from her children, Leto's maternal dignity and the Delian/Delphic birth-cycle make her a structurally central figure of the Olympian system.
