# Kanaloa

Kanaloa is one of the **four great gods of Hawaiʻi** — with **[[kane-hawaiian|Kāne]]**, Kū, and Lono — and the deity of the **ocean**, particularly its far deeps, and of the dead. He is the Hawaiian continuation of the great Polynesian sea-god **[[tangaroa|Tangaroa]]** (Tahitian *Taʻaroa*, Samoan *Tagaloa*), a Proto-Polynesian inheritance — though his **cult position varies sharply across the Pacific**: where Taʻaroa is the supreme creator of the Society Islands, the Hawaiian Kanaloa is one god among four, defined above all by his pairing with **Kāne**. The two are inseparable in Hawaiian myth, journeying together across the islands thrusting their staffs into the ground to open **springs of fresh water** — Kāne the god of life and flowing water, Kanaloa of the salt deep, the bright and dark complements of the waters. He is associated with the **squid/octopus** (*heʻe*) and with healing, and in some later, missionary-influenced tellings his opposition to Kāne is recast in quasi-Satanic terms — a distortion of his original role as the necessary dark twin of the life-giving god. His listing only as a Tangaroa-cognate aka understated a genuinely major Hawaiian god.
