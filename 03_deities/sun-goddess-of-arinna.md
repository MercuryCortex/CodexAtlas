# Sun Goddess of Arinna

## Identity
The Sun Goddess of Arinna was the chief goddess and royal patron-deity of the Hittite empire. Her Hattic name was Wuru(n)šemu; her Hittite/Luwian epithet Arinniti ("she of Arinna") names her after her principal cult-city Arinna (location unidentified but somewhere in the Hittite heartland north of Hattusa). She is one of the most prominent gods of the Hittite imperial pantheon: the Great Kings invoke her alongside the Storm-God of Hatti as the divine sovereigns under whom they hold the kingship. Her cult goes back to the Hattic (pre-Indo-European) substrate of Anatolia, predating the Indo-European Hittite arrival.

Distinct from Hebat, although the late-13th-century Hittite queen Puduhepa identifies them in her prayer (CTH 384) — an imperial theological move integrating her Hurrian-Kizzuwatnan cult into the Hittite imperial system, not an original equivalence.

## Earliest attestation
- Hattic substrate cult — Hattic theonyms like Wuru(n)šemu attested from the earliest Hittite period (Old Kingdom, ~-1650).
- Boğazköy archive royal prayers and treaties throughout the Hittite imperial period (~-1400 to -1180).
- Most theologically explicit texts: Mursili II's Plague Prayer (CTH 376) and Puduhepa's Prayer (CTH 384).

## Mythological role
She is principally a *cult* deity rather than a mythological character. Her function in Hittite religion is sovereign-royal: she legitimizes the kingship, receives the king's most solemn prayers in time of crisis (Mursili II's prayers during the plague that swept Hatti after his father Suppiluliuma I's reign), and is sworn-by in international treaties as supreme divine guarantor.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[hebat]]** — *ancient-identification* under Queen Puduhepa, ~-1250. Best understood as imperial theological merger of two originally distinct goddesses.
- **Solar Goddess of Earth** — distinct chthonic counterpart in the Hittite system (the underworld sun); the Sun Goddess of Arinna is the celestial daytime sun.

## Iconography
Enthroned matron in polos crown; winged solar disc appears on Hittite royal seals (also used for the king himself, marking his solar-sovereign status under her patronage).

## Disputes
- **Identity with the Sun God of Heaven.** Hittite religion has multiple sun deities — the (feminine) Sun Goddess of Arinna, the (masculine) Sun God of Heaven, and the Sun Goddess of Earth (chthonic). Their relations and overlaps are complex and contested (Singer 2002 reviews positions).
- **Original Hattic vs. Hittite identity.** The cult is clearly Hattic-substrate, but how much of the imperial-period theology is Indo-European reinterpretation is contested.
