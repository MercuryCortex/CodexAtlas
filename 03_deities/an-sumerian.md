# An / Anu

The Sumerian sky-god, originally the supreme deity of the Mesopotamian pantheon. By the second millennium BCE Anu's active worship was largely superseded by Enlil (Nippur) and later Marduk (Babylon), though Anu retained nominal seniority as "king of the gods." Cult center: Uruk (the Eanna temple, shared with Inanna).
