# Gibil

Gibil (Sumerian *Bil-gi*; Akkadian **Girra/Girru**) is the Mesopotamian **fire-god of the forge and of ritual purification** — the divine flame in its constructive and cleansing aspects, as against the destructive conflagration. As god of **smelting and metallurgy** he presides over the crucible and the refining of metals, his fire the agent that separates the pure from the dross. As god of **purifying fire** he is a central deity of apotropaic ritual: in the great anti-witchcraft series **Maqlû** ("Burning") it is Gibil/Girra's flame that consumes the figurines of the warlocks and witches and burns away their evil, turning their sorcery back on themselves. His refining, purgative fire makes him the close counterpart of **[[nuska]]**, the lamp-and-fire vizier of Enlil — the benevolent, ordering face of the same element that, unleashed, becomes catastrophe.
