# Louhi

Louhi is the formidable **sorceress-queen of Pohjola**, the cold and hostile land of the North, and the great **antagonist of the Kalevala**, the Finnish national epic compiled by Elias Lönnrot from Karelian and Finnish oral runo-poetry. A mistress of powerful **magic** — she can raise storms, conjure plagues, and even **hide the sun and the moon** and steal fire from the people of Kalevala — she rules Pohjola as a shrewd and dangerous matriarch, mother of beautiful daughters whom the heroes desire. Her pivotal act sets the epic's central treasure in motion: she promises the hand of the **Maid of the North** to whoever can forge the **Sampo**, a magical mill of endless plenty, and the smith **[[ilmarinen|Ilmarinen]]** accomplishes it — but Louhi keeps the Sampo for Pohjola. When the heroes led by **[[vainamoinen|Väinämöinen]]** sail to steal it back, Louhi pursues them transformed into a **gigantic eagle**, and in the climactic struggle the Sampo is shattered and lost to the sea. Louhi embodies the wild, magical, adversarial power of the North — a worthy and never-wholly-defeated opponent to the heroes of song.
