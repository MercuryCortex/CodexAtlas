# Jagannath

Jagannath (Sanskrit *Jagannātha*, "Lord of the Universe") is the presiding deity of **Puri** in Odisha, worshipped as a form of **[[krishna]]**/**[[vishnu]]** in a strikingly **abstract wooden image** — a dark, stump-armed figure with huge round eyes, utterly unlike standard Hindu iconography. He stands in a triad with his white-faced brother **Balabhadra** (Balarama) and his yellow-faced sister **Subhadra**. Jagannath is the textbook case of the **Sanskritization of a tribal cult**: scholarship traces his *daru-brahman* (wood-form) to an older Savara (tribal) deity, *Nila Madhava*, absorbed into Vaishnava orthodoxy — and uniquely the images are ritually rebuilt from neem wood every few years (the *Nabakalebara*). His **Rath Yatra**, in which the three deities are pulled through Puri on colossal temple-cars, is one of the largest religious festivals on earth — and gave the English language the word *juggernaut*, for the unstoppable car of the god.
