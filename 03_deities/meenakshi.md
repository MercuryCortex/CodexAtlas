# Meenakshi

Meenakshi (Tamil/Sanskrit *Mīnākṣī*, "fish-eyed," for her long fish-shaped eyes) is the sovereign goddess of **Madurai** and one of the great regional goddesses of Tamil Nadu — the presiding deity of the vast **Meenakshi Amman temple**, whose towering, polychrome *gopuram*s are among the icons of South Indian sacred architecture. Her myth is one of divine sovereignty: born from a Pandya king's fire-sacrifice as a warrior-princess with **three breasts**, raised and crowned as a conquering queen, she sweeps across the world in victory until she confronts **Shiva** on Mount Kailasa — at the sight of him her third breast vanishes, the sign foretold of meeting her destined husband. He follows her to Madurai as **[[sundareswarar]]** ("the beautiful lord"), and their celestial wedding, the **Tirukalyanam**, is re-enacted each year as the city's grandest festival, with Vishnu giving away the bride. Theologically she is a form of **[[parvati]]**/Devi, but the Madurai cult inverts the usual hierarchy: here the **goddess reigns** and Shiva is her consort — a striking instance of Tamil goddess-primacy.
