# Laima

Laima ("Luck/Fate") is the Lithuanian-Latvian goddess of fate — she determines the life-destiny (*laima*, the same word as the deity name) of each person at the moment of birth. She appears at the threshold during childbirth, is consulted in life-transition moments, and her cuckoo-bird calls in spring prophesy the years of life remaining to whoever counts them. Death too is her domain: she establishes when and how each life ends.

The cuckoo's call as a fate-oracle is one of the most widely attested elements of Baltic folk religion, preserved into the 20th century: counting the cuckoo's calls in spring answers the question "how many more years do I have?" — Laima answers through her bird.

Laima is the Baltic form of the PIE fate-goddess tradition, paralleling Greek [[the-moirai]] and Roman Parcae in function, though as a single deity rather than a triple. Her intimate household presence (at births and deaths) mirrors [[zemyna]]'s intimate earth-presence — both are Baltic deities embedded in domestic practice rather than in separate temple cult.
