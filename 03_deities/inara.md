# Inara

Inara is the Hittite **tutelary or protective goddess** — a deity of the **LAMMA** type, guardians of the wild animals, the open land, and those under divine protection — and the **daughter of the Storm-god [[tarhunna|Tarhunna]]** (Teshub in the Hurrian layer). Her great role is in the **Illuyanka myth**, recited at the **purulli** spring-festival that celebrated the renewal of the land. After the dragon **[[illuyanka|Illuyanka]]** defeats the Storm-god, Inara devises the counter-stratagem: she lays out an enormous **feast** with vats of drink and lures the serpent and its young from their lair to gorge themselves; once they are too bloated and drunk to slither back into their hole, her mortal lover-and-helper **Hupasiya** binds them with a rope, and the Storm-god comes and slays Illuyanka. Inara rewards Hupasiya with a house on a cliff but forbids him to look out toward his human family — a taboo he breaks, to his ruin. She embodies the cunning, protective divine power that turns the tables on chaos so that order and fertility can return.
