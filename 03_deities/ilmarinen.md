# Ilmarinen

Ilmarinen (Finnish, from *ilma*, "air/sky") is the **eternal smith** of Finnic mythology and one of the three central heroes of the **Kalevala**, alongside the sage **[[vainamoinen]]** and the rash Lemminkäinen. His primordial credential is cosmic: "he it was who forged the sky, who hammered out the lid of heaven, so that no hammer-mark is seen, no trace of the tongs remains" — marking him as a survival of an older **sky-/weather-god** layer (overlapping the supreme **[[ukko]]**) re-cast as a culture-hero artisan. His great deed is forging the **Sampo**, the magical mill that grinds out grain, salt, and gold without end, which the witch-queen **Louhi** of the Northland (Pohjola) demands as the bride-price for her daughter; later, when she refuses to share its plenty, Ilmarinen joins Väinämöinen's expedition to steal it back, in the course of which the Sampo is shattered into the sea — explaining why the world has both abundance and want. He belongs to the great cross-cultural family of **divine smiths** (compare **[[goibniu|Goibniu]]**, Hephaestus, Kothar, and Wayland).
