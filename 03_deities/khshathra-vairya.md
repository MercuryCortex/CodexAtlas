# Khshathra Vairya

## Identity
**Khshathra Vairya** (Avestan *Xšaθra Vairya*, "Desirable Dominion / Chosen Sovereignty"; Pahlavi *Shahrēvar*; Modern Persian *Shahrivar* — the sixth month of the Iranian calendar) is **the third of the six Amesha Spentas** (the "Bountiful Immortals" through whom Ahura Mazda creates and rules). Khshathra personifies **rightly-exercised divine sovereignty** — the kingdom-of-Ahura-Mazda as it ought-to-be and as it will-be at the cosmic renovation. Among the seven created elements assigned to the heptad, Khshathra presides over **metals** — a connection that becomes eschatologically operative when, at the Frashokereti (cosmic renovation), the molten-metal river purifies the resurrected bodies.

## Earliest attestation
- **Gathas** ([[phase-2-002-gathas-of-zarathustra]]): named throughout Yasna 28-53, often paired with Asha-Vahishta (Truth) as the cosmic-governance principle.
- **Younger Avesta** ([[phase-2-004-yasna-younger-avesta]]): personified Amesha Spenta with hymnic invocation.
- **Pahlavi tradition** (especially Bundahishn 34): Khshathra's metals-element governance becomes eschatologically active at Frashokereti.
- **Modern Iranian calendar**: *Shahrivar* (August–September) preserves the Amesha Spenta name in continuous Iranian cultural identity.

## The metals-eschatology connection
The seemingly-mundane element-assignment (Khshathra = metals) is not cosmologically incidental. At the end of history, the cosmic renovation ([[doctrine-frashokereti]]) includes a **river of molten metal** through which all resurrected humanity passes — for the righteous, it feels like warm milk; for the wicked, it consumes them. The metals-assignment makes Khshathra the **instrument of eschatological justice** in the renovation. The cosmic-kingdom and the cosmic-purification share a single Amesha-Spenta-patron because the kingdom is realized through the purification.

## Cross-tradition: Khshathra Vairya ↔ "Kingdom of God"
The Avestan *xšaθra vairya* — "the kingdom that is desirable / to be chosen" — is structurally and semantically parallel to the Second-Temple Jewish *malkut elohim* and the early-Christian *basileia tou theou* (kingdom of God). Both are:
- **Eschatological-future divine sovereignty** arriving at history's end.
- **Personally-chosen / desirable** (Hebrew *malkut* + Avestan *vairya* both carry the "choosing / accepting the kingdom" sense).
- **Cosmic-scope** rather than territorial.

The Avestan formulation **predates the Jewish-Christian formulation by centuries**. The Persian-period (-539 to -332) cultural-contact window makes Zoroastrian influence on the developing Second-Temple kingdom-of-God conception plausible (Hultgård 1998). Direct lexical borrowing is not demonstrable; structural-conceptual influence is the standard scholarly position.

## Disputes
- **Gathic personification.** Whether the Gathic stratum treats Khshathra as primarily abstract-principle or already-personified-divine-being is debated. Boyce 1975 reads the Gathic Khshathra as principle-with-implicit-personification; the Younger-Avestan stratum fully personifies.
- **Persian → Jewish kingdom-of-God transmission scope.** How much of the Second-Temple shift toward eschatological-future-kingdom framing is Persian-influenced vs. internal Jewish development is contested. Hultgård 1998 favors substantial Persian contribution; some Jewish-Studies scholarship resists.
