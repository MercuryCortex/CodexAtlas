# Uriel

Archangel of light and apocalyptic interpretation. Most prominent in [[phase-3-004-1-enoch|1 Enoch]] (the Book of the Watchers, the Astronomical Book) and in 4 Ezra (2 Esdras), where Uriel serves as Ezra's interpreting angel. The name (Hebrew *Uri'el*) means "God is my light" or "light of God."

Uriel is the most variably-attested of the four chief archangels. Listed in some Christian and Jewish sources but never canonically named in the Hebrew Bible or the New Testament. The Catholic Church removed him from the calendar of saints in +745 (Synod of Rome) after the apocryphal traditions about him were judged unreliable, but Eastern Orthodox tradition retained him.

In the Hekhalot literature and later Kabbalistic angelology, Uriel is one of the four cardinal-direction archangels (Michael east, Gabriel west, Uriel north or south depending on tradition, Raphael the remaining direction).
