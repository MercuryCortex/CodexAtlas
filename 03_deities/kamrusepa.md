# Kamrusepa

Kamrusepa (*Kamrušepa*; Hattic **Kataḫzip/uri**) is the Hittite **goddess of healing, medicine, and magic** — the divine patroness of the *ritual word*, the incantation by which sickness, anger, and impurity are driven out. Her central mythological moment comes in the great **Telipinu myth**: once the vanished god has been found (stung awake by Hannahanna's bee) but is still seething with destructive rage, it is **Kamrusepa** who steps forward to perform the elaborate **purification ritual** — gathering the anger, the wrath, the "evil" from his body and limbs and dispelling them, so that Telipinu returns at last as a benevolent god of growth. This episode served as the mythic charter for Hittite healing- and purification-magic, and Kamrusepa is repeatedly invoked in actual ritual texts as the goddess whose precedent the human practitioner re-enacts. She embodies the Hittite conviction that disorder — whether in a god, a body, or a household — is cured by the right rite and the right words.
