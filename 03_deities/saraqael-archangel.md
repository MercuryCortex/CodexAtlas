# Saraqael (Archangel)

## Identity
**Saraqael** (also *Sariel* in some recensions; the manuscript tradition shows variant orthography) is one of the **seven archangels** named in 1 Enoch 20 — specifically *"set over the spirits of those who sin in spirit"* (1 Enoch 20:6, Nickelsburg-VanderKam 2004). The role is the *inner-moral-life* guardian: where Michael guards the people, Gabriel paradise and cherubim, Raphael the spirits-of-humans-generally, Uriel the world-and-Tartarus, Saraqael's specific portfolio is the moral interior — the *spirits* of those whose sin is internal-spiritual rather than external-bodily.

The Ge'ez recension preserves the name in the form *Saraqael*; some Greek mss have *Sariel* (which creates confusion with the figure called *Sariel* in some Qumran fragments — a separate Watchers-related figure). Modern critical editions (Nickelsburg 2001) generally accept *Saraqael* as the original form in 1 Enoch 20 with *Sariel* as a recensional variant.

## Earliest attestation
- **1 Enoch 20:6** — the canonical seven-archangels naming.
- **Ge'ez Mashafa Henok** — only complete textual witness.
- **4QEn^c (4Q204)** — Aramaic fragment containing portions of 1 Enoch 20 with variant name-forms.

## Mythological role
The moral-interior archangel. Where the broader Enochic angelology assigns to each archangel a domain (cosmic, anthropological, eschatological), Saraqael's domain is the *inner spiritual life* of humans — those who sin in thought, intention, or hidden disposition rather than in overt act. The role is theologically continuous with later Christian and Jewish development of conscience-and-interior-judgment angelology.

In Ethiopian Orthodox iconography, Saraqael appears in seven-archangel compositions alongside Michael, Gabriel, Raphael, Uriel, Raguel, and Remiel.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[uriel-archangel]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: variant-name conflation between Sariel and Uriel in some recensions.
- Mentioned in some Gnostic and Manichaean angelological texts under variant names.

## Iconography
Not strongly standardized. In Ethiopian Orthodox seven-archangel paintings, distinguished by position in the heptad rather than by specific attributes.

## Disputes
- **Saraqael vs. Sariel as the original 1 Enoch 20:6 name.** Nickelsburg 2001 accepts *Saraqael* as primary; some older scholarship treats *Sariel* as primary.
- **Identity with "Sariel" in Qumran Watchers fragments.** A different "Sariel" appears in 4Q201/4Q202 in the Watchers-by-name list — clearly *not* an archangel. The two Sariels are distinct figures sharing a name.
