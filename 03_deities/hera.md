# Hera

## Identity
Hera is the queen of the Greek Olympian gods, consort and sister of Zeus, presiding over marriage, women, and legitimate sovereignty. Her major cult centers were Argos (her oldest and largest temple, the Heraion of Argos), Samos (the Samian Heraion, one of the largest temples of the Greek world), Olympia, and Tiryns. The Linear B tablets attest "e-ra" already in the 14th c. BCE, making her one of the Mycenaean-attested Greek deities (alongside Zeus, Poseidon, Hermes, Dionysus, and others).

In Homer, Hera is the jealous wife of a serially adulterous Zeus, particularly hostile to his mortal lovers and their offspring (Heracles is her name's enemy from birth — *Hēra-klēs* "glory of Hera" is one ironic etymology). In cult she is something larger: a goddess of female adult sovereignty and legitimate marital alliance, central to the religious life of Argive women. The Roman interpretatio identified her with Juno from early contact.
