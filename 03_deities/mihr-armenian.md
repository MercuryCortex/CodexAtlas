# Mihr

## Identity
Mihr is the **Armenian god of the sun, fire, and heavenly light** — the son of [[aramazd]] in the pre-Christian Armenian pantheon, and the Armenian form of the Iranian [[mithra-zoroastrian]] (Mithra), god of the covenant and the sun.

## Earliest attestation
Worshipped at the temple of **Bagayarich** in the province of Derjan; attested in the classical Armenian historians (Agathangelos, Movses Khorenatsi) among the gods overthrown at the Christianization of Armenia (c. 301 CE).

## Mythological role — the sun and the rock-door
As an Iranian-derived solar and fire god, Mihr embodies light, truth, and the heavenly order. His name and persona pass into the great Armenian epic *Sasna Tsṛer* (**Daredevils of Sasun**) as the hero **Mher**, who at the epic's close is shut inside the rock of **Agravakar**, to emerge only when the world is made just — a striking survival of a pre-Christian solar-deity into Christian-era epic.

## Cross-tradition
Mihr is a node on the **Mithra** transmission and a member of the **sun-god** neighborhood: Iranian [[mithra-zoroastrian]] → Armenian Mihr, alongside the solar deities Ra, Surya, Helios, Sol, Shamash, Amaterasu, and Inti.

## Iconography
Associated with the sun-disc and fire; in epic, with the rock-door of Agravakar.
