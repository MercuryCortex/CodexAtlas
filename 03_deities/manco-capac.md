# Manco Cápac

Manco Cápac (Quechua *Manqu Qhapaq*) is the **deified first Inca** — the founder-ancestor of the imperial dynasty and, as the son of the sun-god **[[inti]]**, the divine origin of the Inca claim to rule. Two great origin myths center on him. In the **Lake Titicaca** version, Inti, grieved by the savagery of early humanity, sends his children Manco Cápac and his sister-wife **Mama Ocllo** from the lake with a **golden staff** (*tapac-yauri*), commanding them to settle wherever it sinks fully into the earth; it sinks at the hill of Huanacauri above **Cusco**, and there they found the city and teach men agriculture and women weaving. In the **Pacaritambo** version, Manco and his three brother-and-sister pairs (the *Ayar*) emerge from a cave, and through a series of petrifactions and contests Manco alone reaches Cusco to found it. Either way he is the bridge between the solar god and the human dynasty — worshipped as a *huaca* whose mummy and golden image the later Incas venerated as the source of their sacred kingship.
