# Namtar

Namtar (*Namtaru*, "Fate" / "the Cutting-off") is the Mesopotamian personification of **doom** — specifically the **fated hour of death** — and the chief **vizier (sukkal) of [[ereshkigal|Ereshkigal]]**, queen of the underworld. As the decree of death made into a being, he is also the bringer of **disease**, commanding sixty afflictions of the body: in *Inanna's Descent to the Netherworld* it is Namtar who fastens the sixty diseases upon the captive Inanna at Ereshkigal's word. He serves as the **messenger** who moves between the netherworld and the assembly of the gods above, summoning and fetching the dead. Inexorable and unbribable, Namtar embodies the Mesopotamian sense that the time of death is *fixed* and carried out by an administrative power of the underworld — fate as a functionary.
