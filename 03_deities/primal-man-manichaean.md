# Primal Man (Manichaean)

## Identity

The **Primal Man** (Aramaic *Naša Qadmāyā*; Middle Persian *Ohrmizd Bay*) is the central redemptive figure of Mani's cosmogony — the divine being voluntarily sent into the realm of Darkness by the **Father of Greatness** to prevent the invasion of Darkness into the Realm of Light.

## The Cosmogonic Drama

Mani's cosmogony (the most elaborate and systematic of the Gnostic-adjacent traditions) unfolds in three stages:

**Stage 1 — The Attack**: The Prince of Darkness invades the Realm of Light from below. The Father of Greatness, not wishing to send one of his eternal aeons into danger, "calls forth" (creates) the **Primal Man** as a defender. The Primal Man dons five divine armors (Air, Wind, Light, Water, Fire — his five Light-sons) and descends to fight Darkness.

**Stage 2 — Defeat and Swallowing**: The Primal Man is defeated. The demons of Darkness swallow his five Light-sons, consuming the divine light. The Primal Man himself is left in a stupor (*sinking* or *numbness*) in the realm of Darkness, separated from his Light-elements.

**Stage 3 — Rescue and Cosmic Machinery**: The Father of Greatness sends the **Living Spirit** to rescue the Primal Man (the handshake of the Living Spirit with the Primal Man's raised hand is a founding icon of Manichaean art). The **bodies of the defeated demons** are used to create the cosmos — earth, heavens, sun, moon — as machinery for extracting and liberating the swallowed divine light. Humans are created last, containing the densest concentrations of divine light in matter.

## Soteriological Significance

The Primal Man's five swallowed Light-sons are the divine spark in every living being. The entire cosmos is a light-extraction device. Human gnosis — the recognition of one's divine origin — is the primary mechanism of liberation. When enough light is extracted and returned to the Realm of Light, the cosmos is destroyed in a final conflagration, and the Primal Man's liberation is complete. This is the most explicitly Anthropos-structured soteriology in world religion: **the cosmic project is the liberation of the dismembered divine Cosmic Human from matter**.
