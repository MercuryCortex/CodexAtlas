# Dian Cécht

Dian Cécht is the **divine physician of the Tuatha Dé Danann**, the healer-god of Irish myth. His two most famous deeds frame the **Second Battle of Mag Tuired**. First, when King **[[nuada]]** loses a hand in the First Battle — a blemish that disqualifies him from kingship — Dian Cécht fashions him a fully articulated **silver hand**, earning the king his epithet *Nuada Airgetlám*, "Nuada of the Silver Arm." Second, during the great battle Dian Cécht and his children tend a healing **Well of Sláine** (Health) into which mortally wounded warriors of the Tuatha Dé are cast and over which incantations are sung, so that they rise whole the next day — a divine field-hospital that gives the gods a decisive edge. Yet his story carries a dark thread of professional jealousy: when his gifted son **Miach** outdoes him by regenerating Nuada's own flesh-and-blood hand, Dian Cécht kills him in a rage, and from Miach's grave grow 365 healing herbs — which Dian Cécht then scatters so that no one would learn their full virtue. He is the Irish counterpart of the divine-healer type (compare **[[asclepius-greek|Asclepius]]** and Eshmun).
