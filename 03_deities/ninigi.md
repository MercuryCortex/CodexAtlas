# Ninigi-no-Mikoto

Ninigi-no-Mikoto is the **heavenly grandson** at the hinge of Japanese imperial mythology — the **grandson of the sun-goddess [[amaterasu|Amaterasu]]**, sent down from the High Plain of Heaven to rule the islands of Japan in the foundational episode known as the ***tenson kōrin***, "the descent of the heavenly grandson." Amaterasu entrusts him with the **Three Sacred Treasures** — the mirror (Yata no Kagami), the sword (Kusanagi), and the jewel (Yasakani no Magatama) — which become the **imperial regalia**, the tangible proof of heaven's mandate; he descends upon **Mount Takachiho** in Kyūshū. There he marries the blossom-princess **[[konohanasakuya-hime|Konohanasakuya-hime]]**, choosing her over her enduring rock-sister Iwanaga-hime — a fateful choice that, the myth explains, made the lives of the emperors (and of all people) **beautiful but brief, like blossoms** rather than lasting like stone. Through his great-grandson, the first emperor **Jimmu**, Ninigi is the divine **ancestor of the imperial dynasty**. His very name evokes the ripening abundance of rice, binding sovereignty to the fertility of the land.
