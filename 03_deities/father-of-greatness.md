# Father of Greatness

The supreme deity of Manichaean theology — the personification of the Realm of Light, co-eternal with the Prince of Darkness across the boundary that the rigorously dualistic Manichaean cosmology posits. The Father dwells in his five dwellings of intelligence, knowledge, reason, thought, and consideration, and emanates a series of five emanations (Friend of the Lights, Great Builder, Living Spirit, Mother of Life, Primal Man) to respond to the Darkness's invasion of the borderlands. In the Iranian Manichaean register, Mani himself substitutes Zurvan as the Father's name — a deliberate missionary equivalence designed to make Manichaeism legible to Zoroastrian audiences (though Zoroastrian theology proper does not endorse Zurvanism's primacy of Zurvan).
