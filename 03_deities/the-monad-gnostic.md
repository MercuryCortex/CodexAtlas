# The Monad (Gnostic)

A concept-entity: the absolutely transcendent and apophatic divine source in Sethian and Valentinian systems. The Apocryphon of John opens with an extended negative theology: "He is the invisible Spirit, of whom it is not right to think of him as a god, or as anything similar [...] he is illimitable, unsearchable, immeasurable, invisible, eternal, ineffable." The Monad's first self-thought is Barbelo; from this initial dyad the entire Pleroma proceeds.

Parallels with the Neoplatonic One (Plotinus, Enneads V–VI) are extensive enough that Turner 2001 argues for genuine intellectual interaction between Sethian Gnostic and Middle Platonist circles in the second and third centuries; whether the influence runs both ways or primarily Platonist-to-Gnostic is debated.
