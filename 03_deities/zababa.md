# Zababa

Zababa is the Mesopotamian **war-god and tutelary deity of the city of Kish** — one of the oldest and most prestigious cities of Sumer, whose kingship ("the kingship of Kish") was a byword for hegemony over the land. As Kish's city-god, enthroned in his temple **É-meteursag**, Zababa is a god of **battle and royal legitimacy**, frequently equated with the great warrior-gods **[[ninurta]]** and **[[nergal]]**, and invoked in the titulary and curses of kings (Hammurabi calls on Zababa and Ishtar in the epilogue of his law-code to break the weapons of any who deface it). His consort is **[[ishtar-akkadian|Ishtar]]** in her warlike aspect, the two together making Kish a double-temple city of martial divinity. Zababa exemplifies the Mesopotamian fusion of **city, war, and kingship** in a single tutelary god.
