# Damballa

## Identity
The primordial *lwa* — sky-serpent, creator-god, source of wisdom and fertility. Damballa is one of the oldest and most senior *lwa* in Haitian Vodou, transmitted directly from Fon-Dahomean Dan / Aida-Hwedo religion (the cosmic serpent who surrounds the world, holding it together). He moves slowly and speaks little; he is the elder of the Rada nasyon.

## Earliest attestation
Direct West-African origin (Fon Dan); Saint-Domingue documentation from 18th c.; standard ethnographic treatments from Métraux onward.

## Mythological role
Damballa created the world by uncoiling himself. He is paired with Aida-Wedo, the rainbow-serpent, his cosmic consort. He moves in white — devotees wear white when serving him; he is offered fresh water, an egg, white foods, and white flowers. He does not speak through his possessed; instead, possession by Damballa manifests as serpentine movement, hissing, climbing.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **Saint Patrick** — *folk-syncretism*: the snake-iconography associated with St. Patrick (who, by Catholic legend, drove the snakes from Ireland) pairs with Damballa-as-snake. The pairing is operational rather than theological: St. Patrick *removes* snakes, Damballa *is* the cosmic snake — the pairing rides on the iconographic association without theological alignment.
- **Moses** (in some lineages) — *folk-syncretism*: Moses's serpent-staff iconography.
- **Dan / Aido-Hwedo (Fon West Africa)** — *manuscript-transmission*: direct origin.

## Iconography
White — exclusively. Snake imagery; an egg (cosmic-egg cosmogony); fresh water.
