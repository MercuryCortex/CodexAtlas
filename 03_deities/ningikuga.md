# Ningikuga

The **"Lady of the Pure Reed"** — a Sumerian reed-marsh goddess of the southern Mesopotamian wetlands. Her significance in the vault is genealogical: she is named as the mother of [[nin-gal-sumerian]] (Ningal) in the Ur cult-tradition, which makes her grandmother of [[inanna-sumerian]] (Venus) and [[utu-shamash]] (the sun) in that genealogy.

The reed-marsh complex of the Persian Gulf head — the original landscape of Sumerian civilization before historical drying and damming — is preserved in deity-names like Ningikuga and in the reed-bundle standard of Inanna at Uruk, suggesting an older substratum of wetland-cult that the formal pantheon partially overlays.
