# The Erinyes (the Furies)

## Identity
Chthonic avenging goddesses, three (sometimes more) — **Alecto** ("the Unceasing"), **Megaera** ("the Grudging"), **Tisiphone** ("Avenger of Murder"). Born from the blood of [[uranus-greek]]'s castration falling on Gaia (Hesiod *Theogony* 183–185). They hunt the perpetrators of crimes against the **blood-kin order** — matricides, patricides, kin-murderers, oath-breakers, suppliant-violators. Their pursuit is implacable; they cannot be bribed, persuaded, or evaded.

## Mythological role
**Aeschylus's *Eumenides*** (the third play of [[phase-2-031-aeschylus-oresteia|the Oresteia]], 458 BCE) is the foundational dramatic-theological treatment: the Erinyes pursue Orestes for the matricide of Clytemnestra; Athena, presiding at the **first homicide trial on the Areopagus**, casts the deciding vote that acquits him; the Erinyes, infuriated at being overruled by the Olympian-civic court, are then persuaded by Athena to accept a new role as **Eumenides ("the Kindly Ones")** — chthonic protectors of Athens, installed under the Areopagus with civic honors. This is **the foundational Greek mythological narrative of the transformation of vengeance into civic justice**, a structural pattern (opus-hellenic-3's cross-tradition edge) that prefigures Christian appropriation-of-pagan-religion patterns: integrate-rather-than-destroy.
