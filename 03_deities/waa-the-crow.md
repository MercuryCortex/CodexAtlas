# Waa the Crow

## Identity

Waa (also Wahn, Waang) is the **crow-ancestor** of the Kulin Nation in southeastern Australia. He is one half of the foundational mythic binary that structures Kulin society: every Kulin person belongs to either the Eaglehawk moiety (descended from [[bunjil]], the wedge-tailed eagle) or the Crow moiety (descended from Waa). The binary determines whom one may marry, who one's kin obligations fall to, and which totemic species one stands in relation to.

In some Kulin traditions Waa is the **fire-bringer** — the deity who steals fire from the seven sisters (the Pleiades) and brings it down to humans, an act either heroic or trickster-ambiguous depending on the telling. Waa's role as fire-bringer parallels the Prometheus / Maui / Coyote pattern across cultures, though the southeastern-Australian fire-bringing myth is independent of all of these.
