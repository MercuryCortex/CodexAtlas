# Huangdi

Huangdi (黄帝, the **Yellow Emperor**) is the foundational sovereign culture-hero of Chinese civilization — first of the legendary Five Emperors, mythic ancestor of the Han Chinese ("descendants of Yan and Huang"), and one of the most important figures in both imperial ideology and Daoist religion. Tradition credits him and his reign with the invention of writing, the calendar, the compass-cart (*south-pointing chariot*), medicine, sericulture (through his wife Leizu), and warfare against the rebel **Chiyou**. As a *Di* ("Thearch") he is the Yellow Emperor of the **Center** in the five-phase cosmology (yellow = earth = center). In **Han Huang-Lao Daoism** he is paired with **Laozi** as joint patron of sage-rulership and the arts of longevity, and the foundational medical classic the *Huangdi Neijing* ("Yellow Emperor's Inner Canon") is cast as his dialogue with his physician. His complete absence from the vault was a major gap, since he stands at the head of the entire Chinese sacred-historical and Daoist tradition.
