# Shapash

Shapash (Ugaritic *špš*) is the sun-goddess of Canaanite Ugarit — titled *nyr ilm*, "the Torch of the Gods." Unlike the masculine sun-gods of Mesopotamia and Egypt, the West-Semitic luminary is **feminine** (a notable gender divergence from Mesopotamian **[[utu-shamash]]**, with whom she is otherwise functionally identical as the all-seeing solar judge). Daughter of **[[el-canaanite]]**, she crosses the sky by day and the underworld by night, which makes her the natural **psychopomp** and arbiter between the living and the dead.

## Role in the Baal Cycle
Shapash is the great mediator of the **[[phase-1-013-baal-cycle]]**. After Baal's death she helps **[[anat]]** recover his body for burial; in the climactic confrontation (KTU 1.6 VI) she warns **[[mot]]** that El the Bull will overturn his throne if he persists against the restored Baal, brokering Mot's submission and Baal's return to kingship. In the Ugaritic ritual and incantation texts she presides over the *rapiʾūma* (the deified dead) and is invoked against serpents and venom — the sun who illuminates and orders the netherworld. Her counterpart-by-function Shamash is a Mesopotamian transmission-parallel, not a shared membership.
