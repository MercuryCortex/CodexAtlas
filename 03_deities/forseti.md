# Forseti

Forseti (Old Norse, "the presiding one / chairman") is the **Æsir god of justice and reconciliation**. Snorri's *Gylfaginning* (32) makes him the son of **[[baldr]]** and Nanna and describes his hall **Glitnir**, roofed with silver and pillared with gold, "and all who come to him with hard legal disputes leave reconciled — it is the best court among gods and men." He is almost certainly identical with the Frisian god **Fosite**, whose sacred island (Fositesland, probably Helgoland) with its holy spring and untouchable cattle is described in Alcuin's *Life of St. Willibrord*. A separate, later Frisian legend (tied to the *Lex Frisionum*) tells how the Frisian law was revealed by a mysterious thirteenth lawspeaker bearing a golden axe — a figure medieval and modern tradition has connected to Fosite/Forseti as the god who presided over the legal assembly. As the divine guarantor of lawful arbitration he is the mythic patron of the *thing* (the Germanic legal assembly), embodying the Norse ideal that even violent disputes should end in settlement rather than feud.
