# Ninhursag / Nintud

The Sumerian mother-goddess, with multiple regional names: Ninhursag ("lady of the foothills/mountain"), Nintud/Nintur ("lady of the birth-giving"), Ninmah ("exalted lady"), Damgalnuna ("great spouse of the prince" — when paired with Enki), and Akkadian Bēlet-ilī ("lady of the gods"). She is the principal addressee of [[phase-1-001-kesh-temple-hymn]] under her local name Nintud, ruler of the Keš temple. In [[phase-1-006-atrahasis]] she co-creates the first humans with Enki by mixing clay with the flesh and blood of a slain god.
