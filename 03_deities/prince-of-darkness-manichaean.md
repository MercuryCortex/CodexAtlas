# Prince of Darkness (Manichaean)

The supreme principle of evil in Manichaean theology — co-eternal with and ontologically independent from the Father of Greatness. The Prince of Darkness rules the Realm of Darkness with its five elements (smoke, fire, wind, water, darkness) and its monstrous denizens. The cosmic story turns on his invasion of the borderlands of Light and the Father's emanative response, leading to the present *mixture* of Light and Darkness in the cosmos that Manichaean ascetic practice attempts to undo. In Iranian Manichaean register, identified with Ahriman.
