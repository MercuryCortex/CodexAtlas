# Raphael

Healer-archangel of the Abrahamic traditions. Centrally featured in the deuterocanonical Book of Tobit (~-200), where Raphael — appearing in human form as "Azarias" — guides young Tobias on a journey, helps him win his bride Sarah, and heals Tobit's blindness. The narrative is the longest archangel-as-character episode in Jewish-Christian canon.

Raphael's name in Hebrew (*rafa'el*) means "God heals." Catholic and Eastern Orthodox tradition venerates him as patron of healing, travelers, and the medical profession. He appears in the Hekhalot literature and in Christian apocalypses (1 Enoch's list of seven archangels). Less prominent in Quranic angelology than [[gabriel-archangel|Jibril]] but tradition-attested as **Israfil** in Islamic eschatology (the angel who will sound the trumpet of resurrection).
