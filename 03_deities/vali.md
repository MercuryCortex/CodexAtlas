# Váli

Váli is the Norse god of **vengeance** — a son of **[[odin]]**, begotten expressly to avenge the murder of **[[baldr]]**. The Eddic poem *Baldrs draumar* foretells his birth: Odin lies with the giantess **Rindr**, and their son Váli, driven by a single purpose, **grows to manhood in one day** and — without washing his hands or combing his hair until the deed is done — slays **Höðr**, the blind brother who had unwittingly killed Baldr with the mistletoe. Váli embodies the Norse imperative of blood-vengeance compressed into mythic form: the avenger who suspends all else until justice is exacted. He is also one of the small band of gods named to **survive Ragnarök** and dwell in the renewed world (*Vafþrúðnismál* 51), alongside the returning Baldr he was born to avenge — vengeance and reconciliation closing the same circle. (He is distinct from Váli son of Loki.)
