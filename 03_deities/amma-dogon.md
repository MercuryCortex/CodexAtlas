# Amma

## Identity
Amma is the supreme creator god of the Dogon people of the Bandiagara Escarpment (Mali and Burkina Faso). He is an absolute, self-sufficient first principle who existed before all things — a spinning egg containing the four cardinal directions and the seeds of creation. Amma's creative act is double: he first throws a clod of clay outward to form the Earth (a female being), but this act is impeded — the termite mound (*the female organ*) prevents proper union, producing chaos and the primordial trickster-being Ogo (the Pale Fox). Amma corrects this by excising the obstruction, and his successful second union with Earth produces the eight perfect Nommo ancestors — the paired fish-beings who weave the cosmic order. The primary fieldwork source is Marcel Griaule and Germaine Dieterlen's *Dieu d'eau* (1948) and *Le Renard Pâle* (1965), representing decades of collaborative work with the Dogon sage Ogotemmêli and subsequent fieldwork. The Griaule-Dieterlen corpus is foundational but has been critiqued (Walter van Beek, 1991) on questions of representativeness; subsequent scholars have both defended and complicated their findings.

## Earliest attestation
Amma is attested through 20th-century ethnographic documentation — there is no textual or iconographic record predating European contact. Marcel Griaule's first extended fieldwork with the Dogon began in 1931. The systematic theological cosmology attributed to Amma was published in *Dieu d'eau* (1948) and fully elaborated in *Le Renard Pâle* (1965, posthumous for Griaule). Dogon oral tradition itself is not datable by external evidence; the architectural and cosmological symbolism of Dogon villages (oriented according to Amma's cosmic body) implies long-standing tradition.

## Mythological role
Amma's key mythological acts:
1. **Creation by ejection**: Amma throws primordial clay outward; it becomes the Earth (female).
2. **Impeded union**: Amma attempts union with Earth; the termite mound obstructs; chaos results in the Pale Fox (Ogo/Yourougou), a disruptive figure who steals a piece of placenta and disrupts the cosmic order.
3. **Successful union**: After excision of the obstruction, Amma's union with Earth produces the Nommo twins (see [[nommo]]) — eight perfect paired ancestors who inhabit the sky and primordial waters.
4. **The word Kize Uzi**: Amma's creative vibration/primordial speech precedes the physical act. The word is both the means and the model of creation — each thing has an intrinsic speech-signature.
5. **The cosmic body**: The Dogon village is laid out as Amma's body; the granary replicates his egg. Sacred geography is inscribed with cosmogonic structure.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[ptah]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: Ptah creates through the vibrating heart and tongue — the Memphite theology's word-creation doctrine. Amma's Kize Uzi (primordial creative speech) maps onto Ptah's creative utterance. Both are supreme god, craftsman-creator, generating through spoken/vibrated word rather than physical struggle.
- **[[vac-goddess]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: Vāc, the Vedic divine speech, is self-generating and cosmos-producing. Amma's creative word-vibration is a structurally exact parallel — both treat speech itself as the ontological ground of creation.
- **[[logos-stoic]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: The Stoic Logos as the rational, generative word structuring matter parallels Amma's Kize Uzi as the formative vibration structuring the cosmic egg.
- **[[poimandres]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: In the Hermetic *Poimandres*, the Nous-creator generates by word / light-utterance. The Amma cosmogony independently develops the same pattern: a transcendent, non-anthropomorphic supreme being creates through vibrational speech.
- **[[phanes-protogonos]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: The Orphic golden egg from which Phanes hatches and illuminates creation is the same cosmogonic type as Amma's spinning egg. Both: self-enclosed primal potential → differentiated cosmos.
- **[[brahma]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: The Hiranyagarbha (golden womb/egg) in Vedic cosmogony — Brahma (or Prajapati) hatching from the cosmic egg — is the same typological structure as Amma's egg-creation.
- **[[pangu]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: Pangu hatching from a cosmic egg to create heaven and earth by differentiation — the same world-egg type, independently instantiated.
- **[[yaldabaoth]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: Amma's impeded first union producing the chaotic, disruptive Ogo (Pale Fox) parallels Sophia's fall producing the flawed demiurge Yaldabaoth. In both cases, an asymmetry or imperfection in the primary creative act generates a disruptive secondary being who introduces disorder.
- **[[sophia-gnostic]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: Same impeded-creation typology — Sophia's unauthorized act producing chaos parallels Amma's impeded union producing Ogo.

## Iconography
Amma is not typically represented in anthropomorphic form. The Dogon represent him through: the spinning egg (cosmogonic structure), the four-directional cross (the cardinal seeds implanted in the egg), the granary as cosmic body (the granary architecture of Dogon villages replicates the shape of Amma's creation), and the weaving frame (a technology associated with Nommo's speech-and-weave cosmology). The lack of anthropomorphic iconography aligns Amma with the aniconic supreme-being pattern found in Olodumare ([[olodumare]]), Nzambi ([[nzambi]]), and Nyame ([[nyame]]).

## Disputes
- **Griaule controversy**: Walter van Beek's 1991 fieldwork (published in *American Ethnologist*) questioned whether the elaborate cosmological system Griaule documented was known beyond Ogotemmêli and a small initiatory elite — or was co-constructed in the fieldwork encounter. Most subsequent scholars accept that Dogon cosmological knowledge is real and rich, but that Griaule's presentation may be a synthetic idealization of elite knowledge rather than a description of pan-Dogon belief.
- **Sirius connection**: Robert Temple's *The Sirius Mystery* (1976) claimed Amma's cosmology reflects ancient knowledge of Sirius B (a white dwarf invisible to the naked eye). This claim is widely rejected by astronomers and anthropologists — see [[nommo]] for the Sirius controversy node.
