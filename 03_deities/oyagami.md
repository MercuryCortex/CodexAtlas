# Oyagami — God the Parent

**Oyagami** (親神, "God the Parent") is the supreme deity of [[tradition-tenrikyo|Tenrikyo]], the divine being who possessed [[nakayama-miki]] beginning in 1838 and through her composed the [[phase-7-043-ofudesaki|Ofudesaki]]. Oyagami is distinctively conceived as the *parent* of humanity — not a lord or judge but a nurturing creator who desires humanity's *Joyous Life* (*yokigurashi*) and grieves at the suffering caused by accumulated mental dust (*hokori*). The parental metaphor places Tenrikyo's theology closer to mystical strands of Christianity (God as Abba / Father) and Daoist cosmogony (the Dao as mother of all things) than to the imperial-hierarchy god-concepts of most Japanese religious tradition.
