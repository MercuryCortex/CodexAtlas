# Dievas

## Identity
The Baltic Sky-Father — the **single most directly-preserved reflex of the Proto-Indo-European *\*Dyēus ph₂tēr*** ("Sky Father") anywhere in Europe, both phonologically and semantically. Where Greek *Zeus*, Latin *Iuppiter* (< *Diespiter*), and Vedic *Dyaus Pitar* all show some specialization or compounding, Lithuanian *Dievas* and Latvian *Dievs* still mean simply "God" / "Sky" / "Sky-Father" as their primary semantic content — recoverable in the Latvian formula *Dievs Tēvs* "God-Father" in 19th-c. ethnographic collections that long postdate Christianization.

## Mythological role
**Pastoral visitor**: in Latvian *dainas*, Dievs is depicted walking the rye-fields wearing a grey linen mantle, blessing farm and homestead. **Heavenly household**: lives at the top of a mountain-of-heaven with his sons (*Dieva dēli*) and the sun-maiden Saulė as bride for one of them. Strikingly **non-thunderous**: thunder belongs to [[perkunas]]; Dievas is a benign sovereign whose role is precisely *not* to rule by force.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[dyaus-pita]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: direct Indo-European cognate.
- **[[zeus]] / [[jupiter]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: same root, divergent semantic development.
- **[[tian-heaven]]** — *parallel-motif*: abstract / impersonal sky-father.
