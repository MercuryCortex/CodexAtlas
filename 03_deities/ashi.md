# Ashi

Ashi (Avestan *Aši*; Pahlavi **Ard**) is the Zoroastrian **yazata of reward, recompense, and good fortune** — the divine personification of the **blessings that rightly follow a righteous life**: wealth, flourishing flocks and family, prosperity, and well-being. In the Avesta she is the **daughter of Ahura Mazda** and of **[[spenta-armaiti|Spenta Armaiti]]** (the Amesha Spenta of devotion and the earth), and the **sister of the Amesha Spentas**, and she is honoured in her own great hymn, the **Ard Yasht (Yasht 17)**, where she rides forth distributing fortune to those who serve the Good Religion while withholding her gifts from the wicked, the barren, and the unrighteous. She is closely paired with **[[daena|Daēnā]]**: where Daēnā is the conscience and the Religion itself, Ashi is the **reward that right religion earns** — the two together expressing the Zoroastrian moral economy in which good thoughts, words, and deeds bear concrete, blessed fruit. Ashi embodies the faith's deep optimism that the righteous life is not only its own reward but is genuinely **recompensed**, in this world and the next, by a divine power who keeps the accounts of fortune.
