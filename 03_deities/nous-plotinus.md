# Nous (Plotinus)

The Second Hypostasis in Plotinian metaphysics — the eternal contemplation of the One that arises immediately and necessarily from the One's superabundant simplicity. In Nous, the Platonic Forms exist as living thoughts thinking themselves; the structure is reflexive and complete. Nous in turn produces the World Soul as its outward expression. The hierarchy One → Nous → Soul → cosmos becomes the canonical Neoplatonist scheme inherited by Porphyry, Iamblichus, Proclus, and (in Christian register) Pseudo-Dionysius.
