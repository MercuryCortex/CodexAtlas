# Tammuz / Dumuzi

Alias node. See [[dumuzi-tammuz]] for the full entry.

Tammuz (Akkadian) / Dumuzi (Sumerian) is the dying-and-rising shepherd-god, consort of [[inanna-sumerian]] / [[ishtar-akkadian]]. His annual death (descent to the underworld) and return maps the agricultural seasons. The Tammuz mourning rite is attested in the Hebrew Bible at Ezekiel 8:14 — "women weeping for Tammuz" in the Jerusalem Temple precincts — demonstrating that the cult had penetrated Israelite practice deeply enough to require prophetic condemnation.

His syncretic relationship to [[adonis]] (the Hellenized Phoenician form of the same figure) and to [[osiris]] (the Egyptian dying-vegetation god) makes him a central node in the [[dying-rising-god]] cluster.
