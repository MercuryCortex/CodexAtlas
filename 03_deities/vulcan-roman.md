# Vulcan

## Identity
Roman god of fire — especially destructive fire — and of metalworking. His Forum shrine the **Volcanal** is one of the oldest at Rome, possibly antedating the Forum itself. The **Volcanalia** (August 23) was held when fires posed greatest danger to the harvest stores; live fish were thrown into the fire in place of human life. Husband of Venus; in Virgil's *Aeneid* 8 he forges the shield of Aeneas, the central ekphrasis-of-Roman-destiny passage.
