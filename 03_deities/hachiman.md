# Hachiman

## Identity
Tutelary kami of warfare and the samurai class, identified with the legendary emperor **Ōjin** (traditionally r. 270–310 CE) and his mother Empress Jingū. Hachiman is the **single most syncretic deity in Japan**: declared *Daibosatsu* (Great Bodhisattva) by imperial edict in **783 CE** — the first formal canonization of a kami as a Buddhist bodhisattva, the origin point of the *honji-suijaku* theological framework. Patron of the Minamoto and later all samurai lineages; **Tsurugaoka Hachimangū** in Kamakura (founded by Minamoto Yoritomo, 1180) was the political-religious capital of the warrior class for centuries.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[amitabha|Amida Buddha]]** — *folk-syncretism*: the honji (Buddhist origin) for which Hachiman is the suijaku (Shinto trace).
- **[[ares]] / [[mars-roman]] / [[ogun]]** — *parallel-motif*: war-god patron of warriors.
