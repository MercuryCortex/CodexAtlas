# Tuoni

Tuoni is the Finnish god of **death** and the lord of **Tuonela** (also called **Manala**), the land of the dead — a dark realm encircled by a **black river** across which the living cannot easily pass, and from which they are not meant to return. With his wife **Tuonetar**, the grim mistress of the underworld, Tuoni rules over the dead and the powers of decay and disease; their fearsome **daughters** include **Loviatar**, the blind hag who gives birth to the **nine diseases**, and the spirits of **pain** (Kivutar) and sickness (Kipu-Tyttö). In the *Kalevala*, the great sage **[[vainamoinen|Väinämöinen]]** dares to journey to Tuonela in search of lost magic words: he is ferried across the deathly river by Tuoni's daughter, but the family means to trap him there forever, and he escapes only by shapeshifting and cunning, slipping back to the land of the living past the iron nets strung across the river. As sovereign of the river-girt realm of the dead, Tuoni occupies the same structural place in the Finnish cosmos that **[[hades|Hades]]** holds in the Greek — the inescapable lord of the country beyond the dark water.
