# Awonawilona

The Zuni **primordial-being** — "the All-Container," the unmanifest from which the cosmos emerges through self-thought. The Zuni creation cosmology (Cushing 1896; Bunzel 1932): in the beginning was Awonawilona alone — a kind of primordial fog or mist — who, by thinking himself, condenses into the **Sun-Father** (Yatokka taccu); the Sun-Father then generates the **Earth-Mother** (Awitelin Tsita); from their union the manifest world arises in stages.

**Cross-tradition finding: structural parallel with [[ein-sof]].** Awonawilona is the most explicit Native American instance of the **apophatic-unmanifest-prior-to-manifestation** theological structure — a category Western comparative religion has historically located primarily in mystical Judaism (Kabbalistic Ein Sof), Mahayana Buddhism (the Dharmakaya), Hindu Advaita (Brahman without attributes), and Neoplatonism (the One beyond being). Tedlock (1983) explicitly notes the Zuni-Kabbalah parallel: both are the unmanifest principle from which manifestation begins through self-contraction or self-thought.

The **zero-possible-contact** between 13th-century Castilian Kabbalists and pre-Conquest Zuni makes Awonawilona a high-quality case of **convergent emergence** of an apophatic-primordial theological category. Either the category reflects a deep cognitive-universal of religious thinking, or two independently-arising theological traditions reached the same place from radically different starting points.

The Zuni cosmology continues through eight underworld emergences — humanity climbs up through stratified worlds — that structurally parallels the [[tradition-hopi]] emergence-cosmology and is one of the most-developed Native American creation-architectures. Awonawilona is the unmanifest top of that architecture.
