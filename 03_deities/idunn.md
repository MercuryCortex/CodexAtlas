# Iðunn

## Identity
Goddess of the **apples of immortality** — the fruit that the Æsir consume to remain perpetually young. Without her apples the gods age and weaken; her temporary abduction by the giant Þjazi (Skáldskaparmál) brings the Æsir close to senescence until Loki, forced by their wrath, rescues her in falcon-form. **The principal Norse instance of the immortality-fruit mytheme** that runs from the apples of the Hesperides through Xi Wangmu's peaches to the Tree of Life in Genesis 3.

## Cross-tradition identifications

- **Hesperides** — *scholarly-parallel*: golden apples.
- **Tree of Life (Genesis 3:22)** — *parallel-motif*: fruit-of-immortality denied to humans.
