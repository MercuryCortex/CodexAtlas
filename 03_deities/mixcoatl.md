# Mixcoatl

The **"Cloud Serpent"** — Chichimec hunter-god of the central Mexican peripheries, identified with the **Milky Way** (the celestial serpent of stars across the night sky). Under the name **Camaxtli**, Mixcoatl was the principal patron deity of **Tlaxcala** — the Aztec rival city-state that allied with Cortés in 1519 and was the central enabling factor in the Aztec defeat. The Tlaxcalan/Camaxtli cult continued through the Spanish Conquest and into the colonial period, making Mixcoatl-Camaxtli one of the few Mesoamerican deities with continuous post-Conquest cult records.

Mixcoatl is the **father of [[quetzalcoatl]]** in the central Aztec mythography (specifically the father of Topiltzin Quetzalcoatl, the legendary Toltec priest-king conflated with the deity). In the **Tezcatlipoca tetrad** theology, Mixcoatl is the *red* Tezcatlipoca — one of four directional/colour aspects of a single primordial god.

Iconography: bow and arrow, hunter's net, black band across the eyes (Chichimec face-paint), red-and-white striped body — the visual signature of the Chichimec hunter-and-warrior identity that the central-Mexican civilizations (Aztec, Toltec) treated as their ancestral substrate.
