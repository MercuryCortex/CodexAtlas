# Perkūnas

## Identity
The Baltic thunder-god — the **closest-preserved reflex of the Indo-European Thunderer** (*\*Perkʷunos*) anywhere in Europe, because **Lithuania was the last pagan state in Europe** (Christianized in 1387; Samogitia in 1413). Sacred oak-groves of Perkūnas were still actively maintained when missionaries arrived; the Jesuit reports of the 16th c. provide direct ethnographic detail on the living cult of Perkūnas that we lack for any other extinct Indo-European thunder-god.

## Mythological role
**Thunderer**: hurls lightning-axes (the *Perkūno akmenukai*, "Perkūnas's little stones" — Neolithic stone axes interpreted by Baltic folk as lightning-fossils, the source of the European-wide *strzała Peruna* / Donner-Keil folk-belief). **Cosmic enforcer**: strikes evildoers, drives out the devil. **Slayer of Velnias** (cognate of Slavic [[veles]]): the Baltic version of the IE storm-vs-chthonic-serpent chaoskampf.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[perun]]** — Slavic cognate.
- **[[thor]] / [[indra]] / [[zeus]] / [[teshub]] / [[baal-hadad]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: IE thunderer cluster.
- **Saint Elijah (Šventas Elijas)** — *folk-syncretism*: post-Christianization absorption of thunder-role.
