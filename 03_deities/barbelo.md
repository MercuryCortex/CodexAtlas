# Barbelo

The first emanation of the unknowable Monad in Sethian Gnosticism — "the perfect Pronoia (forethought), the universal womb, who is before all things." Irenaeus describes a Christian Gnostic group as *BarBeloite* (Adv. Haer. 1.29), our earliest external witness. The name's etymology is contested — proposed derivations include Aramaic *b-arba El* ("in the four is God"), a corruption of Greek *parthenos* ("virgin"), or a magical-name formation. Barbelo is androgynous in Sethian texts and serves as the source of the Pleroma's structure (typically four Luminaries: Harmozel, Oroiael, Daveithai, Eleleth).
