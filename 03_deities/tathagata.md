# Tathāgata — The Thus-Come-One

The principal honorific title used by the Buddha for himself in the Pāli and Sanskrit canonical literature — etymologically ambiguous between *tathā-gata* ("thus-gone") and *tathā-āgata* ("thus-come"), the term marks the Buddha as one who has gone (to nirvāṇa) / come (to teach) in accord with *tathatā* ("suchness"). The Mahāyāna develops the title into a metaphysical category encompassing all Buddhas; *tathāgata-garbha* (the buddha-embryo) is the principal Mahāyāna development of the term. Standard across Theravāda + Mahāyāna + Vajrayāna corpora.
