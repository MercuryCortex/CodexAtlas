# Ptahil

The Mandaean demiurgic figure — fashioner of the material world, in many Mandaean texts an ambiguous or partly failed creator. The name combines the Egyptian creator-god Ptah with the Aramaic *-il* (god), suggesting Mandaean engagement with Egyptian theological vocabulary, though the degree of theological continuity beyond the name is debated. In Mandaean cosmogony Ptahil is below Hayyi Rabbi and Manda d-Hayyi and produces the material world with mixed results — a structural parallel with Gnostic demiurge narratives without exact identification.
