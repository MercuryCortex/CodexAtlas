# Kingu

Kingu is a minor but theologically pivotal god of [[phase-1-008-enuma-elish]]. After Apsu's death he is elevated by Tiamat as her new consort and given command of her army of chaos-monsters; Tiamat hands him the **Tablet of Destinies** (*ṭuppi šīmāti*), the cosmic charter that grants its bearer ultimate authority over the gods' decreed fates. After Marduk defeats Tiamat, he takes the Tablet from Kingu, binds him, and orders his execution. From Kingu's flowing blood mixed with clay, the god Ea (Enki) fashions humanity. This anthropogony parallels the older [[phase-1-006-atrahasis]] account in which humans are made from clay + the blood of slain Wê-ila; in Enuma Elish the slain god is identified specifically with the rebel-leader Kingu, theologically encoding human existence as bearing the residue of slain rebellion. Kingu's name is the textbook example of a Mesopotamian "filler" deity — invented (or elevated to importance) by the Enuma Elish narrator to serve the plot of the Marduk-Tiamat combat.
