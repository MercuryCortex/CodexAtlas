# Sokar

Sokar (Egyptian *Skr*; Greek *Sokaris*) is the falcon god of the **Memphite necropolis** — the lord of *Rosetau*, the underworld region of the Saqqara cemeteries — and one of Egypt's oldest funerary deities. Depicted as a mummiform hawk or hawk-headed man, he presides over death and the regeneration of the dead, and (as a god of the necropolis workshops) over the craftsmanship of funerary equipment. His great annual rite was the **Festival of Sokar**, in which his sacred barque, the *Henu*, was drawn around the walls in a procession marking the regeneration of Osiris and the king. Sokar is the classic Egyptian example of theological fusion: at Memphis he merges with the creator-craftsman **[[ptah]]** and the resurrection-god **[[osiris]]** into the composite **Ptah-Sokar-Osiris**, the form in which countless wooden funerary statuettes were made — a single god of creation, death, and rebirth.
