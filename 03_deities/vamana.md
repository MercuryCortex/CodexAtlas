# Vamana

Vamana ("the Dwarf") is the **fifth avatar of [[vishnu]]** and the first to take fully human (rather than animal or hybrid) form in the **Dashavatara** sequence. His myth is one of the most beloved in the Puranas: the *asura* king **Bali**, though virtuous and generous, has conquered the three worlds and grown dangerously over-mighty, displacing even the gods. Vishnu descends as a tiny Brahmin dwarf and approaches Bali during a great sacrifice, humbly begging only as much land as he can cover in **three steps**. Bali, scorning the modest request, grants it — whereupon Vamana swells into the cosmic giant **Trivikrama** ("three strides"): with his first step he spans the earth, with his second the heavens, and for the third — there being nowhere left — Bali offers his own head, and Vamana presses him down into the netherworld (Patala), over which the humbled but honored Bali is made king. The myth encodes the reclamation of cosmic order from disproportionate power, and the truth that the divine, however small it appears, contains all worlds. Vamana completes the vault's Dashavatara coverage alongside Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, Narasimha, Rama, Krishna, and Kalki.
