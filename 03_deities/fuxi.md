# Fuxi

## Identity
First of the *San Huang* (Three Sovereigns) of Chinese mythology and the primordial culture-hero who introduces civilization. Credited with:
- Observing the patterns of heaven and earth to invent the **Bagua** (Eight Trigrams), foundational to the [[phase-1-026-yijing|Yijing]] divination system.
- Inventing the **fishing net** and the **eight-stringed zither**.
- Establishing **marriage** as institution (with [[nuwa]]).
- Inventing the **system of pictographic notation** which became Chinese writing (in the Confucian recension; another tradition credits the minister Cangjie).

## Earliest attestation
The Bagua-invention narrative is in the *Great Treatise* (Xici) of the [[phase-1-026-yijing|Yi Jing]] (~late Warring States to early Han). Han iconography (Wu Liang Shrine reliefs, 151 CE, Shandong) depicts Fuxi and Nüwa as serpent-tailed culture-heroes holding compass and square — among the most iconographically distinctive cosmological images of the Han.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[nuwa]]** — primordial sister-consort.

- **[[prajapati]]** — *parallel-motif*: primordial generative culture-hero.
