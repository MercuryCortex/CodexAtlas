# Ninshubur

Ninshubur (Sumerian *Nin-šubur*, "Lady/Lord of Subartu") is the **sukkal** — the vizier and messenger — of the goddess **[[inanna-sumerian]]**, and one of the most appealing minor figures of the Sumerian pantheon for her absolute loyalty. In the great myth *Inanna's Descent to the Netherworld*, it is Ninshubur whom Inanna instructs to raise the alarm if she fails to return: faithfully, Ninshubur dresses in mourning, beats the drum, and pleads before the great gods Enlil, Nanna, and finally Enki — whose intervention alone resurrects Inanna. Her gender shifts with her service (a handmaid to Inanna, a male vizier *Ilabrat* to the sky-god An in Akkadian tradition), and in later first-millennium theology she is absorbed into **Papsukkal**, the general messenger of the gods. She is the divine archetype of the trusted intermediary whose intercession saves.
