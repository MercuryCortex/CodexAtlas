# Albion

## The Figure

**Albion** is Blake's name for the primordial Cosmic Human — the giant figure whose body *is* England, Europe, and ultimately the entire universe. He is not a human being *in* the cosmos; the cosmos is *within* him, or rather: the cosmos as we experience it (broken, divided, mechanical) is what Albion looks like when he is *asleep*.

Blake's geography is literal cosmology: the hills of England are Albion's limbs; the Thames is his bloodstream; London is his brain-city. When Albion awakes, England will be the New Jerusalem — not metaphorically but ontologically. The geography of *Jerusalem* (1804–1820) is simultaneously the map of England and the body of the Cosmic Human.

> "The fields from Islington to Marybone, / To Primrose Hill and Saint John's Wood, / Were builded over with pillars of gold, / And there Jerusalem's pillars stood."
> — *Jerusalem*, Plate 27

---

## The Fall of Albion

Albion's fall is the central event of Blake's cosmogony — the event that creates the fallen world of ordinary experience. Albion:

1. **Turns away from Jerusalem** (his Emanation, the divine feminine) — an act of self-enclosure, analogous to Ein Sof contracting in the Lurianic *tzimtzum*
2. **Falls into sleep** — not death but a deep narcissistic withdrawal into ratio-bound materialism
3. **His Four Zoas dissociate** — Urizen, Luvah, Tharmas, and Urthona/Los separate from their unity and begin contending for dominance. This is structurally the Lurianic *shevirat ha-kelim*: the vessels/faculties of the Cosmic Human shatter and fall.
4. **Urizen seizes power** — the Reason-faculty alone rules, creates the mechanical prison of Newtonian nature, and becomes the Demiurge

The fallen world — Newton's universe of mathematical law, Locke's empiricist epistemology, industrial London — is what Albion's body looks like when divided, sleeping, Urizen-dominated.

---

## The Restoration: *Jerusalem*

Blake's longest and most complex work (*Jerusalem: The Emanation of the Giant Albion*, 1804–1820) narrates the process of Albion's gradual awakening. The poem ends with one of the most ecstatic passages in English literature — Albion's awakening and reunion with Jerusalem, the restoration of the Cosmic Human:

> "England who is Britannia awoke from Death on Albion's bosom / She awoke pale & cold she fainted seven times on the Body of Albion... / Then Albion rose & stood before Jesus in the clouds of heaven. / Fourfold his vision & his hands he took Jerusalems veil. / The breath Divine went forth over the morning hills Albion rose / In anger: the wrath of God breaking bright flaming on all sides around / His awful limbs: into the Heavens he walked clothed with fires / The fires of Intellect that reunited as Evening shades."

This is tikkun at cosmic scale: the Cosmic Human reassembled, the divine masculine and feminine reunited, the fallen creation transfigured.

## Adam Kadmon: The MASSIVE WIN

Kathleen Raine's *Blake and Tradition* (1968, 2 vols.) demonstrated what the convergence means: Blake derived the Albion concept from Thomas Taylor's Neoplatonic translations (the Hermetic *Anthropos* falling through the spheres) and from Jacob Boehme's theosophy (which itself had absorbed Kabbalistic ideas through the Christian Kabbalah channel of Pico, Reuchlin, and Agrippa). Blake had no access to the Zohar, no access to Lurianic texts, no Hebrew. He arrived at Adam Kadmon from the Protestant end of the transmission chain.

The structural identity is complete:

| Blake's Albion | Lurianic Adam Kadmon |
|---|---|
| Primordial Cosmic Human whose body IS the universe | First partzuf; the cosmic template precedes all worlds |
| Falls into sleep / self-enclosure | Shevirat ha-kelim: the vessels shatter |
| Four Zoas dissociate from the unified body | The partzufim fragment; lights fall as kelipot |
| Urizen seizes power and creates the material prison | The broken shards generate the sitra aḥra |
| Jerusalem (feminine Emanation) goes into exile | Shekinah separates from Tiferet; God experiences exile |
| Los forges within time toward restoration | Human tikkun-practice works within history toward repair |
| Albion's awakening = cosmic renovation | Tikkun olam = restoration of Adam Kadmon's wholeness |

A London engraver in 1804, working from the King James Bible and a Lutheran cobbler-mystic's visions, independently mapped the same cosmogonic structure that 16th-century Safed Kabbalists developed from the Zohar.
