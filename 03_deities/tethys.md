# Tethys

Tethys (*Tēthýs*) is the Titaness of the **nurturing fresh waters** — sister and wife of **[[oceanus]]**, and with him the **mother of all the world's rivers** and of the three thousand **Oceanid** nymphs of springs and streams. In *Iliad* 14 the goddess Hera names "**Oceanus, origin of the gods, and mother Tethys**" as the primal couple who reared her — making Tethys, like her husband, a figure of the world's watery beginning. She plays little active role in myth (she is said to have hidden the constellation Callisto/the Bear from setting in her waters, at Hera's request), but her cosmological place is fundamental. West (1997) notes the comparison of the Oceanus-Tethys water-parents to the Mesopotamian **[[tiamat|Tiamat]]**-Apsu cosmogony, and the possible echo of the name *Tethys* in *Tiamat/Tawtu*.
