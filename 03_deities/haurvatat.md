# Haurvatat

## Identity
**Haurvatat** (Avestan *Haurvatāt*, "Wholeness / Health / Integrity"; Pahlavi *Hordād*; Modern Persian *Khordad*) is **the fifth of the six Amesha Spentas** and the **second of the three feminine members** of the heptad. Haurvatat personifies **wholeness-of-being** — physical-and-spiritual integrity, the perfected-state of created beings. Among the seven created elements assigned to the heptad, Haurvatat presides over **the waters**. In nearly every liturgical context, Haurvatat is **paired with [[ameretat]] (Immortality)** as the twin eschatological-reward-principles — the gifts the righteous receive at the cosmic renovation.

## Earliest attestation
- **Gathas** ([[phase-2-002-gathas-of-zarathustra]]): named throughout, almost always paired with Ameretat.
- **Younger Avesta** ([[phase-2-004-yasna-younger-avesta]]): personified Amesha Spenta with hymnic invocation; the **Haoma Yašt** explicitly associates Haurvatat-Ameretat with the qualities the Haoma sacred-drink confers.
- **Pahlavi tradition**: Hordād-Amurdād remain paired through the Bundahishn + Denkard tradition.
- **Modern Iranian calendar**: *Khordad* (May–June) preserves the Amesha Spenta name in continuous Iranian identity.

## The reward-Amesha-Spenta pair
Haurvatat-Ameretat function as a **theological-eschatological unit**. The pairing is so tight that:
- In Gathic + Younger-Avestan attestations, the two are almost never invoked separately.
- The pair is the eschatological-reward-principle: at the cosmic renovation, the righteous receive **wholeness + immortality** as the substance of their renewed-condition.
- The pair is the theological-content of the **Haoma sacred-drink ritual**'s gift: the drink confers Haurvatat-Ameretat on the worshipper, making the rite a sacramental-anticipation of the eschatological reward.

This tight pairing is **structurally cognate with the Christian eucharistic-template** (sacred-drink-conferring-eternal-life via communion). The transmission-path runs Avestan Haoma → Indo-Iranian *\*sauma-* substrate → Vedic Soma → wider Hellenistic-Mediterranean sacred-drink imagery, mediated also through Mithraic-mysteries cultic adaptation. The Christian eucharist inherits from a Hellenistic-Jewish covenant-meal tradition that itself sits in a broader Indo-Iranian + Mediterranean sacred-drink-template.

## Cross-tradition: waters-feminine-divine cognates
The feminine-personification of waters has **Vedic cognates**: Sarasvatī (river-goddess) and the *Āpas* (the divine Waters). The cultic-pattern of waters-as-feminine-divine appears across Indo-Iranian + Indo-European religion (cf. the Roman *Lymphae*, Greek nymphs, Celtic spring-goddesses), suggesting a deep PIE substrate that Zoroastrian Haurvatat preserves on the Iranian branch.

## Disputes
- **Gathic personhood.** Whether Haurvatat-Ameretat are abstract-principles or personified-divine-beings in the Gathic stratum is debated; Boyce 1975 reads them as principle-with-implicit-personification.
- **Anahita-Haurvatat fusion.** In Achaemenid + Sasanian state cult, [[anahita]] absorbs aspects of waters-feminine-divine, partially eclipsing Haurvatat's independent cultic profile.
