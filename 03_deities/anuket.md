# Anuket

Anuket (Greek **Anukis**) is the Egyptian goddess of the **Nile cataract and its nourishing waters**, third member (daughter) of the **Elephantine triad** with the ram-creator **[[khnum]]** and the flood-releaser **[[satet]]**. Her name is connected to "the embracer," and she personifies the **clasping, life-giving embrace of the inundation** as it floods the fields. She is shown in a distinctive tall crown of reeds or feathers, and her sacred animal is the **gazelle**. Her cult centered on **Sehel Island** in the First Cataract and reached deep into **Lower Nubia**, where she was a major goddess of the river frontier. With Satet and Khnum she completes the divine family that the Egyptians held responsible for the annual miracle on which their whole civilization depended — the rising of the Nile.
