# Bragi

## Identity
The Æsir god of poetry, husband of [[idunn]]. Welcomes the slain heroes to Valhöll with mead and song. Bragi may be a personified abstraction of the noun *bragr* "poetry" / "the best of" — or, on de Vries's older hypothesis, may represent the deification of the historical 9th-century skald **Bragi Boddason "the Old"**, whose *Ragnarsdrápa* is among the earliest preserved skaldic poems and whose name became attached to a generalized god-of-poets figure. Either way, the principal Norse personification of the poetic art whose presence is otherwise distributed across Odin's various functions.
