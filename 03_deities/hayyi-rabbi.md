# Hayyi Rabbi

"Great Life" — the supreme Mandaean divinity, the absolute Life that is the source of the World of Light and of all that exists. Often invoked in the plural ("the Great Lives") in liturgical contexts, expressing both unity and abundance. The recurring Mandaean formula "in the name of the Great Life" opens prayers and ritual acts. Hayyi Rabbi is the *kosmokrator* of the Mandaean Light-realm; the lower worlds and their problematic creators (Ptahil, Abathur) are emanations or productions further removed from this absolute Life.
