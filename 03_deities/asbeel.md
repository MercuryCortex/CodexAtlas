# Asbeel

## Identity
**Asbeel** is the second named **Watcher** in the 1 Enoch 69 catalogue — *"the one who gave evil counsel to the holy sons of God, and led them astray, so that they corrupted their bodies with the daughters of men."* He is the Watcher of *evil counsel* — the figure who *advised* the angelic rebellion rather than (like [[semyaza]]) commanding it or (like [[penemue]], [[kokabiel]], [[gadreel]], [[baraqel]], [[kasdeja]], [[hermoni]], [[armaros]]) teaching forbidden arts to humans after the rebellion.

1 Enoch 69:5 (Nickelsburg-VanderKam 2004): *"And the second was called Asbeel. This one gave evil counsel to the holy sons of God, and led them astray, so that they corrupted their bodies with the daughters of men."*

The name *Asbʼeel* etymologically suggests *"counsel of God"* — an *ironic* theonym since the figure's role is to corrupt rather than convey divine counsel. The pattern parallels other Watcher-names that are -El-suffixed (Gadreel "wall of God"; Kokabiel "star of God"; Baraqel "lightning of God") — names that retain the divine-suffix even as the figures become rebels.

## Earliest attestation
- **1 Enoch 69:5** — the Book of Parables Watcher-catalogue.
- **Ge'ez Mashafa Henok** — only complete textual witness.

## Mythological role
Asbeel is theologically distinctive in the Watcher-roster as the *counsellor* who *advised* the rebellion. The text presents the angelic fall as having a *deliberative* dimension — Semyaza led, but Asbeel *counselled*. This is structurally close to the Edenic-serpent role of [[gadreel]] (who *led astray Eve*) — both figures function as *seducers* of holy/innocent beings into transgression.

The theological move is to add to the Enochic etiology of cosmic evil a *temptation-and-counsel* dynamic — the Watchers were not merely *willful* in their rebellion but were *persuaded* by Asbeel's counsel. This makes the Watcher-rebellion structurally parallel to the Adamic-Edenic temptation (where the serpent persuades Eve, who persuades Adam) — the Enochic doubling of the temptation pattern.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[satan-christian]]** / **[[satanael]]** — *parallel-form*: Asbeel's *counsellor-of-evil* role parallels the Christian Satan's role as tempter/adversary. The Enochic Asbeel is one of several pre-Christian Jewish figures contributing to the consolidated Christian Satan.
- **[[gadreel]]** — *complementary-figure*: Gadreel led astray Eve (the Edenic temptation); Asbeel led astray the angelic-holy-sons-of-God (the angelic temptation). The two are structurally parallel temptation-figures operating at different cosmic levels.

## Iconography
Not standardized.

## Disputes
- **Etymology.** "Asbeel" / "Asbʼ-El" — the second element is clearly *El* (God); the first element is variously read as *counsel*, *strong/firm*, or a transliteration. The *counsel-of-God* reading fits the textual role best.
