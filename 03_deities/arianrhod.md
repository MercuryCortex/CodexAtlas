# Arianrhod

Arianrhod (Welsh, "Silver Wheel") is a central figure of the **Fourth Branch of the Mabinogi** and one of the great goddess-figures of medieval Welsh tradition. Daughter of the mother-goddess Dôn, she is presented to her brother-magician Math as a candidate for royal "foot-holder," a position requiring virginity; tested, she instantly gives birth to two sons — one of whom her brother Gwydion spirits away and rears as **[[lleu-llaw-gyffes]]**. Humiliated, Arianrhod lays three implacable *tynged* (destiny-curses) on this son: that he shall have **no name**, bear **no arms**, and wed **no human woman** — each of which Gwydion overcomes by trickery (culminating in the creation of the flower-woman Blodeuwedd). Her sea-girt fortress **Caer Arianrhod**, a real reef off the Gwynedd coast, was also identified with the constellation Corona Borealis, marking her as a celestial sovereignty-figure whose silver wheel turns the heavens.
