# Wadjet

The tutelary goddess of **Lower Egypt** — the cobra-formed protectress of the pharaoh, paired with Nekhbet (Upper Egyptian vulture-goddess; no node yet) of Upper Egypt in the most enduring binary-deity pairing in world religion (the pharaonic *Nebty* / "Two Ladies" titulary, attested continuously from the Early Dynastic Period c. -3000 through the Roman period — over 3,000 years).

Wadjet's form is the **uraeus** — the rearing cobra worn on the pharaoh's brow, spitting venom-fire at the king's enemies. The uraeus is also the cobra of the solar disc and the cobra in the iconography of [[ra]], [[horus]], and many other Egyptian deities — Wadjet is the *form*, not just a separate goddess. She is one of the three goddesses of the **Eye of Ra** trinity (Wadjet–Hathor–Sekhmet), the rage-and-protection complex of the solar god.

Her cult centre **Per-Wadjet** (Greek **Buto**, modern Tell el-Fara'in in the Nile Delta) was one of the oldest cult sites in Egyptian religion, sacred from the predynastic period.
