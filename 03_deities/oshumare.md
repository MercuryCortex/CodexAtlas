# Oshumare

Oshumare (*Òṣùmàrè*) is the Yoruba orisha of the **rainbow** — and, through that shimmering arch between earth and sky, the deity of **continuity, cyclical renewal, and the regeneration of wealth and fortune**. Conceived as a great cosmic **serpent**, often shown biting its own tail, Oshumare embodies movement and alternation: between the dry season and the rains, between poverty and prosperity, even between male and female (the orisha is said to alternate sex), making him/her a power of the endless turning cycles on which life depends. A central image is the rainbow as the serpent that **draws the waters up from earth to the sky** so that they may fall again as rain — Oshumare keeping the great hydrological and fortune cycles in motion, and in some accounts serving the thunder-orisha **[[shango|Shango]]** in this. Oshumare is the close Yoruba counterpart of the Fon **[[dan-aida-hwedo|Dan / Aido-Hwedo]]**, the rainbow-serpent of neighbouring Dahomey, the two reflecting a shared West African vision of the rainbow-snake as the binder of cosmos and the symbol of unbroken continuity.
