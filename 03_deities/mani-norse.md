# Máni

Máni ("Moon") is the personified **Moon** of Norse myth and the brother of **[[sol-norse|Sól]]** the Sun. When the gods ordered the heavens, they set the two luminous siblings to drive the moon and sun across the sky and so to **measure time** — the days, the months, the years. Snorri's *Gylfaginning* tells that Máni guides the moon's course and governs its waxing and waning, and that he took up from the earth two children, **Hjúki and Bil**, who follow him (a figure some compare to the folkloric "man in the moon" and the nursery pair Jack and Jill). Like his sister, Máni is **pursued across the sky by a wolf** — Hati Hróðvitnisson — who at **Ragnarök** finally catches and swallows him. He is the modest but cosmologically essential Norse god of the moon and the reckoning of time.
