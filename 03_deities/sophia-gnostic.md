# Sophia (Gnostic)

The fallen aeon of the Pleroma — divine Wisdom personified — whose attempted unilateral emanation (without her consort) produces the misshapen Yaldabaoth and so triggers the existence of the material cosmos. The myth varies across schools: in the Apocryphon of John her error is to "wish to bring forth a likeness from herself without the consent of the spirit"; in Valentinian systems there is often a higher Sophia (in the Pleroma) and a lower Sophia (Achamoth, expelled into the lower realm). Sophia's redemption — and the recovery of the divine spark caught in matter — is the central drama of Gnostic soteriology. The figure inherits Hebrew Wisdom (Hokmah, Proverbs 8) and Hellenistic Sophia (Wisdom of Solomon) but transforms personified Wisdom from God's playmate at creation into the tragic agent of cosmic disaster and the seed of cosmic redemption.
