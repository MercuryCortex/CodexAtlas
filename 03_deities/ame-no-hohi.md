# Ame-no-Hohi

## Identity

Second of the five male kami born from Amaterasu's *magatama* beads in the *ukehi* (oath-pledge) contest with her brother Susanoo (Kojiki 1.13). Because they came from her beads, the five are formally her sons. Ame-no-Hohi's distinctive narrative is the **failed Izumo embassy**: when Amaterasu decides the earthly realm must be transferred from the *kunitsukami* (earthly kami) to her own heavenly lineage, she sends Ame-no-Hohi first as her envoy to [[okuninushi]], the kami-sovereign of Izumo. Ame-no-Hohi never returns. He befriends Ōkuninushi, settles in Izumo, and is silent for three years. The mission's failure forces Amaterasu to send a second emissary (Ame-no-Wakahiko, who also defects, marrying Ōkuninushi's daughter) and eventually a third (Takemikazuchi), who succeeds by armed threat.

The narrative gives Ame-no-Hohi a permanent dual identity: nominally heavenly (Amaterasu's son), functionally earthly (Izumo's adopted founder). The Izumo Grand Shrine's hereditary priestly clan — the **Izumo no kuni no miyatsuko** (later Izumo Kokusō) — claim direct descent from Ame-no-Hohi through his son Takehiratori. The defection therefore reads in Izumo tradition not as failure but as the legitimizing origin of Izumo's distinct priestly authority within the broader Yamato-Shinto sphere.
