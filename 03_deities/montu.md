# Montu

Montu (Egyptian *Mntw*) is the falcon-headed **war-god of the Theban region**, worshipped in a quadrangle of cult-cities around Thebes — Armant (*Hermonthis*), Tod, Medamud, and Karnak. He rose to national prominence in the Eleventh Dynasty, when several Theban kings took the name **Mentuhotep** ("Montu is content") and reunified Egypt under his patronage; thereafter he is the divine embodiment of the **victorious pharaoh**, and New Kingdom warrior-kings like Thutmose III and Ramesses II are praised as "raging like Montu" in battle. Crowned with the sun-disc and twin plumes and wielding the *khepesh* scimitar, he was solarized as **Montu-Re**, the fierce aspect of the sun. His living sacred animal was the **Buchis bull** of Armant, buried in its own necropolis (the Bucheum). As Amun rose to become Thebes' supreme god, Montu's role narrowed to the specifically martial.
