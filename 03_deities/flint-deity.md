# Flint (Evil Mind Twin)

The adversarial creator-twin in Haudenosaunee cosmogony. While his brother [[sapling-deity]] (the Good Mind) created life-sustaining animals, gentle rivers, and abundance, Flint (the Evil Mind) countered by creating rocks that blunt tools, thorns on plants, monsters, winter cold, and all difficult aspects of the world. The twins ultimately contend in direct combat; Flint is defeated but not destroyed — the dualism is permanent. The creative tension between the Good Mind and Evil Mind is the cosmological explanation for why the world contains both beauty and difficulty.
