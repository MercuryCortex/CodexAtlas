# Arawn

Arawn is the king of **Annwn**, the Welsh **Otherworld**, and the deity who opens the **First Branch of the Mabinogi**. There he meets the mortal prince Pwyll of Dyfed, who has unwittingly offended him by setting his own hounds on a stag that Arawn's white, red-eared Otherworld dogs (the *Cŵn Annwn*) had brought down. In recompense Arawn proposes an extraordinary exchange: the two men **swap shapes and kingdoms for a year**, each ruling the other's realm, and Pwyll is to fight Arawn's rival Hafgan in his stead — which Pwyll does, scrupulously declining even to touch Arawn's queen though he shares her bed, an act of honor that founds a lasting friendship between Dyfed and Annwn. Arawn thus embodies the Welsh Otherworld not as a place of horror but as a mirror-kingdom bound to the human world by reciprocity, hospitality, and exact honor. His Otherworld hounds feed into the wider British myth of the **Wild Hunt**.
