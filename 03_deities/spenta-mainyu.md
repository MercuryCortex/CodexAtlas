# Spenta Mainyu

## Identity
Spenta Mainyu ("Bounteous Spirit" or "Holy Spirit") is one of the two primordial Spirits of Gathic Zoroastrianism — the one who chose Truth at the cosmogonic moment (Yasna 30:3–5). The relationship between Spenta Mainyu and Ahura Mazda is the central interpretive puzzle of Zoroastrian theology: in some readings of the Gathas, Spenta Mainyu and Angra Mainyu are *both children of Ahura Mazda* (a transcendent first principle); in other readings, Spenta Mainyu *is* Ahura Mazda in his creative-active aspect, and Angra Mainyu is the co-eternal opposing principle.

In the Younger Avesta and Pahlavi books, Spenta Mainyu is increasingly identified with Ahura Mazda himself, leaving Angra Mainyu / Ahriman as the standalone cosmic adversary — the form of strict dualism that became dominant. The vocabulary "Bounteous/Holy Spirit" enters wider history through Hellenistic-era Jewish and Christian terminology of *ruaḥ ha-qodesh* / *pneuma hagion* / "Holy Spirit"; whether this is independent or partly Zoroastrian-influenced is debated. Spenta Mainyu's seven aspects, the *Amesha Spentas*, are an organized angelology (Vohu Manah, Aša Vahišta, Xšaθra Vairya, Spənta Ārmaiti, Haurvatāt, Amərətāt) frequently compared with later Jewish, Christian, and Islamic archangel hierarchies.
