# Huracan

Huracan (K'iche' *Jun Raqan*, "One-Leg"; called **Heart of Sky**, *Uk'u'x Kaj*) is the storm- and creator-god of the **Popol Vuh**, the sacred book of the K'iche' Maya. He is the lightning-power of the sky, manifest in three hypostases ("Thunderbolt Hurricane, Newborn Thunderbolt, Sudden Thunderbolt"), and at the opening of the world it is Heart-of-Sky who, together with the feathered serpent **[[kukulkan|Sovereign Plumed Serpent]]** (Q'uq'umatz) lying in the primordial sea, **conceives the earth and speaks it into being** — "Earth!" they say, and it arises. When the early wooden people fail to honor the gods, it is Huracan who sends the **great flood** and the rain of resin and the revolt of the animals and tools to destroy them. His name passed, by way of the Taíno *hurakán* and Spanish *huracán*, into English as **hurricane** — the storm-god whose word both makes and unmakes the world.
