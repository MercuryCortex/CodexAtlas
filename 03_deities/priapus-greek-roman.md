# Priapus

Greco-Roman minor fertility god of the male generative organ + gardens + orchards; cult-centre at Lampsacus on the Hellespont. Iconographically an ithyphallic apotropaic guardian. Frequently identified with Egyptian [[min-egyptian|Min]] in Greco-Roman Egypt (Herodotus *Histories* 2.51 equates the two).
