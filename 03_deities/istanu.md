# Ištanu

Ištanu (from the Hattic sun-god **Estan**) is the Hittite **sun-god of heaven** — and above all the divine **judge**, the all-seeing eye who crosses the sky each day and "sets the bounds of justice for the land." Hittite sun-prayers address him as the shepherd of mankind who beholds every deed of the just and the wicked alike, lord of judgment before whom oaths are sworn and wrongs redressed — imagery that closely parallels the Mesopotamian sun-judge **[[utu-shamash|Utu/Shamash]]**, part of a shared ancient Near Eastern conception of the sun as the cosmic magistrate. He must be carefully distinguished from the **[[sun-goddess-of-arinna|Sun-goddess of Arinna]]**, the *female* chief solar deity who was patroness of the king and the realm: the Hittites worshipped both a male solar judge (Ištanu) and a female solar sovereign (Arinna). His Luwian counterpart was the sun-god **Tiwaz**. Ištanu embodies the link between sunlight, all-seeing knowledge, and the administration of justice.
