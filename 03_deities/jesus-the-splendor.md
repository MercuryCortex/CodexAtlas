# Jesus the Splendor

In Manichaean theology, "Jesus the Splendor" is one of several distinct figures named Jesus — also Jesus the Boy (the heavenly child Jesus emblematic of the Light suffering in matter), Jesus the Suffering (*Jesus patibilis*, identified with the Light dispersed in nature), and the historical Jesus seen as a previous prophet in the Manichaean prophetic succession. Jesus the Splendor is the revealer who teaches Adam the cosmic situation and thus inaugurates the salvific transmission of knowledge culminating in Mani.
