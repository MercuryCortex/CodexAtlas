# Remiel (= Jeremiel in 4 Ezra)

## Identity
**Remiel** (Aramaic *Ramiel*, also rendered *Remi'el*; Latin *Jeremiel* in 4 Ezra) is the seventh and last of the canonical **seven archangels** of 1 Enoch 20, *"set over those who rise"* — the archangel of the **resurrection**. He is one of the three Enochic archangels not retained in the Western Christian short-list of four but canonical in [[tradition-ethiopian-orthodox-tewahedo]].

A distinctive feature of Remiel/Jeremiel is that he appears *under different name-forms across the major texts of the Enochic-Ezran apocalyptic family*:

- **1 Enoch 20:8** — *Remiel* / *Ramiel*: "the angel of God set over those who rise"
- **4 Ezra 4:36** — *Jeremiel* (Latin Vulgate; also in the Ge'ez canonical recension *Mashafa Ezra*) — answers Ezra's questions about the eschatological number of the righteous
- **2 Baruch (Syriac Apocalypse of Baruch) 55:3** — *Ramael* / *Ramiel* (Syriac equivalent) — appears as an interpreting angel

Modern critical scholarship (Stone 1990; Nickelsburg 2001) treats Remiel/Jeremiel/Ramael as a single archangelic figure across these closely-related Second-Temple apocalyptic texts.

## Earliest attestation
- **1 Enoch 20:8** — last of the seven, with the resurrection portfolio.
- **4 Ezra 4:36** (Vulgate Latin; Ge'ez canonical) — interpreting angel.
- **2 Baruch 55:3** (Syriac) — interpreting angel.
- **Ge'ez Mashafa Henok** + **Ge'ez Mashafa Ezra** — both canonical in Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo; both attest the figure.

## Mythological role
Remiel's specific portfolio is **the resurrection of the dead** — "those who rise." This is theologically distinctive: in the heptadic archangel-hierarchy, he is the eschatological-resurrection angel where Michael is the people-warrior, Gabriel the paradise-guardian, Raphael the soul-healer, Uriel the cosmic-Tartarus, Raguel the luminary-judge, Saraqael the inner-moral-judge. Remiel's domain is the most explicitly *eschatological* of the seven — the gathering and raising of the dead at the final-judgment.

In 4 Ezra, Jeremiel is the angel who answers the seer's question about *when* and *how many* of the righteous dead will be gathered — coupling the resurrection role with eschatological-chronological revelation. The 4 Ezra context is the post-70-CE Jewish apocalyptic response to the destruction of the Temple; the angel who answers the *when* of resurrection is theologically critical in that moment.

In Ethiopian Orthodox liturgical and theological tradition, Remiel-Jeremiel is preserved as one of the seven archangels with explicit resurrection-domain identification.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **Jeremiel of 4 Ezra** — *direct-identification*: same figure.
- **Ramael of 2 Baruch** — *direct-identification*: same figure.
- Some later Coptic and rabbinic angelologies retain or transform the figure.

## Iconography
Not strongly standardized in Ethiopian Christian art beyond appearance in the seven-archangel compositions.

## Disputes
- **Remiel vs. Ramiel as the original Ge'ez form.** Manuscript-recension variation; the figure is the same.
- **Jeremiel as the same as Remiel.** Stone 1990 treats them as a single figure across the apocalyptic family; older scholarship sometimes distinguished.
