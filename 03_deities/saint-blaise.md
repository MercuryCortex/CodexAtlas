# Saint Blaise (Vlasiy)

Armenian Christian bishop of Sebaste martyred ~316 CE under Licinius. The hagiography (Acta Sanctorum, February 3) describes him as a physician-bishop who lived in solitude with wild beasts, blessing them, before his arrest and martyrdom by being torn with iron wool-combs and then beheaded. The detail that grounds his Western European cult is the rescue of a child choking on a fishbone, which made him the patron-saint of throat ailments — the *Blessing of the Throats* on his 3 February feast (candles crossed at the throat) remains a Catholic practice.

## Filed as deity, not person

This node is filed under `03_deities/` rather than `04_persons/` because **in Eastern Slavic folk-Christianity Saint Blaise functions as a divine cult-receiver inheriting Veles's livestock-patron domain** (see [[veles]]). Parallel to how Saint Lazarus in Cuban Santería receives orisha-style devotion as Babalú-Ayé, and how Elijah in Eastern Slavic folk-Christianity becomes Ilya-Gromovnik the thunderer.

## The dvoeverie transfer

The Christianization of Kievan Rus' (988 CE) transferred Veles's cattle-and-livestock domain to Saint Blaise via a striking phonetic + functional alignment:

| Layer | Slavic-pagan | Christian-overlay |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Volos / Veles | Vlasiy / Blasius |
| Domain | god of cattle, oaths, wealth | patron of cattle and livestock-blessing |
| Feast | (pre-Christian winter rites) | 11 February (Old Style 3 February) |
| Iconography | (horned, serpent-form) | bishop + cattle (in Slavic folk art) |

The phonetic near-identity Volos ↔ Vlas + the shared cattle-patron function made this the cleanest dvoeverie transfer in Eastern Slavic religion alongside Perun → Elijah. Tokarev 1957 is the canonical Russian-language treatment; Fedotov 1948 covers it in English. Old Russian hagiography records *idoly Volosa* (idols of Volos) being destroyed in Rostov and Yaroslavl in the 11th c. — the historical moment of the substitution.
