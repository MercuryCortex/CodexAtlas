# Spenta Armaiti

## Identity
**Spenta Armaiti** (Avestan *Spəntā Ārmaiti*, "Holy/Bounteous Devotion"; Pahlavi *Spandarmad*; Modern Persian *Esfand*) is **the fourth of the six Amesha Spentas** and the **first of the three feminine members** of the heptad. Armaiti personifies **right-minded devotion** — the human-side correlate of the cosmic principles Asha (Truth) and Vohu Manah (Good Mind). Among the seven created elements assigned to the heptad, Armaiti presides over **the earth** — making her the cultic Mother-Earth figure of Zoroastrianism, in whom right-piety and right-cultivation of the earth-as-creation merge.

## Earliest attestation
- **Gathas** ([[phase-2-002-gathas-of-zarathustra]]): named throughout Yasna 28-53 as the devotional-attitude principle paired with Asha (Truth) and Vohu Manah (Good Mind).
- **Younger Avesta** ([[phase-2-004-yasna-younger-avesta]]): personified Amesha Spenta with hymnic invocation, explicitly identified with the earth element.
- **Pahlavi tradition** (especially Bundahishn): Spandarmad as cultic Mother-Earth of the Zoroastrian community.
- **Modern Iranian calendar**: *Esfand* (February–March) preserves the Amesha Spenta name; the *Esfandagan* festival (5 Esfand) is a women's-day continuous from Sasanian times.

## The feminine Amesha Spentas
Spenta Armaiti is the principal of the **three feminine Amesha Spentas** (with [[haurvatat]] / Wholeness and [[ameretat]] / Immortality). Their grouping is theologically significant: the feminine triad presides over the three created elements that sustain bodily life (earth, waters, plants), while the masculine principles preside over fire, metals, and animals + humanity. The complementarity is theologically deliberate (Boyce 1975).

## Cross-tradition: Spenta Armaiti ↔ Vedic Aramati
The Vedic *Aramati* (RV 5.43.6, 10.92.4-5) is **directly cognate** with Avestan *Ārmaiti* — both derive from Proto-Indo-Iranian *\*ārmati-* ("right-devotion / right-thinking-toward"). Both:
- Are **feminine-personified** right-devotional-attitude principles.
- Are **associated with earth** in cultic-ritual contexts.
- Are **paired with cosmic-truth-and-order** (Asha / Ṛta) as the human-correlate of cosmic-principle.

This is **one of the cleanest Proto-Indo-Iranian feminine-divine retentions** in the comparative-religion record (West 2007 ch. 7). The cosmic-piety-as-feminine-earth-mother template predates the Vedic/Avestan split and is preserved on both branches.

## The earth-piety ritual nexus
Right-cultivation of the earth, right-care of cattle, right-pastoral-discipline are **forms of worship of Spenta Armaiti**. The Avestan *Vendidad* (Vidēvdat 3) makes pious agriculture a religious obligation. The Zoroastrian community's agricultural-pastoral ethic — which the Gathas attribute polemically to Zarathustra against the cattle-raiding war-band religion he opposes — is theologically grounded in Armaiti's earth-element-patronage.

## Disputes
- **Anahita-Armaiti fusion.** In some cultic contexts (particularly Achaemenid + Sasanian state cult), [[anahita]] absorbs aspects of Spenta Armaiti's feminine-divine domain. Whether this is functional-fusion or cultic-parallel is contested.
- **Persian-period transmission to Israelite *adamah*-feminine-personification.** Boyce 1991 raises possible structural-parallel to Second-Temple feminine-personifications of the land; not a mainstream position.
