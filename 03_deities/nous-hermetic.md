# Nous (Hermetic)

The divine Mind in Hermetic literature — both the supreme Nous (often identified with the Father / Sovereignty) and the divine intellect emanated as the first principle of the cosmos. The relationship to Logos differs across treatises; in CH I, the Father is Nous and the Logos is his Son. Late-antique Hermetism shares with Middle Platonism and Neoplatonism a hierarchical scheme in which Mind / Intellect mediates between the absolute and the cosmos.
