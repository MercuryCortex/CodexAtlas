# Ops

Ops (also **Opis**; cult-titles **Ops Consiva**, "Ops the planter," and **Ops Mater**) is the Roman goddess of **plenty and abundance** — the wealth of the harvest, the stored grain, the riches drawn from the soil (her name, *ops*, means "abundance, resources, power"). She belongs to the oldest layer of Roman agrarian religion, served in the **Regia** (the official seat of the *pontifex maximus*), where the archaic **Opiconsivia** (25 August) was celebrated. She is the **consort of [[saturn-roman|Saturn]]**, the god of sowing, and her festival the **Opalia** (19 December) falls within his **Saturnalia** — the planter and the plenty paired across the agricultural year. In the Greek interpretation of the Roman gods she was equated with **[[rhea|Rhea]]** (Saturn standing for Kronos) and with the Great Mother Cybele. Ops embodies the Roman ideal of stored abundance — the full granary as a divine blessing.
