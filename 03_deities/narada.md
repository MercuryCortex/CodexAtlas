## Narada — Divine Messenger and Musician

Narada (*Nārada*, Sanskrit: नारद) is the pan-Hindu divine sage, messenger, and musician who travels freely between the human and divine realms. He carries the *Mahati* vina ("the great one") as his cosmic credential and instrument. In the Natya Shastra origin narrative, Brahma transmits music and the arts to Narada, who then teaches them to Bharata Muni, the author of the Natya Shastra. This positions Narada as the crucial intermediary in the divine-to-human transmission of all sacred musical knowledge.

Narada appears throughout Hindu literature as a storyteller, divine messenger, and occasionally a trickster figure who sets events in motion. He is the patron of narrative itself as well as music, making him unique among Hindu deities as the figure who embodies *both* sonic and textual transmission.
