# Mnemosyne

## Identity
Titan goddess of **memory** and mother by [[zeus]] of the **nine Muses** — Clio, Euterpe, Thalia, Melpomene, Terpsichore, Erato, Polyhymnia, Urania, Calliope. In Hesiod's *Theogony* the poet's first invocation is to her: memory is the primary precondition of poetic and historical truth in the pre-literate world.

## Mythological role
**Orphic afterlife instruction**: the **Orphic Gold Tablets** (Petelia, Hipponion, Thurii — c. -400 to -300) instruct the deceased: *"You will find on the right in Hades' halls a spring, and beside it standing a white cypress. Do not approach this spring at all. Further on you will find from the lake of Memory, cold water flowing forth; and there are guardians before it. Say: 'I am the child of Earth and starry Heaven; this you yourselves know. I am parched with thirst and dying; quickly give me the cold water flowing forth from the lake of Memory.'"* The Orphic-Pythagorean afterlife geography requires the soul to drink from **Mnemosyne** (Memory) rather than from **Lethe** (Forgetting) — recollection of past lives, the doctrine that grounds Plato's *anamnesis*.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[saraswati]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: feminine-divine intellect/memory/flow.
- **Plato's *anamnesis* (Meno, Phaedo)** — *manuscript-transmission*: the Orphic-Pythagorean memory-doctrine philosophically systematized.
