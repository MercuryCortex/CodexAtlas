# Kasdeja

## Identity
**Kasdeja** is one of the named **Watchers** of 1 Enoch 69, with the specific transgression of teaching humanity "the bites of the serpent" and "the smitings of the embryo in the womb" — i.e., medical knowledge that the Enochic tradition codes as illicit because it touches abortifacients and pharmacological control of life.

1 Enoch 69:12 (Nickelsburg-VanderKam 2004): *"The name of the fifth is Kasdeja. This is the one who showed the sons of men all the wicked smitings of spirits and demons, and the smitings of the embryo in the womb so that it miscarries, and the smitings of the soul, the bites of the serpent, and the smitings that occur at noon by the son of the serpent, who is called Tabaʿet."*

The figure adds to the broader Watcher-culture-bringer pattern: where [[semyaza]] is chief, [[azazel]] teaches metallurgy and cosmetics, [[penemue]] teaches writing, [[kokabiel]] teaches astrology, [[baraqel]] teaches lightning-divination, [[gadreel]] teaches weapons-and-seduces-Eve, Kasdeja teaches medical / abortifacient / demonic-smitings knowledge. The cumulative pattern is the Enochic theological etiology of forbidden human knowledge.

## Earliest attestation
- **1 Enoch 69:12** — Book of Parables (~1st c. BCE–1st c. CE).
- **Ge'ez Mashafa Henok** — only complete textual witness.

## Mythological role
Kasdeja is the Watchers-tradition culture-bringer of *medical-pharmacological-demonological* knowledge. The combination of (a) abortifacient knowledge, (b) demonic-smitings (illness as demonic affliction), and (c) the *son of the serpent* called *Tabaʿet* makes him theologically the broadest of the Watchers-teachers in scope — the etiological origin in Enochic theology of human suffering through medical-demonological-pharmacological intervention.

The Tabaʿet "son of the serpent" appearing in his teaching-list is theologically interesting: a *sub-demonic* figure (son of the snake) under Kasdeja's mediation. This three-tier structure (Watcher → demon → sub-demonic) elaborates the Enochic cosmic-evil hierarchy.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- Parallel structurally to figures elsewhere associated with abortifacient and medical-pharmacological knowledge in Mediterranean traditions.

## Iconography
Not standardized.

## Disputes
- **Name variants.** "Kasdeja" / "Kasbeel" / "Kasdiel" — recensional variation; refers to the same figure.
