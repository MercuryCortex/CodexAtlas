# Sarpanit

Consort of [[marduk]] and the divine queen of the Babylonian state pantheon — her cult was housed alongside Marduk's at the **Esagila** in Babylon, the temple-complex from which the *Enūma Eliš* New Year ritual was performed. Sarpanit was invoked in matters of pregnancy, childbirth, and royal succession. Mother of [[nabu]] (the scribe-god of Borsippa).
