# Shaushka

## Identity

**Shaushka** (Hurrian: *Šauška*) is the supreme goddess of the Hurrian pantheon and the single most important female deity in both Hurrian and Hittite religion. She is the **Hurrian Ishtar** — the direct Anatolian equivalent of Mesopotamian [[ishtar-akkadian]], with whom she was fully identified in Hittite treaty lists, royal correspondence, and ritual practice.

She is depicted as a **winged goddess flanked by lions**, carrying weapons of war alongside the accouterments of love and sexuality — the full Ishtar iconographic package transplanted into an Anatolian theological framework. Her two attendants, Ninatta and Kulitta, accompany her in Hittite art and ritual.

## Role in Hurrian–Hittite Religion

Shaushka occupies the role of **the great goddess** in the Hurrian divine hierarchy: sister of the storm-god [[teshub]], companion of the queen-goddess [[hebat]], and the deity called upon by Hittite kings to protect their military campaigns and confirm their divine favor. She was one of a small number of Hurrian deities adopted wholesale into the Hittite state pantheon — an adoption that transformed Hittite religion in the imperial period.

## The Amarna Syncretism (EA 23)

The most striking documentation of Shaushka's prestige is **Amarna letter EA 23** (c. 1350 BCE): Tushratta, king of Mitanni, writes to pharaoh Amenhotep III (and later Akhenaten) reporting that he is sending a **statue of Shaushka of Nineveh** to Egypt — as if dispatching a living divine ambassador. Tushratta treats Shaushka and Ishtar as interchangeable: the goddess of Nineveh is Ishtar, and Ishtar is Shaushka. This is Bronze Age international diplomacy conducted through goddess-identification.

## Cross-Tradition Significance

Shaushka is the **Hittite missing link** in the Ishtar transmission chain:

- [[inanna-sumerian]] (Sumerian) → [[ishtar-akkadian]] (Akkadian/Mesopotamian) → **Shaushka** (Hurrian/Anatolian) → [[astarte-canaanite]] (Canaanite/Levantine) → [[aphrodite-greek]] (Greek)

The Hurrian mediation of Mesopotamian goddess-theology into Anatolia and thence into the Aegean is one of the under-documented arcs of ancient religious history. Shaushka is the name on the arrow.
