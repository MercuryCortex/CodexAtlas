# Ketu

Ketu (Sanskrit *Ketú*, "banner / comet") is the immortal **headless body** that pairs with **[[rahu]]** as the second of the two Hindu "shadow-planets" and the descending lunar node. The two are the sundered halves of the single asura Svarbhanu, beheaded by Vishnu-as-Mohini during the churning of the ocean (see [[rahu]]): the head became Rahu, the trunk became Ketu, and both were fixed in the heavens as the points where the moon's path crosses the ecliptic — the nodes at which eclipses occur. Where Rahu embodies grasping worldly desire, **Ketu** is his mirror-opposite: the planet of **detachment, loss, and spiritual liberation (moksha)**, associated with the uncanny, with past-life residues, and with the renunciate's release from the very desires Rahu inflames. Together Rahu and Ketu complete the nine-fold Navagraha, the planetary deities who govern the unfolding of karma in time.
