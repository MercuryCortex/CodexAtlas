# Vak / Vac — Goddess of Sacred Speech

**Stub.** Vak (Sanskrit: वाक्, also Vac) is the Vedic goddess of sacred speech, one of the oldest divine figures in the Rigvedic corpus. She appears in her most powerful form in the Devi Sukta (Rigveda 10.125), where she speaks in the first person as a cosmic power that precedes and enables all the gods. Vak is not a messenger of the gods — she precedes them and enables them. Sacred speech is the primary divine power; the named deities are secondary manifestations.

Vak is a structural parallel to the Greek [[logos-cosmic-reason]] (pre-existent divine speech-principle), to Kalam Allah in Islamic theology (the uncreated Word of God), and to the Memphite Theology's Ptah (who creates through his tongue). The Rigvedic statement of Vak's cosmic priority predates both Greek Logos theology and Islamic Kalam Allah by at least a millennium.
