# Guhyasamāja

Guhyasamāja ("Secret Union" or "Secret Assembly"; Tibetan *Sangwa Düpa*) is a central **meditational deity (yidam)** of **Vajrayāna Buddhism** and the focus of the **Guhyasamāja Tantra**, so esteemed in the Tibetan tradition that it is called **"the king of tantras."** It is the paradigmatic **Father tantra** of the Anuttarayoga class, emphasizing the practices of the **illusory body** and **clear light**. The deity appears as **Akṣobhyavajra** — a dark-blue form with **three faces and six arms**, in union with his consort at the heart of an elaborate **thirty-two-deity maṇḍala** that arranges the five Buddha-families in a single integrated structure. The cycle is built upon the Buddha **[[akshobhya|Akshobhya]]**, and an important variant identifies it with the wisdom-bodhisattva **[[manjushri|Mañjuśrī]]** (as Mañjuvajra). Guhyasamāja was given enormous weight in the **Gelug** school, whose founder **Tsongkhapa** wrote extensively on it as the model for the entire Father-tantra path. It represents the systematic, maṇḍala-centered pole of Tibetan tantric practice.
