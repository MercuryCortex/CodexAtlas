# Ningal

Alias node. See [[nin-gal-sumerian]] for the full entry.

Ningal is the Sumerian/Akkadian goddess of the moon — consort of [[nanna-sin]] (the moon god), and mother of [[inanna-sumerian]] (the evening star, Venus) and [[utu-shamash]] (the sun). Her *Lament of Ningal* over the destruction of Ur is one of the earliest first-person poetic laments in recorded history. Her Akkadian counterpart and cognate is [[nikkal-akkadian]].
