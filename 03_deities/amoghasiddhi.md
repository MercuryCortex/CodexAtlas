# Amoghasiddhi

## Identity
The **Northern Buddha** of the Five Dhyani Buddha mandala. *Amoghasiddhi* — "Infallible Accomplishment" — embodies the **All-Accomplishing Wisdom** (*kṛtyānuṣṭhāna-jñāna*), the wisdom that brings all bodhisattva-activities to fruition, the antidote to the affective poison of envy/jealousy. His consort is **Green [[tara]]**, the most-invoked female bodhisattva of Tibetan Buddhism — and Tārā's protective-from-the-eight-fears function flows downward from her position in the Amoghasiddhi sector of the mandala.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **Five Dhyani Buddhas**: with **[[vairocana]]** (center), **[[akshobhya]]** (east), **[[ratnasambhava]]** (south), **[[amitabha]]** (west).
- **[[tara]]** — canonical consort (Green Tārā).
