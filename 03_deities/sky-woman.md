# Sky Woman

**Alias stub** — the full metadata node is at [[sky-woman-haudenosaunee]].

Primordial mother of Haudenosaunee cosmogony; falls from the Sky World through a hole opened by an uprooted tree onto the back of a great turtle in the primordial waters; water birds catch her; muskrat brings earth from the ocean floor which expands to form Turtle Island (North America). Sky Woman's granddaughter (or in some tellings, direct daughter) gives birth to the twins [[flint-deity]] and [[sapling-deity]]. Sky Woman is the cosmological ancestor of all humans in the Haudenosaunee world. See the full node for the earth-diver MASSIVE WIN cross-tradition table and the axis-mundi connections.
