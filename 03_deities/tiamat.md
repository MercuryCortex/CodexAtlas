# Tiamat

The primordial salt sea, mother of the first gods. In [[phase-1-008-enuma-elish]] Tiamat and her consort Apsu are the original divine principles; she leads the chaos forces against the younger gods after Apsu is killed. Marduk slays her in single combat, splits her body to form the firmament and the earth, and pierces her eyes to make the Tigris and Euphrates.

## Etymological link to Hebrew `tehom`
Akkadian *tiāmat* (salt sea) and Hebrew *tehôm* (the deep, Gen 1:2) are commonly held to be cognate Semitic forms (both from a proto-Semitic *tihām-* root). Whether this implies direct literary dependence of Genesis 1 on Enuma Elish, or merely a shared Semitic cosmogonic vocabulary, has been debated since Gunkel (1895). The grammatical fact that `tehom` lacks the definite article in Gen 1:2 (treated almost as a proper name) is often cited as evidence of personified-chaos residue. See [[chaoskampf]].
