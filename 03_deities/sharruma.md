# Sharruma

Sharruma (Hurrian *Šarruma*) is the Hurrian-Hittite **mountain god**, son of the storm-god **[[teshub]]** and the great goddess **[[hebat]]** — the divine prince of the imperial Hittite pantheon. He is most famously depicted at the open-air rock sanctuary of **Yazılıkaya** near Hattusa, where, in the empire's central theological statement, he appears embracing and protecting the Great King **Tudhaliya IV** — literally enfolding the monarch in his arm, his pointed horned cap pressed to the king's, as the deity who guards the royal person. Associated with bulls and called "the Calf of Teshub," he strides on panthers or mountains in art. As a personal protector-god of Hittite kings and queens (the influential queen Puduhepa promoted his cult), Sharruma is the embodiment of the divine sponsorship of Hittite kingship.
