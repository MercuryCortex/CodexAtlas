# Tsukuyomi-no-Mikoto

## Identity
Moon-deity of Shinto, born from the right eye of [[izanagi]] during his post-Yomi purification, paired with [[amaterasu]] (left eye / sun) and [[susanoo]] (nose / storm). In the *Nihon Shoki* variant, Tsukuyomi kills the food-goddess Uke Mochi after being disgusted by her producing food from her orifices — provoking Amaterasu's permanent separation of sun and moon (etiological for day–night alternation).

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[selene]]** / **[[khonsu]]** / **[[nanna-sin]]** — *parallel-motif*: lunar deity in personal-divine form.
- **[[amaterasu]]** — sibling complementary opposite (sun ↔ moon).
