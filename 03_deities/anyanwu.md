# Anyanwu

Anyanwu (*Anyanwụ*, literally "the eye of the sun," or simply "the sun") is the Igbo deity of the **sun and its light** — and, in the layered theology of the Igbo, the **visible manifestation of the supreme God**. Igbo religion centers on a transcendent high god, **[[chukwu-igbo|Chukwu]]** (Chineke, "the creating spirit"), who is rather remote; Anyanwu is the brilliant, life-giving **radiance through which that high god's power and benevolence are seen and felt** in the world — the warmth that ripens crops, the light by which one lives and labours, the "eye of the day" that sees all. He is frequently named together with **Agbala**, the fertile generative power (especially of the earth and of human increase), the pair expressing the sky-light and earth-fertility through which the supreme God sustains creation. To swear by Anyanwu, the all-seeing sun, was to call the highest witness to one's truth. Anyanwu embodies the Igbo intuition that the distant Creator is nonetheless present and visible in the daily, indispensable miracle of sunlight.
