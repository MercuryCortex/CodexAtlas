# Xolotl

Xolotl (Nahuatl *Xólotl*) is the **dog-headed** god of Venus-as-evening-star and the **twin** of **[[quetzalcoatl]]** (the morning star) — together the two faces of the planet Venus as it leads and follows the sun. Where Quetzalcoatl rises bright before dawn, Xolotl is the deity who **escorts the sun down into the underworld** each night and guards it on its passage, and who likewise guides the souls of the dead on the road to Mictlan — a canine psychopomp, fittingly, since the Aztecs buried a dog to lead the deceased across the underworld's rivers. In the *Legend of the Fifth Sun*, it is Xolotl (or Quetzalcoatl) who descends to **[[mictlantecuhtli]]**'s realm to retrieve the **bones of the previous humanity**, from which, sprinkled with the gods' own blood, the people of the present age are remade. Associated with twins, monstrosity, and reversal (he is shown with backward feet), he lends his name to the *xoloitzcuintli* (the Mexican hairless dog) and the **axolotl** salamander — said to be Xolotl himself, transformed into water to escape sacrifice.
