# Surtr

Surtr ("the Black / Swarthy one") is the great **fire-giant of Muspellheim**, the primordial realm of fire that existed before the worlds — and the agent of the world's fiery end at **Ragnarök**. He stands at the burning frontier of Muspell wielding a **flaming sword "brighter than the sun,"** and the *Völuspá* and Snorri's *Gylfaginning* tell that when the doom of the gods comes, Surtr rides forth at the head of **Muspell's sons**, shattering the rainbow-bridge Bifröst beneath them, to do battle on the plain of Vígríðr. There he slays the sword-less **[[freyr]]**, and at the end **flings fire across the whole world**, so that flame engulfs earth and heaven and the cosmos is consumed — before it rises again, green and renewed, from the sea. Surtr is the Norse embodiment of the primal, world-ending fire: older than the gods, and outlasting them, the necessary conflagration between one world-age and the next.
