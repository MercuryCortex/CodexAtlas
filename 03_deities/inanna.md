# Inanna

Alias node. See [[inanna-sumerian]] for the full entry.

Inanna is the supreme Sumerian goddess — Lady of Heaven, queen of love and war, embodiment of the planet Venus. Her Akkadian counterpart is [[ishtar-akkadian]]. As consort of [[dumuzi-tammuz]] / [[tammuz-dumuzi]], she is the principal divine figure in the Descent of Inanna narrative, the oldest surviving descent-to-the-underworld myth (~2100 BCE), which provides the structural template for the [[dying-rising-god]] pattern across the ancient Near East.
