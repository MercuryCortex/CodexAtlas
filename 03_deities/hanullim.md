# Hanullim — Lord of Heaven

**Hanullim** (하느님, "Lord of Heaven" or "Heavenly One") is the supreme deity of the Donghak and Cheondogyo traditions — the divine being encountered by Choe Je-u in his revelatory experience of 1860 and addressed throughout the [[phase-7-042-donggyeong-daejeon|Donggyeong Daejeon]]. Hanullim is simultaneously transcendent creator and immanent presence in every human being — the source of the Donghak doctrine *innaecheon* ("each person is Heaven").
