# Eris

Eris (Greek *Éris*, "Strife"; Roman *Discordia*) personifies discord. Hesiod gives two contradictory genealogies that became a famous moral teaching: in the *Theogony* she is a grim daughter of **[[nyx-primordial]]** (Night), mother of Toil, Famine, Battles, and Oath; but in *Works and Days* (11–26) he distinguishes **two** Erides — one the destructive strife of war, the other the *good* Eris of healthy competition that drives potter against potter and farmer against farmer to excel. Her most consequential myth is the wedding of Peleus and Thetis: uninvited, Eris tosses among the goddesses the golden apple inscribed *kallistē* ("for the fairest"), igniting the **Judgment of Paris** between **[[aphrodite-greek]]**, Hera, and Athena — and thus the Trojan War (*Cypria*).
