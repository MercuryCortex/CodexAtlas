Avestan Arǝdvī Sūrā Anāhitā — Zoroastrian goddess of waters, fertility, and warriorship. Younger Avestan *Yašt* 5 (the Ābān Yašt) is dedicated to her. Iconographically and functionally cognate with Mesopotamian Inanna-Ishtar in Achaemenid syncretic contexts. State cult promoted under Artaxerxes II Mnemon (-405 to -358) per Berossus.
