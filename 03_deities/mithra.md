# Mithra

Avestan / Zoroastrian deity of the **covenant** + the **sworn oath** — the divine guardian of contract-keeping who sees the false-swearer with the unblinking sun. Cognate of Vedic *Mitra*. The Younger-Avestan *Mihr Yašt* (Yašt 10) is the principal extended hymn. Distinct from but ancestral to the Roman-imperial-era [mithras] of the mystery cult.
