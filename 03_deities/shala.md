# Shala

Shala (*Šala*) is the Mesopotamian goddess of **grain and the fertile furrowed field** and the consort of the storm-god **[[adad]]** — a pairing that unites the two halves of agricultural life: the **rain** (Adad) and the **grain-field** it waters (Shala, "Lady of the Furrow"). She is a goddess of fecundity and, in her epithets, of **mercy and compassion**. Her emblem the ear of grain became the **constellation of the Furrow** — the figure the Greeks later knew as **Virgo**, the maiden holding the spike of wheat (Spica) — making Shala one of the threads connecting Mesopotamian agricultural religion to the Western zodiac. As Adad's wife she shares in the storm-god's wide Near-Eastern diffusion (her West-Semitic counterpart **Shalash** is the consort of Dagan).
