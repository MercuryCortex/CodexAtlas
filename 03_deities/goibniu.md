# Goibniu

Goibniu (Old Irish *Goibniu*, from *goba*, "smith") is the **divine smith of the Tuatha Dé Danann** — one of the *trí dée dáno*, the "three gods of craft," alongside Luchta the wright (carpenter) and Credne the brazier. In the run-up to the **Second Battle of Mag Tuired** the three work as a miraculous assembly line: Goibniu forges a spearhead in three blows, Luchta makes the shaft, Credne rivets it, and any weapon Goibniu makes flies true and kills — and weapons he repairs overnight cannot fail again, a divine armaments advantage the Fomorians try desperately to sabotage. He also presides over the **Fled Goibnenn**, the "Feast of Goibniu," whose otherworldly ale confers **immortality and freedom from disease** on the gods who drink it. In Welsh tradition he appears as **Govannon**. Goibniu belongs to the wide Indo-European and Near-Eastern type of the **divine smith** (compare Hephaestus, **[[kothar-wa-khasis|Kothar]]**, Ilmarinen, and Wayland), the craftsman whose forge underwrites both war and the feast.
