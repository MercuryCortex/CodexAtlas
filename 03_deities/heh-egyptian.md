# Heh

## Identity

Heh ("infinity," "boundlessness," "millions") is one of the eight primordial deities of the Hermopolitan Ogdoad and the male member of its second pair, personifying the limitless, boundless quality of the pre-creation abyss alongside his feminine counterpart [[hauhet]]. The four Ogdoad pairs — Nun/Naunet (waters), Heh/Hauhet (infinity), Kek/Kauket (darkness), Amun/Amaunet (hiddenness) — together describe the conditions of the world before the first sunrise. Heh's domain is endlessness: the unbounded extent that has no edge, no limit, and no measure.

## The god of "millions of years"

Beyond the Ogdoad, Heh became the standard Egyptian personification of *millions of years* — and in this capacity he is one of the most recognizable figures in Egyptian iconography. He is shown as a crouching man holding a notched palm-rib (the hieroglyph for "year") in each hand, often kneeling on the *neb*-basket and sometimes with a *šen*-ring of eternity or an *ankh*. Pharaohs were depicted receiving "millions of years" from Heh, and the motif decorated thrones, jewelry (it appears on objects from the tomb of Tutankhamun), and temple reliefs as a wish for an unending reign. This makes Heh the rare Ogdoad member with an active, widely-attested cult-iconography that long outlived the Hermopolitan system itself.

## Cross-tradition identifications

- **[[hauhet]]** — *ancient-identification*: Heh's female counterpart; together they are the boundlessness-pair of the Ogdoad.
- **[[chaos-primordial]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: the limitless, formless expanse that Heh personifies parallels the Greek primordial void (Khaos) and resonates with the *tohu wa-bohu* ("formless and void") of Genesis 1:2 — the pre-creation condition of unbounded formlessness.
- **the Ogdoad complex ([[nun]], [[amun]], [[amaunet]])** — Heh operates within the same eightfold theology of the pre-cosmic abyss centered on Hermopolis under the patronage of [[thoth]].

## Disputes

A long-standing question is whether the Ogdoad's "Heh" (infinity as a cosmic principle) and the popular "Heh" of the millions-of-years iconography are one figure or two homonymous deities who merged. Most Egyptologists treat them as the same theological concept — boundless extent of time and space — developed in two registers (cosmogonic and royal-iconographic).
