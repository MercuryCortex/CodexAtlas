# Pöqánghoya (Elder Twin War God)

In Hopi cosmogony, Pöqánghoya is the Elder of the Twin War Gods, created (or thought into existence) by [[spider-grandmother-hopi]]. He is sent to the North Pole to keep the earth properly rotating and stable. He governs physical structure, mountains, rocks, and the earth's solidity. Together with his younger brother [[palongawhoya]], he maintains the proper vibration and order of the world. As one of the divine twins, he is an instance of the [[divine-twins]] motif — compare the Haudenosaunee Good Mind / Evil Mind twins ([[sapling-deity]] / [[flint-deity]]), though the Hopi Twins are cooperative rather than adversarial.
