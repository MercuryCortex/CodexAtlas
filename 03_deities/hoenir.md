# Hœnir

Hœnir is an enigmatic but structurally important Æsir god — one of the trio who **create the first humans**. In the *Völuspá* and Snorri's account, **[[odin]]**, Hœnir, and Lóðurr find the lifeless tree-trunks **Ask and Embla** on the shore and give them life: Odin gives breath/spirit, **Hœnir gives *óðr*** (sense, reason, the power of movement and feeling), and Lóðurr gives *lá* (blood / vital fluids) and *litu góða* (good, fair complexion) per Völuspá st. 18 — so Hœnir is the giver of the human *mind* (the óðr-to-Hœnir mapping follows the common Lindow/Turville-Petre reading; it is debated). After the **Æsir–Vanir war**, Hœnir is handed over to the Vanir as a **hostage** (with the wise Mímir), where the *Ynglinga saga* tells a wry tale of his indecisiveness without Mímir to counsel him. Finally, he is named among the gods who **survive Ragnarök**, and in the renewed world it is Hœnir who "chooses the prophetic wood-lots" — reading the future of the reborn cosmos. He embodies the gift of the human mind and the thread of prophecy carried across the world's destruction.
