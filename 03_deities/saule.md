# Saulė

## Identity
Baltic sun-goddess; the feminine sun preserved in Baltic (and Germanic, where *Sól*/*Sunne* is feminine) against the masculine sun of Greek-Latin (Helios, Sol). Drives a chariot of copper across the sky; her **amber tears** are the etiological origin of Baltic amber, the principal trade-commodity of the Bronze Age Baltic and the long-distance import the Mediterranean knew as Baltic amber from at least Mycenaean times. She is paired with the Moon (Mēness) in a fraught marriage in the *dainos*: Mēness is unfaithful, looking on the Morning Star (Auseklis / Aušrinė); the marriage breaks; Perkūnas splits the Moon with his lightning — etiological of lunar phases.
