# Ogou — Iron-and-War Lwa

The Haitian Vodou lwa of **iron, war, smithing, and political/military power** — diasporic continuation of the Yoruba [[ogun]] and Fon [[gu-vodun]] iron-deity tradition transmitted through the transatlantic slave trade. Standard Petwo/Nago rite figure. Multiple manifestations: **Ogou Feray** (the warrior soldier), **Ogou Badagri** (the diplomat), **Ogou Balendjo** (the healer-warrior). Identified syncretically with **Saint James the Greater** (Santiago) in Vodou's Catholic-saint correspondence-system. Played a documented role in the religious-political organization of the **Haitian Revolution** (1791-1804) as a patron-lwa of military victory.
