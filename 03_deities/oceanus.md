# Oceanus

Oceanus (*Ōkeanós*) is the Titan of the **great freshwater river that encircles the flat disk of the world** — the boundary of the known earth and the source from which all rivers, springs, and seas flow. Eldest of the twelve Titans, children of Ouranos (Sky) and **Gaia** (Earth), he weds his sister **[[tethys]]**, and together they engender the world's **rivers** and the three thousand **Oceanids** (water-nymphs). Notably, Oceanus takes **no part** in the Titans' war against Zeus, remaining neutral at the world's edge, and so keeps his domain. In *Iliad* 14 Homer twice calls him "the origin (*genesis*) of the gods" and of all things — a primal-water cosmogony that comparativists set beside the Mesopotamian **[[tiamat|Apsu-Tiamat]]** freshwater/saltwater origin of the gods (West 1997). He is the mythic horizon: where Oceanus runs, the world ends.
