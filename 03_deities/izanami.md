# Izanami-no-Mikoto

## Identity
Primordial creator-goddess of Shinto, sister-consort of [[izanagi]]. She dies in childbirth after birthing Kagutsuchi the fire-god, descending to **Yomi-no-kuni** where she eats the food of the underworld and is thereby bound there — a structural parallel to [[persephone-greek]]'s pomegranate-eating. When Izanagi pursues her and breaks the taboo of looking at her, she becomes hostile, sending the *yomotsu-shikome* (women of the underworld) after him; the cosmological etiology of death-as-final attaches to her bargain that 1,000 humans will die each day to her 1,500-births bargain.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[persephone-greek]]** — *parallel-motif*: food-taboo binds the living to the underworld.
- **[[ereshkigal]]** — *parallel-motif*: queen of the dead, intercepts living visitors (Inanna's Descent, Nergal-Ereshkigal).
- **[[hel]]** — *parallel-motif*: ruler of the dead in a non-paradisal afterlife.
