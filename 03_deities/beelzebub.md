# Beelzebub

## Identity
A **Philistine Ba'al cult-form** transformed by Hebrew Bible polemic into a paradigmatic foreign-deity-as-demon. The original epithet was most likely **Ba'al Zebul** ("Prince Ba'al" or "Lord of the Lofty Abode") — a standard West-Semitic Ba'al-title attested at Ugarit; the Deuteronomistic editors of 2 Kings 1:2–16 alter it to **Ba'al Zebub** ("Lord of Flies"), a derisive pun. By the Second Temple period (Testament of Solomon, ~1st–3rd c. CE) Beelzebub has become a high-ranking demonic prince; by the New Testament, **Beelzeboul** is the name the Pharisees use when accusing Jesus of casting out demons through demonic agency (Mark 3:22, Matt 12:24, Luke 11:15).

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[baal-hadad]]** — *ancient-identification*: original Ba'al-epithet of Ekron.
- **[[satan-christian]] / [[lucifer]]** — *ancient-identification*: identified with the prince of demons in NT polemic, with Lucifer in medieval Christian demonology.
