# Rhea (Greek Titaness)

Alias node. See [[rhea]] for the full entry.

Rhea is the Greek Titaness, daughter of [[uranus-greek]] and [[gaia]], consort of [[kronos]], and mother of the six principal Olympians: [[zeus]], [[hera]], [[poseidon]], [[hades]], [[demeter]], and [[hestia]]. Her defining mythological act is hiding the infant Zeus from Kronos (who swallowed each of his children) by substituting a stone wrapped in swaddling clothes. When Zeus matured, he freed his siblings and overthrew the Titans.

In the Greek-Hellenistic world, Rhea was identified with the Phrygian [[cybele]] (Magna Mater) — both are mountain mother-goddesses associated with lions and ecstatic ritual. The ancient identification is documented from Pindar and Euripides; the Hellenistic-period cultic merger was complete enough that the Romans who received the Cybele cult in 204 BCE understood her as Rhea.
