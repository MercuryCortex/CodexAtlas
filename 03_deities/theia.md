# Theia

Theia (*Theía*, "divine"; also **Euryphaessa**, "wide-shining") is the Titaness of **light and sight** — specifically the light *by which* things are seen and the gleam that makes gold, silver, and gems precious. Sister-wife of **[[hyperion]]**, she is the **mother of the three luminaries**: **[[helios]]** (Sun), **[[selene]]** (Moon), and **[[eos-greek]]** (Dawn). Pindar (*Isthmian* 5) opens by hailing "Mother Theia of many names," crediting her with the human esteem for gold and the power of the seeing eye — an unusually philosophical conception of a goddess as the principle of luminosity and visual value itself. Like Hyperion she belongs to the abstract Titan stratum behind the personified lights of the Greek sky.
