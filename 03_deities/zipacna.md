# Zipacna

## Identity

Zipacna (K'iche' **Sipakna**) is a great caiman-like earth-monster of the *Popol Vuh* — the boastful elder son of the false "sun" [[vucub-caquix]]. He claims to be the **maker of the mountains**, heaping them up overnight, and embodies the arrogant, brute power of the unordered earth that the Hero Twins must subdue before the true cosmos can be established.

## Mythological role

Zipacna's two episodes both turn on his pride and his fall. First, he encounters the **Four Hundred Boys** struggling to raise a great log for their house; he carries it alone with ease, and when they plot to kill him by crushing him in a pit, he outwits and **kills them all** — the slain youths rising into the sky to become the **Pleiades**. To avenge them, the Hero Twins [[hunahpu]] and Xbalanque devise a trap: they fashion a great artificial **crab** and place it in a ravine; the gluttonous Zipacna, lured down to eat it, is caught when the Twins bring the **mountain down upon him**, crushing him and turning him to stone. With the deaths of Vucub Caquix and his sons Zipacna and Cabracan, the false, prideful powers are cleared away.

## Cross-tradition significance

Zipacna belongs to the *Popol Vuh*'s great theme of the **humbling of the false and arrogant powers** before the right order of the world can be set. As a caiman-like maker and embodiment of the earth and mountains, he parallels the Aztec [[cipactli]], the primordial earth-caiman — a shared Mesoamerican image of the earth as a vast reptilian monster. His defeat by the Hero Twins is part of the larger pattern of **monster-slaying that establishes cosmic order**, the Mesoamerican kin of the chaoskampf.
