# Maitreya

## Identity
The **Future Buddha** — the next Buddha to appear in our world, after the dharma of the historical [[siddhartha-gautama-buddha|Śākyamuni]] has been forgotten. He currently resides in **Tuṣita Heaven** waiting for the right kalpa to descend; his name *Maitreya* / *Metteyya* derives from *maitrī / mettā* = loving-kindness, indicating his characteristic virtue. **Universal across all Buddhist traditions** — Theravāda, Mahāyāna, Vajrayana — making him the rare pan-Buddhist messianic figure.

## Earliest attestation
- **Cakkavatti-sīhanāda Sutta** (Pāli, Dīgha Nikāya 26) — predicts Maitreya's coming after the dharma has been forgotten.
- **Maitreyavyākaraṇa** ("Prophecy of Maitreya") — Sanskrit/Tibetan/Chinese.
- **Mahāvastu** — early Mahāsāṃghika biography of the Buddha with Maitreya cosmology.

## Mythological role
**Eschatological savior**: appears at the end of a long period of dharmic decline; restores the teaching to its full radiance. **Bodhisattva of inspiration**: in East Asian folk practice, the laughing fat-bellied **Budai / Hotei** is taken as an incarnation of Maitreya — the friendly grandfatherly figure who tousles children's hair and dispenses sweets. **Political-religious explosive**: Maitreya-messianic uprisings recur across Chinese history (White Lotus Society, 1351 Red Turban Rebellion that founded the Ming dynasty, 1796 White Lotus Rebellion, 19th-c. Eight Trigrams revolt) — Maitreya is the Buddhist millenarian-revolutionary figure. **Tibetan**: the great future-coming throne-figure Maitreya statues at Tabo, Thiksey, Diskit etc. are the largest sculpted figures in the Himalayan tradition.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[jesus-christ-deity]] (parousia)** — *scholarly-parallel*: Second-Coming structural cognate (Sponberg & Hardacre 1988).
- **[[muhammad-al-mahdi]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: Islamic Mahdi.
- **Kalki** — *parallel-motif*: Vishnu's tenth avatar; future world-restorer.
- **Saoshyant** — *parallel-motif*: Zoroastrian world-renovator.
