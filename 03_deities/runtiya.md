# Runtiya

Runtiya (also **Kurunta**, written with the Sumerogram **KAL/LAMMA**) is the **Luwian protective stag-god** — the divine lord of the **wild animals and the hunt**, whose sacred beast is the **stag** and who is depicted standing upon a deer, bow in hand. He belongs to the tutelary (LAMMA) class of Anatolian guardian-deities, presiding over the open country and the game that lives in it, and he is regularly paired with the Storm-god **[[tarhunna|Tarhunza]]** in Luwian cult, where he remains prominent through the Hittite empire and into the Iron-Age hieroglyphic-Luwian states (the personal name *Kurunta* — borne by a 13th-century rival for the Hittite throne — is built on his name). As the Anatolian deity of deer and hunting he invites comparison with the later Greek-Anatolian hunting divinities and the wild-game sphere of **[[artemis|Artemis]]**, though the relationship is typological rather than a direct descent. (The Luwian *sun*-god, by contrast, was **Tiwaz** — a separate deity, cognate to the Hittite Ištanu.) Runtiya embodies the sacred, dangerous abundance of the Anatolian wilderness.
