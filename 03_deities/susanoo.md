# Susanoo-no-Mikoto

## Identity
Storm-god and disruptive brother of [[amaterasu]], born from Izanagi's nose. Expelled from heaven for his violations against Amaterasu (smearing the sacred hall, flaying the heavenly piebald horse), he descends to Izumo, slays the eight-headed serpent **Yamata-no-Orochi**, recovers the sword **Kusanagi** from its tail, and becomes the founding ancestor of the Izumo line — a mythological counter-genealogy to the Amaterasu-Yamato line.

## Mythological role
The **Orochi-slaying** is the cleanest Japanese instance of the **[[chaoskampf]]** pattern — Indo-European mythological template (Indra vs Vritra, Marduk vs Tiamat, Thor vs Jormungandr, Apollo vs Python). Watkins 1995 traces the formula. Kusanagi later becomes one of the **three imperial regalia** when transferred via Amaterasu to Ninigi — so the storm-god's spoils underwrite imperial legitimacy.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[marduk]] / [[teshub]] / [[indra]] / [[thor]] / [[baal-hadad]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: storm-god vs. cosmic-serpent.
- **[[amaterasu]]** — sibling-and-rival; sun/storm complementary pair.
- **Gozu Tennō** — *folk-syncretism* (medieval honji-suijaku equated Susanoo with the Buddhist plague-deity Gozu Tennō, basis of the Gion festival).
