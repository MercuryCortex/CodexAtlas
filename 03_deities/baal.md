# Baal

**Alias node.** See [[baal-hadad]] for the full node.

*Baal* (Ba'al, "lord") is the generic West Semitic title and the common name for the Canaanite storm-deity whose full canonical form in the vault is [[baal-hadad]]. The title *ba'al* means "lord/owner" and was applied to multiple Canaanite and Ugaritic deities, but in the context of the Ugaritic Baal Cycle and Hebrew Bible polemics, it refers specifically to Baal Hadad — the storm god, cloud-rider, and opponent of [[mot]] and [[yam]].

For the full mythological profile, cross-tradition edges (including the [[chaoskampf]] pattern, the Baal-Yahweh polemic in Elijah narratives, and the connection to [[thor]] and [[indra]] as Indo-European/Semitic storm-deity parallels), see [[baal-hadad]].
