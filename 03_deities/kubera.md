# Kubera

## Identity
Hindu and Buddhist **lord of wealth** — half-brother of Rāvaṇa (in the Ramayana lineage), king of the Yakṣas, guardian of the **North** direction in the four-Lokapāla cosmology (with [[indra]] East, [[yama-vedic|Yama]] South, [[varuna]] West). Resides in **Alaka** on Mount Kailash, near Shiva. **Buddhist redeployment** is unusually clean: Kubera becomes **Vaiśravaṇa**, one of the Four Heavenly Kings of the Buddhist cosmology, guarding the dharma in the North; from there as **Bishamonten** (Japanese) and **Jambhala** (Tibetan, wealth-yidam) he becomes one of the most widely-invoked Buddhist wealth-deities across East Asia.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **Vaiśravaṇa / Bishamonten / Jambhala** — *ancient-identification*: Buddhist absorptions.
- **Plutus** (Greek) — *parallel-motif*: god of wealth.
- **[[hades]]** — *parallel-motif*: rich-one of the underworld (Hades = "the unseen" but Pluto / Ploutōn = "the wealthy").
