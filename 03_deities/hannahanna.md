# Hannahanna

Hannahanna (*Ḫannaḫanna*, literally "Grandmother") is the great **mother-goddess** of Hittite-Anatolian religion, written with the Sumerogram **DINGIR.MAH** ("the exalted goddess"). She presides over **birth and motherhood** and is associated with **fate** through the Gulšeš, the goddesses of destiny. Her most memorable role is in the cycle of **Disappearing-God myths**: when the angry god **[[telipinu|Telipinu]]** (or the Storm-god) vanishes and the world withers — fertility, fire, and growth all failing — it is the wise Hannahanna who advises the desperate gods and sends out the **bee** to find the missing deity, sting him awake, and anoint him, so that he can be coaxed home and the land revived. She is the grandmotherly counselor at the heart of the pantheon, the maternal wisdom that knows how to restore broken order. She belongs to the wider ancient-Anatolian mother-goddess tradition that also includes **[[kubaba|Kubaba]]**.
