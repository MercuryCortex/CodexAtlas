# Tlazolteotl

The Aztec earth-mother goddess of **sexual transgression and its absolution** — the "**Filth-Eater**" (*Tlaelquani*). Tlazolteotl is the personified theological mechanism by which sexual transgression is absorbed into the earth and absolved: the penitent (especially the dying) confessed sexual sins to a priest of Tlazolteotl, who recorded them and transmitted them to the goddess, who consumed them. The black paint around her mouth in iconography is the absorbed filth.

**Aztec confession.** Tlazolteotl-mediated confession is one of the most striking structural parallels between Aztec and Christian theology — the Spanish conquerors and missionaries themselves remarked on it (sometimes claiming pre-Christian Tlazolteotl confession as evidence of devil-mimicry of Christian sacrament, sometimes more soberly as cross-cultural-religious convergence). Confession was once-in-a-lifetime, typically at the deathbed, and absolved the confessor before earthly punishment for sexual crimes (which the Aztec state otherwise punished severely). The convergence with Catholic auricular confession is a documented missionary observation.

Her origin is **Huastec** (the Gulf Coast Maya-adjacent culture), absorbed into the central Mexican Aztec pantheon during the Mexica imperial expansion. The Huastec stratum makes Tlazolteotl one of the most clearly tracked deity-absorption cases in Mesoamerican religious history.
