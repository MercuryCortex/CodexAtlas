# Vucub Caquix

Vucub Caquix (K'iche' *Wuqub' Kaqix*, "Seven Macaw") is the proud macaw-demon of the **Popol Vuh** who, in the twilight age before the true sun rises, **falsely proclaims himself to be both the sun and the moon** — a glittering imposter perched in his nanze tree, his teeth set with jewels and his eyes shining with metal, lighting a world that has no real luminaries yet. His vainglory, and that of his two monstrous sons **Zipacna** (the mountain-maker) and **Cabracan** (the earthquake), is an affront the gods cannot allow, and so the **Hero Twins** Hunahpu and Xbalanque set out to humble him: Hunahpu shoots Seven Macaw from his tree with a blowgun, and through a ruse the Twins **extract his jeweled teeth and his metal eyes**, robbing him of his false brilliance so that he withers and dies. Maya art identifies him with the **Principal Bird Deity** that perches atop the World Tree, and his defeat (linked to the setting of the Big Dipper) clears the way for the real sun — making Vucub Caquix the embodiment of the false pride that the order of the cosmos must overthrow.
