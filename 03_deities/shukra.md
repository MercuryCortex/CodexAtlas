# Shukra

Shukra (*Śukra*, "the bright/white one"; Vedic **Ushanas Kavya**) is the **preceptor of the asuras** and the **Navagraha deity of the planet Venus**. His unique power is the ***sanjivani vidyā*** — the secret knowledge of **reviving the dead** — which he uses to resurrect the asuras slain by the gods in their endless wars, making him indispensable to the demonic side and a perpetual problem for the devas (the gods send Brihaspati's son Kacha to steal the secret from him, in a famous tale of love and trickery involving Shukra's daughter Devayani). As Venus he is a great **benefic** — the planet of love, marriage, beauty, the arts, and pleasure — and the counterpart-rival of **[[brihaspati]]**, guru of the gods. With **[[shani]]** (Saturn), Mangala (Mars), Brihaspati (Jupiter), and the rest he forms the **Navagraha**, the nine planetary powers that govern the unfolding of karma. He gives his name to Friday (*Śukravāra*).
