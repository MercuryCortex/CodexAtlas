# Apsu / Abzu

The primordial fresh subterranean waters. In Sumerian tradition Abzu is both a cosmic region (the underground reservoir on which the earth floats) and the domain of [[enki-ea]] (whose cult-temple at Eridu was named E-abzu). In Akkadian Enuma Elish Apsu is personified — Tiamat's male consort, killed by Ea early in the story, his body becoming Ea's abode. The killing of Apsu motivates Tiamat's later war against the younger gods.
