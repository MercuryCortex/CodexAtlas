# Wí (The Sun)

One of the four Superior Wakan Beings in the Lakota Sixteen Wakan hierarchy, documented by Walker (1980). Wí is the Sun — the primary visible agent of order, rhythm, and time in the Plains world. The *Wiwáŋyaŋg Wačhípi* (Sun Dance) is the most sacred communal ceremony of the Lakota and is performed in relation to Wí — dancers gaze at the sun, fast, and in some cases pierce their chests with skewers attached by rope to the central sacred tree, as a form of communal sacrifice for the renewal of the people and the cosmos. The Sun Dance was outlawed by the US government 1883–1934 (the Lakota Jurisdiction Act / Bureau of Indian Affairs religious suppression); its revival is one of the central acts of 20th-c. Lakota ceremonial revitalization. See [[tradition-lakota]] and [[wakan-tanka]].
