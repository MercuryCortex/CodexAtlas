# Anthropos (Gnostic)

## Identity

In Sethian and Valentinian Gnostic systems, the **Anthrōpos** ("Human") is a pre-existent divine aeon — a member of the Pleroma (the divine fullness) — who serves as the perfect heavenly template of humanity. The earthly Adam is the Demiurge's inferior copy, created in imitation of the divine Anthrōpos whose image (reflected in the lower waters at the moment of creation) the Demiurge saw and tried to replicate.

**In the *Apocryphon of John***: the figure appears as **Adamas** (*Geradamas*, "the Perfect Human") — the highest aeon of the divine humanity, existing in the Pleroma before Sophia's fall that generates the Demiurge and the material world. The divine spark in human beings comes from this heavenly Adamas, not from the Demiurge's creation.

**In the Hermetic *Poimandres*** (Corpus Hermeticum I): the heavenly **Anthrōpos** — image of the Father, beautiful and radiant — descends through the seven planetary spheres, falls in love with Nature (the material principle), and joins her. From that union come embodied humans, who carry a divine spark (from their father Anthrōpos) imprisoned in matter (from their mother Nature). The Hermetic Anthropos drama is the anthropogony: *why humans are simultaneously divine and material*.

## The Demiurgic Inversion

The Gnostic Anthropos tradition inverts the Platonic Demiurge-story: in Plato's *Timaeus*, the Demiurge creates a good cosmos from the Forms. In the Gnostic versions, the Demiurge creates an *inferior* cosmos from an *inferior* image — the reflection of the Anthrōpos seen in the lower waters. The very beauty that makes humans partially divine is the beauty of the Anthrōpos that the Demiurge stole to animate his clay creation.
