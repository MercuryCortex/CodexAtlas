# Tirawa

Tirawa (Pawnee *Atius Tirawa*, "Father Above" or "the Expanse of the Heavens") is the **supreme creative power** of the Pawnee of the central Plains — less an anthropomorphic god than an all-encompassing **sky-and-cosmos power** who orders creation and then governs it **through the star-deities** to whom he assigns their stations and tasks. In the distinctive Pawnee **star cosmology**, Tirawa places the **Morning Star** (a fierce masculine power of the east) and the **Evening Star** (a fertile feminine power of the west), whose cosmic union creates the first human girl, and sets the **North Star** as the unmoving "chief," the **South Star**, and the four world-quarter stars to hold up the heavens — a sacred astronomy that the Pawnee mapped onto their earth-lodges and ceremonies. From Tirawa flows the **breath of life** and all sacred power, mediated downward to humans by the stars and the *kawaharu* bundles. The Skidi Pawnee's notorious **Morning Star sacrifice** (a captive maiden offered at the heliacal rising of the planet) was performed within this Tirawa-ordered star-theology — making Tirawa one of the most cosmologically elaborated supreme beings of Native North America.
