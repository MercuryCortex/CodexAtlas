# Brihaspati

Brihaspati (*Bṛhaspati*; also **Brahmanaspati**, "Lord of Sacred Speech") is the Vedic god who is **chaplain and preceptor of the gods** — the divine *purohita* whose prayer and ritual potency win the gods their battles, especially for **[[indra]]**. In the Rigveda he is a deity of the sacred word and of cosmic order, who shatters the Vala cave with his roar to release the imprisoned cows (the dawns). In classical Hinduism he becomes **Guru**, the **Navagraha deity of the planet Jupiter** — benefic, golden, the planet of wisdom, expansion, and right counsel — and the **guru of the devas**, eternal counterpart and rival of **[[shukra]]**, the guru of the asuras. The intellectual-priestly pole of the Hindu cosmos, he lends his name to the *Bṛhaspati-sūtra* of the materialist Charvaka school and to Thursday (*Bṛhaspativāra*). He completes, with Shukra and Mangala, the vault's coverage of the planetary-teacher Navagraha.
