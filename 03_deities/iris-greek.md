# Iris

Iris (Greek *Îris*, "Rainbow") is the personification of the rainbow and a swift, winged messenger of the gods — pre-eminently of **[[hera]]** — who carries word between Olympus, earth, and the sea along the rainbow that bridges sky and water. Daughter of the sea-god Thaumas ("Wonder") and the Oceanid Electra, sister of the Harpies (Hesiod, *Theogony* 265–269), she is the principal divine herald of the *Iliad* (where she relays Zeus's and Hera's commands), filling the role that **[[hermes-greek]]** more often plays in the *Odyssey*. In the *Theogony* she also fetches the water of the Styx in a golden pitcher for the gods' most binding oaths. Unlike the masculine Hermes she has little mythology of her own beyond errands, but as the rainbow she is the visible sign of communication between the divine and human worlds.
