# Spider Woman (Na'ashjé'ii Asdzáá)

One of the most active and beloved of the Navajo Holy People. Spider Woman taught the Diné to weave — the loom she gave them has cosmological structure (warp = rain, weft = sunlight, the whole = the pattern of hózhó realized in material form). She guided and protected the Hero Twins on their journey to find their father [[sun-bearer]], advising them at each dangerous passage and giving them the medicine *nayee'nii* (life feathers) for protection. Spider Woman is associated with Spider Rock (Tse Na'ashjé'ii) in Canyon de Chelly, where she is said to live at the top of the 800-foot sandstone column. She is a figure of profound wisdom, not the malevolent spider of European fairy tales. See [[tradition-navajo]].
