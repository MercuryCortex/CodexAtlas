# Skaði

## Identity
Daughter of the giant Þjazi; **a jötunn who joins the Æsir** after the gods kill her father, choosing a husband from among them by their feet alone (she chooses Njörðr's beautifully-shaped feet, mistaking them for Baldr's). The marriage fails because Skaði cannot endure the seaside Nóatún and Njörðr cannot endure her mountain Þrymheimr; they separate. **Place-name evidence**: Scandinavia (Skandinavía, Scania) is etymologically derived by Vikstrand 2009 and others from her name — *Skaðin-avia* "Skaði's island."
