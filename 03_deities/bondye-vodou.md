# Bondye

## Identity

*Bondye* (from French Creole *Bon Dieu* — "Good God") is the supreme being of Haitian Vodou: the ultimate creator, cosmic ground, and inaccessible divine sovereign. Like [[olodumare]] in Yoruba religion and [[nzambi]] in Kongo cosmology, Bondye is a *deus otiosus* — the highest divine being, but not the object of ordinary cult or petition. Bondye created the cosmos and the *lwa* (intermediary divine beings), but does not intervene directly in human affairs; the *lwa* are the mediating divine presences approached in Vodou ritual. The theological structure is precise: Bondye : lwa :: [[olodumare]] : orishas :: Nyame : abosom.

## Theological structure

The inaccessibility of Bondye and the centrality of the *lwa* as intermediaries directly parallels the Yoruba [[olodumare]]-orisha structure — both transmitted from West/Central Africa to Haiti via the Middle Passage. Haitian Vodou's theological architecture preserves this African structural principle despite surface-level Catholic vocabulary (*Bon Dieu* replacing the African name). Desmangles (1992) argues this is not merely semantic but theological: the Catholic God and Bondye are functionally identified in Haitian religious practice, but the Vodou structure remains African in its intermediary-deity architecture.

## Cross-tradition identifications

- **[[olodumare]]** (Yoruba) — *manuscript-transmission*: the Yoruba supreme-being concept was transmitted to Haiti via enslaved Yoruba populations in Saint-Domingue; the structural parallel is not coincidental. Ref: Brandon 1993.
- **[[nzambi]]** (Kongo) — *scholarly-parallel*: substantial Kongo populations in Saint-Domingue contributed theological vocabulary; Thompson (1983) argues Kongo cosmological concepts permeate Haitian Vodou's *Petwo* rite in particular.
- **Catholic Bon Dieu** — *folk-syncretism*: the French colonial Catholic vocabulary (*Bon Dieu*) was absorbed into Vodou as the name for the supreme being; the Catholic God and Bondye are theologically identified in Haitian popular practice.
