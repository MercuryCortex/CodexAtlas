# Balarama

Balarama (also **Baladeva**, **Balabhadra**) is the **elder brother of [[krishna]]** and a god of agricultural strength — his signature weapon the **plough** (*hala*, hence *Halayudha*, "plough-armed"), with which he is said to have dragged the river Yamuna into a new course. Fair-skinned where Krishna is dark, fond of wine and prodigiously strong, he is Krishna's constant companion through the Vrindavana and Mathura cycles of the *Bhagavata Purana* and *Harivamsa*. Theologically his status is fluid and revealing: he is widely held to be an incarnation of the cosmic serpent **Shesha/Ananta** (on whom Vishnu reclines), and in a number of Dashavatara lists he is counted as **the eighth avatar of [[vishnu]]** — taking the slot that other lists give to the Buddha. As **Balabhadra** he is enshrined as one of the three deities of the **[[jagannath|Jagannath]]** triad at Puri. He embodies the pre-eminence of the elder brother, the sacredness of farming, and the deep link between Vaishnava devotion and the serpent-cosmology of Shesha.
