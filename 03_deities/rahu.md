# Rahu

Rahu (Sanskrit *Rāhu*) is the immortal **severed head** that devours the sun and moon — the Hindu explanation of eclipses and one of the two "shadow-planets" (*chhaya graha*) that complete the **Navagraha**. His origin is the great myth of the **Churning of the Ocean** (*Samudra Manthan*): when the gods and demons churn the cosmic sea for the nectar of immortality (*amrita*), the asura **Svarbhanu** disguises himself among the gods to steal a sip; the sun and moon expose him, and **[[vishnu]]**, in his enchantress form Mohini, beheads him with the discus — but the nectar has already passed his throat, so the head cannot die. The deathless head, **Rahu**, and the deathless body, **[[ketu]]**, become the two lunar nodes, and Rahu pursues Sun and Moon across the sky forever in revenge, swallowing them (the eclipse) only for them to slip out of his open neck. Astrologically Rahu signifies obsessive worldly desire, foreign influence, and sudden upheaval.
