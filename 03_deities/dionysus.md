# Dionysus

## Identity
Dionysus is the Greek god of wine, ecstatic possession, theatre, vegetation, and the death-and-return mystery cycle. He is the unsettling Olympian — twice-born (once from Semele, then from Zeus's thigh after her destruction by Zeus's manifestation), foreign-coded (mythically arriving from the East / Phrygia / Thrace), the god whose worship overflows the ordinary civic order in *thiasos*-bands of *bacchantes* / *maenads*. The Linear B tablets attest "*di-wo-nu-so*" at Pylos and Khania already in the 13th c. BCE — overturning the older scholarly view that Dionysus was a "late arrival" from Thrace and establishing his Mycenaean depth.

Dionysus is the most syncretism-prone Greek deity. Herodotus identifies him with Egyptian Osiris (both are dying-rising vegetation gods, both undergo dismemberment); the Orphic theogonies recast him as Dionysus-Zagreus, son of Zeus and Persephone, dismembered and devoured by the Titans, whose ashes Zeus uses to make humans — establishing the Orphic anthropology that humans contain a divine spark (Dionysian) embedded in a Titanic body. This Orphic Dionysus becomes a major source for later mystery-cult and philosophical speculations about the soul. The Roman Bacchus cult was so politically destabilizing that the Senate suppressed it in 186 BCE (the *Senatus consultum de Bacchanalibus*).
