# Amadioha

Amadioha (Igbo *Amadịọha*, often glossed "free will of the people") is the **sky- and thunder-god** of the Igbo of southeastern Nigeria — the most prominent active male deity of the pantheon and the chief executor of **divine justice**. Among the *alusi* (the great spirits serving under the supreme creator **[[chukwu-igbo|Chukwu]]**), Amadioha is the one whose **lightning** strikes down the guilty: thieves, oath-breakers, and those who pollute the land are said to be "killed by Amadioha," and to be struck by his bolt was taken as proof of secret crime (the corpse, marked as accursed, sometimes denied ordinary burial). His sacred animal and avatar is the **white ram**, and white is his color, signifying the purity of his judgments; in the Owerri region he is called **Kamalu/Kamanu**. He is closely paired with the earth-mother **[[ala-igbo|Ala]]**, the two together upholding the moral order — Ala defining right conduct (*omenala*) and Amadioha enforcing it from the sky. He remains a living figure of Igbo identity and Odinani revival today.
