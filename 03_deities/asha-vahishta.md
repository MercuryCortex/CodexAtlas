# Asha Vahishta

## Identity
Asha Vahishta (Avestan *Aṣ̌a Vahišta*, "Best Truth/Order"; Old Persian *Arta Vahišta*; Pahlavi *Ardwahišt*; Modern Persian *Ordibehesht*) is **the first-and-foremost of the six Amesha Spentas** (the "Bountiful Immortals," the seven supreme divine principles of Zoroastrian theology including Ahura Mazda himself). Asha represents **cosmic Truth-and-Order-and-Righteousness** — the **moral-metaphysical principle that the cosmos ought-to-be**. The concept is **directly cognate with Vedic *Ṛta***, making Asha-Ṛta **one of the most-load-bearing single concepts in Proto-Indo-Iranian religious-thought reconstruction**. Asha is **named more than any other principle in the Gathas** — the Gathic Zarathustra's entire theological program centers on Asha-Truth vs. Druj-Lie as the foundational cosmic opposition. **Asha's material manifestation is the sacred fire ([[atar]])** — the visible-divine-presence is the visible-manifestation of cosmic Truth-and-Order. The modern Iranian calendar's **second month** (April-May) is named *Ordibehesht* after Asha Vahishta — preserving the Amesha Spenta name in continuous Iranian cultural identity from Avestan to modern times.

## Earliest attestation
- **Gathas** ([[phase-2-002-gathas-of-zarathustra]]): **named throughout** — more frequently than any other Zoroastrian principle. The Gathas are theologically structured around the Asha-Truth vs. Druj-Lie cosmic opposition.
- **Younger Avesta** ([[phase-2-004-yasna-younger-avesta]]): extensive. **Yasht 3 (the Ardwahišt Yasht)** is the principal liturgical hymn.
- **Achaemenid Old Persian inscriptions** (Darius I onward, -518 onward): the cognate **Arta** appears in royal-religious-political vocabulary. Darius and successors style themselves as servants of *arta* (cosmic Truth) defeating *drauga* (cosmic Lie).
- **Pahlavi tradition**: Ardwahišt continues as central Amesha Spenta.
- **Modern Iranian calendar**: *Ordibehesht* as second month-name preserves the principle in continuous Iranian cultural identity.

## Mythological role

### Cosmic-principle-and-personified-being simultaneously
Asha is **both** an abstract cosmic principle (cosmic Truth-and-Order) **and** a personified divine being (the first Amesha Spenta). The double-aspect is theologically deliberate — the cosmic principle of Truth IS the divine being.

### Asha and the sacred fire
The **material manifestation** of Asha is the sacred fire ([[atar]] / [[sacred-fire-atash]]). The fire is visible-Truth; the Zoroastrian's daily-prayer-before-the-fire is daily-orientation-toward-cosmic-Truth.

### Asha-Druj cosmic opposition
The **foundational Zoroastrian cosmic-dualism**: Asha vs. [[druj]]. Every act of truthfulness, every act of cosmic-order-maintenance, every act of righteous-thought-word-deed strengthens Asha. Every act of falsehood, every act of pollution, every act of evil strengthens Druj. The cosmic combat is daily and personal.

## Cross-tradition identifications

### Asha ↔ Vedic Ṛta
**Direct Indo-Iranian etymological-and-functional cognate.** Both Avestan *Aṣ̌a* and Vedic *Ṛta* derive from Proto-Indo-Iranian \**Hr̥tó-* — "the right cosmic order; the truth that the cosmos is set into; that which has been set / ordered correctly." Both:
- Are **cosmic principle and divine being** simultaneously.
- Are **the foundational truth-and-order** of the cosmos.
- Are **opposed to the principle of lie/disorder** (Druj in Iran; *Anṛta* in India).
- Have **fire as material manifestation** in both traditions (Vedic Agni is closely associated with Ṛta).

**Asha-Ṛta is one of the most-load-bearing single Proto-Indo-Iranian religious concepts** in comparative religion. (West 2007 ch. 3 treats it extensively.)

### MASSIVE-WIN: Asha → Christian "righteousness" / Jewish *tzedek-tzedaqah*
The Persian-period Jewish religious-vocabulary transformation (under Achaemenid rule, -539 to -332) **substantially expands the Hebrew vocabulary of *tzedek* (righteousness, justice) and *tzedaqah* (righteous-action)** into a more-cosmic-metaphysical-principle-resembling-Asha than was the case in pre-exilic Hebrew Bible. The shift is documented in Second Isaiah, the Psalms, and especially the Qumran scrolls (the *Yahad* community's *tzedek*-vocabulary is extensively cosmic-dualistic). The structural parallel with Asha is real and probably substantially-influenced (Boyce 1991, Berquist 1995).

### Asha → Christian "righteousness of God" / *dikaiosynē theou* (Paul)
**Paul's vocabulary of *dikaiosynē theou*** ("righteousness of God," Romans 1:17, 3:21-26) — the principle through which God rectifies the cosmos in Christ — has structural-parallel to Asha as cosmic-Truth-and-Order. The Pauline doctrine inherits from Second-Temple Jewish vocabulary (which was Persian-period-influenced) and is mediated through Hellenistic-Jewish + early-Christian theological development. Direct Persian-influence on Pauline *dikaiosynē* is unprovable but the structural inheritance is real.

## Iconography
- **No standard ancient Zoroastrian anthropomorphic iconography** for Asha specifically.
- **Indirect iconographic-presence** through the **sacred fire** ([[atar]] / [[sacred-fire-atash]]) — Asha's material manifestation.

## Disputes
- **Gathic Asha — abstract principle or personified divine being?** Whether the Gathic stratum treats Asha primarily as (a) abstract cosmic-principle or (b) personified-divine-being is debated. Most scholars treat the Gathic Asha as a both-simultaneously concept; the systematization into Amesha-Spenta-as-divine-person is more clearly Younger-Avestan.
- **Persian → Jewish *tzedek* transmission scope.** How much of the Second-Temple Jewish expansion of *tzedek*-vocabulary is Persian-influenced vs. internal Hebrew-Bible-development is debated. Boyce 1991 + Berquist 1995 favor substantial Persian influence; conservative scholarship resists.
