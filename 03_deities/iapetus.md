# Iapetus

## Identity
Titan son of [[uranus-greek]] and [[gaia]]; father — by the Oceanid Clymene — of [[atlas-titan]], [[prometheus]], **Epimetheus**, and **Menoetius**. His descendants are the Titans most consequential for humanity: Atlas bears the heavens, Prometheus brings fire and shapes humans, Epimetheus releases evils through Pandora. Iapetus himself is imprisoned in Tartarus after the Titanomachy, but the entire post-Olympian relationship between gods and humans runs through his lineage.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **Japheth (Genesis 10)** — *scholarly-parallel*: the *Iapetos*/*Yepheth* naming-pair as ancestor-of-Europeans, proposed from Bochart 1646 onward; West 1997 treats as suggestive, not established. **[claim: contested]**
