# Tarhunna

Tarhunna (Hittite *Tarḫunna*; Luwian *Tarhunt/Tarhunz*) is the **native Anatolian Storm-god** — the indigenous chief deity of the Hittite and Luwian peoples, whose name means roughly "the Conqueror" (from the root *tarḫ-*, "to overcome"). He is the same great weather-god whom the Hurrians of the empire call **[[teshub]]**: in the religiously syncretic Hittite state the two names denote one storm-god, lord of the sky, granter of kingship and victory, riding a chariot drawn by his sacred bulls. His central myth is the **chaoskampf** against the dragon **[[illuyanka]]**, recited at the *purulli* New-Year festival to renew cosmic order. After the empire's fall, Tarhunt/Tarhunz lived on for centuries as the chief god of the Iron Age Luwian states (the Hieroglyphic Luwian inscriptions invoke "Tarhunza of the Vineyard" and "Tarhunza of Heaven"), the durable Anatolian face of the Indo-European sky-storm sovereign.
