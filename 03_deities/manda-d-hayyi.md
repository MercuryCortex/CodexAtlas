# Manda d-Hayyi

"Knowledge of Life" — the Mandaean revealer-figure who descends into the lower worlds bearing gnosis from the World of Light and ascends, guiding souls. The Mandaean religion is named for this figure: *manda*aeans = those of *manda* (gnosis). Manda d-Hayyi is sometimes equivalent to or paired with Hibil-Ziwa (Abel-Splendor), an emanation of Hayyi Rabbi who descends to defeat the demons.
