# Cao Đài — the Supreme Being

**Cao Đài** (高臺, "High Tower" or "Supreme Palace") is the name given to the supreme deity of [[tradition-cao-dai|Cao Dai]], conceived as the one monotheistic God who stands above all religions and whose prior alliances with humanity were expressed through Moses, Buddha, Confucius, Laozi, and Jesus. Cao Dai theology holds that all previous religions are incomplete expressions of the same divine truth, and that the Third Divine Alliance — Cao Dai itself — represents the complete and final revelation. The deity is most commonly represented by the **Divine Eye** (*Thiên Nhãn*) — a single eye in a triangle over a globe, which serves as the principal symbol of the religion and is prominently featured on the main altar of the Tây Ninh Holy See.
