# Hyperion

Hyperion (*Hyperíōn*, "he who goes above / travels on high") is the Titan of **heavenly light** — one of the twelve Titan children of Ouranos and **Gaia**, and the **father of the three great luminaries**: with his sister-wife **[[theia]]** he engenders **[[helios]]** (the Sun), **[[selene]]** (the Moon), and **[[eos-greek]]** (the Dawn). He is light as a generative principle, the source from which the visible lights of the sky descend. Homer often uses "Hyperion" as an epithet of Helios himself (Helios Hyperion, "the Sun on high"), reflecting how closely the Titan of light and his solar son were identified. He represents the older, abstract layer of Greek sky-divinity standing behind the personified Sun, Moon, and Dawn.
