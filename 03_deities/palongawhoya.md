# Palöngawhoya (Younger Twin War God)

Stub — to be expanded. In Hopi cosmogony, Palöngawhoya is the Younger of the Twin War Gods, created (or thought into existence) by [[spider-grandmother-hopi]]. He is sent to the South Pole to keep the earth resonating properly — his function is to ensure the earth remains *vibrating*, alive, in motion. Sound and echo are his domain; wherever his voice is heard as echo in the canyons, that is Palöngawhoya maintaining the world's living resonance.

**Cross-tradition connection**: Palöngawhoya's cosmic function — keeping the world alive through sound and vibration — is the Hopi cognate of the Vedic *nada brahman* concept (cosmic sound as the sustaining creative force; *Om* as the primordial vibration that both creates and maintains the universe). In both traditions, the world is not merely *created* by a primordial sound-act but must be *sustained* by ongoing vibration. This places Hopi cosmogony closer to the Vedic than to the Genesis-1 model of creation-by-word: the sound is not just the initiating act but the continuous sustaining condition of existence.
