# Garuḍa

## Identity
The great eagle-king who is **[[vishnu]]'s vehicle (*vāhana*)** — flying Vishnu across the cosmos at unbearable speed. Son of the sage Kashyapa and Vinata; born to free his mother from servitude to Kadru (mother of the nāgas), Garuda stole the **amrita (soma)** from the gods, defeating Indra in the process; Vishnu, impressed, granted him immortality and made him his mount in return. From this origin Garuda has been the **eternal enemy of the nāgas (serpents)** — the iconographic motif of Garuda grasping/devouring a snake is one of the most-deployed images in South and Southeast Asian sacred art.

## Mythological role
**National symbol** of Thailand (the *Krut* of the royal seal) and Indonesia (*Garuda Pancasila* of the state coat-of-arms and the airline). **Buddhist absorption**: as **Karura** in Japanese Buddhism Garuda becomes one of the eight legions guarding the Dharma. **Vajrayana**: a garuḍa-form *yidam* (deity-yoga form) is taught for clearing obstructions and naga-related illness.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[vishnu]]** — *ancient-identification*: vāhana.
- **Roc / Anqa / Simurgh** — *parallel-motif*: cross-tradition cosmic-bird.
- **The Phoenix / Bennu** — *parallel-motif*.
