# Camazotz

Camazotz (K'iche' *Kame Sotz'*, "Death Bat") is the monstrous **bat-god of the Maya underworld**, Xibalba — a great bat with a blade-like snout who deals death by **decapitation**. He is best known from the **Popol Vuh**, where the **Hero Twins** Hunahpu and Xbalanque, descending to Xibalba to avenge their father, must survive a series of lethal trial-houses; in the **House of Bats** (*Zotzihá*) they shelter inside their blowguns through the night, but when Hunahpu peeks out to see if dawn has come, Camazotz swoops down and **bites off his head**, which the lords of Xibalba hang up as their ballgame ball — until Xbalanque cleverly substitutes a carved squash and restores his brother. The killer-bat motif is ancient and widespread in Mesoamerica: bat imagery with sacrificial and decapitation associations appears among the Zapotec (the "Bat God" urns of Oaxaca) and across the Maya region, marking the bat as a creature of the cave, the night, and the bloody threshold of death.
