# The Three Pure Ones (Sanqing)

## Identity
The supreme triad of Daoist theology, three hypostases of the Dao itself:

1. **Yuqing Yuanshi Tianzun** (元始天尊) — *Celestial Worthy of Primordial Beginning*, eldest, dwelling in the Heaven of Jade Purity (Yuqing).
2. **Shangqing Lingbao Tianzun** (靈寶天尊) — *Celestial Worthy of the Numinous Treasure*, in the Heaven of Upper Purity (Shangqing).
3. **Taiqing Daode Tianzun** (道德天尊) — *Celestial Worthy of the Way and Its Power*, identified with deified Laozi, in the Heaven of Great Purity (Taiqing).

Together they are the cosmogonic source of all revelation: each of the three principal Daoist textual corpora (the *Dongzhen*, *Dongxuan*, and *Dongshen* divisions of the Daozang) is canonically attributed to one of the three.

## Mythological role
The Sanqing represent **graded emanation of the Dao** — from the formless Primordial Beginning (Yuanshi), through the Numinous Treasure (Lingbao) by which the cosmos is structured, to the manifest revealer of the Dao (Daode Tianzun = Laozi as cosmic teacher). The structural triadic-emanation pattern crystallized in the **Six Dynasties period (220–589 CE)** under the *Lingbao* and *Shangqing* school revelations, in conscious response to Buddhist competition.

- **[[laozi]]** — deified third Pure One; the principal Daoist instance of euhemerization-in-reverse (human sage deified as cosmic principle).
