# Sakpata

Sakpata (also **Sagbata**, **Sapata**) is the Fon (Dahomean) **vodun of the earth and of smallpox** — one of the most feared and most carefully propitiated powers of the Vodun pantheon. Conceived as **"the great earth" itself** (and as a whole family of earth-deities under the title *Ainon*, "king/owner of the earth"), Sakpata holds a terrible double power: the **pustules of smallpox** were understood as the "grains" or "seeds" of the earth breaking out on the body, so that the very ground that feeds humankind can also rise up to **punish it with epidemic disease** — and the same deity who sends the pox is the one who can withhold or heal it. His cult was hedged with **secrecy, taboo, and a specialized priesthood**, and his name was often avoided and spoken only in euphemism. Sakpata is the direct counterpart of the Yoruba **[[babalu-aye|Shopona / Babalú-Ayé]]** — essentially the same earth-and-smallpox deity across the neighbouring Fon and Yoruba peoples — and through the Atlantic slave trade he became a major power in **Vodou, Candomblé, and Santería**. In his ambivalent dominion over disease he parallels the South Asian pox-goddesses **[[shitala|Shitala]]** and Mariamman. Sakpata embodies the awful reciprocity between humankind and the earth that both sustains and destroys it.
