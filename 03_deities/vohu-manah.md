# Vohu Manah

## Identity
Vohu Manah (Avestan *Vohu Manah*, "Good Mind / Good Thought"; Pahlavi-Persian *Wahman* or *Bahman*) is **the second Amesha Spenta** and **the first Amesha Spenta Zarathustra addresses in the Gathas** (Yasna 28.1) — the principle of **Good Mind / Right Thought** that grounds the entire Zoroastrian moral-ethical structure. The Zoroastrian moral triad **humata-hukhta-huvarshta** ("good-thoughts, good-words, good-deeds") begins with humata, and humata is the human manifestation of Vohu Manah. Vohu Manah is **the protector of beneficent animals** (especially cattle — a major Indo-Iranian religious-cultic concern) and **the messenger between Ahura Mazda and humans**. The modern Iranian calendar's **eleventh month** (January-February) is *Bahman* — preserving the Amesha Spenta in continuous Iranian cultural identity.

## Earliest attestation
- **Gathas** ([[phase-2-002-gathas-of-zarathustra]]): **Yasna 28.1** — Zarathustra opens his Gathic prayer by addressing Vohu Manah, making Vohu Manah the first divine-being named after Ahura Mazda. Frequent throughout the Gathas.
- **Younger Avesta** ([[phase-2-004-yasna-younger-avesta]]): **Yasht 2** (the Bahman Yasht) — the principal liturgical hymn.
- **Pahlavi tradition**: Bahman continues as central Amesha Spenta + protector of cattle.
- **Modern Iranian calendar**: *Bahman* as eleventh month preserves the Amesha Spenta in continuous Iranian cultural identity.

## Mythological role

### Cosmic-intellect-principle
Vohu Manah is the **cosmic principle of Right Thought** — the principle that grounds all subsequent right-action. The Zoroastrian *humata-hukhta-huvarshta* triad ("good thoughts, good words, good deeds") makes Vohu Manah (right-thought) the foundational ethical principle.

### Protector of cattle
Vohu Manah's specific tutelary domain in Younger Avesta is **the protection of beneficent animals**, especially **cattle**. This reflects the **Indo-Iranian pastoral religious-cultural priority** — cattle are economically central, and their cosmic-religious protection is divine-business. The pre-Zarathustra Iranian religious situation involved **violent cattle-raiding** between settled pastoralists and warrior-nomads; Zarathustra's reform is partly an articulation of the religious-cosmic legitimation of settled pastoral society against the destructive cattle-raiding of the warrior-nomad cult. Vohu Manah's cattle-protection role reflects this.

### Messenger between Ahura Mazda and humans
Vohu Manah comes to Zarathustra in vision (Yasna 28-30) and instructs him; the Gathic Zarathustra's prophetic call is mediated through Vohu Manah.

## Cross-tradition identifications

### Vohu Manah ↔ Hellenistic *Nous*
The structural parallel between Vohu Manah (Good Mind / Cosmic Intellect) and Hellenistic philosophical *Nous* (Intellect / Mind — central to Plato's *Timaeus*, Aristotle's *Metaphysics*, Plotinus's *Enneads* I-VI, the Hermetic *Pimander*) is **real and provocative**. Both are:
- **Personified cosmic intellect** as principle of cosmic order.
- **Mediator between supreme divinity and human-or-cosmic-individuated being**.
- **Source of right-thought** as the foundation of right-action.

**Whether the Hellenistic philosophical Nous has any Persian-influence** is genuinely debated. The Pythagorean-Platonist tradition was in active dialogue with Persian religious-philosophical material from the 6th-c. BCE onward (Pythagoras's reputed Persian-priestly studies; Plato's *Alcibiades I* 122a mentioning Persian *magianism*). Direct genealogical claim is unprovable; the structural parallel is striking.

### Vohu Manah → Christian "Good Mind" / Pauline *nous*
**Pauline use of *nous*** (Romans 7:23-25, 12:2 — "be transformed by the renewing of your *nous*"; 1 Cor 14:14-19; Phil 4:7) develops Hellenistic-Jewish-Christian theology of the renewed/transformed cosmic-and-personal intellect. The Persian-period Jewish wisdom-theology background + Hellenistic philosophical-vocabulary background are both substantially Persian-influenced at multiple removes. The Vohu-Manah → Pauline-nous chain is genealogically unprovable but the structural inheritance through multiple cultural-intermediate steps is real.

### Vohu Manah → Christian "Holy Spirit" / Jewish *ruach*
Some scholars argue Vohu Manah's role as **messenger between Ahura Mazda and humans + indwelling principle of right-thought** has structural-parallel to the Christian Holy Spirit's role. The transmission is unprovable; the structural-thematic parallel is real.

## Iconography
- **No standard ancient anthropomorphic iconography** specifically for Vohu Manah.
- **Indirect iconographic-presence** through cattle-iconography in Zoroastrian religious-cultic art.

## Disputes
- **Hellenistic Nous-influence question.** Whether Hellenistic philosophical Nous has any Persian-Vohu-Manah influence is genuinely contested. Most current scholarship is cautious about direct claims; the structural parallel is uncontested.
- **Vohu Manah → Jewish-Christian transmission scope.** Same general issue as the broader Persian-influence-on-Second-Temple-Judaism question (see [[saoshyant]], [[frashokereti-cosmic-renovation]]).
