# Sól

Sól (Old Norse, "Sun"; Old High German **Sunna**) is the **personified Sun** of Norse and Germanic myth — a goddess who drives the solar chariot across the sky, drawn by the horses Árvakr ("Early-waker") and Alsviðr ("All-swift"), shielded from the burning by the shield **Svalinn**. She and her brother **Máni** (the Moon) were set in the heavens by the gods to measure time. Her defining feature is her doom: she is forever pursued by the wolf **Sköll** (of **[[fenrir]]**'s brood), who at **Ragnarök** finally catches and devours her — though the *Vafþrúðnismál* promises that she will have borne a daughter as fair as herself to ride the sky-road after the world's renewal. Sól continues the **Proto-Indo-European feminine sun-deity** (cognate with Baltic **Saulė**), a striking gender-counterpoint to the masculine sun-gods of Greece, Egypt, and Vedic India. The Old High German *Merseburg Charm* invokes her as Sunna, healing-sister of Sinthgunt.
