# Mawu-Lisa

The Fon / Ewe **moon-and-sun creator-pair** — **Mawu** (feminine, moon, night, west, wisdom) and **Lisa** (masculine, sun, day, east, strength) — joined as a hyphenated single deity-name **Mawu-Lisa** representing the **gendered-cosmic-procreative-pair as ultimate principle**. The pair is the cosmic source from which all the working Vodun deities (Sakpata for disease, Heviosso for thunder, Gun for iron-and-war, the many Tohwiyo lineage-spirits) emanate.

**The three-tier Fon pantheon.** Fon cosmology (Herskovits 1938; Mercier 1954) places three levels in vertical sequence:
1. **Nana Buluku** — the transcendent primordial great-mother / androgynous originator (rarely invoked, beyond the working pantheon)
2. **Mawu-Lisa** — the gendered cosmic-pair, children/emanation of Nana Buluku, source of the working deities
3. **The working Vodun** — the deities operative in human life (Sakpata, Heviosso, Gun, etc.)

This is one of the most-cleanly-articulated cosmogonic-architectures in West African religion — the **emanation from transcendent-androgynous through gendered-pair to operative-multiplicity** is a structural form documented in only a few world religions (Kabbalistic Sefirot emanation from Ein Sof being the most-cited parallel, though the Fon and Kabbalistic traditions are independently arrived).

**The Atlantic transmission.** The Fon pantheon, including the Mawu-Lisa pairing, is the principal source of the New World **Vodou** religion (Haiti) and parallel **Candomblé Jeje** (Brazil). The cosmic-pair structure is preserved with regional adjustment in the diaspora.
