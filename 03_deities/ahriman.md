# Ahriman (Angra Mainyu)

The destructive principle in Zoroastrian theology — the cosmic adversary of [[ahura-mazda]]. In the Gathas (Yasna 30), Zarathustra describes a primordial moment when two twin spirits (Spenta Mainyu, the beneficent spirit, and Angra Mainyu, the destructive spirit) made opposite choices — one chose Asha (truth/order), the other chose Druj (the Lie/destruction). All goodness in the world flows from Spenta Mainyu; all evil, disease, death, and destruction flow from Angra Mainyu (later personified as Ahriman in the Pahlavi texts of Sasanian Zoroastrianism). The cosmic conflict between them will be resolved at the Frashkard (the renovation of the world), when Ahriman is finally defeated. Until then, humans must choose: maintain Asha through righteous thought, word, and deed, or fall into Druj.

For the cross-tradition comparison with Haudenosaunee Flint (Evil Mind twin), see [[tradition-haudenosaunee]]. For the broader cosmic-dualism pattern, see [[ethical-dualism]] and [[tradition-zoroastrianism]].
