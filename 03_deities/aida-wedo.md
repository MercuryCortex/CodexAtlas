# Aida-Wedo

## Identity
Rainbow-serpent lwa of Haitian Vodou — the feminine cosmic-serpent paired with [[damballa]] as the canonical Vodou divine-pair. Where Damballa is the male sky-serpent of primordial creation, Aida-Wedo is the female rainbow that joins sky to water and binds the cosmos in living color. The pair is direct transmission from Fon-Dahomey [[dan-aida-hwedo]] / Aido-Hwedo, the rainbow-serpent who supports the cosmos in West-African Vodun theology (Mercier 1954).

## Earliest attestation
Fon-Dahomey oral tradition (pre-Atlantic); transmitted via Saint-Domingue slave trade; documented in Haitian Vodou ethnography from the 19th c. onward; Deren (1953) and Métraux (1959) are the standard mid-20th-c references.

## Mythological role
Aida-Wedo is the rainbow seen after rain — and simultaneously the cosmic-arc that holds creation together. In paired worship with Damballa, the two snakes are imagined intertwined: Damballa the white serpent, Aida-Wedo the rainbow. The ceremonial protocols for the pair are joint — offerings of egg, white flour (Damballa) plus rainbow-colored cloth (Aida-Wedo); a bowl of clean water at the foot of the *poto-mitan* (center-post of the peristyle) receives both.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[dan-aida-hwedo]]** (Fon-Dahomey) — direct-borrowing; the Vodou pair preserves the Dahomey rainbow-serpent intact.
- **[[damballa]]** (Vodou consort) — paired as the male-female cosmic snake.
- **Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception** — folk-syncretism with Catholic iconography (Métraux 1959).
- **Quetzalcoatl** — parallel-form rainbow/feathered-serpent cosmic figure (no transmission).

## MASSIVE WIN — Damballa-Aida-Wedo as Atlantic transmission anchor
The Damballa-Aida-Wedo pair is one of the cleanest documented transmissions of the Atlantic slave trade per Mercier (1954) and Métraux (1959). The Fon-Dahomey Aido-Hwedo (the rainbow-serpent supporting the cosmos and carrying Mawu-Lisa across the sky) crosses to Saint-Domingue and becomes the Vodou Damballa-Aida-Wedo pair, preserving:
- The rainbow-iconography
- The cosmic-support role
- The male-female snake-pairing
- The ceremonial protocols (egg, water, white cloth)

The transmission is documented at the level of liturgical text, ritual protocol, and theological structure. The Vodou pair is not merely "inspired by" the Dahomey pair — it IS the Dahomey pair under continued cult-veneration. This is among the cleanest documented direct-borrowing chains in Atlantic religious history.

The pair also stands at one node of the broader [[theme-rainbow-serpent-cross-tradition]] archetype — a structural pattern that ALSO appears (parallel-form, no transmission) in Mesoamerican [[quetzalcoatl]] and Australian Aboriginal Rainbow Serpent. Damballa-Aida-Wedo is the Atlantic-Vodou instantiation of that universal pattern.

## Iconography
Rainbow imagery (often painted on Vodou *poto-mitan* center-posts and on flags); snake-form intertwined with Damballa's snake; white cloth + rainbow-colored cloth; eggs (the snake's gift); water bowl.
