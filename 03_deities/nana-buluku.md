# Nana Buluku

The Fon / Ewe **primordial great-mother** — the apophatic-transcendent origin at the top of the Vodun cosmogonic architecture. Nana Buluku is the **oldest deity**, the unmanifest origin from which [[mawu-lisa]] (the gendered moon-sun cosmic-pair) emerged as her children/emanation, and from whom the entire working Vodun pantheon (Sakpata, Heviosso, Gun, the Tohwiyo lineage-spirits) ultimately descends.

**MASSIVE WIN: West African apophatic-transcendent primordial.** Nana Buluku is one of the clearest African instances of the **apophatic-unmanifest-primordial-being** theological category — structurally parallel to Kabbalistic [[ein-sof]], Zuni [[awonawilona]], Mahayana Dharmakaya, and Hindu Advaita Brahman-without-attributes. Each of these is the **unmanifest prior to manifestation**, the origin from which operative deities emerge via emanation, self-thought, or self-contraction. Their independent emergence on four continents (West Africa, Mediterranean Kabbalah, Pueblo Southwest, Mahayana East Asia, Vedantic India) is among the strongest cases in comparative religion of **convergent emergence of a deep cognitive-religious-structural form**.

**The Atlantic diaspora.** Nana Buluku passed into the New World African religions with the Atlantic slave trade. In **Brazilian Candomblé** she is **Nanã** (or Nanã Burukê) — the oldest orixá, deity of swamps and ancestor-receiving; in **Haitian Vodou** she becomes **Nanan-bouclou**; she is variously received as a senior matriarch-figure across the Afro-Atlantic religious continuum. The continuity of her apophatic-transcendent role from West Africa to the New World is one of the most-traceable Atlantic-religious transmissions.
