# Shani

Shani (Sanskrit *Śani*) is the Hindu deity of the planet **Saturn** and the most propitiated — and most feared — of the **Navagraha**, the nine planetary deities. The son of the sun-god **[[surya]]** (by his shadow-wife Chhaya) and the elder brother of **[[yama-vedic|Yama]]**, Shani is the cosmic dispenser of **karmic justice**: slow-moving (*Shanaishchara*), exacting, and impartial, he rewards or punishes each soul strictly according to its deeds. His "gaze" (the *sade-sati*, the seven-and-a-half-year Saturn transit) is dreaded as a period of hardship and discipline, and an enormous popular ritual industry — black-clad Saturdays, sesame-oil offerings, the iconic open-air shrine of **Shani Shingnapur** — exists to appease him. Yet his severity is corrective rather than malicious: he is the teacher whose trials burn off karma. The Navagraha as a set are absent from the vault; Shani, as the most-worshipped, anchors them.
