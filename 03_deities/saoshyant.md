# Saoshyant

## Identity
The Saoshyant (Avestan *Saošyant*, "one who will bring benefit" — etymologically related to the Avestan root *sav-* "to benefit, to save," ultimately the same root as Sanskrit *śu-* "to swell, to grow") is the **future cosmic savior of Zoroastrianism** — a figure who, at the end of the present cosmic age, will be **born of a virgin impregnated by Zarathustra's miraculously-preserved seed** (preserved in Lake Kąsaoiia in eastern Iran), will **raise the dead**, will **defeat [[angra-mainyu-ahriman]]** in final cosmic combat, and will **inaugurate the frashokereti** — the renovation of the cosmos, the universal resurrection, the abolition of evil, and the inauguration of eternal blessedness. The figure is **the principal pre-Christian Near Eastern future-messiah-figure** and provides the most-direct cross-tradition theological parallel to Christian Christology. Boyce 1975-91 and Hultgård 1998 argue substantial influence on Second-Temple Jewish apocalyptic-messianism via the Persian-period Jewish-Persian contact.

The Avestan tradition specifies **three successive Saoshyants** in 1000-year cycles after Zarathustra: **Ushedar** (1000 years after Zarathustra), **Ushedar-mah** (2000 years), and the final **Astvat-ereta** = "embodying righteousness" (3000 years; the climactic-and-true Saoshyant who completes the cosmic renovation). The threefold-Saoshyant scheme is Pahlavi-period elaboration; the Gathic Avestan stratum has a more diffuse savior-figure.

## Earliest attestation
- **Gathas** ([[phase-2-002-gathas-of-zarathustra]], ~-1200 to -1000): Yasna 34.13, 45.11, 48.9, 53.2 — the Saoshyant appears in plural "saoshyants" (saviors/benefactors) including Zarathustra himself and his followers. The figure is **not yet the singular eschatological savior** in the Gathic stratum.
- **Younger Avesta** ([[phase-2-004-yasna-younger-avesta]], ~-700 to -300): the Saoshyant emerges as a more singular eschatological figure; **Yasht 13.62, 13.142, 19.88-89** treat Saoshyant as the final renovator-of-the-cosmos.
- **Pahlavi-period elaboration** (~+300 to +900): the three-Saoshyant scheme + the virgin-birth narrative crystallize in texts like the **Bundahishn** (the principal Pahlavi cosmological work), the **Denkard**, and the **Zand i Wahman Yasn** apocalypse (Cereti 1995).

## Mythological role

### The eschatological scenario
At the end of the present cosmic age, after the universal turmoil and apocalyptic battles between the forces of [[ahura-mazda]] and [[angra-mainyu-ahriman]]:

1. **A virgin bathing in Lake Kąsaoiia** is impregnated by Zarathustra's preserved seed (preserved by 99,999 *fravashis*).
2. The Saoshyant is **born of this virgin** and grows into divinely-endowed savior-status.
3. The Saoshyant **raises the dead** for the universal judgment. Bodies are reconstituted from their elements.
4. The dead pass through a **river of molten metal** — to the righteous it feels like warm milk; to the wicked it burns away their evil but leaves them purified for resurrection.
5. The Saoshyant **defeats Angra Mainyu** in final cosmic combat.
6. **Frashokereti** is achieved — the cosmos is renovated, evil is abolished, all creatures dwell in eternal righteousness with Ahura Mazda.

## Cross-tradition identifications

### THE MASSIVE-WIN: Saoshyant → Christian Messiah / Second-Coming Christology

The Saoshyant provides **the principal pre-Christian Near Eastern future-savior figure** whose structural elements directly anticipate Christian Christology:

1. **Virgin birth** — Saoshyant born of a virgin (Pahlavi tradition); Christ born of the Virgin Mary. The structural parallel is uncontested; the question of direct genealogical transmission is debated (Boyce 1991 vs. Yamauchi 1990).
2. **Future-coming savior** — Saoshyant as the not-yet-arrived eschatological figure; Christ as the second-coming-eschatological figure. The Christian Parousia / Second Coming theology has direct structural parallel.
3. **Raising of the dead** — Saoshyant raises the dead at the end of time; Christ at the Last Judgment.
4. **Final cosmic combat** — Saoshyant defeats Angra Mainyu; Christ defeats Satan/Antichrist (Revelation 19–20).
5. **Universal renovation / new heavens and earth** — Frashokereti; Revelation 21:1 "*καινὸν οὐρανὸν καὶ γῆν καινήν*" (new heavens and new earth).
6. **Purifying ordeal by fire/metal** — Zoroastrian molten-metal-river; Christian purgatorial fire, the river of fire in some apocalyptic texts (Daniel 7:10 has the river of fire issuing from the throne).

### The transmission window
The **Achaemenid period (-550 to -330)** is the period of MAXIMUM Zoroastrian-Jewish religious contact: the Jewish exiles return from Babylon to Yehud under Persian patronage (Cyrus's edict, -539), live under Persian-Zoroastrian imperial-religious culture for two centuries, and develop the **distinctively-Second-Temple theological innovations** (cosmic dualism, angelology and demonology, resurrection-of-the-dead, future-messiah-figure, final judgment) that distinguish Second-Temple Judaism from First-Temple. Boyce 1991 and Hultgård 1998 treat these developments as substantially Persian-influenced; conservative-evangelical scholarship (Yamauchi 1990) resists the influence-claim.

The scholarly debate is real but **the structural parallels are uncontested**. The Codex Atlas's "MASSIVE-win" mission is exactly to surface these comparative-structural patterns regardless of where one stands on the direction-of-influence question.

### Other cross-tradition parallels
- **Vedic Kalki avatar** (the future Vishnu-avatar who will end the present Kali Yuga) — *parallel-form*. Indo-Iranian shared future-savior religious-imaginative pattern.
- **Mahdi** (Islamic eschatological savior) — *appropriated-by* / *scholarly-parallel*. Islamic Mahdi-doctrine in some streams shows substantial Zoroastrian-and-Christian influence on its eschatological structure.
- **Maitreya** (Buddhist future-Buddha) — *parallel-form*. Convergent future-savior pattern; some scholars argue Zoroastrian influence on Mahayana Maitreya-doctrine via the Iranian-Buddhist Kushan-period contact zone.

## Iconography
- **No standard ancient iconography** — Saoshyant is the not-yet-arrived savior; he is not depicted in surviving ancient Iranian art.
- **Modern Parsi-Iranian devotional art** sometimes depicts Saoshyant as warrior-figure with the *vazra* (divine club) and a banner; the iconography is post-classical and varies.

## Disputes
- **Zoroastrian → Jewish → Christian transmission.** The scholarly debate (Boyce 1991 yes-substantial; Yamauchi 1990 no-or-minimal) is real and continues. Most current scholarship accepts **substantial influence** on Second-Temple Jewish apocalyptic-messianic theology while remaining cautious about specific genealogical claims.
- **Dating of the three-Saoshyant scheme.** The threefold-savior scheme is Pahlavi-period (~+500 onward); the singular-Saoshyant figure is older (Younger Avesta, ~-700 to -300); the Gathic figure (~-1200) is more diffuse.
- **Virgin-birth narrative.** The lake-Kąsaoiia virgin-birth story is Pahlavi-period elaboration. Whether the Gathic-Avestan stratum already implied a virgin birth or whether this is later mythological development is debated.
