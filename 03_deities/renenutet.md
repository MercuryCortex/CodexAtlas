# Renenutet

Renenutet ("the nourishing snake") is the Egyptian **cobra-goddess of the harvest, nourishment, and fate**. Her name derives from the verb *rnn* ("to nurse, suckle, rear"), and she governs the **granary and the harvest** — shrines to her stood at threshing-floors and wine-presses, and the harvest month was sacred to her. As the goddess who **suckles and rears** she nourishes both infants and the king (her milk confers vitality), and by extension she assigns **fate and the true name** given to a child at birth (a role linking her to *Shai*, destiny — the two often paired). As a rearing cobra she is also fiercely protective: her gaze, like the uraeus, strikes down enemies of the king and the dead. In the Greco-Roman Fayum, as **Thermouthis**, she merged increasingly with **[[isis-egyptian|Isis]]** in serpent form. She binds together the three things a society most depends on — food, the nurture of the young, and the destiny that is fixed at birth.
