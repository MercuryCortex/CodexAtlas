# Reue (Lusitanian Thunder-God)

## Identity
Reue is the thunder/sky deity of the Lusitanian pantheon — attested in approximately 10 Latin votive inscriptions from the eastern Lusitanian highlands (Beira Alta, modern central Portugal). Like [[bandua]], Reue appears with multiple local epithets (*Reue Marandigui*, *Reue Vakaleo*, *Reue Paramaeco*) indicating geographic or tribal specification, but unlike Bandua (which may be a deity-type rather than a single deity), Reue's inscriptions cluster more tightly geographically, suggesting a specific regional cult rather than a pan-Lusitanian generic.

## Etymology: The PIE Thunderer Root
Prósper (2002) proposes the etymology PIE *\*reu-* — "to roar, to thunder" — as the root of *Reue*. If correct:
- **Vedic**: *Parjanya* (rain/thunder deity) derives from *\*per-gʷʰ-* (an alternate PIE weather root), but the *\*reu-* root appears in the wider Vedic *ṛ́śvan* family
- **Slavic**: *Perun* (pan-Slavic thunderer; "Oak of Thunder") has a different etymology (*per-* = to strike) but the same functional role
- **Lithuanian**: *Perkūnas* (Baltic thunderer; cognate with *per-* strike root) — attested as the pre-Christian lightning/oak deity
- **Germanic**: *Þórr* (Norse Thor; from *\*þunraz* — "thunder") — different root but same function
- **Gaulish**: *Taranis* (from *\*taran-* = thunder; Celtic cognate of *\*reu-* zone)

Whether or not the etymology is correct (it is contested), what is beyond dispute is that **Reue occupies the functional slot of the PIE thunderer** in the Lusitanian pantheon — the atmospheric deity of the upper world, associated with rain, agricultural abundance, and the sovereign power of the sky. Every attested IE tradition has this figure; Lusitanian was the westernmost IE tradition, and Reue is its western terminus of the PIE thunderer type.

## MASSIVE WIN — The PIE Thunderer at the Atlantic Edge

The PIE thunderer archetype extends from India to the Atlantic coast of Iberia:

| Tradition | Name | Meaning / Root |
|-----------|------|---------------|
| Vedic (India) | **Indra** / **Parjanya** | "The Thousand-Eyed" / rain-deity |
| Greek | **Zeus** | PIE *\*dyeu-* (sky, to shine) |
| Roman | **Jupiter** | *\*Dyeus Pater* (Sky Father) |
| Celtic (Gaulish) | **Taranis** | *\*taran-* (thunder) |
| Celtic (Irish) | **The Dagda** (club/thunder function) | The Good God |
| Norse (Germanic) | **Þórr / Thor** | *\*þunraz* (thunder) |
| Baltic | **Perkūnas** | *\*per-* (to strike) |
| Slavic | **Perun** | *\*per-* (to strike) |
| Hittite | **Teshub** | Non-IE cognate; parallel type |
| Lusitanian (Iberia) | **Reue** | PIE *\*reu-* (to roar/thunder) |

This is the most geographically complete attestation of a single PIE theological construct in the vault. The thunderer — the sky deity who strikes with lightning, commands rain, and holds sovereign power over the upper world — appears in every attested IE tradition from the Ganges to the Douro. Reue is the westernmost attestation of a deity-type that goes back 5,000+ years to the Proto-Indo-European homeland.

The **oak connection** is consistent across multiple traditions (Zeus's oracle oak at Dodona; Thor's oak Donar tree, felled by Saint Boniface in 723 CE; Perkūnas's sacred oaks in Lithuania; the Druids' sacred oak) — suggesting that the PIE thunderer's preferred sacred tree was the oak (the tree most likely to be struck by lightning in European forests), and this tree-deity association persisted independently across the full range of IE dispersion.

## Reue and the Lusitanian Functional Theology
In the Lusitanian pantheon as reconstructable from epigraphy:
- **Reue** = sky, thunder, upper world (the domain above)
- **[[bandua]]** = territorial protection, community binding (the domain of social life on earth)
- **[[nabia]]** = water, rivers, fertility (the domain of the living land surface)
- **[[ataecina]]** = underworld, death, regeneration (the domain below)
- **[[endovelicus]]** = oracular communication, healing (the domain between worlds)

This is a complete **vertical cosmological map** of Lusitanian sacred space: sky → social life → living earth → underworld, with a mediating oracle figure. The vertical structure is consistent with the [[world-axis]] theme: the sacred pole connects the upper (Reue), middle (Bandua-Nabia), and lower (Ataecina) realms, with Endovelicus as the figure who can move between all three.

The structural parallel to Indo-European trifunctional theology (Dumézil's framework: sovereignty-warriors-producers) is visible but not exact: Reue (sovereignty/sky) + Bandua (martial protection) + Nabia (fertility/abundance) maps onto the Dumézilian first/second/third functions, with Ataecina and Endovelicus as additions that don't map cleanly onto the schema.
