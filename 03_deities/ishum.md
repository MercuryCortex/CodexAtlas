# Ishum

Ishum (*Išum*; Sumerian **Hendursanga**) is the Akkadian **benign fire-god and divine herald** — specifically the **night-watchman**, the guiding lamp who walks the dark streets and protects those abroad at night. His great literary role is in the **[[erra-akkadian|Erra Epic]]**, where he is the **restraining counselor**: as the war-and-plague god Erra works himself into a frenzy and unleashes slaughter on Babylon, Ishum is the loyal vizier who repeatedly **counsels restraint**, voices the cost of the carnage, and in the end **talks Erra down**, persuading him to relent and even bless the survivors. Ishum is thus the personified voice of measured counsel against destructive rage — the cool flame beside the consuming fire. He belongs with **[[nuska]]** and Gibil among the gods of fire turned to benevolent, ordering ends.
