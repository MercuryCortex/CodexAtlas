# Ein Sof (The Infinite)

## Identity
*Ein Sof* — literally "without end" / "the Infinite" — is the Kabbalistic name for God-in-himself, beyond and prior to all manifestation, beyond and prior to the ten *sefirot*. The term emerges in late 12th- to 13th-century Provençal and Spanish Kabbalah, where it names the absolute apophatic transcendence: that which cannot be predicated, named, or thought, and that which is the unmanifest source from which the sefirot emanate. *Ein Sof* is sometimes paired with even further-apophatic designations (*Ayin* — Nothingness; *Or Ein Sof* — Infinite Light), and the relation between *Ein Sof* and the highest sefirah *Keter* is variously construed across Kabbalistic schools — some treat *Keter* as almost identical with *Ein Sof*, others as the first emanation from it. The doctrine permits Kabbalah's bold inner-divine descriptive language (about sefirotic dynamics, divine sacred marriages) without compromising God's absolute transcendence: all such descriptions apply to the manifest emanations, never to *Ein Sof* itself.

## Earliest attestation
The term first appears in 12th-c. Provençal Kabbalist writings (Isaac the Blind's circle, ~1200), is implicit in [[phase-5-026-sefer-ha-bahir]], and becomes central in [[phase-5-027-sefer-ha-zohar]] (~1280). The fully developed doctrine — including *Ein Sof*'s self-contraction (*tzimtzum*) — is later, in Lurianic Kabbalah (Phase 6, ~1570).

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[the-godhead-eckhart]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: structurally parallel apophatic Godhead-beyond-God. McGinn 2001.
- **[[al-haqq]]** (Ibn ʿArabī's "Real") — *scholarly-parallel*: both name the Real-in-itself beyond manifestation, structurally parallel apophatic moves. Sells 1994.
- Plotinian One — *scholarly-parallel*: the apophatic source-from-which-emanations-proceed is structurally Plotinian.

## Disputes
- **Relationship to Keter.** Different Kabbalistic schools place *Ein Sof* outside the sefirotic system (most Castilian Kabbalists) or partially within it (some Geronese views).
- ***Tzimtzum* doctrine.** Whether *Ein Sof* literally self-contracts to make space for creation (Luria) or this is metaphorical (Cordovero) — a Phase 6 debate.
