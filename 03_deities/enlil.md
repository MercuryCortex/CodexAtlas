# Enlil

Lord of the wind/breath and the executive ruler of the Sumerian pantheon. From the mid-third millennium onward, Enlil — not the more distant Anu — was the active "king of the gods" who decreed destinies. Cult center: Nippur (the Ekur temple), the religious capital of Sumer. Enlil was overtaken in Babylonian theology by [[marduk]] (whose elevation Enuma Elish narrates), but remained central in Assyrian and northern Mesopotamian worship.
