# Ratnasaṃbhava

## Identity
The **Southern Buddha** of the Five Dhyani Buddha mandala. *Ratnasaṃbhava* — "Jewel-Born" — embodies the **Wisdom of Equality** (*samatā-jñāna*), the recognition that all beings are equally fundamental at the level of buddha-nature, the antidote to the affective poison of pride. He bestows the wish-fulfilling jewel (*cintāmaṇi*) — the iconographic source of the universally-available compassionate provision that Buddhist devotional practice addresses.

- **Five Dhyani Buddhas**: with **[[vairocana]]** (center), **[[akshobhya]]** (east), **[[amitabha]]** (west), **[[amoghasiddhi]]** (north).
