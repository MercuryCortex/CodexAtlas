# Atlas

## Identity
Titan son of [[iapetus]], brother of [[prometheus]] and Epimetheus. After the Titanomachy he is **condemned by Zeus to bear the heavens on his shoulders** (Hesiod *Theogony* 517–520) at the far western edge of the world — the position the Greeks identified with the great mountain range across the strait from Iberia, hence the name *Atlas Mountains* (NW Africa). His daughters: the seven Pleiades, the Hyades, and in some recensions the Hesperides whose golden apples Heracles wins for his eleventh labor. **Plato's *Timaeus-Critias***: the first king of **[[phase-3-022-plato-timaeus-critias-atlantis|Atlantis]]** is named Atlas, son of Poseidon and Cleito (not the Titan but eponymous of him), making Atlantis = "land of Atlas."
