# Òrúnmìlà

## Identity
**The orisha of wisdom and divination** — and the deity *of* Ifá, the entire Yoruba divinatory system already in the vault as [[phase-8-007-ifa-divination-corpus|Phase 8-007]]. Orunmila was present at the creation when Olodumare distributed destinies — *Eleri-Ipin* "Witness of Destiny" — and is therefore the only being who knows the destiny that each soul (*ori*) chose for itself before birth. His function in Yoruba theology is precisely to **recover that lost foreknowledge** for living humans, through the divination system.

## Mythological role
**Ifá**: the divination is performed by a **babalawo** ("father of secrets") who casts the **opele** chain or the **sixteen ikin** sacred palm-nuts; the pattern that results is one of the **256 *odu*** (figures), each of which is associated with a corpus of memorized verses (*ese*) that the babalawo recites — the diviner-as-encyclopedic-poet who applies the relevant verses to the client's question. **Each odu can produce up to 800 *ese***, making the total Ifá corpus one of the largest oral textual corpora in any tradition (Abimbola 1976).

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[thoth]] / [[hermes-trismegistus]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: patron of writing/knowledge/divination/destiny.
- **Saint Francis of Assisi** — *folk-syncretism*: Cuban Lucumí equation.
