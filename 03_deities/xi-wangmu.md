# Queen Mother of the West (Xiwangmu)

## Identity
The most enduring independent female deity of Chinese religion. In the archaic *Classic of Mountains and Seas* (~-300 BCE) Xiwangmu is a hybrid being — leopard tail, tiger fangs, dishevelled hair — dwelling on Mount Kunlun and presiding over plague and the celestial punishments. By the Han dynasty (2nd c. BCE – 2nd c. CE) she has been re-imagined as a regal courtly sovereign distributing the **peaches of immortality** that ripen every 3,000 years; mass spontaneous millenarian pilgrimage-cults addressed to her in 3 BCE are recorded in the *Hanshu*. By the Tang she is fully integrated into Daoist celestial theology as the supreme female immortal.

## Mythological role
**Bestower of immortality**: hosts the **Banquet of Peaches** to which all immortals are invited (the Monkey King Sun Wukong steals from this in *Journey to the West*). **Patron of women's salvation**: Tang-Song women-specific Daoist cults addressed petitions directly to her, bypassing the male celestial bureaucracy. **Sovereign of the Western Paradise on Mount Kunlun** — a feminine cosmic counterweight to the male emperor-of-Heaven.
