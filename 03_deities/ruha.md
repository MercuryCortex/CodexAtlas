# Ruha

The Mandaean Ruha is a complex feminine figure — etymologically "Spirit" (cognate with Hebrew/Aramaic *ruach*, "spirit" — and with the *Ruach* of Genesis 1:2 that hovers over the waters). In Mandaean texts she is often presented negatively: spirit of seduction, mother of the planets and zodiacal powers, opposed to the redemptive descent of Manda d-Hayyi. The Mandaean inversion of *Ruha qudsha* — what for Jewish and Christian traditions is the Holy Spirit — is one of the most striking polemical re-codings in Mandaean theology. Buckley 2002 cautions against treating Ruha as a one-dimensional negative figure; she is genuinely ambivalent across the Mandaean corpus.
