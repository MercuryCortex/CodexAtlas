# Akṣobhya

## Identity
The **Eastern Buddha** of the Five Dhyani Buddha mandala. *Akṣobhya* means "Imperturbable" — his vow when he first set out toward Buddhahood was that he would never give in to anger toward any sentient being. He presides over the Eastern Pure Land of **Abhirati** ("Delight"), the destination of the Vimalakīrti-nirdeśa Sūtra's protagonist at the sutra's close. Tantric Buddhism associates him with the **Mirror-like Wisdom** (*ādarśa-jñāna*) — wisdom that reflects all phenomena without judgment or distortion, the antidote to the affective poison of anger/aggression.
