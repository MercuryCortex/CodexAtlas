# Maponos

Maponos (Latinized **Maponus**) is the Celtic god of **youth, music, and poetry** — his very name meaning "the Great Son" or "the Divine Youth" (from Celtic *maqwos*, "son / boy"). He is attested chiefly in **northern Roman Britain**, around Hadrian's Wall, and in Gaul, where dedications equate him with **[[apollo|Apollo]]** as **Apollo Maponus** — a fitting *interpretatio*, since it aligns the eternally young Celtic god with Apollo the youthful lyre-player (*Citharoedus*), patron of music and poetry. Maponos is the continental and British face of a deity well known from later Welsh tradition as **Mabon ap Modron** ("Son, son of Mother"), the wondrous youth who in the tale of *Culhwch and Olwen* was stolen from his mother three nights after birth and must be sought and freed — and he belongs to the wider Celtic complex of the **divine youthful son** that also includes the Irish Mac ind Óc (Aengus). Maponos embodies the Celtic sanctification of youth, beauty, and the music and poetry that are youth's gifts.
