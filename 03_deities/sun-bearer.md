# Sun Bearer (Jóhonaa'éí)

The Sun in Navajo religion — not an impersonal astronomical body but a divine being who carries the sun across the sky each day and returns to his home in the east at night. Jóhonaa'éí ("The One Who Carries the Sun") is the father of the Hero Twins (Monster Slayer and Born-for-Water) by [[changing-woman]]. The Hero Twins journey to his house to receive weapons and power to slay the monsters (*naayéé'*) plaguing the world. Sun Bearer tests them severely before acknowledging them as his sons. He is a morally complex figure — powerful and productive but also demanding; his relationship with Changing Woman is marked by her refusal to live with him in the east (he lives in the east; she prefers the west coast, where she can watch her children).
