# Ameretat

Sixth of the six Amesha Spentas of Zoroastrian theology — "Immortality" personified. Paired with [[haurvatat]] ("Wholeness") in the Avestan formulae; together they govern the eschatological reward of unending healthful life. Tutelary divinity of plants. The Pahlavi name *Amurdad* survives as Modern Persian *Mordad*, the fifth month of the Iranian solar calendar.
