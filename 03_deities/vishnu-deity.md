# Viṣṇu

## Identity
Viṣṇu is the cosmic preserver of the Hindu *Trimūrti* (Brahmā creator, Viṣṇu preserver, Śiva destroyer) and the supreme deity of Vaiṣṇava Hindu traditions. He is the *avatāric* god par excellence — descending into the world in age after age, in various forms (the standard list: Matsya/Fish, Kūrma/Tortoise, Varāha/Boar, Narasiṃha/Man-lion, Vāmana/Dwarf, Paraśurāma, Rāma, Kṛṣṇa, Buddha, Kalki) — "to restore *dharma*" (*Gītā* 4:7–8). Iconographically: dark-blue or dark-skinned, four-armed, holding *śaṅkha* (conch), *cakra* (discus), *gadā* (mace), and *padma* (lotus); cosmically reclining on the great serpent *Ananta-Śeṣa* in the milk-ocean (*Kṣīra Sāgara*) between cycles of creation, his consort *Lakṣmī* massaging his feet; from his navel grows a lotus on which sits Brahmā the creator-deity, who then makes a new cosmos.

In Vedic religion ([[phase-2-001-rig-veda-family-books]], ~ -1200 to -1000) Viṣṇu is a relatively minor solar deity remembered for his "three strides" (*trivikrama*) by which he measured out the universe. By the later Vedic and epic periods Viṣṇu absorbs the cult of the cosmic *Nārāyaṇa* (cosmic Man / cosmic waters), the Yādava-Vrishni hero-deity *Vāsudeva-Kṛṣṇa*, the *Gopāla* cowherd-deity of the south, and the warrior-king *Rāma* of the *Rāmāyaṇa* — by ~+500 the *avatāra* scheme integrates these formerly distinct cults under Viṣṇu's name. Pāñcarātra and Bhāgavata theological literature systematizes the *avatāra* doctrine.

## Earliest attestation
*Rig Veda* (~-1200 to -1000) — minor solar deity. The cosmic-supreme Viṣṇu develops in the late Vedic period and is fully constituted by the time of the Sanskrit *Mahābhārata* and Viṣṇu Purāṇa.

## Manifestations in vault documents
- **Vedic seed:** [[phase-2-001-rig-veda-family-books]] — the three-strides Viṣṇu, minor solar deity.
- **Epic Vaiṣṇavism:** [[phase-2-017-mahabharata-ramayana-oral-layers]] — Kṛṣṇa-as-Viṣṇu in the *Bhagavad Gītā*; Rāma-as-Viṣṇu in the *Rāmāyaṇa*.
- **Theological systematization:** [[phase-5-016-ramanuja-sribhasya]] — Rāmānuja's *Śrī Vaiṣṇava* theology: Viṣṇu-Nārāyaṇa with consort Śrī as the personal supreme. [[phase-5-022-madhva-brahma-sutra-bhasya]] — Madhva's *Dvaita* theology: Viṣṇu as the supreme Lord absolutely distinct from finite souls.
