# Marduk

Originally a minor local Babylonian god, Marduk's rise to "king of the gods" tracks Babylon's political ascendancy under Hammurabi (~1750 BCE) and especially under Nebuchadnezzar I (~1100 BCE), when Enuma Elish was likely composed or finalized. In [[phase-1-008-enuma-elish]] Marduk defeats Tiamat in single combat, splits her body to make heaven and earth, and is granted "fifty names" by the assembly of gods — a theological move that absorbs other gods' functions into his.

Cult center: Babylon, the Esagila temple with the Etemenanki ziggurat (the historical "tower of Babel" referent). Marduk's name in the Hebrew Bible: "Merodach" (Jer 50:2).
