# Izanagi-no-Mikoto

## Identity
Primordial creator-god of Shinto; with his sister-consort [[izanami]] he stirs the primal brine with the Heavenly Spear, the drops congealing to form the islands of Japan (Onogoro). The two then perform the world's first marriage-ritual on Onogoro and birth the islands, then the *kami*. After Izanami's death in childbirth (Kagutsuchi the fire-god), Izanagi descends to **Yomi-no-kuni** (the land of the dead) to retrieve her — a katabasis with structural parallels to Orpheus/Eurydice and Inanna/Dumuzi. The taboo-violation (looking at her decaying body) drives him out; the post-Yomi purification *misogi* gives birth to [[amaterasu]], [[tsukuyomi]], and [[susanoo]].

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[izanami]]** — primal sister-consort.
- **[[ranginui]] + [[papatuanuku]]** — *parallel-motif*: Sky-Father / Earth-Mother creation-couple in Polynesian myth.
- **[[apsu]] + [[tiamat]]** — *parallel-motif*: primordial creator-couple in Mesopotamian Enuma Elish.
- **Orpheus → Eurydice katabasis** — *parallel-motif*: failed retrieval of dead spouse from the underworld.
