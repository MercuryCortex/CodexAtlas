# Lugh

## Identity
The **multi-skilled high-king** of the Tuatha Dé Danann — *Samildánach*, "skilled in all arts together." In the famous **Tuatha Dé doorman scene** at Tara (Cath Maige Tuired), Lugh is refused entry because each art he names is already represented in the court; he challenges them: "but is there any one man among you who is master of *all* these arts at once?" — gaining admission and ultimately the kingship. Wins the Second Battle of Mag Tuired against the Fomorians by slaying his grandfather Balor of the Evil Eye with a sling-stone through his single fatal eye.

## Mythological role
**Lughnasadh**: his feast on August 1 marks the start of harvest — one of the four great Celtic quarter-festivals (Imbolc, Beltane, Lughnasadh, Samhain). **Pan-Celtic**: the Continental Lugus, Welsh Lleu Llaw Gyffes, and Irish Lugh are linguistic and functional cognates — likely the most widely-worshipped Celtic deity. Caesar (*Bellum Gallicum* 6.17) identifies the Gauls' chief god — i.e., Lugus — with **Mercury**, making Lugh the principal Celtic deity in Roman ethnographic documentation.
