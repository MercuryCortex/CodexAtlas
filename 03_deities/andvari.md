# Andvari

Andvari is the Norse **dwarf** whose **cursed gold** sets in motion the great tragedy of the Völsungs. He dwells as a **pike in a waterfall**, guarding an immense hoard. When **[[loki]]**, needing to pay the blood-ransom for killing the otter-shaped Otr, catches Andvari and strips him of all his treasure, the dwarf tries to keep back a single golden ring — **Andvaranaut**, which can breed more gold — but Loki takes that too. In revenge, Andvari lays a **curse**: the ring and the gold will bring **death to every owner**. That curse then drives the whole Sigurd/Nibelung cycle — the slaying of Fáfnir (who hoards the gold as a dragon), the doom of Sigurd, and the fall of the Burgundians. Andvari is the Germanic prototype of the **dwarf with the cursed ring**, the ultimate source (via the *Völsunga saga* and *Nibelungenlied*'s Alberich) of Wagner's Ring of the Nibelung and the long literary afterlife of the gold that destroys all who hold it.
