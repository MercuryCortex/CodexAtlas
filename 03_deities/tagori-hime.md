# Tagori-hime

Tagori-hime (also **Takiribime**) is the eldest of the three **Munakata goddesses**, born with her sisters **[[ichikishima-hime]]** and **[[tagitsu-hime]]** from the sword of **[[susanoo]]** during the *ukehi* pledge with **[[amaterasu]]** (*Kojiki*, *Nihon Shoki*). Her name evokes the misting, foaming sea, and she is enshrined at **Okitsu-miya** on **Okinoshima** — the remote, forbidden "island of the gods" in the Genkai Sea, the farthest of the three Munakata shrine-sites on the ancient sea-route to Korea, where archaeology has recovered an extraordinary sequence of state ritual offerings (4th–9th c.). In the Izumo mythological cycle she also appears as a consort of **[[okuninushi]]**, by whom she bears the deities Ajisukitakahikone and Shitateruhime — linking the maritime Munakata cult to the great Izumo lineage. With her sisters she is the divine guardian of safe passage across the straits.
