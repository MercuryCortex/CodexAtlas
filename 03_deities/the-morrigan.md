# The Morrígan

## Identity
Triple war-goddess of the Tuatha Dé Danann — usually appearing as three: **Morrígan** ("Phantom Queen"), **Macha**, and **Badb** ("Hooded Crow"), sometimes with **Nemain** ("Panic-Frenzy") as a fourth. Sovereignty-goddess in the **sacred-marriage-with-the-king** sense: she sleeps with the [[dagda]] at Samhain at the ford of the river Unius to bring victory in the Second Battle of Mag Tuired; the king's legitimate rule depends on her acceptance.

## Mythological role
**Táin Bó Cúailnge**: she pursues, threatens, propositions, and is rejected by the hero Cú Chulainn — then attacks him in three animal forms (eel, wolf, heifer) which he wounds; ultimately reconciled, she appears as the **Washer at the Ford** washing his bloody armor before his final battle, the canonical Celtic death-omen. **Battle-crow**: settles on the shoulder of the dying Cú Chulainn at his end, the etiological origin of the *badb-crow* on the battlefield omen.

## Cross-tradition identifications
- **[[the-erinyes]]** — *scholarly-parallel*: triadic-feminine vengeance/death powers.
- **[[freyja]]** — *parallel-motif*: Norse battle-goddess who claims half the slain for Fólkvangr.
- **Valkyries** — *parallel-motif*: female battle-spirits choosing the slain.
