# The Pleroma

A concept-entity: the "fullness" of the divine — the totality of aeons emanated from the unknowable Father. The Valentinian Pleroma has 30 aeons in 15 male-female pairs; the Sethian Pleroma is structured around Barbelo, Autogenes, and the four Luminaries (Harmozel, Oroiael, Daveithai, Eleleth). The Pleroma is the divine realm proper; everything outside it (and below Sophia's fall) is the kenōma, the deficiency. Salvation in Gnostic systems is recovery of the divine spark's place in the Pleroma.
