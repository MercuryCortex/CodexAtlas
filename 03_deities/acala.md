# Acala

Acala (Sanskrit *Acalanātha*, "the Immovable Lord"; Japanese **Fudō Myōō**) is the foremost of the **Five Wisdom Kings** (*Vidyārāja*) of esoteric Buddhism — a wrathful protector who, far from contradicting the Buddha's compassion, expresses it in fierce form: he terrifies and compels the stubborn and deluded toward awakening when gentle means fail. He is depicted as a squat, fanged, blue-black figure wreathed in flame (which burns away defilements), holding the **sword** that cuts through ignorance and the **lasso** that binds the wicked, seated immovably on a rock. Theologically he is the **wrathful emanation of the cosmic Buddha [[vairocana]]** (Mahāvairocana / Dainichi). Though Indian in origin, Acala became one of the most intensely worshipped deities of **Japanese Shingon and Tendai** Buddhism — the focus of fire rituals (*goma*), waterfall austerities, and the devotion of *yamabushi* mountain ascetics — making him a major living deity of East Asia.
