# Papatūānuku

## Identity
Earth-mother of Māori cosmogony; consort of Ranginui (Sky Father). Papatūānuku is the substantive earth — the land on which Māori people live, the source of all that grows and lives, the matrix from which the gods and humans descend. In tikanga (proper practice), Māori people are *of the land* (*tangata whenua*, "people of the land") — Papatūānuku is the literal ancestress.

## Earliest attestation
Māori oral tradition; documented in 19th-c. compilations.

## Mythological role
Primordial earth-mother; embraced Ranginui in primordial darkness; forcibly separated by Tāne; sends mist (her sighs) upward to her beloved Rangi.

## Iconography
The earth itself. Aniconic.
