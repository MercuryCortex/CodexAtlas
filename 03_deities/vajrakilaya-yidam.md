# Vajrakīlaya — Wrathful Tantric Buddha-Form

The wrathful Buddha-form embodying the **phurba** (three-edged ritual dagger), one of the Eight Heruka practices of the Nyingma school and a principal yidam (meditation-deity) of the Tibetan tantric tradition. Vajrakīlaya is iconographically a three-headed, six-armed wrathful deity holding the phurba and engaging in the *liberation through compassionate destruction* of obstacles to enlightenment. His textual basis is the **Vajrakīla-tantra** (Indian, ~8th c.), and he was transmitted to Tibet by Padmasambhava as one of his eight principal practices.
