# Gugalanna

The **Bull of Heaven** — first husband of [[ereshkigal]] in Sumerian underworld theology, and the celestial-bull antagonist sent by Anu (at [[inanna-sumerian]]'s request) against [[gilgamesh-king]] in Tablet VI of the *Epic of Gilgamesh*. Gilgamesh and Enkidu kill him; Ereshkigal's mourning for her dead husband is the pretext Inanna uses to descend to the underworld (the *Descent of Inanna* opens with the funeral of Gugalanna).

Gugalanna is also associated with the constellation **Taurus** — the celestial bull whose precession marked the spring equinox in the Bronze Age (c. -4500 to -1700), the period of Sumerian-Akkadian astronomical observation that fixed the foundational zodiacal vocabulary.
