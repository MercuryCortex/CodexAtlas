# Sif

## Identity
Wife of [[thor]], goddess of the Æsir, distinguished principally by her **golden hair**. The Edda recounts that Loki maliciously cut off her hair in her sleep; Thor's fury forced Loki to descend to the dwarves Sons of Ivaldi, who forged her replacement hair from real gold that grew as living hair on her head — at the same forge-bargain that produced Skíðblaðnir (Freyr's ship) and Gungnir (Odin's spear), and that won the wager establishing Mjöllnir. Her golden hair has been read by Davidson 1964 and de Vries 1956 as a grain-cult vestige — the goddess as the ripe wheat-field — paralleling Demeter / Ceres.
