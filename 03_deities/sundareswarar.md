# Sundareswarar

Sundareswarar (Tamil/Sanskrit *Sundareśvara*, "the beautiful lord"; also **Chokkanathar**) is the form of **[[shiva]]** worshipped at **Madurai**, and the divine consort of the city's sovereign goddess **[[meenakshi]]**. In the Madurai myth he is the lord of Kailasa whom the warrior-queen Meenakshi confronts in her world-conquest; recognizing him as her destined husband, she brings him home to Madurai, where their **celestial wedding (Tirukalyanam)** becomes the central festival of the great temple. As the local Shiva he is the hero of the **Tiruvilaiyadal Puranam**, the Tamil account of his **64 "sacred games"** (*tiruvilaiyadal*) — wonders performed in and around Madurai, such as turning a jackal into a horse, or being fed sugarcane by a stone elephant. Notably, in the Madurai cult the usual Shaiva hierarchy is reversed: it is **Meenakshi who reigns** as the temple's principal deity, with Sundareswarar enshrined beside her as consort — a vivid Tamil expression of goddess-centred devotion.
