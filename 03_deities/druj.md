# Druj

## Identity
Druj (Avestan *druj*; Pahlavi *drug*; Old Persian *drō*) is **the personified cosmic principle of Lie / Untruth in Zoroastrian theology** — the **metaphysical counter-principle** of [[asha-vahishta]] (Truth, Right Order, the cosmic ought-to-be). Where [[aeshma]] is the daeva of *active* violent wrath and [[angra-mainyu-ahriman]] is the personal Adversary, **Druj is cosmic-falsity as such** — the abstract principle that grounds all deviation from cosmic order. Druj is grammatically and theologically **feminine** in Avestan (the noun is feminine; the personified figure follows the grammar). She has multiple manifest-forms — **Drug-i Nasus** (the corpse-demoness, the principle of pollution-from-death), **Jeh / Jahi** (the cosmic-whore-demoness who in the Pahlavi Bundahishn awakens Ahriman from his stupor and prompts him to assault Ahura Mazda's creation). For the Codex Atlas mission Druj is load-bearing as the **metaphysical foundation of Zoroastrian cosmic-dualism** — the principle without which the Asha-vs-Druj cosmic-combat would not exist as a *cosmic-principle* opposition (as opposed to merely a personal-adversary opposition). This is the **structural-conceptual core** of the dualism that influences Second-Temple Jewish and Christian theology.

## Earliest attestation
- **Gathas** ([[phase-2-002-gathas-of-zarathustra]]): Druj appears throughout — Yasna 30.5, 30.10, 31.8, 32.11-12, 33.4, 49.3 — as the cosmic-principle Zarathustra denounces. **The Asha-vs-Druj opposition is the foundational theological move of the Gathic stratum.**
- **Younger Avesta** ([[phase-2-004-yasna-younger-avesta]]): Druj elaborated into multiple personified-forms. Vendidad (the "Anti-Demonic Law") extensively concerned with maintaining purity against Druj.
- **Old Persian inscriptions** (Achaemenid period, -550 to -330): **Darius I's Behistun Inscription** uses *drauga* (the Old Persian cognate of *druj*) as the political-religious term for the rebels Darius defeats — explicitly framing his rebellion-suppression as the cosmic-religious defeat of Lie-against-Truth. **This is one of the few cases in ancient royal-political inscriptions where the king's enemies are framed in explicit cosmic-religious metaphysical terms.**
- **Pahlavi tradition** (~+300 onward): Druj elaborated systematically. The Bundahishn treats Druj's hierarchical manifestations.

## Mythological role

### Cosmic-dualistic foundation
The **Asha-vs-Druj cosmic opposition** is the foundational metaphysical move of Zoroastrian theology. Where most Near Eastern religions have either (a) monistic systems (all cosmic causality from one divine source) or (b) polytheistic-conflictual systems (gods fighting other gods), **Zoroastrianism formalizes cosmic-evil as a metaphysical principle** — Druj as cosmic-Lie is not merely a personal adversary but a structuring cosmic principle.

### The female-cosmic-evil tradition
Druj is feminine. The female-demonic-cosmic-evil tradition is a major Zoroastrian-religious feature with substantial reception-history:
- **Jeh / Jahi** (the cosmic-whore-demoness who awakens Ahriman in the Bundahishn cosmogony) — the principal female-demonic figure.
- **Drug-i Nasus** (the corpse-demoness, manifested in actual physical corpse-pollution) — the principal ritual-cult Druj-manifestation.
- Influence on later female-demonic figures (Lilith in Jewish tradition; the *succubae* of Christian medieval demonology) is debated but the structural-thematic parallels exist.

### Darius I's political-religious deployment
**The Behistun Inscription** (~-518) explicitly frames Darius's defeat of the nine rebel-kings as the cosmic-religious defeat of *drauga* (Druj-Lie) by *arta* (Asha-Truth). This is **one of the earliest known cases of cosmic-religious metaphysical-dualism deployed for political-legitimation** — the king as cosmic-warrior against cosmic-evil. The pattern recurs in later imperial-religious legitimation (Sasanian, Byzantine-Christian, medieval-Christian-vs-Islamic, modern political-religious uses).

## Cross-tradition identifications

### Druj → Second-Temple Jewish cosmic-evil
The Asha-vs-Druj cosmic-dualism is **the principal pre-Christian Near Eastern model** for the cosmic-dualism that emerges in **Second-Temple Jewish apocalyptic literature**:
- **Qumran** *War Scroll* (1QM) — cosmic combat between Sons of Light and Sons of Darkness; **explicitly cosmic-dualist** in a way pre-exilic Hebrew Bible is not. The Qumran community uses **Belial** as the cosmic-evil principal-figure, structurally Druj-functional.
- **1 Enoch** — the Watchers narrative + the cosmic-warfare apocalyptic structure.
- **The Book of Daniel** — cosmic-warfare-among-angels (Michael vs. the prince of Persia, Dan 10).

### Druj → Christian Satan
The Druj-Asha cosmic-dualism transposes (via Second-Temple Jewish intermediaries) into the **Christian Satan-vs-God cosmic-combat theology**:
- **Christian Satan** as personified cosmic-Adversary opposed to God (Job's adversary is closer to the Persian-period theological development than to pre-exilic Hebrew Bible).
- **Christian eschatological cosmic-combat** (Revelation 12, 19-20) — structurally Frashokereti-and-Druj-defeat.

Direct genealogical transmission of the *Druj-figure* into Christian Satan is unclaimed; the **structural cosmic-dualism pattern** is the documented inheritance.

### Druj and Manichaean Princess of Darkness
**Manichaeism** (founded by [[mani]] in 3rd c. CE Sasanian Persia, working within Zoroastrian-Christian-Jewish-Buddhist religious-cultural matrix) preserves the **female-cosmic-evil** principle more explicitly than Christianity does. The Manichaean "Realm of Darkness" has a feminine-personified principle (the "Mother of Darkness" or similar in some Manichaean texts) that is structurally Druj-Jeh-derived.

## Iconography
- **No standard ancient Zoroastrian iconography** — Druj as cosmic-principle is not anthropomorphically depicted in surviving ancient Iranian art.
- The **Drug-i Nasus corpse-pollution** is treated extensively in Vendidad ritual-purification prescriptions but is not visually depicted.

## Disputes
- **Cosmic-principle vs. specific-demoness**. Whether Avestan *druj* is primarily (a) an abstract metaphysical principle that *can* be personified as specific demonesses (Drug-i Nasus, Jeh), or (b) a category-name for a class of demonesses with primary reality in the specific figures, is debated. The dominant view: both, with the abstract cosmic-principle being the more-fundamental theological-structural move.
- **Female-demonic transmission to Lilith / succubae**. Whether Jewish and Christian female-demonic figures are influenced by the Iranian Druj-Jeh tradition is debated. Most current scholarship treats it as plausible but not directly demonstrable.
- **Behistun *drauga* — political or metaphysical?** Whether Darius's *drauga*-vs-*arta* framing is genuine theological-religious-imperial expression or rhetorical-political deployment of religious vocabulary is debated. Most scholars treat it as genuinely religious — Darius understood his rebellion-suppression in cosmic-religious-metaphysical terms.
