# Sapling (Good Mind Twin)

The life-creating twin in Haudenosaunee cosmogony. Sapling (the Good Mind) created gentle animals, fertile plains, clear rivers, life-giving plants, and the conditions for human flourishing. His adversarial twin [[flint-deity]] (the Evil Mind) countered his creations with obstacles. The Haudenosaunee Great Law of Peace (*Gayanashagowa*) is understood as the political institutionalization of the Good Mind's principle — governance by consensus and care rather than by violence and domination.
