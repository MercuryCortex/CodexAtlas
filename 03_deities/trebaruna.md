# Trebaruna

## Identity
Trebaruna is one of the most etymologically interesting deities in the Lusitanian corpus — attested in fewer inscriptions than [[bandua]] or [[nabia]] (~5 inscriptions) but with a name that encodes a remarkable combination of conceptual domains. Prósper's (2002) analysis proposes:

- **\*trebā** (PIE *\*treb-*: "settlement, homestead, dwelling" — cognate with Latin *trabs* = beam/roof-timber, Gaulish *trebā* = settlement → French *tref*, Welsh *tref* = town, Irish *treb* = farm)
- **\*runa** (PIE *\*rewnā*: "mystery, secret knowledge, hidden thing" — cognate with Gothic *rūna* = mystery, Old Norse *rún* = rune/secret, Old English *rūn* = secret counsel → English "rune")

**"Trebaruna" = "Mystery/Secret of the Settlement"** or **"Hidden Knowledge of the Homestead"** — a name that combines the most domestic of concepts (the farm, the house, the community settlement) with the most esoteric (hidden knowledge, mystery, the sacred secret). This combination is structurally identical to the idea that sovereignty knowledge is held within the land itself — that the *trebā*/homestead contains a sacred mystery, a hidden power that the sovereignty goddess embodies and reveals.

## Etymology as Theology
The *trebā* + *runa* compound is theologically rich:
- The settlement (*trebā*) is not just a physical place but a **sacred zone** — the community's bounded territory, its land, the thing worth defending with your life
- The *runa* (hidden knowledge) suggests that this sacred zone contains a **secret** — perhaps the deity herself, perhaps the relationship between the living and the dead within the ancestral territory, perhaps the ritual knowledge that makes the community cohesive

This is the theology of **territorial sovereignty as mystery** — a concept that appears across IE traditions:
- **Irish** *díchetal di chennaib* (chanting from the tops of heads) — the druidic knowledge of territorial memory, literally knowing the land from its sacred secrets
- **Welsh** *awen* (poetic/prophetic inspiration arising from the land, from the depths)
- **Norse** *rúnar* (runes as the hidden forces of nature, requiring the sacrifice of Odin's hanging to access)
- **Germanic** *sīthfhergund* (hidden power of the earth)

Trebaruna's name may point to a **land-oracle** function: the deity who knows the hidden knowledge of the territory, who guards the community's sacred mystery, and who must be propitiated for the settlement to survive.

## The Feminine Sovereignty Pattern
Trebaruna belongs to the IE **sovereignty goddess** type — the divine feminine figure who embodies the power of the land and whose relationship with the male leader/king determines the community's prosperity:

| Tradition | Figure | Domain |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Irish | **Ériu / Banba / Fódla** | Ireland's sovereignty goddesses (three aspects of the land itself) |
| Irish | **The Morrigan** | Battle-sovereignty; the land's power over life and death |
| Irish | **Medb** | Sovereignty embodied in a queen; war-leader and land-power |
| Welsh | **Rhiannon** | Horse-sovereignty goddess; her marriage legitimizes kingship |
| Gaulish | **Epona** | Horse/sovereignty; her images found across the entire Gaulish zone |
| Roman | **Terra Mater** | Earth as sovereign mother; her protection enables Roman power |
| Lusitanian | **Trebaruna** | Settlement-sovereignty; the mystery of the homestead |

In this schema, Trebaruna is the **western terminal node of the IE sovereignty goddess tradition** — the same divine feminine power-of-the-land that appears from Ireland (Ériu) to Gaul (Epona) to Rome (Terra Mater) has its Lusitanian instantiation at the Atlantic edge of Europe.

## Trebaruna and the Lusitanian Divine Landscape
In the full Lusitanian divine map:
- **[[reue-lusitanian]]** = sky-sovereignty (the upper domain)
- **[[bandua]]** = territorial-protective sovereignty (the binding of community)
- **Trebaruna** = homestead-mystery sovereignty (the hidden power of the settlement)
- **[[nabia]]** = water-life sovereignty (the living surface)
- **[[ataecina]]** = underworld-regeneration sovereignty (the chthonic power)
- **[[endovelicus]]** = oracular-mediation (between all domains)

Trebaruna adds the specifically **domestic sacred mystery** dimension — the power that inheres in the settlement itself, not just in its defense (Bandua) or its water (Nabia). She is the deity of the *castro* as a sacred mystery rather than just a defended community.
