# Konohanasakuya-hime

Konohanasakuya-hime ("Princess who makes the tree-flowers bloom") is the Shinto goddess of the **cherry blossom** — and so of **beautiful, fleeting life** — and the deity of **Mount Fuji**, worshipped as **Sengen (Asama)** at the shrines that ring the great volcano. Daughter of the mountain-god **Ōyamatsumi**, she is the radiant bride chosen by the heavenly grandson **[[ninigi|Ninigi]]**, who however **rejected her plainer elder sister Iwanaga-hime** ("rock-long princess," who would have granted enduring life) — the reason, the myth says, that the imperial line and all human life is lovely but **transient, like blossom rather than stone**. When she conceived after a single night and Ninigi doubted the child was his, she made a fierce vow: she sealed herself in a birth-hut and **set it on fire**, declaring that if the children were truly the heavenly grandson's they would be born unharmed by the flames — and so they were, her sons becoming forebears of the imperial dynasty. Her association with the volcano's fire and with safe delivery through the burning hut made her a beloved goddess of **childbirth** as well as of Fuji and the cherry blossom.
