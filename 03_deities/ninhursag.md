# Ninhursag

Sumerian / Akkadian mother-goddess, midwife of the gods, co-creator of humanity in the *Atrahasis* and *Enki and Ninhursag* myths. Her name *Nin-hur-sag* literally means "Lady of the Mountain-Peak"; her symbol is the omega-shaped uterus glyph, associated with birth and the cosmic horseshoe-shape of the mountain.

## Role in the creation of humanity

In *Atrahasis* (Old Babylonian, ~17th c. BCE), the lower-class working gods (the *Igigi*) revolt against the labor of digging the rivers and canals. The senior gods (Anu, Enlil, Enki, Ninhursag) convene; Enki proposes creating humans to bear the labor. Ninhursag shapes the clay; Enki contributes the divine component (the blood of the slain god We-ila, mixed with the clay); fourteen birth-goddesses (the *bēlet-ilī* "Ladies of the Gods") gestate the first humans. Standard Sumerian-Akkadian cosmogonic mythology.
