# Ullr

Ullr (Old Norse *Ullr*, "glory") is the Norse god of **archery, skiing, and the hunt** — a figure who, though minor in the surviving Eddas, is revealed by the evidence to have been a **major deity of an older cult layer**: his name is embedded in an enormous number of Norwegian and Swedish place-names (Ullensaker, Ullevål, Ulleråker), a density rivaled only by Thor and Freyr, implying widespread worship that the Christian-era poets no longer remembered. Snorri lists him among the Æsir, son of Sif (and thus stepson of Thor), dwelling in **Ýdalir** ("Yew-dales" — yew being the bow-wood), and says he is "good to call on in single combat." The *Grímnismál* hints he once held a kind of sovereignty (men should pray to him and "to all the gods"). Saxo's Latinized **Ollerus** even rules in Odin's place during the latter's exile. A kenning makes his shield a "ship," and he glides on ski or bone-skate over the winter snow — the divine archer-hunter of the frozen north.
