# Ganga

Ganga (*Gaṅgā*) is the **personified river Ganges** — the most sacred river of Hinduism and one of its most beloved goddesses, whose waters purify sin, grant merit, and (when ashes are immersed in her at Varanasi/Kashi) release the dead toward *moksha*. Daughter of the mountain-god Himavat (and so sister of **[[parvati]]**), she is in her great myth a **celestial river** brought down from heaven by the austerities of King **Bhagiratha**, to liberate the ashes of his cursed ancestors — but her fall would have shattered the earth had **[[shiva]]** not caught her in his **matted hair** and let her trickle out gently (hence Shiva *Gangadhara*). She is *Tripathaga*, "the three-pathed," flowing through heaven, earth, and the underworld. As a deified river she belongs with **[[saraswati]]** and Yamuna in the sacred-river complex of Hindu geography (the Triveni Sangam at Prayag), and her daily worship in the *Ganga aarti* at Haridwar and Varanasi remains one of the most vivid living rites of Hinduism.
