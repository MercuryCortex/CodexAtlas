# The Spanish Inquisition (1478–1834)

## Establishment and Purpose
The Spanish Inquisition was established by **papal bull** (*Exigit Sincerae Devotionis Affectus*) of **Pope Sixtus IV** on **November 1, 1478**, at the request of **Ferdinand II of Aragon** and **Isabella I of Castile**. Unlike earlier papal inquisitions, the Spanish Inquisition was a **royal institution** — under the crown's control, not Rome's — making it simultaneously a religious and political instrument.

Its stated purpose: to investigate the orthodoxy of **conversos** — Jews (and Muslims) who had converted to Christianity, sometimes under duress during the 1391 pogroms, sometimes voluntarily. The target was not unconverted Jews (who were outside the church's jurisdiction) but baptized Christians suspected of secretly practicing their former religion (*crypto-Judaism* or *Marranism*).

Its first Inquisitor General: **Tomás de Torquemada** (1483–1498) — himself of converso ancestry, a biographical irony that has generated extensive historical analysis.

## Scale and Methods
Historical revision (Kamen 1997) has substantially revised the Black Legend numbers:
- Total executions across the Inquisition's 356-year existence (~1478–1834): estimated **3,000–5,000** (not the hundreds of thousands of popular imagination)
- **Torture was used** but was constrained by procedure (duration limits, no blood, no permanent injury) — comparably less brutal than contemporary secular courts
- The primary punishments were **penance, confiscation of property, public humiliation** (*sanbenito* — distinctive penitential garment), and imprisonment
- **The auto-da-fé** ("act of faith") — the public ceremony of sentencing — was a theatrical-political-religious event; actual burning was performed by secular authorities after the church "relaxed" (handed over) the convicted

The Inquisition kept **extraordinarily detailed trial records** — an inadvertent gift to historians. The records document the tenacity with which crypto-Jewish practice survived: circumcision, Sabbath lighting, dietary laws, Hebrew prayers — practices maintained in secret for generations in households presenting a fully Catholic exterior.

## The 1492 Expulsion — The Critical Escalation
The Inquisition targeted conversos but could not touch unconverted Jews. The solution chosen: **[[event-jewish-expulsion-spain-1492]]** — Ferdinand and Isabella's edict expelling all unbaptized Jews from Spain by August 1492. Estimates of those expelled: 40,000–200,000 (the range reflects genuine documentary uncertainty).

The expulsion directly generated the **Sephardic diaspora** — the dispersal of Iberian Jewish communities to:
- **Ottoman Empire** (the largest reception — Constantinople, Thessaloniki, Izmir)
- **North Africa** (Morocco, Algeria)
- **Italy** (Venice, Ferrara, Livorno)
- **Netherlands** (Amsterdam — Spinoza's community)
- **New World** (through converso networks)

The Ottoman Sultan Bayezid II reportedly said of Ferdinand and Isabella: "Can you call such a king wise and intelligent? He is impoverishing his country and enriching my kingdom."

## The Safed Connection — Persecution → Greatest Kabbalistic Renaissance
The connection between the Expulsion and subsequent Jewish intellectual explosion is direct and documented:
- **Isaac Luria** (the Ari, 1534–1572) — born to Sephardic parents; his family was among the expelled
- **Joseph Karo** (1488–1575) — born in Spain, expelled as a child, eventually settled in Safed; composed the *Shulchan Aruch* (the definitive code of Jewish law) and the mystical diary *Maggid Mesharim* in Safed
- **Moses Cordovero** (1522–1570) — systematizer of Kabbalah at Safed
- **Solomon Alkabets** (composer of *Lecha Dodi*, still sung every Shabbat worldwide)

**Safed, Palestine, 1540–1580 is the greatest creative burst in Jewish mystical history — and it is populated almost entirely by families traumatized by the 1492 Expulsion and the Inquisition.** The persecution does not prevent the renaissance; it is its proximate cause.

## The Converso Networks — The Hidden Transmission
The crypto-Jewish converso communities created one of history's most fascinating underground transmission networks:
- **The Marrano diaspora** maintained secret Jewish practice while presenting as Catholics for generations
- When converso families reached Amsterdam (the "Jerusalem of the North") or the Ottoman Empire, they reconnected with openly Jewish communities
- Spinoza's family were Portuguese conversos who had returned to Judaism in Amsterdam — making **Baruch Spinoza** (1632–1677), one of the founding philosophers of Western modernity, a product of this persecution-and-survival network
- The converso networks were also critical transmission channels for the Italian Renaissance, for early proto-Enlightenment thought, and for the development of early capitalism (through Sephardic trading networks)

## The Inquisition as Instrument Against Multiple Traditions
Though most famous for its work against conversos and crypto-Jews, the Inquisition also processed:
- **Moriscos** — Muslim converts (*mudéjares*) suspected of crypto-Islam, especially after the 1502 forced conversion of Granadan Muslims
- **Protestants** — particularly after the Reformation; auto-da-fés against Lutheran heresy occur especially 1557–1562
- **Alumbrados** — Spanish mystics accused of excessive spiritual individualism (foreshadowing Quietism)
- **Indigenous peoples** in the Spanish colonies — the colonial Inquisitions of Mexico and Peru targeted indigenous religious practice

## The "Black Legend" Problem
Henry Kamen's revisionist work (1997) requires historiographical care:
- The Black Legend (Protestant and Enlightenment polemic against the Inquisition) exaggerated numbers and brutality
- The revisionist correction does not *exonerate* the Inquisition — state-sponsored religious coercion, property confiscation, forced public humiliation, and the creation of a *limpieza de sangre* ("purity of blood") racial ideology are serious historical wrongs regardless of the execution statistics
- The **limpieza de sangre** concept — that converso blood remained inherently suspect regardless of sincere conversion — is arguably the first institutionalized racial ideology in Western history, with direct genealogical connections to later racial science
