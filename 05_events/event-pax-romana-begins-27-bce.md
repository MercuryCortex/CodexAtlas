# Augustan Settlement / Pax Romana Begins (27 BCE)

On 16 January 27 BCE the Roman Senate awarded Octavian the title **Augustus** ("revered"), inaugurating what later historiography calls the *Principate* — a *de facto* monarchical settlement dressed in Republican constitutional vocabulary. The Augustan settlement closed the gates of the Temple of Janus three times during his reign (a Roman symbolic act for universal peace), restructured provincial government (imperial vs senatorial provinces), professionalized the legions at the frontiers, and inaugurated the **Pax Romana** that lasted ~200 years. The political-economic infrastructure (single legal-monetary-postal system across the Mediterranean basin) became the substrate on which Christianity propagated in the 1st century. The Augustan religious-political program (restoration of 82 temples in Rome; revival of archaic priesthoods; the *divi filius* titulature inherited from the deified [[julius-caesar]]) established the imperial-cult foundation against which early Christian Christology articulated its own theology.
