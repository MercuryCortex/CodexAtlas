# Saqifa Bani Saida (8 June 632 CE)

A stub. The Saqifa is the foundational political moment of Islam: the
selection of a successor to Muhammad immediately upon his death. The
proceedings — debate between Medinan Ansar (proposing one of their own
or a dual caliphate) and Meccan Muhajirun (proposing Quraysh leadership)
— ended with Umar ibn al-Khattab giving allegiance to Abu Bakr, who
became the first caliph.

The Sunni and Shia traditions reconstruct Saqifa from different angles
and to different conclusions. The Sunni reading: the umma chose the
most qualified senior companion through consultation (shūrā), which
becomes the model for legitimate succession. The Shia reading: Saqifa
bypassed the explicit designation of Ali at Ghadir Khumm and inaugurated
a 25-year usurpation rectified only by Ali's accession as fourth caliph
in 656.
