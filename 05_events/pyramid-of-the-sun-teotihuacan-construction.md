# Pyramid of the Sun (Teotihuacan)

## Overview

The **Pyramid of the Sun** at **Teotihuacan** (central Mexico) is the third-largest pyramid by volume in the world (after the Great Pyramid of Cholula and Khufu's pyramid), constructed approximately 100–200 CE. It stands at the northern terminus of the **Avenue of the Dead** — the 2.4-km main axis of Teotihuacan, oriented approximately 15°25' east of true north in alignment with the **setting point of the Pleiades** on the horizon.

**Teotihuacan** ("Place Where the Gods Were Created" — an Aztec-era name; the original name is unknown) was the largest city in the pre-Columbian Americas at its peak (~450 CE), with 125,000–200,000 inhabitants. Its builders left **no surviving written records**, no identified royal names, and no clear dynastic sequence. It is one of the largest archaeological mysteries in the Western Hemisphere: a city that shaped every subsequent Mesoamerican civilization, whose builders remain anonymous.

**Dimensions:**
- Base: ~222 m × 225 m (nearly identical to the Great Pyramid of Khufu's 230 m base — a coincidence that has attracted much attention)
- Height: ~65 m (currently ~71 m, but the Batres restoration added an unauthorized extra tier)
- Volume: ~1 million cubic metres

## The cave beneath: the most important discovery

In **1971**, archaeologist **Jorge Acosta** discovered a **natural lava tube cave** extending approximately 100 m beneath the pyramid's center, ending in a **four-lobed chamber** approximately 20 m below the pyramid's central axis. The cave predates the pyramid; Teotihuacan's builders oriented the entire pyramid directly above it.

**What was found in the cave:**
- Ritual offerings: figurines, pottery shards, obsidian blades, mirrors
- Evidence of repeated ceremonial use over centuries
- The four-lobed terminal chamber — a shape that in Mesoamerican iconography represents the **four cardinal directions / the four cosmological regions**
- Walls originally sealed with mica sheets (since removed; see below)

**Interpretation:** The pyramid was built *over* the cave as a cosmological statement — the terrestrial monument anchored to an underworld axis. The cave is the **navel of the world** (*axis mundi*), the point of creation and emergence. The pyramid marks its location for the sky. This is structurally identical to the theological role of the Sacred Rock beneath the Dome of the Rock in Jerusalem, or the *omphalos* at Delphi — a world-navel covered by a monument.

The discovery permanently changed the interpretation of Teotihuacan: the city was not built in a convenient location — it was built in a specific location because of the cave.

## The mica mystery

Two thick layers of **mica** — up to 30 cm thick in places — were found beneath the pyramid's outer surface, specifically under a section now known as the "Mica Temple" (an annexed structure immediately south of the pyramid). Mica in such quantities is not a local material: the nearest source is Brazil, 3,000+ km away.

**Mica's unusual properties:** It is highly refractive, heat-resistant, and — relevant to modern observers — blocks certain types of radiation. Whether the Teotihuacan builders were aware of its radiation-blocking properties is unknown, but its use in such quantities, at such cost, for a purpose that is not architecturally obvious, has attracted speculation.

**The utilitarian explanation:** Mica may have been used as a reflective surface for ritual effect (it makes floors shimmer), as insulation, or as a symbolic representation of water (Teotihuacan art frequently depicts water).

## Astronomical orientation

The Avenue of the Dead's 15°25' deviation from true north aligns the **Pyramid of the Sun's** east-west axis with:
- The point on the horizon where the **Pleiades** set on August 12 — a date corresponding to the Mesoamerican calendar's mythological creation date
- The **sunset position at the equinoxes** when viewed from the **Pyramid of the Moon** (northern terminus of the Avenue)

The orientation is not toward true north/south but toward the Pleiades setting — suggesting the city's grid was oriented by astronomical observation rather than by magnetic north.

## The Great Pyramid coincidence

The base dimensions of the Pyramid of the Sun (~222–225 m per side) are remarkably close to those of the Great Pyramid of Khufu (230.33 m per side). This has generated:
- **Alternative-history claims** (the two were planned together, or derived from a common source)
- **Independent convergence arguments** (large pyramid platforms naturally fall within this size range for structural and logistical reasons; the similarity is coincidental)

The volumes and internal structures are completely different — the Pyramid of the Sun has no internal chambers (only the cave below it) and is solid fill. No credible evidence of a common design source exists.
