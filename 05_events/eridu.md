# Eridu

Stub. The oldest attested Sumerian city and sacred cult center of [[enki-ea]], located in what is now southern Iraq. In Mesopotamian mythology, Eridu was the first city established after kingship descended from heaven (see [[phase-1-015-sumerian-king-list]]), and the home of the seven *apkallu* antediluvian sages — see [[phase-1-034-adapa-myth]] for the Adapa narrative centered here. Eridu represents the archetype of civilization at its point of origin in Mesopotamian cosmology. Archaeological occupation from the Ubaid period (~5400 BCE) through the Ur III period (~2000 BCE).
