14th-c. theological controversy in Eastern Orthodoxy. Two main parties: the Athonite hesychasts (defending the Jesus Prayer and the essence-energies distinction, led by Gregory Palamas) vs. Barlaam of Calabria + Gregory Akindynos + Nikephoros Gregoras (rejecting Palamite uncreated-energies as introducing composition in the divine essence). Three Synods of Constantinople (1341, 1347, 1351) confirmed Palamas's position as Orthodox dogma. Foundational for later Orthodox theology + the Russian hesychast revival.
