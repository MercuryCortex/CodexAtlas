# Saxon Wars (772–804 CE)

A stub. The 32-year Frankish campaign that brought Germany north of
the Rhine into Carolingian Christendom by military force. Key
turning-points: Charlemagne's destruction of the Irminsul (the Saxon
sacred world-tree pillar) in 772; the Massacre of Verden in 782; the
issuance of the Capitulatio de Partibus Saxoniae in 785; the final
Saxon submission ~804.

The Saxon Wars are doctrinally significant because they document the
explicit fusion of imperial-political power and religious mission
that would characterize Western Christendom through the Reformation
and beyond. The Capitulatio's prescription of death for refusing
baptism is one of the most-cited canonical-law examples of forced
conversion.
