# Battle of Yarmuk (636)

Six-day battle (August 636) on the Yarmuk River that destroyed the Byzantine field army under [[heraclius-byzantine-emperor|Emperor Heraclius]]'s commanders and gave the Rashidun caliphate under [[umar-ibn-al-khattab|ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb]] decisive control of Syria and Palestine. The battle is one of the most consequential military engagements in late-antique history: it ends the Byzantine recovery from the 26-year war with Sasanian Persia (the True Cross had been restored to Jerusalem only six years before) and inaugurates the durable Arab-Muslim occupation of the Levant that has lasted (in successive variant forms) to the present. Khalid ibn al-Walid's tactical command is canonical in Islamic military historiography.
