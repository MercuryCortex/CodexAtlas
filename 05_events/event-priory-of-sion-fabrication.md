# Priory of Sion fabrication (1956–1993)

**Critical-tiering note:** Per the Codex Atlas methodology on contested-bridge nodes — the Priory of Sion is **definitively fabrication** per French court records (1993 Plantard sworn admission), per the paleographic analysis of the *Dossiers Secrets*, and per the consensus of modern critical scholarship. The reception-history is real and globally-consequential, and is documented here as such. Holy Blood Holy Grail (1982) and Da Vinci Code (2003) are Tier-4 / reception-history sources, not historical evidence.

## What happened
The **Priory of Sion** (*Prieuré de Sion*) was a small French legal association registered in **Annemasse on 7 May 1956** by **[[pierre-plantard|Pierre Plantard]]** and three associates. The association's registered purposes were minor and local. **Between 1956 and 1989**, Plantard and his collaborator **Philippe de Chérisey** progressively constructed an elaborate fabricated historical narrative attributing to the Priory of Sion a **continuous 1099–present-day existence** as a secret society — claimed to have been founded by **Godfrey of Bouillon** at the time of the [[event-first-crusade-1095-1099|First Crusade]], to have **shadowed and controlled the [[tradition-knights-templar|Knights Templar]]**, to have **survived the [[event-trial-of-templars-1307-1314|Templar suppression of 1307–1314]] independently**, and to have guarded **the Merovingian-Jesus-bloodline secret** (descendants of Jesus and Mary Magdalene who allegedly escaped to southern France and became the Merovingian Frankish dynasty), with a continuous Grand-Master line claimed to include **Leonardo da Vinci, Isaac Newton, Victor Hugo, Claude Debussy, Jean Cocteau, and Plantard himself**.

The construction was institutionalized through **the *Dossiers Secrets d'Henri Lobineau*** — a series of forged documents deposited at the **Bibliothèque nationale de France between 1964 and 1967** under the cover-name *Henri Lobineau*. Modern paleographic analysis has established that the *Dossiers Secrets* are **20th-century fabrications**: the typewriter-typefaces, the paper, the ink, and the textual content all postdate the medieval and early-modern dates the documents claim.

## The reception-history phases

1. **1956–1967** — Plantard registers the association; constructs the historical narrative; deposits the forged *Dossiers Secrets* at the BnF.
2. **1967** — **Gérard de Sède's *L'Or de Rennes*** is the first mass-market presentation of Plantard's claims in French, framing them as authentic historical-religious revelation.
3. **1970s** — BBC television documentaries (Henry Lincoln, 1972; 1974; 1979) bring the Plantard material to English-language audiences.
4. **1982** — **Baigent, Leigh, and Lincoln, *Holy Blood, Holy Grail*** (London: Jonathan Cape) — the mass-market international book, elevating Plantard's claims globally. **The book sells millions of copies and remains in print.**
5. **1989** — Plantard names Roger-Patrice Pelat (a wealthy French businessman with political connections) as a Priory of Sion Grand Master. This is **the trigger for the 1993 French magistrate investigation**.
6. **1993** — French magistrate **Thierry Jean-Pierre**, investigating a Pelat-related financial scandal, takes Plantard's sworn testimony. **Plantard admits under oath that the Priory of Sion's historical-medieval claims are fabrications.** Plantard subsequently disowns his earlier published claims.
7. **2003** — **Dan Brown's *The Da Vinci Code*** is published. The novel explicitly cites the Plantard-Baigent-Leigh-Lincoln material as background-historical. Massive global bestseller (~80 million copies).
8. **2006** — Baigent and Leigh sue Dan Brown for plagiarism in UK High Court; the case is dismissed. Court records establish both books drew on the same Plantard material.
9. **2000** — Plantard dies.

## Religious and cultural consequences
- **Most-influential modern Western Templar-and-Christianity conspiracy construction.** The Plantard-Baigent-Lincoln-Brown narrative is the principal modern mass-cultural vehicle for the broader [[templar-gnostic-transmission-hypothesis]].
- **Substantial mass-cultural impact** independent of historical-validity. Tourist industries (Rennes-le-Château in southern France), mass-market publishing, films (*The Da Vinci Code* 2006; *Angels & Demons* 2009), and broader popular-conspiracy-culture all draw on the construction.
- **Documented modern example of forged-document-tradition.** Joins the Larmenius Charter (Fabré-Palaprat 1804), the *Protocols of the Elders of Zion* (1903), and the [[event-leo-taxil-hoax-1885-1897|Taxil Hoax]] (1885–1897) as canonical 19th-20th-c. examples of sustained forged-religious-political-document campaigns.

## Documents in play
- The *Dossiers Secrets d'Henri Lobineau* (BnF, 1964–1967 deposits) — paleographically demonstrated as 20th-c. forgeries.
- Plantard's published works (1956–1989).
- Baigent-Leigh-Lincoln *Holy Blood, Holy Grail* (1982).
- Dan Brown *The Da Vinci Code* (2003).
- French court records (1993) — Plantard's sworn admission.

## Disputes
- **The Priory of Sion's historical-medieval existence.** **Definitively rejected as fabrication** by modern critical scholarship and by Plantard's own 1993 admission.
- **The Priory of Sion as 1956 French legal association.** This is a real 20th-c. legal entity; the historical-medieval-claim is the fabrication.
- **The mass-cultural reception.** Plantard's 1993 admission **did not eliminate the popular-cultural narrative**, which persists through Baigent-Leigh-Lincoln's continued sales and the Da Vinci Code reception.
