# Ramsay's Oration (1737)

## What Happened
**[[chevalier-ramsay]]** delivered a speech (the "Oration") to a French Masonic lodge in Paris, approximately March 1737. The Oration exists in two manuscript variants (the "London" and "Paris" versions, with minor differences) — evidence of its immediate wide circulation. It was never formally published in Ramsay's lifetime but was copied and distributed across European Freemasonry within years of delivery.

## The Argument

Ramsay's Oration made a single, consequential claim: **Freemasonry did not begin with London stonemasons or even with Solomon's Temple — it began with Crusader knights in the Holy Land**.

The argument runs:
1. During the Crusades, noble Christian knights formed brotherhoods to protect pilgrims and restore the Temple of Jerusalem
2. These knights preserved "ancient signs and symbolic words" drawn from the religion of Noah — antediluvian wisdom transmitted through the mystery traditions
3. Returning to Europe, they established lodges in various countries; Scotland was a particularly fertile ground
4. Modern Freemasonry is the heir to this Crusader-knight fraternity — not an artisan's guild but a chivalric order

**The three moves this makes:**

**Social elevation**: Attaching Masonic origin to *noble Crusader knights* rather than artisan stonemasons elevated the fraternity's social prestige dramatically, making it appropriate for aristocratic membership. This was enormously consequential for the rapid spread of Freemasonry through French and European nobility.

**Institutional genealogy**: Creating a continuous chain from Crusader military orders (implicitly, Knights Templar) through Scottish operative masonry to French speculative lodges. This is the narrative architecture on which the Scottish Rite's Templar-themed degrees were subsequently constructed.

**Jacobite political coding**: The Crusader-knight analogy was politically legible to the Jacobite exile community in France. The knights *restoring the Jerusalem Temple* = the Stuart loyalists *restoring the rightful British monarch*. The "lost word" being recovered = the legitimate king's return. Ramsay worked in the household of the Stuart pretender in France; this coding was not accidental.

## Immediate Effects

The Oration catalyzed an explosion of "high degree" Masonry in France over the following two decades. Lodges began developing new degrees beyond the standard three — each claiming to transmit ancient wisdom recovered from the Crusader-Templar tradition. By 1760, dozens of competing "Scottish Rite" lodges were operating in France, each with variant degree systems. The consolidation into the formal **Ancient and Accepted Scottish Rite** (33 degrees) came later (Charleston, 1801), but Ramsay's Oration is the intellectual seed.

## Why It Matters for the Vault

The Oration is the **founding text of the most consequential misreading in Masonic history** — and its influence is *greater* for being a misreading. Whether or not the Templars actually transmitted secret wisdom to Scottish masons, Ramsay's claim created an institution (the Scottish Rite) that now has millions of members worldwide and whose degree system encodes one of the most elaborate symbolic structures in Western esotericism. The historical accuracy of the origin claim is irrelevant to its institutional power — which is itself a finding about how sacred genealogy works.
