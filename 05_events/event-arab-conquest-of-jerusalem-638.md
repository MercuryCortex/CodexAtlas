# Arab Conquest of Jerusalem (638)

Jerusalem surrendered to Caliph [[umar-ibn-al-khattab|ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb]] in person in 638, after the [[event-battle-of-yarmuk-636|Battle of Yarmuk]] (636) had destroyed the Byzantine field army. Patriarch Sophronius surrendered the city on terms; the celebrated *al-ʿuhda al-ʿUmariyya* (the Pact of ʿUmar) granted religious-freedom and property protections to the Christian population subject to *jizya* (poll-tax) and various status restrictions. The historicity of the precise surviving Pact-of-ʿUmar text is contested but the historical core of a 638 ʿUmar-Sophronius arrangement is secure. The event closes the brief Byzantine reconquest of Jerusalem under [[heraclius-byzantine-emperor|Heraclius]] (recovered from Sasanian Persia in 630) — eight years of Christian rule between two non-Christian dominations.
