# Great Pyramid of Giza (Khufu / Cheops)

## The monument

The **Great Pyramid of Khufu** (*Akhet Khufu* — "Horizon of Khufu") is the largest of the three Giza pyramids, the oldest of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, and the only one still substantially intact. Built during the reign of **[[khufu|Pharaoh Khufu]]** (~2589–2566 BCE), it was the tallest structure on Earth for approximately 3,800 years.

**Statistics (Petrie 1883; confirmed by subsequent survey):**
- Base: ~230.33 m per side
- Original height: ~146.5 m (now ~138.8 m after capstone and casing-stone loss)
- Volume: ~2.6 million cubic metres
- Estimated 2.3–2.6 million limestone blocks averaging ~2.5 tonnes; granite blocks in the King's Chamber up to 80 tonnes
- Base oriented to true north within **0.05°** — one of the most precisely oriented structures in the ancient world

## Construction

Built under the direction of **Hemiunu** (Khufu's nephew; vizier; "Overseer of All Royal Works") over ~20 years. The workforce was **not slave labor** — Lehner's excavations at the workers' village (Heit el-Ghurab) revealed a permanent community of craftsmen receiving rations, medical care, and honorable burial. Estimated: 20,000–30,000 workers in rotating teams.

Construction method remains debated. The **ScanPyramids mission** (2015–2019) used muon tomography and discovered in 2017 a large previously unknown cavity directly above the Grand Gallery — the **"Big Void"** (30+ m) — suggesting the interior is less thoroughly understood than assumed.

## Internal structure

Three known chambers connected by passages — an unusual design possibly reflecting changes of plan or a deliberate theological program:

## Stellar alignments and the Orion connection

The four narrow shafts (two from King's Chamber, two from Queen's Chamber) pointed to specific stars c. 2500 BCE:
- King's south shaft → **Orion's Belt** (Al Nitak; Osiris constellation) at lower culmination
- King's north shaft → **Thuban** (pole star c. 2787 BCE; α Draconis)
- Queen's south shaft → **Sirius** (Sopdet; associated with Isis; marked Nile flood)
- Queen's north shaft → **Kochab** (β Ursae Minoris; circumpolar)

The King's Chamber shafts exit to the exterior; the Queen's Chamber shafts end 20 cm short of the outer face (sealed). Egyptological consensus: the shafts had a theological purpose related to the king's soul (*ba*) ascending to the circumpolar stars. **[[robert-bauval|Robert Bauval]]**'s **[[theme-orion-correlation-theory|Orion Correlation Theory]]** (1994) extends this to the three Giza pyramids as a whole representing Orion's Belt.

## Cross-tradition connections

**[[tradition-hermeticism]]**: Egypt-as-source-of-hidden-wisdom is the foundation of Hermetic tradition. The *Corpus Hermeticum* claims its teaching descends from Thoth/Hermes Trismegistus; the pyramid became the Hermetic emblem of encoded gnosis.

**[[tradition-freemasonry]]**: The Temple of Solomon and the Great Pyramid are the two primary architectural symbols in Freemasonic ritual. The all-seeing-eye pyramid on the US **Great Seal** (1782; dollar bill since 1935) is a Masonic-Egyptian synthesis — not directly from ancient Egypt, but from the 18th-century imagination of Egypt-as-initiatory-source.

**[[theme-pyramid-as-resurrection-machine]]**: The pyramid's theological purpose was the pharaoh's *transfiguration* (*akh*) — solar resurrection via union with Re. The **[[phase-1-002-pyramid-texts]]** inscribed in Unas's pyramid at Saqqara (~2375 BCE) are the surviving textual record of this theology.

**[[sphinx-of-giza]]**: The Sphinx, carved from the same bedrock, sits at the eastern foot of the Giza Plateau. Whether the Sphinx was carved by Khafre (conventional view) or predates the 4th Dynasty (Schoch's geological hypothesis) is one of the most contested questions in Egyptology — with enormous implications for the timeline of Egyptian civilization.
