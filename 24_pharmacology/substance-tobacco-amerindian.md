# Sacred tobacco (Amerindian ritual)

The Americas-domesticated genus *Nicotiana* — particularly the high-nicotine *Nicotiana rustica* — was almost universally a ritual substance across pre-Columbian Native American societies, from northern South America through Mesoamerica and the Caribbean into the boreal North. The post-Columbian global diffusion of low-dose commercial *N. tabacum* is a derivative cultural form; the original Amerindian frame is shamanic, divinatory, and ceremonial-pipe, with doses and methods that produce frankly altered states.

## High-dose Amazonian shamanism

Wilbert's 1987 monograph established that Warao and other Amazonian tobacco-shamans (*tabaqueros*) consume *mapacho* (*N. rustica*) cigars at doses orders of magnitude above contemporary recreational use — the resulting nicotine plasma levels can approach pharmacologically lethal levels in habituated practitioners. The pharmacology produces frank visionary, dissociative, and oneiric states; tobacco-shamanism is not a side-practice but a principal continuous practice across the Amazon basin, often paired with [[substance-ayahuasca]] or used independently.

## The North American pipe

The calumet pipe ceremony of the plains and woodland traditions — the seven-direction offerings, the smoke as conveyor of prayer to the spirits — is the most-documented North American sacred-tobacco frame. The Lakota *čhaŋnúŋpa* is the canonical instance; the pipe is the ritual instrument by which a community speaks to the more-than-human world.

## Antiquity

Tushingham et al. 2018 reported direct chemical evidence of tobacco residue (nicotine + cotinine) inside a stone pipe from a Snake River archaeological context, radiocarbon-dated to ~12,300 cal BP — pushing North American sacred tobacco use to the Pleistocene-Holocene transition, making it one of the oldest dated entheogen-substrate uses in human archaeology.

## Cross-tradition connections

- **[[substance-ayahuasca]]**: commonly paired in Amazonian practice — tobacco serves as the *master plant* in many vegetalista lineages.
- **[[substance-peyote]]**: NAC ritual integrates tobacco smoking (in pipe form) alongside peyote sacramental use.
