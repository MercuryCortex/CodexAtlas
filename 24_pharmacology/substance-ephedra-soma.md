# Ephedra (the soma / haoma candidate)

Not the mythic drink but the **botanical identity** most scholars now assign to it. This node holds the *plant* and the identification argument; the deified drink lives at [[substance-soma]] / [[substance-haoma]].

## Why ephedra leads

- **The living ritual.** Zoroastrian priests still press **haoma** (*parahaoma*) from **ephedra** (*hōm*) in the Yasna — an unbroken line of practice. Whatever haoma was, this is what its heirs use; and soma = haoma by Indo-Iranian descent ([[language-proto-indo-iranian]]).
- **The pharmacology fits a stimulant rite.** Ephedra yields **ephedrine**, a genuine stimulant (the same plant is Chinese *má-huáng*). This matches a reading of the soma-ecstasy as energizing/wakeful rather than visionary.
- **Linguistic + geographic fit.** Falk (1989) argues ephedra best matches the texts' mountain origins and the Avestan descriptions.

## Why it's not settled

Ephedra produces no hallucinations, so it cannot explain readings of the *Rigveda*'s soma as a visionary psychedelic — the opening Wasson exploited for the **fly-agaric** ([[substance-fly-agaric]]) thesis, with Syrian rue (Peganum) and cannabis as further candidates. The stimulant-vs-hallucinogen split is the live crux, and it bears directly on [[substance-entheogen-hypothesis]]: if soma was merely ephedra, the founding case of the entheogen program loses its strongest form.
