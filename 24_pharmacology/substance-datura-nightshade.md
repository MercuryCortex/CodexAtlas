# Datura

A sacred plant of **both hemispheres** — and the vault's clearest case of a *deliriant* rather than a psychedelic: genuinely dangerous, producing delirium and amnesia rather than lucid vision.

## The two-hemisphere record

- **India.** **Dhatūra** — flowers and seeds offered to [[shiva]], bound to his ascetic, poison-holding aspect.
- **The Americas.** Aztec **toloache** and the Native-Californian datura-cult initiation ([[tradition-mesoamerican]] and beyond) — a plant of ordeal, vision-quest, and rite-of-passage.
- **Old-World nightshade lore.** Datura joins henbane, belladonna, and mandrake in the European witches' nightshade complex (see [[substance-henbane-witches]]).

## The category caveat

Datura's active principles are **tropane alkaloids** (scopolamine, hyoscyamine, atropine) — anticholinergic **deliriants**. The experience is true delirium: disorientation, waking hallucination indistinguishable from reality, amnesia — and the therapeutic-to-toxic margin is narrow enough to kill. This is why the node files datura *apart* from the classic serotonergic psychedelics and treats the [[substance-entheogen-hypothesis]] category with care: "sacred psychoactive" spans pharmacologies that are not remotely alike.
