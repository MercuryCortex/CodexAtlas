# The Sacred Coca Leaf

**Mama Coca** — the Andes' central plant-sacrament for four millennia, and the clearest case of a *sacred stimulant of reciprocity* rather than of vision.

## The sacrament

- **The k'intu.** Three perfect leaves, held together, breathed over (the *phukuy*), and offered to [[pachamama]] and the **apus** (mountain-lords) before chewing — the elementary act of Andean reciprocity (*ayni*).
- **Chewed with lime.** The leaf is chewed with an alkaline *llipta/tocra* (lime or ash) that releases its mild stimulant alkaloids — sustaining work at altitude, blunting hunger and cold.
- **Divination + healing.** Coca leaves are cast and read; the *curandero* uses coca in the healing mesa.

## Coca is not cocaine

The node insists on the distinction the modern drug-war erased: the whole leaf, chewed, is a mild sacred stimulant embedded in reciprocity and landscape — categorically unlike the isolated, concentrated alkaloid **cocaine**. This is why coca belongs beside [[substance-tobacco-amerindian]] (offering/divination) and *apart* from the visionary [[substance-ayahuasca]].
