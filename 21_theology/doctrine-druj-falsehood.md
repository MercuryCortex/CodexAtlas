# Druj (Zoroastrian Doctrine of Cosmic Falsehood / the Lie)

The foundational cosmic-anti-principle of Zoroastrianism: **Druj is the cosmic Lie, the cosmic Disorder, the cosmic anti-Truth** — the cosmic-counter-principle to [[doctrine-asha-cosmic-order|asha]] (Cosmic Truth-and-Order). The cosmic-dualism structuring all Zoroastrian theology, ethics, and ritual is the **asha-druj cosmic axis**. Every act of truthfulness, ritual-purity, and cosmic-order-maintenance strengthens Asha; every act of falsehood, pollution, and disorder strengthens Druj.

## The Gathic core
- **Yasna 30.4-5**: at cosmic origin, the two primordial spirits ([[spenta-mainyu]] + [[angra-mainyu-ahriman]]) made a free-cosmic-choice; the wicked spirit chose druj. The cosmic-dualism is constituted by this original-choice.
- Druj is named throughout the Gathas as the cosmic-counter-principle to asha.
- The Gathic ethical demand: humans must align thought-word-deed with asha against druj. Wrong-thought, wrong-word, wrong-deed strengthen druj.

## Doctrine + deity
Like asha, druj is **simultaneously** an abstract cosmic-principle (the cosmic Lie) AND a personified-divine-being (the daevic-class divine being [[druj]]). The personified druj is one of the principal daevas opposing the Amesha Spentas + yazatas.

## The Achaemenid political articulation
**Darius I** and his successors articulated the cosmic-doctrine in political-theological inscriptions:
- "Ahura Mazda is the great god... who has placed me on this throne. By the grace of Ahura Mazda I am king."
- "The Lie (drauga) had been increasing in the country... I crushed the Lie. I made the people steadfast on the path of truth (arta)."

The Achaemenid royal-religious vocabulary thus makes **arta vs. drauga = truth vs. lie** the cosmic-political organizing principle. The Old Persian inscriptional formulation propagates the cosmic-dualism across the Achaemenid empire.

## MASSIVE-WIN: druj = druh- (PII cognate)
The Avestan **druj / drug** and Vedic **druh-** are direct etymological cognates from **Proto-Indo-Iranian *\*dhruj-* < Proto-Indo-European *\*dhrewgh-*** ("to harm by deception, to lie"). The cognate extends across Indo-European (Old English *dreogan* "to suffer / commit deceit"; Old Norse *draugr* "ghost / undead"). The PII Lie-as-cosmic-disorder concept goes back to at least -2000 — predating the Vedic-Avestan religious-reform split (Watkins 1995, West 2007 ch. 3).

## MASSIVE-WIN: druj → Christian Satan
The Persian-period transmission of cosmic-evil-as-principled-anti-cosmic-Lie contributes structurally to the Second-Temple Jewish transformation of Satan from "adversary" (Job, Zechariah — a heavenly-courtroom functionary) into "cosmic adversary" (1 Enoch, Qumran, NT — the cosmic-counter-principle to God). Druj is **one of the cosmic-falsehood-principle source-templates** for the Christian Satan = "father of lies" (John 8:44).

Russell 1977 *The Devil* + Forsyth 1987 *The Old Enemy* + Boyce 1991 vol. 3 document the structural inheritance chain.

## Disputes
- **Direct vs. structural transmission.** Whether the druj → Christian-Satan inheritance is direct verbal-doctrinal transmission or structural-conceptual influence is debated.
- **Druj-personification vs. principle.** Whether the personified daevic-druj is a Gathic or Younger-Avestan development is contested; the Gathic stratum favors principle-with-implicit-personification.
