# Saoshyant (Zoroastrian Doctrine of the Future Savior)

The Zoroastrian doctrine of the **three end-time saviors who execute the cosmic renovation**. The Avestan *saošyaṇt* literally means "**one who will benefit**" — the future-tense participle of the verb *sav-* ("to benefit / to save"). In the developed Zoroastrian eschatology, three Saoshyants will appear at successive millennia from Zoroaster's preserved seed; the third and final, **Astvat-ereta** ("embodied righteousness"), will execute the [[doctrine-frashokereti]] (cosmic renovation) — defeating Angra Mainyu, resurrecting the dead, and restoring the cosmos to perfection.

## The Gathic seed
The term *saoshyant* appears already in the Gathas (-1200 to -1000) — but in a **plural-collective sense**: "those who will benefit" the cosmos through their righteous-deeds. Yasna 48.9 + 53.2 are the principal Gathic occurrences. The Gathic-Zarathustra envisions a collective of righteous-helpers participating in the cosmic-redemption; the eschatological-personalization into "the singular savior" is later (Younger Avestan + Pahlavi).

## The Younger Avestan development
By the Younger Avesta (~-700 to -300), the singular eschatological-Saoshyant has crystallized:

- **Yašt 13.128-129** (Frawardīn Yašt) names the *fravashis* (pre-existent souls) of the three eschatological Saoshyants — **Ukhshyat-ereta** (the one who makes righteousness grow), **Ukhshyat-nemah** (the one who makes reverence grow), **Astvat-ereta** (embodied righteousness).
- **Yašt 19.88-96** (Zamyād Yašt) describes Astvat-ereta as the final Saoshyant who **resurrects the dead, executes the Frashokereti, and defeats Angra Mainyu**.

## The Pahlavi-period systematization
The Greater Bundahishn (ch. 33-34) + Denkard Book 7 articulate the developed doctrine:

1. **Zoroaster's seed is preserved miraculously** in Lake Kąsaoya (later identified with Lake Hāmūn in Sistan).
2. **At each successive millennium**, a virgin bathes in the lake, conceives by Zoroaster's preserved seed, and bears a Saoshyant.
3. **Ukhshyat-ereta** is born 1000 years after Zoroaster's death.
4. **Ukhshyat-nemah** is born 2000 years after.
5. **Astvat-ereta**, the final Saoshyant, is born 3000 years after — the end of the fourth 3000-year period of cosmic-history.
6. **Astvat-ereta executes the Frashokereti**: defeats Angra Mainyu, resurrects all the dead, leads them through the molten-metal river of judgment, and inaugurates the renovated cosmos.

## MASSIVE-WIN 1: Saoshyant → Jewish-Christian Messiah
The Saoshyant template — **preserved seed of the founding prophet awaits the cosmic crisis, then is born to execute the cosmic renovation** — is the cleanest structural-source for the Second-Temple Jewish + early-Christian **messianic-future-savior expectation**. Key features:

- **Personalized eschatological savior** (vs. the diffuse Hebrew Bible *mashiach* = "anointed one" = generally human-king or priest).
- **Tied to cosmic-renovation event** (vs. Hebrew Bible's primarily-historical mashiach contexts).
- **Born of miraculous-conception from preserved-prophet-seed** (cf. Christian virgin-birth-of-Jesus = preserved-Davidic-seed structural inheritance).
- **Returns at history's end to defeat the cosmic adversary** (cf. Christian Second-Coming of Christ).

The Avestan formulation predates the Jewish-Christian formulation by centuries. The Achaemenid-period (-539 to -332) cultural-contact window makes substantive transmission plausible. The Persian-period Jewish apocalyptic literature (1 Enoch, Daniel, Qumran Messianic Rule, Psalms of Solomon 17) carries clear features of the Iranian template.

## MASSIVE-WIN 2: Saoshyant → Twelver Shi'a Hidden Imam
The **most-direct single inheritance of the Saoshyant template** in the post-Zoroastrian world is the **Twelver Shi'a Hidden-Imam doctrine**. The 12th imam, **Muhammad al-Mahdi**, entered **occultation** in 874 CE and awaits cosmic-crisis to return + execute the final eschaton. This doctrine **crystallized in 9th-10th century majority-Zoroastrian Iran** where the Saoshyant template was culturally available. Structural features inherited:

- **Preserved-prophet-figure in occultation** (Saoshyants in the lake; Hidden Imam in occultation).
- **Awaiting cosmic-end** to return.
- **Returns to execute final cosmic-renovation + justice**.

(Amir-Moezzi 1994 *The Divine Guide in Early Shi'ism*; Sachedina 1981 *Islamic Messianism*; Gnoli 1987 EIr 'Zoroastrianism and Islam'.) This is the cleanest single Iranian-to-Islamic eschatological inheritance documented.

## Disputes
- **Gathic personalization scope.** Whether the Gathic *saoshyant* is already personalized-singular or only plural-collective is debated; Boyce 1975 favors plural-collective with implicit future-singular, the Younger Avesta crystallizes the singular.
- **Persian-Jewish transmission directness.** Direct lexical borrowing is not demonstrable; structural inheritance is the standard position (Cohn 1993, Hultgård 2000).
- **Shi'a inheritance directness.** Some Islamic-Studies scholarship resists the directness of Saoshyant → Hidden Imam (cf. internal Islamic-jurisprudential development from earlier Mahdi-vocabulary); Amir-Moezzi 1994 makes the structural-inheritance case forcefully.
