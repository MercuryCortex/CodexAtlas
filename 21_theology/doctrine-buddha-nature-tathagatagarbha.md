# Tathāgatagarbha — The Buddha-Nature Doctrine

The Mahāyāna Buddhist doctrine that **all sentient beings possess the seed/embryo/womb (garbha) of buddhahood** (*tathāgata*) — i.e., the potential to attain full enlightenment is intrinsic, not acquired. Foundational sūtras: *Tathāgatagarbha-sūtra*, *Śrīmālādevī-sūtra*, *Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra* (Mahāyāna recension), *Laṅkāvatāra-sūtra*. Systematized in the *Ratnagotravibhāga* (*Uttaratantra Śāstra*, ~5th c., attributed to Maitreya/Asanga). The doctrine became central to East Asian Buddhism (Chan/Zen + Tiantai + Huayan + Pure Land) and to the Tibetan Shentong + Rangtong debates. Comparable to (and a major source of) the [[non-duality]] cluster of Mahāyāna doctrines.
