# Asha (Zoroastrian Doctrine of Cosmic Order / Truth)

The foundational structuring doctrine of Zoroastrianism: **the cosmos is structured by Truth-and-Order (asha), and the human task is to align with asha against its cosmic opposite, the Lie ([[doctrine-druj-falsehood|druj]])**. Asha is **both** an abstract cosmic-metaphysical principle AND a personified divine being (the first Amesha Spenta, [[asha-vahishta]]). It is the **most-frequently-named concept in the Gathas** — the Gathic-Zarathustra's entire theological program centers on the Asha-Truth vs. Druj-Lie cosmic opposition.

## The Gathic core
- **Yasna 30.4** — the canonical formulation: at cosmic origin, the two primordial spirits ([[spenta-mainyu]] + [[angra-mainyu-ahriman]]) made a free-cosmic-choice between asha + druj. The choice constituted the cosmic-dualism that structures all existence.
- Asha appears in approximately every other Gathic stanza, in both abstract-principle and personified-deity forms.
- The Gathic ethical demand: humans must align thought + word + deed with asha. The threefold formula *humata-hūxta-huvaršta* ("good thoughts, good words, good deeds") is asha-aligned action.

## Doctrine + deity in one
A theologically-distinctive feature: asha is **simultaneously** an abstract cosmic-principle (cosmic Truth-and-Order) AND a personified-divine-being (the first Amesha Spenta, [[asha-vahishta]]). The Zoroastrian theological position is that **the cosmic principle of Truth IS the divine being** — the double-aspect is not contradiction but the deepest unity of the system. This pattern propagates to all the Amesha Spentas + many yazatas (Mithra = the cosmic-contract-principle AND the deity; Sraosha = the divine-word-hearkening-principle AND the deity).

## MASSIVE-WIN: asha = ṛta (PII cognate)
The Avestan **aṣ̌a** and Vedic **ṛta** are direct etymological cognates from **Proto-Indo-Iranian *\*ŕ̥ta-*** < **Proto-Indo-European *\*h₂értus / *\*h₂r̥tó-*** ("the right cosmic order; the truth that the cosmos is set into"):

- **Both are cosmic-principle and divine-being simultaneously.**
- **Both are the foundational cosmic Truth-and-Order.**
- **Both are opposed to a "lie/disorder" principle** (Druj in Iran; *Anṛta* in India).
- **Both have fire as material manifestation** in their respective ritual systems (Vedic Agni is closely associated with Ṛta; Atar [[atar]] is closely associated with Asha).
- **Both are the cosmic-meta-principle that structures all theological-ethical thought.**

This is **the single most-load-bearing concept in the entire comparative Indo-Iranian religious-reconstruction corpus** (Schlerath 1968, West 2007 ch. 3, Watkins 1995). The Asha=Ṛta cosmic-Truth concept survives the Vedic-Avestan religious-reform intact, attesting to its PII depth.

## MASSIVE-WIN: asha → cosmic-truth-and-order template
The Avestan asha is the cleanest documented branch of a broader cross-tradition cosmic-truth-and-order template that includes:

- **Egyptian Maat** (cosmic-order + truth + justice, personified-feminine; Old Kingdom onward).
- **Greek Themis** (cosmic-order + divine-law, personified-feminine; Hesiod *Theogony* onward).
- **Hebrew Chokmah / cosmic Wisdom** (Proverbs 8, Sirach 24; the cosmic-organizing-principle of God).
- **Vedic Ṛta** (the cosmic-truth-and-order; cognate of asha).
- **Chinese Dao** (the cosmic-way; cosmic-organizing-principle).

Whether by **convergent religious-imagination**, **PIE substrate**, **Egyptian-Near-Eastern transmission**, or **Persian-period influence** on Hellenistic-Jewish Wisdom-literature, the convergence is documented (Boyce 1991, Hultgård 1998, West 2007).

## Persian-period transmission: asha → Second-Temple Jewish *tzedek*
The Persian-period Jewish religious-vocabulary transformation (under Achaemenid rule, -539 to -332) substantially expands the Hebrew vocabulary of **tzedek (righteousness, justice)** and **tzedaqah (righteous-action)** into a more-cosmic-metaphysical-principle-resembling-asha. The shift is documented in Second Isaiah, the Psalms, and especially the Qumran scrolls (the *Yahad* community's *tzedek*-vocabulary is extensively cosmic-dualistic, with explicit Two-Spirits theology paralleling Yasna 30 — see 1QS). The structural parallel is real and probably substantially-influenced (Boyce 1991, Berquist 1995).

## Disputes
- **Gathic personification.** Whether the Gathic stratum treats asha primarily as (a) abstract cosmic-principle or (b) personified divine-being is debated. Most scholars (Boyce 1975, Skjærvø 1987) treat the Gathic asha as both-simultaneously; the systematization into Amesha-Spenta-as-divine-person is more clearly Younger-Avestan.
- **Persian → Jewish *tzedek* transmission scope.** How much of the Second-Temple Jewish expansion of *tzedek*-vocabulary is Persian-influenced vs. internal Hebrew-Bible-development is debated. Boyce 1991 + Berquist 1995 favor substantial Persian influence; conservative scholarship resists.
