# Frashokereti (Zoroastrian Doctrine of Cosmic Renovation)

The foundational eschatological doctrine of Zoroastrianism: the **renovation of the existence at the end of cosmic time**. The Avestan *Frašōkərəti* (Pahlavi *Frašegird*) literally means "**making wonderful**" — at the appointed end, Ahura Mazda + the Amesha Spentas + the [[saoshyant]] (three end-time saviors) defeat Angra Mainyu, the dead are resurrected bodily, all humanity passes through a river of molten metal that purifies the righteous and consumes the wicked, and the cosmos is **made wonderful** — restored to the pristine state Ahura Mazda originally intended.

## The Gathic seed
The doctrine appears already in the Gathas (-1200 to -1000), the oldest stratum of Avestan religious literature:

- **Yasna 30.9**: "May we be those who make this existence *frasha* (wonderful)."
- **Yasna 34.15**: "The renovation of the existence shall be brought about."
- **Yasna 48.12**: "Those by whose deeds the existence advances toward Truth shall make it wonderful."

The Gathic-Zarathustra's theological program is already eschatologically-oriented: the cosmic-history has a goal, and the righteous human's task is to participate in the bringing-about of that goal. The cosmos is moving from creation through cosmic-mixture-with-evil toward final-renovation.

## The Pahlavi-period systematic articulation
The full Frashokereti cosmology is most-systematically articulated in the **Greater Bundahishn** (Pahlavi, ~9th-10th c. CE compilation of older material), the **Pahlavi Rivayat**, and **Denkard Books 7-9**:

1. **Cosmic history is 12,000 years**, divided into four 3,000-year periods.
2. The first 3,000 years: pure spiritual existence.
3. The second 3,000 years: spiritual creation manifests material existence; Angra Mainyu attacks; cosmic-mixture begins.
4. The third 3,000 years: the cosmic-struggle, history as we know it; Zarathustra is born at the start of the fourth period.
5. The fourth 3,000 years: cosmic-struggle continues; at successive millennia three saviors appear from Zarathustra's preserved seed — **Ukhshyat-ereta**, **Ukhshyat-nemah**, and **Astvat-ereta** (the final [[saoshyant]]).
6. At the end of the fourth period: the final battle; the dead are resurrected; the molten-metal river of judgment; the cosmos is renovated.

## MASSIVE-WIN: Frashokereti and Western eschatology
Cohn 1993 (*Cosmos, Chaos and the World to Come*) is the principal modern monograph documenting the Iranian-source-Jewish-Christian-Islamic-eschatology inheritance chain. The Frashokereti is **the structural source of four load-bearing doctrines** that emerge in Second-Temple Judaism (~300 BCE - 100 CE) and propagate into Christianity + Islam:

1. **Cosmic dualism**: history is the battle of two cosmic principles (good vs. evil).
2. **Final judgment at history's end**: all the dead face cosmic judgment.
3. **Bodily resurrection of the dead** (Daniel 12:2, 2 Maccabees 7, 1 Corinthians 15).
4. **Eschatological cosmic-kingdom of God** restored at history's end.

The Avestan formulations predate the Jewish formulations by centuries; the Achaemenid-period (-539 to -332) cultural-contact window makes substantive transmission plausible. The Jewish apocalyptic literature emerging from this window (1 Enoch, Daniel, Qumran scrolls) carries clear Iranian-eschatological-vocabulary features.

This is **arguably the most-consequential single doctrine in the history of Western religious eschatology** that did not originate in the Hebrew Bible.

## The Saoshyant mechanism
The Frashokereti is executed by the **[[saoshyant]]** (literally "future benefactor / coming savior") — three end-time saviors born from Zoroaster's preserved seed. The third and final, **Astvat-ereta** ("embodied righteousness"), executes the final cosmic-renovation. This template is the structural-source of:

- The **Jewish-Christian Messiah** (Davidic-king-as-cosmic-redeemer at history's end).
- The **Christian Second Coming of Christ** (preserved-resurrected-savior returning at the eschaton).
- The **Twelver Shi'a Hidden Imam** (the preserved-imam awaiting cosmic-crisis to return).

## Disputes
- **Direct vs. structural transmission.** Whether Persian-period transmission was verbal-doctrinal-borrowing or structural-conceptual-influence is debated. Boyce + Cohn + Hultgård favor substantial transmission; some Jewish-Studies scholarship resists.
- **Avestan stratum dating.** The Frashokereti's full articulation is Pahlavi-period (Bundahishn); the Gathic seed-form is older but contested in scope. The chronological gap raises questions about which formulation Jewish-period Persians actually transmitted.
- **Norse Ragnarök relationship.** Whether Frashokereti + Ragnarök share a deeper PIE cosmic-eschatology substrate or are independently developed is contested (Hultgård 2000 vs. West 2007).
