# Frashokereti

The Zoroastrian doctrine of the **final renewal of the world** — the eschatological event at which Angra Mainyu / Ahriman is finally defeated, the dead are raised in their bodies, the molten-metal ordeal purifies all souls, and the cosmos is restored to perfect goodness. Mediated by the eschatological saviour-figure Saoshyant. One of the earliest articulated linear-eschatological doctrines in world religion + a principal background for the apocalyptic traditions of Second-Temple Judaism, Christianity, and Islam (Hultgård 1998).
