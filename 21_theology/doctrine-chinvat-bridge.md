# Chinvat Bridge

Zoroastrian eschatological doctrine of the **bridge of judgment** that every soul must cross on the fourth day after death. For the righteous, the bridge widens into a path; for the wicked, it narrows to a knife-edge and the soul falls into the abyss. Presided over by [[mithra]], [[sraosha]], and Rashnu as the judging trio. Influential template for the eschatological bridge-crossing motif in Islamic *Ṣirāṭ*, certain Jewish + Christian apocalyptic visions, and Dante's threshold geography.
