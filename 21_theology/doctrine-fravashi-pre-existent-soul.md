# Fravashi (Zoroastrian Doctrine of the Pre-Existent Soul)

The Zoroastrian doctrine of the **fravashi** (Avestan *fravaši*, Pahlavi *frawahr*, modern Persian *faravahar*): a **pre-existent guardian-soul** that exists eternally as the **cosmic-prototype of the individual** + accompanies the individual through life + cosmic-history. The fravashi is one of the four-to-five soul-parts in the developed Zoroastrian soul-anthropology + a key cosmological agent in the creation + cosmic-renovation narratives.

## The Farvardin Yašt — the principal source

**Yašt 13** (the Farvardin Yašt, Younger Avesta) is the principal hymn dedicated to the fravashis: **158 stanzas** invoking the fravashis of:

- **Ahura Mazda himself + the Amesha Spentas + the great yazatas** (vv. 1-29).
- **The primordial righteous figures** — Gayōmard (the cosmic-first-man), Yima/Jamshid, Thraētaona-Feridun, Kavi Vishtaspa (Zarathustra's royal-patron), Zarathustra himself (vv. 87-110).
- **The eschatological righteous figures** — the three Saoshyants (vv. 128-129).
- **All righteous Zoroastrian humans** — past, present, and future (vv. 130-158).

The Farvardin Yašt is one of the most-significant theological documents of the Younger Avesta + the principal liturgical resource for the contemporary **Fravardigan festival** (the 10 last days of the Iranian year + the first 5 days of Farvardin — the period when the fravashis of the dead return to visit their living families).

## The multi-part soul

The developed Zoroastrian soul-anthropology distinguishes **four-to-five soul-parts**:

1. **Fravashi (*frawahr*)**: the pre-existent cosmic-prototype + guardian-spirit. Eternal; pre-existent to embodiment; present throughout life as guardian; returns to its cosmic-home at death + at the cosmic-renovation.
2. **Daēnā ([[doctrine-daena-zoroastrian]])**: the conscience-and-religion-soul. Encountered post-mortem at the [[cinvat-bridge-judgment|Chinvat Bridge]] as a beautiful or hideous maiden depending on the soul's righteousness.
3. **Ruvān**: the operational soul during life.
4. **Bōy**: consciousness.
5. **Ahu**: the vital-spirit / life-force.

The fravashi + the daēnā are the **two cosmologically-most-significant** soul-parts.

## Cosmological role

The fravashi has multiple cosmological roles:

- **Pre-existence**: each individual's fravashi exists eternally as the cosmic-prototype of that individual. The historical individual is the entry-into-time of the eternal fravashi.
- **Guardian during life**: the fravashi protects + accompanies + guides the historical individual.
- **Creation-agency**: the Greater Bundahishn (ch. 1-3) describes the fravashis offering themselves to Ahura Mazda for incarnation into the historical-cosmos as cosmic-agents against Angra Mainyu. The fravashi-host is the cosmic-army of Ahura Mazda.
- **Eschatological agency**: at the [[doctrine-frashokereti|Frashokereti]], the fravashis of all the righteous participate in the cosmic-restoration. The three Saoshyant-fravashis (Yašt 13.128-129) execute the renovation.

## The faravahar iconography

The contemporary Zoroastrian iconographic symbol — the **winged-disc-with-figure** that is also the principal Iranian-cultural-identity emblem — is identified by modern Zoroastrian + Iranian tradition as the **iconographic representation of the fravashi** (though scholarship debates whether the ancient symbol originally represented the *khvarenah* [royal-divine-glory] vs. the *fravashi*; both interpretations have ancient roots). The modern identification is overwhelmingly fravashi-centered. The symbol propagates downstream into Iranian Pahlavi-era + contemporary national symbolism.

## MASSIVE-WIN: fravashi → Logos/pre-existent-Christ?

The fravashi-as-pre-existent-cosmological-prototype-that-enters-historical-incarnation has **structural-parallel** to the Christian Logos-doctrine (the pre-existent divine-prototype enters historical incarnation in Christ — John 1:1-14) + to the Platonic Forms-and-incarnation framework. Whether the structural-influence runs through Persian-Hellenistic mediation is debated; the structural-parallel is real (Hintze 2000; Sundermann 2000 'The Avestan Daena').

The Logos-pre-existence template has multiple potential PII + Platonic-Iranian source-channels; the fravashi is **one of the cleaner Persian-source-candidates** for the cosmological-template the developing Christian Logos-doctrine inherits.
