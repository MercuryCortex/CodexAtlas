The harmonic series is the physical phenomenon that the entire music-cosmos tradition was tracking. Every vibrating string, column of air, or resonating body naturally produces not one tone but a series: the fundamental frequency plus overtones at integer multiples of that frequency (2x, 3x, 4x, 5x...). The ratio relationships between these overtones produce, in ascending order: octave (2:1), perfect fifth (3:2), perfect fourth (4:3), major third (5:4), minor third (6:5). These are the same intervals Pythagoras derived from his monochord experiments around 550 BCE — not because he invented them, but because he discovered a mathematical structure already encoded in every vibrating body.

The critical implication: the harmonic series is not culturally constructed. It is a physical property of vibration that operates at every scale where matter vibrates — from a plucked string to the oscillation of atoms. When Vedic texts claim the universe IS sound (Nada Brahma), when Pythagoras claims cosmic structure is musical proportion, when Marcus the Magician maps 24 Greek letters to 24 musical intervals, they are each — from different cultural directions — arriving at a genuine physical fact: the mathematical structure of the harmonic series is not imposed on nature by human musical convention. It is discovered in nature by human musical practice.

The Tuvan throat-singing tradition (khoomei) is the most direct demonstration of this. A single Tuvan singer simultaneously produces the drone fundamental AND distinct overtone melodies above it by shaping the oral cavity to amplify specific harmonics. The overtones the singer isolates — the octave, the fifth, the octave-and-fifth — are not culturally chosen notes. They are the same overtones that appear in every vibrating body everywhere. The singer is not doing music theory. They are doing acoustic physics with the human voice. The intervals they select are already present in the sound they produce; they are only made audible. This is why the Tuvans describe khoomei as "singing with the mountains and wind" — the natural world produces the same overtone structure, and the voice joins rather than creates it.

The modern scientific picture: the harmonic series appears in planetary orbital resonances (Jupiter and Saturn near 5:2, Neptune and Uranus near 2:1, Pluto and Neptune at exactly 3:2), in the spectral lines of hydrogen (the Balmer series ratios approximate musical intervals), and in the self-similar structure of snowflakes, coastlines, and spiral galaxies (fractal geometry is fundamentally about ratio repetition at multiple scales — the same principle as the harmonic series). This does not mean the planets produce audible music. It means that certain ratio structures recur across different physical domains because they are stable equilibrium states. The music-cosmos intuition — that music and cosmos share mathematical structure — may not be mysticism. It may be imprecise physics.
