# 31_consciousness — Consciousness as a lens onto religion

*Lens skeleton seeded 2026-05-24. First figure: Carl Gustav Jung (see `figures/jung-analytical-psychology.md`). Full lens design at `AUDIT/consciousness-lens-spec-2026-05-23.md` — awaiting John's per-item §10 checklist greenlight before bulk content batches land.*

This lens holds the intersection of cognitive science, contemplative neuroscience, mysticism studies (Stace/Katz/Forman debate), and the empirical study of religious / mystical experience.

**Distinct from `29_philosophy/`** — philosophy is conceptual / analytic; consciousness is empirical-meets-philosophical with neuroscience, comparative psychology, and contemplative-traditions inputs.

## Three primary research streams (per audit §1–§2)

1. **Cognitive Science of Religion (CSR)** — Boyer · Barrett · Whitehouse · Norenzayan · McCauley · Atran. T1 mainstream.
2. **Pure-consciousness studies / philosophy of mysticism (empirical side)** — Stace · Katz (contextualist) · Forman (perennialist) · Hood · Proudfoot · Jones. T1 with care; the perennialist↔contextualist axis is live.
3. **Contemplative neuroscience** — Varela · Davidson · Lutz · Brewer · Newberg (boundary T1/T2) · Carhart-Harris (psychedelic-mysticism). T1 emerging field.

- **CON-1 — Perennialist ↔ contextualist symmetric pairing.** Every Forman/Stace pure-consciousness-event claim ships with a Katz/Proudfoot counter in the same batch. The debate is live; the vault never sides.
- **CON-2 — Neuroscience-finding ≠ popular-extrapolation.** Carhart-Harris DMN-suppression-during-psilocybin (T1) ≠ "psychedelics prove mysticism" (T2-T3 popular reception). Nodes carrying both should split into two sections internally.
