# Jung — analytical psychology

*The school constituted by Carl Gustav Jung (1875–1961) and his successors. This node represents the corpus of theory + clinical practice + institutional infrastructure. The biographical individual is at [[carl-gustav-jung]] (`04_persons/`). The CON-2 split-node convention from the consciousness-lens audit applies: a single historical figure can be a `consciousness-figure` lens-node AND a `person` biographical node — different ontology positions, different cross-link surfaces.*

## What the school carries

- **Collective unconscious** — proposed psychic substrate shared cross-culturally (1916 onward; canonical statement in *The Archetypes and the Collective Unconscious*, CW 9.i, 1959).
- **Archetypes** — universal structural patterns of psychic experience (the Hero, the Wise Old Man, the Shadow, the Anima/Animus, the Self). Read mythologically across cultures.
- **Individuation** — lifelong process of integrating unconscious material into a coherent Self.
- **Synchronicity** — "meaningful coincidence" hypothesis, formalized with Wolfgang Pauli (Jung & Pauli 1952 *The Interpretation of Nature and the Psyche*).
- **Psychology and alchemy** — Jung's late-period reading of alchemical symbolism as projected individuation imagery (*Psychology and Alchemy* 1944; *Mysterium Coniunctionis* 1955-56). Cross-link to [[alchemy-corpus-hermeticum]] / [[alchemy-rosarium-philosophorum]] for the texts Jung re-read.
- **Psychology and religion** — analytic engagement with Christianity, Gnosticism, Eastern traditions; the most contested is *Answer to Job* (1952).

## Why T2 — the tier-discipline call

The audit's CON-2 rule: distinguish neuroscience-finding from popular-extrapolation. Jung's claims sit in three layers, each with its own tier:

| Layer | Tier | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Jung's HISTORICAL IMPACT + INSTITUTIONAL FOOTPRINT | T1 | Universally documented; Bollingen / Princeton archives; standard biography canon |
| Jung's TYPOLOGY as a CLINICAL HEURISTIC | T1 | Hood's *Mysticism Scale* uses it; Jungian institutions maintain clinical practice; M-Scale has 40+ years empirical follow-up |
| Jung's METAPHYSICAL CLAIMS (collective unconscious as real entity, synchronicity as causal-acausal principle, archetypes as ontologically primitive) | **T2** | Not falsifiable in the Popperian sense (Eysenck 1985); mainstream cognitive psychology does not endorse; contested but defended in religious-studies / depth-psychology academic communities |

This NODE represents the SCHOOL, which sits at the intersection — T2 is the honest center of mass.

## Paired counter per CON-1

Per the consciousness-lens audit's three-rule neutrality discipline (§4), every contested-claim node ships with its mainstream counter in the same batch. For Jung the counter is **Noll 1994 *The Jung Cult***. Noll argues:

> The Jungian movement of 1916-1925 operated as a charismatic-quasi-religious movement structured around Jung as prophet, with the Zürich Psychological Club functioning as an initiatory community rather than a scientific society. The "scientific psychiatry" framing was institutional cover for a religious-mythological program.

Noll is contested by **Shamdasani 1998 *Cult Fictions* + 2003 *Jung and the Making of Modern Psychology*** — the standard Jungian-institutional rebuttal. Shamdasani argues Noll over-reads early-period intra-clinical dynamics + misrepresents the 1925 seminars; the scientific-clinical wing of Jungian institutions has been a genuine professional development of psychology, not crypto-religion.

The vault treats this as a **live academic dispute**, not settled. Both sides are at T1 tier (peer-reviewed, major-publisher). The reader sees both framings.

## Investigation-as-prompt openings (CODEX §IV)

What Jung *noticed* that legitimate scholarship corroborates:

- **Cross-cultural recurrence of certain motifs** (hero-journey, dying-and-rising god, mandala, world-tree). The MOTIFS are documented (Eliade, Campbell — themselves contested per §VI rejection #1 Eliade-style telescope). The *interpretation* as evidence for a metaphysical collective unconscious is the contested layer.
- **The somatic + emotional dimensions of mystical experience** are real and now studied empirically (Hood M-Scale 40+ years; contemplative neuroscience as a field).
- **Dream-narrative as cognitive structure** has independent empirical support (Hobson activation-synthesis; Domhoff cognitive theory; threat-simulation theory).

Where legitimate scholarship corroborates the *underlying pattern*, the lens routes via `scholarly-parallel` edges. Where Jung-specific claims are the contested layer (collective unconscious as ontological entity), the routing is via `polemic-against` or notes-flagged `parallel-motif` per CODEX §IV's investigation-as-prompt discipline.
