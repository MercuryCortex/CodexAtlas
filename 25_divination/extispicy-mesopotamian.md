# Extispicy — Mesopotamian Entrails Divination

The 2,000-year Mesopotamian state-religion divinatory institution: ritual inspection of a sacrificed sheep's entrails (especially the liver) for omens, cross-referenced against canonical compendia. The longest-running organized divination institution in human history with documented textual continuity, operating from ~-2000 in Sumerian-Akkadian contexts through to the late-Babylonian and Hellenistic periods.

## The system

The diviner (Akkadian *bārû*) performed extispicy according to highly-formalized procedure:

1. **Question formulated** — typically a yes/no question of state interest (will the king succeed in the campaign? is this an auspicious day for action?)
2. **Sacrifice** — a sheep (sometimes other animals) ritually slaughtered with prayer
3. **Inspection** — the liver, lungs, intestines examined systematically. The liver was subdivided into named regions (each associated with specific gods); abnormalities (color, shape, markings, parasites) read as omens
4. **Cross-reference** — findings checked against the *Bārûtu* compendium (multi-tablet canonical text preserved from Ashurbanipal's library at Nineveh)
5. **Verdict** — yes/no answer + interpretive framework

The system was *expert-mediated* (only trained *bārû* could read), *state-institutional* (royal patronage), and *evidentiary-systematic* (the compendium accumulated centuries of correlations between observed omens + outcomes).

## MASSIVE-WIN — the Etruscan-Roman transmission

Mesopotamian extispicy is one of the cleanest documented divinatory-transmission chains in ancient world religious history. The technique passes:

**Mesopotamia (-2000+) → Hatti + Mari + Ugarit + Hittite (-1500-) → Etruria (-700-) → Roman haruspicy (-300- to +300 CE)**

The transmission is documented through:
- Liver-models (teaching-aids) found in Mesopotamian + Hittite + Etruscan + Italian contexts using compatible regional-division schemes
- The *Piacenza Liver* (~-200, Italian-Etruscan bronze sheep-liver model) preserves divisions cognate with the Mesopotamian *Bārûtu*
- Greco-Roman testimony (Cicero *De Divinatione*; Tacitus *Annals*) recognizes haruspicy as Etruscan in origin
- Furley & Gysembergh 2015 document the Mesopotamian → Greek transmission via Hellenistic period
- See [[haruspicy-etruscan-roman]] for the daughter-tradition

This is one of the longest documented technical-religious transmission chains in world history: ~1,700 years from Sumerian-Akkadian extispicy to Roman imperial haruspicy via a continuous chain of named cultural mediators. The structural similarity between the Mesopotamian and Etruscan liver-division schemes is too detailed to be coincidence; the transmission is real.

## Cross-tradition position

Extispicy anchors the Mesopotamian wing of the cross-tradition divination cluster + the principal documented divinatory-transmission case in ancient Mediterranean religious history. It complements the bibliomancy cluster (see [[bibliomancy-sortes]]) by showing the *non-textual* end of cross-tradition divination — the bodily / sacrificial / institutional pole.
