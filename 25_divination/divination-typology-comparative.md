# Divination — Comparative Typology (Natural vs Artificial + by Mechanism)

The organizing spine of the Atlas's divination lens. The lens holds twenty-seven method-nodes — cleromancy, extispicy, augury, oneiromancy, geomancy, the binary-permutation systems, the omen-series, the horary-astrological systems — but no node that lays out how they relate. This node supplies that frame. It is a **typology, not a practice**: it does not describe a way of divining but classifies the ways already documented, on two axes inherited from the ancient and modern literature.

## The two axes

### Axis 1 — natural vs artificial (Cicero's divide)

The oldest analytical division of divination is set out by **Cicero** in *De Divinatione* (-44). Through the mouth of his brother Quintus (a Stoic), book 1 splits divination into two genera (1.11-12, 1.34):

- **Natural / inspired divination** (*genus naturale*): the foreknowledge arises within the diviner directly, in a state of frenzy (*furor*) or in sleep (dreams). There is **no learned technique** — the diviner is the conduit of the message. Cicero's instances are oracular possession and prophetic dreams. Modern scholarship often renames this axis **intuitive** or **inspired**.
- **Artificial / inductive divination** (*genus artificiosum*): foreknowledge is reached by a **learned craft** (*ars*) that reads an external sign-system — entrails, lightning, bird-flight, lots, the stars. The diviner is a trained interpreter, not a medium. Modern scholarship renames this **inductive** or **technical**.

The division has a Greek philosophical precursor in **Plato**, *Phaedrus* 244a-d, which contrasts *manikē* — the god-sent "madness" of true prophecy — with the merely technical *oionistikē*, the bird-augur's craft, and ranks the inspired form higher. Cicero systematizes the contrast; the later Neoplatonists (Iamblichus, *De Mysteriis* 3) re-rank it, defending inspired theurgic divination over the mechanical kind.

Mapped onto the lens, the divide sorts the method-nodes cleanly at the poles and messily in the middle:

- **Natural / inspired**: [[greek-oracular-divination]] (the Delphic Pythia's possessed utterance), [[dream-incubation-asklepieion]] (revelatory incubated dreams), and the visitation/message stratum of [[oneiromancy-cross-tradition]].
- **Artificial / inductive**: [[extispicy-mesopotamian]], [[haruspicy-etruscan-roman]], [[scapulimancy-oracle-bones]], [[augury-roman-bird-omens]], [[shumma-alu-terrestrial-omens]], [[cleromancy-lot-casting]], [[astragalomancy-knucklebone]], [[geomancy-raml-cross-tradition]], the binary systems ([[yi-jing-divination]], [[ifa-odu-divination]]), [[divination-jyotisha-prashna-horary]], and [[bibliomancy-sortes]].
- **Straddling the divide**: dream-divination splits *within itself* — a direct message-dream from a god is natural; the rule-governed reading of a symbolic dream against a dream-key (Artemidorus's *Oneirocritica*; the Mesopotamian dream-omen series) is artificial. [[oneiromancy-cross-tradition]] therefore sits on both sides, which is one of the standing limits of Cicero's scheme (see Disputes).

### Axis 2 — by mechanism

The artificial branch subdivides by **what is read**. This mechanism axis is how the modern comparative literature (Bouché-Leclercq 1879-82; Caquot & Leibovici 1968; Loewe & Blacker 1981) actually organizes its surveys:

| Extispicy / hepatoscopy | a sacrificed animal's entrails, esp. the liver | [[extispicy-mesopotamian]], [[haruspicy-etruscan-roman]] |

## Cross-tradition significance (transmission vs convergence)

The whole point of laying the typology over the lens is that it makes the **transmission-versus-convergence** distinction legible — the distinction that is the Atlas's central investigative claim. The honest reading is mixed and must be stated method by method, not as a blanket.

**Convergence (independent recurrence).** Some mechanisms recur across unconnected cultures because they solve a recurrent human problem in a structurally minimal way:

- **Cleromancy** — the simplest possible divinatory technology (any randomizer plus a decision-frame). It appears in the Israelite cult (Urim and Thummim), Greek sortition, Roman *sortes*, Germanic lot-staves, and Chinese, African, and Mesoamerican practice, with no need to posit borrowing. The convergence is on plausible structural grounds (see [[cleromancy-lot-casting]]).
- **Omen-reading** as a habit of mind — reading the natural world as a coded message — is near-universal and need not be inherited.
- **Binary-permutation** systems ([[yi-jing-divination]] 64, [[ifa-odu-divination]] 256, [[geomancy-raml-cross-tradition]] 16) emerged in Chinese, West African, and Islamic-North-African contexts with **no documented borrowing in their formative periods** — one of the cleanest cases of independent structural-mathematical convergence in world divinatory history.

**Transmission (documented inheritance).** Other lines are demonstrable, named, datable chains, not parallels:

- **Extispicy / hepatoscopy** — Sumero-Akkadian liver-divination → the Hittites → the Etruscans (the Piacenza bronze liver, c. -150, preserves the ANE division-scheme) → Roman state haruspicy. ~1,700 years of continuous, mediated transmission; the structural detail is too specific for coincidence (see [[extispicy-mesopotamian]], [[haruspicy-etruscan-roman]]).
- **Celestial omens → astrology** — the Babylonian omen-series *Enūma Anu Enlil* feeds Hellenistic horoscopic astrology, and through it medieval Arabic, Indian ([[divination-jyotisha-prashna-horary]]), and European astrology (Rochberg, *The Heavenly Writing*).
- **Geomancy** — Arabic *ʿilm al-raml* → Latin Europe via the 12th-13th-c. Toledo translation movement → Agrippa (1531) → the modern occult revival. The same technique carried across a theological boundary that its cosmology did not cross (see [[geomancy-raml-cross-tradition]]).

The typology's payoff is precisely that it **keeps these two stories apart**: the natural/artificial axis is a Greco-Roman analytical inheritance; the mechanism axis exposes which families are convergent base-cases (cleromancy, omen-reading) and which are genuine transmission chains (extispicy, astrology, raml-geomancy).

## Disputes

The natural/artificial divide is a **useful but contested** instrument, and the node should not pretend otherwise.

1. **It is culture-bound.** The category is Greco-Roman (Plato → Cicero) and fits Greco-Roman material best. Johnston & Struck (*Mantikê*, 2005) and several contributors to Annus (2010) caution that it imposes a Western analytical grid on systems — Mesopotamian, Chinese, African — that did not draw the line and whose practitioners would not have recognized it. The "intuitive/inductive" relabeling is partly an attempt to de-Romanize the axis.
2. **The poles leak.** Dream-divination, oracular practice, and even astrology mix inspiration and technique (the Pythia's possessed utterance was shaped by trained priestly mediation; symbolic dream-reading is a learned craft). Cicero himself, in book 2, exploits the messiness to attack the whole enterprise. The straddling of [[oneiromancy-cross-tradition]] is the clearest case.
3. **Whether divination is one thing at all.** A minority position holds that "divination" is a family-resemblance category, not a natural kind, and that any global typology is a scholar's convenience rather than a fact about the practices. On this view the value of the typology is heuristic — a way to navigate the lens — not ontological.

This node takes the heuristic-but-real position: the typology is the best available spine for the lens, provided its Greco-Roman origin and its leaky poles are stated plainly.
