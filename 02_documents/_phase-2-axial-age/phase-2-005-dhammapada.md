# Dhammapada

Canonical Theravada Buddhist verse anthology of 423 stanzas in Pali — part of the *Khuddaka Nikāya* of the Sutta Piṭaka. Probably the single most widely-read Buddhist text in the world. Compiled c. -300 to -100, drawing on a stratum of older oral verse.
