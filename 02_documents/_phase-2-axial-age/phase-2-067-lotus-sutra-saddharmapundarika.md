# Lotus Sutra (Saddharma Puṇḍarīka)

Foundational Mahayana sutra (composed in Sanskrit c. +100 to +200), one of the most influential Buddhist texts in East Asia. Articulates the "One Vehicle" (ekayāna) doctrine + the eternal Buddha (Śākyamuni's lifespan as inconceivably long) + the universal availability of buddhahood. Foundational for Tiantai / Tendai + Nichiren schools.
