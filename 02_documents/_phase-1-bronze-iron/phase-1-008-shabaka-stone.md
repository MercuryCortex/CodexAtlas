# Shabaka Stone (Memphite Theology)

The **Memphite Theology** — inscribed on a basalt slab during the 25th-Dynasty reign of Pharaoh Shabaka (~-710) but claiming to copy a much older Old Kingdom papyrus. Articulates Ptah of Memphis as the creator-god whose **conception in heart + utterance with tongue** create all things — a creation-by-divine-thought-and-speech cosmogony. One of the principal Egyptian creation texts (alongside the Heliopolitan Ennead) + a canonical comparative-religion parallel to the Genesis 1 creation-by-divine-speech + the John 1:1 *Logos*-as-creative-Word.
