# Kephalaia of the Teacher

## What it is
A systematic catechetical work in 122+ chapters presented as Mani's answers to disciples' questions. Each *kephalaion* takes a topic — the structure of Light and Darkness, the five emanations of the Father of Greatness, the Three Times (initial separation, present mixture, final separation), the elect and the hearers, the cosmology of the wheels of fire, water, and wind that purify Light from Matter, the eschatological Great Fire — and lays out the doctrine in patient prose. Together with the Coptic Psalm-Book and the Homilies (also from Medinet Madi), the Kephalaia is our most extensive surviving Manichaean theological corpus.

## Context
- **Religious milieu.** Manichaeism as a fully developed world-religion: cosmic dualism inherited from Zoroastrianism, soteriology adapted from Gnostic Christianity, prophetic-succession theory (Adam → Seth → Enosh → Enoch → Shem → Buddha → Zoroaster → Jesus → Mani as the seal of the prophets), and an ascetic hierarchy of Elect and Hearers.
- **Discovery / transmission.** The Medinet Madi find (1929–1930) was the first major Manichaean manuscript discovery; the codices arrived in Berlin and Dublin in poor condition and World War II damaged some. Polotsky and Böhlig's German edition (begun 1940) and Gardner's English translation (1995) are the working scholarly versions.

## Themes and motifs
- [[dualism-cosmic]] — the most rigorous antique dualism: two co-eternal principles of Light and Darkness, neither created from the other.
- [[end-times-judgment]] — the Great Fire that burns 1468 years and finally separates Light from Darkness permanently.
- [[divine-feminine]] — the Mother of Life and the Maiden of Light are major emanations.
- [[gnosis-as-salvation]] — knowledge of the cosmic situation and one's place in it, combined with the Manichaean ethical discipline.

## Connections (provisional)
- → [[phase-4-014-cologne-mani-codex]] — *shared-milieu*: same prophet, same religion, different genre (autobiography vs. catechism).
- → [[phase-4-016-shabuhragan]] — *direct-source*: the Šābuhragān is Mani's own summary; the Kephalaia elaborates the same system in question-and-answer form.
- → [[phase-2-002-gathas-of-zarathustra]] (Phase 2) — *direct-influence*: Manichaean cosmic dualism is the most explicit late-antique transmission of Zoroastrian dualism into the Mediterranean and beyond.
- → [[phase-4-002-apocryphon-of-john]] (Sethian Gnosticism) — *parallel-motif* (Light-dark dualism, soteriology by knowledge); but Manichaeism's dualism is more rigorous (two co-eternal principles, not Sophia's fall).
- → Augustine's anti-Manichaean works (Phase 4 — Augustine corpus) — *polemic-against* (reverse): Augustine spent nine years as a Manichaean Hearer and then attacked the system extensively; his *Contra Faustum* and *De Natura Boni* are among our best external witnesses to Manichaean teaching.
