# Jerusalem Talmud

## What it is
A commentary on the Mishnah produced by the Palestinian rabbinic schools — the gemara of the Land of Israel. The structure: Mishnah passages followed by amoraic discussion, debate, citation of tannaitic traditions, and legal application. The Yerushalmi is shorter, more terse, and textually more difficult than the Babylonian Talmud, partly because of its less stable transmission history.

## Context
- **Political milieu.** Roman/Byzantine Palestine; closure under deteriorating conditions including Christianization of the empire under Theodosius and the dissolution of the Patriarchate ~425 CE.
- **Religious milieu.** Palestinian amoraic schools — Tiberias, Sepphoris, Caesarea, Lydda. Less centralized than the Babylonian academies.
- **Discovery / transmission.** Continuous but precarious; the Leiden manuscript (Yerushalmi, 1289) is the only complete medieval copy.
