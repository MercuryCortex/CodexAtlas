# Corpus Hermeticum

Greek hermetic-philosophical treatises (~1st–3rd c. CE) attributed to Hermes Trismegistus. Foundational document of the [[tradition-hermeticism|Hermetic tradition]]; Ficino's 1471 Latin translation triggers Renaissance Hermeticism.
