# Discourse on the Eighth and Ninth

## What it is
An initiatory dialogue: Hermes Trismegistus leads his son Tat through a guided visionary ascent. The text combines instruction, prayer, hymn, and a final command to inscribe the revelation on turquoise stelae. It is among our best evidence that the Hermetic literature was used in *ritual* contexts — not merely literary philosophical reading — and that the Egyptian Hermetic community had genuine mystery-cult practice.

## Context
- **Religious milieu.** Egyptian Hermetic community with initiatory practice. NHC VI groups this text with the Coptic Asclepius excerpt and a Hermetic prayer (NHC VI,7), suggesting the codex was assembled as a small Hermetic library.
- **Discovery / transmission.** Nag Hammadi VI (1945). Mahé's 1978 study established its centrality for understanding Hermetic religion as practice.

## Themes and motifs
- [[mystery-initiation]] — the text *is* a ritual transcript or ritual script.
- [[psychopomp]] — guided ascent past the seven spheres into the Eighth and Ninth.
- [[gnosis-as-salvation]] — the consummation is direct vision of the divine.
