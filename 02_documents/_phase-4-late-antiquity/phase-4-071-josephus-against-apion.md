# Against Apion

## What it is
A two-book Greek apologetic treatise by **[[josephus|Flavius Josephus]]**, ~+95/+100, defending the **antiquity, rationality, and ethical superiority of Judaism** against Greek-Roman *cultured anti-Jewish polemic*. Book 1 establishes the **antiquity** of the Jewish nation (older than the Greeks; preserved in better-disciplined records) and refutes the **Egyptian-historiographic counter-narratives** of the Exodus (esp. Manetho's account of the Jews as expelled lepers); book 2 systematically attacks the **Alexandrian Greek grammarian Apion** and other named anti-Jewish polemicists, then articulates a **positive constitutional-philosophical defense of the Mosaic constitution** as a *theokratia* (Josephus's coinage, *CA* 2.165). Both **the founding text of Jewish apologetic-against-pagans** and **the principal surviving source for the writings of Manetho and Apion** that it refutes.

## Context
- **Political milieu.** Late Domitian / early Trajan. Greco-Roman cultured antisemitism was an established literary phenomenon, expressed in Manetho (3rd c. BCE), Apion of Alexandria (1st c. CE), Posidonius (transmitted via Diodorus 34/35), Lysimachus, Chaeremon, and shortly after Josephus by **Tacitus *Histories* 5.1–13** (the most systematic surviving pagan attack on Judaism, contemporaneous with *Against Apion*). The Alexandrian-Egyptian context is especially important: the bitter Greek-Jewish-Egyptian three-way ethnic conflict at Alexandria had broken out repeatedly (notably the 38–41 CE pogrom that occasioned Philo's *Embassy to Gaius*).
- **Religious milieu.** Post-+70 Judaism is reconstructing without the Temple. Christianity is consolidating as a distinct movement. Josephus is writing from Rome, in Greek, for a Greek-Roman elite readership, defending Judaism *in pagan terms* — and **inadvertently providing the rhetorical template for later Christian apologetic against pagans** (Justin, Athenagoras, Tertullian inherit Josephan strategies).
- **Who was behind it.** Josephus himself, working with Greek-secretarial assistance. The work is *technically sophisticated* — it deploys Greek philosophical-rhetorical conventions, Greek literary-criticism (textual reliability, chronology), and Greek political-philosophical vocabulary.
- **Discovery / transmission.** Continuous Christian transmission preserved the text (with the lacuna in book 2 filled by Latin version). **Eusebius's *Praeparatio Evangelica* quotes Against Apion at length** — Christian apologists found Josephus's anti-pagan arguments useful for their own anti-pagan apologetic.

## Themes and motifs
- [[apologetic-historiography]] — load-bearing locus. *Against Apion* is **the founding text of Jewish-apologetic-against-pagans as a literary genre**, providing the rhetorical-structural template that Christian apologetic will inherit and reuse (defense-of-antiquity; refutation of named-polemicists; positive-constitutional-defense; ethical-superiority argument).
- **Hellenistic anti-Jewish polemic substrate-wire** — load-bearing. Bar-Kochva 2010 documents the *Greco-Roman cultured anti-Jewish polemic* tradition Josephus is responding to:
  - **Manetho's *Aegyptiaca*** (3rd c. BCE Egyptian priest-historian, writing for Ptolemy II): recasts the Exodus as the *expulsion of polluted lepers and Hyksos-Asiatic-shepherds* from Egypt; Moses is a renegade Egyptian priest named Osarseph.
  - **Apion of Alexandria** (1st c. CE grammarian): repeats and extends the leper-expulsion narrative; ass-headed cult-statue libel; child-sacrifice-and-cannibalism blood-libel; Sabbath-misanthropy charge.
  - **Posidonius / Apollonius Molon / Lysimachus / Chaeremon**: variants on the same topoi.
  - This is **the founding tradition of Western antisemitism**, in continuous tradition from Hellenistic Egypt to Tacitus *Hist.* 5 to medieval Christian inheritance to modernity. Josephus's refutation is **simultaneously a preservation** of the polemic he refutes — Manetho's *Aegyptiaca* survives almost only in fragments preserved by Josephus.
  - **Christianity later inherits and partially redirects** this Greco-Roman anti-Jewish polemic (notably in the *Adversus Judaeos* tradition descending from [[phase-4-064-epistle-of-barnabas|Barnabas]] and Justin), while also being itself targeted by the same polemicists (Pliny, Tacitus, Suetonius write of Christians in cognate-anti-Jewish-misanthropy terms).
- [[critique-of-idolatry]] — Josephus mounts a sustained Greek-philosophical critique of Greek-Egyptian polytheism: the gods are immoral, contradictory, recent inventions; the Greek poets blaspheme their own gods. The standard Hellenistic-Jewish anti-polytheism arsenal in its most concentrated form.
- [[ethical-monotheism]] — Book 2.190–219 articulates the **Mosaic constitution as the most rational form of government**: one God, simple ritual, ethical universalism, dignity of all human beings, prohibition on cruelty even to animals, dignity of labor. **Josephus coins the term *theokratia* (CA 2.165) to describe this**: a constitutional form alongside monarchy / aristocracy / democracy in which sovereignty rests with God and is administered through divine law. This is the **first occurrence of *theocracy* as a political-theological concept** — the term subsequently inherited and re-deployed across Western political theology.
- [[chosen-people]] — defended in Greek-philosophical-constitutional terms: chosenness as covenant-relationship around a uniquely rational divine law, not as ethnic exclusivism.

Without *Against Apion*, Manetho's anti-Jewish Exodus-counter-narrative would survive only in tiny fragments.
- → **Apion of Alexandria** — *direct-polemic*: *Against Apion* book 2 is the principal surviving source for Apion's writings (otherwise nearly lost).
- → **Tacitus *Histories* 5.1–13** — *parallel-target* / *contemporary*: Tacitus's anti-Jewish digression is contemporaneous with *Against Apion* and articulates the same Greco-Roman cultured-anti-Jewish-polemic tradition Josephus is refuting. Bar-Kochva 2010.
- → [[phase-4-024-tertullian-apology]] / Christian apologists — *founding-genre*: the rhetorical structure of Josephus's apologetic is inherited by Christian apologists (Justin, Athenagoras, Tertullian) as they shift the project from defending-Judaism-to-pagans to defending-Christianity-to-pagans.
- → [[phase-4-027-eusebius-ecclesiastical-history]] / Eusebius *Praeparatio Evangelica* — *direct-quote*: Eusebius quotes Against Apion extensively to use Josephus's pagan-polytheism critique for Christian purposes.

## Disputes
- **Date.** ~+95/+100 mainstream (post-Antiquities; Domitian or early Trajan).
- **Reliability of Josephus's representation of Manetho and Apion.** Mostly considered fair-paraphrase in book 1 (Bar-Kochva 2010 substantially confirms Josephus's Manetho-fragments against independent evidence); somewhat more polemical in book 2.
- **The book-2 lacuna.** The Greek-mss lacuna at *CA* 2.52–113 is filled by Latin translation; mainstream view accepts the Latin as substantially reliable witness to the missing Greek.
- **Whether *theokratia* is genuinely Josephus's coinage** — universally accepted yes; the term is unattested before *CA* 2.165 and rare even in subsequent ancient usage, becoming a major Western political-theological term only via modern reception.
- **The status of Josephus's positive *politeia* description (CA 2.190–219).** Some scholars read it as a quasi-philosophical synthesis of Mosaic Law in idealized form, not a literal description; others as substantially descriptive. Mason 2003, Barclay 2007.
