# Elements of Theology

## What it is
A systematic Neoplatonist metaphysics in 211 propositions, each with a proof — modeled on Euclid's *Elements*. Proclus articulates the entire hierarchy of being (the One, the henads/gods, Intellects, Souls, the cosmos) as a deductively derived structure: every effect is in its cause, every cause in its effects; the procession-reversion-remaining triad is the engine of the entire system; the gods are henads, "ones" subordinate only to the One itself. The work is the most rigorous formal exposition of any late-antique theological system.

## Context
- **Political milieu.** Mid-5th-century Athens; Proclus headed the Platonic Academy from ~437 until his death in 485. The Academy was finally closed by Justinian's edict in 529 (see [[event-closure-platonic-academy-529]]) — a borderline-late event we group with this phase.
- **Religious milieu.** Athenian Neoplatonism as the last institutional bastion of philosophical paganism against the now-state Christianity.

## Themes and motifs
- [[hidden-god]] — the One in its rigorous apophatic axiomatic form.
- The procession-reversion triad — Proclus' fundamental theological grammar; later absorbed into Christian theology.

## Connections (provisional)
- → [[phase-4-019-plotinus-enneads]] / [[phase-4-020-iamblichus-on-the-mysteries]] — *direct-influence*: Proclus systematizes the prior Neoplatonic tradition.
- → Pseudo-Dionysius the Areopagite — *direct-influence*: passages of Dionysius are extracts from Proclus with Christian terminology overlaid (demonstrated by Koch and Stiglmayr ~1895).
- → *Liber de Causis* (Arabic / Latin) — *redaction-of*: an Arabic Neoplatonist redaction of Proclus that entered Latin scholasticism.
- → Aquinas — *commentary-on*: once the Proclean source of the *Liber de Causis* was identified, Aquinas wrote a commentary on the *Elements* itself.

## Disputes
- **Authorship of the *Liber de Causis*.** Once thought to be by Aristotle (its Latin title was *Aristotelis liber de expositione bonitatis purae*); Aquinas identified the true Proclean source ~1268.
