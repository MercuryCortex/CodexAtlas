# Mysterium Coniunctionis

## What it is
Jung's final and most ambitious work, published in two German volumes 1955–1956 (English CW vol. 14 1970). The work synthesizes Jung's decades-long engagement with alchemy around the central alchemical motif of *coniunctio oppositorum* — the union of opposites — read as the symbolic operator of psychological individuation. Six major chapters: (1) "Components of the Coniunctio"; (2) "The Paradoxa"; (3) "The Personification of the Opposites"; (4) "Rex and Regina" (the alchemical King-Queen); (5) "Adam and Eve"; (6) "The Conjunction." Jung's central case study (especially in Part 4) is the alchemy of Gerhard Dorn (16th c.), whose three-stage *unio mentalis* → *unio corporis* → *unio mundi* Jung treats as the most articulate alchemical description of the individuation process.

## Context
- **Political milieu.** Postwar Switzerland; Jung was 80 when the German publication completed in 1956. His health was failing (he died 1961); *Mysterium Coniunctionis* is the final systematic work.
- **Religious milieu.** Postwar Eranos Conferences continued through the 1950s; the alchemical-comparativist project Jung had pursued for two decades reached its full systematization.
- **Who was behind it.** Jung; von Franz's parallel work on the *Aurora Consurgens* (a 13th-c. Latin alchemical text attributed to Thomas Aquinas) was published as the explicit companion volume.
- **Discovery / transmission.** German edition Rascher Verlag 1955–56; CW vol. 14 1970.

## Themes and motifs
- [[coniunctio]] — alchemical union of opposites (Sol-Luna, Sulphur-Mercury, King-Queen, body-soul-spirit); Jung's master symbol for individuation's culmination.
- **Three-stage individuation** (after Dorn) — *unio mentalis* (the integration of consciousness with the unconscious), *unio corporis* (the integration of mind with body), *unio mundi* (the union of integrated self with the world / *unus mundus*).
- **Unus mundus** — Jung's late metaphysical-speculative concept of a unified psychophysical substrate from which both psyche and matter precipitate. The basis for his synchronicity work with Pauli (1952 *Naturerklärung und Psyche*).

## Disputes
- **Alchemy-as-psychology again.** The same scholarly issue as in *Psychology and Alchemy*: post-1990s history of science (Principe, Newman) has restored alchemy's experimental-chemical dimension that Jung minimized. Jung's reading remains influential in psychology and in some history-of-religion contexts but is not the standard historical reading of the alchemical texts themselves.
- **Unus mundus speculation.** Jung's late metaphysical speculation (with Pauli) is treated cautiously by mainstream Jungian psychology.
