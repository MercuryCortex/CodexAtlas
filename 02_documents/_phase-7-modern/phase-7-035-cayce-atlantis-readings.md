# The Edgar Cayce Atlantis Readings

## What it is
A subset of approximately 700 of the ~14,306 trance readings given by [[edgar-cayce]] (1877–1945) between 1923 and 1944, concerned specifically with Atlantis. The readings were delivered by Cayce in a self-induced sleep state in response to questions posed by his wife Gertrude Cayce (conductor); the stenographer Gladys Davis Turner transcribed them verbatim. The Atlantis material falls into two broad categories: (a) "world-affairs" readings (principal series 364-1 through 364-13, ~1932) giving general historical-cosmological information about the lost continent; (b) "life readings" (the larger category, mid-1923 onward) in which Cayce traced individual clients' past-life karmic patterns, of which a disproportionate share traced back to Atlantean lifetimes.

The cumulative content describes Atlantis as a vast civilization in the mid-Atlantic, destroyed in three successive cataclysms (~50,700 BCE, ~28,000 BCE, ~10,500 BCE — the third final), pre-flood and pre-Egyptian. Cayce's Atlantis is morally dualistic: the "Sons of the Law of One" (spiritual-monotheist faction) vs. the "Sons of Belial" (materialistic-hedonist faction); a "Firestone" or "great crystal" provides energy and was misused; Atlantean refugees seeded Egypt, the Yucatán, the Pyrenees, and inland portions of the Americas. The famous Bimini prediction (reading 958-3, 28 June 1940, and 996-12, 1933) anticipated that "a portion of the temples may yet be discovered, under the slime of ages of sea water — near what is known as Bimini, off the coast of Florida" with timing language interpreted by A.R.E. as pointing to 1968–1969.

## Context
- **Political milieu.** Interwar and WWII-era United States. Cayce's hospital in Virginia Beach (1928–1931) failed in the Depression; the Association for Research and Enlightenment was founded 1931 as a successor research-and-archive entity. Cayce's clientele included middle-class spiritualist-curious Americans, some socially prominent (Thomas Sugrue's 1942 *There Is a River* biography brought wider attention).
- **Religious milieu.** Cayce was raised Disciples of Christ and remained a serious lay Bible-reader and Sunday school teacher throughout his life; the readings retain biblical-prophetic vocabulary alongside Theosophical-coloured content. The American esoteric milieu of the 1910s–1940s was saturated with Atlantean-Lemurian material from Scott-Elliot, the Theosophical Society, and popular periodicals; Cayce *did not* claim to read Theosophical literature, but operated in a culture where the framework was ambient.
- **Who was behind it.** Cayce personally; Gertrude Cayce; Gladys Davis Turner; the early A.R.E. circle. Cayce was personally a modest small-town photographer with limited formal education; the contrast between his ordinary waking person and the encyclopedic, vocabulary-rich, prophetic-toned readings is the central biographical puzzle of his life.
- **Discovery / transmission.** Stenographic records were preserved continuously by the A.R.E.; major publication of the Atlantis material came posthumously with Edgar Evans Cayce's 1968 compilation (timed deliberately to coincide with the Bimini-prediction window).

## Themes and motifs
- [[lost-continent]] — the readings inherit the Plato-Donnelly-Theosophical Atlantis but specify dates, locations, and technological detail with much greater precision than any prior source.
- [[antediluvian-civilization]] — Cayce's Atlantis is pre-flood; the Genesis flood and the Atlantean final destruction are aligned.
- [[reincarnation]] — distinctive to Cayce: the readings treat Atlantean past-lives as karmic determinants of present circumstances. This is a Hindu-Theosophical-styled doctrine embedded in a nominally Christian framework, and was the most theologically uncomfortable feature for Cayce's Protestant background.
- [[pseudoarchaeology]] — the Atlantis content has been comprehensively rejected by professional archaeology and geology. The Bimini "Road" prediction is the most-tested specific claim and is discussed under Disputes.
- [[apocalyptic-revelation]] — Cayce predicted dramatic "Earth Changes" for the late 20th c. (axis-shift, California-Atlantic-coast inundations, Atlantis rising); none of these have occurred. The genre is biblical-prophetic.
- [[prophecy-as-political-critique]] — the Sons-of-the-Law-of-One vs. Sons-of-Belial dualism reads as moral commentary on Cayce's Depression / WWII present (materialism vs. spirituality) projected onto deep history.

## Disputes
**[Substantial Tier-4 caution applies. The Atlantis content of the Cayce readings has not been corroborated by archaeology, geology, or paleoclimatology, and several specific predictions have failed observably. The following items are documented external findings; A.R.E. positions differ on most.]**

- **Bimini "Road."** Cayce's 1940 reading predicted Atlantean remains "near Bimini" would surface; in 1968, a natural underwater rock formation off North Bimini (now called the "Bimini Road") was discovered by Joseph Manson Valentine. The formation has been comprehensively examined by professional geologists (Eugene Shinn USGS 1978, 2004, and others) and identified as **natural Pleistocene beachrock** with fracture patterns consistent with marine cementation and tidal stress. The 1978 USGS samples include carbonate dating consistent with natural formation. Counter-claims (David Zink, Greg Little) have not been peer-reviewed and rely on visual-similarity arguments. The mainstream geological consensus is settled. (Feder 2020 ch. 7; Shinn 2004.)
- **"Earth Changes" failed predictions.** Cayce predicted Japan would "go into the sea" and the western United States would experience massive coastal inundation, with timing language pointing to the 1958–1998 window; New York City would be "destroyed" and inland portions of the East Coast inundated. None of these have occurred. A.R.E. interprretive responses have variously rescheduled the predictions, treated them as "potential" rather than fated, or applied them metaphorically.
- **Atlantean-civilization claims.** No archaeological, geological, climatological, or genetic evidence supports a pre-Holocene transoceanic high civilization. The dates Cayce gave (~50,700 BCE through 10,500 BCE) place the Atlantean civilization in the Upper Paleolithic, for which there is comprehensive archaeological evidence of small-band hunter-gatherer life worldwide, with no settled urbanism before ~9,000 BCE (early Neolithic).
- **Methodology of the readings.** Hanegraaff 1998 and other scholars of New Age religion analyze the readings as a culturally-shaped trance-product drawing on (a) Cayce's substantial Bible knowledge, (b) the saturated Atlantean-Lemurian content of the American esoteric press of his youth and middle age, (c) the questions posed by the conductor (which constrained and primed the output), and (d) suggestion effects in repeated readings for individual clients. The "where did Cayce get the material from" puzzle dissolves substantially under cultural-substrate analysis.
- **Medical and biographical readings.** Some Cayce medical readings appear, in the A.R.E. archive's own follow-up documentation, to have produced therapeutic effects; these readings are a separate phenomenon from the Atlantis material, and even sympathetic biographers (Bro 1989) acknowledge the medical and the cosmological readings are evidentially distinct.
