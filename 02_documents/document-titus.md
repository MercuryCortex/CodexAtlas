# Epistle to Titus

## What it is
The shortest of the three Pastoral Epistles, instructing Paul's delegate Titus to set the Cretan churches in order — appointing qualified elders, silencing disruptive teachers, and teaching sound, sober conduct to every group in the household. Brief as it is, it carries two compact, creed-like summaries of salvation by grace.

## Primary text

### Titus 3:4–5
*Trans. NRSV*
> But when the goodness and loving-kindness of God our Savior appeared, he saved us, not because of any works of righteousness that we had done, but according to his mercy, through the water of rebirth and renewal by the Holy Spirit.

## Context
- **Religious milieu.** Church-planting and ordering on Crete; the institutional concerns of the Pastorals in miniature.
- **Authorship.** Part of the Pastoral sub-corpus.
- **Discovery / transmission.** Within the Pauline corpus.

## Themes and motifs
- Church order — the appointment and qualifications of elders (1:5–9).
- Grace and regeneration — salvation "through the water of rebirth" (3:5), a baptismal formula.
- [[messianism]] — "our great God and Savior Jesus Christ" (2:13), a notable high-Christology phrase.
