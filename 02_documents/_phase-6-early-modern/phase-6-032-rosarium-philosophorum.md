# Rosarium Philosophorum (*Rosary of the Philosophers*)

## What it is
An anonymous compiled Latin alchemical-mystical work, first printed Frankfurt 1550 as part of the *De Alchimia Opuscula complura veterum philosophorum* anthology. The text compiles material from the medieval Latin alchemical tradition ([[albertus-magnus|Albertus]] / Pseudo-Albertus, Latin Geber, Pseudo-Lull, Petrus Bonus *Pretiosa Margarita Novella*) into a unified 20-stage exposition of the alchemical *opus*. **The 20 accompanying woodcuts depicting the King and Queen alchemical marriage are the most-reproduced single set of alchemical images in Western history.**

The visual sequence:
1. The mercurial fountain.
2. The King and Queen meet (the *coniunctio* begins).
3. Naked confrontation.
4. Immersion / dissolution.
5. *Coniunctio* proper — the King and Queen in the alchemical bath.
6. Death — the conjoined hermaphrodite as corpse.
7. Ascent of the soul (*animae ascensio*).
8. Putrefaction.
9. Soul descends and reunites with body (*animae reditus*).
10. Sublimation / the new hermaphrodite emerges.
… etc., culminating in the *unus mundus* hermaphroditic resurrection.

## Context
- **Religious milieu.** Mid-16th-c. Lutheran Frankfurt printing. The *Rosarium* operates in a self-consciously *philosophical-Christian* register; its woodcuts integrate alchemical and biblical imagery (the descending dove of the Holy Spirit appears in stage 7).
- **Iconographic origin.** The woodcuts have an earlier ms tradition (the Marburg ms. and others, ~15th c.); the 1550 printing fixes them as a canonical sequence.

## Themes and motifs
- [[alchemical-marriage]] — the *coniunctio* / chymical wedding of King and Queen is the *Rosarium*'s structural core.
- [[alchemy-as-spiritual-discipline]] — the work explicitly aligns material *opus* with spiritual transformation.
- [[bridal-mysticism]] — the Latin alchemical bridal imagery converges with the Bernardine bridal-mystical tradition.
- [[coniunctio]] — the canonical alchemical-Jungian term.
- [[hermetic-correspondences]] / [[as-above-so-below]] — the integrated visual-philosophical framework.
