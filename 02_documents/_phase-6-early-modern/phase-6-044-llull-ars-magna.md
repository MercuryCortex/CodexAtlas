# Ars Magna / Ars Generalis Ultima / Ars Brevis

## Overview

**Ramon Llull** (c.1232–1316) — Catalan philosopher, mystic, Franciscan tertiary, and missionary — created what he believed was a divinely revealed universal method for demonstrating the truth of Christian doctrine to Muslims and Jews without presupposing the authority of Scripture. The *Ars Magna* (first version 1274, final version the *Ars Generalis Ultima* 1308) is a **combinatorial logical machine**: a set of rotating concentric wheels (*volvelles*) and triangular *figures* that, when turned, generate every possible combination of divine attributes (*Dignities*), relational categories, questions, subjects, predicates, and virtues/vices.

It is one of the most structurally original works in medieval thought and the **missing common ancestor** of five of the vault's most important Hermetic nodes: Pico, Agrippa, Bruno, Kircher, and Leibniz.

## The Nine Dignities

The Ars is built on nine *Dignitates Dei* (Dignities of God): **Goodness, Greatness, Eternity, Power, Wisdom, Will, Virtue, Truth, Glory**. These are not simply attributes — they are the *active principles* by which the divine emanates into creation. The rotating wheels combine and recombine these nine dignities, generating all true propositions about God, nature, the soul, and the virtues.

**The Sefer Yetzirah parallel**: Sefer Yetzirah (a foundational Kabbalistic text, phase-5) builds the universe from 22 letters in combination; Llull builds it from 9 divine attributes in combination. The structural logic is identical. Llull lived in Mallorca, which had a substantial Jewish community, and scholars (Yates 1966, Bonner 2007) have documented his engagement with Kabbalistic sources. The Ars Magna and Sefer Yetzirah are convergent solutions to the same problem: how does infinite divine being become a differentiated cosmos?

## The Transmission Chain

**1. Llull → Bruno**: Giordano Bruno was a Lullist before he was a Copernican. He wrote three commentaries on Lull's *Ars* (including the *De Compendiosa Architectura* and *Lampas Triginta Statuarum*) and transformed the combinatorial wheels into a system of memory and magic — infinite combinatorial wheels generating all possible thoughts as memory images. Bruno's burning (1600) ended the most radical development of Lullism. Frances Yates: "Bruno was the last great Lullist."

**2. Llull → Pico**: Pico della Mirandola's 900 *Conclusiones* (1486) include a dedicated section of Lullian theses alongside Kabbalistic, Hermetic, and Aristotelian ones. Pico regarded Lullism as the Christian-rational counterpart to Kabbalah's letter-permutation method.

**3. Pseudo-Lull → alchemical tradition**: A vast body of alchemical texts attributed to "Raimundus Lullus" circulated in the 14th–16th centuries, including the *Testamentum*, the *Codicillus*, the *Ars Operativa*, and others — all forged, but enormously influential. The Rosarium Philosophorum cites Pseudo-Lull; Paracelsus engaged with the tradition. This pseudonymous corpus made "Lull" a household name in the Latin alchemical tradition.

**4. Llull → Leibniz**: Leibniz's *Dissertatio de Arte Combinatoria* (Leipzig, 1666) — his first major work, written at 20 — explicitly cites Llull as the ancestor of his program for a universal logical calculus (*characteristica universalis*). The *Ars* is the direct ancestor of combinatorial logic, formal grammar, and ultimately computer science.

## The Ars and the Divine Names

Llull's nine Dignities correspond structurally to the Kabbalistic sefirot (ten attributes of divine being), the Islamic ninety-nine divine names (*asma Allah al-husna*), and the Neoplatonic divine hypostases. The Ars is an attempt to formalize the logic of **divine-names theology** — the doctrine that the names of God are not merely linguistic labels but actual principles of divine and cosmic structure.

Ibn Arabi (the foundational Sufi Hermetic thinker) developed the same logic of divine names in parallel at almost exactly the same time (Ibn Arabi died 1240; Llull born c.1232). Both were working in the same Mediterranean world — Andalusia/Mallorca — saturated with Jewish, Christian, and Islamic mystical logic.
