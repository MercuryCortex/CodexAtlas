# Libri Mysteriorum — Dee's Angelic Conversations and the Enochian System

## Overview

Between 1581 and 1587, the mathematician, geographer, and Hermetic philosopher **[[john-dee]]** conducted a series of scrying sessions in which his colleague **[[edward-kelley]]** acted as medium, receiving dictated messages from angels through a polished black obsidian mirror and a *shewstone* (crystal ball). Dee transcribed the conversations in meticulous detail across six manuscript books (the *Libri Mysteriorum*, BL Sloane MSS) plus the *De Heptarchia Mystica* and the *Liber Scientiae Auxilii et Victoriae Terrestris*.

The sessions produced what Dee believed was the **original language of God** — the pre-Adamic tongue in which God created the universe and called its parts into being — now known as **Enochian**, after the patriarch Enoch who walked with God and was taken (Genesis 5:24). The language includes a grammar, alphabet of 21 letters, and 48 *Enochian calls* or *Keys* — incantatory verses that invoke the angelic governors of the 30 Aethyrs (cosmic regions) and the four Watchtowers of the world.

## The Sessions

The principal sessions ran at:
- **Mortlake, Surrey (1581–1583)** — Dee's home on the Thames; using first Barnabas Saul then Edward Kelley as scryers
- **Prague (1584)** — at the court of Emperor Rudolf II; the angel Nalvage dictates the forty-nine Tables of the Enochian language
- **Kraków (1584)** — under the patronage of Albert Laski
- **Třeboň, Bohemia (1585–1587)** — at the court of Wilhelm von Rosenberg; the most spiritually intense phase, ending with the *Actio Tertia* command for wife-swearing (which precipitated the partnership's collapse)

The angelic communicants identified themselves as: **Uriel**, **Michael**, **Raphael**, **Gabriel**, **Nalvage** (who dictated the Enochian calls), and most significantly **Madimi** — a shape-shifting female spirit who issued the controversial command.

## The Angelic Language

The Enochian alphabet of 21 letters was dictated letter-by-letter in reverse order (to protect the scryer from the power of the words); a series of tables (the *Tabula Sancta* and the *48 Claves Angelicae*) organizes the language into a cosmological grid. The language has:
- A documented grammar and syntax distinct from English and Latin
- Place-names for 91 regions of the earth, each with an angelic governor
- A cosmological hierarchy of **30 Aethyrs** (levels of the heavenly realm) each guarded by three governors

**The Hekhalot parallel**: the 30 Aethyrs and their gatekeeping governors mirror the structure of the Hekhalot ascent — seven heavenly palaces, each with guardians demanding passwords. Dee likely encountered the Hekhalot tradition through Reuchlin's and Agrippa's Kabbalistic sources; the Enochian system is the Elizabethan reformulation of the same angelological-ascent structure.

## Cross-Tradition Significance

The *Libri Mysteriorum* are the primary transmission node between Renaissance Hermeticism and 19th-century Western ceremonial magic:

1. **Dee → Rosicrucians**: Dee's Bohemian mission 1583–1589 — in which he attempted to announce an angelic reformation of Christian civilization to the courts of Central Europe — directly fed the milieu from which the Rosicrucian manifestos (1614–1616) emerged. Frances Yates (1972) argued that Dee was the founding spirit of the Rosicrucian movement.
2. **Dee → Golden Dawn**: S.L. MacGregor Mathers (1890s) reconstructed Dee's Enochian tablets from the British Library manuscripts and made the Enochian system the highest-degree initiatory framework of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. Every subsequent Western magical order (A∴A∴, Thelema, Wicca's high-degree system) inherits this reconstruction.
3. **Dee → Crowley**: Aleister Crowley's *The Vision and the Voice* (1909–1911) used the 30 Aethyrs and 48 Enochian calls as the vehicle for his Thelemic visionary program — the Enochian calls remain the highest operative technology in modern Western ceremonial magic.
