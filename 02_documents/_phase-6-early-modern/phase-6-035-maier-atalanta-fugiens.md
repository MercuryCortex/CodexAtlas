# Atalanta Fugiens (*Fleeing Atalanta*)

## What it is
[[michael-maier|Michael Maier's]] integrated **emblem-music-alchemy** masterwork — first edition Oppenheim 1617 — **unique in 17th-c. European literature** as a coherently-organized 50-stage alchemical *opus* presented simultaneously as:

1. **50 engraved emblems** (by Matthäus Merian)
2. **50 Latin epigrams** (mottos for each emblem)
3. **50 Latin discourses** (prose theological-alchemical exposition)
4. **50 three-voice fugues** (printed musical scores for performance)

Each emblem-cycle (image + epigram + discourse + fugue) advances the alchemical *opus* through one of its 50 stages. The work is meant to be **simultaneously read, meditated upon, and sung**: the three musical voices represent the alchemical King (the *fugiens*, fleeing voice), Atalanta (the chase voice), and Hippomenes (the slowing voice with the apples) — operating as a sonic allegory of the alchemical *coniunctio*.

**One of the iconic flagship holdings of the Bibliotheca Philosophica Hermetica / Ritman Library** and a centerpiece of the Yale Beinecke Mellon Collection.

## Context
- **Religious milieu.** Post-1614 Rosicrucian milieu. The 1614 *Fama* and 1615 *Confessio* had generated Europe-wide search for the Rose Cross fraternity; Maier (the principal post-1614 Rosicrucian apologist) intends *Atalanta Fugiens* as a sophisticated symbolic-musical exposition of the Rosicrucian Christian-Hermetic-alchemical synthesis.
- **Patronage and printing.** Oppenheim was a Palatinate Rosicrucian-sympathetic publishing center under Elector Frederick V (the same Frederick whose 1620 disastrous Bohemian kingship triggers the Thirty Years' War). Johann Theodor de Bry's Oppenheim press produced Maier's principal works as well as Robert Fludd's, in a publishing program that effectively *was* the Rosicrucian-publication infrastructure.
- **Atalanta-myth as alchemical allegory.** The Greek myth — Atalanta the fleet-footed huntress, Hippomenes the suitor, the three golden apples of Aphrodite — is read as a fugitive Mercury (Atalanta), a fixative Sulphur (Hippomenes), and the three Hermetic apples that arrest the volatile-fugitive principle. The musical fugue form (in which one voice flees and another chases) is the perfect musical-formal expression of this allegory.
