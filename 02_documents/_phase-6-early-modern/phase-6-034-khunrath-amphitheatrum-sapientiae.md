# Amphitheatrum Sapientiae Aeternae (*Amphitheatre of Eternal Wisdom*)

## What it is
[[heinrich-khunrath|Heinrich Khunrath's]] integrated Hermetic-Kabbalistic-Christian-alchemical magnum opus. First edition Hamburg 1595 (incomplete); the **iconic posthumous second edition was published at Hanau 1609** with the full series of engraved plates by Hans Vredeman de Vries. The work fuses Lutheran biblical theology, Christian Kabbalah (Pico-Reuchlin lineage), Hermetic-alchemical natural philosophy, Paracelsian medicine, and visual-emblematic iconography into a single integrated artwork-treatise. **The prototype of the early-17th-c. illustrated Christian-Hermetic-alchemical-Rosicrucian emblem book** — the genre Maier's *Atalanta Fugiens* (1617) and Fludd's *Utriusque Cosmi Historia* (1617–1621) would develop further.

The full title gives the integrative ambition: *Amphitheatrum of Eternal Wisdom, Solely-True, Christian-Kabbalistic, Divine-Magical, and likewise Physico-Chemical, Threefold-One, Catholic*.

The most-reproduced single plate is the **'Oratorium-Laboratorium'** image — the alchemist's chamber depicted with a prayer-altar (*oratorium*) on one side and a laboratory bench (*laboratorium*) on the other, the alchemist himself kneeling between them. The plate's inscription: *Christo dvce per ardva ad astra* (*With Christ as guide, through difficulty to the stars*). **This image is the canonical visual emblem of alchemy-as-spiritual-discipline** and has been continuously reproduced for four centuries.

## Context
- **Religious milieu.** Late-16th-c. Lutheran Germany; the integrated Hermetic-Kabbalistic-Christian register operates *within* Lutheran piety while ranging across philosophical-Hermetic traditions.
- **Khunrath-Dee connection.** Khunrath met John Dee at Bremen in 1589. The 1589 encounter is a network-link between English Hermeticism (Dee, later Fludd) and continental Hermeticism (Khunrath, later Maier, Boehme).
- **Transmission.** The Hanau 1609 edition with Vredeman de Vries plates is the canonical witness. Major holdings: Bibliotheca Philosophica Hermetica (Ritman Library) Amsterdam, British Library, Yale Beinecke Mellon Collection, Bayerische Staatsbibliothek Munich, Bibliothèque nationale de France.
