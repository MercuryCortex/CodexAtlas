# Gayanashagowa (The Great Law of Peace)

The constitutional covenant of the Haudenosaunee Confederacy, attributed to the semi-divine Peacemaker ([[deganawidah-peacemaker]]) and his orator [[hiawatha-haudenosaunee]]. The Great Law unified five warring nations (Seneca, Cayuga, Onondaga, Oneida, Mohawk — later six with the Tuscarora ~1722) under a system of consensus governance encoded in wampum belts. Its provisions include: a federal structure with local sovereignty; a bicameral-equivalent deliberation process; clan-mother power to appoint and remove chiefs; condolence protocols for installing new leadership; freedom of speech in deliberation; and explicit prohibitions on revenge-based conflict between member nations.

The Great Law is the oldest continuously used democratic constitutional system in the world (dating contested: oral Haudenosaunee tradition ~1090 CE; outside scholars ~1450–1600 CE). Its documented influence on American Founding Fathers Benjamin Franklin and Thomas Jefferson — who explicitly cited it in designing the Articles of Confederation and Constitution — is one of the vault's most significant political-theology transmission edges.
