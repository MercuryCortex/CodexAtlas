# Mahāparinibbāna Sutta

Pali canonical text (*Digha Nikaya* 16) recounting the Buddha's last days, the parinibbāna, and the instructions on building stupas — the foundational textual basis of the stupa form ([[sanchi-great-stupa]], [[borobudur-stupa-mandala]]).
