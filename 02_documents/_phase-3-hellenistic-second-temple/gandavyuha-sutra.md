# Gandavyuha Sutra

Mahayana Buddhist sutra (~3rd c. CE); narrates the pilgrim Sudhana's journey to 53 spiritual teachers; the principal textual programme illustrated in the upper galleries of [[borobudur-stupa-mandala|Borobudur]].
