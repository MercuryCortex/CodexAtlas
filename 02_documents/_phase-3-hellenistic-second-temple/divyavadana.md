# Divyāvadāna

Sanskrit collection of Buddhist legends + narratives (~2nd c. CE); narrative source for many of the *jataka* and *avadana* reliefs at [[sanchi-great-stupa|Sanchi]] and [[borobudur-stupa-mandala|Borobudur]].
