The Charaka Samhita is the foundational internal medicine text of [[medicine-ayurveda]] — an 8-book encyclopedia covering physiology, pathology, pharmacology (600+ plant medicines, 200 animal substances, 80 mineral preparations), diet, lifestyle, and medical ethics. It is the oldest surviving comprehensive medical text of the Indian tradition and one of the most extensive pharmacopoeias from the ancient world.

## The Eight Books

1. **Sūtrasthāna** (General Principles): fundamental concepts — the tridosha, diet, seasons, physician ethics
2. **Nidānasthāna** (Pathology): causes and symptoms of major diseases
3. **Vimānasthāna** (Specific Determination): pharmacology, taste theory, rasa (taste) classification
4. **Śārīrasthāna** (Anatomy/Embryology): the body's structure; embryology; the nature of consciousness
5. **Indriyasthāna** (Sensory Assessment): prognosis through sensory examination
6. **Cikitsāsthāna** (Therapeutics): treatment of all major diseases
7. **Kalpasthāna** (Pharmaceutical Preparations): pharmacy; drug processing; dosage forms
8. **Kalpasthāna** (Successful Treatment): physician qualifications; case studies

## Medical Ethics — The Caraka Oath

The Charaka Samhita contains one of the oldest medical ethics codes in the world (Vimānasthāna 8.13) — the oath taken by students entering medical study:

"Having entered into a house, you shall not injure anyone... You shall not eat flesh that has not been prescribed, you shall not take gifts from your patients beyond what they voluntarily offer... You shall devote yourself entirely to helping the sick."

The structural parallel with the Hippocratic Oath (written c. simultaneously, in the same era, independently) is striking: both require sworn commitment to patient welfare, non-maleficence, and physician conduct.

## The Pharmacopoeia

The Charaka Samhita's materia medica (Sūtrasthāna 4, Vimānasthāna) contains:
- 600+ plant medicines organized by taste (rasa), potency (vīrya), and post-digestive effect (vipāka)
- Systematic dosage protocols for each substance
- Contraindications for specific constitutional types (prakriti)
- Combination formulas for complex conditions

Many of these substances have confirmed pharmacological activity by modern analysis: turmeric (curcumin — anti-inflammatory), ashwagandha (withanolides — adaptogenic), brahmi (bacosides — cognitive), triphala (polyphenols — antioxidant/antimicrobial).
