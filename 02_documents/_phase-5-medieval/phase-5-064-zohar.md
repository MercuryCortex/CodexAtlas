# Zohar (Sefer ha-Zohar)

**Stub.** Auto-created 2026-05-25 by `watcher-claude-lead` to resolve a TYRANT Wave-3b flagged dead-link case (see `AUDIT/2026-05-25-wave-3b-flagged-cases.md`). Awaits full treatment.

Principal text of Kabbalistic mysticism. Late-13th c. Castilian Spain (Moshe de León active in the 1280s circulated the bulk of the work). The Zohar's own claim is that it transmits teachings of the 2nd c. CE tanna Shimon bar Yochai. Modern critical scholarship — beginning with Scholem 1941 and consolidated by Liebes 1993 — treats Moshe de León (and possibly a small circle of associates) as the historical author. Foundational for all later Kabbalistic literature (Lurianic + Sabbatean + Hasidic + modern academic + cross-tradition).
