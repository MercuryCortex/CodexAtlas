# Kitāb al-Shifāʾ (The Book of Healing)

## What it is
**Avicenna's encyclopedic philosophical *summa*** — a four-part Aristotelian curriculum-systematization in some 22 volumes, composed in Arabic by [[ibn-sina|Ibn Sīnā / Avicenna]] over the period c. 1014-1027 at the Buyid and Kakuyid courts in Iran (Hamadān, Iṣfahān). The four parts are: **(1) Logic** (9 sub-parts paralleling Aristotle's *Organon* + the *Isagoge* + *Rhetoric* + *Poetics*); **(2) Physics** (8 sub-parts, covering Aristotle's *Physics*, *De Caelo*, *De Generatione*, *Meteorologica*, *De Anima*, the natural-philosophical writings); **(3) Mathematics** (4 sub-parts — geometry / arithmetic / music / astronomy); **(4) Metaphysics** (*al-Ilāhiyyāt*, "the Divine Things" — 10 *maqālāt* / "treatises," covering being qua being, the categories, substance and accident, the modalities, the proof of the Necessary Being, the emanational cosmology, providence, prophecy and political philosophy). The work is **not a commentary** on Aristotle (unlike Averroes's commentaries) but **a re-derivation in Avicenna's own systematic voice** of the philosophical sciences, with substantial original contributions especially in metaphysics (essence/existence distinction; necessary/possible/impossible modality; the Necessary Being proof) and psychology (the *flying-man*; the immateriality of the rational soul). The title — *Shifāʾ*, "Healing" / "Cure" — is theological: philosophy as the *healing* of the soul from intellectual ignorance, paralleling the *Canon of Medicine*'s *Qānūn* as the healing of the body.

## Context
- **Political milieu.** The post-Samanid eastern Persianate world: Buyid amīrate at Hamadān (Avicenna serves as vizier to Shams al-Dawla); Kakuyid amīrate at Iṣfahān (Avicenna's principal patron in the productive final period). Mobile court-life with substantial library access; the *Shifāʾ* is partly composed in prison (Hamadān), partly on horseback during military expeditions (Avicenna's autobiographical *Sīra* via al-Jūzjānī).
- **Religious milieu.** Sunnī-Persian milieu with substantial *kalām*-theological context. Avicenna engages but does not narrowly belong to either Muʿtazilī or Ashʿarī kalām positions; his philosophical theology is its own program. **The *Shifāʾ* deliberately positions itself as philosophy, not kalām** — philosophy as autonomous demonstrative science of the principles of being, alongside but not subordinate to revealed religion.
- **Who was behind it.** Avicenna himself, with the disciple-scribe al-Jūzjānī recording portions as Avicenna dictated and continuing Avicenna's autobiography after his death. The composition is fast (~13 years for the whole; the Metaphysics in something like 20 days according to al-Jūzjānī's continuation).
- **Discovery / transmission.** Continuous Islamic / Persian manuscript transmission from the 11th c. forward. The Metaphysics section was translated into Latin in the 12th c. at the Toledo School (Avendauth, a Spanish-Jewish convert, working with Dominicus Gundissalinus) as ***Liber de philosophia prima sive scientia divina*** — the **principal channel for Avicennian metaphysics into medieval Latin scholasticism**. Modern critical edition: the multi-volume Cairo edition (Madkūr et al., 1952-1983); modern critical Latin edition Van Riet 1977-1983. Marmura 2005 English translation of the Metaphysics.

## Themes and motifs
- [[falsafa]] — the *Shifāʾ* is the canonical text of the falsafa tradition; "what falsafa is" can be substantially read off the *Shifāʾ*.
- [[unmoved-mover]] / Aristotelian primum movens — engaged and transformed into the Avicennian Necessary Being.
- [[ground-of-being]] — the Necessary Being whose essence is identical to existence is the *ground* of all contingent being; the metaphysics of *wujūd* (existence).
- [[hidden-god]] — Avicenna's God-of-the-philosophers is radically other; knowledge of God is by argument-from-effect to cause, not direct sensory encounter.
- [[divine-emanation]] — the Neoplatonic emanational cosmology (Necessary Being → First Intellect → cascade of celestial intellects + souls + spheres → sublunary Active Intellect) is the Avicennian cosmology.
- [[soul-immortality]] — the rational soul is substantially separable from body and so survives death (the *flying-man* and the proofs of soul-substantiality).
- [[tahafut-controversy]] — the *Shifāʾ* is the principal target of [[phase-5-045-al-ghazali-tahafut-al-falasifa|Ghazālī's *Tahāfut al-Falāsifa*]] and the principal defended-text of [[phase-5-046-ibn-rushd-tahafut-al-tahafut|Averroes's *Tahāfut al-Tahāfut*]].

## Connections (provisional)
- → [[phase-3-003-aristotle-metaphysics]] — *commentary-on* / *redaction-of*: the *Shifāʾ* Metaphysics is the principal Avicennian engagement with Aristotle's *Metaphysics*; **the medieval Latin reception of Aristotle's *Metaphysics* runs through the *Shifāʾ* Metaphysics**, not the Aristotelian text directly.
- → [[phase-4-019-plotinus-enneads]] — *redaction-of* / *misattributed-source*: the Neoplatonic emanational cosmology of the *Shifāʾ* derives from Plotinus *Enneads* IV-VI, received via the [[phase-5-048-theology-of-aristotle-arabic|*Theology of Aristotle*]] that Avicenna and the falsafa tradition believed was Aristotelian. **The Plotinian core of Avicennian cosmology is a misattribution-induced feature** that the Christian Renaissance only corrects with Casaubon-Ficino in the 16th c.
- → [[phase-5-048-theology-of-aristotle-arabic]] — *commentary-on* / *foundational-source*: Avicenna's own glosses on the *Theology of Aristotle* survive (the *Tafsīr* / commentary on the Theology of Aristotle).
- → [[phase-5-045-al-ghazali-tahafut-al-falasifa]] — *polemic-against*: Ghazālī's *Tahāfut* attacks the *Shifāʾ* on 20 specific theses (most famously: eternity of the world, God's knowledge of particulars, bodily resurrection).
- → [[phase-5-046-ibn-rushd-tahafut-al-tahafut]] — *defense-of*: Averroes's *Tahāfut al-Tahāfut* defends the *Shifāʾ* against Ghazālī's anathema (with some critical revision toward stricter Aristotelianism).
- → [[phase-5-019-maimonides-guide-for-the-perplexed]] — *shared-milieu* / *influenced-by*: Maimonides's *Guide* is substantially Avicenna-formed; many *Guide* arguments track Avicennian theses.
- → [[phase-5-024-aquinas-summa-theologiae]] — *commentary-on* / *direct-source*: Aquinas engages Avicenna more than any other non-Christian philosopher (~400 citations); the Third Way is an Avicennian argument; the essence/existence distinction structures the *De Ente et Essentia*. **The Latin scholastic reception of Aristotelian metaphysics is, in substantial part, the reception of Avicennian metaphysics.**

## Disputes
- **The eternity-of-the-world doctrine** (engaged at length in the *Shifāʾ* Physics and Metaphysics). Avicenna's mature view: the world is *eternal in time* but *contingent in essence* — eternally caused by the Necessary Being, but its being depends on God at every moment. Ghazālī read this as the Aristotelian eternity-of-the-world claim incompatible with Quranic creation-doctrine and made it Tahafut-charge #1.
- **God's knowledge of particulars.** Avicenna's mature view: God knows particulars *in a universal mode* (knowing the eternal causal nexus that produces each particular). Ghazālī read this as effectively denying that God knows particulars; Avicenna's defenders argue Ghazālī mis-states the position.
- **Bodily resurrection.** Avicenna's philosophical-theological view: the rational soul is separable from body and survives death; the eschatological promise of bodily resurrection is preserved in his system as a politically-pedagogically necessary teaching for the multitude. Ghazālī read this as denial of bodily resurrection; Tahafut-charge #3.
- **The *flying-man* and the *cogito*.** Avicenna's *flying-man* — imagine a person created in mid-air, blindfolded, with limbs separated — argues for the substantial self-existence of the soul apart from body. Whether this is genealogically connected to Descartes's *cogito* via Augustine and Suarez is partly contested; the structural parallel is striking and the historical pathway through scholasticism is reconstructible.
