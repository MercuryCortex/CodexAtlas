# Tablet of the Foliated Cross (Palenque)

## What it is
The hieroglyphic tablet of the **Temple of the Foliated Cross**, the second of Palenque's three Cross Group temples, carved for K'inich Kan Bahlam II and dedicated in 692 CE. Where the [[palenque-tablet-of-the-cross|Tablet of the Cross]] shows the world-tree as a cosmic axis, here the same tree is rendered as a flourishing **maize plant** — its leaves sprouting human heads as ripening cobs — emerging from a sacred-mountain (*witz*) monster above the waters of the underworld. It is the **sustenance and agricultural** face of the Cross Group program, bound to the Triad god **GII** (the infant lightning-and-maize deity K'awiil) and to the Maya theology of the king as guarantor of the maize harvest.

## Context
- **Political milieu.** Kan Bahlam II's Palenque at its zenith.
- **Religious milieu.** The Maya maize cosmology, in which the Maize God's death and rebirth model both the agricultural cycle and the king's regenerative role.
- **Who was behind it.** The Palenque royal workshop.
- **Discovery / transmission.** Recorded by early explorers; deciphered through 20th-century Maya epigraphy.

## Themes and motifs
- [[divine-kingship]] — the king is figured as the source of agricultural plenty, his rule identified with the life-giving maize-tree.

## Connections (provisional)
- → [[palenque-tablet-of-the-cross]] / [[palenque-tablet-of-the-sun]] — *set*: the maize/sustenance member of the three-temple Triad program.
- → [[hun-hunahpu]] — *thematic*: the Maya Maize God (Hun Hunahpu/First Father) whose cycle underlies the foliated-maize imagery.

## Disputes
- **GII / K'awiil identification.** The precise theology of the Triad's second member and its maize associations is refined in ongoing epigraphic work.

## MASSIVE WIN — cross-tradition edges
- **Maize as the tree of life.** The foliated cross is the clearest Classic Maya statement of the **maize-as-cosmic-tree** theology — the staple crop as the axis and substance of the world and of human flesh (the *Popol Vuh*'s humans are made of maize). It is the Mesoamerican form of the universal sacred-plant/tree-of-life motif, grown wholly independently of the Old World (Schele & Mathews 1998).
