# Second Epistle of John

## What it is
A note of thirteen verses — among the shortest books of the Bible — from "the elder" to a sister congregation figured as "the elect lady and her children." It urges mutual love and the old commandment, warns sharply against the "deceivers" who do not confess Jesus Christ "coming in the flesh," and instructs the community to deny such teachers hospitality, lest they share in their evil work.

## Primary text

### 2 John 7
*Trans. NRSV*
> Many deceivers have gone out into the world, those who do not confess that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh; any such person is the deceiver and the antichrist!

## Context
- **Religious milieu.** The same Johannine schism as 1 John, now bearing on inter-congregational hospitality.
- **Authorship.** "The elder," sharing the Johannine community's voice.
- **Discovery / transmission.** Among the antilegomena; its brevity and narrow circulation delayed canonical acceptance.

## Themes and motifs
- Love and truth — walking in the commandment of mutual love.
- Christological boundary — the refusal of fellowship to those who deny the incarnation.
- [[messianism]] — confessing "Jesus Christ coming in the flesh."
