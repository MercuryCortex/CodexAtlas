# Tonalpohualli

## What it is
The *tonalpohualli* ("count of days") is the **260-day ritual-divinatory calendar of the Aztecs and other Nahua peoples** — the Central Mexican counterpart of the Maya *tzolk'in*. It is generated by interlocking a cycle of **20 day-signs** with a cycle of **13 numbers**, yielding 260 unique day-names (20 × 13) before the sequence repeats. Each day-sign and number carries divinatory significance, and specialist diviners (*tonalpouhque*) read the calendar to determine fate, name children, and schedule ritual.

## Earliest attestation
The 260-day count is the deepest and most widely shared calendrical institution of Mesoamerica, attested from the mid-first-millennium BCE (Oaxaca/Zapotec inscriptions) and continuing to the Spanish conquest. The [[phase-8-002-codex-borgia|Codex Borgia]] is one of the great surviving divinatory manuscripts structured around it, laying out the 20 day-signs with their patron deities, the nine Lords of the Night, and the associated ritual prescriptions.

## Structure and function
- **20 day-signs** (Cipactli/crocodile, Ehecatl/wind, Calli/house, … Xochitl/flower) each with a patron deity.
- **13 numbers** cycling independently, so each day is a unique sign-plus-number pair.
- The tonalpohualli meshes with the **365-day solar count** (*xiuhpohualli*) so that a given combination of ritual-day and solar-day recurs only once every **52 years** — the **Calendar Round**, whose completion was marked by the New Fire Ceremony.

## Cross-tradition findings
- **Independent permutation-calendar.** The 260-day count is a self-contained **permutational time-reckoning system** — time organized not by astronomical period but by the meshing of two number-cycles. This makes it a structural cousin of other combinatorial ritual systems (the Chinese sexagenary 60-day/year cycle of stems-and-branches; binary-permutation divination such as the Yi Jing) — an independent Mesoamerican solution to the problem of generating a long non-repeating ritual sequence. Wired as a parallel to the [[venus-cycle]] Venus-reckoning that shares the same Mesoamerican calendrical matrix.
