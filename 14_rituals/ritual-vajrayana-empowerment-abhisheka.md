# Abhiṣeka — Vajrayāna Empowerment

The principal initiatic ritual of tantric Buddhism — the *empowerment* (Sanskrit *abhiṣeka*, Tibetan *wang*) by which a qualified teacher transmits authorization to practice a specific tantric cycle to the disciple. The word *abhiṣeka* literally means "sprinkling" or "anointing" — the ritual incorporates royal-coronation imagery (the disciple is consecrated as a sovereign of the maṇḍala). Standard structure is the four-empowerment sequence (vase-empowerment + secret-empowerment + wisdom-knowledge-empowerment + word-empowerment) in the Anuttarayoga tantras. Without abhiṣeka the disciple cannot practice or read the corresponding tantric texts. Found across Indian Vajrayāna, Tibetan Buddhism, and Japanese Shingon.
