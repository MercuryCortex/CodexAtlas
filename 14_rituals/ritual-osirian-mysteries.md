# Osirian Mysteries

The annual ritual re-enactment of the death + resurrection of [[osiris]], performed at [[place-abydos]] in the month of Khoiak. One of the most important annual religious festivals of pharaonic + later Egypt; the participating community performed a ritual drama of Osiris's death, mourning by Isis + Nephthys, mummification, and resurrection. The Abydos site preserves the so-called "Mysteries Stela" and the Osirion (the Osiris-temple complex behind Seti I's mortuary temple).
