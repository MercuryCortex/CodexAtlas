# Maya Long Count

The Long Count is the **cosmic-chronological calendar of the Classic Maya** — a positional notation that locates any date in a 5,125-year cosmic cycle (the b'ak'tun cycle), with a mythic zero at **11 August -3114 (Gregorian)** that the Maya scribal tradition treats as the creation event of the current world-age, and a cycle-completion 13.0.0.0.0 that fell on **21 December +2012**.

The Long Count is **the chronological substrate of all Maya astronomical record-keeping** — including the [[astronomy-venus-tables-dresden|Dresden Codex Venus tables]] — and the foundation of the most precise pre-modern non-Old-World astronomical-calendrical system.

## The structure

A Long Count date is a 5-position number written like 9.16.4.10.8:

- **B'ak'tun** (144,000 days = ~394.3 years)
- **K'atun** (7,200 days = ~19.7 years)
- **Tun** (360 days)
- **Winal** (20 days)
- **K'in** (1 day)

Combined with the 260-day **tzolkin** (sacred count) and the 365-day **haab** (civil year), which together produce the 52-year **Calendar Round**, the Maya calendrical system uniquely identifies any day across millennia.

## The 13.0.0.0.0 cosmic cycle

A full b'ak'tun cycle is 13 × 144,000 = 1,872,000 days = ~5,125.37 years. The Maya scribal tradition projects the current cycle backwards to the **mythic creation event of 13.0.0.0.0 = 4 Ahau 8 Cumku = 11 August -3114** (using the standard GMT correlation 584283). Quiriguá Stela C explicitly records this creation event — the gods raising the world-tree in primordial darkness — using Long Count notation.

The next 13.0.0.0.0 cycle-completion fell on **21 December +2012** — the focus of substantial popular misinterpretation as an "end of the world" event. The Maya texts that mention this date treat it as a cycle-completion + a renewal, not as cosmic destruction (Stuart 2011 ch. 8 demolishes the 2012-doomsday reading).

## The astronomical substrate

Long Count operation is the chronological-mathematical infrastructure of:

- **The Dresden Codex Venus tables** — Venus heliacal-rising predictions to hour-precision across a 104-year (Venus × 65 = Earth × 104) supercycle
- **Lunar series notation** on Classic stelae — moon-age + lunation-period observations integrated into dynastic chronology
- **Eclipse warnings** in the Dresden Codex (the Eclipse Table)
- **Mars + Jupiter periods** preserved in the codices

This is the **largest-scale predictive astronomical-calendrical system developed independently in the pre-modern Americas** + one of the cleanest documented cases of fully indigenous mathematical-astronomical sophistication outside the Old-World Babylonian–Greek–Indian–Chinese chain.
