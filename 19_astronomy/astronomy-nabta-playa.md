# Nabta Playa Calendar Circle

**Nabta Playa** is a Late Neolithic ritual landscape in the Egyptian Sahara (Western Desert, ~100 km west of Abu Simbel), occupied c. -7500 to -3500 by pre-pharaonic Nubian cattle-pastoralists. Around the centre of the site is a **megalithic stone circle** — a small (~4 m diameter) calendar circle whose sightlines align with the **summer-solstice sunrise** + the **cardinal points**. The circle is dated c. -4500 by associated C-14 contexts, making it **older than Stonehenge by ~2000 years** and **older than the Giza pyramids by ~2000 years**.

Nabta is the **earliest preserved astronomically-aligned stone monument identified by archaeologists** + the first of the three great pre-pyramidal solar-oriented Neolithic landscapes (Nabta + [[astronomy-newgrange-solstice|Newgrange]] + [[astronomy-stonehenge-alignment|Stonehenge]]).

## The site

Nabta Playa was a seasonal lake during the African Humid Period (the early-mid Holocene wet phase before the Sahara dried into desert c. -3000). The lakebed supported substantial pastoralist occupation; the site includes cattle-burial pits, megalithic alignments, sandstone slabs deliberately quarried + transported from up to 1 km away, and several stone-circle features.

The **calendar circle** itself is small — ~4 m diameter, with a ring of low slabs + two pairs of taller "gate-stones" forming sightlines. Malville et al. 1998 identify two astronomical alignments:

- **A north–south meridian line** (cardinal orientation)
- **A summer-solstice sunrise sightline** (the dominant horizon event of the season the lake was full)

## Significance

If the alignments are correctly identified — and the publication in *Nature* indicates broad scholarly acceptance — Nabta is the earliest preserved evidence of:

- **Observational astronomy** integrated with monumental architecture
- **Cardinal orientation** as a deliberate ritual-spatial principle
- **Solar-calendar timing** of a seasonal occupation cycle

The Nabta builders are **2000 years before Stonehenge** and **1500 years before the unified Egyptian state**. Whether their tradition fed forward into Predynastic Egyptian astronomical-symbolic culture (cattle-cult, cardinal orientation, megalithic burial) is contested (Wengrow 2006); the simplest reading treats Nabta as an independent Saharan Neolithic development extinguished by the mid-Holocene desertification.
