# Chinese 28 Lunar Mansions (xiu)

The **28 xiu** (二十八宿 *èrshíbā xiù* — "twenty-eight lodges") are the canonical Chinese division of the lunar ecliptic-band into 28 asterisms through which the Moon passes during its sidereal month of ~27.32 days. The system is the structural Chinese parallel to the Indian [[astronomy-indian-nakshatras|27/28 nakshatras]] and the [[astronomy-mul-apin|MUL.APIN]] Mesopotamian 18-station Path of the Moon — one of the three great pre-modern lunar-mansion frameworks.

The earliest preserved labelled list of the 28 xiu is the **lacquer-chest cover from the tomb of the Marquis Yi of Zeng** (Hubei province), dated by the funerary inscription to -433. The 28 names + four-palace organization are written across the top of the chest in clear Warring-States script — pushing the closed-system attestation back into the 5th century BCE.

## The four palaces

The 28 mansions are grouped into **four palaces** of 7 mansions each, corresponding to the four cardinal directions + four seasons + four totem animals:

- **東方青龍 Eastern Azure Dragon** (spring) — Jiǎo 角 / Kàng 亢 / Dī 氐 / Fáng 房 / Xīn 心 / Wěi 尾 / Jī 箕 (includes Spica, Arcturus, Antares, the Scorpius–Sagittarius region)
- **北方玄武 Northern Black Tortoise** (winter) — Dǒu 斗 / Niú 牛 / Nǚ 女 / Xū 虛 / Wēi 危 / Shì 室 / Bì 壁
- **西方白虎 Western White Tiger** (autumn) — Kuí 奎 / Lóu 婁 / Wèi 胃 / Mǎo 昴 / Bì 畢 / Zī 觜 / Shēn 參 (includes the Pleiades + Hyades + Orion)
- **南方朱雀 Southern Vermilion Bird** (summer) — Jǐng 井 / Guǐ 鬼 / Liǔ 柳 / Xīng 星 / Zhāng 張 / Yì 翼 / Zhěn 軫 (includes the head of Hydra + Cancer)

## The parallel-form problem

The Chinese 28 xiu + Indian 27/28 nakshatras share **structural identity** (~28 lunar stations) + **several anchor stars in common**:

- Antares (Alpha Scorpii) = Indian Jyeṣṭhā = Chinese Xīn 心 "Heart"
- Pleiades = Indian Kṛttikā = Chinese Mǎo 昴
- Aldebaran region = Indian Rohiṇī = Chinese Bì 畢

This is one of the deepest unresolved questions in comparative history of astronomy. Two camps:

- **Common-origin (Pingree 1989)** — argues for a Mesopotamian ancestor (MUL.APIN's 18-station Path of the Moon) transmitted independently to India + China before -1000, surviving as parallel daughter-systems. Strongest evidence: the shared anchor-stars at the same ecliptic positions are hard to explain by independent invention.
- **Independent-invention (Needham, Sun-Kistemaker)** — argues that ~28 lunar stations is the natural arithmetic solution to the 27.32-day sidereal lunar month; the shared anchor-stars are the brightest stars on the ecliptic-band and any system will pick them up. No documented pre-Han Chinese–Mesopotamian astronomical contact has been established.

Both positions are academically respectable; Pankenier 2013 ch. 1 gives the contemporary survey.
