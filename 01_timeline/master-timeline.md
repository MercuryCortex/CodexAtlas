# Master Timeline — Codex Atlas

The chronological spine of the vault. Every document, event, and (where dated) deity attestation gets a row here. Sorted by earliest-date.

> **Convention.** Dates as integers, negative for BCE. "~" prefix = approximate / scholarly range. Where a range exists, we use the *earliest* end for sorting and note the range in the entry.

> **How this populates.** The phase research agents seed this file as they create document nodes. Manual edits welcome — but maintain sort order.

**Phase 3 cross-current notes.**
- **Plato's *Timaeus* demiurge → Gnostic demiurge** (Phase 4) — pivotal; Plato's *good* demiurge is inverted into the Sethian Yaldabaoth and Valentinian craftsman (Turner 2001).
- **1 Enoch's Watchers → Jewish apocalyptic and early Christian demonology**.
- **Zoroastrian dualism → Hellenistic-Jewish angelology + Christian eschatology** (continuing from Phase 2).
- **Mystery cults (Eleusinian, Dionysiac, Mithraic) → resonances with early Christian sacramental theology** — *parallel-motif*, not direct dependence (Bremmer 2014, Bowden 2010).
- **Stoic *logos* → Philo's *logos* → Johannine *logos* prologue (Jn 1:1)** — three-step transmission of the single most consequential pre-Christian theological concept.

**Phase 5 cross-current notes.**
- **Bogomil/Cathar dualism ↔ Manichaeism (Phase 4)** — the most-debated transmission edge in medieval religious history. Stoyanov 2000, Hamilton 1979 affirm continuous transmission via Paulicians; Moore 2012, Pegg 2008 dispute. Documented in [[phase-5-020-interrogatio-iohannis]] and [[phase-5-023-liber-de-duobus-principiis]].
- **Pseudo-Dionysius → Maximus → Eriugena → Eckhart → Cusa (Phase 6)** — the Christian Neoplatonic chain, load-bearing for later Christian mysticism.
- **Vedanta debate triangle** — Shankara (Advaita) vs Ramanuja (Vishishtadvaita) vs Madhva (Dvaita) — all three commenting on the same *Brahmasūtra*, wired together as polemic-against edges.
- **Sufi ↔ Neoplatonism** — Ibn ʿArabī's *waḥdat al-wujūd* and Suhrawardī's Illuminationism both structurally Plotinian, mediated via the Arabic *Theology of Aristotle* and the Procline *Liber de Causis*.
- **Kabbalah ↔ Gnostic emanationism** — Scholem's contested thesis of structural parallels Sefirot↔Pleroma, Shekhinah↔Sophia. Idel 1988 substantial pushback.
- **Cordoba and Toledo translation movements ↔ Latin scholasticism** — Aristotle, Galen, Avicenna, Averroes, and "Algazel" (Ghazālī) entered Christian Europe via Arabic translations; foundational for Aquinas's project.
- **Heart Sutra Chinese-apocrypha thesis** (Nattier 1992) — one of the most consequential late-20th-c. shifts in Buddhist textual scholarship.
- **Zohar pseudepigraphy** — Scholem 1941's philological demonstration that the *Zohar*'s Aramaic is artificial; Moses de León as the principal author.

**Phase 6 cross-current notes — the esoteric revival is what makes Phase 6 distinctive.**
- **Corpus Hermeticum (Phase 4) → Ficino's Pimander (Phase 6, 1463)** — direct manuscript-transmission edge; the founding act of Renaissance Hermeticism. Phase 4 content returns to active circulation through Phase 6.
- **Plato/Plotinus (Phase 3/4) → Ficino's Theologia Platonica (1474) and Latin Plotinus (1492)** — direct revival of ancient philosophical-religious texts for Christian Europe.
- **Zohar / Sefer Yetzirah (Phase 5) → Pico's 900 Conclusions (1486) and Reuchlin's De Arte Cabalistica (1517)** — direct Kabbalistic-transmission edge; Christian Kabbalah is born by Pico's commissioning of Flavius Mithridates' Latin translations.
- **1492 Sephardic Expulsion → Safed Kabbalah → Lurianic system (1570–1572)** — Scholem's central thesis: cosmic exile in Lurianic theology theologizes Sephardic historical exile.
- **Lurianic Kabbalah → Sabbatean / Frankist theology** — internal Jewish polemic; Nathan of Gaza's theological transformation of Lurianic categories sustains belief through and beyond the 1666 apostasy.
- **Casaubon's 1614 redating** — demarcation event between credulous and historical-critical reception of esoterica. Hermetic *authority* ends; Hermetic *historical study* begins. Yates' classic analysis.
- **Ficino's *prisca theologia*** — the central thesis that Hermes, Zoroaster, Orpheus, Pythagoras, Plato all taught the same ancient revealed wisdom — is the *Phase 6 thesis* about Phases 1–4. A meta-edge: a Phase 6 theory *about* the relations between ancient traditions.
- **Böhme → German Idealism (Phase 7)** — Hegel calls Böhme "the first German philosopher"; Schelling's late *Weltalter* and *Philosophie der Offenbarung* are extended Boehmian engagements. Flag for Phase 7.
- **Swedenborg → Blake, Emerson, Spiritualism (Phase 7)** — Swedenborgian afterlife and esoteric Christianity enter 19th-c. American and English literature and religion. Flag for Phase 7.
- **Rosicrucian manifestos → 18th-c. Freemasonry (Anderson 1723) and higher-degree systems** — esoteric initiatory tradition continues across the century.
- **The printing press (Gutenberg 1450s+) is the material condition of Phase 6.** Ficino's 1471 Pimander, Luther's 1517 Theses, Calvin's 1559 Institutes, the Rosicrucian manifestos, the Tridentine documents, the Adi Granth: without rapid printing-press distribution, none of the Phase 6 movements happen as they did.
- **The Yates thesis** — Frances Yates (1964, 1972) reconstructed Renaissance Hermeticism as a coherent magical-philosophical project running from Ficino through Bruno to Dee, the Rosicrucians, and (in modified form) into Freemasonry and the Scientific Revolution. Current scholarship (Copenhaver, Hanegraaff, Gilly, Stevenson) has substantially refined the thesis but the broad trajectory remains the working framework.

- **Catholic-saint syncretism in Afro-diasporic religions.** Each Yoruba orisha is paired with a Catholic saint in operational syncretism — Obatalá ↔ Las Mercedes; Oshún ↔ Caridad del Cobre; Shangó ↔ Santa Bárbara; Yemayá ↔ Virgen de Regla; Ogún ↔ St. Peter / Santiago; Elegguá ↔ Niño de Atocha / San Antonio. The Vodou *lwa* have parallel pairings (Legba ↔ St. Peter / St. Lazarus; Damballa ↔ St. Patrick; Erzulie Dantor ↔ Black Madonna of Czestochowa). The pairings are theologically internalized, not mere camouflage. Ref: Brandon 1993; Murphy 1988; McAlister 2002; Desmangles 1992.
- **Quetzalcoatl ↔ Christ syncretism in colonial Mexico.** Spanish missionaries and Indigenous-Christian writers (Sahagún, Durán, Ixtlilxóchitl) identified Quetzalcoatl with St. Thomas the Apostle or with Christ. Lafaye 1976 *Quetzalcoatl and Guadalupe* is the standard study. The Cortés-as-returning-Quetzalcoatl narrative is itself a post-conquest construction (Lockhart 1993; Restall 2003; Townsend 2003).
- **Coatlicue → Tonantzin → Virgin of Guadalupe.** The 1531 Tepeyac apparition is reported at the site of a Tonantzin (or Coatlicue) temple. Lafaye 1976; Brading 2001.
- **Mormonism's "Lamanite" appropriation of Indigenous American identity** (Phase 7 cross-current with Phase 8): the Book of Mormon (1830) claims pre-Columbian American peoples descend from a lost Israelite tribe; this Phase-7 NRM thesis is substantially disconfirmed by genetic and archaeological evidence and is treated by Indigenous scholars as religious appropriation of Indigenous American identity. Ref: Givens 2002 (defensive); Murphy 2003 (DNA critique).
- **Black Elk → Jung; Black Elk → Joseph Campbell.** Carl Jung explicitly read *Black Elk Speaks* (1932, Neihardt) and incorporated material from Black Elk's great vision into his archetypal psychology; Joseph Campbell drew on Black Elk for *The Hero with a Thousand Faces* and *The Power of Myth*. This is a Phase-7 → Phase-8 reception edge running both productively (legitimating Indigenous religious materials in non-Indigenous discourse) and problematically (projecting depth-psychological frames onto Lakota material). Ref: DeMallie 1984; Holler 1995.
- **Theosophy's "root races" / Blavatsky → Indigenous religions** (Phase 7 misappropriation flag). Blavatsky's *Secret Doctrine* (1888) constructs a pseudo-scientific racial-religion synthesis appropriating Indigenous religious materials worldwide (Atlanteans, Lemurians); modern New Age and Hancock-style pseudoarchaeology continues this pattern with Aboriginal religion and Indigenous American religion. Ref: Mulcock 1997; Sutton 2003. Flag as Phase 7 misappropriation edge.
- **Norse → Wagner → Tolkien.** Snorri Sturluson's *Prose Edda* is the principal channel through which post-medieval Europe knew Norse religion; Wagner's *Ring* (1869–76) and Tolkien's mythopoetic project both depend on Snorri. A Phase-8 → Phase-7 reception edge.
- **Kalevala → Tolkien → Sibelius.** Tolkien's Kullervo material (Túrin Turambar in *Silmarillion*) descends directly from the Kalevala; Sibelius's *Lemminkäinen Suite*, *Kullervo*, *Tapiola* set Kalevala material. A Phase-8 → Phase-7 reception edge.
- **Kebra Nagast → Rastafari.** Ethiopian Solomonic-genealogical theology (Kebra Nagast ~1322) provides the foundational text for Rastafarian identification of Haile Selassie as messianic Black King. Phase-8 internal cross-current.
- **Cosmogonic-genealogical parallels.** Independent convergence of cosmogonic patterns across Phase 8 traditions and earlier Phases (creation-by-word in Genesis 1 ↔ Popol Vuh; primordial-waters in Enuma Elish ↔ Kumulipo; cosmic-egg in Orphic ↔ Kalevala; sky-earth separation in Egyptian Geb-Nut ↔ Maori Ranginui-Papatūānuku) demonstrates the structural-typological richness of comparative cosmogony without requiring contact hypotheses.
- **Phase-8 "modern recovery" parallel to Phase 7.** The Phase-7 pattern of modern recovery (Champollion 1822, George Smith 1872, Ras Shamra 1928, Nag Hammadi 1945) has Phase-8 parallels: Kalevala compilation (Lönnrot 1835/1849); Popol Vuh's broader reception (Brasseur de Bourbourg 1861); Maya hieroglyphic decipherment (Knorozov 1952 → 1990s); Huarochirí Manuscript's modern circulation (Arguedas 1966 → Salomon-Urioste 1991); academic Ifá textualization (Abimbola 1968 onward).
