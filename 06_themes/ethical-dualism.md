# Ethical Dualism

Two *moral* paths or inclinations confront the human being: a way of righteousness and a way of wickedness, or an impulse to good and an impulse to evil, within a single creation under a single God. Distinguished from cosmic dualism ([[dualism-cosmic]]) — there two principles war on a cosmic scale; here moral combat is internal to humans, who must choose. The same culture (e.g., Qumran) can host both simultaneously.

## Manifestations
- **Deuteronomic "two ways":** [[phase-2-011-hebrew-bible-d-source]] — Deut 30:15: "See, I have set before you today life and good, death and evil"; covenant blessing-curse pattern. The earliest articulation in the Hebrew tradition.
- **Zoroastrian:** [[phase-2-002-gathas-of-zarathustra]] — *asha* (truth) vs. *druj* (the Lie); humans must choose. Inseparable from but distinct from Zoroastrian cosmic dualism — the Twin Spirits chose, and humans must analogously choose.
- **Sirach 15:** [[phase-3-007-sirach]] — God set fire and water, life and death, good and evil before the human; "whichever he chooses will be given to him." Classic statement of moral choice within monotheism.
- **Qumran:** [[phase-3-011-dead-sea-scrolls]] — 1QS 3–4 "Two Spirits" — the Prince of Light and Angel of Darkness contend in each human heart; sectarian community is the realm of light. *Both* cosmic and ethical dualism interwoven.
- **Rabbinic *yetzer*:** *Yetzer ha-tov* (good inclination) and *yetzer ha-ra'* (evil inclination) — moral psychology developed in the tannaitic and amoraic periods. Notably the *yetzer ha-ra'* is not pure evil but the necessary drive (sexuality, ambition) that, controlled, is creative.
- **Pauline anthropology:** [[phase-3-015-pauline-epistles]] — Romans 7's internal conflict ("I do not understand my own actions"); Galatians 5's flesh-vs-spirit. Less metaphysical than Qumran's two spirits, more psychological.
- **Didache and *Two Ways*:** *Didache* 1–6 (ca. early 2nd c.) presents a "way of life" and "way of death" with extensive ethical specification. The independent *Doctrina Apostolorum* and Barnabas 18–20 preserve parallel Two Ways tradition. Van de Sandt & Flusser (2002) demonstrate substantial Jewish provenance.
- **Apocalyptic typological:** [[phase-3-009-jubilees]] sets sharp light-darkness boundaries on calendrical and ritual lines.

## Why this matters as a separate category
Cosmic dualism is a *metaphysical* claim about the structure of reality. Ethical dualism is a *practical-moral* claim about the human moral situation. The two can be combined (Qumran, Manichaeism) but a tradition can have one without the other. Mainstream Judaism and Christianity carry strong ethical dualism with monistic metaphysics ("one God, one creation; two ways before each person").
