# Anti-Roman Polemic

The literary-theological tradition in Jewish, Christian, and adjacent texts of the late Republic through late Empire (roughly 1st c. BCE – 5th c. CE) that codes the Roman imperial power as cosmic-evil, eschatological-doomed, idolatrous, or as the latest in a sequence of world-empires destined for destruction by divine intervention. The standard figural ciphers — *Babylon* for Rome (Revelation, Sibylline Oracles, rabbinic *Esau* / *Edom*), the *fourth beast* of Daniel, the great whore of Babylon — code resistance in apocalyptic language safe from prosecution. After Constantine the polemic mutates: Christian Rome becomes positively typed in some texts, retains negative *Edom* coding in rabbinic literature.

## Manifestations
- **Daniel's four kingdoms:** [[phase-3-008-book-of-daniel]] — Dan 2 (statue), Dan 7 (four beasts) — the fourth beast / kingdom of iron, with little horn and blasphemous mouth, is by the Hellenistic-redaction reading Antiochus IV Epiphanes (167–164 BCE); but the schema is open and rapidly reapplied to Rome in Roman-era rereadings (Josephus, *Antiquities* 10.276 reads Daniel's fourth as Rome explicitly).
- **Sibylline Oracles:** [[phase-3-010-sibylline-oracles]] — the Jewish-Hellenistic Sibylline corpus, especially *Sib. Or.* 3, 4, 5 (composed 2nd c. BCE – 2nd c. CE), prophesies the destruction of Rome in apocalyptic-vatic Greek hexameter; pseudo-pagan-vehicle for Jewish anti-Roman prophetic content.
- **4 Maccabees and martyrology:** [[phase-3-019-4-maccabees]] — Antiochus IV is the immediate target, but Roman-era readers easily transposed; the philosophical-martyrological content of *4 Maccabees* feeds Christian anti-Roman martyrology (see [[martyrdom-theology]]).
- **Revelation of John.** Rev 13 (the beast), Rev 17–18 (the great whore of Babylon, seven hills) — the most sustained anti-Roman apocalypse, late 1st c. CE, probably composed in the aftermath of Domitianic local difficulties or as memory of Neronian-Flavian violence. Babylon = Rome; the beast = the imperial cult; 666 is gematric for Nero. The locus classicus.
- **Luke-Acts complication:** [[phase-3-018-luke-acts]] — Luke-Acts is structurally more accommodating to Rome: Pontius Pilate finds Jesus innocent; Roman officials repeatedly defend Paul; the Empire is a stage for the gospel rather than the cosmic enemy. The relative Lukan irenicism is striking against the apocalyptic anti-Roman strand and is variously interpreted (apologetic for Roman audiences; eschatological deferral; genuine theological pluralism within early Christianity).
- **Rabbinic *Edom* / *Esau* coding:** [[phase-4-031-mishnah]], [[phase-4-033-babylonian-talmud]] — post-70 CE rabbinic literature regularly codes Rome as Edom / Esau, the perpetual enemy-brother of Israel-Jacob. The coding survives the Christianization of Rome and becomes a way of marking *Christianitas* itself as Edomite from a Jewish standpoint — a persistence with consequences for later Jewish-Christian relations.

## Disputes
- **How "really" anti-Roman is Revelation?** Howard-Brook & Gwyther 1999 and earlier liberation-theological readings stress its anti-imperialism; conservative readings have preferred a *spiritual* or *futuristic* Babylon. Recent historical-critical consensus reads the anti-Roman polemic as historically primary, with subsequent allegorical-spiritualizing readings as later overlays.
- **Lukan irenicism puzzle.** Why does Luke-Acts soften where Mark and Matthew don't? Cassidy and others read it as Christian apologetic for Roman officials; Esler reads it as social-stratificational (Luke's community includes higher-status Romans); Walaskay reads it as theologically principled.
- **Continuity / transformation post-Constantine.** Eusebius's *Ecclesiastical History* and *Vita Constantini* invert the polemic — Christian Rome becomes God's chosen instrument. The polemical tradition survives in Donatist and later Augustinian critique of imperial pretensions to mediate divine grace; the *Civitas Dei* / *civitas mundi* contrast preserves the bipolarity.
