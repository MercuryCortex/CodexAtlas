# The Five Buddha Families

The Vajrayana / Tibetan-Buddhist organization of the principal cosmological-symbolic mandala into five Buddha-families (*pañcakula*), each headed by a *Tathāgata* / Dhyāni-Buddha and associated with a primal wisdom, a defiled passion converted to wisdom, a direction, a color, a Buddha-realm, a consort, and a bodhisattva. The canonical arrangement:

- **Buddha family** — Vairocana, center, white, *dharmadhātu* wisdom (delusion → discriminative wisdom).
- **Vajra family** — Akṣobhya, east, blue, mirror-like wisdom (hatred → clear seeing).
- **Ratna family** — Ratnasambhava, south, yellow, wisdom of equality (pride → equanimity).
- **Padma family** — Amitābha, west, red, discriminating-awareness wisdom (desire → loving discernment).
- **Karma family** — Amoghasiddhi, north, green, all-accomplishing wisdom (envy → effective compassion).

- [[phase-5-029-bardo-thodol]] — the Tibetan Book of the Dead organizes the after-death visions around encounters with the Five Tathagatas of the five families across consecutive days of the bardo.
- [[phase-5-006-vijnana-bhairava-tantra]] — Indian-tantric mandala-traditions provide the Hindu-Śaiva counterpart structures.

The schema is one of the most influential Vajrayana cosmological frameworks; it informs Tibetan iconography, mantra, mudrā, sādhana, and psychological-practice instruction.
