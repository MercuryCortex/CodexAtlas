# Eschatological War

The motif of a decisive final battle — supernaturally orchestrated, often cosmically scaled, dividing humanity (and angels) into definitive opposing camps — that concludes the present age and inaugurates the eschaton. The Qumran *War Scroll* (1QM) gives the most elaborate Second-Temple choreography (Sons of Light vs. Sons of Darkness with Belial, in seven engagements over forty years); Daniel 7–12 frames history as a sequence of beast-kingdoms culminating in the "one like a son of man." The Christian Apocalypse (Rev 16, 19–20) inherits and re-deploys the motif (Armageddon, the battle of the great day of God).

- [[phase-3-011-dead-sea-scrolls]] — the *War Scroll* (1QM) — the most systematic ancient eschatological-war scenario.
- [[phase-3-008-book-of-daniel]] — Daniel 11–12; the eschatological deliverance of Israel after the final tyrant.
- [[phase-3-004-1-enoch]] — Book of the Watchers / Book of Dreams; the angelic battles preparatory to judgment.
- [[phase-3-010-sibylline-oracles]] — pagan-Jewish-Christian apocalyptic war material.

The motif is *ethical-dualist* in structure (cf. [[ethical-dualism]]) — humanity is sorted into two camps; the war is the resolution. Iranian (Zoroastrian) influence on the schema is widely argued (Boyce, Hultgård); the case is debated.
