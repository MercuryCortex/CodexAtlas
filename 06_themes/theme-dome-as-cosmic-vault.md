# Theme Dome As Cosmic Vault

Architectural reading of the dome as image of the celestial vault — explicit in Cassius Dio's reading of [[pantheon-rome|the Pantheon]] and in the Byzantine theological commentary on [[hagia-sophia|Hagia Sophia]] (cf. Patriarch Photios' dedicatory homily).
