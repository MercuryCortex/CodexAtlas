# Divine Emanation

The doctrine that the manifest world unfolds *from* an ineffable source by graded, necessary degrees — *not* by free creation *ex nihilo*. Plotinus' *Enneads* gives the canonical Western formulation: from the One proceeds Intellect (*Nous*), from Intellect proceeds Soul, from Soul the sensible cosmos. Each lower hypostasis is a less unified, more pluralized expression of what was already total in the higher.

The same structural pattern (different vocabulary) shows up in:
- Gnostic *pleromas* (e.g., [[phase-4-002-apocryphon-of-john]]): aeons proceed from the Monad in syzygies
- Kabbalistic *sefirot* (Phase 5–6): ten emanations from Ein Sof; Idel's contested thesis is that the structural similarity to Gnostic pleromas is genuine influence not coincidence
- Sufi *wahdat al-wujud* (Ibn Arabi): all things are theophanies / *tajalliyat* of the Real
- Christian Neoplatonism via Pseudo-Dionysius → Eriugena → Eckhart

The contrast case is **creatio ex nihilo** (free divine creation from nothing), the official Rabbinic / Christian / Islamic doctrine. Emanationist theologies sit uneasily inside official monotheisms and have repeatedly been disciplined as heresy (Bruno, Eckhart's condemned propositions, the Islamic *falsafa* critiques by Ghazali).
