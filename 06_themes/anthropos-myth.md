# Anthropos Myth

A primordial cosmic *Man* (Greek *Anthrōpos*, Sanskrit *Puruṣa*, Hebrew *Adam Kadmon*, Iranian *Gayōmart*, Manichaean *Primal Man*) is either *dismembered* to make the cosmos, or *falls* into matter (whose later redemption is the cosmic project), or *contains* the cosmos as macranthropos, or is the *first-emanation* of the deity in human form. The motif is independent of (but interacts with) the biblical Adam-as-first-creature motif, and is a major substratum of [[microcosm-macrocosm]] thought.

## Manifestations
- **Vedic *Puruṣa-sūkta*:** [[phase-2-001-rig-veda-family-books]] — *Rig Veda* 10.90: the cosmic Puruṣa, a thousand-headed thousand-eyed cosmic person, is dismembered in primordial sacrifice; from his mouth come the Brahmins, from his arms the Kshatriyas, etc. — the locus classicus of cosmogonic dismemberment-of-anthropos, and of the *varṇa* social-cosmological doctrine.
- **Iranian Gayōmart:** [[phase-2-002-gathas-of-zarathustra]] (via later Zoroastrian Pahlavi sources) — the primal man Gayōmart is the first human, killed by Ahriman; from his body grow the metals; from his seed the first human couple. Manichaeism inherits this directly.
- **Mesopotamian:** [[phase-1-008-enuma-elish]] — humans created from the blood of slain Kingu mixed with clay (*Enūma Elish* tablet VI); the slain-divine-being motif behind anthropogony. Not exactly the same as Puruṣa-style cosmogony but the same family of "cosmos / humans from a slain divine body."
- **Hebrew Adam:** [[phase-2-005-hebrew-bible-j-source]] — Genesis 2 — Adam formed from dust, breath of life from God. The Hebrew Adam is *not* the cosmic Anthropos but the *first creature*; later Jewish mystical traditions ([[phase-5-027-sefer-ha-zohar]]) develop *Adam Kadmon* — the primordial cosmic Anthropos, the *sefirot*-bodied first emanation — distinct from the biblical Adam.
- **Hermetic *Anthrōpos*:** [[phase-4-011-corpus-hermeticum-i]] *Poimandres* CH I.12–14 — the heavenly *Anthrōpos*, image of the Father, descends through the spheres, falls in love with Nature, joins her — and from that union arise embodied humans, who therefore have a divine spark in mortal flesh. Cosmic-Anthropos as the *anthropogonic explainer*.
- **Gnostic / Sethian:** [[phase-4-002-apocryphon-of-john]] — the upper-aeon Adamas / *Geradamas*, of which earthly Adam is the inferior demiurgic copy. The Sethians elaborate a multi-layered Anthropos doctrine.
- **Manichaean Primal Man:** [[phase-4-014-cologne-mani-codex]], [[phase-4-015-kephalaia-of-the-teacher]] — Mani's cosmogonic drama: the Father of Greatness sends the *Primal Man* to fight Darkness; the Primal Man is defeated; his five "Sons" (Light-elements) are swallowed by Darkness; the cosmos is the machinery by which they are liberated. The most fully cosmogonic-soteriological Anthropos doctrine.
- **Kabbalistic *Adam Kadmon*:** [[phase-5-027-sefer-ha-zohar]] — the *Zohar*'s primordial cosmic Anthropos: the body of *Adam Kadmon* is the *etz ḥayyim* of the [[sefirot]]; the human body is the microcosm. Lurianic Kabbalah makes *Adam Kadmon* the central cosmodramatic figure.

## Disputes
- **Iranian origin thesis.** Bousset and the *Religionsgeschichtliche Schule* argued the Gnostic Anthropos derives from Iranian Gayōmart speculation; Boyce, Colpe and others have refined the case. Some scholars (Williams, King) downplay the Iranian channel and emphasize Greek-philosophical and Jewish-mystical sources.
- **Whether Vedic Puruṣa-sūkta and Iranian Gayōmart share Indo-European prehistory.** Lincoln (*Myth, Cosmos, and Society* 1986) argues an inherited Indo-European cosmogonic-dismemberment myth lies behind both — Norse Ymir, Roman Romulus, Vedic Puruṣa, Iranian Gayōmart are local realizations of one inherited mythologem. Disputed in detail.
- **Christological convergence.** Late-Pauline Christology (Colossians, Ephesians) and patristic theology cast Christ as the *second Adam* / cosmic Anthropos in whom all things are summed up (*anakephalaiōsis*, Eph 1:10); the Christological Anthropos draws on both Hebrew Adam and Hellenistic-Hermetic Anthropos imagery.

## MASSIVE WIN: The Cross-Tradition Convergence

The Anthropos Myth is one of the most widely distributed cosmogonic structures in world religion. In at least six independent traditions, the **cosmos is the body of a Primordial Human** — and the **history of the cosmos is the history of that Human's fragmentation and restoration**. The fact of independent convergence is the MASSIVE WIN.

### The Six-Tradition Grid

| Tradition | Figure | Cosmogonic Role | Fall / Catastrophe | Restoration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Vedic** (RV 10.90, ~1200 BCE) | Puruṣa | Primordial cosmic Person; universe emanated from his dismembered body | Cosmogonic sacrifice / dismemberment | The Vedic yajna (sacrifice) re-performs cosmogony; *dharma* as cosmic order |
| **Iranian** (~600 BCE, Pahlavi) | Gayōmart | First man; metals and humans generated from his slain body | Killed by Ahriman; cosmic dualismbreaks the primal unity | Frashokereti — cosmic renovation; Gayōmart's seed produces first human pair |
| **Gnostic / Hermetic** (~2nd c. CE) | Anthrōpos / Adamas | Divine image descends through spheres; embodied humans carry divine spark | Fall through Sophia's error / descent into matter | Gnosis liberates the sparks; apokatastasis |
| **Manichaean** (~250 CE) | Primal Man (Ormuzd) | Sent against Darkness; defeated; five Light-sons swallowed | Defeat by Darkness; divine light imprisoned in matter | Cosmological machinery extracts light particles; final fire consumes the cosmos |
| **Kabbalistic** (~13th–16th c.) | Adam Kadmon | First partzuf; lights flow from his eyes/nose/ears/mouth | Shevirat ha-kelim — the vessels shatter; kelipot and sitra aḥra created | Tikkun olam — incremental repair through Torah, prayer, kavvanah |
| **Blakean** (~1800 CE) | Albion | Primordial cosmic Human whose body is England and the universe | Falls into self-divided sleep; the Four Zoas dissociate | Albion's awakening = cosmic tikkun; Jerusalem = reunion with divine feminine |

### The Deep Structure

Across all six traditions, the same four-phase logic:

1. **A Primordial Human who *is* the cosmos** — not a human *in* the cosmos, but a Human whose body *constitutes* it
2. **A catastrophe of fragmentation** — whether sacrifice, fall, defeat, breaking, or sleep — that generates the broken, material, fallen world
3. **The dispersal of divine substance into matter** — sparks, limbs, seeds, light-particles, Zoas — constituting the hidden divinity within the material world
4. **A cosmic restoration project** — dharma, apokatastasis, frashokereti, tikkun, Albion's awakening — in which the fragmented Cosmic Human is reassembled

### Why This Structure Recurs

Three explanations (not mutually exclusive):

**A. Diffusion from common sources.** The Iranian Gayōmart may have influenced Manichaeism (directly) and Gnosticism (partially); the Vedic Puruṣa may share Indo-European roots with Iranian Gayōmart (Lincoln 1986). But the Kabbalistic and Blakean traditions arrive through very different channels (Blake explicitly through Boehme and the KJV, not through Iranian or Vedic sources).

**B. Independent invention of a universal insight.** The insight that **the universe has a human form** — that reality is not alien to mind but is the projection of a primordial mindedness — may be an independently discoverable insight. Wherever human reflection reaches the question "what is the nature of the cosmos?" deeply enough, the answer "a human form" recurs, because the human is the only form we know from the inside.

**C. A shared archaic substrate.** The dismemberment-of-the-cosmic-being motif (Tiamat in Enuma Elish, Puruṣa in the Vedas, Ymir in Norse mythology, Gayōmart in Iranian, Osiris in Egyptian) may preserve a prehistoric cosmogonic pattern, distributed across Eurasian cultures in the Neolithic or earlier, which resurfaces in multiple axial-age theological systems.

## The Pauline Christological Instance

The most consequential single deployment of the Anthropos Myth in Western history is the Pauline Cosmic Christ. Paul writes (~55 CE):

> "He is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation. For in him all things were created, in heaven and on earth... all things were created through him and for him. He is before all things, and in him all things hold together. And he is the head of the body, the church." (Colossians 1:15–18)

> "The first man Adam became a living soul; the last Adam became a life-giving spirit... The first man was from earth, a man of dust; the second man is from heaven." (1 Corinthians 15:45, 47)

This is **Adam Kadmon language applied to Jesus of Nazareth**:
- "Image of the invisible God" = Adam Kadmon as divine reflection / first partzuf
- "Firstborn of all creation" = pre-existent before the universe
- "In him all things were created / hold together" = the cosmos IS his body
- "Head of the body" = the macranthropic function: the Cosmic Human whose body is the church / the cosmos
- "Last Adam" = the restored Cosmic Human; the tikkun of the original Adam Kadmon

Irenaeus (~180 CE) developed this into the *anakephalaiōsis* doctrine: Christ "sums up" (*recapitulatio*) all humanity and all history — re-heading the Cosmic Human broken at Adam's fall. This is the Western Christian version of tikkun.

## Blake's Albion as Modern Reception

**William Blake's *Albion*** is the clearest modern instance of the Anthropos Myth, independently reconstructed by a Protestant English engraver without access to Kabbalistic or Gnostic primary texts (~1800):

> "The fields from Islington to Marybone, / To Primrose Hill and Saint John's Wood, / Were builded over with pillars of gold, / And there Jerusalem's pillars stood." — *Jerusalem* (1804–1820)

Blake's Albion:
- Is the **Primordial Human whose body is England / the universe** — landscape as body of the Giant
- **Falls into self-divided sleep** (= shevirat ha-kelim): the "fall of Albion" generates the broken world of ordinary experience
- His **Four Zoas dissociate** (= the fragmentation of the divine limbs): Urizen, Luvah, Tharmas, Urthona fight for dominance
- **Urizen** seizes power and creates the material prison (= the Demiurge created from the broken-off Reason-faculty)
- **Jerusalem** (Albion's feminine Emanation = Shekinah/Sophia) is driven into exile
- The whole project of *Jerusalem* is the **awakening and restoration of Albion** (= tikkun, apokatastasis, frashokereti)

Kathleen Raine (1968) demonstrated that Blake derived the Albion concept from Thomas Taylor's Neoplatonic translations and from Boehme (who was himself influenced by Christian Kabbalah). The structural identity with Kabbalistic Adam Kadmon was independently arrived at — which is precisely the point.
