# Katabasis and anabasis (descent and ascent of the soul)

## Definition
The trans-traditional motif of the soul's (or the soul-of-the-initiate's) **descent (κατάβασις, *katabasis*)** into the underworld / death / lower cosmos, **and subsequent ascent (ἀνάβασις, *anabasis*)** through cosmic regions or stages to a higher state — divinity, illumination, integration, or *return to the source*. The motif appears in mature form across multiple Mediterranean traditions in late antiquity and is a load-bearing structural element of:
- **Mystery-cult initiation** (Eleusinian, Bacchic-Orphic, Mithraic, Isiac) — the initiate symbolically descends-and-returns.
- **Hellenistic-Hermetic soul-ascent** ([[phase-4-011-corpus-hermeticum-i|*Poimandres*]], [[phase-4-073-tabula-smaragdina|Tabula]], the broader Hermetic corpus).
- **Gnostic ascent-and-descent** narratives (the Sethian-Valentinian Pleroma-fall + soul-ascent + Redeemer-descent triad).
- **Neoplatonic soul-procession-and-return** ([[plotinus]] *Enneads*; [[phase-4-030-pseudo-dionysius]]).
- **Christian descent-into-Hell + resurrection-ascension** (Christ's *descensus ad inferos* and *anabasis*).
- **Jewish-mystical Merkavah / Hekhalot ascent-through-the-seven-palaces** ([[phase-5-013-hekhalot-literature]]).
- **Islamic Sufi *mi'raj* / Muhammad's ascent**.
- **Earlier Mesopotamian prototype**: the **[[phase-1-017-descent-of-inanna|Descent of Inanna]]** (Sumerian; ~2000 BCE) is the documentary first major Western Asian katabasis-and-anabasis narrative, structurally underlying the Persephone-Demeter-Eleusinian myth and (more diffusely) the Osiris-descent narrative.

## Earliest attestations (tradition-by-tradition)

- **Mesopotamian:** [[phase-1-017-descent-of-inanna|*Descent of Inanna*]] (Sumerian ~2000 BCE) and the Akkadian *Descent of Ishtar* (~1700 BCE). The goddess descends through seven gates of the underworld, is stripped at each, dies in the underworld, is rescued and ascends. **The Western Asian katabasis prototype.**
- **Greek:** Demeter's grief and Persephone's recovery (Homeric Hymn to Demeter ~600 BCE) — *Persephone* descends-and-(partially)-returns; *Demeter* descends as grieving goddess. Cf. Heracles's underworld journey (Odyssey 11; *Nekyia*); Orpheus's descent for Eurydice; Theseus's underworld journey.
- **Egyptian:** Osiris's death-and-restoration; the *Book of the Dead* afterlife-journey schema.
- **Hellenistic:** Apuleius's Latin Isis-initiation (Met. Bk 11 ch. 23 — *"accessi confinium mortis…"*).
- **Hermetic:** [[phase-4-011-corpus-hermeticum-i|*Poimandres*]] (CH I) — the soul's ascent through the seven planetary spheres at death, shedding the planetary-passions at each.
- **Mithraic:** the seven-grade initiation system as soul-ascent through the planetary spheres (Beck 2006).
- **Gnostic:** the Sethian-Valentinian Pleroma → Sophia-fall → soul-trapped-in-matter → Redeemer-descent → soul-ascent.
- **Christian:** Christ's *descensus ad inferos* (1 Peter 3:19, 4:6) + resurrection + ascension. The *Apocalypse of Paul* and *Apocalypse of Peter* (2nd-3rd c.) develop the visionary-katabasis genre.
- **Jewish-mystical:** [[phase-5-013-hekhalot-literature|Hekhalot literature]] — the *yored merkavah* (descender to the chariot) ascends through seven palaces to the throne of Glory.
- **Islamic:** Muhammad's *mi'raj* (Quran 17:1) and the developed *Isra' and Mi'raj* narrative.
- **Medieval Christian:** Dante's *Divine Comedy* — the canonical Western literary integration of katabasis-and-anabasis.

## Variants

- **Underworld-descent (literal)**: Inanna/Ishtar, Persephone, Orpheus, Heracles, Aeneas (Aeneid 6), Christ.
- **Soul-ascent (through cosmic spheres)**: Hermetic, Mithraic, Gnostic, Hekhalot, Islamic *mi'raj*.
- **Mystery-initiation symbolic katabasis-and-anabasis**: Apuleius Met. 11; Eleusinian and Bacchic-Orphic initiations.
- **Eschatological soul-journey after death**: Egyptian Book of the Dead; Tibetan *Bardo Thodol*; Orphic gold tablets.
- **Christ's *descensus* + ascension as cosmic-redemption type**: New Testament + patristic; iconographic *Anastasis* tradition.

## Connections claimed

- **MASSIVE-win cross-tradition substrate.** The Mesopotamian Inanna-descent prototype → Mediterranean mystery-cult katabasis → Hellenistic-Hermetic soul-ascent → Christian descent-and-resurrection → Hekhalot Jewish-mystical ascent → Islamic *mi'raj* → Sufi soul-journey is one of the **single most-continuous and most-documented cross-tradition motif-lineages** in the entire Mediterranean religious-textual world.
