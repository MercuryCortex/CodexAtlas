# The plant-teacher (*planta maestra*)

**Stub.** The cross-cultural ethnobotanical claim — common across [[tradition-amazonian-shamanism]], Mazatec [[substance-psilocybin-mushroom|mushroom]] practice, Wixárika [[substance-peyote|peyote]] tradition, and many others — that the entheogenic plant or fungus is itself a *person* who teaches the human practitioner. Knowledge of preparation, dietary restrictions, and ritual song is described as transmitted by the plant during dreams or trance, not derived from human experimentation.

The pattern resists ordinary epistemological description — it is offered by practitioners as literal, by ethnographers as a culturally-mediated knowledge-transmission system. The discovery problem (how indigenous Amazonians paired the MAOI vine with the DMT leaf for [[substance-ayahuasca]] given the low chance of trial-and-error pairing) is the cleanest empirical case the model is invoked to explain.

Referenced from [[substance-ayahuasca]], [[substance-psilocybin-mushroom]].
