# Divine Council

A heavenly assembly of gods presided over by a high god, deciding cosmic affairs. Common across Mesopotamian, Ugaritic, and Hebrew traditions; the Hebrew Bible preserves traces (Ps 82, 1 Kings 22:19–22, Job 1–2, Isa 6) even as monotheistic redaction reduces other "gods" to angelic intermediaries.

## Attestations
- **Mesopotamian:** Anunnaki / Igigi assemblies in Enuma Elish, Atrahasis.
- **Ugaritic:** "Sons of El" (bn ilm) — Baal Cycle.
- **Hebrew:** "Sons of God" (bene elohim), divine assembly (sod) — see esp. Ps 82, Deut 32:8–9 (with DSS reading restoring "sons of God").
- **Canaanite/Phoenician:** divine councils led by El.

## Significance
The Hebrew Bible's preserved divine-council passages are central evidence for an earlier Israelite henotheism within a Canaanite divine-council framework (Smith 2001; Heiser 2004). This is a primary axis of the "El-and-Yahweh" question.
