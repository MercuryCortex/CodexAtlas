# Logos as Cosmic Reason

The *logos* — Greek word covering "word, speech, reason, account, ratio, principle" — as the rational structuring principle of the cosmos. Distinguished from *logos* as merely "speech" or "argument." When the cosmos *has* a logos, the order of things is intelligible because it is ordered by reason; ethics, physics, and theology converge.

## Manifestations
- **Heraclitus (c. 500 BCE):** The earliest philosophical use — fragment B1: "of this logos that is forever men prove to be uncomprehending"; B50: "listening not to me but to the logos it is wise to agree that all things are one." Foundational but cryptic.
- **Stoic logos:** [[phase-3-005-stoic-foundational-texts]] — Cleanthes, Chrysippus develop logos into a comprehensive cosmology. The cosmos is a single rational living being; *logos spermatikos* (seminal reasons) generates and structures every particular. The cosmic Logos is identical with Zeus, with *pneuma* (divine fire-breath), with Fate, with Providence. Living *kata logon* (according to reason) is the goal of human life. See [[logos-stoic]].
- **Philo of Alexandria:** [[phase-3-013-philo-of-alexandria]] — synthesizes Stoic logos with the Hebrew Bible's *dabar* (word) and *hokmah* (wisdom) and Plato's intelligible realm. The Logos is God's "second god," the first-begotten, the high priest of the cosmos, the intermediary between transcendent God and material world. The single most consequential bridge concept between Hellenistic philosophy and biblical theology. See [[logos-philonic]].
- **Johannine prologue:** [[phase-3-020-gospel-of-john]] 1:1–18 — "In the beginning was the Logos, and the Logos was with God, and the Logos was God." Whether John depends on Philo, on a shared Hellenistic-Jewish logos theology, or on independent Wisdom traditions is debated, but the prologue is the canonical Christian appropriation of philosophical Logos. See [[logos-johannine]].
- **Hermetism:** [[phase-4-011-corpus-hermeticum-i]] — the Logos is a luminous Word springing from Nous; structures creation.
- **Neoplatonism:** [[phase-4-019-plotinus-enneads]] — *logoi* are the rational forms by which Nous expresses itself in Soul and through which the sensible cosmos is informed. Plotinus's Logos doctrine sits between Stoic and Christian articulations.

## Disputes
- **John's prologue source.** Hellenistic-Jewish logos (Philonic, c. Bultmann); Wisdom tradition (Dunn, Hurtado); pre-Christian Aramaic Wisdom-poem; or direct Christian creation drawing eclectically. Currently Wisdom-tradition-via-Hellenistic-Jewish-synthesis dominates.
- **Stoic logos: pantheist or panentheist?** Whether the Stoic Logos *is* the cosmos or *pervades* the cosmos. Most current readings: immanent, materially identified with *pneuma*; "pantheist" in the standard sense.
- **Continuity from Heraclitus to Stoics.** The Stoics claimed Heraclitus as ancestor; whether Heraclitus's *logos* is doing the cosmological work the Stoics retroject onto him is genuinely uncertain.
