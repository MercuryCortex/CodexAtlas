# Ancient-astronauts hypothesis

T4-popular-claim-rejected framework — the umbrella claim that ancient civilizations were visited or genetically influenced by extraterrestrial beings, and that religious texts globally describe these visits in mythological language.

Founding modern work: [[erich-von-daniken]] *Chariots of the Gods?* (1968). Mesopotamian specialization: [[zecharia-sitchin]] *The 12th Planet* (1976) — see [[nibiru-anunnaki]].

## Tier-4 — same posture as Sitchin

Rejected by mainstream archaeology, anthropology, and Egyptology. No peer-reviewed defense of any major claim. The vault includes the framework because of its enormous popular reach (~65M von Däniken books + millions of Sitchin + the Ancient Aliens TV franchise generating multi-billion-view popular media). The vault serves in-graph rebuttal.

_Stubbed during the 2026-05-23 alt-school Batch 1 sweep. Full treatment in a subsequent batch will cover specific claims:_

- *Nazca lines as alien landing-strips* (rebutted by Aveni 2000, Reinhard 1996)
- *Pyramids built with extraterrestrial technology* (rebutted by Lehner & Hawass 2017)
- *Palenque sarcophagus = Mayan astronaut* (rebutted by Coe + David Stuart Mayanist scholarship)
- *Ezekiel's vision as UFO encounter* (rebutted by Blenkinsopp + standard biblical-prophetic scholarship)
- *Easter Island moai = alien intervention* (rebutted by Van Tilburg 1994 + Bahn & Flenley)

Each becomes a dedicated rebuttal-bearing node in the next batch.

## Per CODEX §IV — investigation-as-prompt note

The structural feature ancient-astronaut authors "noticed" is **real**: many ancient texts globally describe encounters with non-human-but-humanoid beings (Mesopotamian *anunnaki*, Hebrew *bene elohim*, Greek *theoi*, Indian *deva*, etc.) and many ancient cultures built monumental structures whose engineering remains impressive. The *interpretation* — that the descriptions are encoded ET-visit reports — is what mainstream rejects. The actual scholarship on each is rich and well-documented; the alien-substitution adds nothing the texts and the engineering studies don't already explain.
