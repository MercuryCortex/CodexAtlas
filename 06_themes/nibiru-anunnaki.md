# Nibiru-Anunnaki hypothesis

A T4-popular-claim-rejected framework originated by [[zecharia-sitchin]] in *The 12th Planet* (1976), which reinterprets Sumerian and Akkadian cuneiform texts as describing extraterrestrial events.

## The claim

Sitchin proposed:

1. A 12th planet **Nibiru** exists on a 3,600-year elliptical orbit beyond Pluto
2. Its inhabitants — the **Anunnaki** — visited Earth ~445,000 years ago to mine gold
3. ~200,000 years ago they genetically engineered *Homo sapiens* by combining their DNA with *Homo erectus*
4. The Sumerian-Akkadian gods (An, Enki, Marduk, Inanna, Ninhursag) were historical Anunnaki individuals — kings, scientists, princes — whose lives are recorded in cuneiform "as history, not myth"

## The vault's posture (per CODEX §IV)

The framework is rejected by mainstream Assyriology and astronomy. **No peer-reviewed defense exists.** Sitchin had no academic credential in Assyriology, Sumerology, or astronomy.

The vault includes this theme because Sitchin's books have sold millions, the framework dominates ancient-aliens popular media, and any user investigating Anu / Enki / Marduk via this vault will encounter the framework on the open web. The vault serves **the in-graph rebuttal alongside the popular claim**.

## Investigation-as-prompt — the real Mesopotamian theology Sitchin's framework distorts

The structural features Sitchin "noticed" are real:

### The Anunnaki ARE a real divine class

Sumerian *Anunnaki* (𒀭𒀀𒉣𒈾) means literally "those of princely seed/lineage" — the great gods of the divine council, An's offspring + the Igigi (working gods of the heavens). The term is well-attested across the Sumerian-Akkadian textual corpus. Bottéro 2001, Lambert 2013, Black & Green 1992 — the scholarship is robust. **What the texts describe is a divine council, not a spacefaring race.**

### Astral correspondences ARE real Mesopotamian theology

The Sumerians associated deities with celestial bodies (Inanna ↔ Venus; Utu ↔ Sun; Nanna ↔ Moon). The astral-deity correspondence is real Mesopotamian astrotheology — but it doesn't equate Nanna's "moon-residence" with the moon as an inhabited spaceship. It's mythological-astronomical poetics.

### Creation-of-humans-by-gods IS a real Mesopotamian motif

*Atrahasis*: the gods create humans from clay mixed with the blood of a slain god (We-ila) to bear the labour the lower gods refused. *Enuma Elish*: Marduk forms humanity from the blood of Kingu. These are mythological cosmogonies, well-documented (Lambert 2013) — they are NOT genetic-engineering protocols.

### The pattern

The structural features Sitchin notices are real. His *interpretation* — that the texts describe historical extraterrestrial events — is what mainstream rejects. The cuneiform texts are read as religious mythology by every academic scholar; they are read as literal extraterrestrial history only by Sitchin and his followers.

## Why mainstream rejection holds

Multiple converging lines of evidence:

- **Sitchin's flagship visual evidence — cylinder seal VA 243 — does NOT depict 12 planets.** Heiser 2001 shows the iconography is a standard Mesopotamian "constellation-around-a-star" pattern; the "planets" Sitchin counts are stars and divine symbols, not orbital bodies.
- **No Akkadian or Sumerian text uses *Nibiru* to mean a planet on a 3,600-year orbit.** *Nibiru* in cuneiform is a star (Jupiter at certain seasons, per the *MUL.APIN* astronomical compendium), not Sitchin's extra-solar wanderer.
- **Sitchin's claimed orbital mechanics are physically impossible.** A 3,600-year-period planet that periodically swings close enough to Earth to be visible would gravitationally disrupt the inner solar system in ways modern astronomy would have detected.
- **Sitchin had no academic credential** in Assyriology, Sumerology, or astronomy. He never published in peer-reviewed venues. He never engaged with Heiser's published rebuttal in academic forums.
