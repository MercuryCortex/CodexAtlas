# Theme Pilgrimage As Religious Architecture

The architectural-and-economic infrastructure of long-distance religious pilgrimage — the road, the way-station shrine, the pilgrimage-terminus monumental complex. Examples: the Hajj infrastructure around [[kaaba|Mecca]]; the medieval Christian pilgrimage routes to Santiago de Compostela / Rome / Jerusalem; the Indian pilgrimage circuits.
