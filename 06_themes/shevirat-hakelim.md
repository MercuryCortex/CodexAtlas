# Shevirat ha-Kelim — Breaking of the Vessels

Second act of the Lurianic cosmogonic drama. After [[tzimtzum]] created the empty space for creation, divine light flowed back as ten *sefirot* — but the lower seven vessels could not contain the intensity of the light and *shattered*. Sparks of the divine light fell into the resulting chaos and became trapped in the husks (*kelipot*) of broken vessels. The world we inhabit is the *post-shevira* world: a cosmos where divine sparks are scattered, exiled, and need to be gathered.

Theological work this concept does: it explains the existence of *evil* not as a positive principle but as a *malfunction of containment*, and it explains *exile* as a cosmic condition not just a historical one. Scholem read it as Luria's Spanish-Expulsion theodicy in metaphysical key.

- [[phase-6-015-luria-vital-etz-chayyim]] — Chaim Vital's *Etz Chayyim* contains the most systematic exposition
- The Lurianic scheme tzimtzum → shevirat-hakelim → tikkun is the Kabbalistic answer to theodicy

## Cross-tradition parallels

**Valentinian Gnostic Sophia fall / kenōma**
The structural parallel is exact and predates Luria by 1,400 years. In Valentinian cosmogony: the Pleroma (divine fullness) is disrupted when Sophia acts without her consort — a shattering of the divine order. From this rupture, a *kenōma* (deficiency / emptiness) is produced; divine sparks (*pneuma*) fall into matter and are held captive in the *hylē* (material chaos) by archons. Salvation = the pneumatics recognizing their divine nature and returning (*apokatastasis*). The Lurianic map: divine vessels shatter → *nitzotzot* (divine sparks) fall into *kelipot* (husks of the shattered vessels) → *tikkun* (repair) = gathering the sparks. Structure-for-structure identical. Scholem argued the parallel was evidence of Gnostic influence on medieval Kabbalah; Idel disputes the transmission but not the structural resonance. See [[valentinus]], [[tradition-gnosticism]].

**Manichaean light-captivity**
Mani (~240 CE) built his cosmology on the primal catastrophe of divine light particles being seized and imprisoned in matter by the Prince of Darkness. The entire Manichaean project — ethical life, ascetic practice, the cosmic cycles — is the project of liberating trapped light. This is *shevirat-hakelim* with a different origin story but identical functional structure: divine light fragmented and trapped in darkness; human spiritual practice = participating in the liberation/repair. The parallel runs through the broader *sparks-in-matter* archetype that appears across Near Eastern and Mediterranean traditions.

**Hindu Pralaya / cosmic dissolution**
In Hindu cosmology, each cosmic cycle (*kalpa*) ends in *pralaya* — total dissolution back into the unmanifest Brahman. Creation is always preceded by destruction; the cosmos is always a second-generation structure emerging from a prior shattering. The Lurianic drama is not cyclical in the Hindu sense (each pralaya is simply a reset; shevirat is a unique primordial catastrophe), but both posit that the present cosmos is *fallen* in relation to a prior divine wholeness and that the repair is the joint project of God and humanity.

**Gnostic Demiurge — accidental creation**
The Valentinian Demiurge (Yaldabaoth) creates the material world *not* by deliberate divine act but as a byproduct of the cosmic rupture — an accidental, imperfect creator producing an imperfect world. Luria's lower seven sefirot shatter *because the divine light was too strong for the vessels* — the same logic of inadvertent catastrophe producing a deficient creation. Both traditions use this to explain evil and suffering without making God its deliberate author.
