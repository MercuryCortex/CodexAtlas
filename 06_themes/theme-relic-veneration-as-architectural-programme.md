# Theme Relic Veneration As Architectural Programme

Cross-tradition motif of sacred architecture organised around the housing + display + circumambulation of relics. Canonical Buddhist case: the stupa (whose form descends from the [[sanchi-great-stupa|Sanchi]] type) as relic-container. Christian parallel: the reliquary church / Holy Sepulchre.
