# Psychopomp

A divine, semi-divine, or instructed-human figure who *escorts the soul* through the post-mortem journey — across thresholds (rivers, gates, planetary spheres), past hostile powers (demons, archons, judges), to a destination (paradise, the gods' realm, dissolution, rebirth). Distinguished from a *judge* of the dead (who decides outcome) and from a *ruler* of the dead (who governs the destination). The psychopomp's role is route-finding.

## Manifestations
- **Egyptian:** [[anubis]] leads the dead to the Hall of Two Truths; [[thoth]] records the verdict. Earliest articulation in [[phase-1-002-pyramid-texts]]; democratized in [[phase-1-009-coffin-texts]] and [[phase-1-010-book-of-the-dead]] (the entire text is essentially a psychopompic manual — spells that the soul itself, or its guide, uses to navigate).
- **Greek:** [[hermes-greek]] *psychopompos* — escorts shades to Hades. Already in [[phase-2-008-homeric-epics]] (Odyssey 24.1–14: Hermes leads the suitors' souls to the underworld). The classic Greek functional psychopomp.
- **Roman:** [[mercury-roman]] inherits Hermes's psychopompic role.
- **Platonic:** In the *Phaedo* and the Myth of Er (Republic 10) souls are led by *daimones* assigned at birth; in the *Phaedrus*, souls follow the gods in heavenly procession.
- **Hermetic:** [[hermes-trismegistus]] is both initiator and ascent-guide; [[phase-4-011-corpus-hermeticum-i]] details the soul's ascent through the seven planetary spheres, shedding accretions at each.
- **Gnostic:** Christ in many Gnostic texts is the ascending soul's guide past the archons; passwords and seals are given to bypass each. In [[phase-4-009-pistis-sophia]] this is elaborated systematically.
- **Mandaean:** [[manda-d-hayyi]] ("Knowledge of Life") guides the soul through the watch-houses (*matarata*) back to the World of Light. See [[phase-4-017-ginza-rba]].
- **Zoroastrian:** Daēnā (the soul's own conscience personified as a maiden or hag) meets the soul at the Chinvat Bridge — a psychopomp who is the soul's own moral record.

## Connections across traditions
- **Hermes-Thoth identification:** [[hermes-trismegistus]] is the syncretic crystallization. Both gods are scribes, messengers, and psychopomps; the equation is ancient (already in Herodotus 2.138) and entirely natural — these were the closest functional matches in their two pantheons.
- **Planetary ascent.** The seven-sphere ascent appears in Hermetism (Poimandres), in Gnosticism (Apocryphon of John, Pistis Sophia), in Mithraism (the seven grades), and probably in Mandaeism. Whether this is one shared cosmology or independent convergence on the seven visible planets is debated.
