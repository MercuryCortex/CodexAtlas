# Soul as Divine Spark

A fragment of the divine — light, breath, pneuma, nous — is lodged in the human being (or in matter generally), and the goal of salvation is to recognize, liberate, and return this fragment to its source. Sharply distinct from creation-from-clay anthropologies (Genesis 2, Atrahasis) where humans are made from non-divine substances and *then* given divine animation as a gift; here humans contain literal divine substance from the start — typically the result of a primordial accident, theft, or sacrifice.

## Manifestations
- **Orphic:** Anthropogony from the ashes of the Titans who consumed [[zagreus]] (Dionysus) — humans contain both Titanic (evil) and Dionysian (divine) substance. The earliest articulation in Greek of the spark-trapped-in-flesh pattern. Documented in late sources (Olympiodorus); the antiquity of the Zagreus myth is disputed (Edmonds 2013 vs. Bernabé and Bremmer).
- **Platonic:** [[phase-3-002-plato-dialogues]] — *Phaedrus* and *Timaeus*: the immortal rational soul (*logistikon*) is divine and pre-existent; embodiment is fall and forgetting. The metaphysical scaffolding that later Gnostic and Hermetic systems build on.
- **Hermetism:** [[phase-4-011-corpus-hermeticum-i]] — Anthropos (the heavenly Man) descends through the spheres, picks up vices from each planet, becomes embodied; the goal is the reverse ascent shedding accretions. See [[phase-3-021-hermetic-corpus-earliest]], [[phase-4-013-discourse-on-the-eighth-and-ninth]].
- **Sethian Gnosticism:** [[phase-4-002-apocryphon-of-john]] — Sophia's power is stolen by Yaldabaoth and breathed into Adam; the pneumatic spark is the divine substance trapped in matter that gnosis liberates. See [[phase-4-007-on-the-origin-of-the-world]], [[phase-4-006-hypostasis-of-the-archons]].
- **Valentinian:** Three classes of human (pneumatic, psychic, hylic) — only the pneumatic has the spark, by election.
- **Thomasine Christianity:** [[phase-4-001-gospel-of-thomas]] logia 50, 70, 77 — "If they ask, 'What is the sign of your father in you?' say, 'It is movement and rest'"; "If you bring forth what is within you, what you bring forth will save you."
- **Manichaean Light:** [[phase-4-015-kephalaia-of-the-teacher]] — divine Light particles are scattered through matter, especially in plants; the Elect liberate them through ritualized eating.
- **Stoic *logos spermatikos*:** A non-soteriological cousin — each soul is a fragment of the cosmic Logos. Differs in that no fall and no return are needed; the structure is participatory, not redemptive.

## Disputes
- **Direction of influence.** Whether Gnostic spark-anthropology is a derivation of Platonic soul-doctrine, an indigenous Jewish-apocalyptic development from Genesis 2:7 (the divine in-breathing), or a fusion. Pearson (1990) emphasizes Jewish-Wisdom sources; Turner (2001) emphasizes Platonic transmission.
- **Whether Orphic anthropogony is pre-Hellenistic.** Edmonds (2013) argues the Zagreus-anthropogony as a coherent doctrine is a late (perhaps Neoplatonic) systematization of older fragmentary myths; Bernabé argues for substantial early provenance.
