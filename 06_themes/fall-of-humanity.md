# Fall of Humanity

Humans once enjoyed a higher state — proximity to god(s), immortality, ease, knowledge, harmony — and lost it through some action (disobedience, mixing of categories, accident, theft, sexual transgression). The world we inhabit is *fallen* relative to that primordial state.

## Manifestations
- **Hesiodic Greek:** [[phase-2-009-hesiod-theogony-works-and-days]] — Five Ages (Gold, Silver, Bronze, Heroes, Iron); a *decline* myth. Pandora's jar / box releases evils. Distinct from a single "fall" — gradual deterioration with multiple humanities.
- **Mesopotamian (no fall):** [[phase-1-006-atrahasis]] — humans created already mortal as labor for the gods; there is no primordial higher state to lose. Mesopotamian anthropology lacks a clear fall motif; the Genesis innovation is striking against this background.
- **Genesis 2–3 (J):** [[phase-2-005-hebrew-bible-j-source]] — Eden, the prohibition, the serpent, the eating of the fruit, expulsion, loss of access to the tree of life. The classic Jewish-Christian fall narrative. Notably, Genesis 3 itself is moderate: humans get *knowledge* (which is what God has) but lose *immortality*; pain in childbirth and toil in agriculture follow; "original sin" is not in the text.
- **1 Enoch and Watchers:** [[phase-3-004-1-enoch]] (Book of the Watchers) — alternative fall narrative: angels (Watchers) descend, mate with women, teach forbidden arts (metalworking, cosmetics, divination); their offspring are giants; the world is corrupted. The dominant fall narrative in Second Temple Judaism — Adam-and-Eve was *not* yet the standard etiology of evil.
- **Pauline reading of Genesis:** [[phase-3-015-pauline-epistles]] — Romans 5: "as sin came into the world through one man (Adam), and death through sin." Paul transforms Genesis 3 into a *cosmic* fall narrative; sets up Adam-Christ typology. Augustine later develops this into "original sin."
- **Gnostic re-readings:** [[phase-4-002-apocryphon-of-john]], [[phase-4-006-hypostasis-of-the-archons]] — the Sethian inversion: the serpent is the wisdom-giver, the expulsion is the demiurge's punishment for awakening; the real "fall" was the cosmogonic descent of Sophia and the entrapment of pneumatic substance in matter.
- **Augustinian original sin:** [[phase-4-028-augustine-confessions]], [[phase-4-029-augustine-city-of-god]] — Adam's sin is *inherited* by all descendants through propagation (Augustine reads Romans 5:12 via Vulgate's *in quo* "in whom"); becomes Western Christian orthodoxy.

## Significance
- **The Watchers vs. Adam-and-Eve question.** Through most of Second Temple Judaism the Watchers narrative was the primary etiology of evil, not Adam's sin. Paul's choice to fix on Adam is a theological move that changed everything — it gave Christianity its anthropological starting-point. See Boccaccini, Stuckenbruck.
- **Eastern Christianity** (Greek-speaking patristic + Orthodox tradition) never accepted Augustinian original sin in the inherited-guilt sense; this is one of the doctrinal divisions between Eastern and Western Christianity.
