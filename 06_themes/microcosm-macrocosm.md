# Microcosm-Macrocosm

The doctrine that the human being (or some other particular order — the temple, the city, the alchemical vessel) reflects, contains, or is structurally homologous to the cosmos as a whole. "As above, so below" — the Hermetic formula from the Emerald Tablet — is the slogan-form. The motif undergirds astrology (planetary correspondences to body parts), magic (manipulating macrocosm via microcosm), medicine (Paracelsian doctrine of signatures), temple-architecture symbolism, and Kabbalistic anthropomorphism. Distinct from but adjacent to [[anthropos-myth]] (primordial human as cosmogonic body).

## Manifestations
- **Platonic source:** [[phase-3-002-plato-dialogues]] — *Timaeus* makes the cosmos itself a *zōon empsychon* (ensouled living being) on whose model the human soul-body is fashioned; the rotations of the heavens correspond to rational thought.
- **Hermetic locus classicus:** [[phase-4-011-corpus-hermeticum-i]], [[phase-4-012-asclepius]] — the *Asclepius* names humanity a "great miracle" (*magnum miraculum*) precisely because of the human's middle position uniting heaven and earth; the *Emerald Tablet* (medieval Hermetic, transmitted through Arabic) provides the "as above, so below" maxim.
- **Kabbalistic Adam Kadmon:** [[phase-5-027-sefer-ha-zohar]], [[phase-5-012-sefer-yetzirah]] — the primordial human *Adam Kadmon* is the cosmos in anthropic form; the ten *sefirot* map onto a vast cosmic body, and onto the empirical human body, allowing Kabbalistic exegesis of bodily acts as cosmic-theurgic operations.
- **Tantric body:** [[phase-5-014-abhinavagupta-tantraloka]] — the body contains the entire cosmology (cakras, *nāḍī*s, *kuṇḍalinī*, the 36 *tattva*s); yogic practice is cosmography enacted internally.
- **Vitruvian / architectural:** Vitruvius (1st c. BCE) puts the human body in a circle and square — the source of Leonardo's *Vitruvian Man*. Temple architecture from Solomon's Temple onward is microcosmic.
- **Renaissance Hermetism:** [[phase-6-001-ficino-pimander]], [[phase-6-009-agrippa-de-occulta-philosophia]] — Ficino's *De Vita* prescribes microcosmic regimens (planetary diet, music, talismans) to align the human with cosmic forces.
- **Paracelsian medicine:** [[phase-6-008-paracelsus-corpus]] — body-organs correspond to planets and metals; *doctrine of signatures* — every plant bears outward signs of its medical sympathies; the iatrochemical paradigm.
- **Robert Fludd's diagrams:** [[phase-6-022-fludd-utriusque-cosmi-historia]] — the most lavish visual encyclopedia of microcosm-macrocosm correspondences ever printed; the *Utriusque Cosmi Historia* (1617–21) is the genre's high baroque monument.
- **Kircher's universal system:** [[phase-6-024-kircher-oedipus-aegyptiacus]] — incorporates microcosm-macrocosm into a global Egyptian-Hermetic-Hieroglyphic decoding.

## Disputes
- **Continuity vs. recurrence.** Conger 1922 documents the motif's appearance across Greek, Stoic, Hermetic, Kabbalistic, Indian, Chinese, and African systems; he is divided on whether this is independent invention from common pre-philosophical "magical thinking" or transmission. Modern consensus: both — the cognitive intuition is widespread, the specific developed doctrines (Paracelsian medicine, Kabbalistic Adam Kadmon) are textually traceable.
- **Modern survival.** The motif survives in anthroposophical, theosophical, and New Age cosmologies (Phase 7) and feeds into 19th-c. correspondence theories of Swedenborg and later Steiner.
