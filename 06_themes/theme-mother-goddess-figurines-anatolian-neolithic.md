# Theme Mother Goddess Figurines Anatolian Neolithic

The (substantially contested) hypothesis (Gimbutas; earlier Mellaart) that the Anatolian-Neolithic female figurine corpus at [[catalhoyuk-sacred-site|Çatalhöyük]] and elsewhere reflects a unified Neolithic Mother-Goddess religion. Post-2000 scholarship ([[stephanie-meece|Meece]], [[lynn-meskell|Meskell]], [[ian-hodder|Hodder]]) substantially complicates this reading.
