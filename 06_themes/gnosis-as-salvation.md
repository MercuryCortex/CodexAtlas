# Gnosis as Salvation

Salvation is accomplished by *knowledge* — a revelatory recognition of the soul's true (divine) origin, present condition (entrapment in ignorance / matter / the lower cosmos), and means of return. Distinct from salvation by faith (Pauline), by works (Torah-observance), by sacrament alone, or by ritual purity, though it frequently overlaps with sacrament and ritual in practice. The knowledge in question is not propositional learning but a transformative *gnosis* (Greek), Coptic *sooun*, that re-identifies the knower with the divine source.

## Manifestations
- **Sethian Gnosticism:** In [[phase-4-002-apocryphon-of-john]] the savior figure descends to awaken sleepers; the elect recognize themselves as belonging to the upper pleroma. Cf. [[phase-4-006-hypostasis-of-the-archons]], [[phase-4-008-trimorphic-protennoia]].
- **Valentinian Gnosticism:** [[phase-4-003-gospel-of-truth]] frames gnosis as the dissolution of "Error" and the awakening from a nightmare; salvation is the named return of those who have the name of the Father.
- **Thomasine Christianity:** [[phase-4-001-gospel-of-thomas]] logion 3: "the kingdom is inside you and outside you. When you know yourselves, then you will be known."
- **Hermetism:** [[phase-4-011-corpus-hermeticum-i]] (*Poimandres*) — Hermes is initiated by Poimandres into the cosmology and self-knowledge that lifts the soul through the planetary spheres back to the Ogdoad. See [[phase-4-013-discourse-on-the-eighth-and-ninth]].
- **Mandaeism:** [[phase-4-017-ginza-rba]] — knowledge (*manda*) of the World of Light, mediated by [[manda-d-hayyi]] ("Knowledge of Life"), saves the soul from the world of the Seven.
- **Manichaeism:** Cosmological knowledge of how the Light became entangled with Darkness is itself salvific; see [[phase-4-015-kephalaia-of-the-teacher]].

## Disputes
- **Is gnosis Christian heterodoxy or a separate religion?** Older scholarship (Jonas, Rudolph) treated Gnosticism as a discrete religious phenomenon; King (2003), Williams (1996) have argued the category obscures more than it illuminates and that what we call "Gnosticism" is better described as varieties of Christian, Jewish, and Hermetic thought sharing certain motifs.
- **Origins.** Iranian (Reitzenstein-Jonas), Jewish (Pearson, Quispel), Platonic (Turner), or polygenetic? Currently the Jewish-Platonic synthesis hypothesis dominates, with Iranian elements acknowledged in Manichaean and Mandaean cases.
