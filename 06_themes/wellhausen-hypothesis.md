# Wellhausen Documentary Hypothesis

The Documentary Hypothesis (J/E/D/P sources of the Pentateuch), formalised by Julius Wellhausen (1844–1918) in *Prolegomena zur Geschichte Israels* (1878). Standard critical framework for the textual stratigraphy of Genesis through Deuteronomy.
