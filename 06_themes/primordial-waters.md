# Primordial Waters

## Identity

The **primordial waters** are the pre-cosmic state of undifferentiated water (or watery void) from which the gods, the cosmos, or both emerge — the single most widely distributed cosmogonic image in world religion. Before light, dry land, or living beings, there is formless water: dark, boundless, and without time. Creation is the act of distinguishing the ordered world from this deep. This is the flagship "the deep" cluster of the Atlas — the parallel pitched as `the deep → Tiamat · Nun · Ginnungagap`.

## The universal structure

Three features recur across the traditions:

1. **Pre-differentiation** — the waters are featureless: no above or below, no form, no time. The Vedic Nāsadīya calls it *salila*, an "undistinguished flood"; Genesis calls it *tōhû wābōhû*, "formless and void".
2. **Creation as separation** — the cosmogonic act divides the deep: sky from sea, land from water, light from dark (Genesis 1 performs all three; the Popol Vuh parts the sea to raise the land).
3. **Persistence and return** — the deep survives beneath and beyond creation; Egyptian theology has the cosmos eventually dissolve back into Nun, and the Hebrew Flood is the deep momentarily reclaiming the world (the "fountains of the great deep", Gen 7:11).

## The cross-tradition roster

- **Sumerian** — [[nammu]] (the primordial sea-mother) and [[apsu]] (the fresh-water deep / Abzu).
- **Babylonian / Akkadian** — [[tiamat]] (the salt sea) and [[apsu]], whose intermingling opens the *Enuma Elish*.
- **Egyptian** — [[nun]], the infinite dark waters from which Atum rises on the first mound.
- **Hebrew** — [[tehom]], the deep of Genesis 1:2, linguistically cognate with Akkadian *Tiāmat*.
- **Greek** — [[chaos-primordial]], Hesiod's "yawning gap" before the ordered world.
- **Norse** — [[ginnungagap]], the void between fire and ice (water's structural twin).
- **Vedic** — [[apas]], the deified Waters and the *salila* of the Nāsadīya Sūkta.
- **Maya** — the [[primordial-sea-popol-vuh|calm primordial sea]] of the Popol Vuh.

The symbol-level form of the archetype is [[cosmic-ocean]]; the combat variant is [[chaoskampf]]; and the deep's destructive return is the [[flood-motif]].

## Transmission and its limits

What makes this cluster an investigation object rather than a list is that it mixes **documented transmission** with **genuine convergence**, and the difference is knowable. The Hebrew *tĕhôm* and Akkadian *Tiāmat* are a **cognate** pair — Gunkel (1895) made this the foundation of the case that Genesis 1 carries a demythologised inheritance of Mesopotamian cosmology (with Tsumura 2005 the principal dissent: the cognate is a common-Semitic inheritance, not a loan, and Genesis shows no combat). The Vedic *salila* and the Norse Ginnungagap are linked to the Greek and to each other not by Near-Eastern transmission but by common **Indo-European** inheritance (West 2007). And the Maya primordial sea — developed in total isolation from the Old World — is the cluster's cleanest case of **independent invention**. So the parallels here are a spectrum: secure descent (Babylon → Israel), shared deep ancestry (the Indo-European deeps), and pure convergence (Mesoamerica). Keeping the three apart is the whole discipline of reading "the deep".
