# Pratyabhijñā (Recognition-Liberation)

## The structural move
*Pratyabhijñā* (Sanskrit: "recognition," *prati* = back/again + *abhijñā* = knowing/recognition) is the soteriological doctrine originating in Kashmir Shaivism, formalized by **Utpaladeva** (~925 CE) and synthesized by **Abhinavagupta** (~975–1025 CE): liberation (*mokṣa*) is not the acquisition of anything new, the performance of any ritual, or the elimination of ignorance as something externally imposed. It is the **recognition of what was always already the case** — namely, that the practitioner's own consciousness *is* Śiva, the sole reality. The apparent condition of bondage (*saṃsāra*) is not a metaphysical fact but a self-imposed contraction (*āṇava-mala*) of infinite Śiva-consciousness into a limited, individual perspective.

The recognition move has three elements:
1. **The claim**: You already are the absolute (Śiva / consciousness / the divine ground)
2. **The diagnosis**: The obstacle is not external — it is a contracted self-identification with limited individuality
3. **The path**: The *guru* points out what is already present; the practitioner recognizes it. This is the *shaktipāta* (descent of grace) + recognition event.

## Cross-tradition pattern
This exact soteriological structure appears across multiple traditions — making Pratyabhijñā the clearest identifiable cross-tradition structural pattern in the vault:

| Tradition | Recognition move | Source quality |
|---|---|---|
| Kashmir Shaivism (origin) | Pratyabhijñā: you ARE Śiva; recognize it | Tier-1 |
| Valentinian Gnosticism | Pneumatics are "already saved by nature"; gnosis = recognizing pre-existing divine spark | Tier-1 source; parallel-form only |
| Zen Buddhism | "Original face before your parents were born" — Buddha-nature always already present; *shikan-taza* (just sitting) as recognition | Parallel-form; Sanderson's Shaiva→Buddhist tantra case makes indirect influence plausible |
| Dzogchen (Tibetan) | *Rigpa* (pure awareness) is primordially present; "pointing-out instruction" = recognition event — structural parallel to *shaktipāta* | Plausible indirect via Kashmir translator corridor (Rinchen Zangpo); no direct textual proof |
| Advaita Vedanta | *Tat tvam asi* — you ARE Brahman; *ajñāna* is the only obstacle | Abhinavagupta influenced later Advaita (not the reverse); documented |
| Meister Eckhart | Seelenfünklein (soul's spark) IS already the Godhead; *Durchbruch* (breakthrough) = recognition | Parallel-form; closest Western parallel to Pratyabhijñā because Eckhart's world is real (not Māyā) |
| Ibn Arabi's Sufism | *Kashf* (unveiling) — the divine makes itself known through the perfect human as mirror; recognition of self as divine self-disclosure | Parallel-form; Suhrawardi may be an indirect route |

## The Eckhart / Trika comparison
Rudolf Otto's 1926 comparison of Eckhart with Śaṅkara (*Mysticism East and West*) is the standard reference — but the Kashmir Shaivism parallel is the tighter fit for one critical reason: **Eckhart's world is real**. In Śaṅkara's Advaita, the manifest world is *māyā* (illusion). In Trika Shaivism, the world is Śiva's real self-expression (*svātantrya*). Eckhart similarly does not deny the reality of creation — he affirms it as the Godhead's self-expression through the Son. The *structural isomorphism* is: world = real + single absolute = the ground of consciousness + liberation = recognition (not acquisition).

## Disputed elements
- The extent of historical transmission between the traditions listed is debated or unconfirmed for most cases
- The comparison with Dzogchen is the most contested: plausible via the Kashmir translator corridor but no smoking-gun text
- The Valentinian parallel was noted by several scholars of Gnosticism but without proposing transmission
