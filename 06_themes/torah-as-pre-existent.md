# Torah as Pre-Existent

The Second-Temple Jewish development by which Torah is identified with primordial Wisdom — the *ḥokmâ* / *sophia* present with God at creation — and so is *pre-existent*, the very pattern by which the world was made. The locus classicus is Sirach 24: Wisdom comes forth from the mouth of the Most High, seeks a home among the nations, settles in Israel, and *is identified* with "the book of the covenant of the Most High God, the Law that Moses commanded us" (Sir 24:23). The conceptual move is enormous: Torah is not merely Israel's covenant document but the *cosmic blueprint*.

- [[phase-3-007-sirach]] — Sir 24 is the foundational text; Sir 1:1–4 also makes Wisdom pre-existent.
- [[phase-3-012-wisdom-of-solomon]] — Wis 7–9 develops the Wisdom-Christology: pre-existent, with God at creation, ordering all things.
- [[phase-3-009-jubilees]] — the heavenly tablets on which Torah is inscribed before creation; the calendrical and ritual observances of Israel are pre-cosmic.
- [[phase-4-031-mishnah]] / [[phase-4-033-babylonian-talmud]] — rabbinic development: *Pirkei Avot* 3:14 ("beloved is Israel for to them was given a precious instrument by which the world was created"); *Genesis Rabbah* 1:1 (God consulted Torah as the architectural plan of the world).

The development feeds Christology indirectly: the Pauline pre-existent Christ (Phil 2; Col 1) and the Johannine Logos (Jn 1) reproduce the Wisdom-Torah structure with Christ in the slot. The Islamic doctrine of the pre-existent / eternally created Qur'an is the cognate Islamic development (a major theological controversy of the 9th c. CE Mu'tazila/Ash'ari debate).
