# Initiation

The ritual transition from one social-religious-cosmological status to another, marked by structured separation, liminal threshold, and reincorporation (van Gennep's tripartite schema). Distinct from [[mystery-initiation]] (which is specifically the cult-of-the-mysteries initiation type) by being a broader anthropological category that covers puberty rites, religious-confraternity entrance, secret-society admission, monastic ordination, esoteric-school degree-conferral.

- [[phase-4-013-discourse-on-the-eighth-and-ninth]] — a Hermetic initiatory dialogue: the master leads the disciple through the eighth (Ogdoad) into the ninth (Ennead) sphere.
- [[phase-3-021-hermetic-corpus-earliest]], [[phase-4-011-corpus-hermeticum-i]] — Hermetic-initiatory framing of philosophical-theological learning as graded mystery-revelation.
- [[phase-6-026-anderson-constitutions-free-masons]] — modern Masonic three-degree initiation system, with its iconography drawing on classical-Hermetic-Egyptian materials.

Modern reception: Joseph Campbell's monomyth (the *separation-initiation-return* schema of the hero's journey) is van Gennep's *rites de passage* applied to mytho-narrative. Eliade's *Rites and Symbols of Initiation* (1958) synthesized cross-cultural initiatory patterns.
