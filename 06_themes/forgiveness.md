# Forgiveness

The remission of a wrong or debt; the abandonment of warranted resentment, claim, or punishment. Central Christian ethical category — Matthew 6:12 ("forgive us our debts as we forgive our debtors"); Lk 6:37 ("forgive and you will be forgiven"); the Petrine question and the seventy-times-seven (Mt 18:21–22). Jewish ethical tradition develops a structured account: the Yom Kippur teshuvah (return/repentance) sequence requires (1) confession to God, (2) restitution to the wronged human party, (3) explicit request for forgiveness, before divine forgiveness becomes operative — sins between people require interpersonal repair, not only divine pardon (Maimonides, *Hilkhot Teshuvah*).
