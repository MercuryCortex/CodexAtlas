# Theme Initiation As Architectural Descent And Ascent

Esoteric / mystery-tradition motif of initiation enacted as a bodily descent-into-and-ascent-from a sacred space. Canonical case: the Initiation Well at [[quinta-da-regaleira-sintra|Quinta da Regaleira]] (1904–1910); ancient analogues include the Eleusinian mysteries' descent into the Telesterion.
