# Isis Mysteries (Mediterranean Dispersal)

The Hellenistic + Roman-imperial-era spread of the Isis mystery cult across the Mediterranean — from Alexandrian origins under the early Ptolemies (Ptolemy I Soter establishing the Sarapis-Isis cult c. -300 as Hellenistic-Egyptian synthesis) through the Greco-Roman world (Aegean islands, Athens, Rome, the Iberian peninsula, the Rhine frontier). One of the principal mystery-cult dispersals of the Roman imperial period; closely studied for the Isis → Mary Theotokos iconographic continuity in the Christian transition (Witt 1971).
