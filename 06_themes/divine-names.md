# Divine Names — the cross-tradition mystical-contemplative theology of God's names

A cross-tradition theological-mystical doctrine treating the *names of God* not as labels but as *contemplative-cognitive paths into divine reality*. Each Name reveals an aspect of God; meditating on the Names is a path of mystical knowledge; the sum of the Names approaches but never exhausts the divine essence. The doctrine appears in four major traditions independently and converges historically in the medieval Mediterranean.

## Cross-tradition instances

### 1. Islamic *al-Asmāʾ al-Ḥusnā* (the 99 Beautiful Names)

The Quranic doctrine of the 99 Names of Allah (Quran 7:180; 17:110; 20:8; 59:24) develops into a major Sufi contemplative practice: *dhikr* (remembrance) of the Names; classification of Names by attribute-categories (jamāl/jalāl, beauty/majesty); the cosmological theology of [[ibn-arabi]]'s *al-Asmāʾ wa al-Ṣifāt* (Names + Attributes). For Ibn Arabi, every cosmic reality is the manifestation of a divine Name in created form; cosmology IS the deployment of the Names. See Chittick 1989.

### 2. Jewish Kabbalistic Sefirot

The 10 Sefirot of Kabbalistic theology (formalized in the *Zohar*, ~1280s, and earlier *Sefer Yetzirah* + Bahir traditions) are simultaneously divine Names + cosmological emanations + paths into divine reality. The Tetragrammaton + Adonai + Elohim + the other biblical divine designations are systematized as access-points to specific Sefirot. Scholem 1941 documents the Kabbalistic Names-theology.

### 3. Christian-Lullian *Dignitates Dei*

[[ramon-llull]] (~1232-1316) develops the Christian articulation in the *Ars Magna* (Lullian Combinatorial Art): nine *Dignitates Dei* — Goodness, Greatness, Eternity, Power, Wisdom, Will, Virtue, Truth, Glory — combinatorially manipulated to generate theological truths. The Ars Magna is a contemplative-cognitive-mathematical engagement with the Names. Bonner 2007 documents.

### 4. Sufi-Lullian contact

Llull explicitly draws on Islamic Names-theology (he learned Arabic + spent extensive time in Tunis + Bejaia in Muslim apologetics-and-conversion missions). The 9-Dignitates list is structurally cognate to Islamic Names-classifications. This is one of the documented Christian-Muslim theological-borrowings of the medieval Mediterranean.

## MASSIVE-WIN — Names theology as cross-tradition synthesis-vocabulary

The Names-of-God doctrine is one of the most readable cross-tradition theological convergences: four traditions (Islam, Judaism, Christianity-Lullian, and earlier patristic-Christian Dionysian Names-theology via [[pseudo-dionysius]] *On the Divine Names*) develop structurally-similar contemplative-mystical frameworks for engaging the divine via its named attributes. The convergence has documented contact-points (Llull ↔ Islamic theology; Dionysius ↔ Plotinian Neoplatonism) and structural-philosophical resonances (the apophatic / cataphatic tension; the One-vs-Many problem; the divine-essence-vs-attributes distinction).
