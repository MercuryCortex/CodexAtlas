# Evolutionary Cosmogony

Cosmogony as **progressive evolution or gradual differentiation** from simpler to more complex forms: the cosmos unfolds in ordered stages, proceeding from undifferentiated unity or primal simplicity through increasingly differentiated and complex orders of being. Distinguished from both instantaneous creation (ex nihilo fiat) and combat cosmogony by its emphasis on *sequence* and *emergence*.

## Core definition

Where combat cosmogony emphasizes the dramatic overthrow of chaos, and craftsman cosmogony the artisan's shaping work, evolutionary cosmogony presents creation as an **organic unfolding** — each stage producing the conditions for the next. The classic marker is the enumeration of distinct eras, stages, or grades of being.

## Primary attestation: The Hawaiian Kumulipo

The [[phase-8-010-kumulipo]] is the world's most remarkable pre-modern evolutionary cosmogony. The chant proceeds through **16 named eras (*wā*)** that correspond to a rough sequence of biological complexity:

- *Wā 1–2*: Sea creatures (corals, sea-urchins, mollusks, shellfish — the earliest and simplest marine life)
- *Wā 3–4*: Fish, larger marine creatures
- *Wā 5–6*: Plants and land creatures
- *Wā 7–8*: Birds, insects, higher vertebrates
- *Wā 9–10*: Transition from *pō* (night/primal) to *ao* (day/manifest)
- *Wā 11–14*: Humans, social relations, genealogical lineage
- *Wā 15–16*: The chant culminates in the Hawaiian royal lineage

Mary Kawena Pukui and Martha Beckwith (1951) noted that the Kumulipo's biological progression — from invertebrate sea creatures through vertebrates to mammals to humans — *anticipates* the broad sequence of evolutionary biology independently of Western science by centuries. The chant was orally maintained and composed in the early 18th century CE, predating Darwin by over 100 years, yet the sequence of life-form complexity it encodes matches the broad arc of evolutionary theory.

This is not an argument for proto-scientific empiricism in Hawaiian culture; rather, it reflects a careful cross-generational observation of natural order encoded in mythic-genealogical form.

## Maori staging of nothingness

The [[phase-8-011-maori-cosmogonic-chants]] develop a philosophically unusual sub-type: the **staged void**. The sequence of *Te Kore* (the Nothing/Void) stages includes:

- *Te Kore-tē-whiwhia* — the Void without Possession
- *Te Kore-tē-wāwā* — the Void without Sound
- *Te Kore-i-ai* — the Void in which there is Nothing
- *Te Kore* — simply the Void

This graduated enumeration of nothingness before being has no Western parallel before the Neoplatonic "negative theology" traditions (Plotinus's The One beyond Being, Pseudo-Dionysius's apophatic darkness). The Maori tradition approaches *philosophical cosmogony* — distinguishing grades of non-being — unusual in indigenous oral traditions globally.

## Hebrew P-source comparison

The Priestly cosmogony of Genesis 1 is also an evolutionary cosmogony of sorts: 7 sequential days, each producing a new level of order — light, firmament, dry land, vegetation, astral bodies, sea creatures, land animals, humanity. The sequential *ordering* of creation in stages parallels the Hawaiian and Maori structures, though the theological frame differs (divine artisan vs. genealogical birth).

## Cross-tradition significance

**MASSIVE-win**: The Hawaiian Kumulipo's proto-evolutionary framework and the Maori staged-void cosmogony represent the deepest speculative cosmogonic thinking in Pacific traditions. Beckwith (1951) emphasizes this as a remarkable intellectual achievement: the encoding of biological observation in genealogical cosmogonic form, independently of any contact with Mediterranean or Asian scientific traditions.

The structural parallel: **Kumulipo's 16 wā ≈ Genesis P's 7 days ≈ Hesiod's Theogony's generational unfolding** — all three are staged-emergence cosmogonies in which each stage enables the next, and the final stage (royalty / humanity / Zeus's sovereignty) retrospectively legitimates the whole sequence.
