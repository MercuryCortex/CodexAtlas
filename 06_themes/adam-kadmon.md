# Adam Kadmon

In Kabbalistic cosmology Adam Kadmon is the first manifestation of the Infinite (Ein Sof) into the structured worlds — a primordial cosmic Man whose body is the configuration of the sefirot. In Lurianic Kabbalah, lights emanate from Adam Kadmon's eyes, ears, nose, and mouth, and the rupture of those lights (*shevirat ha-kelim*) is the origin of the broken world that needs *tikkun*.

Adam Kadmon is one local instance of a wider [[anthropos-myth]] running through Gnostic, Hermetic, Manichaean, and Kabbalistic cosmologies: the universe is *anthropomorphic* at its origin, not its end. Pico della Mirandola brought the figure into Christian Kabbalah; Blavatsky's Theosophy adopted it via Christian Kabbalist intermediaries (Knorr von Rosenroth's *Kabbala Denudata*, 1677).

- [[phase-5-027-sefer-ha-zohar]] — the Zohar's *Idra Rabba* and *Idra Zuta* sections develop Adam Kadmon imagery (the *Arikh Anpin* / "Long Face" and *Zeir Anpin* / "Short Face")
- [[phase-6-015-luria-vital-etz-chayyim]] — Lurianic systematization: Adam Kadmon as the first of the *partzufim* (divine countenances)
- [[phase-6-003-pico-oration-900-conclusions]] — Pico transmits Adam Kadmon into Christian Kabbalah
