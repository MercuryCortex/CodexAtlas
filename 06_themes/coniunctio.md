# Coniunctio — Union of Opposites

In the alchemical tradition, the *coniunctio* is the union of *Sol* and *Luna*, *King* and *Queen*, *Sulphur* and *Mercury* — symbolic of the chemical and spiritual marriage that produces the philosopher's stone. The image is older than Latin alchemy: it has roots in late-antique Greek alchemy (Zosimos), Islamic *jābir* corpus, and Hermetic literature.

Jung's last great work *Mysterium Coniunctionis* (1955) reads the entire alchemical opus as projected [[individuation]]: the chemical operations are unconscious enactments of psychic integration. The *coniunctio* of male and female alchemical principles symbolizes the integration of conscious and unconscious, ego and shadow, anima and animus. The product — the philosopher's stone, the *lapis* — is the Self.
