# Prisca Theologia

The doctrine that a single original revelation of theological truth was given to humanity in primordial antiquity, and was then transmitted — partly garbled, partly preserved — through a chain of ancient sages: typically Hermes Trismegistus, Zoroaster, Orpheus, Pythagoras, and Plato, culminating in (or converging with) the Mosaic and Christian revelations. The thesis dignifies pagan-philosophical and "Eastern" wisdom by reading them as fragments of one universal *prisca theologia* (ancient theology) whose final consummation is Christianity. The motif allows Renaissance Christians to study Hermetic, Platonic, Kabbalistic, and (later) Egyptian, Persian, and Indian materials *as Christian theology*.

## Manifestations
- **Ficino's central thesis:** [[phase-6-001-ficino-pimander]], [[phase-6-002-ficino-theologia-platonica]] — Marsilio Ficino's 1463 translation of the *Corpus Hermeticum* (with Cosimo de' Medici reportedly interrupting the Plato translation to demand Hermes first) is the founding act. Ficino frames Hermes as a contemporary of Moses and ancestor of Plato; the *prisca theologia* lineage is Hermes → Orpheus → Aglaophemus → Pythagoras → Plato. Augustinian sanction (Augustine, *City of God* VIII) for treating Hermes as authentic ancient sage.
- **Pico's expansion:** [[phase-6-003-pico-oration-900-conclusions]], [[phase-6-004-pico-heptaplus-conclusiones-cabalisticae]] — Giovanni Pico della Mirandola adds **Kabbalah** to the *prisca* chain: the Mosaic oral tradition transmitted alongside Hermes preserved deeper truths than even the Hermetic line, all converging on Christ. The Christian Kabbalah project.
- **Reuchlin and the Christian Kabbalah:** [[phase-6-006-reuchlin-de-arte-cabalistica]] — Johannes Reuchlin systematizes Pico's Cabala-as-*prisca*-theology in *De Arte Cabalistica* (1517).
- **Agrippa:** [[phase-6-009-agrippa-de-occulta-philosophia]] — the *prisca* framework legitimizes magical, astrological, and Kabbalistic operations as ancient God-given sciences rather than diabolical novelties.
- **Giordano Bruno:** [[phase-6-014-bruno-de-la-causa-eroici-furori]] — Bruno radicalizes: ancient Egyptian religion (Hermes again) is *truer* than corrupted Christianity, which should reform back toward Hermetic paganism. Burned 1600.
- **Athanasius Kircher:** [[phase-6-024-kircher-oedipus-aegyptiacus]] — the late-Baroque encyclopedic culmination: every world religion is decoded as a corruption of an original Egyptian-Hermetic monotheism. Even after Casaubon (1614) re-dated the *Corpus Hermeticum* to late antiquity, Kircher continues the *prisca* program for another half-century.
- **Casaubon's bombshell.** Isaac Casaubon's 1614 *De Rebus Sacris* demonstrates philologically that the *Corpus Hermeticum* dates from the 2nd–3rd c. CE, not the age of Moses. The historical premise of Ficino's *prisca* dates collapses — but the *cultural* program continues for another two centuries on inertia and motivated reasoning.

## Disputes
- **Status of the *prisca* claim.** Frances Yates (1964) argued the Hermetic *prisca theologia* drove Renaissance science as much as Christian Aristotelianism resisted it (the "Yates thesis"); Vickers, Westman, Copenhaver have critiqued the scope of Yates's claim while accepting the basic philological story.
- **Continuation in modern esotericism.** Hanegraaff (2012) traces the *prisca theologia* as the conceptual ancestor of the modern category of "Western Esotericism" and of the 20th-century [[perennial-philosophy]] of Huxley and Schuon — the latter being a Romantic-Theosophical descendant rather than an independent discovery.
