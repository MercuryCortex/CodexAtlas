# Torah and Wisdom Identified

The second-century-BCE Jewish theological move (Sirach, later developed in Baruch, Wisdom of Solomon, Philo) by which universal Wisdom and particular Torah are *identified*: the cosmic principle that orders creation *is* the Mosaic Law. This solves a sapiential-theological problem (how can universal Wisdom be available only to Israel?) and a particularist-theological problem (how can Israel's Torah claim universal authority?) — both poles are met. The move generates a sub-family of related claims: [[torah-as-pre-existent]], Torah as cosmic blueprint, Torah as the throne of the Most High.

- [[phase-3-007-sirach]] — Sir 24:23 is the founding identification: "All this is the book of the covenant of the Most High God, the Law which Moses commanded us." Wisdom = Torah, full stop.
- [[phase-3-012-wisdom-of-solomon]] — Wis 7–9 develops the cosmic Sophia poetics; Wis 18:4 frames Torah-light as universal.
- [[phase-3-013-philo-of-alexandria]] — Philo extends the identification with the Stoic *logos* and Middle-Platonic Forms; Torah is the *constitution of the cosmos*.

The identification is theologically generative for early Christianity (Wisdom-Logos-Christ chain in Paul, Hebrews, John), for rabbinic Judaism (Torah as the world's blueprint, *Gen. Rab.* 1:1), and for Islamic *Umm al-Kitāb* (the Mother of the Book / pre-existent Qur'an).
