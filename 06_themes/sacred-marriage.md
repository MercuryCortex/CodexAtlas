# Sacred Marriage (Hieros Gamos)

Ritual or mythic union between a god and goddess (or between deity and human representative — usually king-as-Dumuzi mating with priestess-as-Inanna). Tied to fertility, agricultural cycles, and royal legitimacy.

## Attestations
- **Sumerian:** Inanna–Dumuzi marriage hymns (Iddin-Dagan, Shulgi). Earliest and clearest examples.
- **Akkadian:** Ishtar–Tammuz successor.
- **Ugaritic:** El–Athirat / Baal–Anat references.
- **Greek (later, transformed):** Zeus–Hera, mystery-cult initiations.
- **Jewish:** Song of Songs (debated — secular love poetry vs. allegorical bride/groom theology).
- **Christian (transformed):** Christ–Church bride imagery (Ephesians, Revelation 19).
- **Gnostic:** Bridal chamber mystery in [[phase-4-004-gospel-of-philip]] — explicit sacramental reinterpretation.

## Significance
The motif appears in *contexts of royal legitimation* (king proves divine favor by enacting union) and *agricultural cycles* (fertility renewal). Its transformation across Jewish → Christian → Gnostic streams into spiritual/sacramental allegory is a key chain in religious-symbol evolution.
