# Solomonic Transmission Spine

## Definition

The **Solomonic transmission spine** is the cross-tradition phenomenon whereby the figure of [[solomon-king]] — one king, historical status disputed, reigning c. 970–931 BCE — becomes the **generative node** for at least five independent living religious or initiatory traditions, none of which acknowledge their mutual Solomonic root. The figure is not merely cited; he is *constitutive*: remove Solomon from any of these traditions and the tradition's core ritual, myth, or legitimacy claim collapses.

This is the vault's clearest example of a single family figure transmitting across millennia into living forms. The transmission threads do not all go through the historical Solomon; they go through what each tradition *needed* Solomon to be — which is itself a finding.

---

## The Five Transmission Threads

### Thread 1 — Ethiopian Solomonic Dynasty → Rastafari (c. 10th c. BCE → 1930 → present)

The [[kebra-nagast]] narrative: Solomon sleeps with the Queen of Sheba ([[makeda-queen-of-sheba]]), she returns to Ethiopia pregnant, and bears [[menelik-i-legendary]]. Menelik grows up, visits his father Solomon, and returns home with the Ark of the Covenant — secretly substituting a replica in the Temple. Ethiopia is now the true Zion; Israel's election has transferred. The Ethiopian imperial line claims continuous Davidic-Solomonic descent from this Menelik through to **Haile Selassie I** (r. 1930–1974), whose 1955 constitution encoded the genealogical claim as constitutional law.

Rastafari (1930+) takes this a step further: Haile Selassie is not merely a Davidic descendent but the *returned Christ* — the Lion of the Tribe of Judah (Rev 5:5), the King of Kings. The Solomonic bloodline is the mechanism by which God walks on earth in Black Africa. Haile Selassie's 1974 deposition and death do not terminate the tradition; most Rastafari interpret them as a test of faith or a mystery.

**Key nodes**: [[solomonic-genealogy]] · [[event-solomonic-restoration-1270]] · [[tradition-rastafari]] · [[phase-8-008-kebra-nagast]]

### Thread 2 — Hiram Abiff → Freemasonry (c. 970 BCE legendarily; operative Masonry medieval; speculative 1717+)

1 Kings 7:13–14 names **Hiram of Tyre** (a craftsman, not a king) as the artisan Solomon commissioned to build the Temple's bronze furnishings. In operative stonemasonry guilds (medieval) this figure evolves into **Hiram Abiff**, the Master Mason who alone knows the Master Word (the sacred name of God / the secret of the Temple's proportions). He is murdered by three Fellow Crafts who try to extort the word; he dies without revealing it; Solomon presides over his burial and institutes a substitute word.

This legend is the *operative core* of **all** Masonic initiation. Every Freemasonic lodge worldwide, regardless of rite, degree, or nationality, enacts the death and symbolic raising of Hiram Abiff in the Third Degree ritual. The Scottish Rite's 33 higher degrees are *elaborations* of this Solomonic Temple drama. Royal Arch Masonry specifically enacts the *recovery* of the lost word — framed as re-excavating the Temple foundation post-destruction. No other figure structures Freemasonry; remove Solomon's Temple and the entire Masonic symbolic system has no referent.

**Key nodes**: [[tradition-freemasonry]] · [[tradition-scottish-rite-freemasonry]] · [[tradition-knights-templar]]

### Thread 3 — Sulayman → Islamic tradition (7th c. CE, Quran)

The Quran names **Sulayman** (Suleiman) as a prophet-king who commands the *jinn* (Quran 27:17, 34:12–14, 38:36–40), understands the language of animals (27:16), and rules the wind. His is one of the densest portraits of any prophet in the Quran. The Sulayman-and-jinn complex becomes the seedbed for Islamic demonology, angelology, and eventually the vast **grimoire tradition** — the manuals of spirit-conjuration that circulated from Baghdad through Mediterranean Islam into Christian Europe.

### Thread 4 — Key of Solomon → Western ceremonial magic (compiled c. 14th–17th c. CE, roots earlier)

The *Clavicula Salomonis* (Key of Solomon) and the *Lemegeton* (Lesser Key of Solomon, including the *Goetia*) are the foundational grimoires of Western ceremonial magic. They claim Solomonic authorship: Solomon, who controlled the jinn/demons, left instructions for how to summon and bind them. Pseudo-historical pedigree aside, these texts became the operating manuals for **every major Western magical order** from the Renaissance onward — the Golden Dawn, Thelema (Crowley), modern Wicca, and contemporary chaos magic all draw on the Solomonic grimoire tradition. The influence passes through: [[edward-kelley]] · [[john-dee]] (who worked with Enochian angels partly in dialogue with this tradition) · [[aleister-crowley]] · [[marsilio-ficino]] (indirectly, via Solomonic-derived Platonic magic).

**Key nodes**: [[tradition-hermeticism]] · [[tradition-rosicrucianism]] · [[aleister-crowley]] · [[john-dee]]

### Thread 5 — Wisdom Christology → Christian tradition (1st c. CE)

The New Testament's [[jesus-christ-deity]] is explicitly positioned as "greater than Solomon" (Matthew 12:42, Luke 11:31) in a wisdom-contest framing. The Wisdom books pseudonymously attributed to Solomon (Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs, Wisdom of Solomon, Sirach) become the **substrate of early Christian Wisdom Christology** — Jesus as the personified Wisdom (Sophia) who was with God before creation (John 1, Proverbs 8:22–31). The Solomonic attribution is the mechanism: Solomon as the paradigmatic Wisdom-bearer makes his texts the raw material for the Christological synthesis.

## Why Nobody Maps This

Each of these traditions has its own scholarly literature, its own practitioners, its own self-understanding. Ethiopian Studies scholars don't read Masonic history. Masonic historians don't read Islamic demonology. Grimoire scholars don't read Rastafari theology. The Solomonic spine that underlies all five is only visible from a cross-tradition vantage point.

The figure is also *not the same* in each tradition:
- In Ethiopia: Solomon is a royal ancestor, a human king
- In Freemasonry: Solomon is the archetypal patron of sacred architecture and initiatory mysteries
- In Islam: Sulayman is a prophet given miraculous command over spirits
- In the grimoire tradition: Solomon is a master magician who bound 72 demons in a brass vessel
- In Christianity: Solomon is a wisdom-figure and Christological type

The polyphony of Solomonic images is itself the finding: one historical (disputed) king generates five incompatible but equally alive mythic forms.
