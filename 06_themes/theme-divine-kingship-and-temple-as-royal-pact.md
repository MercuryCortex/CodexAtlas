Cross-tradition motif of the monumental temple as the architectural expression of the king's covenant with the patron deity. Anchor cases: [[solomons-temple-first|Solomon's First Temple]] + the king's legitimating relationship with YHWH; Mesopotamian temple-building inscriptions (Gudea, Nabonidus); Egyptian pharaonic temple expansion programmes.
