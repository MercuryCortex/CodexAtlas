# Root Races (Theosophical doctrine)

## Definition
Helena Blavatsky's doctrine, systematized in *The Secret Doctrine* vol. II (1888, "Anthropogenesis"), of *seven successive cosmic-evolutionary "Root Races"* of humanity, each inhabiting a different continent and embodying a stage of spiritual evolution. The seven Root Races in canonical Theosophical sequence: (1) *Polarian* (etheric, ungendered); (2) *Hyperborean* (still subtle); (3) *Lemurian* (the first physical race, on the lost continent of Lemuria; gendered embodiment emerges; the "Fall into matter"); (4) *Atlantean* (on Atlantis; advanced civilization that destroyed itself; further descent into matter); (5) *Aryan* (the present race, with seven sub-races); (6) and (7) future races on continents not yet emerged. Each Root Race subdivides into seven *Sub-Races*; each Sub-Race into seven *Branch-Races*. The doctrine is *cosmic-evolutionary* rather than biological — it presupposes spiritual evolution through reincarnation, periodic continental cataclysms (see [[lost-continent]], [[cosmic-cataclysm]]), and a vast time-scale measured in *manvantaras*.

Diagnostic markers: (a) the sevenfold cosmic-evolutionary succession; (b) the *continent-per-race* schema (Lemuria, Atlantis, etc.); (c) the *spiritual-not-biological* framing in Blavatsky's own articulation; (d) the integration with reincarnation and karma; (e) downstream Ariosophist and partially-Nazi-adjacent appropriations that *racialize* the doctrine in ways Blavatsky did not.

## Earliest attestations
- **Blavatsky, *The Secret Doctrine*** ([[phase-7-006-secret-doctrine]], London 1888): the canonical articulation. Volume II ("Anthropogenesis") develops the seven Root Races in extensive cosmogonic-anthropological detail, framed as commentary on the (unverified) "Stanzas of Dzyan."
- **Earlier Theosophical antecedents:** A.P. Sinnett's *Esoteric Buddhism* (1883) sketched some of the cosmic-cyclical framework; Blavatsky's *Isis Unveiled* (1877, [[phase-7-005-isis-unveiled]]) lacks the developed Root-Race scheme. The full systematic doctrine is original to *Secret Doctrine* vol. II.
- **Later Theosophical elaboration:** C.W. Leadbeater and Annie Besant in *Man: Whence, How and Whither* (1913); W. Scott-Elliot's *The Story of Atlantis* (1896) and *The Lost Lemuria* (1904) — extensive map-and-narrative elaboration of Atlantean and Lemurian histories.
- **Anthroposophical reception:** Rudolf Steiner's *Outline of Esoteric Science* ([[phase-7-009-outline-of-esoteric-science]], 1909) carries forward and modifies the doctrine with a Christ-centered Western framing.
- **Blavatsky / Theosophy Atlantis materials:** [[phase-7-034-blavatsky-atlantis-root-race]] (extracts and elaborations of Atlantis as Fourth Root Race continent).

## Variants
- **Strict Blavatsky (1888):** the doctrine as in *Secret Doctrine*. Spiritual-evolutionary; universalist (every human soul will pass through all seven Root Races by reincarnation); the "Aryan" is a *cosmic-evolutionary stage*, not a biological category.
- **Leadbeater-Besant elaboration:** mapping of specific cities, royal lineages, and continental geographies; substantially more concrete (and to outside observers, fanciful) than Blavatsky's vaguer cosmic narrative.
- **Steiner's Anthroposophical version:** retains the seven-stage framework but reorganizes around Christ as cosmic turning-point; the post-Atlantean "epochs" are re-Christianized.
- **Ariosophist racialization (Guido von List, Jörg Lanz von Liebenfels, c. 1905–1920):** *radically transformed* the Theosophical doctrine into a biological-racial mysticism centered on a *master Aryan race* with explicitly antisemitic and supremacist content. This is at substantial distance from Blavatsky's own doctrine but uses Theosophical vocabulary. Goodrick-Clarke 1992 documents the chain of textual influence.
- **Nazi-adjacent occultism:** the Ariosophist tradition fed into Thule Society and certain SS subcultures (Himmler's Ahnenerbe). The mainstream Nazi racial-ideological apparatus drew chiefly on 19th-c. scientific racism (Gobineau, Chamberlain) rather than Theosophy directly, but Ariosophy provided a *mystical-esoteric* register that some Nazi-adjacent figures inhabited (Goodrick-Clarke 1992, 2002).
- **Mid-20th-c. and modern Theosophical responses:** later Theosophical writers (e.g., Geoffrey Hodson, Adyar leadership) explicitly disavowed racialized readings; the doctrine continues to be taught in spiritualized terms in current Theosophy.

## Connections claimed
- **Hindu / Brahmanic cosmic cycles** (*yugas*, *manvantaras*): Blavatsky framed Root Races as a Western-accessible version of Indian cosmic-cyclical anthropology. The fit is loose — *yugas* are temporal moral declines (Krita, Treta, Dvapara, Kali), not racial-continental successions. The borrowing is selective and substantial.
- **19th-c. comparative philology:** the "Aryan" term enters Theosophy from 19th-c. Indo-European linguistics (Max Müller, etc.), where it denoted a *language family*. Blavatsky's spiritualization of "Aryan" inherits the philological term but uses it idiosyncratically.
- **Lost-continent literature:** [[lost-continent]] — Blavatsky's Lemuria and Atlantis stand in the lost-continent literature alongside Ignatius Donnelly's *Atlantis: The Antediluvian World* ([[phase-7-033-donnelly-atlantis-antediluvian-world]], 1882, which influenced Blavatsky) and the broader 19th-c. theosophical-pseudoarchaeological imagination.
- **Theosophy → New Age continuity:** Root-Race doctrine in modified form continues in New Age cosmology (the "shift to a new age," "fifth-dimensional humanity," etc.) — see [[tradition-new-age]].

## Disputes
- **Universalist or racialist?** The strict-textual reading of Blavatsky 1888 is that the Root Races are *cosmic-evolutionary stages every soul passes through by reincarnation* — a doctrine in form universalist and anti-discriminatory. Blavatsky's own writings include explicit anti-racist statements. *However*: the *terminology* ("Aryan" as fifth Root Race, mentions of less-evolved races) is racially charged, was downstream picked up by Ariosophists, and contemporary Theosophical scholarship (Lubelsky 2013, Trompf, Hanegraaff) treats the doctrine as **textually defensible as universalist** but **historically entangled with the racial-mystical tradition that downstream-mutated it into something morally serious**. Both judgments must be stated together. Goodrick-Clarke 1992 and Poewe 2006 are the standard sources.
- **Stanzas of Dzyan.** The alleged ancient Tibetan source-text for *Secret Doctrine* has never been independently attested. External scholarship treats Blavatsky as the actual author of the Stanzas; Theosophical defenders maintain authentic ancient transmission.
- **Continuity with Ariosophy.** Goodrick-Clarke 1992 documents the textual chain Blavatsky → Lanz von Liebenfels → some Nazi-adjacent figures; the chain exists and matters. Theosophical defenders note (correctly) that the *content* of the doctrine was substantially *mutated* in the racializing direction — Ariosophist racial theology is not what Blavatsky taught. Both findings are accurate; they coexist in the literature.
- **Pseudoarchaeology.** Lemuria and Atlantis as continents have no support in modern geology, paleoanthropology, or archaeology. The doctrine survives in Theosophical and New Age communities as a *spiritual-mythological* cosmology rather than a physical-historical claim. See [[pseudoarchaeology]].
