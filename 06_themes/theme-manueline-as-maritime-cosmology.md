# Theme Manueline As Maritime Cosmology

Portuguese late-Gothic architectural style (~1490–1525) incorporating ropes, corals, armillary spheres, [[cross-order-of-christ|Crosses of the Order of Christ]], and maritime iconography. Reads architecturally as the iconography of [[henry-the-navigator|Henrican]]-era exploration in carved stone. Canonical examples: [[convent-of-christ-tomar|Tomar Chapter House window]] (Diogo de Arruda, c. 1510); Jerónimos Monastery (Lisbon); Torre de Belém.
