# Forms and Archetypes (Platonic)

Eternal, immaterial, unchanging *paradigms* (*paradeigmata*) in which sensible particulars *participate* (*metechousi*). The Form of Beauty makes beautiful things beautiful; the Form of Justice makes just acts just; the sensible cosmos is a "moving image of eternity" ([[phase-3-002-plato-dialogues]] *Timaeus* 37d). The most consequential metaphysical thesis in Western philosophy; its long career runs from Plato through Middle Platonism, Philo, Christian Logos-theology, Plotinus, the Christian Neoplatonists, all the way to modern mathematical Platonism.

## Manifestations
- **Plato's middle dialogues:** *Phaedo, Republic, Symposium, Phaedrus* — the classical theory. Forms are objects of knowledge; sensible things only of opinion.
- **Plato's late dialogues:** *Parmenides* — Plato's own self-critique of the theory; the "Third Man" regress. *Sophist, Statesman, Philebus, Timaeus, Laws* — the theory is modified (kind-and-individual; the "greatest kinds"; mathematical principles behind Forms).
- **Aristotelian critique:** [[phase-3-003-aristotle-metaphysics]] — Aristotle argues Forms are unnecessary and contradictory; form must be immanent in particular substances. The single most influential historical critique.
- **Middle Platonist development:** The Forms become *thoughts in the divine mind* (Antiochus, Albinus). This consolidation is decisive — it allows the Forms-theology to merge with theistic religious traditions.
- **Philo:** [[phase-3-013-philo-of-alexandria]] — Forms are God's thoughts; the Logos contains them; the sensible cosmos is created after their model (*De opificio* 16–25). Bridge to Jewish-Christian theology.
- **Hermetic:** [[phase-4-011-corpus-hermeticum-i]] — Nous is the paradigm of the cosmos; ideational priority of the intelligible.
- **Plotinus:** [[phase-4-019-plotinus-enneads]] — the Forms reside in Nous; Plotinus refuses to make them merely God's thoughts ("Are the intelligibles outside Nous?" V.5) — insists they are *constitutive* of Nous, not external objects. The most metaphysically refined Form-theory in antiquity.
- **Proclus:** [[phase-4-021-proclus-elements-of-theology]] — Forms differentiated, hierarchized, integrated into a triadic system; the late ancient maximum.

## Continuing significance
- **Christian theology:** Forms-as-divine-thoughts become standard Augustinian doctrine of *rationes seminales* and the divine ideas; preserved in scholastic theology (Aquinas, *ST* I q.15).
- **Mathematics:** Modern mathematical Platonism — the view that mathematical entities exist independently of minds — is a direct descendant.

## Disputes
- **Number and scope of Forms.** Plato's *Republic* 596a suggests there is a Form for each common noun; the *Parmenides* worries about Forms of hair and mud. Where the boundaries lie was already disputed in antiquity.
- **The "unwritten doctrines."** Whether Plato had an esoteric oral teaching (the *agrapha dogmata*) that grounded the Forms in mathematical principles (the One and the Indefinite Dyad). Tübingen school (Krämer, Gaiser) yes; mainstream Anglophone Plato scholarship traditionally no but increasingly open.
