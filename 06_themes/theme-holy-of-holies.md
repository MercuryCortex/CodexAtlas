# Theme Holy Of Holies

The innermost-restricted-most-sacred space in a temple — accessible only to the highest priesthood. Canonical case: the *Devir* of [[solomons-temple-first|Solomon's First Temple]] (entered only by the High Priest, only on Yom Kippur). Compare the inner sanctum / *adyton* / *naos* in Egyptian + Greek temples.
