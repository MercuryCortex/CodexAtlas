# Theme Monumental Architecture Before Agriculture

Schmidt's headline thesis from [[gobekli-tepe|Göbekli Tepe]]: hunter-gatherer-foragers organised the building of T-pillar enclosures *before* they had domesticated wheat — reversing the previous assumption that sedentary agriculture preceded monumental ritual building.
