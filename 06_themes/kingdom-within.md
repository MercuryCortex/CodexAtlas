# The Kingdom Within

The interpretation of Jesus' kingdom proclamation as referring to an *interior* / present reality — not (or not only) a future cosmic event. The locus classicus is Luke 17:21 ("the kingdom of God is *entos hymōn*"), variously translated "among you" / "within you" / "in your midst"; and Gospel of Thomas saying 3 ("the kingdom is inside of you, and it is outside of you"), saying 113 ("the kingdom of the Father is spread upon the earth, and men do not see it"). The Thomasine form is more radical and consistent than Lukan; for Thomas the kingdom is *already-realized-but-unrecognized*, accessed by self-knowledge.

- [[phase-3-018-luke-acts]] — Lk 17:21 *entos hymōn* — translation contested ("within" vs. "among"). Most modern critical translations render "in your midst," but the "within" reading has a long history (Patristic and modern mystical-interpretive).
- [[phase-4-001-gospel-of-thomas]] — Logia 3 and 113 (and 51, 82, 113) — the consistent realized-and-interior framing.

The contrast with [[eschatological-imminence]] is the diagnostic axis: imminent-future vs. realized-interior. Most modern Jesus-scholars (Ehrman, Allison, Sanders) judge the imminent-future framing historically primary and the Thomasine-interior framing a later development; a minority (Crossan, Mack, Funk) prefer the realized-sapiential framing as primary.
