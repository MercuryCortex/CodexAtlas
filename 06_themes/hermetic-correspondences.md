# Hermetic Correspondences

## Definition

The doctrine that the cosmos is built of **chains of correspondence** — that each planet is bound to a metal, a day, a colour, a plant, a stone, an animal, an organ of the body, a divine name, a number and a musical interval — and that these bindings are *real*, so that acting on one link in a chain acts on the rest. A talisman cast in tin under a favourable Jupiter draws down Jupiter; a plant whose leaf is shaped like a liver treats the liver; blood is not let while the Moon is in the sign governing the afflicted limb.

Three components have to be present before the doctrine can do any work:

1. **A stock of correspondences** — the tables themselves: seven planets, twelve signs, thirty-six decans, four elements, and the sublunary things distributed among them.
2. **A theory of why the tables bind** — supplied by Neoplatonic *sympatheia*: the cosmos is a single living animal whose parts affect one another at a distance because they participate in the same intelligible causes. This is what raises the tables above a mnemonic list.
3. **An operative programme** — magic, medicine, alchemy, election of times. A correspondence system that is never *used* is a cosmology; one that is used is a technology.

The compressed slogan of the whole doctrine is the Emerald Tablet's *quod est inferius est sicut quod est superius* — see [[as-above-so-below]]. Its anthropological form, the human being as a small cosmos containing the large one, is [[microcosm-macrocosm]]. Its underlying logic of like-affects-like is [[sympathetic-magic]], and its botanical-medical branch is [[signatures-of-things]]. **This node holds the system itself**: what is in the tables, where they came from, what they were used for, and which of their cross-cultural cousins are related and which merely rhyme.

## The tables

| Planet | Metal | Day | Body / faculty (standard Western assignment) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saturn | lead | Saturday | spleen; melancholy; limits, time, endings |
| Jupiter | tin | Thursday | liver; sanguine expansion; law, increase |
| Mars | iron | Tuesday | gall / bile; heat, cutting, war |
| Sun | gold | Sunday | heart; vital spirit; kingship |
| Venus | copper | Friday | kidneys, generative organs; concord |
| Mercury | quicksilver | Wednesday | brain, tongue, nerves; speech and exchange |
| Moon | silver | Monday | stomach, humours, growth; flux |

Alongside the planetary table run three more:

- **Zodiacal melothesia** — the *homo signorum*, the "zodiac man": Aries governs the head, Taurus the neck, and so down the body to Pisces at the feet. Attested already in Manilius's *Astronomica* (1st c. CE) and reproduced in almanacs into the 18th century as a bloodletting guide. See [[astrology-horoscope-twelve-places]].
- **The thirty-six decans** — the Egyptian ten-degree divisions of the zodiac, absorbed into Hellenistic astrology and given plants, stones, seals and images in the technical Hermetica. Festugière's first volume is the standard study of this material. See [[astronomy-egyptian-decans]].
- **The elemental / qualitative table** — four elements, four qualities, four humours, four seasons, four ages, four temperaments; the Galenic apparatus, which fuses with the astrological one in late antiquity. See [[medicine-galenic-humoral]].

The chains were never fully standardized. Different authorities assign the same plant to different planets, and the "correct" attribution is often decided by an author's argument rather than by any tradition. This is a feature of the system, not a defect in the record: it is a **generative grammar** for finding hidden connections, not a fixed catalogue.

## Where the tables came from — the documented chain

- **Mesopotamian celestial omen science** supplies the observational apparatus and the zodiac ([[astronomy-babylonian-zodiac]]); **Egyptian** star-clock practice supplies the decans ([[astronomy-egyptian-decans]]).
- **Hellenistic Alexandria** fuses them with Greek elemental physics and Stoic-Neoplatonic *sympatheia*, and attributes the result to a Greco-Egyptian revealer, [[hermes-trismegistus]]. The "technical Hermetica" — the decan-plant-stone lists, the *Kyranides*, the iatromathematical texts, the material in [[phase-4-106-greek-magical-papyri]] — is where the correspondence content actually sits; the philosophical Hermetica ([[phase-4-011-corpus-hermeticum-i]], [[phase-4-012-asclepius]]) supply the cosmology that licenses it.
- **Arabic transmission**, c. 8th–10th c., is the decisive relay. The Harranian Sabians preserve and elaborate the planetary cult and its correspondences ([[event-arabic-harranian-hermetica-c800-1000]]); the Jābirian alchemical corpus systematizes substance-correspondence ([[alchemy-jabir-ibn-hayyan]], [[alchemy-al-razi-rhazes]]); Abū Maʿshar's *Great Introduction* ([[phase-5-064-abu-mashar-great-introduction]]) carries the astrological framework into Latin. **The Emerald Tablet itself arrives this way** — its earliest known text is Arabic, embedded in the *Kitāb sirr al-khalīqa* attributed to Balīnūs (pseudo-Apollonius of Tyana), and it reaches Latin Europe from the 12th century. It is **not** part of the Greek Corpus Hermeticum.
- **Ficino, 1463** ([[event-ficino-corpus-hermeticum-translation-1463]]): Cosimo de' Medici has [[marsilio-ficino]] set aside Plato to translate the *Pimander* first, because Hermes is believed to be older. Ficino's own *De vita libri tres* (1489) turns the tables into a practical regimen of astral medicine.
- **Pico and Reuchlin** graft Kabbalah on ([[giovanni-pico-della-mirandola]], [[phase-6-003-pico-oration-900-conclusions]]; [[johannes-reuchlin]], [[phase-6-006-reuchlin-de-arte-cabalistica]]), adding Sefirot, Hebrew letters and divine names to the chains — see [[tradition-christian-kabbalah]] and [[sefirot-tree-of-life]].
- **Agrippa** ([[cornelius-agrippa]], [[phase-6-009-agrippa-de-occulta-philosophia]], written 1510, printed 1531–33) produces the encyclopedia. Its three books are the system's own self-description: Book I natural magic (the elemental and sublunary chains), Book II celestial magic (number, proportion, the planetary tables and their seals), Book III ceremonial magic (names, intelligences, the religious tier).
- **Paracelsus** ([[paracelsus]], [[phase-6-008-paracelsus-corpus]]) converts the doctrine into medicine: the *signatura rerum*, by which a plant's outward form declares its inward virtue ([[signatures-of-things]]); the *tria prima* of sulphur, mercury and salt as the operative principles of body and cosmos alike ([[alchemy-tria-prima]], [[alchemy-sulfur-mercury-theory]]); and iatrochemistry as the applied result ([[medicine-paracelsus-iatrochemistry]], [[medicine-astrological-iatromathematics]]).
- **Fludd** ([[robert-fludd]], [[phase-6-022-fludd-utriusque-cosmi-historia]], 1617–21) gives the system its greatest visual form, tying cosmic proportion to musical interval in the divine monochord ([[music-fludd-world-monochord]]).
- **Kircher** ([[athanasius-kircher]], [[phase-6-024-kircher-oedipus-aegyptiacus]], 1652–54) mounts the last great pre-critical defence, still reading hieroglyphs as encoded Hermetic theology forty years after Casaubon.
- **The modern occult revival** rebuilds the tables wholesale: the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn's correspondence system, codified by [[aleister-crowley]] in *Liber 777* (1909) and deployed in [[phase-7-016-magick-in-theory-and-practice]], cross-indexes Sefirot, tarot trumps ([[tarot]]), Hebrew letters, planets, elements, plants, perfumes and gods of half a dozen pantheons in a single table. It is the Agrippan project rebuilt on comparative-religion raw material — and it is the form in which most people encounter "correspondences" today.

## ⚠ The antiquity claim — what Casaubon settled in 1614

The Renaissance did not adopt the correspondence system as an interesting late-antique curiosity. It adopted it because it believed the system was **older than Moses** — a fragment of a primordial revelation given to an Egyptian sage contemporary with or prior to the patriarchs, running Hermes → Zoroaster → Orpheus → Pythagoras → Plato and confirming Christian truth from outside it. That frame is [[prisca-theologia]], and it is the reason Ficino, Pico and Agrippa believed they could use pagan astral magic without apostasy.

In 1614 **Isaac Casaubon** ([[isaac-casaubon]]) destroyed the pedigree ([[event-casaubon-redates-hermetica-1614]]). Working philologically in the *De rebus sacris et ecclesiasticis exercitationes XVI*, he showed that the Greek Hermetica are written in Koine of the early Christian centuries, quote Plato and the Septuagint, use Greek philosophical vocabulary that has no Egyptian counterpart, and contain no demonstrably ancient-Egyptian material. The modern consensus dating — **c. 100–300 CE**, Greco-Egyptian Alexandria — follows directly (Copenhaver 1992; Fowden 1986).

**The tier rule on this node, stated plainly.** Any claim that the correspondence system is a transmission from Mosaic-era or older Egypt — or from any earlier lost civilization — is, after 1614, a **T3/T4 claim and must be labelled as one wherever the vault carries it.** The T3 alternative-school literature (Hancock's *Fingerprints of the Gods* and successors; the Schwaller de Lubicz symbolist line) frequently reasserts a deep-antiquity pedigree for Egyptian sacred knowledge, and the vault catalogues that literature under CODEX §I.3 (disclosure, not concealment) with the rebuttal attached. It is never wired here as transmission.

Two further precisions the vault should not blur:

- **The redating killed the pedigree, not the practice.** The tables went on being compiled, printed and used for four centuries after Casaubon, and are in active use now. What died in 1614 was the *authority argument*, not the system.
- **There is a real Egyptian element, and it is much smaller than the claim.** The **decans** genuinely are Egyptian, and they genuinely are in the tables. That is a documented Egyptian contribution to Hellenistic astrology — not evidence that the philosophical Hermetica are pharaonic.

## MASSIVE-WIN — correlative cosmology as a convergent human form

The finding this node carries across traditions is **not** that anyone taught anyone else. It is that correspondence cosmology is a recurring solution to a recurring problem: how to make a very large number of particulars manageable and actionable by binding them into a small number of families.

| Tradition | Organizing set | What it binds | Operative use | Kind of claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hermetic / Western | 7 planets, 12 signs, 36 decans, 4 elements | metal, day, plant, stone, organ, name, note | talisman, election of times, medicine, alchemy | the node itself |
| Chinese | 5 phases (*wuxing*) plus yin-yang | direction, season, colour, flavour, viscus, planet, note, emotion | medicine, statecraft, calendar, music | **independent** — [[medicine-chinese-traditional]]; [[yin-yang]] |
| Greek medical | 4 humours | element, quality, season, age, temperament | diagnosis, diet, regimen | shared late-antique milieu — [[medicine-galenic-humoral]] |
| Indian (Ayurvedic) | 5 *mahābhūtas*, 3 *doṣas*, 6 tastes | season, time of day, constitution, food | diagnosis and regimen | **independent** — [[medicine-ayurveda]] |
| Indian (Jyotiṣa) | 9 *grahas* | gem, metal, colour, deity, mantra, weekday | remedial gemstone and mantra prescription | partly transmitted from Hellenistic astrology — [[tradition-jyotisha-indian-astrology]] |
| Jewish Kabbalistic | 10 Sefirot | divine name, planet, colour, body part, patriarch | contemplative ascent; later, magic | **fused into the Western system in the Renaissance** — [[sefirot-tree-of-life]] |
| Yoruba Ifá | 256 Odù | orisha, leaf (*ewé*), sacrifice, verse | divination and prescribed offering | **independent** — [[ifa-odu-divination]] |

**What is genuinely shared and what is not.** The Chinese, Indian-Ayurvedic and Yoruba systems are independent of the Hermetic one; the resemblance is at the level of *cognitive form* — a closed set of organizing terms, exhaustive assignment of the world's furniture among them, and licensed inference from one member of a class to another. A.C. Graham's category **"correlative thinking"** is the standard comparative frame, and using it is a claim about how humans organize knowledge, **not** a claim of contact. Against it: the Jewish-Christian Kabbalistic graft *is* transmission, with names and dates; the Indian planetary week and zodiac *are* partly Hellenistic imports; and the Arabic relay is the best-documented single link in the whole Western chain.

Stated as the Atlas states such things: **the Western system is diffused, the Kabbalistic fusion is transmitted, and the cross-civilizational pattern is convergent** — and each of those three claims is wired with the edge type it deserves and no other.

## Disputes

- **The Yates thesis.** Frances Yates argued in 1964 that Renaissance Hermeticism was a principal cause of the Scientific Revolution — that the magus's will to operate on nature became the scientist's. The thesis is now substantially qualified (Vickers, Copenhaver, Hanegraaff): the recovered *material* stands, the causal claim does not, in that form. Cite Yates for what she found, not for what she concluded.
- **How far the tables were ever fixed.** There is no canonical correspondence list. Authorities disagree, and the disagreement is usually settled by argument from resemblance rather than by tradition. Whether this makes the system a "cosmology" or a rhetorical technique is a live question in the historiography.
- **Whether "correlative thinking" is one thing.** Needham's and Graham's category has been criticized for lumping systems whose internal logics differ sharply — Chinese *wuxing* is a theory of *phase and transformation*, the Hermetic chains a theory of *influence at a distance*. The parallel rows above are labelled as structural for exactly this reason.
- **The Egyptian residue.** How much genuine Egyptian religious content survives in the Hermetica is a real scholarly question, separate from the discredited antiquity claim. Fowden 1986 argues for meaningful continuity with Egyptian temple culture; others read the corpus as Greek philosophy in Egyptian costume. Do not confuse this legitimate debate with the T3/T4 pre-Mosaic claim.
