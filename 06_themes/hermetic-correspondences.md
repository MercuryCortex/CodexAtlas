# Hermetic Correspondences

The systematic doctrine that the cosmos consists of *chains of correspondence* — each planet linked to a metal, a color, a day of the week, a plant, a stone, a body part, an animal, a deity — running from celestial through elemental through sub-lunary levels. The Hermetic-Renaissance synthesis combines Hellenistic astrology, alchemical-metallurgical tradition, herbal medicine, and theurgic magic into encyclopedic correspondence systems. Agrippa's *De Occulta Philosophia* (1531–1533) is the classical encyclopedia; Paracelsus extends correspondence into iatrochemical medicine ([[microcosm-macrocosm]]); Fludd visualizes the system in great encyclopedic diagrams.

- [[phase-4-012-asclepius]] — Hermetic source; the divine-cosmic correspondences.
- [[phase-6-009-agrippa-de-occulta-philosophia]] — the systematic-encyclopedic Renaissance form.
- [[phase-6-008-paracelsus-corpus]] — the medical-iatrochemical application; doctrine of signatures.
- [[phase-6-022-fludd-utriusque-cosmi-historia]] — the high-baroque diagrammatic form.

The chains structure all subsequent Western ceremonial magic, astrological practice, and esoteric correspondences down to the present. Modern occult and New Age literature continues to deploy the chains.
