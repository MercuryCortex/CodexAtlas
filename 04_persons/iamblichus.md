# Iamblichus

Syrian Neoplatonist (~245–325), founder of the Syrian school at Apamea. Studied with Porphyry and broke with him over theurgy: Iamblichus argued that the soul's ascent to the divine required ritual practice, not philosophical contemplation alone. His *De Mysteriis* (replying as "the Egyptian priest Abammon" to Porphyry's *Letter to Anebo*) is the defining defense of theurgic Neoplatonism. Iamblichus's students Aedesius and Maximus of Ephesus formed the chain that reached Emperor Julian. Subsequent Athenian Neoplatonism (Plutarch of Athens, Syrianus, Proclus) is Iamblichean in lineage.
