# Uthman ibn Affan

Third Rashidun caliph (644–656). Succeeded [[umar-ibn-al-khattab|ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb]] via the six-member *shūrā* council ʿUmar designated. Standardized the text of the [[phase-4-034-quran|Quran]] under his caliphate (~650): the *Uthmanic codex* (*muṣḥaf ʿUthmān*) became the canonical text of Sunni Islam, with variant codices ordered destroyed. His policy of appointing [[muawiya-ibn-abi-sufyan|Umayyad relatives]] to key gubernatorial posts alienated provincial elites; his assassination in 656 triggered the [[event-first-fitna-656-661|First Fitna]] (civil war) and the Sunni-Shi'a fracture.
