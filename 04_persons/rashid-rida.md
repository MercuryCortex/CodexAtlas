# Rashid Rida

## Identity
Syrian-Egyptian reformer (1865–1935); collaborator with Muhammad Abduh; founder of the influential reformist journal *al-Manar* (Cairo, 1898–1935). Began as an Abduh-modernist; moved over time toward a more conservative Salafi position emphasizing return to the practice of the *salaf* (the first three generations of Muslims). The transition from late-19th-century Islamic modernism to early-20th-century Salafism passes through Rida's later writing.

## Role in the tradition
A pivotal transitional figure; the genealogy modernism → Rida → Salafism / Muslim Brotherhood is well-documented in 20th-century Islamic intellectual history.
