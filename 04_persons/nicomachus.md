Nicomachus is the key transmitter of Pythagorean number theology and music theory into late antiquity and the Latin West. His *Introduction to Arithmetic* became the dominant arithmetic textbook — translated by Boethius into Latin (*De Institutione Arithmetica*) and used in the medieval quadrivium for a thousand years. His *Manual of Harmonics* provides the fullest ancient statement of the music-cosmos homology: 7 strings of the lyre = 7 planets = 7 vowels = 7 modes. See [[music-7-string-lyre-cosmos]] and [[music-pythagorean-harmony]]. Nicomachus is the bridge node in the chain: [[pythagoras]] → [[philolaus]] → Nicomachus → [[boethius]] → medieval quadrivium → [[mathematics-pythagorean-sacred]] reception in the Latin world.
