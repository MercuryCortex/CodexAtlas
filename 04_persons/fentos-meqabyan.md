# Fentos (Meqabyan III)

## Identity
**Fentos** (also rendered *Pintos* or *Phintos* in some recensions) is the eponymous figure of **Meqabyan III**, the third of the three Ge'ez Books of Meqabyan ([[phase-5-040-meqabyan-ethiopian-maccabees]]) — uniquely canonical in [[tradition-ethiopian-orthodox-tewahedo]]. Like [[abya-meqabyan]] and [[seela-meqabyan]], Fentos is a legendary-pseudonymous figure rather than a historical individual; the three together constitute the eponymous trio of the Ethiopian "Maccabees" — entirely distinct from the Jewish-Hellenistic-period Maccabean revolt leaders.

The name *Fentos* may reflect a Coptic-Egyptian or Greek onomastic stratum (with possible relation to *Phintias* or *Phintos*); the exact etymology and source-derivation are not securely established.

## Historical evidence
Legendary. Preserved only in Ge'ez and only canonically in the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Bible.

## Works
None.

## Role in the tradition
Fentos completes the three-Meqabyan-figure structure — a **martyr-and-witness exemplar** within the canonical-scriptural-pastoral frame of the Ge'ez Old Testament. The corpus's theological function is the systematization of *fidelity-under-persecution* within Ethiopian canonical scripture; Fentos's specific narrative-arc within Meqabyan III, like that of his two pair-figures, varies across recensions and is under-studied in modern Western scholarship.

## Disputes
- **Identity with Greek-Hellenistic onomastic figures.** The name *Phintos / Phintias* exists in Hellenistic Greek onomastics (Pythagorean Phintias of Syracuse, etc.) — whether the Ge'ez Meqabyan name derives etymologically from a Hellenistic-Coptic intermediation or is an independent Ge'ez coinage is debated.
- See the parallel disputes documented at [[abya-meqabyan]] for date, source-history, and historicity questions affecting all three Meqabyan figures.
