# Thomas Vaughan

**Thomas Vaughan** (1621–1666) — Welsh Anglican clergyman writing as **Eugenius Philalethes** — produced the most philosophically sophisticated body of English Rosicrucian literature and published the first English translations of the Rosicrucian manifestos (1652). His philosophical polemic with Henry More (Cambridge Platonist) is the key 17th-century English debate between rational Platonism and alchemical Hermeticism. His twin brother was the poet Henry Vaughan. See [[phase-6-050-thomas-vaughan-anthroposophia]].
