# Ptolemy XII Auletes

Ptolemaic pharaoh of Egypt (r. -80 to -58 + restored -55 to -51), father of Cleopatra VII. Sponsor of major late-Ptolemaic temple-construction including the completion of [[place-dendera|Dendera]] and continued work at Philae. The reign overlaps with the early Roman intervention in Egypt that culminates in the Antony–Cleopatra alliance + Octavian's annexation.
