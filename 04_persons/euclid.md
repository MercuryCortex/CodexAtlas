Euclid's *Elements* (c. 300 BCE) is the most reproduced and studied mathematical text in history — used as the primary geometry textbook from its composition through the 20th century. Its method of deriving all theorems from a small set of axioms and common notions established the **axiomatic method** that still defines mathematical proof. The *Elements* was transmitted to Europe via Arabic translation at the [[mathematics-house-of-wisdom|House of Wisdom]] (Gerard of Cremona's Latin translation, Toledo, c. 1120 CE).
