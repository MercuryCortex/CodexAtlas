Al-Khwarizmi was the pre-eminent mathematician of the [[mathematics-house-of-wisdom|House of Wisdom]] under Caliph al-Ma'mun. His *Kitāb al-mukhtaṣar fī ḥisāb al-jabr wal-muqābala* (c. 830 CE) founded algebra; the word "algebra" derives directly from *al-jabr* in his title. His latinized name *Algoritmi* gave the modern world the word "algorithm." His *Kitāb al-ḥisāb al-hindī* introduced the Hindu-Arabic numeral system (including zero) to the Islamic world and, via Latin translation, to Europe.
