# Gerald Brosseau Gardner

## Identity
British colonial customs officer and amateur archaeologist (1884–1964); spent much of his career in Malaya and Borneo; on retirement to England (1936) became involved with the Folk-Lore Society and the New Forest occult-folkloric circle. Reported initiation into a surviving witch-cult coven (1939); after the 1951 repeal of the Witchcraft Act, published *Witchcraft Today* (1954) introducing Wicca to the world.

## Works
See texts-authored. The unpublished *Book of Shadows* — Wicca's central liturgical-ritual text — substantially composed by Gardner with extensive collaboration / revision from Doreen Valiente.

## Role in the tradition
Founder of modern Wicca; the most consequential figure in 20th-century Pagan revival.

## Disputes
- **Antiquity claims**: Wicca's claim of pre-Christian continuity is now generally rejected by external scholarship (Hutton 1999) and increasingly by internal Wiccan scholarship; modern Wicca is understood as newly founded mid-20th century.
