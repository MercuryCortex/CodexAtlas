# Walter Terence Stace

British-American philosopher (1886-1967), professor at Princeton 1932-1955, principal mid-20th-century systematizer of the **perennialist** account of mysticism. His *Mysticism and Philosophy* (Macmillan 1960) proposes that mystical experiences across traditions share a common **"introvertive"** core (pure consciousness without content) and a parallel **"extrovertive"** core (unitive perception of the manifold world as one). Stace's typology is the framework Steven Katz's constructivist critique (1978) responded against — the perennialist-vs-constructivist debate that has organized modern academic mysticism studies for sixty years.
