# Ludwig Wittgenstein

Austrian-British philosopher. Author of *Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus* (1921) and *Philosophical Investigations* (1953). Two distinct philosophical periods: the early Wittgenstein (picture theory of meaning; "Whereof one cannot speak, thereof one must be silent") and the late Wittgenstein (language games; meaning as use; dissolution of philosophical problems). Both phases share the move of using philosophy's own tools to dissolve philosophy's pretensions — a structural parallel to [[nagarjuna]]'s prasaṅga method.
