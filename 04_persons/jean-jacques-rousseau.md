# Jean-Jacques Rousseau

Geneva-born Enlightenment philosopher whose *Discourse on the Origin of Inequality* (1755) and *The Social Contract* (1762) argued that natural humans are good (*amour de soi* + *pitié*) and that social institutions, property, and inequality are the sources of moral corruption. His "noble savage" hypothesis is structurally parallel — independent and 2,000 years later — to Mengzi's innate-goodness doctrine (*xìng shàn*). Key node in the vault's human-nature cluster: Mengzi (−300 BCE China) ↔ Rousseau (1755 CE France) ↔ Augustine/Xunzi (fallen/corrupt nature counter-position).
