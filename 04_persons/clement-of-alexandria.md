# Clement of Alexandria

Head of the Alexandrian catechetical school until ~202, when persecution under Septimius Severus drove him from the city. Clement is the great early-Christian harmonizer of faith and Hellenistic philosophy. His trilogy — the *Protrepticus* (exhortation), *Paedagogus* (educator), *Stromata* (miscellanies) — moves from conversion to discipline to philosophical maturity. Clement defends a Christian "true gnostic" (against the heretical gnostics) — the philosophically and ethically mature Christian — and absorbs Philonic biblical exegesis and Middle Platonist negative theology into Christian use. He was probably the teacher of Origen.
