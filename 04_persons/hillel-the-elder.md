# Hillel the Elder

Babylonian-born Pharisaic sage who migrated to Jerusalem and led the Pharisaic school known as *Beit Hillel*; his rival was Shammai (*Beit Shammai*). Active in the late Herodian period; floruit ~30 BCE – 10 CE. The Mishnah and Talmud preserve hundreds of Hillel-Shammai disputes; the Hillelite positions are usually followed by later rabbinic law. His ethical teachings ("Do not do unto others what is hateful to yourself; this is the whole Torah, the rest is commentary") are among the most-quoted Jewish ethical sayings.
