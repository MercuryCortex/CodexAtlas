# Nisargadatta Maharaj

## Identity
Mumbai cigarette-shop owner (1897–1981) who became one of the principal 20th-century teachers in the Inchagiri branch of the Navnath sampradaya (an Advaita lineage). His recorded dialogues with seekers, translated by Maurice Frydman as *I Am That* (1973), became a major late-20th-century Advaita text in Western seeker circles.
