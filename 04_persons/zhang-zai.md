# Zhang Zai (張載)

Northern Song Neo-Confucian (1020–1077). Famous for the *Western Inscription* (*Ximing*): "Heaven is my father and Earth is my mother, and even such a small creature as I finds an intimate place in their midst... All people are my brothers and sisters, and all things are my companions." This cosmic statement of universal kinship — grounding all human ethics in the qi-constitution of the cosmos — became one of the most celebrated passages in Neo-Confucian literature.
