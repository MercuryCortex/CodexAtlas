# Matteo Ricci

Jesuit missionary whose *accommodatio* strategy (adapting Christian teaching to Chinese intellectual culture) required deep mastery of the Confucian classics. The first European to enter the Forbidden City, he translated Euclid into Chinese and the Confucian Four Books into Latin. His approach shaped all subsequent Jesuit China mission strategy and was central to the Chinese Rites Controversy that erupted after his death. Key transmission node: Confucian thought → European Enlightenment.
