# Mazdak

**Mazdak** (~470 to ~528 CE) was the **founding heresiarch of Mazdakism** — a radical reform-movement within late-Sasanian Zoroastrianism that preached **a Light-vs-Darkness cosmic-dualism**, **common-ownership of property + women**, **anti-violence**, and **vegetarianism**. He was a court-favorite of Sasanian shah **Kavad I** (-488 to -496 + 498 to 531) during the period of Kavad's first reign + remained politically influential until his execution in 528 CE by then-crown-prince **Khosrow I Anushiruwan**.

## Historical context

Mazdak emerged in a period of severe **late-Sasanian political-economic crisis** (480s-520s): drought, famine, defeated wars against the Hephthalites, and intra-aristocratic conflict. The Mazdakite egalitarian-redistribution program — common-ownership of property, abolition of the aristocratic-property-and-marriage system, voluntary-poverty — drew on cosmic-theological premises to justify radical social-economic restructuring.

Shah **Kavad I** initially adopted the Mazdakite program (probably for political reasons: weakening the high-aristocracy + the orthodox Zoroastrian priesthood who limited his royal authority). Kavad's deposition (-496) was partially provoked by the Mazdakite controversy; on his restoration (498), he initially continued Mazdakite-sympathetic policies but gradually shifted toward orthodox restoration as part of consolidating royal authority against the movement that he had helped empower.

## The theological position

Mazdak's theology fused **Zoroastrian dualism** with **Manichaean Light-vs-Darkness cosmology**:

1. **Cosmic dualism**: the cosmos is the mixture of Light + Darkness. Light is intrinsically good, Darkness intrinsically evil. (Closer to Manichaean than orthodox Zoroastrian framing.)
2. **Salvation = separation**: cosmic and personal salvation is the **separation of Light from Darkness**.
3. **Social-economic conclusion**: the inequality + violence + acquisitiveness of late-Sasanian society are **cosmic-Darkness instantiations**. Property + arms + the hoarding of women are mechanisms by which Darkness binds Light. The corrective is **redistribution + non-violence + vegetarianism + sexual-communalism**.

The position is **structurally radical**: it derives a fully-egalitarian social-program from the cosmic-dualism that orthodox Zoroastrianism + Manichaeism both shared without drawing the egalitarian conclusion.

## The execution + suppression (528 CE)

By the late 520s, then-crown-prince **Khosrow I Anushiruwan** had aligned with the orthodox Zoroastrian priesthood + the surviving high-aristocracy against the Mazdakite movement. In 528, a great convocation was staged at which Mazdak + his principal followers were arrested + executed. Tabari + the *Shahnameh* preserve the elaborate execution-narrative (Mazdak being buried upside-down in a pit + his followers similarly executed in mass-burial).

The suppression secured the **orthodox Zoroastrian restoration** that defined Khosrow I's subsequent reign (531-579) — the high-point of late-Sasanian orthodox-Zoroastrian state-religion + the immediate precursor to the Arab-Islamic conquest (-651).

## MASSIVE-WIN: Mazdak → Khurramites → Shi'a-Mahdi-egalitarianism

The Mazdakite egalitarian-redistribution + anti-elite-authoritarian doctrine **survives the early-6th-c. crackdown** + emerges in modified form in the **Khurramite (Khorramdin) movements** of early-Islamic Iran (8th-9th c.). The Khurramites — most famously Babak Khorramdin's 20-year revolt against the Abbasid caliphate (816-837) — preserve substantial Mazdakite-derived theological + social-economic content under Islamic-period conditions.

The Khurramite tradition subsequently **contributes to the heterodox social-religious-protest tradition** that feeds into **Twelver Shi'ism's expectation of the Mahdi-as-social-restorer**. The Mazdak → Khurramite → Shi'a-Mahdi-egalitarianism chain is one of the **documented Iranian-heterodox-religious-protest inheritance chains** (Crone 2012 *Nativist Prophets of Early Islamic Iran*).
