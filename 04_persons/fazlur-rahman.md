# Fazlur Rahman Malik

## Identity
Pakistani-American Islamic scholar (1919–1988); Oxford DPhil 1949; Director of the Central Institute of Islamic Research (Karachi, 1962–1968) under Ayub Khan; left Pakistan after opposition from religious-conservative ulama; Harold H. Swift Distinguished Service Professor at the University of Chicago Divinity School from 1969 to his death. The principal late-20th-century academic representative of liberal-modernist Islamic interpretation.

## Role in the tradition
A major figure in 20th-century Islamic intellectual history; his "double-movement" Quranic hermeneutic (from particular Quranic injunction to general principle and back to contemporary application) has been influential in reformist Islamic thought.
