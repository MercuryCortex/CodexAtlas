# Moses Cordovero (ha-RaMaK)

## Identity
Sephardic rabbi and Kabbalist of Safed (1522–1570), the principal systematizer of Kabbalah before Luria. Studied halakhah under Joseph Karo and Kabbalah under his brother-in-law Solomon Alkabetz. From age 26, composed the massive *Pardes Rimmonim* (1548), the systematic exposition of Kabbalah; pursued his lifelong project of a comprehensive Zohar commentary (*Or Yakar*, ~16 modern volumes). His students included briefly Isaac Luria (1569–1570) and Chaim Vital. Cordoveran Kabbalah remained authoritative alongside Lurianic Kabbalah in subsequent transmission and was foundational for the Hasidic movement of the 18th century.

## Historical evidence
Documented: his own substantial corpus, Safed community records, the testimony of his students.

## Works
*Pardes Rimmonim* (1548). *Or Yakar* on the Zohar. *Elimah Rabbati*. *Tomer Devorah*. *Or Ne'erav*. *Sefer Gerushin*.

## Role in the tradition
Cordovero is the architect of systematic Kabbalah. The *Pardes* is the indispensable systematic text of the Safed-Kabbalistic renaissance; the *Or Yakar* is the longest pre-modern Zohar commentary. Cordoveran theology was partly displaced by Lurianic theology after 1570 but remained continuously studied; Hasidism integrates Cordoveran and Lurianic categories.
