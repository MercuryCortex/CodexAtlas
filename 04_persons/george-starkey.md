# George Starkey

**George Starkey** (1628–1665) — born in Bermuda, educated at Harvard College (class of 1646), later active in London where he died in the plague — wrote as **Eirenaeus Philalethes** and became the most important practical alchemist of the 17th century. His operational methods directly shaped Robert Boyle's experimental program, and Isaac Newton copied his manuscripts by hand. He is the vault's key figure in the transmission from Renaissance Hermetic alchemy into early modern chemistry and Newtonian physics. See [[phase-6-049-starkey-secrets-revealed]].
