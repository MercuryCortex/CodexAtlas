# George Ripley

**George Ripley** (c.1415–1490) — Augustinian Canon of Bridlington Priory — is the canonical English alchemist of the 15th century. His *Compound of Alchemy* (1471) describes the Great Work through twelve allegorical Gates; the associated *Ripley Scroll* (painted parchment roll, 23 known copies) is one of the most remarkable visual documents in English mystical history. Elias Ashmole placed him at the centre of English alchemical tradition. See [[phase-6-046-ripley-compound-of-alchemy]].
