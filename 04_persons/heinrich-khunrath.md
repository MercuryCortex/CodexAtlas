# Heinrich Khunrath

## Identity
Late-16th-c. German Lutheran physician, alchemist, and Christian-Kabbalistic Hermetic author (1560–1605). Studied at Leipzig and Basel (MD Basel 1588). **Met John Dee at Bremen ~1589** — a major encounter for both men, evidenced in Dee's diaries. Author of ***Amphitheatrum Sapientiae Aeternae*** (first incomplete edition Hamburg 1595; second posthumous-complete edition Hanau 1609 with engraved plates) — **the prototype Christian-Hermetic-Kabbalistic-alchemical emblem book**, the genre Maier's *Atalanta Fugiens* (1617) and Fludd's *Utriusque Cosmi Historia* (1617–1621) would develop further.

Khunrath's *Amphitheatrum* is **one of the iconic flagship holdings of the Bibliotheca Philosophica Hermetica / Ritman Library** and a centerpiece of the Beinecke Mellon Collection. The engraved plates — especially the **'oratorium-laboratorium'** plate showing the alchemist's chamber with simultaneous prayer-altar and laboratory-bench — are the **canonical visual emblem of alchemy-as-spiritual-discipline** and have been continuously reproduced for over four centuries.

## Works
- ***Amphitheatrum Sapientiae Aeternae*** (Hamburg 1595, Hanau 1609) — see [[phase-6-034-khunrath-amphitheatrum-sapientiae]].
- ***Confessio de Chao Physico-Chymicorum Catholico*** (1597).

- ***De Igne Magorum Philosophorumque*** (1608, posthumous).

## Role in the tradition
- **Inaugurator of the Christian-Hermetic-Kabbalistic alchemical emblem-book.** Khunrath's *Amphitheatrum* synthesizes (a) Lutheran biblical-Christian piety, (b) Christian Kabbalah (Pico-Reuchlin lineage), (c) Hermetic-alchemical natural philosophy, (d) Paracelsian medical alchemy, (e) iconographically-elaborated emblem and engraving — into a single integrated work. The synthesis is the prototype for the Rosicrucian-era emblem books.
- **Alchemy-as-spiritual-discipline canonical image.** The 'oratorium-laboratorium' plate is THE single most-reproduced visual emblem of the spiritual-material integration that the Western alchemical tradition aspired to.
- **Dee-encounter.** The 1589 Bremen meeting with John Dee is one of the key network-connections between English (Dee, later Fludd) and continental (Khunrath, later Maier, Boehme) Hermetic-Kabbalistic circles.
