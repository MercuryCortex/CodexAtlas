# Doreen Valiente

## Identity
British poet and Wiccan priestess (1922–1999); initiated into Gardner's Bricket Wood coven 1953; served as Gardner's High Priestess for several years before parting with him over disputes about publicity and authority. Substantially rewrote Gardner's draft *Book of Shadows*, replacing recognizably Crowleyan and Masonic material with original poetic-liturgical compositions including the canonical "Charge of the Goddess" (Valiente's adaptation/rewrite of an earlier Charles Leland-derived source).

## Role in the tradition
Often called "the mother of modern witchcraft" for her liturgical contributions; without Valiente's poetic re-working, much of standard Wiccan ritual would not exist in its current form.
