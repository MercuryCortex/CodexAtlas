# Proclus

Scholarch of the Athenian Platonic Academy from ~437 until his death in 485. Born in Constantinople, educated in Lycia and Alexandria, came to Athens to study with Plutarch of Athens and then Syrianus, whom he succeeded. Proclus is the most systematic Neoplatonist — the *Elements of Theology* presents 211 numbered propositions with deductive proofs, and the *Platonic Theology* expounds the entire pagan pantheon in Neoplatonist hypostatic order. His Christian-Neoplatonist redaction by Pseudo-Dionysius and the Arabic *Liber de Causis* made him decisive (often unacknowledged) for both Eastern and Western medieval theology.

## Cross-tradition position

**Proclus henads → Kabbalistic sefirot (the Scholem thesis)**
Proclus's *henads* — divine unities that mediate between the transcendent One and lower hypostatic levels — are structurally identical to the Kabbalistic sefirot. Each henad is a real aspect of the One's self-differentiation, particularized as a divine power (a "god" in Proclean usage), neither identical with the One nor fully separate from it. The channel: ibn Gabirol's *Fons Vitae* (~1050 CE, written in Arabic in Andalusia, translated into Latin as *Fons Vitae* ~1150) is a Neoplatonic-Jewish synthesis that entered Provençal Jewish intellectual culture and influenced Isaac the Blind's Kabbalistic circle (~1200). Scholem (1962) argued this is the documented transmission of the Proclean emanation scheme into Kabbalah. The Kabbalistic innovation: the abstract henads become mythological, personified, sexually-charged divine figures (with gendered pairing: Tiferet/Shekhinah) — the emotionalization of the Proclean scheme. See [[sefirot]], [[tradition-kabbalah]].

**Proclus → Pseudo-Dionysius — the most consequential philosophical disguise in history**
Pseudo-Dionysius the Areopagite rewrote Proclus in Christian dress. The *Celestial Hierarchy*'s nine orders of angels map directly onto Proclean hypostatic levels; the apophatic logic of *Mystical Theology* is Ennead V/VI Proclean theology with "God" substituted for "the One." Thomas Aquinas, citing Ps-Dionysius, is citing Proclus without knowing it. Meister Eckhart's *Gottheit* (Godhead) — the ground beyond God, beyond all predication — is Proclean apophaticism filtered through the Dionysian tradition. The medieval West's most important mystical theologians are all unknowing Procleans. See [[pseudo-dionysius-the-areopagite]].

**Proclus → *Liber de Causis* → Aquinas → the scholastic synthesis**
The Arabic *Liber de Causis* (9th c. Baghdad) excerpts and redacts the *Elements of Theology*. It was translated into Latin ~1185, widely read as Aristotelian, and constituted a primary source for medieval cosmology — until Aquinas, reading Moerbeke's new Latin translation of the *Elements* (1268), recognized: "in this book almost everything is taken from Proclus." The correction did not diminish the influence; it just clarified the attribution. Proclus's emanation scheme is structurally present in the metaphysics of Aquinas, Duns Scotus, and the entire medieval *esse* tradition. See [[tradition-neoplatonism]].
