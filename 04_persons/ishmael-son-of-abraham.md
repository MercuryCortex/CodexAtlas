# Ishmael

## Identity
Firstborn son of Abraham, by Hagar; circumcised at thirteen (Gen 17); expelled with Hagar to the wilderness after Isaac's weaning (Gen 21); blessed by YHWH to become a great nation (Gen 17:20). The eponymous ancestor of the Ishmaelite tribes inhabiting the wilderness south and east of Canaan and — in the developed Islamic tradition — the ancestor of the Arabs and specifically of the Quraysh from whom Muhammad descends.

## Historical evidence
None extrabiblical. The Ishmaelite traders of Gen 37 (who carry Joseph to Egypt) and Judges 8 are plausibly a real Iron-Age tribal designation; the Ishmael-figure himself is internal to the patriarchal narrative.

## Role in the tradition
- **Hebrew Bible.** Ishmael represents the *recognized but non-covenantal* Abrahamic line.
- **New Testament.** Paul's Galatians 4:21-31 reads Ishmael allegorically as the child of the slave-woman / Sinai / present Jerusalem / persecutor (Gen 21:9 of Isaac).
- **Islamic tradition.** Isma'il is a prophet (Quran 19:54, 21:85). Together with his father Ibrahim he raises the foundations of the Ka'ba in Mecca (Quran 2:127). In dominant Sunni tafsir (though not in the Quranic text itself, which leaves the son unnamed), Isma'il — not Ishaq — is the son nearly sacrificed in the *Aqedah* parallel.
