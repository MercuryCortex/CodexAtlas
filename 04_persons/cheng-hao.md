# Cheng Hao (程顥)

Northern Song Neo-Confucian (1032–1085); elder of the two Cheng brothers. Where his brother [[cheng-yi]] emphasized rigorous investigation of external things, Cheng Hao emphasized the moral heart's direct recognition of its unity with Heaven — a position closer to what Wang Yangming would later develop as *liangzhi*. The two brothers' different emphases generated the two main streams of later Neo-Confucianism.
