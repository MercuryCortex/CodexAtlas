# Jörg Lanz von Liebenfels (Adolf Josef Lanz)

**Critical-tiering note:** This node documents a **racist-occult figure** whose contribution to early-20th-c. Ariosophical and proto-Nazi ideational currents is real, documented, and consequential. Per the Codex Atlas methodology (see [[../00_meta/methodology.md|methodology]] on the swastika rule and on handling far-right religious-political figures), the entry **explicitly tiers** Lanz's content: the **historical-attestation-and-influence-claims** are Tier-1-documented; the **ideational content itself is presented as historically-attested racist-occult material**, not as defensible religion or scholarship. **Goodrick-Clarke 1985 is the load-bearing critical source.**

## Identity
Austrian ex-Cistercian, racist-occult author, and **founder of the [[tradition-ordo-novi-templi|Ordo Novi Templi]]** (Order of the New Templars) — the principal Ariosophical racist-occult Templar-revivalist organization (1907 onward). Born **Adolf Josef Lanz** in Vienna 1874; entered the Cistercian novitiate at Heiligenkreuz Abbey 1893; left under disputed circumstances in 1899; thereafter assumed the fictional aristocratic name **Jörg Lanz von Liebenfels** and developed a **synthesis of Theosophical racial-evolutionary metaphysics, Wagnerian-Romantic Germanic-pagan-Christian revival, and 19th-c. occult Templar-Gnostic-Baphomet literature** — in an **explicitly racist political register**.

In 1907 Lanz **purchased the ruined Burg Werfenstein** (Upper Austria, on the Danube) explicitly to **refound the Templar order on racist-Ariosophical lines** — the **Ordo Novi Templi (ONT)** was formally founded on **25 December 1907** at Werfenstein with **a swastika banner raised over the castle tower** (one of the earliest 20th-c. uses of the swastika as racist-political symbol). Lanz published the periodical ***Ostara*** (89 issues, 1905–1931) as the principal vehicle of his Ariosophical content.

**The young Adolf Hitler is documented to have read *Ostara* during his Vienna years (1908–1913)**, on the basis of Lanz's own subsequent claims and circumstantial period evidence (Daim 1958). **The strength of the Hitler-Lanz ideational influence is disputed** — Goodrick-Clarke 1985 treats it as documented-but-probably-overstated by Lanz, but accepts the basic influence-chain. **Lanz's content unquestionably entered the broader fin-de-siècle Vienna racist-Wagnerian-occult milieu** that Hitler also drew from independently.

The Ordo Novi Templi was **suppressed by the National Socialist regime in 1942** — the Nazis suppressed Ariosophy and other occult-racist groups as ideological competitors. Lanz's post-1945 self-reframing claimed the Nazis had "stolen his ideas"; the claim is **partially-defensible** (the 1942 ONT suppression is real) but **elides Lanz's own foundational racist-occult contribution** to the broader ideational current.

## Role in the tradition
- **Founder of the Ordo Novi Templi (ONT)** — the principal Ariosophical racist-occult Templar-revivalist organization.
- **Architect of the integrated Ariosophical synthesis** — Templar-revival + Theosophical race-evolution + Wagnerian-Romantic + Nordic-pagan-and-Christian.
- **Documented ideational source in the Hitler-Vienna context** — the strength of the influence-claim is debated but the basic influence-channel is documented.
- **One of the foundational 20th-c. examples of how Templar-revival iconography migrated into racist-political-religious systems** — see [[templar-gnostic-transmission-hypothesis]] reception-history analysis.

## Disputes
- **Strength of the Hitler-Lanz influence.** Daim 1958 strong-claim; Goodrick-Clarke 1985 critically-refined; modern consensus: documented-influence-channel-but-not-sole-or-primary-source.
- **Whether Lanz himself was personally racist by conviction or opportunistically.** Documented: he was personally racist. The 1942 Nazi suppression of his organization complicates retrospective Lanz self-positioning as Nazi-victim.
- **The Werfenstein 25 December 1907 swastika-raising as foundation-event.** Goodrick-Clarke documents this; it is one of the **earliest documented 20th-c. uses of the swastika as racist-political symbol** (predating the broader völkisch-and-Nazi adoption).
