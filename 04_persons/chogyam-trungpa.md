# Chögyam Trungpa Rinpoche

## Identity
Tibetan lama (1939–1987); 11th Trungpa Tulku in the Kagyu lineage; fled Tibet 1959 after the Chinese invasion; Oxford 1963; founded the first Western Buddhist contemplative university (Naropa Institute, Boulder, 1974, later Naropa University). Major vector of Tibetan Vajrayana to North America. Created the synthetic "Shambhala Buddhism" framework integrating Vajrayana with elements of the Shambhala mythos.

## Disputes
- **Conduct allegations**: serious documented allegations of sexual misconduct, abuse of power, and alcohol-related deaths within Trungpa's community (Sara Bell, Stephen T. Butterfield, others); the 2018 Wickwire-Holecek Project Sunshine reports document extensive subsequent misconduct in the Shambhala lineage under Trungpa's son Sakyong Mipham. The historical-scholarly evaluation of Trungpa's "crazy wisdom" framing as cover for abuse is an active subject. Treat sympathetic-internal sources (Diana J. Mukpo memoirs etc.) and external critical sources together.
