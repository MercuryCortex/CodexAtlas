# Sun Myung Moon

## Identity
Korean religious leader (1920–2012); founder of the Unification Church (1954 in Seoul, renamed Family Federation for World Peace and Unification 1996). Claimed an Easter 1935 vision of Jesus commissioning him to complete Christ's mission. Taught (in *Divine Principle*) that Jesus had successfully accomplished spiritual but not physical salvation, and that a "Lord of the Second Advent" born in Korea ~1920 would complete the physical-salvific work — Moon himself (publicly acknowledged in 1992 announcements). The mass weddings ("Blessing Ceremonies") performed by Moon are the church's most publicly visible practice.

## Role in the tradition
Founder; central authority during his lifetime. Significant downstream institutional empire including media (*Washington Times*, founded 1982) and businesses.
