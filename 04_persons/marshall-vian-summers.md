# Marshall Vian Summers

## Identity
American religious figure (b. 1949); founder of The Society for the New Message from God / The Worldwide Community of the New Message from God. From 1981 onward has claimed continuous reception of revelation from "the Angelic Assembly" producing a multi-volume scriptural corpus. The teachings include a distinctive "Greater Community" cosmology positing humanity's imminent contact with extraterrestrial civilizations and the spiritual challenges this entails.

## Role in the tradition
Founder; principal recipient of the channeled corpus.
