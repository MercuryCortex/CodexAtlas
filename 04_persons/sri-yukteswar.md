# Sri Yukteswar Giri

## Identity
Bengali yogi (1855–1936); pupil of Lahiri Mahasaya; guru of Yogananda. Author of *The Holy Science* (1894), a short text arguing for the underlying unity of the Christian Bible and the Sanskrit scriptures.
