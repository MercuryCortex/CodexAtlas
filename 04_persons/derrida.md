# Jacques Derrida

Algerian-born French philosopher, founder of deconstruction. Key concept: *différance* — meaning is never simply present, always deferred and differentiated; no "transcendental signified" anchors the chain of signifiers. Anti-foundationalist: there is no bedrock, no first principle, no finally present ground. Structurally parallel to [[nagarjuna]]'s śūnyatā: no phenomenon is self-present; all meaning is relational and empty of inherent presence. Robert Magliola (*Derrida on the Mend*, Purdue UP, 1984) documented this parallel systematically.
