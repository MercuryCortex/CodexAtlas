# Zhou Dunyi (周敦頤)

First of the Northern Song Neo-Confucian masters (1017–1073). His *Taijitu shuo* ("Explanation of the Diagram of the Supreme Ultimate") derives Confucian ethics from cosmological first principles: Wuji (without limit) → Taiji (Supreme Ultimate) → yin-yang → Five Phases → human moral nature. This cosmological grounding of ethics — drawing on Daoist cosmological vocabulary while insisting on Confucian ethical conclusions — establishes the pattern for all subsequent Neo-Confucian synthesis.
