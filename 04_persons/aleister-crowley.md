# Aleister Crowley

## Identity
English occultist, poet, mountaineer, and chess player (1875–1947). Initiated into the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn (1898); split with Mathers; received *Liber AL vel Legis* in Cairo (April 1904); founded the A∴A∴ (1907); took over the Ordo Templi Orientis (1912 onward); the "Beast 666" of his magical-mystical identity; "the wickedest man in the world" of the British tabloid press of the 1920s.

## Works
See texts-authored. The *Equinox* periodical (1909–1919) contains much of his foundational technical writing.

## Role in the tradition
Founder of Thelema. Significant downstream influence on 20th-century occultism, modern Paganism (via Gardner), Satanism (via LaVey), chaos magick (via Spare and Carroll), and contemporary occult culture broadly.
