# L. Ron Hubbard

## Identity
American science fiction and pulp-fiction writer (1911–1986); founded Dianetics (1950) and Scientology (1953–1954); developed the Sea Org (1967) as the senior organization of Scientology; produced thousands of bulletins, policy letters, lectures, and "technical" materials that constitute the Scientology scriptural corpus. **Substantial biographical controversy: external scholarship (Miller 1987, Wright 2013, Atack 1990, Urban 2011, Reitman 2011) documents that many of the church-published biographical claims do not match documentary evidence (US Navy records, university records, expedition records). The church disputes external accounts.** [claim: contested]

## Role in the tradition
Founder; the principal author of all Scientology scripture; the central authority of the movement during his lifetime. Successor David Miscavige has led the church since 1986/87.
