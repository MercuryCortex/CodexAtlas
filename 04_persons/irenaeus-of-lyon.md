# Irenaeus of Lyon

Bishop of Lugdunum (Lyon) from ~177 (succeeding the martyr Pothinus) until his death ~202. Born in Smyrna in Asia Minor; heard Polycarp, who claimed to have heard the apostle John. Irenaeus's five-book *Against Heresies* (~180) is the foundational document of Christian heresiology and our richest external evidence for second-century Gnostic systems — Sethian (the BarBeloites), Valentinian (Ptolemaeus, his immediate target), Marcionite, Carpocratian, Ophite. Irenaeus also articulates: the apostolic-succession argument; the four-Gospel canon as exclusive (Adv. Haer. 3.11.8); the rule of faith; and the theology of recapitulation. His role in shaping what would become orthodoxy is hard to overstate.
