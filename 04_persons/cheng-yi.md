# Cheng Yi (程頤)

Northern Song Neo-Confucian (1033–1107); younger of the two Cheng brothers (with [[cheng-hao]]). Cheng Yi systematized the concept of *li* (Principle) as the metaphysical-ethical ground of all things and emphasized *gewu* (investigating things) as the path to moral knowledge — the position Zhu Xi would later inherit and Wang Yangming would reject.
