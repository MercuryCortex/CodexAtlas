# Anton Szandor LaVey

## Identity
American occultist (1930–1997); founder of the Church of Satan (incorporated 1966, in his Black House at 6114 California Street, San Francisco). Composed *The Satanic Bible* (1969), *The Satanic Rituals* (1972), *The Satanic Witch* (1971), and *The Devil's Notebook* (1992).

## Role in the tradition
Founder of modern LaVeyan (symbolic-atheistic) Satanism, the largest and most influential branch of organized modern Satanism. Distinct from theistic Satanism (Temple of Set 1975+, the Satanic Temple 2013+).

## Disputes
- **Biographical claims**: LaVey's pre-Church-of-Satan biography contains contested elements (carnival lion-tamer, organist, San Francisco police photographer); some are corroborated, some contested.
