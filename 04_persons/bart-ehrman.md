# Bart D. Ehrman

## Identity
American New Testament textual critic and historian (b. 1955); James A. Gray Distinguished Professor at the University of North Carolina, Chapel Hill. Doctoral student of Bruce Metzger at Princeton Theological Seminary. Underwent a documented religious trajectory from evangelical (Moody Bible Institute, Wheaton) through liberal Christianity to agnosticism, partly through his text-critical work. His many trade-press books (over 30) have made him a principal popular vector for late-20th-/early-21st-century New Testament textual scholarship to general audiences.

## Works
See texts-authored. *The Orthodox Corruption of Scripture* (1993) is the foundational scholarly work; *Misquoting Jesus* (2005) the principal popular-bestseller.

## Role in the tradition
Together with Pagels, Ehrman is the most widely-read late-20th-/early-21st-century North American scholar of early Christianity. His textual-critical work on the "orthodox corruption" of the New Testament text (deliberate scribal alterations theologically motivated) is influential within the discipline.

## Connections
- Third Quest historical-Jesus scholarship alongside [[geza-vermes]], [[n-t-wright]], Sanders, Crossan, Allison — Ehrman's apocalyptic-prophet Jesus is methodologically aligned with Vermes/Sanders/Allison and opposed on resurrection methodology to Wright.
