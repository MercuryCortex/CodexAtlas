# Tiberius

Second Roman emperor (r. 14–37), adopted son and successor of [[caesar-augustus|Augustus]]. His reign covers Jesus of Nazareth's adult ministry: [[phase-3-018-luke-acts|Luke 3:1]] dates John the Baptist's mission to "the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar" (c. 28–29 CE); Pontius Pilate was Tiberius's appointed prefect of Judaea (26–36 CE) — the Roman authority under whose jurisdiction the Crucifixion occurred. Tiberius is mentioned by Tacitus in the famous passage on the Christians (*Annals* 15.44, where the Neronian persecution context provides the earliest non-Christian historical reference to "Christus"-as-historical-figure-executed-under-Pontius-Pilate-under-Tiberius). His late-reign retirement to Capri and the Sejanus crisis are documented in detail by Tacitus and Suetonius.
