# Augustus

First Roman emperor (r. -27 to +14); established the Principate after the civil wars + dedicated the rebuilt Pantheon (in its early form by Agrippa) + restored Roman religious institutions. Reigning at the time of the birth of Jesus (Luke 2:1). Deified after death — the first of the Roman imperial-cult divi.
