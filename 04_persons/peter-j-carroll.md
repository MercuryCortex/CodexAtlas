# Peter J. Carroll

## Identity
British esotericist (b. 1953); with Ray Sherwin, founder of the Illuminates of Thanateros (IOT, 1978) and principal theoretician of *chaos magick*. *Liber Null* (1978) and *Psychonaut* (1982) inaugurated the chaos-magick current, which strips magickal technique from Thelemic and Golden-Dawn cosmological commitments and reframes "belief" itself as a magickal tool (paradigm-shifting).

## Role in the tradition
Heir to the [[aleister-crowley]] / [[tradition-thelema]] lineage ([[phase-7-008-book-of-the-law]], [[phase-7-016-magick-in-theory-and-practice]]) and the Austin Osman Spare current; the principal architect of late-20th-century post-Thelemic ritual magick that uses Spare's sigil-method while rejecting Crowley's cosmological apparatus.
