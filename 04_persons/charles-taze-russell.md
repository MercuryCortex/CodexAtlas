# Charles Taze Russell

## Identity
American restorationist preacher (1852–1916); founded what became Jehovah's Witnesses out of the Bible Student / Watch Tower movement. His *Studies in the Scriptures* expounded a millennialist Christology with specific date-predictions (1874 invisible return of Christ; 1914 end of the Gentile Times). The 1914 prediction was partially preserved in JW theology (as the date of Christ's invisible enthronement, with the actual visible kingdom yet to come).

## Role in the tradition
Founder of the institutional ancestor of Jehovah's Witnesses; the Watch Tower Society (1881 onward) is the principal corporate continuation. Joseph F. Rutherford (presiding 1917–1942) significantly restructured the movement after Russell's death and renamed it "Jehovah's Witnesses" in 1931.
