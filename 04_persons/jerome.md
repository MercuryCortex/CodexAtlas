# Jerome

Latin Christian scholar (~347–420). Produced the Vulgate — the Latin translation of the Bible that became the standard Western text for a millennium — by working from Hebrew rather than the Greek Septuagint for the Old Testament, a controversial move at the time (Augustine objected). Lived as an ascetic in Bethlehem from ~388. His correspondence and his learned vituperation against opponents (Origenists, Pelagians, Jovinian) make him a vivid presence in late-fourth-century church politics.
