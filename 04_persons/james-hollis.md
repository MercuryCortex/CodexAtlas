# James Hollis

## Identity
American Jungian analyst (b. 1940); trained at the C.G. Jung Institute in Zürich; former Executive Director of the Jung Centers of Houston and Washington DC. Among the most widely-read contemporary Jungian-analyst writers for general audiences, continuing the [[james-hillman]] and [[carl-gustav-jung]] line of mid-late-life individuation and mythic-psychological writing.
