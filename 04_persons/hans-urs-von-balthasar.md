# Hans Urs von Balthasar

## Identity
Swiss Catholic theologian (1905–1988); briefly a Jesuit; later co-founder with Adrienne von Speyr of the Johannesgemeinschaft (Community of St John). Author of the great 15-volume trilogy *Herrlichkeit / Theodramatik / Theologik* (English: *The Glory of the Lord* / *Theo-Drama* / *Theo-Logic*) — a theological-aesthetic / dramatic / logical synthesis on a scale comparable to Barth's *Church Dogmatics*. Not a *peritus* at Vatican II but a major intellectual presence in mid-late-20th-c. Catholic theology.

## Role in the tradition
With [[henri-de-lubac]] and [[joseph-ratzinger-benedict-xvi]], founder of the *Communio* journal (1972) — the *ressourcement* alternative to the more progressive *Concilium* journal (founded 1965). His Christology, aesthetic-theology, and (mildly universalist) eschatology have shaped contemporary Catholic theology substantially. *Dare We Hope That All Men Be Saved?* (1986) places him at the start of the recent Catholic engagement with the universalism question that [[david-bentley-hart]] would more strongly develop.
