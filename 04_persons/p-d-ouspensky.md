# P.D. Ouspensky

## Identity
Russian esotericist (1878–1947); met Gurdjieff in Moscow 1915; pupil 1915–1924; thereafter taught the Gurdjieff "system" independently in London and surrounding circles (1924–1947). His posthumous *In Search of the Miraculous* (1949) systematized Gurdjieff's oral teaching and remains the principal entry-point to the Fourth Way.

## Works
See texts-authored.

## Role in the tradition
Ouspensky's systematic exposition substantially shaped how the Fourth Way reached the English-speaking world.
