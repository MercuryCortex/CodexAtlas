# Stephen McNallen

## Identity
American founder of the Asatru Folk Assembly (AFA, 1994; predecessor organization Asatru Free Assembly 1972–1986); a principal American figure in the late-20th-century Norse-pagan / Heathen revival. McNallen's "folkish" Asatru position — that Asatru is the ancestral religion of those of Northern European ethnic descent — has been one focus of long-running internal Heathen disputes (folkish vs. universalist Asatru), and McNallen's AFA has been categorized as racist or white-nationalist by the Southern Poverty Law Center; McNallen and his followers contest this characterization.

## Role in the tradition
A central figure in 20th-century American Asatru; the universalist branch of American Heathenry (The Troth, the Asatru Alliance partly, Heathens Against Hate) is in significant tension with McNallen's folkish position.

## Disputes
- **Folkish vs. universalist Asatru**: the principal internal Heathen dispute; McNallen represents the folkish wing.
