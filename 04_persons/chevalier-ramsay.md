# Andrew Michael Ramsay (Chevalier Ramsay)

## Identity
Scottish-born writer, tutor, Jacobite exile, Catholic convert, and Freemason. Born in Ayr, Scotland; educated at Edinburgh and later under Archbishop Fénelon at Cambrai (France), whose mystical Catholic spirituality profoundly influenced him. He became tutor to the Stuart pretender Charles Edward Stuart's brother Henry in France. A Knight of the Order of St. Lazarus (hence "Chevalier"). He delivered the single most consequential oration in Freemasonic history in 1737.

## Historical Evidence
Ramsay is well documented: his correspondence, his published work *The Travels of Cyrus* (1727, a philosophical-religious novel), and the reception of his Oration in contemporary Masonic and ecclesiastical records are all attested. The Oration itself exists in multiple manuscript versions (the "London" and "Paris" variants, with small differences).

## The 1737 Oration

Delivered to a French Masonic lodge (~March 1737), the Oration made the claim that became Freemasonry's most influential founding myth:

> "At the time of the Crusades in Palestine, many princes, lords, and citizens associated themselves, and vowed to restore the Temple of the Christians in the Holy Land, to employ themselves in bringing back their architecture to its first institution; they agreed upon several ancient signs and symbolic words drawn from the religion of the nature of Noah, in order to recognize one another among the heathen and Saracens."

The argument: **Freemasonry did not begin with London operative stonemasons or even with Solomon's Temple builders — it began with Crusader knights in Jerusalem, heirs to the ancient mysteries, who brought sacred architectural knowledge back to Europe.** This Crusader-origin myth:
1. Attached Freemasonry to **chivalric-noble** rather than artisan origins — socially elevating the fraternity
2. Created the genealogical link from Masonic ritual (Solomon's Temple) through **Crusader military orders** to contemporary lodges
3. Provided the narrative architecture on which the **Scottish Rite's high degrees** (especially the Rosicrucian 18th degree and the Templar-themed degrees) were subsequently built
4. Encoded **Jacobite political symbolism**: the Crusader knights returning to restore the rightful king → the Stuart exiles returning to restore the rightful British monarch. The "lost word" whose recovery is the Royal Arch's goal = the legitimate king's return. The parallel was intelligible to contemporaries.

**The Oration is the founding document of the Scottish Rite's ideological self-understanding** — even though the Scottish Rite's formal structure emerged decades later (Charleston, South Carolina, 1801 for the AASR). Ramsay's Crusader-Templar-Masonic genealogy is the conceptual skeleton.

## Ramsay and the Stuart Cause
Ramsay was a committed Jacobite (Stuart supporter). His position in the household of the Stuart pretender in France meant the Oration was composed in an explicitly Stuart-loyalist context. The coded political reading — Freemasonry as a Crusader-descended fraternity awaiting the restoration of the rightful king — was not accidental. The Scottish Rite's 33 degrees crystallize this symbolism into a graduated initiatory system, with the higher degrees encoding increasingly explicit Templar-Crusader-Stuart imagery.
