# Paul Tillich

## Identity
German-American Lutheran theologian (1886–1965); served as German military chaplain WWI (a major formative experience); academic posts at Marburg (briefly with Heidegger), Dresden, Leipzig, Frankfurt; dismissed in 1933 as among the first non-Jewish German academics to be removed by the Nazi regime; emigrated to Union Theological Seminary, NYC, 1933; Harvard from 1955; Chicago from 1962. Author of the 3-volume *Systematic Theology* and the influential popular *Courage to Be* (1952).

## Role in the tradition
The principal mid-20th-century philosophical-existential Christian theologian in the English-speaking world; "God as the ground of being" is his signature formulation; his "Protestant Principle" of constant self-critique against any absolutization remains widely influential in liberal Christianity.
