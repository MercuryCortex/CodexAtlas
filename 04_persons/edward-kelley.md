# Edward Kelley

## Identity
English alchemist and scryer (1555–1597), best known as John Dee's medium in the Enochian-angelic-communication sessions of 1582–1589. Background obscure (possibly born Edward Talbot; reported to have had his ears cropped for forgery before meeting Dee). Joined Dee at Mortlake in 1582 and became his principal scryer (Dee himself could not see in the shew-stone); accompanied Dee on the 1583–1589 continental tour. Quarreled with Dee in 1587 (the notorious "wife-swapping" angel-command episode); ennobled by Rudolf II at Prague as Sir Edward Kelley for alchemical work; subsequently imprisoned at Krivoklát Castle for failing to produce gold; died after a fall during an escape attempt 1597.

## Historical evidence
The Dee diaries are the primary source; Rudolfine court records document the Prague years; Elias Ashmole's 17th-c. recovery of the manuscripts established the textual basis.

## Works
The Enochian materials are jointly Dee-and-Kelley productions; Kelley's role is as the scryer who reported the angelic visions and the Enochian language. Whether the Enochian language is Kelley's invention, a glossolalic production, or (as Dee believed) a genuine angelic transmission is variously debated.
