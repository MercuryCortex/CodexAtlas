# Mary Baker Eddy

## Identity
American religious leader (1821–1910); founder of the Church of Christ, Scientist (chartered 1879; restructured 1892 as the Mother Church in Boston). Author of *Science and Health with Key to the Scriptures* (1875), revised through ~430 editions in her lifetime; founder of the *Christian Science Monitor* newspaper (1908).

## Role in the tradition
The dominant institutional and theological figure of Christian Science; her authority extends posthumously through the Mother Church *Manual* (1895).
