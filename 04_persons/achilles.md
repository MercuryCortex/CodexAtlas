# Achilles

The premier hero of the Trojan cycle and central figure of Homer's *Iliad* — son of the mortal Peleus and the sea-nymph Thetis; commander of the Myrmidons; the *aristos Akhaiōn* (best of the Achaeans). His *mēnis* (wrath) over Briseis is the *Iliad*'s opening word and structuring theme. He killed Hector and was killed in turn (off-stage in the *Iliad*) by Paris's arrow guided by Apollo. [[alexander-the-great|Alexander]] consciously modelled himself on Achilles — carried Aristotle's edition of the *Iliad* on campaign, slept with it under his pillow per Plutarch, ran around the tomb of Achilles at Troy at the start of the Asian campaign in 334 BCE. The Achilles-Patroclus pairing has been read across millennia as the archetypal heroic-companion bond.
