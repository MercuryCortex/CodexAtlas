Ibn al-Haytham (Alhazen) is the first recorded scientist to use systematic controlled experiment to refute a theory. His *Kitāb al-manāẓir* (1021 CE) disproved the Greek emission theory of vision through experiment, established optics as an empirical science, and introduced the camera obscura as a demonstration device. See [[mathematics-house-of-wisdom]]. Directly transmitted to Roger Bacon, Witelo, and ultimately Kepler (*Dioptrics*, 1611).
