# Israel Regardie

## Identity
English-born American occultist (1907–1985); Aleister Crowley's secretary 1928–1932 ([[aleister-crowley]]); later a member of the Stella Matutina temple of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. His 1937–1940 publication of the Golden Dawn's complete instructional and ritual material (in defiance of his oaths) made the system publicly accessible and is the basis for the substantial late-20th-century Western ceremonial-magick revival.

## Role in the tradition
The principal vector through which the late-19th-c. Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn material entered post-war public domain. Without Regardie's 1937–1940 publication, much of subsequent Western ceremonial magick — Wicca's borrowings ([[gerald-gardner]]; [[tradition-wicca-modern-pagan]]), chaos magick ([[peter-j-carroll]]), the Llewellyn / Weiser publishing wave — would have been impossible.
