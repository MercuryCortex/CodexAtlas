# Akiva ben Yosef

One of the most influential tannaim — Akiva's exegetical method (every word, every letter, every crown above each letter of Torah carries meaning) shaped the maximalist rabbinic approach to scripture. He reportedly supported the Bar Kokhba Revolt (132–136), proclaiming Bar Kokhba as the messianic redeemer — a position the Talmud both records and partly disowns after the revolt's catastrophic failure. Tortured to death by the Romans for teaching Torah under Hadrian's ban; the Talmud preserves his death scene as a paradigmatic martyrdom.
