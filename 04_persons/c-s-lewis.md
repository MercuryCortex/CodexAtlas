# C.S. Lewis

## Identity
British academic and writer (1898–1963); Fellow of Magdalen College, Oxford (English) 1925–1954; Chair of Medieval and Renaissance English at Cambridge from 1954. Atheist until ~1929; convert to Christianity (Anglican) 1931 partly through conversations with Tolkien. The Inklings (Lewis, Tolkien, Charles Williams) met weekly at the Eagle and Child pub in Oxford. The principal 20th-century popular Christian apologist in the English-speaking world.

## Role in the tradition
A major vector of mid-20th-century popular Christian apologetics, especially through the BBC wartime broadcasts that became *Mere Christianity*. His use of Otto's *numinous* (in *Problem of Pain*) and of "deep magic" in the Narnia books popularized Otto-derived numinous-experience vocabulary.
