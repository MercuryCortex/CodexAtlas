# Dietrich Bonhoeffer

## Identity
German Lutheran pastor and theologian (1906–1945); doctoral work at Berlin under Reinhold Seeberg; participated in the Confessing Church's opposition to the Nazi-aligned Deutsche Christen movement; involved in the resistance to Hitler through the Abwehr-based conspiracy; arrested April 1943; executed at Flossenbürg concentration camp 9 April 1945, three weeks before the end of the war in Europe. His *Letters and Papers from Prison* contain the influential late thoughts on "religionless Christianity" that shaped the 1960s "death of God" theology.

## Role in the tradition
Among the most-cited 20th-century Christian theologians; a martyr-figure across confessional lines.
