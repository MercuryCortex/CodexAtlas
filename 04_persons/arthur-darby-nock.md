# Arthur Darby Nock

## Identity
British-American classical philologist and historian of religion; Frothingham Professor of the History of Religion at Harvard University from 1930 until his death in 1963. With André-Jean Festugière, established the standard critical edition of the Hermetica (Budé, 4 vols., 1945–1954). Author of *Conversion* (1933), the foundational scholarly monograph on Hellenistic-to-late-antique religious conversion.

## Historical evidence
Documented academic career; Harvard archive; voluminous publications. Cited 5+ times across the Codex Atlas Hermetic node-cluster as a co-editor of the Budé Hermetica.

## Works
- (with A.-J. Festugière) *Hermès Trismégiste: Corpus Hermeticum* (Budé, 4 vols., 1945–1954) — the critical Greek edition.
- *Conversion: The Old and the New in Religion from Alexander the Great to Augustine of Hippo* (OUP 1933).
- *Sallustius: Concerning the Gods and the Universe* (Cambridge UP 1926).
- *Essays on Religion and the Ancient World* (collected; ed. Zeph Stewart, Harvard UP 1972, 2 vols.).

## Role in the tradition
The Budé Hermetica is the *Greek text* on which all subsequent Hermetic translation has worked — Copenhaver 1992 acknowledges Nock's text as the basis for the English Cambridge UP edition. Nock's *Conversion* is the methodological framework still in use for understanding Hermetic / mystery-cult / Christian religious shifts in the Mediterranean.
