# Zhuangzi

Warring-States-period Daoist philosopher, traditional author of the *Zhuangzi* (the eponymous text whose Inner Chapters are widely accepted as Zhuangzi's own work, with the Outer + Mixed Chapters compiled by his school + later editors). One of the two foundational texts of philosophical Daoism alongside the *Daodejing*. Famous for the butterfly-dream parable, the cook Ding's ox-cutting, the perspectival skepticism of the *Qiwulun*, and the radical paradoxical-mystical use of language.
