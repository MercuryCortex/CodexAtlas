Muhammad ibn Ismaʿil al-Bukhari (810-870 CE), Sunni Persian hadith scholar from Bukhara. Compiler of *Sahih al-Bukhari* — the most canonical of the six Sunni hadith collections (*Kutub al-Sittah*), traditionally regarded as the most authentic book after the Qur'an in Sunni Islam. Methodologically foundational to Islamic hadith criticism through his *isnad*-rigor.
