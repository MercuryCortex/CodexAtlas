# Trajan

Roman emperor (r. 98–117 CE); maximum territorial extent of the Roman Empire; conqueror of Dacia + Parthia. Sponsored substantial Egyptian temple-construction including the famous Kiosk of Trajan at Philae (one of the most-photographed Roman-period structures in Egypt). Reigned during the transition period documented in Pliny's letters from Bithynia about early Christian assemblies.
