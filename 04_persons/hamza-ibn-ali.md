# Hamza ibn ʿAlī

**Hamza ibn ʿAlī ibn Aḥmad** (fl. 1017–1043 CE) — chief founder-theologian of the Druze movement in Fatimid Cairo — composed the majority of the [[phase-5-059-rasail-al-hikma|Rasāʾil al-Ḥikma]] (Epistles of Wisdom), the closed scriptural canon of the Druze. He organized the Druze cosmological hierarchy of five divine principles (*ḥudūd*), systematized the theology of al-Ḥākim's theophany, and formulated the doctrine of soul transmigration within the community. He is regarded by the Druze as the highest of the *ḥudūd* (the Universal Mind / *al-ʿAql al-kullī*). He reportedly disappeared around 1021 CE — the same year as al-Ḥākim — and is expected to return with al-Ḥākim at the end of time. See [[tradition-druze]].
