# Tertullian

First major Latin Christian theologian (~155–~240). Carthaginian; trained in Roman rhetoric and possibly law. He wrote some thirty-one extant works in Latin covering apologetics, theology, ethics, and anti-heretical polemic. Tertullian coined or fixed much of the Latin theological vocabulary still in use: *trinitas*, *persona*, *substantia*, *sacramentum*, *vetus testamentum* / *novum testamentum* (Old / New Testament as covenant). Late in his career he joined the Montanist New Prophecy movement, judged heretical by the catholic church, making Tertullian himself an ambiguous figure in subsequent orthodoxy — read selectively rather than canonically.
