# Helena Petrovna Blavatsky

## Identity
Russian-born co-founder (with Henry Steel Olcott and William Quan Judge) of the Theosophical Society (New York, 17 November 1875); claimed contact with hidden Tibetan / Indian "Masters" (Mahatmas); produced the two principal foundational works of Theosophy, *Isis Unveiled* (1877) and *The Secret Doctrine* (1888). Moved with Olcott to India (1879) and established the international Theosophical Society's permanent headquarters at Adyar, near Madras (1882).

## Works
See texts-authored. *Isis* and *Secret Doctrine* are the principal works.

## Role in the tradition
Founder of Theosophy, one of the most influential 19th-century esoteric movements; her work substantially originated the modern Western synthesis of Indian, Egyptian, Hermetic, Kabbalistic, and Gnostic materials into a "perennial-philosophy" framework, which became foundational for the broader New Age movement.

## Disputes
- **Plagiarism (Coleman 1893–1895)**: ~2,000 passages copied from contemporary works without attribution.
- **Mahatma reality (SPR Hodgson Report 1885)**: the SPR judged the Master-letter phenomena fraudulent; the 1986 Harrison reassessment challenged Hodgson's methods but did not establish Mahatma reality.
