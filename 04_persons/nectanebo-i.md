# Nectanebo I

First pharaoh of Egypt's 30th Dynasty (r. -380 to -362) — the last great native Egyptian dynasty before the Persian reconquest and the Macedonian conquest. Sponsor of substantial temple-construction including major work at Philae (initiating the Greco-Roman-period Isis-temple complex) + the kiosk at Karnak + the Iseum at Hermopolis.
