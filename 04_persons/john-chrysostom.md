# John Chrysostom

Bishop of Constantinople from 397 until his exile and death in 407. The most celebrated preacher of patristic Greek Christianity — "Chrysostom" (Golden-Mouthed) is a posthumous title. His homilies on the New Testament are among the principal sources for Antiochene literal-historical exegesis. His eight *Sermons Against the Jews* (386–387) attacking Christian participation in synagogue worship are now widely studied as foundational documents of Christian anti-Judaism — important historical sources whose theological violence is acknowledged in contemporary scholarship.
