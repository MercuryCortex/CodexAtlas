# Hōnen (1133–1212)

Japanese Buddhist monk and founder of Jōdo-shū, the first independent Pure Land school in Japan. His *Senchakushū* (Selection of the Nembutsu of the Original Vow, 1198) established exclusive nembutsu practice — the recitation of *Namu Amida Butsu* — as the sole sufficient practice for the Latter Day of the Dharma. Hōnen drew primarily on the [[phase-4-104-sukhavativyuha-larger]] and on the Chinese Pure Land master Shandao (613-681). His disciple [[shinran]] radicalized his teaching further toward a *tariki* (Other Power) soteriology structurally parallel to Lutheran *sola gratia*.
