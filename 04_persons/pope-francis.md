# Pope Francis

## Identity
Jorge Mario Bergoglio, S.J. (b. 17 December 1936, Buenos Aires; living as of vault cutoff May 2026). Argentine of Italian descent. First Jesuit pope, first pope from the Americas, first pope from the Southern Hemisphere. Ordained priest 1969; Provincial of the Argentine Jesuit Province 1973–1979 during the Argentine "Dirty War"; Archbishop of Buenos Aires 1998–2013; created cardinal 2001; elected Pope on 13 March 2013 following the resignation of Benedict XVI (Joseph Ratzinger).

## Works
See texts-authored. *Evangelii Gaudium* (2013) is his programmatic apostolic exhortation — Gospel-of-joy, missionary-discipleship Catholicism, critical of "trickle-down economics" and clerical careerism. *Laudato Si'* (2015) is the first papal encyclical centered on ecological crisis and climate change, framing care for "our common home" as a moral and religious duty; it had wide secular as well as Catholic reception. *Fratelli Tutti* (2020) extends a comparable framework to social and political fraternity. *Traditionis Custodes* (2021) substantially restricted the use of the pre-Vatican-II Tridentine ("Latin") Mass, reversing Benedict XVI's 2007 *Summorum Pontificum* expansion — the principal recent episode in the Vatican-II-traditionalist conflict.

## Role in the tradition
A pivotal figure in the long [[event-vatican-ii-aftermath-1965-present]]. Doctrinally substantively continuous with John Paul II / Benedict XVI, but with marked changes in tone, pastoral emphasis, and ecclesial style: greater openness to divorced-and-remarried Catholics (*Amoris Laetitia* 2016 ch. 8), to LGBTQ pastoral inclusion (without doctrinal change), to inter-religious dialogue (the Abu Dhabi 2019 Document on Human Fraternity with Sunni Grand Imam Ahmed al-Tayeb), and stronger institutional reform impulses (curial reorganization *Praedicate Evangelium* 2022; synodality and the Synod on Synodality 2021–2024). Strongly opposed by a vocal traditionalist Catholic minority (Cardinals Burke, Sarah, etc.).

## Connections
- Vatican II legacy: continues the Paul-VI / John-XXIII-line of [[event-second-vatican-council-1962-1965]] implementation, in contestation with the Lefebvrist (SSPX) and broader traditionalist resistance.
- Jesuit theology: drawn from Ignatian discernment, the Latin American *teología del pueblo* (Methol Ferré, Scannone), and elements of liberation theology — though Francis is not a doctrinaire liberation theologian.
- Climate ethics: *Laudato Si'* drew on Patriarch Bartholomew I (Ecumenical Patriarchate's environmental theology) and earlier Catholic social teaching ([[phase-5-024-aquinas-summa-theologiae]] natural-law tradition); has been deeply influential on Christian environmental discourse.

## Disputes
- **Tridentine Mass restriction** (*Traditionis Custodes* 2021). Strongly opposed by traditionalist Catholics; ongoing intra-Catholic conflict.
- **Doctrinal vs. pastoral footnotes** (*Amoris Laetitia* 2016 ch. 8). Four cardinals (Burke, Brandmüller, Caffarra, Meisner) issued the 2016 *dubia* requesting clarification on the divorced-and-remarried discipline. Francis declined to answer.
- **China agreement** (provisional 2018, renewed 2020, 2022, 2024). Concerning the appointment of bishops in the PRC. Substantially contested; Cardinal Zen has been the most prominent Catholic critic.
