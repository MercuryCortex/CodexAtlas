Aksumite-installed Christian king of Himyar (r. c. 530-c. 553-570). Mounted the *ʿām al-fīl* (Year of the Elephant) campaign against Mecca, traditionally placed at the year of Muhammad's birth (c. 570). Surah 105 (al-Fīl) commemorates the campaign's failure.
