# Phạm Công Tắc

**Phạm Công Tắc** (1890–1959) — titled *Hộ Pháp* ("Protector of the Dharma") — was the primary medium and principal architect of [[tradition-cao-dai|Cao Dai]] as an institutional religion. The Cao Dai canonical scriptures ([[phase-7-044-cao-dai-canon]]) were received primarily through his *cơ bút* (spirit-writing planchette) sessions. He designed Cao Dai's elaborate hierarchical structure modeled partly on the Catholic Church (a Pope, cardinals, bishops, priests), constructed the Tây Ninh Cathedral (Holy See), and led the movement through the politically turbulent 1930s–1950s, during which Cao Dai armed forces at various points aligned with France, Japan, the Viet Minh, and the Republic of Vietnam.
