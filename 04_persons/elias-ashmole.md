# Elias Ashmole

**Elias Ashmole** (1617–1692) — astrologer, antiquarian, Windsor Herald, and one of the first documented speculative Freemasons in England (initiated at Warrington Lodge, 16 October 1646) — compiled the *Theatrum Chemicum Britannicum* (1652), the canonical anthology of English alchemical poetry. His collection of manuscripts, natural history specimens, and curiosities became the founding bequest of the Ashmolean Museum (Oxford, 1683). He sits at the intersection of English alchemy, Rosicrucianism, and speculative Freemasonry. See [[phase-6-047-ashmole-theatrum-chemicum-britannicum]].
