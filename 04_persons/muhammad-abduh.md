# Muhammad Abduh

## Identity
Egyptian jurist and reformer (1849–1905); pupil and collaborator of Jamal al-Din al-Afghani; Grand Mufti of Egypt from 1899; principal architect of late-19th-century Islamic modernism. Argued for *ijtihad* (independent juristic reasoning) as against blind *taqlid* (imitation); for the compatibility of Islam with modern science and constitutional government; for educational reform.

## Role in the tradition
Foundational figure of Islamic modernism; influence runs through Rashid Rida (his collaborator) and through Rida (more conservatively-mutated) toward both the Muslim Brotherhood (al-Banna 1928) and the Salafi reform movement.
