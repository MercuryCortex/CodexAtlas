# Theodosius I

Roman emperor 379–395; the last to rule the unified empire. Issued the Edict of Thessalonica (380) making Nicene Christianity the official state religion. Convened the First Council of Constantinople (381). Closed pagan temples and banned sacrifices (391–392 anti-pagan laws). Closed the Olympic Games as a pagan festival (393). His reign marks the legal end of Roman paganism as a recognized public religion.
