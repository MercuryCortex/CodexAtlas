# Karen Armstrong

## Identity
British author and former Roman Catholic nun (b. 1944); among the most widely-read living popularizers of comparative religion in the English-speaking world. Founder of the Charter for Compassion (2009).

## Works
*A History of God* (1993) is her best-known work — a popular history of monotheistic theology from the Patriarchs through the 20th century. *The Great Transformation* (2006) popularizes Jaspers's "Axial Age" thesis.

## Role in the tradition
A popular-scholarly bridge figure between academic religious studies and mass readership; not a research-academic in the disciplinary sense, but her synthesis-and-popularization work has reached audiences inaccessible to specialist scholars.
