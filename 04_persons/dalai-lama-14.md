# Tenzin Gyatso, the 14th Dalai Lama

## Identity
Tibetan Buddhist religious leader (b. 1935); enthroned as 14th Dalai Lama 1940; fled to India after the Chinese suppression of the 1959 Lhasa uprising; head of the Central Tibetan Administration in exile (Dharamsala) until his 2011 devolution of political authority to elected officials. Nobel Peace Prize 1989. The most globally visible Buddhist leader of the late 20th and early 21st century.

## Role in the tradition
Spiritual head of the Gelug school of Tibetan Buddhism; *symbolically* the religious head of the Tibetan people across schools (Nyingma, Sakya, Kagyu, Gelug); the principal global vector of Tibetan Buddhism in the contemporary world.

## Recent developments (vault cutoff May 2026)
- Turned 90 on 6 July 2025; the 90th-birthday year was framed by his office as a major occasion for setting out the *succession* framework.
- On 2 July 2025 (just before the 90th birthday), the Dalai Lama issued a statement affirming that the institution of the Dalai Lama will continue after him, that the search for the 15th Dalai Lama is the sole responsibility of the Gaden Phodrang Trust / Dalai Lama Trust, and that no party — explicitly including the People's Republic of China — has authority in that succession. This directly counters Beijing's claimed authority over Tibetan Buddhist reincarnations under the 2007 State Administration for Religious Affairs Order No. 5.
- The succession question is therefore likely to produce *competing claims* (a Trust-recognized successor outside China, a PRC-recognized successor inside China) — a partial parallel to the still-disputed Panchen Lama succession after the 1995 PRC abduction of Gedhun Choekyi Nyima.
