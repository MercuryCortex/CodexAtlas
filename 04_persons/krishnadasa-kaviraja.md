# Krishnadasa Kaviraja

## Identity
Bengali Vaishnava theologian-hagiographer (c. 1530–c. 1615), author of the canonical *Chaitanya Charitamrita*. Born in Bengal; moved to Vrindavan, the principal Gaudiya Vaishnava center; studied under the Six Goswamis (especially Raghunatha Dasa and Rupa). Composed the *Chaitanya Charitamrita* in his eighties at the urging of the Vrindavan community, who needed a comprehensive theological-hagiographical Chaitanya-biography to consolidate the tradition. The work was completed c. 1612–1615 and became the canonical Gaudiya text. Also composed *Govinda Lilamrita* (a Sanskrit work on the eternal *lila* / sport of Krishna) and commentaries on Goswami works.

## Works
*Chaitanya Charitamrita*. *Govinda Lilamrita*. Sanskrit commentaries.

## Role in the tradition
The canonical hagiographer-theologian of Gaudiya Vaishnavism. The *Chaitanya Charitamrita* fixes the theological and biographical framework of the tradition; ISKCON's modern global Gaudiya Vaishnavism is unimaginable without it.
