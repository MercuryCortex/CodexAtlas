# Shenhui (~670–762 CE)

Tang-dynasty Chinese Chan Buddhist master; pivotal polemicist who established the Southern School (associated with [[huineng]]) as the orthodox Chan transmission line over the Northern School (associated with [[shenxiu]]). Shenhui's polemical campaign at the 730 Dafayuan Temple debate in Hua-tai is the principal historical event that crystallized the Chan-Zen tradition's self-understanding for the next 1,200 years.

## The Sudden-vs-Gradual debate

Shenhui's central polemical claim: the authentic Chan teaching is *sudden enlightenment* (頓悟, *dunwu*) — recognition of one's intrinsic Buddha-nature occurring in a single moment — and this is the teaching of the Sixth Patriarch [[huineng]] (~638-713) at Caoxi. The rival Northern School (under [[shenxiu]], ~605-706) teaches *gradual enlightenment* (漸修, *jianxiu*) — progressive cultivation through meditation practice. Shenhui's polemic frames Northern-gradualism as inferior + heretical.

McRae 2003 documents that the historical Huineng-Shenxiu opposition was substantially constructed by Shenhui's polemic itself — the historical reality was more complex than the binary the polemic established. But the polemic *succeeded*: by ~800 CE the Southern School / sudden-enlightenment framework was the orthodox Chan tradition; the Northern School disappeared.

## The Platform Sutra

The [[phase-5-004-platform-sutra-huineng|Platform Sutra]] (the principal Chan Buddhist scripture, c. 780 CE) reflects the Southern School / Shenhui polemic framework. The Dunhuang manuscript versions of the Platform Sutra recovered in the 1900 Library Cave 17 discovery (see [[event-nag-hammadi-discovery]] for the structural-parallel evidentiary watershed in Gnostic studies) include earlier strata partly attributable to Shenhui's lineage.

## Cross-tradition position

Shenhui's polemical-constructive establishment of the Chan tradition parallels several cross-tradition phenomena:
- The Justin-Martyr-style polemical-constructive establishment of patristic Christianity's relationship to its pagan-philosophical predecessors
- The Athanasian polemical-constructive establishment of Nicene Christology over Arianism
- The Massignon-style polemical-constructive establishment of Sufi orthodoxy around Hallaj

In each case: an ambiguous historical situation gets crystallized into a stable orthodoxy through the polemical campaigning of one decisive figure. The Codex Atlas catalogs Shenhui as the Chan instance of the *orthodoxy-as-polemical-construction* pattern.
