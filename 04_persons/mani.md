# Mani

## Biography

**Mani** (216–274/277 CE) was a prophet, artist, and founder of Manichaeism — possibly the most deliberately syncretistic religious founder in documented history. He was born on 14 Nisan 527 of the Babylonian era (= April 216 CE by modern reckoning) near **Seleucia-Ctesiphon**, the Sasanian imperial capital in Mesopotamia (modern Iraq). His father, Pattikios (Patteg), was a Parthian nobleman who had joined the **Elkasaites** — a Jewish-Christian baptismal community that practiced ritual immersion, vegetarianism, and angelology. Mani grew up in the Elkasaite community, learning their practices of purification.

### The Cologne Mani Codex and the Twin

The primary source for Mani's early life is the **Cologne Mani Codex** (*CMC*) — the smallest Greek manuscript in existence (38mm × 45mm), discovered 1969, dated 4th–5th century CE. See [[phase-4-014-cologne-mani-codex]] for full details. The *CMC* preserves Mani's own autobiography (Syriac original, Greek translation) and records three pivotal moments:

1. **Age 12** (~228 CE): Mani receives a first revelation from his *al-Tawm* (Aramaic: "the Twin" — his heavenly divine double / *syzygos*), who commends him but says the time to preach has not come.
2. **Age 24** (~240 CE): The Twin appears again and commands Mani to leave the Elkasaites and proclaim his own religion.
3. **The Break with the Elkasaites**: Mani confronts the Elkasaite elders, demonstrating that their ritual baptisms are inadequate — true purification is cognitive (gnosis), not ritual.

The heavenly Twin (*al-Tawm*) is Mani's divine alter-ego — not a separate external angel but the transcendent, perfect counterpart of Mani's earthly soul. This parallels: the Gnostic *pneuma* as divine spark separated from its heavenly origin; the Sufi concept of the pre-eternal *rūh*; Paul's "I, yet not I" (Galatians 2:20).

### Missionary Career, Royal Patronage, and the Arzhang

After the commissioning vision (~240 CE), Mani:
- Traveled to northwest India (the Kushan Empire), bringing his religion into contact with Buddhist communities
- Obtained audience with Sasanian king **Shapur I** (~242 CE), to whom he dedicated his only Persian-language scripture, the *Šābuhragān* (see [[phase-4-016-shabuhragan]]). Shapur gave Mani freedom to preach throughout the Sasanian Empire.

**The Arzhang (Illustrated Scripture)**: Mani was the **first prophet to personally author his own canonical scriptures** — writing in seven languages. He also created the *Arzhang* — visual paintings of the cosmic drama — making Manichaeism the **first documented religion to use religious art as a systematic missionary tool**. The Arzhang survives only in descriptions and in Turfan/Dunhuang manuscript art.

### Execution and the Martyrdom Pattern

Mani died (~274 CE) under **Bahram I** at the instigation of the Zoroastrian high priest **Kartir**, who perceived Mani's syncretism as a threat to Zoroastrian orthodoxy. After twenty-six days of imprisonment, Mani died; his skin was reportedly stuffed and hung at the Gundeshapur gate.

**MASSIVE WIN — martyrdom parallel**: The pattern of the mystic-prophet killed by the religious establishment recurs:
- Jesus crucified at the instigation of the Temple priesthood (Caiaphas) + Roman authority
- Al-Hallaj hanged (922 CE) by the Abbasid Caliph at the urging of orthodox jurists
- Mani killed by the Zoroastrian priesthood under Sasanian authority
- Giordano Bruno burned by the Roman Inquisition (1600)

In each case: the mystic claims direct unmediated divine revelation, threatening the institutional intermediary; the institution uses political power to eliminate the threat; the death becomes the seed of the tradition's spread. See [[martyrdom-theology]].

## Mani's Explicit Syncretism

Mani is unique in religious history for **articulate, deliberate, systematic syncretism**:

- Zarathustra received a genuine but geographically limited revelation for Iranian peoples; the Buddha received one for India; Jesus for the West. Each was real but partial.
- Mani received the **complete and universal revelation** — for all humanity, transcending all cultural and geographic boundaries.
- He was the **Paraclete** (*Holy Spirit / Comforter*) promised by Jesus in John 14:16.
- He was the **Seal of the Prophets** in his own system — the final prophet whose revelation completes all previous ones. (Islam later independently applies the same title to Muhammad.)
- He was the **first prophet to personally author his own scriptures**, ensuring transmission fidelity.

This meta-religious claim — "I know the other religions, I use them, I improve on them, I complete them" — is without parallel in religious history until modern reformers.
