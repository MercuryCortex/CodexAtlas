# Michael Sendivogius

**Michał Sędziwój** (c.1566–1636) — Polish nobleman and diplomat who moved in the Rudolf II Prague court and published the *Novum Lumen Chymicum* (1604/5) under his latinized name. His concept of *aerial niter* — the life-giving principle in air — ran through Boyle and Newton and became a conceptual precursor to the discovery of oxygen. See [[phase-6-045-sendivogius-novum-lumen-chymicum]].
