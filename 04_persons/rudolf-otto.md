# Rudolf Otto

## Identity
German Protestant theologian and phenomenologist of religion (1869–1937), professor at Marburg from 1917. His *Das Heilige* (1917, "The Idea of the Holy") is the founding text of the phenomenological-experientialist approach to comparative religion. Travelled in India 1911–1912 and developed an explicit comparative-mysticism program (*Mysticism East and West*, 1926, comparing Sankara and Meister Eckhart).

## Historical evidence
Marburg university records; his correspondence; Almond's 1984 standard monograph.

## Works
See texts-authored. *Das Heilige* is the central work; *Mysticism East and West* is the major comparative-mysticism work.

## Role in the tradition
Otto's coinage *numinous* and the formula *mysterium tremendum et fascinans* entered the comparative-religion lexicon permanently and through Eliade's adoption became standard religious-studies vocabulary. Eliade, Jung, and C.S. Lewis all explicitly use his framework.
