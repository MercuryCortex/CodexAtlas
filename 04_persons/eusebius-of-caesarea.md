# Eusebius of Caesarea

Bishop of Caesarea Maritima from ~313 until his death ~340. Disciple of Pamphilus (who curated Origen's library in Caesarea) and the great inheritor of Origen's library and exegetical legacy. Attended the Council of Nicaea (325) and wrote the *Life of Constantine* — a major primary source for the Constantinian turn despite its panegyrical character. His ten-book *Ecclesiastical History* invents the genre of Christian historiography and preserves substantial quotations from otherwise-lost early Christian writers (Papias, Hegesippus, Julius Africanus).
