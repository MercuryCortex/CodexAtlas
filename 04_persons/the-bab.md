# The Báb

## Identity
Iranian merchant of Shiraz (1819–1850) who declared on 22 May 1844 that he was the *Báb* ("Gate") foretold in Shi'a Twelver eschatology — and eventually that he was himself the return of the Hidden Imam Mahdi and a Manifestation of God in his own right. His radical re-reading of Quranic and Shi'a categories provoked violent state suppression; the Bábí community was crushed in a series of military engagements (Shaykh Tabarsi 1848–1849; Zanjan, Nayriz). The Báb was executed by firing squad in Tabriz, 9 July 1850.

## Role in the tradition
The precursor Manifestation in Bahá'í theology; founder of the Bábí movement, whose successor is the Bahá'í Faith under Bahá'u'lláh. The Báb's shrine on Mount Carmel (Haifa) is the second-holiest Bahá'í site after Bahji.
