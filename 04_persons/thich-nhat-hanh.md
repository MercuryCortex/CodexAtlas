# Thich Nhat Hanh

## Identity
Vietnamese Zen monk (1926–2022); founder of the Order of Interbeing (1966) and the Plum Village Mahayana monastic community in southwestern France (1982). Coined the term *engaged Buddhism* (~1963) for the application of Buddhist practice to social-political action. Nominated for the Nobel Peace Prize by Martin Luther King Jr. (1967). Exiled from Vietnam 1966–2018; allowed to return to live out his final years in Tu Hieu Pagoda, Hue.

## Role in the tradition
The principal late-20th-century popularizer of Buddhist mindfulness practice to Western audiences; among the most-read living Buddhist authors during his lifetime.
