# Seela (Meqabyan II)

## Identity
**Seela** is the eponymous figure of **Meqabyan II**, the second of the three Ge'ez Books of Meqabyan ([[phase-5-040-meqabyan-ethiopian-maccabees]]) uniquely canonical in [[tradition-ethiopian-orthodox-tewahedo]]. He is paired with [[abya-meqabyan]] (Meqabyan I) and [[fentos-meqabyan]] (Meqabyan III) as the three figures around whom the Ge'ez Meqabyan corpus crystallizes — none of them historical, none of them connected to the Jewish-Hellenistic-period Maccabean revolt despite the shared canonical-name.

## Historical evidence
Legendary. The Ge'ez Meqabyan books are textually under-studied; the precise narrative arc and theological-pastoral functions of Seela within the three-book corpus depend on the specific recension. Like Abya and Fentos, Seela is a pseudonymous-literary figure rather than a historical individual.

## Works
None.

## Role in the tradition
Seela functions as a **martyr-and-witness exemplar** in the Ethiopian canonical-scriptural frame, occupying the canonical-position that the LXX Maccabean martyrs occupy elsewhere — but distinctively, with Ethiopian-specific narrative content. The three-Meqabyan-figures structure (Abya, Seela, Fentos) parallels broader Ethiopian hagiographic-triadic patterns; the precise theological-narrative arc connecting them is recension-specific.

## Disputes
See [[abya-meqabyan]] and [[fentos-meqabyan]] for the same set of textual-historical questions: date, source-history, whether the three figures derive from a common substrate or develop independently, whether any historical kernel underlies them.
