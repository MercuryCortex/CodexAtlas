# Yongming Yanshou (永明延壽)

Tang-Song Chan master (904–975) who presided at Yongming Monastery in Hangzhou and became the foremost architect of the Chan + Pure Land synthesis that would define Chinese Buddhism for centuries. His *Zongjing lu* (Record of the Source-Mirror, 100 fascicles) attempts a comprehensive synthesis of all Buddhist schools; his collected writings show extensive engagement with Confucian ethics and Daoist cosmology. One of the clearest historical examples of a single figure embodying the [[tradition-sanjiao]] synthesis from the Buddhist direction.
