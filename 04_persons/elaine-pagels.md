# Elaine Pagels

## Identity
American historian of religion (b. 1943); Harvard PhD 1970 under Krister Stendahl and Helmut Koester; Barnard College 1970–1982; Harrington Spear Paine Foundation Professor of Religion, Princeton University, from 1982. Member of the Robinson Nag Hammadi translation team. Her trade-press books — *The Gnostic Gospels* (1979), *Adam, Eve, and the Serpent* (1988), *The Origin of Satan* (1995), *Beyond Belief* (2003), *Revelations* (2012) — have made her the most-read scholar of late-antique Christianity of the late 20th and early 21st century.

## Works
See texts-authored.

## Role in the tradition
The principal vector by which the Nag Hammadi codices entered mainstream Western public awareness. *The Gnostic Gospels* (1979) sold ~400,000 copies in the US within a decade. Her larger scholarly work on Valentinian Paul-exegesis (*The Gnostic Paul*, 1975) and on the construction of orthodoxy through exclusion of difference is foundational to one wing of late-antique Christianity studies.
