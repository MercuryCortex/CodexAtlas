# Louis Komjathy

## Identity
American scholar of Daoism (b. 1971); co-founder of the Contemplative Studies Group (American Academy of Religion). PhD Boston University 2005 on early Quanzhen (Complete Perfection) Daoism. Held positions at University of San Diego and has been an independent scholar / public-academic Daoist practitioner.

## Role in the field
A principal active English-language scholar of Daoism focused on the somatic, contemplative, and lineage dimensions of the tradition. His *Daoist Tradition* (2013) is a major textbook synthesis distinguishing classical Daoist literature ([[phase-2-014-daodejing]]; Zhuangzi), organized Daoist religion (Tianshi / Celestial Masters, Shangqing, Lingbao, Quanzhen), and modern adaptations. His work is unusually attentive to the *American Western adaptation / commodification* of Daoism — what he calls "Popular Western Taoism" — separating it analytically from the historical Chinese tradition.

## Connections
- Engages [[tradition-daoism]] both as historical-Chinese phenomenon and as modern-Western reception (the "wellness," "Tao of...", and self-help genre that uses Daoist imagery without substantive contact with Chinese Daoist communities or texts).
- Bridges Daoist studies and the newer "contemplative studies" interdisciplinary field.
