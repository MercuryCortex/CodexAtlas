# Marija Gimbutas

Lithuanian-American archaeologist (1921–1994); author of *The Language of the Goddess* (1989); originator of the (substantially contested) Neolithic Mother-Goddess hypothesis applied to [[catalhoyuk-sacred-site|Çatalhöyük]] and other sites.
