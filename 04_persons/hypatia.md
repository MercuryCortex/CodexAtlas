# Hypatia

Alexandrian Neoplatonist philosopher and mathematician, daughter of the mathematician Theon. Taught at the Mouseion in late-fourth and early-fifth-century Alexandria; her circle of students included Synesius of Cyrene (who became a Christian bishop and continued to address her as his teacher). In 415 she was murdered by a Christian mob during civic conflict between the Christian patriarch Cyril and the pagan prefect Orestes — the event marks a symbolic break-point in the relationship of late-antique philosophy with the now-state-Christian Roman Empire. Her substantive philosophical and mathematical work survives only in fragments and via her students' references.
