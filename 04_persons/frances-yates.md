# Frances Amelia Yates

## Identity
British historian of Renaissance ideas (1899–1981); long associated with the Warburg Institute, London (Reader in the History of the Renaissance from 1956). Her *Giordano Bruno and the Hermetic Tradition* (1964) and subsequent works substantially established Renaissance Hermeticism as a serious academic subject and the broader field of Western esotericism studies as an academic discipline.

## Works
See texts-authored. *Giordano Bruno* (1964) and *The Art of Memory* (1966) are the most-cited.

## Role in the tradition
A central figure in the 20th-century academic recovery of pre-modern Western esoteric traditions. Together with Hans Jonas (Gnosticism), Gershom Scholem (Kabbalah), and (later) Elaine Pagels (Nag Hammadi popular reception), Yates is in the small group of 20th-century scholars whose work made esoteric currents legible as continuous serious history.

## Disputes
- **The "Yates Thesis"** — Hermeticism's role in the Scientific Revolution. Strong form rejected; moderate form widely accepted.
