# Judah ha-Nasi

Patriarch (*nasi*) of the Jewish community in Roman Palestine from ~165 until his death ~217. Known simply as "Rabbi" in the Talmud — his stature is such that he requires no further title. Final redactor of the Mishnah ~200 CE. Reportedly maintained cordial relations with the Roman emperor Antoninus (traditions associate him with Marcus Aurelius or Caracalla; historical identification uncertain) and oversaw the codification of oral Torah at a moment when the dispersed rabbinic schools needed an authoritative compilation. His seats were Sepphoris (later) and Beit She'arim (where he was buried).
