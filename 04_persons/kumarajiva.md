# Kumārajīva

Late-4th / early-5th c. Kuchean-Indian Buddhist translator, brought to Chang'an in 401 CE under the Later Qin. The single most important translator of Buddhist Sanskrit texts into Classical Chinese — his renderings of the Lotus Sutra, the Diamond Sutra, the Vimalakīrti Sutra, the Mūlamadhyamakakārikā, and dozens of other works established the canonical Chinese Buddhist vocabulary and remain the standard liturgical text-form in East Asian Buddhism to the present.
