# Henry Suso

## Identity
14th-c. German Dominican friar, mystic, and theologian (1295–1366). **Pupil of [[meister-eckhart]]** at the Cologne *studium generale* (~1322–1324), and the **principal defender of Eckhartian positions after the 1329 *In Agro Dominico* condemnation** of 28 Eckhart-attributed propositions. Suso's *Little Book of Truth* (~1328, before the condemnation) gives a Dominican-orthodox interpretation of Eckhart that became one of the principal vehicles by which Eckhart's thought was rescued from heretical association. Suso also authored the *Horologium Sapientiae* (~1334), the most-copied Latin mystical work of the later Middle Ages (~500 surviving manuscripts), translated into every major European vernacular.

## Works
- ***Büchlein der Wahrheit*** (*Little Book of Truth*) — vernacular German defense of Eckhartian metaphysics, ~1328.
- ***Büchlein der Ewigen Weisheit*** + ***Horologium Sapientiae*** — devotional treatises featuring personified **Eternal Wisdom** (*Ewige Weisheit* / *Sapientia Aeterna*) as Christ-Bridegroom-Sophia figure addressed by the Servitor (Suso).
- ***Vita*** — strictly a *third-person* autobiography composed with Elsbeth Stagel; a unique medieval Latin-German hagiographic-autobiographical genre.

## Role in the tradition
- **Eckhart-rescuer.** Without Suso (and Tauler) the post-condemnation Eckhartian tradition would have collapsed.
- **Eternal-Wisdom devotional literature.** The *Horologium*'s personified Wisdom is a major late-medieval iconographic and theological figure.
- **Direction of women.** Suso's long spiritual-direction correspondence with Elsbeth Stagel (Töss Dominican convent) is one of the major recorded male-mystic / female-Beguine-or-Dominican-tertiary direction relationships of the 14th c.
