# Aquila

## Identity
A Jewish Christian originally from Pontus (per Acts 18:2), tentmaker by trade, husband and missionary partner of [[priscilla]]. The couple's joint biography is treated more fully under Priscilla's entry; the four-of-six Pauline name-orderings putting Priscilla first suggest Aquila was the junior partner in their teaching ministry, though this is a tendential reading.

## Historical evidence
Same as for Priscilla — Acts and Pauline letters independently attest. Suetonius's *Claudius* 25.4 (the famous "Chrestus" passage) anchors the Roman expulsion to history.

## Works
None.

## Role in the tradition
Subordinated in Christian memory to his wife — an unusual pattern for the period. Distinguish him from **Aquila of Sinope** (~+130, the Jewish proselyte who produced the literal Greek Bible translation patronized by Rabbi Akiva — a different person entirely, post-Christian-NT).

## Disputes
- Distinguishing him from the later Aquila of Sinope (different person, different period).
