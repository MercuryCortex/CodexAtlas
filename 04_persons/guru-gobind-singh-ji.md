# Guru Gobind Singh

**Guru Gobind Singh Ji** (1666–1708) was the tenth and final human Guru of the Sikhs, son of Guru Tegh Bahadur (the ninth Guru executed by [[aurangzeb-emperor]] in 1675). In response to his father's martyrdom, Guru Gobind Singh founded the **Khalsa** (ਖ਼ਾਲਸਾ, "the Pure") on Vaisakhi 1699 — transforming the Sikh community from a devotional tradition into a community of initiated warrior-saints bound by five physical markers (*panj kakars*: uncut hair, comb, steel bracelet, cotton undergarment, steel sword). Before his own death in 1708, he declared that the lineage of human Guruship was complete and that henceforth the living Guru of the Sikhs would be the [[phase-6-016-guru-granth-sahib|Guru Granth Sahib]] — the scripture itself elevated to the status of an eternal, living Guru. This is one of the most distinctive moves in the history of world religion: the replacement of a human prophetic lineage with a canonized text as the living spiritual authority.
