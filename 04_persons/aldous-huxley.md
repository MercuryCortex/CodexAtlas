# Aldous Huxley

Stub — upgrade needed. Key figure in the transmission from William James's *Varieties of Religious Experience* to the modern perennial philosophy tradition and psychedelic research. *The Perennial Philosophy* (1945) synthesizes Christian, Hindu, Buddhist, and Sufi mystical texts arguing for a single universal mystical core; *The Doors of Perception* (1954) connects this to mescaline experience, inaugurating the modern psychedelic-as-mystical-state research program. See [[william-james]], [[perennial-philosophy]], [[swami-vivekananda]].
