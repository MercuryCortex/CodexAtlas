# George Ivanovich Gurdjieff

## Identity
Armenian-Greek esoteric teacher (1866–1949) of contested early biography; emerged into documentation in Moscow ~1912. Taught a system he called "The Work" or "the Fourth Way" — a synthesis of esoteric Sufi, Christian-mystical, and Eastern teachings claiming origin in hidden monasteries of Central Asia. Established the Institute for the Harmonious Development of Man in Tiflis, then Constantinople (1920), then Fontainebleau-Avon (Prieuré, 1922). Taught the doctrine of "sleeping humanity" — most humans are mechanical and unconscious; "the Work" awakens.

## Works
The posthumous "All and Everything" trilogy is his principal written legacy.

## Role in the tradition
Founder of the Fourth Way / Gurdjieffian tradition; substantial influence through P.D. Ouspensky's systematic *In Search of the Miraculous* (1949) and through later teachers (Bennett, de Salzmann, Nicoll).
