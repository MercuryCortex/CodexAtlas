# Thutmose IV

**Thutmose IV** (~1401–1391 BCE) was the eighth pharaoh of Egypt's 18th Dynasty. He is notable in the context of the **[[sphinx-of-giza|Great Sphinx]]** for the **Dream Stele** — a granite stele still standing between the Sphinx's forepaws — which records a dream Thutmose had while resting in the Sphinx's shadow as a prince. The Sphinx appeared to him and promised him the kingship if he would clear the sand that had buried its body. Thutmose became pharaoh and ordered the clearing.

The Dream Stele is the oldest documented account of the Sphinx's condition and contains a partially legible cartouche in the 13th line that some Egyptologists read as "Khafre" — the basis for part of the conventional Sphinx attribution. The line is damaged and the reading is disputed.
