# Rudolf Steiner

## Identity
Austrian philosopher, esotericist, and educational reformer (1861–1925); edited Goethe's scientific works (Kürschner edition, 1883–1897); doctorate in philosophy, Rostock 1891. Joined the Theosophical Society 1902 as General Secretary of the German Section; broke 1913 to found the Anthroposophical Society. Founder of Waldorf education (first school Stuttgart 1919), biodynamic agriculture (1924 Koberwitz lectures), Anthroposophical medicine, and eurythmy.

## Works
See texts-authored. The *Outline of Esoteric Science* (1910) is the principal cosmological work; the lecture corpus is vast.

## Role in the tradition
Founder of Anthroposophy. Substantial downstream applications (Waldorf, biodynamic agriculture, anthroposophical medicine) continue. Documented mixed-political-record (some Anthroposophist Nazis, some Anthroposophist resisters; Anthroposophy was banned by the Nazi regime 1935).
