# Paramahansa Yogananda

## Identity
Bengali yogi (1893–1952); founder of the Self-Realization Fellowship (1920, US) and Yogoda Satsanga Society (India). Sailed to the US in 1920 to attend the Boston International Congress of Religious Liberals and remained for 30 years, propagating Kriya Yoga (a specific pranayama-meditation technique transmitted in his lineage: Babaji → Lahiri Mahasaya → Sri Yukteswar → Yogananda) to Western audiences.

## Role in the tradition
A principal popularizer of Hindu spirituality in mid-20th-century North America; the *Autobiography of a Yogi* (1946) is among the most-read 20th-century works in the genre.
