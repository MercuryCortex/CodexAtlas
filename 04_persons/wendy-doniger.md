# Wendy Doniger

## Identity
American Sanskritist and historian of religions (b. 1940); Mircea Eliade Distinguished Service Professor of the History of Religions at the University of Chicago Divinity School (Eliade's successor chair) from 1986 until retirement. Two Harvard PhDs (Sanskrit / Indology). The most prominent American Indologist of her generation.

## Works
See texts-authored.

## Role in the tradition
Doniger is in the lineage of the Chicago History of Religions program (Eliade, Kitagawa, Long, Doniger). Her *Hindus: An Alternative History* (2009) was withdrawn from sale by Penguin India in 2014 following a lawsuit by Dinanath Batra (a Hindutva-affiliated activist) under India's blasphemy-adjacent legal provisions; the book remains in print outside India. The withdrawal is a significant 21st-century academic-freedom case.
