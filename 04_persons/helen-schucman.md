# Helen Schucman

## Identity
American research psychologist (1909–1981); doctorate from New York University (1957); Associate Professor of Medical Psychology at Columbia University's College of Physicians and Surgeons. From 1965 through 1972 she reported receiving inner dictation from a voice she identified as Jesus, transcribed (with William Thetford's encouragement and collaboration) as what became *A Course in Miracles*. Schucman herself remained a non-practicing-Jewish-background skeptic toward the work's religious claims throughout her life.
