# Candrakīrti

7th-c. Indian Buddhist philosopher, the definitive exponent of the Prāsaṅgika interpretation of [[nagarjuna]]'s Madhyamaka. His *Prasannapadā* (Clear Words) is the only surviving Sanskrit commentary on the *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā*. His *Madhyamakāvatāra* introduces compassion as the philosophical starting point for Madhyamaka. Against Bhāvaviveka's Svātantrika: Candrakīrti holds that even formal inferential arguments are forbidden in the ultimate analysis — only *prasaṅga* (reductio ad absurdum) is permissible because any formal syllogism implicitly grants its terms inherent existence. His interpretation became the definitive Gelugpa standard (via Tsongkhapa) and continues to influence Tibetan Buddhism.
