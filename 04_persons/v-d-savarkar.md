# Vinayak Damodar Savarkar

## Identity
Indian nationalist ideologue (1883–1966); imprisoned 1911–1921 in the Cellular Jail at Port Blair; interned at Ratnagiri 1921–1937; founder of *Hindutva* as a political-cultural ideology distinct from Hindu religion narrowly. President of the Hindu Mahasabha (1937–1943). Tried as co-conspirator in the Gandhi assassination (1948); acquitted; the Kapur Commission (1969) found stronger evidence of his involvement but did not reverse the acquittal.

## Role in the tradition
Founder of Hindutva ideology; the principal intellectual ancestor of the RSS (1925) → BJP (1980) lineage of Hindu nationalist politics.

## Disputes
- **Mercy petitions**: Savarkar's multiple petitions from the Cellular Jail (1911–1921) pledging political quiescence are factually established; their interpretation (opportunism vs. tactics) is contested.
