# Sayyid Qutb

## Identity
Egyptian literary critic, government official, and Islamist ideologue (1906–1966); studied in the US 1948–1950 and returned with deep anti-Western and anti-secular convictions; joined the Muslim Brotherhood ~1953; imprisoned 1954–1964 (a period including documented torture); released briefly; rearrested 1965; tried and executed by hanging 29 August 1966 under Nasser. His *Milestones* (1964), composed in prison, is the foundational text of modern militant Sunni Islamism.

## Role in the tradition
The principal intellectual ancestor of late-20th- and early-21st-century militant Sunni Islamism, including al-Zawahiri's Egyptian Islamic Jihad → al-Qaeda → broader jihadist movements.
