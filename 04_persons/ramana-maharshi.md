# Ramana Maharshi

## Identity
Tamil sage (1879–1950); at 16 underwent a sudden death-experience that he later described as the awakening to the immortal Self; left home for Arunachala (Tiruvannamalai) and spent the rest of his life there. Taught *atma-vichara* (self-inquiry, "Who am I?") as the principal spiritual practice. Visited by Paul Brunton (1934 *A Search in Secret India*) and many other Western seekers; one of the principal mid-20th-century vectors of Advaita Vedanta to Western audiences.

## Role in the tradition
Among the most-cited 20th-century Advaita teachers; foundational influence on the contemporary neo-Advaita / "self-inquiry" Western teacher generation.
