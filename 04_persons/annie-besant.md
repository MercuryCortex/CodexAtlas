# Annie Besant

## Identity
British social reformer, atheist-secularist (1870s–1880s), socialist (Fabian Society), then Theosophist (joined 1889 after reviewing Blavatsky's *Secret Doctrine*); succeeded Olcott as President of the Theosophical Society (1907–1933). Long-time resident of India; President of the Indian National Congress (1917). Co-discoverer (with Charles Leadbeater) of Jiddu Krishnamurti as the "vehicle of the World Teacher" (1909); Krishnamurti dissolved this status himself in 1929 (the "Order of the Star" dissolution).

## Works
See texts-authored.

## Role in the tradition
Institutionalized Theosophy globally; her advocacy of Krishnamurti precipitated the major schism with Steiner's Anthroposophy (1913). Her Indian-political work contributed to the early-20th-century independence movement.
