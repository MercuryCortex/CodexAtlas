# Bahá'u'lláh

## Identity
Founder of the Bahá'í Faith (1817–1892); Iranian nobleman who became an early Bábí (1844 onward); imprisoned in the Síyáh-Chál (Tehran, 1852) and there received the foundational mystical experience; exiled successively to Baghdad (1853–1863), Constantinople (1863), Edirne/Adrianople (1863–1868), and finally Akka in Ottoman Palestine (1868–1892, in confinement). Publicly declared his prophetic station as "He whom God shall make manifest" (the One foretold by the Báb) in the Garden of Ridván outside Baghdad, April 1863. Composed the *Kitáb-i-Íqán* (1862) and the *Kitáb-i-Aqdas* (~1873) during these exiles.

## Role in the tradition
Founder; recognized by Bahá'ís as the Manifestation of God for the present age, in the succession Adam → Noah → Abraham → Moses → Zoroaster → Krishna → Buddha → Christ → Muhammad → the Báb → Bahá'u'lláh.
