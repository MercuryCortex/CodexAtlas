# Michael Maier

## Identity
Late-16th / early-17th-c. German physician, alchemist, and **Rosicrucian apologist** (1568–1622). Trained in medicine at Rostock, Frankfurt, Padua, Bologna, Basel (MD 1596). Court physician to **Emperor Rudolf II** at Prague (~1608–1612) — the Hermetic-alchemical-magical court that brought together Tycho Brahe, Kepler, John Dee (briefly), the alchemist Edward Kelley, the magician Bragadino, and many others; the most extraordinary single-court concentration of Hermetic-scientific personnel in European history. After Rudolf's 1612 death Maier traveled to London (1611–1616) and interacted with Robert Fludd and the English Hermetic-Rosicrucian circle. Later returned to Germany, served Moritz of Hessen-Kassel (the principal Rosicrucian-sympathetic prince), and died in Magdeburg 1622.

Maier is the central post-1614 figure of **continental Rosicrucianism in its alchemical-Hermetic register**, and his ***Atalanta Fugiens*** (1617) is **one of the iconic flagship holdings of the Bibliotheca Philosophica Hermetica / Ritman Library** and a centerpiece of the Beinecke Mellon Collection.

## Works
- ***Atalanta Fugiens*** (1617) — see [[phase-6-035-maier-atalanta-fugiens]]. 50 emblems + 50 mottos + 50 epigrams + 50 prose discourses + 50 three-voice fugues, all in integrated alchemical-mystical-musical work.
- ***Arcana Arcanissima*** (1614) — euhemeristic-alchemical reading of Greco-Roman mythology as encoded alchemical history.
- ***Symbola Aureae Mensae Duodecim Nationum*** (1617) — twelve alchemical authorities from twelve nations.
- ***Themis Aurea*** (1618) — defense of the Rosicrucian fraternity.
- ***Silentium Post Clamores*** (1617) — apology for the Rosicrucian fraternity's silence after the 1614–1615 manifesto-controversy.

## Role in the tradition
- **Architect of post-1614 Rosicrucianism.** When the 1614 *Fama* and 1615 *Confessio* manifestos generated a Europe-wide search for the *Brotherhood of the Rose Cross*, the actual *Brotherhood* refused to surface. Maier's 1617–1618 apologies stabilized the Rosicrucian tradition's self-understanding as primarily symbolic-Hermetic rather than as a discoverable secret society.
- **Continental-English Rosicrucian transmission node.** Maier's 1611–1616 London period and his contact with Fludd is central to the trans-Channel Rosicrucian transmission.
- **Integrated visual-musical-alchemical synthesis.** *Atalanta Fugiens* is unique in 17th-c. European literature: a coherently-organized 50-stage alchemical *opus* presented simultaneously as visual emblem, Latin verse, prose discourse, and three-voice musical fugue. The work has been continuously reissued and performed (modern 21st-c. complete recordings exist).
