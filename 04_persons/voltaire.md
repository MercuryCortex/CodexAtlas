# Voltaire

Central figure of the French Enlightenment whose deployment of "Confucius" as a model of rational ethics without revelation was strategic polemics against the Catholic Church as much as serious sinology. Voltaire's *Essai sur les moeurs* (1756) and the play *L'Orphelin de la Chine* (1755) presented China as a civilization governed by rational philosophy rather than revelation — a direct challenge to the Church's claim that morality requires Christian theology. His "Confucius" was substantially a European construction filtered through Jesuit accounts, particularly the *Confucius Sinarum Philosophus* (Couplet, 1687). Key node in the Chinese → European Enlightenment transmission chain.
