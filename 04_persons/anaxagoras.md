# Anaxagoras

**Stub.** Auto-created 2026-05-25 by `watcher-claude-lead` during TYRANT Wave 3b-tail to resolve dead-link references to this canonical historical figure. Awaits full treatment.

Pre-Socratic philosopher (c. -500 to -428). First philosopher to settle in Athens. Author of *On Nature* (lost; survives in fragments). Famous for cosmology of infinite seed-mixture (*chremata*) ordered by *nous* (cosmic mind), and for explaining the moon's light as reflected from the sun. Tried for impiety, fled Athens c. -437.
