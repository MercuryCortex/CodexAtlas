# Ramon Llull

**Ramon Llull** (c.1232–1316) was a Catalan nobleman-turned mystic who, after a dramatic conversion experience c.1263, devoted fifty years to creating a universal logical system (*Ars Magna*) for demonstrating Christian truth to Muslims and Jews through reason alone — and to missionary activity in North Africa, where he allegedly died a martyr's death (though the martyrdom account is disputed). His combinatorial system became the ancestor of Renaissance Hermeticism (via Pico, Bruno, Agrippa), Leibniz's logical calculus, and the modern theory of computation.
