# Deganawidah (the Peacemaker)

The semi-divine founder of the Haudenosaunee Confederacy, who — together with [[hiawatha-haudenosaunee]] — persuaded the five warring nations (Seneca, Cayuga, Onondaga, Oneida, Mohawk) to accept the *Gayanashagowa* (Great Law of Peace). In Haudenosaunee tradition, Deganawidah was born of a virgin mother among the Huron and possessed a stutter (making Hiawatha his spokesman). He planted the Tree of Peace (a white pine) at Onondaga and buried the weapons of war beneath it. The last convert was Tadodaho, the Onondaga war chief whose twisted body and snake-hair were physically straightened by Deganawidah and Hiawatha as the covenant was sealed. Deganawidah departed at the founding, promising to return if the confederacy was ever threatened.

The Great Law he brought established the oldest continuously operating democratic constitutional system in the world (contested dating: Haudenosaunee oral tradition points to ~1090 CE; outside scholars date ~1450–1600 CE). Its documented influence on the American Founding Fathers — Benjamin Franklin, Thomas Jefferson — is one of the vault's most important political-theology transmission edges.
