# Shammai

Pharisaic contemporary and rival of Hillel; founder of *Beit Shammai* (the House of Shammai). Generally takes the stricter halakhic position in the Mishnah's hundreds of recorded Beit-Hillel-vs-Beit-Shammai disputes. The destruction of the Temple and the subsequent dominance of Yavneh-period rabbinic authority left the Hillelite tradition ascendant; Shammaite positions are usually overruled in standard rabbinic halakha.
