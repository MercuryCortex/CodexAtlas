# Adam

## Identity
Adam — Hebrew *ʾādām* (אדם), originally a common noun ("earth-creature, human-being") cognate with *ʾădāmâ* (earth, soil) — is the first human in the biblical narrative (Gen 1:26–28, 2:7–25, 3, 4–5). Two creation-accounts (Gen 1 [P source] and Gen 2 [J source]) give two ways of telling his origin: the first (Gen 1) "created in the image of God" male and female *together*; the second (Gen 2) formed from dust, with the breath of life from God, then provided with a partner (Eve / Ḥawwâ) drawn from his side. The narrative of disobedience, expulsion from Eden, the curse, and the genealogy through Seth ([[seth]]) constitutes one of the foundational mythological matrices of subsequent Jewish, Christian, Gnostic, and Islamic theology.

In post-biblical theological development Adam becomes many things:
- **Pauline:** [[phase-3-015-pauline-epistles]] — Adam as the first transgressor through whom sin and death entered the cosmos (Rom 5:12–21), with Christ as the *second Adam* who undoes the damage. The Christian doctrine of original sin (developed especially by Augustine, see [[phase-4-028-augustine-confessions]]) builds on the Pauline Adam.
- **Philonic and Hermetic:** [[phase-3-013-philo-of-alexandria]], [[phase-4-011-corpus-hermeticum-i]] — Adam read allegorically as the rational soul / *nous* / cosmic Anthropos; the Edenic narrative as the soul's descent into matter. See [[anthropos-myth]].
- **Sethian Gnostic:** [[phase-4-002-apocryphon-of-john]], [[phase-4-006-hypostasis-of-the-archons]], [[phase-4-007-on-the-origin-of-the-world]] — there is a *heavenly Adam* (the perfect Image, the *Adamas*) above and an *earthly Adam* fabricated by the ignorant Demiurge below; the inner divine spark in earthly Adam is what is awakened by Sophia and saved by gnosis.
- **Kabbalistic *Adam Kadmon*:** [[phase-5-027-sefer-ha-zohar]] — the *Zohar* and especially Lurianic Kabbalah develop *Adam Kadmon* — the primordial cosmic Adam whose body is the [[sefirot]], whose dimensions are the cosmos. The biblical Adam is the *image-on-earth* of Adam Kadmon.
- **Quranic:** [[phase-4-034-quran]] — Adam (Ādam) is the first prophet, taught the names of all things (Sūra 2:31), bowed to by all angels except Iblīs, expelled from Paradise but forgiven; Islamic theology generally avoids the Pauline-Augustinian doctrine of original sin while accepting the Adam-mythological narrative.

## Historical evidence
None — Adam is a *biblical-narrative* and *post-biblical-theological* figure. The historical-critical question is about *the development of Adam-traditions*: the J/P composite origin in the Pentateuch; the post-exilic and Hellenistic-Jewish development (the Pseudepigraphal *Life of Adam and Eve*); the Pauline-Augustinian elaboration; the Gnostic, Hermetic, and Kabbalistic transformations.
