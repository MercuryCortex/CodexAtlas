# Erich von Däniken

Swiss author of *Chariots of the Gods?* (1968) — the founding modern work of popular ancient-astronaut pseudoarchaeology. Has published ~40 books with combined sales around 65 million copies. Held no academic credential in archaeology, ancient history, or aeronautics.

## What he claimed

Across the *Chariots of the Gods?* arc and follow-ups, von Däniken argued that:

1. Ancient civilizations were visited and influenced by extraterrestrial beings.
2. The structures (Egyptian pyramids, Nazca lines, Easter Island moai, Mesoamerican step-pyramids) and iconographies (Palenque "astronaut" sarcophagus lid; Egyptian / Mesopotamian winged figures) of those civilizations are evidence of contact.
3. Religious texts globally describe these visits in mythological language; gods are misremembered aliens.

## Tier-4 — same posture as Sitchin

Rejected by mainstream archaeology and anthropology. No peer-reviewed defense exists. The vault includes the node because the framework has enormous popular reach (65M+ copies) and modern popular media (History Channel *Ancient Aliens*, vast YouTube content) carries his framework into wide circulation.
