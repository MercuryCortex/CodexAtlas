# Eliezer ben Hyrcanus

## Identity
A major late-1st-/early-2nd-century rabbi, one of the **five principal disciples of [[yohanan-ben-zakkai]]** (with Joshua, Yose, Shimon, Elazar), conservative in halakhic tendency (often classed as "Shammaite," though the rabbinic Hillel/Shammai schema is partly later schematization). Two episodes give him exceptional historiographic importance: (a) the **Oven of Akhnai dispute** (Bavli BM 59a–b) — the foundational rabbinic narrative about the majority-rule principle in halakhic decision-making, in which R. Eliezer is overruled despite miraculous and even *bat qol* (heavenly voice) support; and (b) an **interrogation by a Roman official on charges of *minut*** (Christianity / heresy), in which R. Eliezer recalls having once been pleased by a teaching from **Jacob of Sikhnin** that he later realized was from "Yeshu the Notzri" — among the earliest rabbinic-attested encounters with Christian teaching.

## Historical evidence
Rabbinic only; multiple-tradition (Mishnah, Tosefta, both Talmuds). The *minut* episode appears with variation in Tosefta Hullin 2:24 and Bavli Avodah Zarah 16b–17a; Schäfer 2007 treats it as critical evidence for Jewish-Christian relations.

## Works
None. Extensive rabbinic citation.

## Role in the tradition
- **Oven of Akhnai narrative**: foundational text of rabbinic theology of revelation — "not in heaven" (Deut 30:12), meaning the Torah's interpretation has been entrusted to human halakhic majority-decision, even against heavenly voices.
- **Excommunication and rehabilitation**: he died estranged from the rabbinic community; reconciliation came late or only posthumously per the rabbinic narrative.
- ***Minut* encounter narrative**: documents (a) Christian teaching circulating in Galilee in his lifetime; (b) Jacob of Sikhnin as a Jewish-Christian teacher; (c) R. Eliezer's near-vulnerability to it — and the deep anxiety this produced.

## Disputes
- **Historicity of the Akhnai narrative**: largely literary-theological; the *type* is rooted in genuine 1st-c. Yavneh-period halakhic dispute.
- **Yeshu the Notzri**: identification with Jesus of Nazareth — mainstream view: yes, this is a rabbinic reference to Jesus.
- **Date of the *minut* interrogation**: under Trajan (~+115)? Or earlier? The story's date is uncertain.
