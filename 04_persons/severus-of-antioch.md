# Severus of Antioch

## Identity
Severus of Antioch (~465-538) was Patriarch of Antioch from 512 to 518 and the most consequential systematic theologian of the Miaphysite cause. Educated as a pagan rhetorician at Alexandria and Beirut, he converted, became a monk, and emerged as the leading Cyrillian theological voice against the [[event-council-of-chalcedon-451|Chalcedonian]] settlement. As patriarch he refused the Definition of Chalcedon; deposed and exiled by Emperor Justin I in 518, he spent his remaining 20 years in Egypt writing the massive theological corpus that became the doctrinal foundation of Oriental Orthodox Miaphysite Christology. His ~125 *Cathedral Homilies* and ~3,759 surviving letters (preserved mainly in Syriac translation) make him one of the most extensively documented late-antique church fathers — comparable in corpus-size to Augustine. Anathematized by Justinian's Constantinople II council (553) but venerated as **St. Severus the Great** in all Oriental Orthodox churches.

## Historical evidence
- ***Cathedral Homilies*** — 125 sermons preached during his Antiochene patriarchate, preserved in Syriac translation (Greek originals mostly lost).
- **~3,759 surviving letters** — preserved primarily in Syriac translation; Brooks 1902-1904 edited the *Select Letters* in 6 books.
- ***Philalethes*** + ***Liber contra impium grammaticum*** — major theological polemics.
- **Two contemporary *Lives*** — Zacharias of Mytilene's *Life of Severus* (sympathetic) + John of Beth-Aphtonia's *Life of Severus*.
- **Justinian's anti-Severan edicts** of 536 — the imperial-Chalcedonian condemnation.

## Works
Vast. Letters alone (~3,759) outnumber Augustine's letters. The *Cathedral Homilies* (125 surviving of an original 175) are a foundational source for late-antique Antiochene preaching. *Philalethes* is the systematic Miaphysite response to a Diphysite Cyrilline-florilegium attack.

## Role in the tradition
- **In Oriental Orthodoxy**: the foundational Miaphysite systematic theologian after Cyril of Alexandria. The Syriac Orthodox Anaphora of St James is his liturgical legacy. Liturgically commemorated as a major Father.
- **In Chalcedonian Christianity**: anathematized at Constantinople II (553) under Justinian; the Severan corpus is the most-debated Miaphysite theological body in modern ecumenical dialogue.
- **In modern Oriental Orthodox / Chalcedonian dialogue**: the 1971 Vienna + 1989-1990 Chambésy agreed-statements substantially rehabilitated his theological substance (acknowledging that his *mia physis* is not Eutychian monophysitism).
