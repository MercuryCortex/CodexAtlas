# Zecharia Sitchin

Author of *The 12th Planet* (1976) and the seven-volume *Earth Chronicles* series. Born in Baku, raised in Palestine, lived in New York. Self-described as a "research journalist" — held no academic credential in Assyriology, Sumerology, or astronomy.

## What he claimed

Sitchin claimed his independent reading of Sumerian and Akkadian cuneiform texts revealed:

1. A 12th planet **Nibiru** on a 3,600-year elliptical orbit beyond Pluto, periodically swinging close to Earth.
2. Nibiru is home to the **Anunnaki**, an extraterrestrial humanoid species.
3. ~445,000 years ago the Anunnaki visited Earth to mine gold for their atmosphere.
4. ~200,000 years ago they genetically engineered *Homo sapiens* by combining their DNA with that of *Homo erectus*.
5. The Sumerian and Akkadian gods (An, Enki, Marduk, Inanna, Ninhursag, etc.) were Anunnaki individuals — kings, scientists, princes — whose lives and conflicts are recorded in cuneiform "as history, not myth."

The framework appears across all eight of his books, with progressively elaborated claims through the *Earth Chronicles* arc (1976-2007).

## Tier-4 sourcing — why this node exists

Sitchin's Nibiru-Anunnaki hypothesis is **rejected by mainstream Assyriology, Sumerology, archaeology, and astronomy** — there is no peer-reviewed defense of any of his major claims. He is the textbook example of T4-popular-claim-rejected.

The vault includes this node because **the framework is widely circulated** — Sitchin's books have sold millions of copies, the Anunnaki / Nibiru material dominates popular religion / spirituality YouTube and TikTok, and any user landing on Anu, Enki, or Marduk in this vault will encounter Sitchin-derived material in their open-web search. The vault's job per CODEX is to **serve the in-graph rebuttal alongside the popular claim**, not pretend the claim doesn't exist.

## Investigation-as-prompt — the legitimate scholarship Sitchin's claim distorts

Per CODEX §IV's investigation-as-prompt rule, Sitchin's framework points to *real* features of Mesopotamian theology that he reads as evidence of extraterrestrials. The real features:

- **The Anunnaki ARE a real divine class.** In Sumerian theology *Anunnaki* (𒀭𒀀𒉣𒈾) is the collective term for the great gods of the divine council — Sky-god An's offspring + the Igigi (the working gods of the heavens). The term means literally "those of princely seed/lineage". Bottéro 2001 + Lambert 2013 + Black & Green's Brill *Gods, Demons and Symbols of Ancient Mesopotamia* (1992) cover the actual semantics. The texts describe a divine council, not a spacefaring race.
- **The Sumerians did identify divine residences with celestial bodies.** Inanna is associated with Venus; Utu with the sun; Nanna with the moon. The astral-deity correspondence is real Mesopotamian astrotheology — it's not evidence of extraterrestrials.
- **The Sumerian creation accounts DO discuss humans being shaped by the gods.** Atrahasis: the gods create humans from clay mixed with the blood of a slain god (We-ila) to bear the labour-burden the lower gods refused. This is mythological cosmogony, not genetic engineering.

So the **structural patterns Sitchin notices are real** (divine council + astral correspondences + creation-of-humans-by-gods mythology). His **interpretation** is what mainstream rejects: that the texts describe historical extraterrestrial events.

This investigation-as-prompt logic is the canonical pattern for every T3/T4 inclusion in this vault.

## Rebuttal sources

- **Heiser, Michael S. (2001)** — load-bearing rebuttal. Heiser holds a PhD in Hebrew Bible and Ancient Semitic Languages (University of Wisconsin-Madison). His paper *The Myth of a 12th Planet: A Brief Analysis of Cylinder Seal VA 243* demonstrates that Sitchin's strongest visual evidence — cylinder seal VA 243 in the Berlin Vorderasiatisches Museum — does NOT depict 12 planets but a standard Mesopotamian iconographic constellation. Sitchin never engaged the rebuttal in academic forums.
- **Sagan, Carl & Goldsmith, Donald (1976)** — Carl Sagan's correspondence + AAAS review dismissed Sitchin's astronomical claims (a 3,600-year-period planet that periodically approaches Earth without disturbing the inner solar system) as inconsistent with orbital mechanics.
- **Bottéro, Jean (2001)** *Religion in Ancient Mesopotamia* — standard scholarly treatment of Mesopotamian theology without Anunnaki-extraterrestrial reading.
- **Lambert, W.G. (2013)** *Babylonian Creation Myths* — definitive treatment of Enuma Elish on academic terms.

## Why he matters anyway

Sitchin's books outsold most peer-reviewed Assyriology by orders of magnitude.
