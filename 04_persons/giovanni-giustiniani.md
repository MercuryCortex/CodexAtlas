# Giovanni Giustiniani Longo

Genoese nobleman and mercenary captain (1418–1453). Arrived at Constantinople in late January 1453 with ~700 well-equipped Genoese troops at his own expense; appointed by [[constantine-xi-palaiologos|Emperor Constantine XI]] as supreme commander of the land-wall defence. Coordinated the defence of the critical Mesoteichion sector at the Romanos Gate throughout the 53-day siege. Mortally wounded by a culverin shot during the final assault of 29 May 1453; his evacuation through the inner walls precipitated the Greek collapse and is one of the historiographically-contested moments of the fall ([[event-fall-of-constantinople-1453]]). Died on a Genoese ship reaching Chios two days later.
