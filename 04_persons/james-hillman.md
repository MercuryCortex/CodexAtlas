# James Hillman

## Identity
American depth psychologist (1926–2011); Director of Studies at the C.G. Jung Institute in Zürich 1959–1969; founder of archetypal psychology. Took the post-Jungian movement away from clinical-medical settings toward a polytheistic, imaginal, image-centered re-reading of soul. *Re-Visioning Psychology* (1975) is his programmatic statement.

## Role in the tradition
Principal architect of the post-Jungian "archetypal" school distinct from clinical Jungian analysis. Treats the Greek pantheon (Hermes, Aphrodite, Hades, etc., cf. [[hermes-greek]], [[aphrodite-greek]], [[hades]]) as ongoing imaginal-psychological realities. With his collaborator [[james-hollis]] and others, extended [[carl-gustav-jung]]'s engagement with classical and Renaissance mythology into late-20th-c. American culture.
