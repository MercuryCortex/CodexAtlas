# Robert Bauval

## The Orion Correlation Theory

**Robert Bauval** (b. 1948, Alexandria, Egypt) is an independent Egyptologist and construction engineer who originated the **[[theme-orion-correlation-theory|Orion Correlation Theory]]** (OCT), first published in *Discussions in Egyptology* in 1989 and developed into *The Orion Mystery* (1994, with Adrian Gilbert).

**The core observation:** The three Giza pyramids' arrangement — not perfectly collinear, with the smallest (Menkaure) offset — matches the arrangement of Orion's Belt stars (*Al Nitak*, *Al Nilam*, *Mintaka*) with the Milky Way corresponding to the Nile.

**The shaft alignments (most accepted part):** The King's Chamber south shaft pointed to Al Nitak at lower culmination c. 2500 BCE; the north shaft to Thuban (then the pole star). These are confirmed by astronomical calculation and accepted by mainstream Egyptologists as intentional orientation.

**What is disputed:** The extension from shaft alignments to the full three-pyramid / three-stars ground map. Critics note the correspondence requires ignoring Sigma Orionis, the angle matches only approximately, and the "pre-planned ground layout" interpretation is archaeologically unconfirmed.

**The 10,500 BCE extension** (*Keeper of Genesis*, with Graham Hancock, 1996): Extended the theory to argue the Giza plateau reflects the sky of ~10,500 BCE — when Orion's Belt was at its precessional minimum and the Sphinx faced Leo at vernal equinox sunrise. Not accepted by mainstream Egyptology; requires design to predate construction by ~8,000 years.

## Background

Bauval grew up in Alexandria, studied construction engineering in England, worked as a construction engineer across the Middle East and Africa. He is an independent researcher without an academic institutional position — a significant factor in the mixed reception of his work.

His collaboration with **Graham Hancock** associated the OCT with a broader alternative-history framework. The core shaft-alignment observation remains valid independently of that framework.
