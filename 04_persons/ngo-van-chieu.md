# Ngô Văn Chiêu

**Ngô Văn Chiêu** (1878–1932) was the first person to receive Cao Dai revelation — a French colonial administrator on Phú Quốc Island who, through spirit-writing séances beginning around 1921, received communications from the Supreme Being (*Cao Đài*) including the directive to use the Divine Eye symbol. He later withdrew from the institutional Cao Dai church founded in 1926 and led the more mystical, esoteric Chiếu Minh branch, which rejected the papal organizational hierarchy that [[pham-cong-tac]] built.
