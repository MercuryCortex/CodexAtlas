# Zoroaster (Zarathuštra)

Iranian prophetic religious-reformer of the late Bronze Age / early Iron Age — author of the **Gathas** of the [[phase-2-002-gathas-of-zarathustra|Old Avesta]] and founder of the religious tradition that becomes Zoroastrianism. The Gathas articulate a radical ethical-dualist cosmology: the supreme god [[ahura-mazda]] confronts the destructive spirit [[angra-mainyu-ahriman]], the seven [[the-amesha-spentas|Amesha Spentas]] manifest divine attributes, history moves toward the [[doctrine-frashokereti|frashokereti]] cosmic renovation. Foundational figure for Persian-period religious-eschatological transmission into Second-Temple Judaism + Christianity + Islam (Boyce 1975, Hultgård 1998, Cohn 1993).
