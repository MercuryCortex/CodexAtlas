Brahmagupta was the greatest Indian mathematician of the Gupta era. His *Brahmasphutasiddhanta* (628 CE) contains the first written rules for arithmetic with zero (*n* + 0 = *n*, *n* × 0 = 0), making him the founder of zero as a mathematical concept rather than a mere placeholder. See [[mathematics-zero-india]].
