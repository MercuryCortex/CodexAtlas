# Francisco Javier Clavijero

## Identity
Mexican Jesuit, historian, and ethnographer of Aztec civilization. Clavigero was born in Veracruz to Spanish parents, educated in New Spain, joined the Society of Jesus, and was expelled with all Jesuits from Spanish dominions in 1767. He spent the rest of his life in Bologna, Italy, where he wrote his major work, *Storia antica del Messico* (1780–81) — a four-volume Italian-language history of ancient Mexico. The work was a deliberate defense of Indigenous Mexican civilization against the *de Pauw* / Buffon "degeneracy" thesis (the 18th-c. European view that the Americas were biologically and culturally inferior to the Old World). Clavigero, drawing on Sahagún, Durán, and other 16th-c. sources plus his own knowledge of Nahuatl, presented Aztec civilization as a sophisticated society comparable to Old World classical civilizations.

## Historical evidence
Documented Jesuit records; the *Storia antica del Messico* itself; correspondence; standard biography in Ronan 1977.

## Works
- *Storia antica del Messico* (4 vols, Cesena 1780–81). Spanish translations from 1826 onward. The principal Enlightenment-era defense of pre-conquest Mexican civilization.
- *Storia della California* (1789).
- Smaller works on Mexican natural history.

## Role in the tradition
Clavigero's defense of Indigenous Mexican civilization was foundational for 19th-c. Mexican national identity (Mexican independence 1810–21 deployed indigenista-Aztec imagery, drawing from Clavigero). His work was the principal scholarly source on Aztec religion outside Spain through much of the 19th c. before Sahagún's complete Florentine Codex became accessible in the 20th c.

## Disputes
- **Methodological criticism.** Clavigero's defense of Aztec civilization sometimes shaded into idealization; modern scholarship has corrected various claims (e.g., his somewhat sanitized account of human sacrifice). But the broad thrust — that pre-conquest Mexican civilization was a serious religious-political-cultural achievement — is now unproblematic.
