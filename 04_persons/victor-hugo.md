# Victor Hugo

**Victor Hugo** (1802–1885) — French novelist, poet, and political activist, author of *Les Misérables* (1862) and *Notre-Dame de Paris* (1831) — is one of the three founding patron saints of [[tradition-cao-dai|Cao Dai]], the Vietnamese syncretic religion. Cao Dai spirit-writing sessions of the 1920s identified Hugo as the embodiment of Western wisdom in the Third Divine Alliance — alongside Sun Yat-sen (Asian/Chinese wisdom) and Nguyễn Bỉnh Khiêm (Vietnamese wisdom). His image appears on the main altar of the Tây Ninh Cathedral beside Buddha, Confucius, Laozi, and Jesus. Hugo represents, for Cao Dai, the convergence of French humanist thought and universal religious truth — a fitting choice given that Hugo himself wrote extensively about God, the soul, and universal spiritual progress.
