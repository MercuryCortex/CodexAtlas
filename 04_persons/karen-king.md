# Karen L. King

## Identity
American historian of religion (b. 1954); Hollis Professor of Divinity at Harvard Divinity School from 1997 (the oldest endowed chair in the United States). Major scholar of Gnosticism, the *Gospel of Mary*, the *Apocryphon of John*, and the construction of orthodoxy/heresy categories.

## Works
*What Is Gnosticism?* (2003) is the foundational scholarly dismantling of the "Gnosticism" category alongside Williams 1996. *Gospel of Mary of Magdala* (2003) is the standard scholarly treatment. *Secret Revelation of John* (2006) is the major Apocryphon-of-John study.

## Role in the tradition
A principal figure in the post-1996 revisionist Gnosticism studies that complicated the unified-Gnostic-Religion thesis (Jonas, Pagels). King's earlier-Christian-feminism work on the Gospel of Mary has been widely influential.

## Disputes
- **"Gospel of Jesus' Wife" papyrus fragment** (2012 publication; revealed 2016 as a modern forgery): King had published a controversial reading of a Coptic papyrus fragment containing the phrase "Jesus said to them, 'My wife...'"; subsequent investigative reporting (Ariel Sabar, *The Atlantic* 2016 / *Veritas* book 2020) traced the fragment's provenance to a modern forger (Walter Fritz). King publicly acknowledged the fragment as likely forgery. The episode is a serious cautionary case in 21st-century papyrology, well documented by King herself in subsequent writings.
