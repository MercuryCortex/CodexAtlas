# Nyingma — Ancient Translation School

The oldest of the four major schools of Tibetan Buddhism, tracing its origin to the 8th-c. transmission of Vajrayāna Buddhism into Tibet by Padmasambhava (Guru Rinpoche) under King Trisong Detsen. Holds the **Old Translation** (*snga 'gyur*) tradition — texts translated before the 11th-c. *new translation* (*gsar ma*) period of the Sarma schools. Distinctively centered on **Dzogchen** ("Great Perfection"), the **terma** revealed-treasure tradition, and wrathful tantric cycles including Vajrakīla.
