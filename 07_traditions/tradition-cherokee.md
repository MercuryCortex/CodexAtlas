Stub — Cherokee religious, cultural, and linguistic tradition. The Cherokee (Tsalagi) are an Iroquoian-speaking people of the southeastern Appalachian region. Key vault connection: the Cherokee syllabary ([[alphabet-cherokee]]) invented by Sequoyah (c. 1821) is one of the most documented cases of deliberate script invention and literate-civilization formation under colonial pressure. The Trail of Tears (1838–39) is the major event node. See also [[tradition-haudenosaunee]] for the related Iroquoian family.
