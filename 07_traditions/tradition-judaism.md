# Judaism (Israelite religion through Second Temple seed)

> This is a Phase 2 *seed* node for Judaism, covering the pre-exilic Israelite religion and the early exilic / Persian-period transformations. The full elaboration of Judaism — Second Temple sectarianism, the canon, Mishnah/Talmud, medieval and modern Judaism — belongs to later phases.

## Overview
Israelite religion emerges in the Levantine highlands (~-1200 BCE) within a broadly Canaanite religious matrix, develops a distinctive devotion to YHWH (initially alongside other deities, increasingly exclusively), undergoes major transformations in the prophetic 8th–7th centuries and the catastrophic 6th-century Babylonian conquest and exile, and re-emerges in the Persian period as Second Temple Judaism — recognizably continuous with later rabbinic and modern Judaism, but transformed from its Iron Age forms.

## Historical phases
1. **Pre-monarchic Israel (~-1200 to -1000)** — Iron Age I village confederations in the highlands; YHWH probably introduced from southern desert (Shasu / Midian); coexistence with broader Canaanite religion.
2. **Monarchic (~-1000 to -586)** — United Monarchy (David, Solomon, traditional), Divided Monarchy (Israel and Judah). J source ~-950, E ~-850, classical 8th-c. prophets, Deuteronomic D source / Josianic reform ~-622.
3. **Babylonian Exile (-586 to -539)** — Destruction of First Temple; Ezekiel; Deuteronomistic History; theological transformation under exile.
4. **Persian Period (-539 to -332)** — Return from exile; rebuilding of Second Temple (~520–515); Second Isaiah; emergence of monotheism in fully developed form; Persian / Zoroastrian contact period. P source compiled; Pentateuch reaches substantially its received form (Phase 3 work).
5. **Hellenistic and Second Temple (-332 to +70)** — Phase 3 territory.
6. **Rabbinic (~+70 onward)** — Phase 4 territory.

## Defining doctrines (developing through Phase 2)
- **Henotheism → monolatry → monotheism** — gradual exclusivity.
- **Covenant** — YHWH-Israel covenant grounding obligation.
- **Election** — Israel as YHWH's people.
- **Prophecy** — direct YHWH-speech mediated by named prophets.
- **Eschatology** — *Day of YHWH* (Amos) → developed apocalyptic (Phase 3).
- **Ethics** — social justice; humanitarian Deuteronomic law.

## Relations to neighbors
- vs. [[tradition-canaanite-ugaritic]]: continuity (Canaanite El becomes Israelite El, YHWH absorbs storm-warrior elements of Baal) plus polemic (prophets against Baal worship). Both relations evident in the texts.
- vs. [[tradition-zoroastrianism]]: Persian-period contact (539–332). Strongly hypothesized influence on developing angelology, demonology, dualism, and eschatology — the central cross-phase edge. See [[persian-period-injection]] for the full five-channel analysis of the documented theological injection: cosmic dualism / resurrection-of-the-dead / messianic-savior figure / named angelology / final cosmic judgment — all absent or marginal in pre-exilic Hebrew Bible, all well-developed in Second Temple Judaism following Persian contact.
- vs. [[tradition-egyptian]]: traditional bondage-exodus narrative (J/E/P/D); some Hellenistic philosophical contact.
- vs. [[tradition-mesopotamian]]: shared primeval-history motifs (flood, creation, garden); whether by direct borrowing or shared Near Eastern grammar.
