# Bahá'í Faith

## Overview
Universalist monotheistic religion originating in 1840s Iran from the Bábí movement (Báb's 1844 declaration) and crystallizing as the Bahá'í Faith under Bahá'u'lláh's 1863 declaration (the Ridván declaration in Baghdad). Core doctrine: **progressive revelation** — God is utterly transcendent and unknowable in essence, but progressively reveals himself through *Manifestations of God* (Adam, Noah, Abraham, Moses, Zoroaster, Krishna, Buddha, Christ, Muhammad, the Báb, Bahá'u'lláh), each appropriate to a stage in humanity's collective spiritual maturation. Strongly universalist, anti-clerical, pro-science, pro-gender-equality, pro-universal-education, pro-international-language, pro-global-government.

## Historical phases
1. **Bábí period** (1844–1850) — the Báb's brief mission and execution; the violent suppression of the Bábí community.
2. **Bahá'u'lláh's prophetic ministry** (1853–1892) — successive exiles (Baghdad → Constantinople → Edirne → Akka); the foundational scripture *Íqán* (1862), *Aqdas* (1873), and many other texts.
3. **`Abdu'l-Bahá's leadership** (1892–1921) — global propagation; the principal early-20th-century Western Bahá'í engagement.
4. **Shoghi Effendi's Guardianship** (1921–1957) — institutional consolidation, translations, the development of the Administrative Order.
5. **Universal House of Justice** (1963 onward) — the elected international governing body (Haifa) since Shoghi Effendi's death without a successor Guardian.

## Pantheon
None. Strict monotheism with utterly transcendent unknowable Godhead known only through Manifestations.

## Defining doctrines
- Progressive revelation.
- Unity of religions / unity of God / unity of humanity.
- Abolition of clergy.
- Equality of women and men.
- Universal education.
- Single international auxiliary language.
- Single world tribunal / world federal system.
- Independent investigation of truth.

## Relations to neighbors
- vs. [[tradition-islam]]: Bahá'ís consider their faith the next dispensation after Islam, abrogating Islamic shari'a for the new age; Islamic authorities consider Bahá'u'lláh's claim to post-Quranic prophecy heretical (the legal basis for ongoing Iranian persecution of Bahá'ís).
- vs. [[tradition-shia-islam]]: even sharper — Bábí origins emerged from a Twelver Shi'a milieu and the dispute over the Báb / Mahdi claim is theologically central.
