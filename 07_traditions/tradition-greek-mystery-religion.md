# Greek Mystery Religions

Stub. The initiation-based religious traditions of the Greek and Hellenistic world: Eleusinian Mysteries (Demeter/Persephone), Orphic Mysteries, Dionysian Mysteries, Samothracian Mysteries, Mithraic Mysteries (in Hellenistic context), and later Isiac and other Eastern mystery cults operating in Greek cultural contexts. Characterized by initiation rites, secret knowledge (mysteria), and the promise of blessed afterlife or divine union. Distinguished from the public Olympian religion by their esoteric character and individual transformative focus.
