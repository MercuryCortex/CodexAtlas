# Church of the East (Nestorian Christianity)

The **Church of the East** (pejoratively called "Nestorian" by opponents) is the Christian denomination that rejected the Council of Ephesus (431 CE) and subsequently expanded eastward along the Silk Road, reaching Persia, India, Central Asia, and Tang Dynasty China (635 CE). It used **Syriac** as its liturgical and theological language. The Thomas Christians of Kerala (India) and the Xi'an Nestorian Stone (781 CE, China) are its primary material testimonies. See [[alphabet-syriac]] for the full account of the tradition's geographic reach and graphic transmission.
