# Amazonian shamanism

**Stub.** Pan-Amazonian shamanic complex — hundreds of distinct cultural-linguistic variants across the basin, sharing a recognisable family of practices: plant-teacher cosmology, vertical-axis travel, song-as-operative-technology, *ayahuasquero*/*vegetalista* lineages.

Referenced from [[substance-ayahuasca]].
