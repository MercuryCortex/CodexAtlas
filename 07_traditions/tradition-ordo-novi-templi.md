# Ordo Novi Templi (Order of the New Templars; ONT)

**Critical-tiering note:** Per the Codex Atlas methodology on documented racist-and-far-right religious-political traditions, this entry **documents the ONT as a historically-attested early-20th-c. Austrian racist-occult-Templar-revivalist organization** whose ideational content is the documented racist-occult content of its time. **Goodrick-Clarke 1985 is the load-bearing critical source.** The entry is not a defense of the organization but a documentation of one of the documented 20th-c. examples of how Templar-revival iconography migrated into racist-political-religious systems.

## Overview
The **Ordo Novi Templi** (Order of the New Templars; ONT) was an Austrian racist-occult-Templar-revivalist organization founded on **25 December 1907** at **Burg Werfenstein** (Upper Austria, on the Danube) by **[[lanz-von-liebenfels|Jörg Lanz von Liebenfels]]** (born Adolf Josef Lanz). The Order was the principal institutional vehicle of **Ariosophy** — Lanz's synthesis of:

1. **Modern Templar-revival iconography** (descending from [[tradition-strict-templar-observance|von Hund 1751]] → [[tradition-eglise-johannite|Fabré-Palaprat 1804]] → 19th-c. occult-Templar literature).
2. **Theosophical-racial-evolutionary metaphysics** (Blavatsky's *root-races* framework, racialized and biologized by Lanz).
3. **Wagnerian-Romantic Germanic-pagan-Christian revival**.
4. **Pseudo-scientific racist anthropology** (Lanz's *Theozoologie* 1905).

The ONT raised **a swastika banner over the Werfenstein castle tower at its 1907 foundation** — one of the **earliest documented 20th-c. uses of the swastika as racist-political symbol** (predating the broader völkisch-and-Nazi adoption by ~15–20 years). The Order published the periodical ***Ostara*** (89 issues, 1905–1931), the principal vehicle of Ariosophical content.

The ONT is one of the **documented 20th-c. examples of how Templar-revival iconography migrated into racist-political-religious systems** — a load-bearing deviant-bridge case for the broader [[templar-gnostic-transmission-hypothesis]] reception-history analysis.

**The ONT was suppressed by the National Socialist regime in 1942** — the Nazis suppressed Ariosophy and other occult-racist groups as ideological competitors, despite the substantial ideational overlap.

## Historical phases
1. **Foundation (25 December 1907)** — Lanz founds the ONT at the ruined Burg Werfenstein, which he had purchased explicitly for the purpose. Swastika banner raised.
2. **Pre-WWI development (~1907–1914)** — *Ostara* publication; small ONT membership (Vienna and broader Austrian).
3. **Inter-war reactivation (~1920s–1930s)** — ONT chapters in various Austrian and (limited) German cities; *Ostara* continues with intermittent publication; ideational overlap with the broader völkisch milieu (the Thule Society, the Germanenorden, the early NSDAP).
4. **National Socialist suppression (1942)** — the Nazis dissolved the ONT and other occult-racist groups as ideological competitors.
5. **Post-WWII Lanz self-reframing (1945–1954)** — Lanz claimed the Nazis had "stolen his ideas" and presented himself as a Nazi-suppression-victim; the claim is partially-defensible (the 1942 suppression is real) but elides Lanz's foundational racist-occult contribution.
6. **Post-WWII fringe-far-right reception (1954 onward)** — small fragments of post-Lanz Ariosophical literature persist in various European fringe-far-right circles.

## Core doctrines (documented, not endorsed)
- **Racist-occult-Templar synthesis** — Lanz claimed the medieval [[tradition-knights-templar|Knights Templar]] had been **secret Aryan-racial-purity guardians** whose mission had been corrupted; the ONT was to refound the Order on explicitly-racist lines.
- **Theozoological** anthropology — Lanz's pseudo-scientific claim that humans had originated from a mix of "Aryan god-electron-beings" and "Sodomite-Apeling" sub-races; racial-purity was therefore religious-cosmic obligation.
- **Anti-Christian-Pauline-Petrine polemic** — like [[tradition-eglise-johannite|Fabré-Palaprat's Johannite Church]], the ONT positioned itself as the *true* primitive Christianity against the institutional-Pauline-Petrine corruption — but with racist-political content the Johannite Church did not have.

## Relations to neighbors
- vs. **[[tradition-knights-templar]]**: claimed-but-fabricated medieval ancestor.
- vs. **[[tradition-strict-templar-observance]]** and **[[tradition-eglise-johannite]]**: institutional-ideational predecessors in the broader post-1750 neo-Templarist tradition.
- vs. **[[tradition-theosophy]]**: source of *root-races* framework, racialized and biologized by Lanz.
- vs. **Guido von List's Wotanism + Sebottendorff's Thule Society + the Germanenorden**: contemporary Austrian-and-Bavarian Ariosophical-racist-occult organizations, ideationally adjacent.
- vs. **National Socialism**: ideational predecessor in part (the documented Hitler-*Ostara*-Vienna influence channel) but institutionally a competitor (1942 suppression).

## The Hitler-Vienna question
The documented influence-channel: **the young Adolf Hitler read *Ostara* during his Vienna years (1908–1913)**, on the basis of Lanz's own subsequent claims and circumstantial period evidence (Daim 1958). **Goodrick-Clarke 1985** critically refines but does not reject the basic claim — the channel is documented, the strength is disputed. Most contemporary historians treat Hitler's ideational formation as drawing on Lanz alongside multiple other Vienna-period racist-occult sources rather than as solely-Lanzian.
