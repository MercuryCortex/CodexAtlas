# União do Vegetal (UDV)

**Stub.** Brazilian ayahuasca church (full name: Centro Espírita Beneficente União do Vegetal) founded 1961 by José Gabriel da Costa in Porto Velho, Rondônia. Theologically distinct from [[tradition-santo-daime]]: more austere Christian-Pentecostal frame, no bailado, hierarchical Mestre/Conselheiro structure, ritual ingestion of *hoasca* (their term for ayahuasca).

The 2006 US Supreme Court decision *Gonzales v. UDV* (546 U.S. 418) — unanimous — established that the Religious Freedom Restoration Act protects UDV's sacramental ayahuasca use in the United States, the first such legal protection for any psychedelic in US law.
