# Aztec / Mexica Religion

## Overview
The state religion of the Mexica (Aztec) empire, ~1325–1521, centered on Tenochtitlan. The Mexica religious system was a late-Postclassic central-Mexican religion absorbing earlier Toltec, Teotihuacan, and broader Mesoamerican religious-cultural elements; its distinctive features included an elaborate state-cultic apparatus centered on the Templo Mayor; the five-suns cosmology (the present sun being the Fifth, sustained by sacrifice); the dual calendar (260-day *tonalpohualli* + 365-day *xiuhpohualli* meshing in a 52-year cycle); large-scale state-sponsored human sacrifice; and the Mexica-specific patron god Huitzilopochtli alongside the pan-Mesoamerican Quetzalcoatl, Tlaloc, and Tezcatlipoca.

## Historical phases
1. **Mexica migration (~12th–13th c.)** — Mexica leave mythic Aztlán under Huitzilopochtli's guidance.
2. **Foundation of Tenochtitlan (1325)** — at Huitzilopochtli's vision-marked site.
3. **Imperial expansion (1428–1521)** — Triple Alliance (Mexica-Texcoco-Tlacopan) consolidates central-Mexican imperial power.
4. **Florescence (~1450–1519)** — Templo Mayor expansion; full state-cultic elaboration.
5. **Conquest (1519–1521)** — [[event-spanish-conquest-of-tenochtitlan-1521]] ends Mexica state religion.
6. **Post-conquest ethnographic recovery (1524–1577)** — Sahagún and other ethnographers preserve Mexica religious-cultural memory; popular Mesoamerican religion survives in syncretic Catholic forms.

## Pantheon
- [[huitzilopochtli]] — Mexica tutelary, war, sun
- [[quetzalcoatl]] — Feathered Serpent, creator-civilizer
- [[tlaloc]] — rain, agriculture
- [[tezcatlipoca]] — night, sorcery, kingship
- [[tonatiuh]] — the Sun (Fifth)
- [[coatlicue]] — earth-mother
- [[mictlantecuhtli]] — underworld

## Defining doctrines
- Five-suns cosmology; the present Sun requires sustaining sacrifice
- Dual calendar (260+365 cycle = 52-year Calendar Round)
- Tonalpohualli divination
- Human sacrifice as cosmic-political technology (its scale and significance is itself contested historiographically)
- Templo Mayor as central architectural-religious-political monument

## Relations to neighbors
- vs. [[tradition-maya-religion]]: shared Mesoamerican religious-cultural sphere; substantial overlap (calendar; Feathered Serpent Quetzalcoatl ↔ Kukulkán; rain-god Tlaloc ↔ Chaac); separate ethnic-political traditions.
- vs. [[tradition-christianity-canonical]]: catastrophic post-1521 displacement; subsequent colonial-syncretic forms (Quetzalcoatl-Christ; Coatlicue-Tonantzin-Guadalupe).
