# Chinese Daoism

The Chinese religious-philosophical tradition organized around the *Dao* (Way) — comprising philosophical Daoism (the *Daodejing*, *Zhuangzi*) + organized religious Daoism (the Celestial Masters from +142, the Shangqing + Lingbao revelation traditions, the Daoist canon). One of the three foundational Chinese traditions alongside [[tradition-confucian-religion|Confucianism]] and Chinese Buddhism.
