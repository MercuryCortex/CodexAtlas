# Ancient Greek Civilization

**Stub — upgrade needed.**

The broader cultural tradition of Ancient Greece, spanning the Archaic period (c. 800–480 BCE), Classical period (480–323 BCE), and ending with Roman conquest (31 BCE / Battle of Actium). Distinct from [[tradition-greek-religion]] (which covers the religious dimension specifically) and [[tradition-hellenistic-philosophy]] (which covers the philosophical tradition from c. 323 BCE onward into the late-antique world).

Key development relevant to the vault: the emergence of alphabetic literacy c. 800 BCE (see [[alphabet-greek-vowel-revolution]]) and the Axial Age philosophical tradition it enabled (see logos-stoic, Plato, Aristotle, the Pre-Socratics).
