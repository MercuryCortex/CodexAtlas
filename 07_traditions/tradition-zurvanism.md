# Zurvanism

**Zurvanism** is the **Sasanian-era Zoroastrian heretical-reform tradition** positing **Zurvan** (Cosmic Time-and-Fate) as the cosmic-parent of the twin spirits **Ahura Mazda** + **Angra Mainyu**. The doctrine resolves the theological tension of orthodox-Zoroastrian cosmic-dualism (how can two co-eternal principles be the basis of a coherent monotheism?) by positing a higher meta-principle from which both descend.

The tradition flourished in the Achaemenid + Parthian + Sasanian periods + was decisively suppressed by the orthodox Zoroastrian restoration under Khosrow I + Khosrow II (6th-7th c. CE). It does not survive the Arab conquest of Iran (-651). All surviving primary-textual witnesses are **Greek + Syriac + Armenian sources hostile to Zurvanism** — most importantly Eudemus of Rhodes (-330), Theodore of Mopsuestia (~400 CE), and especially **Eznik of Kolb's polemic *Against the Sects*** (~450 CE).

## The Zurvanite cosmogony

The fully-developed Zurvanite myth is preserved in Eznik of Kolb's polemic + parallel Greek + Syriac witnesses. The narrative:

1. In the cosmic-beginning, **Zurvan existed alone** as Cosmic Time-and-Fate.
2. Zurvan **sacrificed for 1000 years** seeking offspring — a son who would become the cosmic-creator.
3. Toward the end of the 1000 years, **Zurvan experienced doubt** about whether the sacrifice was efficacious.
4. As a result of the doubt, **two twins were conceived**: **Ahura Mazda** (from the efficacious sacrifice) + **Angra Mainyu** (from the doubt).
5. Zurvan vowed that **whichever twin emerged first would be the cosmic-king**.
6. **Angra Mainyu cut himself out of Zurvan's womb prematurely**, emerging first.
7. Zurvan, bound by his vow, granted Angra Mainyu kingship — **for 9,000 years** (the cosmic-mixture-period), after which Ahura Mazda would assume cosmic-rule (the Frashokereti).

The myth resolves the orthodox-Zoroastrian theological tension by positing a **higher cosmic-meta-principle (Zurvan)** + a **time-bounded cosmic-rule of Angra Mainyu** (rather than a co-eternal cosmic-dualism).

## Theological positioning

Zurvanism is **structurally radical** within Zoroastrianism: it preserves the cosmic-dualism between Asha + Druj + Ahura Mazda + Angra Mainyu but **subordinates the dualism** to a higher meta-principle. The orthodox-Zoroastrian objection is that this **subordinates Ahura Mazda** to Zurvan + treats Angra Mainyu as a legitimate (if temporary) cosmic-ruler.

The orthodox-Zoroastrian alternative resolves the theological tension differently: through the **temporal-mixture cosmology** (Bundahishn 12,000-year schema) in which the two cosmic-principles are co-eternal but the cosmic-mixture-period is bounded.

## Scholarly significance

Zurvanism is one of the cleanest documented cases of:
1. **Internal religious-theological-rationalization** within a major tradition — the orthodox-theological tension drives a heretical-reform solution.
2. **Sasanian-period religious-diversity** beyond what orthodox-Pahlavi-canonical sources suggest.
3. **Cross-tradition Christian-Iranian theological controversy** preserved in non-Zoroastrian sources (Eznik of Kolb's polemic *Against the Sects* is the principal modern source for the developed Zurvanite cosmogony).

Modern scholarship (Zaehner 1955; Shaked 1994) has reconstructed the Zurvanite tradition in detail despite the **loss of Zurvanite primary-sources** — a methodologically-important case of religious-historical reconstruction from hostile-witness testimony.

## Relation to Manichaeism

Zurvanism + Manichaeism share certain features: cosmic-dualism + search for a meta-principle resolving the dualism's theological tension. Whether Manichaeism derives from Zurvanism, Zurvanism from Manichaeism, or both develop from a shared Sasanian-period theological-context is debated. Most modern scholarship (BeDuhn 2000; Shaked 1994) favors the **shared-context interpretation**.
