# Wixárika (Huichol) tradition

**Stub.** Indigenous people of the western Sierra Madre (Jalisco, Nayarit, Durango, Zacatecas, Mexico) — c. 50,000 today. The Wixárika preserve the most continuously-documented peyote pilgrimage tradition: the annual ~400 km walk to Wirikuta (the sacred desert in San Luis Potosí) led by a *mara'akame* (singer-shaman). The peyote/deer/corn triple identity is the foundational mythological structure.
