# Sakya — Grey Earth Tradition

One of the four major schools of Tibetan Buddhism, founded at **Sakya Monastery** (1073) in southern Tibet by Khön Könchog Gyalpo. Hereditarily transmitted through the Khön family. Distinctive for the **Lamdré** ("Path and Result") system based on the Hevajra Tantra. Drogön Chögyal Phagpa (1235-1280), the 5th Sakya patriarch, became spiritual preceptor to Kublai Khan and was named imperial preceptor of the Mongol Yuan dynasty — making the Sakya the first Tibetan school to hold pan-Tibetan political power (1264-1354).
