# New Age

## Overview
Diffuse late-20th-century / early-21st-century Western religious-spiritual milieu emerging from Theosophy, Spiritualism, the human potential movement, the 1960s counterculture, and assorted Eastern-religious imports. Not a single organized tradition; rather, a loose constellation of practitioners, teachers, channeled scriptures, healing modalities (Reiki, crystals, energy work, Ayurveda, yoga, meditation), and a recurring vocabulary (chakras, vibrations, manifestation, ascension, the law of attraction, the shift). Distinguished from organized New Religious Movements by its non-institutional character; distinguished from older esoteric traditions by its specifically late-20th-c. synthesis.

## Historical phases
1. **Theosophical inheritance** (1875–1949 Bailey period) — Alice Bailey's ascended-master teachings (1919–1949) bridge classical Theosophy and what becomes the New Age proper.
2. **Pre-New-Age channeled material** (1950s–1960s) — Urantia Book (1955); Edgar Cayce material; "I AM" Activity and Church Universal and Triumphant (Saint Germain ascended-master tradition).
3. **Counterculture period** (1965–1980) — Esalen Institute (Big Sur, 1962+); human potential movement; *Whole Earth Catalog*; Eastern-religious imports (TM, Hare Krishna, Zen); *A Course in Miracles* (1976).
4. **Mainstream period** (1980–2000) — Shirley MacLaine's *Out on a Limb* (1983); the "Harmonic Convergence" (1987); Marianne Williamson on Oprah (1992); proliferation.
5. **Online / contemporary period** (2000–present) — *The Secret* (2006); social-media-mediated spirituality; "spiritual but not religious" demographic; the New Age vocabulary becomes mass-cultural background while institutional structures remain weak.

## Defining motifs
- Channeled scripture as a recurring genre.
- Perennialist framing — all religions point to the same ultimate.
- Holistic / mind-body-spirit health.
- Personal manifestation / law of attraction.
- Ascended masters or extraterrestrial intelligences as teachers.
- Eschatological-shift expectations (the New Age proper, the Aquarian Age, the Shift, 2012, ascension).

## Relations to neighbors
- vs. [[tradition-theosophy]]: direct descent through the Bailey lineage and through Theosophical-vocabulary diffusion.
- vs. [[tradition-spiritualism]]: channeling = mediumship in a new theological register.
- vs. mainstream Christianity: variable; some New Age practitioners self-identify as Christian (with metaphysical reinterpretation); others are explicitly post-Christian.
