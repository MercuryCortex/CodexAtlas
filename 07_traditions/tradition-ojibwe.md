# Ojibwe / Anishinaabe Traditional Religion

Stub — to be expanded. The Ojibwe (Anishinaabe) tradition shares the earth-diver cosmogony (Muskrat brings earth from the deep in the Nanabozho cycle), the Midéwiwin (Grand Medicine Society) healing tradition, and the Manitou concept — a pervasive spiritual force in all beings. The Ojibwe earth-diver narrative is one of the clearest parallels to the Haudenosaunee Sky Woman tradition. See [[earth-diver-creation]] for cross-tradition table.
