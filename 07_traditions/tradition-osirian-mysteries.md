# Osirian Mystery Tradition

The pharaonic + later Egyptian institutional tradition of the annual Osirian ritual cycle — death + lamentation + mummification + resurrection of [[osiris]] — performed at [[place-abydos]] and other Osirian cult-centres. The longest-running mystery tradition in the ancient Mediterranean, continuing through Greco-Roman Egypt into Late Antiquity and feeding into the [hellenistic-mystery-religions] (Isis-Sarapis mysteries).
