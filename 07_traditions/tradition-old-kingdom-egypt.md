# Old Kingdom Egypt

The early-centralized phase of ancient Egyptian civilization, c. -2686 to -2181 — the "Pyramid Age" — comprising the 3rd through 6th Dynasties. The era of [djoser-step-pyramid] + the Giza pyramid complex + the Pyramid Texts of Unas + the consolidation of pharaonic religious-political ideology + the Memphite capital. Ends in the First Intermediate Period collapse.
