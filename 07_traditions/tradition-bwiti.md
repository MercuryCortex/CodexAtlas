# Bwiti

**Stub.** Syncretic religion of Gabon and parts of Cameroon/Equatorial Guinea, fusing pre-existing Pygmy/Babongo iboga-initiation practice with Fang ancestor religion and Catholic-mission Christianity. Three principal branches today: *Bwiti Disumba* (older, Mitsogho), *Bwiti Fang* (later 20th century, more Christianized), and *Bwiti Misoko* (healing-focused).

Recognized as one of three official religions of Gabon (alongside Christianity and Islam).
