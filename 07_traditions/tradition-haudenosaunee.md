# Haudenosaunee Religion (Iroquois Confederacy)

## Overview
*Haudenosaunee* ("People of the Longhouse") is the name by which the Iroquois Confederacy calls itself — the political-religious union originally comprising five nations: Seneca, Cayuga, Onondaga, Oneida, and Mohawk (the Tuscarora joined as the sixth nation ~1722 CE). Their cosmogony, theology, and political theology are inseparable: the Great Law of Peace (*Gayanashagowa*) that structures the confederacy is understood as the implementation of Sky Woman's cosmological vision on earth. Haudenosaunee religion is notable for several extraordinary features: a *creation-from-a-fall* narrative (Sky Woman falls from the Sky World onto Turtle's back); an explicit cosmic-dualism structuring the creator-figure (the Good Mind / Evil Mind twins); and the Peacemaker's founding of the oldest democracy continuously in use, which directly influenced the design of the United States government.

## Historical phases
1. **Deep pre-contact period (before ~1450 CE)** — Woodland-period Haudenosaunee occupation of the Northeast; intertribal warfare; the *Mourning War* complex (raids to replace lost members of clan families, driving cycles of revenge).
2. **The Founding (~1450–1600 CE)** — *Deganawidah* (the Peacemaker) and Hiawatha found the Haudenosaunee Confederacy; the *Gayanashagowa* (Great Law of Peace) codified in wampum; *Tadodaho* (Onondaga war chief) is the last convert (his mind and body are literally straightened by the Peacemaker and Hiawatha). Exact date disputed (scholars: 1450–1600 CE; some Haudenosaunee oral tradition: 1090 CE).
3. **Colonial contact and the Covenant Chain (1600s–1763)** — Haudenosaunee serve as key diplomatic actors between French and English colonial powers; the Covenant Chain alliance with English colonies; trade in beaver pelts restructures traditional subsistence patterns.
4. **American Revolution and confederacy fracture (1777–1784)** — the Revolution splits the confederacy; Mohawk (Brant) and most Seneca fight for Britain; Oneida and Tuscarora support Patriots. Sullivan-Clinton campaign 1779 destroys 40+ Haudenosaunee villages. Post-war: most Haudenosaunee lands seized by the new US government in violation of treaty commitments.
5. **Reservation era and persistence (~1784–present)** — Haudenosaunee reservations in upstate New York, Ontario, and Quebec; Longhouse religion (incorporating Handsome Lake's *Gaiwiio* "Good Message" of 1799, a syncretic reform teaching integrating Christian elements into a Haudenosaunee ceremonial framework while reaffirming the traditional council system); the Grand Council of the Haudenosaunee continues to meet at Onondaga.
6. **Land claims and sovereignty (1970s–present)** — Haudenosaunee refuse to accept US or Canadian citizenship; carry their own passports; ongoing land rights litigation over the 1790 Nonintercourse Act.

## Core texts
- [[phase-8-023-gayanashagowa]] — the Great Law of Peace; transmitted in wampum (a form of mnemonic encoding) and oral recitation; compiled in alphabetic form by Seth Newhouse (Mohawk, ~1900), Arthur Parker (Seneca, 1916), William Fenton (1998).
- *Gaiwiio* (Good Message of Handsome Lake, 1799) — syncretic reform teaching; Quaker-influenced but rooted in traditional Haudenosaunee ceremony; the Longhouse religion as it is practiced today.

## Cosmogony — Sky Woman and Turtle Island

The Haudenosaunee creation narrative begins in the **Sky World**, a realm above the earth-waters, populated by sky people. A pregnant woman — *Aataentsic* in Mohawk, Sky Woman in the standard English rendering — falls (or is pushed, or jumps, depending on the telling) through a hole in the Sky World opened by an uprooted tree. Water birds (swans, geese) catch her and lower her onto the back of a great turtle. Muskrat dives to the bottom of the primordial ocean and brings up a pawful of earth, which expands on Turtle's back to become North America — **Turtle Island**.

Sky Woman gives birth to twins:
- **Sapling** (in Onondaga: *Hahgwehdiyu*, the Right-handed Twin) — the Good Mind; creates animals, plants, gentle rivers, and the sun
- **Flint** (in Onondaga: *Hahgwehdaetgah*, the Left-handed Twin) — the Evil Mind; creates thorns, rocks, monsters, winter, and destructive forces

The twins contend throughout creation and eventually in direct combat. Flint is defeated and confined, but not destroyed — the dualism is permanent, not eschatological. Evil/difficulty are not abolished but bounded.

## Pantheon
- [[sky-woman-haudenosaunee]] — the primordial mother who falls from the Sky World; the earth itself is created by her presence
- [[flint-deity]] — the Evil Mind twin; creator of destructive forces in the world; the adversarial aspect of creation
- [[sapling-deity]] — the Good Mind twin; creator of life-sustaining aspects of the world
- [[deganawidah-peacemaker]] — the Peacemaker; semi-divine founder of the confederacy; brings the Great Law
- Handsome Lake (*Sganyadai:yoh*) — 18th-c. prophet; his *Gaiwiio* reform shapes the contemporary Longhouse religion

## Defining doctrines
- **The Good Mind** — the faculty of right-thinking and cooperative intention is itself the sacred and the political; the confederacy is governed by consensus grounded in the Good Mind of its members
- **Sky Woman's cosmological mandate** — the earth was created for life; the purpose of human existence is to maintain the life and beauty that Sapling initiated and that Flint's forces continuously threaten
- **Wampum as covenant** — wampum belts are not decorative but covenant-documents, encoding agreements with mnemonic precision; the Two-Row Wampum (Guswhenta) encodes the principle of parallel sovereignty — two vessels traveling the same river, each steering its own course
- **Condolence** — the *Condolence Ceremony* restores a grieving person or a community to right-thinking (the Good Mind); it is the foundation of the political system (Fenton 1998: condolence is how new chiefs are installed)
- **Women's authority** — clan mothers (*Gantowisas*) hold the authority to appoint and remove chiefs (Mann 2000); the confederacy has institutionalized female political power as a theological-cosmological principle (Sky Woman is the origin of all)

## Relations to neighbors
- vs. [[tradition-lakota]]: both Indigenous North American traditions; structurally distinct (Lakota: vision-quest-centered, bands; Haudenosaunee: confederacy-longhouse, consensus-council).
- vs. [[tradition-christianity-canonical]]: Jesuit missions from 1640s (Jogues, Brébeuf); Mohawk converts (Kateri Tekakwitha, canonized 2012); the Longhouse religion explicitly resists assimilation to Christianity while selectively incorporating elements.
- vs. tradition of Western political philosophy: documented direct influence on the Articles of Confederation and the US Constitution — see MASSIVE WIN section below.

## MASSIVE WIN — The Great Law of Peace and the Birth of American Democracy

**The claim (documented, not speculative):** The Haudenosaunee Confederacy's *Gayanashagowa* (Great Law of Peace) directly influenced Benjamin Franklin, Thomas Jefferson, and the Founders in designing the Articles of Confederation and the Constitution of the United States. This is not a romantic myth — it is documented in primary sources and acknowledged by the US Congress.

### Documentary Evidence

| Document | Content | Implication |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| Benjamin Franklin, "Albany Plan of Union" 1754 | Franklin hosted the Haudenosaunee in Albany 1754; the Albany Plan explicitly references their confederacy model; Franklin wrote: "It would be a strange thing if six nations of ignorant savages should be capable of forming a scheme for such a union and be able to execute it in such a manner that it has subsisted ages and appears indissoluble, and yet that a like union should be impracticable for ten or a dozen English colonies" | Franklin explicitly names the Haudenosaunee Confederacy as the model for the proposed colonial union |
| Thomas Jefferson's letters (1786–1787) | Jefferson's correspondence with John Adams during Constitutional Convention period discusses Iroquois political structure | Founding-era intellectual awareness of Haudenosaunee government design |
| US Senate Concurrent Resolution 76 (1988) | "the confederation of the original thirteen colonies into one republic was explicitly modeled upon the Iroquois Confederacy" — acknowledged by Congress | Congressional acknowledgment of Haudenosaunee political influence |
| Grinde and Johansen (1991) *Exemplar of Liberty* | Comprehensive documentation of Founding Fathers' engagement with Haudenosaunee political theory; T2 tier (accepted mainstream; controversial in degree-of-influence) | Documents the intellectual transmission chain |

### Structural Parallels: Gayanashagowa ↔ US Constitution

| Feature | Gayanashagowa (Great Law) | US Constitution |
|---------|--------------------------|-----------------|
| Federal structure | Confederacy of sovereign nations; local matters handled locally; confederacy handles external relations | Federal system with states' rights |
| Bicameral-equivalent deliberation | Mohawk/Seneca speak first; Oneida/Cayuga deliberate; Onondaga confirms or returns for re-deliberation | Senate + House deliberation structure |
| Checks on power | Clan mothers can remove chiefs who fail their trust | Impeachment; electoral recall |
| Freedom of speech | Protected for delegates in deliberation | First Amendment |
| Freedom of religion | Longhouse spiritual practice not mandated; individuals may follow other paths | First Amendment |
| Women's political role | Constitutive: clan mothers appoint and remove chiefs; the system cannot function without them | Absent from original US Constitution (women's suffrage 1920) |

### Investigative finding
The Haudenosaunee influence on American democracy is the most consequential case in the vault of **Indigenous political theology directly shaping Western political theory**. This is not a transmission of religious metaphysics (unlike the Mediterranean-to-Christianity chain) but a transmission of *political-theological principles* — the idea that governance can be conducted by consensus, that power must be checked by structural accountability, that a confederacy of sovereign units is superior to a unitary state.

**Critical nuance (scholarly responsibility):** The degree of influence is debated. Historians disagree on whether the founding design was primarily Lockean/Montesquieu-influenced or substantially Iroquois-influenced. The Johansen-Grinde thesis (1991) has been challenged by some historians as overstating the case (Levy 1996; Tooker 1988). The 1988 Senate resolution documents awareness and some modeling, not identical copying. Fenton (1998) — the definitive scholarly account of the Great Law — is careful to treat the parallel as suggestive rather than mechanically causal. **Both the parallel and the scholarly debate should be documented.**

## MASSIVE WIN — Creator-Destroyer Dualism: Structural Parallel to Zoroastrian Ahura Mazda / Ahriman

The Sapling-Flint twin structure maps onto the Zoroastrian Ahura Mazda / Ahriman dualism with striking precision:

| Feature | Haudenosaunee (Sapling / Flint) | Zoroastrian (Ahura Mazda / Ahriman) |
|---------|---------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Structure | Twin brothers; one creates good, one creates obstacles | Twin spirits from a primordial choice (Yasna 30); Spenta Mainyu / Angra Mainyu |
| Outcome of creation | Both good and harmful features of the world exist | Both good (righteousness) and evil (lie/destruction) coexist |
| Resolution | Flint defeated but not eliminated; dualism permanent | Ahriman defeated at Frashkard (eschatological renovation) — Zoroastrian resolution is future; present is dualistic |
| Good Mind principle | *Gayanashagowa* is literally the institutionalization of the Good Mind's victory | Asha (truth-order) is the cosmic principle humans must maintain |
| Human agency | Humans must choose the Good Mind and maintain it in deliberation | Humans must choose Asha over Druj through righteous action |

**Investigative finding:** Creator-destroyer dualism independently appears in Haudenosaunee, Zoroastrian, and arguably Vedic (Deva/Asura), Gnostic, and Norse (Æsir/giants) traditions. This is one of the most widely distributed structural patterns in world religion. The Haudenosaunee case is notable because (1) it is entirely independent of the Mediterranean religious sphere, (2) it produces not just cosmogony but institutional political theology, and (3) the Evil Mind twin is *not fully defeated* — a theological sophistication that recognizes the permanent presence of destructive forces in the world.
