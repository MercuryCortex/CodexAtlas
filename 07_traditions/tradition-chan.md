# Chan Buddhism

**This node is an alias.** Chan Buddhism (*Chán* 禪) is the Chinese form of what became Japanese Zen, Korean Seon, and Vietnamese Thiền. See the canonical node: **[[tradition-zen]]** for the full treatment.

Chan originated in Tang dynasty China (~6th–8th c. CE) through the synthesis of Mahāyāna Buddhism with Daoist sensibility, especially the direct-pointing, anti-verbal, spontaneous style of classical Daoism. The Platform Sutra of the Sixth Patriarch Huineng (~780 CE) is its foundational text. Bodhidharma is its legendary founding ancestor.
