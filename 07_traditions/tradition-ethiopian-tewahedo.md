# Tradition Ethiopian Tewahedo

Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church — the Miaphysite Christian tradition of Ethiopia. Preserves the most extensive Old Testament canon (including 1 Enoch and Jubilees in their complete form). Anchored architecturally at [[lalibela-rock-hewn-churches|Lalibela]] and the Church of Our Lady Mary of Zion at Aksum.
