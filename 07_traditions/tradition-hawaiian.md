# Hawaiian Religion (pre-Christian Kanaka Maoli)

## Overview
The religious tradition of the Kanaka Maoli (Native Hawaiian) people. Settled by Polynesian voyagers (likely from the Marquesas and Society Islands ~400–1200 CE — the dating has been substantially refined and shortened by recent archaeology), Hawaiian religion developed a distinctive variant of broader Polynesian religion over the centuries of relative isolation before re-contact (the second-wave Tahitian arrivals ~1200–1300, and ultimately European contact 1778 — Cook). Distinctive features: the four principal *akua* (Kāne, Kū, Lono, Kanaloa) organized in cosmological-cultic functions; an extensive *kapu* (sacred-prohibition) system regulating all of social life; the Kumulipo cosmogonic-genealogical chant; the distinct Pele volcanic-divine complex; the *Makahiki* annual four-month peace-festival of Lono; *luakini heiau* (war temples) of Kū with associated human sacrifice.

## Historical phases
1. **Settlement (~400–1200 CE)** — Polynesian voyaging; first wave Marquesas-origin; second wave Society Islands.
2. **Pre-contact florescence (~1200–1778)** — development of distinctive Hawaiian religion; ahupuaʻa land-management; major heiau construction; chiefly competition.
3. **Cook contact and Kamehameha unification (1778–1819)** — Cook arrives at Kealakekua Bay 1778, killed 1779 (the famous Lono-identification debated); Kamehameha I unifies the islands ~1795–1810.
4. **Abolition of the *kapu* system (1819)** — under Kamehameha II, influenced by Queen Kaʻahumanu and Keōpūolani, the formal *kapu* system is overturned in a single dramatic act (the *ʻAi Noa* — free-eating). Kū's *luakini heiau* are burned.
5. **Christianization (~1820+)** — American Calvinist missionaries arrive 1820; rapid (though uneven) Christianization through 19th c.
6. **Overthrow and annexation (1893, 1898)** — Queen Liliʻuokalani overthrown by US-backed coup 1893; US annexation 1898. Liliʻuokalani's 1895–98 Kumulipo translation in prison is a sovereignty act.
7. **20th- and 21st-c. revitalization** — Hawaiian Renaissance from 1970s; Hawaiian sovereignty movements; Kumulipo and pre-Christian religion as living tradition for many Native Hawaiians.

## MASSIVE WIN — The Kumulipo Creation Chant: Evolutionary Sequence Before Darwin

**The claim (investigative, well-documented):** The *Kumulipo* ("source of deep darkness" / "the beginning in deep night") is a 2,102-line Hawaiian creation chant of extraordinary sophistication, composed ~18th century or earlier, transmitted orally among the royal ali'i (chiefly) class. Its cosmogonic sequence proceeds from the simplest sea organisms upward through progressively complex life forms in a temporal-developmental sequence — a sequence that 19th-century scholars immediately noted parallels the outline of evolutionary biology, composed centuries before Darwin's *On the Origin of Species* (1859).

**The sequential structure of the Kumulipo (Beckwith 1951 translation):**

| Kumulipo Stage | Content | Biological parallels noted |
|----------------|---------|--------------------------|
| Era of darkness (*Pō*) | The first era; night; primal waters; no light yet | Abiotic oceanic pre-life state |
| First era: coral polyp (*pōhaku*) | The coral polyp (*ko'a*) is the first being named; the coral worm follows | Marine invertebrates: sessile organisms; earliest complex multicellular life |
| Subsequent sections | Sea urchin, starfish, sea cucumber; then fish; then land animals; then plants; then birds | Progression from marine invertebrates → fish → terrestrial fauna → flora → avifauna |
| Final eras | Mammals; then the demigod Māui-type figures; then the first human (Wākea / chief-ancestor) | Primates → human |

**Beckwith's finding (1951):** "The progress from the lowest forms of sea life to the highest forms of land life corresponds in a general way to the modern theory of evolution" (*The Kumulipo*, Beckwith 1951 p. 21). She notes this with scholarly caution — the chant is not proto-Darwinian biology but a cosmogonic genealogy (*whakapapa* in Māori terms; *mo'okū'auhau* in Hawaiian) in which each era gives birth to the next.

**What the comparison reveals:**
The *Kumulipo* reaches the same large-scale picture as evolutionary biology — complexity increases over time; simpler life precedes more complex life; humans are the last-arrived — through a completely different intellectual process: genealogical chanting that traces the descent of the royal line back to the first beings at the creation of the world. The sequence is arrived at through a combination of systematic observation of the natural world (Hawaiian fishermen, farmers, bird-catchers had deep empirical knowledge of species) and theological genealogical logic (the most sacred and complex beings must be descended from the simplest, just as all chiefs are descended from the first chief, who is descended from the gods).

**Scholarly caution (required by methodology):** This is a *structural parallel*, not a prediction. The Kumulipo was not trying to describe biological evolution. It was mapping royal genealogy onto the cosmogonic sequence. The congruence with evolutionary biology is real and remarkable, but its explanation is most likely the combination of (1) careful empirical observation of Hawaiian ecology, (2) the logical structure of genealogical cosmogony (descent from simple to complex), and (3) the fact that evolutionary biology was itself discovering what was in some sense already observable in nature. That multiple traditions independently noticed this sequence (simple → complex, marine → terrestrial → human) may itself be the investigative finding.

**Cross-tradition parallel:** Comparable cosmogonic-evolutionary sequences appear in:
- **Vedic tradition**: the *Purusha Sukta* (Rig Veda 10.90) describes creation from the dismemberment of a primal being; the sequence of creation proceeds in levels
- **Daoist**: *Huainanzi* (~139 BCE) describes the emergence of the ten thousand things from the undifferentiated (*hundun*) through successive stages
- **Orphic**: the *Orphic Rhapsodies* describe successive world-ages; early Orphic cosmogony generates complexity from a primordial egg
None of these are biological sequences in the Kumulipo's specificity, which makes the Kumulipo distinctively notable.

## MASSIVE WIN — The *kapu* Abolition of 1819: Religion Dismantling Itself from Within

In 1819 — one year before American Calvinist missionaries arrived — the Hawaiian *kapu* system was abolished by King Kamehameha II (*'Ai Noa*, the Free-Eating), under the influence of Queen Ka'ahumanu and the queen-dowager Keōpūolani. The luakini heiau (war-god temples of Kū) were burned. This was an internally generated religious revolution — the religious system dismantled its own most oppressive structure before external pressure to do so.

**Investigative significance:** The 1819 *'Ai Noa* event is an extraordinary case study in indigenous religious self-transformation. It is structurally analogous to:
- The Protestant Reformation (internal Christian critique of the institutional church's abuses)
- Buddhist reform movements (e.g., Won Buddhism, Donghak) that stripped back ritual accretion to recover a simpler core
The difference: the Hawaiian case was not a reformation of the tradition to preserve it but a political-theological decision to remove the *kapu* restrictions governing gender separation in eating (women and men could not eat together; women could not eat certain sacred foods like pork, coconut, bananas) — restrictions that disproportionately constrained women in the ruling class.
