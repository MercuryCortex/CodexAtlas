# Christian Kabbalah

## Overview
A Christian engagement with Jewish Kabbalistic literature and categories, founded in 1486 by Giovanni Pico della Mirandola with the assistance of his converso Jewish teacher Flavius Mithridates, systematized in 1517 by Johannes Reuchlin, integrated into Renaissance magic by Cornelius Agrippa, and continued through the 17th- and 18th-century Christian-Hebraist tradition. Christian Kabbalists read the Sefirot as compatible with — or even as proof of — Trinitarian Christianity; they took the divine name YHWH and showed (by Kabbalistic letter-permutation) that the inserted letter shin generated YHShVH / Yeshua / Jesus; they treated Kabbalistic exegesis as Mosaic oral tradition that, properly understood, confirms Christ. The tradition is theologically distinct from the Jewish Kabbalah it claims to inherit; Jewish Kabbalists then and now regard it as a confessional appropriation.

## Historical phases
1. **Founding by Pico** (1486–1494) — 900 Conclusions, the *Heptaplus*, the Mithridates translations.
2. **Reuchlin systematization** (1494–1522) — *De Verbo Mirifico*, *De Arte Cabalistica*, *De Rudimentis Hebraicis*; the Reuchlin Affair galvanizes German humanism.
3. **Agrippa integration into Renaissance magic** (1510–1533) — *De Occulta Philosophia* Book III.
4. **16th-c. Catholic Kabbalism** — Egidio of Viterbo, Guillaume Postel, Francesco Giorgi (Venetian Franciscan, *De Harmonia Mundi* 1525).
5. **17th-c. systematic Kabbalism** — Knorr von Rosenroth's *Kabbala Denudata* (1677–1684) is the major reference work, drawing on Cordoveran and Lurianic materials; Henry More and the Cambridge Platonists engage Kabbalah.
6. **18th-c. integration into esoteric and Masonic streams** — Kabbalistic letters and Sefirot enter higher-degree Masonic and Rosicrucian initiatory schemata.
7. **19th-c. occult revival** — Eliphas Lévi, the Golden Dawn, and modern Western occult Kabbalah descend from this stream.

## Defining doctrines
- The Sefirot as compatible with the Trinity (Christianizing identifications of Keter/Father, Tiferet/Son, etc.).
- The Tetragrammaton-Pentagrammaton (YHWH → YHShVH = Yeshua).
- Kabbalah as Mosaic oral tradition preserved by the Jews and reaching its proper fulfillment in Christianity.
- Kabbalistic letter-mysticism, gematria, notarikon, temurah operationalized within a Christian framework.

## Relations to neighbors
- vs. [[tradition-kabbalah]]: claimed continuity; actual partial discontinuity (the Christological moves are unsupported in Jewish tradition).
- vs. [[tradition-renaissance-hermeticism]]: deeply intertwined; most Christian Kabbalists are also Hermetists.
- vs. [[tradition-rosicrucianism]]: direct influence; Rosicrucian materials draw on Christian-Kabbalist tropes.
- vs. [[tradition-christianity-canonical]]: doctrinally tolerated by some Catholic authorities (Pico eventually rehabilitated, Reuchlin defended); regarded with suspicion by others.
