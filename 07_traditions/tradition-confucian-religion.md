# Confucianism

The Chinese tradition organized around the teaching of Confucius (Kǒng Fūzǐ, -551 to -479) and the canonical literature (the Four Books + Five Classics). Provided the official ideology of imperial China, Joseon Korea, dynastic Vietnam, and Tokugawa Japan. The imperial examination system (605–1905 CE) was conducted entirely in [[language-classical-chinese|Classical Chinese]] on the Confucian canon — one of the most extensive state-cult / scholastic institutions in human history.
