# Shingon — Japanese Esoteric Buddhism

The principal Japanese esoteric Buddhist school, founded by **Kūkai (Kōbō Daishi, 774-835)** after his 804-806 study at Qinglong-si in Chang'an under the Chinese tantric master Huiguo. Kūkai brought back the Mahāvairocana Tantra and Vajraśekhara Tantra transmissions, established the headquarters at **Mount Kōya** (816), and systematized the *Three Mysteries* (body / speech / mind) practice. Shingon is the Japanese-tantric parallel to Tibetan Vajrayāna — both descend from late Indian tantric Buddhism via different mediation routes (Tibet via Padmasambhava + Atiśa; Japan via Tang Chinese transmission).
