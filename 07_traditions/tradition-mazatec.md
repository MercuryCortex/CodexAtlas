# Mazatec tradition

**Stub.** Indigenous people of the Sierra Mazateca highlands in northeastern Oaxaca, Mexico (c. 300,000 speakers of Mazatec languages). Internationally known for the continuously-practiced *velada* — the all-night vigil-ceremony of communal psilocybin-mushroom consumption presided over by a curandero/a (most famously María Sabina, c. 1894-1985, of Huautla de Jiménez).

The 1955 R. Gordon Wasson velada with María Sabina — published in *Life Magazine* 1957 — introduced the practice to global Western awareness, with destructive consequences for the Mazatec community.
