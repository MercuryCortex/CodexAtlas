# Thelema

## Overview
Religion-philosophy founded on the 1904 reception of *Liber AL vel Legis* (the Book of the Law) by Aleister Crowley in Cairo. Core principle: each individual has a True Will (Greek *thelema* = will), the discovery and execution of which is the central spiritual task. "Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law / Love is the law, love under will." Central institutions: A∴A∴ (Astrum Argentum / Argenteum Astrum, founded by Crowley with George Cecil Jones 1907), Ordo Templi Orientis (OTO, originally founded by Theodor Reuss; Crowley took over 1912 and remade as a Thelemic vehicle).

## Historical phases
1. **Reception period** (1904–1909) — *Liber AL* dictation (April 1904); years of inactivity; "rediscovery" 1909.
2. **Active Crowleyan period** (1909–1947) — *Equinox* periodical; institutional founding; Abbey of Thelema, Cefalù, Sicily (1920–1923); the major Crowley publications (*Magick*, *Book of Thoth*).
3. **Post-Crowley period** (1947 onward) — succession through Karl Germer, Grady McMurtry, others; current OTO under various national branches; the Caliphate OTO (Hymenaeus Beta) is the largest international branch.

## Defining doctrines
- The New Aeon of Horus (replacing the Aeon of Osiris).
- True Will as the central spiritual task.
- "Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel" as the goal of the magickal path.
- Sex-magick in the OTO-affiliated higher degrees.

## Relations to neighbors
- vs. [[tradition-wicca-modern-pagan]]: Gardner met Crowley 1947; documented Wiccan ritual borrowings from Crowley (Aidan Kelly 1991); the two traditions are distinct but historically linked at their founding moments.
- vs. LaVeyan Satanism: LaVey acknowledged Crowley as a major influence; the Satanic Bible's Enochian Keys are sourced via Crowley.
