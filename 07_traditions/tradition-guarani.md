# Guaraní religion

**Stub.** The pre-Columbian and continuing religious tradition of the Guaraní peoples (multiple sub-groups: Mbyá, Avá-Chiripá, Pãĩ-Tavyterã, Kaiowá, others) of the Paraná basin — Paraguay, northern Argentina, southern Brazil, eastern Bolivia. Central concepts: Ñanderu (the creator-father), Tupã (thunder-being), ka'a (yerba mate as foundational gift), the Land-Without-Evil (*yvy maraney*) eschatology that motivated centuries of migration searches.

The Jesuit reductions (1609-1767) preserved and transformed Guaraní religion through sustained mission contact; the Spanish-Portuguese suppression of the Jesuits in 1767 broke the reduction system but did not erase the underlying religious tradition.
