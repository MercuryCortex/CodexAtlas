# Meroitic-Nubian Religion

The religious tradition of the Kingdoms of Napata + Meroë in Nubia (Sudan), c. -800 to +350 CE. Heavily Egyptianized in pantheon + iconography (Amun, Isis, Horus all received Meroitic worship) while preserving distinctly Nubian elements (the lion-god Apedemak, royal-burial pyramid traditions at Nuri + Meroë). The Meroitic kingdom maintained close ties with the Isis cult at Philae into the Roman period.
