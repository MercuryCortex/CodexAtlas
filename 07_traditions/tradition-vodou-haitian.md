# Haitian Vodou

## Overview
The religion that emerged in colonial Saint-Domingue (now Haiti) in the late 17th–18th centuries among enslaved Africans, principally from Dahomey-Fon, Yoruba, and Kongo religious backgrounds, blended with French Catholic devotional practice. Vodou is structured around the *lwa* (spirits) organized in *nasyon* (lineages — principally Rada, Petwo, Ghede); the *Bondye* (high god) is transcendent; humans communicate with the *lwa* through possession-trance, drumming, song-cycles, *vèvè* (ground-drawings), and ritual offerings. Each *lwa* has a Catholic-saint syncretic pairing; the syncretism is theologically genuine, not merely operational camouflage.

## Historical phases
1. **Emergence (~1700–1791)** — Vodou develops in Saint-Domingue's plantation context as enslaved Africans of multiple ethnicities synthesize religious traditions under Catholic-colonial conditions.
2. **Bois Caïman (August 14, 1791)** — the ceremony led by [[dutty-boukman]] and Cécile Fatiman that ritually inaugurated the Haitian Revolution.
3. **Revolutionary period (1791–1804)** — Vodou as constitutive religious infrastructure of the Haitian Revolution.
4. **Post-independence (1804–present)** — Vodou in tension with the Haitian state and Catholic Church; periodic anti-superstition campaigns (notably 1941–42); 1860 Concordat with Rome; formal state recognition 2003.
5. **Ethnographic-academic documentation (~1920 onward)** — Jean Price-Mars *Ainsi parla l'oncle* (1928); Maya Deren *Divine Horsemen* (1953); Alfred Métraux *Le Vaudou haïtien* (1959); current scholarly baseline (Brown, McAlister, Cosentino, Desmangles).

## Pantheon
- [[papa-legba]] — crossroads, opener-of-the-gate
- [[erzulie]] — Freda (love) and Dantor (fierce mother)
- [[damballa]] — primordial sky-serpent creator
- [[baron-samedi]] — death, cemetery, head of the Ghede
- Ogou family (warrior); Agwé (sea); Ezili Freda / Dantor; many others
- *Bondye* — transcendent high god

## Defining doctrines
- Layered cosmology: Bondye (transcendent) → lwa (mediators) → humans
- Possession-trance as religious-communication technology
- Rada (cool) / Petwo (hot) nasyon bifurcation
- Genuine Catholic-saint syncretism
- *Konesans* (esoteric knowledge) graded by initiation

## Relations to neighbors
- vs. [[tradition-yoruba-ifa]]: Rada *lwa* are recognizably Yoruba-Dahomean orishas; direct transmission.
- vs. [[tradition-santeria-lucumi]]: Sister Caribbean African-diasporic tradition; different substrate proportions (Vodou: Fon-Yoruba-Kongo; Santería: predominantly Yoruba).
- vs. [[tradition-christianity-canonical]]: Catholic saint-pairings are theologically internalized in Vodou; ongoing complex Catholic-Vodou syncretism.
