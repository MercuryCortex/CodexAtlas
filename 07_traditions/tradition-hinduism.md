# Hinduism — the Sanātana Dharma family

**Hinduism** is not a single religion but a family of related religious traditions indigenous to the Indian subcontinent — the world's oldest surviving religious complex, with roots in the Vedic civilization (~1500 BCE onward) and continuous development to the present. The term "Hinduism" is a 19th-century Western coinage; the tradition itself uses *Sanātana Dharma* ("eternal dharma") as a self-designation.
