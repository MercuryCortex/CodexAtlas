# Santo Daime

**Stub.** Brazilian syncretic church founded c. 1930 by the Afro-Brazilian rubber tapper Raimundo Irineu Serra ("Mestre Irineu") in Brasiléia, Acre. Combines Amazonian ayahuasca ritual with Catholic devotion, Kardecist spiritism, and Afro-Brazilian elements. The ritual *trabalhos* (works) involve communal ingestion of *daime* (ayahuasca), hymn-singing, and bailado (ritual dance).
