# Native American Church (NAC)

**Stub.** Pan-tribal American religious movement founded 1918 in Oklahoma combining peyote ritual with Christian framing. The "Peyote Way" coalesced in the late 19th century from earlier southern-plains practice (Comanche, Kiowa, Apache) and spread north through the central and northern plains 1880-1930. Two principal ritual forms: the Half-Moon Way (Quanah Parker / Comanche-Kiowa lineage) and the Cross Fire Way (John Wilson / Caddo lineage, more explicitly Christian).

Approximately 300,000 members across Canada, the US, and northern Mexico today. Federally protected sacramental peyote use under the American Indian Religious Freedom Act Amendments of 1994 (after *Employment Division v. Smith*, 494 U.S. 872 (1990) denied constitutional protection).
