# Orphism (Orphic mysteries)

## Overview
**Orphism** is a Greek religious-philosophical current claiming descent from the legendary singer-poet **Orpheus** (whose voice could move stones and tame beasts, who descended to the underworld for Eurydice, and who was torn apart by Maenads). Active from the late Archaic period (~-600) through Late Antiquity, Orphism is characterized by: (a) **anthropogonic-theogonic** myths centered on **Dionysus-Zagreus** — the child-Dionysus dismembered by the Titans, from whose ash humanity arises with a dual nature; (b) **soul-immortality and reincarnation** doctrine — soul is divine, body is *sēma* (tomb), and the soul transmigrates through successive lives until purification permits return to its divine origin; (c) **dietary and ritual purification** — vegetarianism, abstention from eggs and beans, ritual purifications; (d) **soteriology by initiation and right ritual** — the gold tablets instruct the soul on how to navigate post-mortem judgment.

**Note**: modern scholarship (Edmonds 2013) increasingly questions whether there was a single "Orphism" — the term may cover a family of related but distinct currents and texts.

## Historical phases
1. **Archaic emergence** (~-600/-500): traditions attached to the name Orpheus circulate; the doctrine of soul-transmigration emerges concurrently with Pythagoreanism (with significant overlap and mutual influence).
2. **Classical period** (~-500/-300): Plato (*Phaedo*, *Phaedrus*, *Republic X*) engages Orphic-Pythagorean ideas extensively; the Derveni Papyrus (~-340) shows a sophisticated allegorical reading of Orphic theogony.
3. **Hellenistic and early Roman period** (~-300/+200): gold tablets distributed across the Greek world; Orphic poems compiled; Bacchic-Orphic ritual associations (*thiasoi*) active.
4. **Late Antique synthesis** (~+200/+400): Neoplatonists (Plotinus, Porphyry, Iamblichus, Proclus) treat Orphic texts as sacred-philosophical authority; Christian apologists alternately ridicule and appropriate.

## Core texts
- **Derveni Papyrus** (~-340) — found in a Macedonian tomb in 1962; commentary on an Orphic Zeus-and-Phanes theogony.
- **Orphic gold tablets** (~-400/-200) — 40+ small inscribed gold leaves buried with initiates; instructions on naming oneself "child of Earth and starry Heaven" before the underworld guardians.
- **Orphic Hymns** (87 hexameter hymns) — preserved in Byzantine manuscripts; late Hellenistic / early imperial composition.
- **Orphic Rhapsodies** (lost compilation; reconstructed via Neoplatonic citations).
- **Plato's Orphic-Pythagorean references** in *Phaedo*, *Cratylus*, *Republic*, *Timaeus*.

## Pantheon
- [[dionysus-mystery]] / [[zagreus]] — the dismembered child-god; the divine element in humanity.
- [[orpheus]] — legendary singer-founder; sometimes treated as quasi-divine.
- [[persephone-greek]] — queen of the underworld; addressed in gold tablets.
- [[phanes]] / Protogonos / Ericapaeus — first-born of cosmic Time and Necessity in Orphic theogony.

## Defining doctrines
- **Body as tomb (*sōma-sēma*)** — soul is divine, body imprisoned (Plato *Cratylus* 400c attributes this to Orphics).
- **Original sin via Titanic ash** — humanity bears guilt for the murder of the divine child; ritual purification is required for liberation.
- **Metempsychosis / reincarnation** — soul transmigrates through bodies until purified.
- **Salvation by initiation + ascesis** — initiates know the password to the underworld and follow purification practices (vegetarianism).
- **Dualism of divine soul vs. material body** — anticipating Platonic dualism and (less directly) Christian-Gnostic dualism.

## Relations to neighbors
- **vs. [[tradition-pythagorean]]**: substantial overlap in reincarnation, vegetarianism, ascesis; mutually influencing; Pythagoreans and Orphics share Magna Graecia milieu.
- **vs. [[tradition-mystery-cults]]**: Orphism is a *mystery-cult subtype*; overlap with Eleusinian and Bacchic mysteries is strong.
- **vs. [[tradition-greek-religion]]**: in tension with civic religion (mocked by Plato's contemporaries as fringe), but not opposed.
- **vs. [[tradition-hellenistic-philosophy]]**: Plato's debt is acknowledged; Neoplatonism integrates Orphism as sacred-philosophical text.
- **vs. [[tradition-gnosticism]]**: structural parallels (body-as-prison, divine spark in matter, salvation by gnosis-and-ascesis) make Orphism a *direct precursor pattern* for later Gnostic systems.
