DEV — THE WORKSHOP one editor at a time
VIEW · LEGEND · ✦ FOLIO STAY CANONICAL — NEVER IN HERE
  • One door. FX, STYLE, LAB and ⌗ leave the bar; the ✦-menu Dev item moves here too. One DEV button remains, in the right cluster where the four used to sit. A gold dot on it means something dev is open somewhere — a stray panel is always traceable to the bar.
  • The launcher is a cockpit remote, not a menu. It stays open while you flip between panels (switching is one click), and closing it does not close an open panel. Click-outside or Esc closes the launcher only.
  • One editor at a time. LAB, FX and STYLE share one fixed slot (right edge, above the bar — the LAB's spot today); opening one closes the others. STATS is exempt: a read-only HUD may sit beside an editor — watching fps while dragging a slider is the whole point — and its PIN mode survives as-is. OVERVIEW is a full surface; opening it closes the launcher.
  • Panels persist until closed. Every dev panel gets the LAB idiom — an × and Esc, no close-on-outside-click. A tuning panel that vanishes when you click the canvas to test the effect is a broken tuning panel (FX and STYLE lose that behaviour, deliberately).
  • Nothing else moves. The four panel modules keep their code, their dials, their LS keys (forge.fxParams.v4 · forge.styleParams.v1 · forge.labRecipe.v1 · forge.viewSettings.v7) and the ?lab boot flag. The launcher rows simply become the old trigger buttons (same ids), so the modules never notice the move.
  • VIEW and LEGEND are canonical, and defended. VIEW is user settings (layers, themes, distribution). LEGEND is the map key and the source-tier filter — it changes what the map claims (T1–T5, political-risk), not how pixels are tuned. Dev = dials that tune the rendering; canonical = controls that change meaning. Same ruling as the ground colours: "these are not dev panel."
