RECIPE
  • The tree is generational, not genealogy-only. Lineage arcs (parent-of / child-of, 478+253 in the vault) are the bones — but they cover 14–86% of a family and Chinese has zero. Every deity carries date_earliest (100% coverage), so every rank is an era: the parented hang from their parents, the unparented stand on their age, grouped by domain. No family can break the layout.
  • Isolate is state, not navigation. Same dots, two resting positions — wheel and house — one position tween between them, then stillness. Rest is still: nothing here animates at rest. Back is click-empty-space or Escape, and it is instant.
  • The Atlas stays present. Every wire that leaves the family lands on a horizon port at that tradition's true bearing on the wheel. Hover a deity to see its wires; hover a port to see everything flowing to that tradition; click a port to travel — family to family, without ever going home first.
  • Multi-parent is drawn, not pruned. The data is a DAG (34 of 80 Greek deities have 2+ parents). One parent anchors placement; every other lineage arc still draws. Consorts are lateral — a bar, never a rank. Avatars and aspects hang dotted beneath their greater form.
  • One label law. Every word on the stage — crown, era dates, deity names, ports, shelf captions — is placed by one priority-ordered pass with one collision list; a word that would land on another hides instead of overlapping (the app's renderLabelsCanvas law, mirrored). Names draw whole with a dark halo, or not at all — no mid-word … anywhere.
  • The rails are shelves, not lists. The Scriptorium groups its documents into era shelves, the Court groups by kind — each shelf a mono caption with a count and one spine name (its highest-degree member). The rest of the mass stays visible as glyphs; titles arrive when the pointer approaches the rail, and the hovercard always carries the full record.
  • Cascade and Fan are peers. Both are view modes in waiting — the same house re-projected, tweened live when you flip the dial. Neither is the fallback: the recipe records whichever John dials, and the engine spec treats them as two entries in the same layout vocabulary the wheel and timeline already share.
