The family toy: the wheel first — the well-shaped logic always comes first. Hover lifts a hull, click dives, the breadcrumb returns.

  • Hairline, never blob. 1px contour at ~25% + 4% fill. The hull is a whisper of territory, not a colored puddle.
  • Labels live on the hull edge, replacing the floating ring of tradition names once you're close enough to see hulls.
  • Isolate is state, not navigation. Same Forge engine, same instanced nodes — a camera fit plus a dim-others flag plus a ground tint. Back is one click, and it's instant.
How it ships

Rest is still — that's the law and the budget. No halo, no motion in the field; only the handful of nodes near the cursor run glow + glass. Aliveness and performance are the same decision.

The recipe is the spec. Whatever you dial here becomes the exact uniform values in the engine shader — bubble scale, zooms, refraction, frost, glow, pulse, finish mix. Copy the line, and that's the handoff.

Two tiers of refraction. The engine starts with the cheap in-shader bend (ring + own glow warp through the bubble — no new pass) and graduates to true scene refraction as the flagship FX — tested carefully on Safari first.
