The Eye — play with it

hover a family = the hull lifts · click = dive in
◂ THE EYE brings you back

The toy you asked for. Families sit in the wheel — the well-shaped logic always comes first. Hover one and its hull lifts (hairline + a breath of fill, label on the edge). Click and the camera dives: siblings recede, the ground tints toward the family. One breadcrumb returns.

  • Hairline, never blob. 1px contour at ~25% + 4% fill. The hull is a whisper of territory, not a colored puddle.
  • Labels live on the hull edge, replacing the floating ring of tradition names once you're close enough to see hulls.
  • Isolate is state, not navigation. Same Forge engine, same instanced nodes — a camera fit plus a dim-others flag plus a ground tint. Back is one click, and it's instant.
How it ships

The ring is canon; light is dress. Dew, Living Ring and Bloom are all in-fragment dress on the existing instanced disks — no new pass, no framebuffer reads, Safari-first.

Minimal toggle = LOD floor = same dial. Dress level is one uniform: the zoom ladder lowers it automatically at small radii, the Minimal toggle pins it to zero. One axis, no forked renderer.

Bloom is a state, not a style. The field stays quiet; only the selected node blooms. Restraint is what keeps this classy at 3,000 nodes.
