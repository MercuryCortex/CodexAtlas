The Eye

hover a family = the hull lifts · click = dive in
◂ THE EYE brings you back

The family toy, unchanged: the wheel first — the well-shaped logic always comes first. Hover lifts a hull, click dives, the breadcrumb returns.

  • Hairline, never blob. 1px contour at ~25% + 4% fill. The hull is a whisper of territory, not a colored puddle.
  • Labels live on the hull edge, replacing the floating ring of tradition names once you're close enough to see hulls.
  • Isolate is state, not navigation. Same Forge engine, same instanced nodes — a camera fit plus a dim-others flag plus a ground tint. Back is one click, and it's instant.
How it ships

Rest is still — that's the law and the budget. A thousand calm rings cost nothing; only the handful of nodes near the cursor ever run the glass. Aliveness and performance are the same decision.

Two tiers of refraction. This toy fakes tier two: the engine version starts with the cheap in-shader bend (the ring and its own glow warp through the bubble — no new pass), and graduates to true scene refraction (photograph the frame, bend it through the lens) as the flagship FX — tested carefully on Safari first.

Finishes ride the wake. Chromatic edge, sheen, iridescence — all exist only while a node is awake or chosen. The resting field never wears them.
