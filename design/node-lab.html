Hulls & the Eye

isolate = a camera move, not a new page

Families get a hull: hairline contour, a breath of fill, the label sitting on the hull. Click a hull and the camera dives — everything else recedes, the ground tints toward the family, and one breadcrumb takes you back to the Eye.

1 · The Eye — full wheel; hover lifts a hull
2 · The Dive — hull expands, siblings recede
3 · Family Field — ground tints; ◂ breadcrumb returns
  • Hairline, never blob. 1px contour at ~25% + 4% fill. The hull is a whisper of territory, not a colored puddle.
  • Labels live on the hull edge, replacing the floating ring of tradition names once you're close enough to see hulls.
  • Isolate is state, not navigation. Same Forge engine, same instanced nodes — a camera fit plus a dim-others flag plus a ground tint. Back is one click, and it's instant.
How it ships

Same instanced disks, same single pass. Every treatment here is dress inside the existing node fragment shader — no framebuffer reads, no extra draw calls, Safari-first.

Minimal toggle = LOD floor = same dial. Dress level is one uniform: the zoom ladder lowers it automatically at small radii, the Minimal toggle pins it to zero. One axis, no forked renderer.

Starglass is a state, not a style. The field stays quiet; only lock/selection earns the halo. Restraint is what keeps the hero look classy at 3,000 nodes.
