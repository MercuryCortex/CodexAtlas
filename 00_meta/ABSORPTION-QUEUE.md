## Tier-1 wedges (highest leverage)

These are documented missing pieces with explicit source guidance. Most are seeded from [`AUDIT/05_priority_queue.md`](../AUDIT/05_priority_queue.md). Pick any.

- [ ] **Hittite / Hurrian layer** — Kumarbi Cycle, Telipinu Myth, Illuyanka Myth + 6 deity stubs (Teshub, Hebat, Sun Goddess of Arinna, Telipinu, Kumarbi, Illuyanka). The structural hinge between Mesopotamia and Greek; Kumarbi → Hesiod succession-myth is documented. **Sources:** Hoffner, *Hittite Myths* (SBL, 1998, T1); Bachvarova, *From Hittite to Homer* (Cambridge UP, 2016, T1); Bryce, *The Kingdom of the Hittites* (Oxford UP, 2005, T1). **~30–50 edges.**
- [ ] **Apostolic Fathers corpus** — Didache, 1 Clement, Ignatius's seven letters (one node, seven sub-sections), Shepherd of Hermas, Epistle of Barnabas, Polycarp's Letter to the Philippians, Martyrdom of Polycarp, 2 Clement. The 90-year textual void between Paul and Justin Martyr. **Sources:** Ehrman, *The Apostolic Fathers* (Loeb, 2003, T1); Holmes, *The Apostolic Fathers in English* (Baker, 2007, T2). **~40+ edges.**
- [ ] **Chinese textual canon (Phase 2 expansion)** — Yi Jing (Zhou layer + Ten Wings), Zhuangzi (Inner Chapters), Mengzi, Xunzi, Mozi, Han Feizi, Shijing, Shujing. Eight nodes. **Sources:** R.J. Smith *Fathoming the Cosmos* (UVA 2008, T2), Legge *I Ching* (1882, T3 PD), Graham *Chuang-Tzu* (Hackett 1981, T2), Ziporyn *Zhuangzi* (Hackett 2009, T2), Van Norden *Mengzi* (Hackett 2008, T2), Hutton *Xunzi* (Princeton 2014, T1), Johnston *Mozi* (Columbia 2010, T1), Watson *Han Fei Tzu* (Columbia 1964, T2), Nylan *Five "Confucian" Classics* (Yale 2001, T1). **~50+ edges.**
- [ ] **East Asian Mahāyāna sūtras** — Lotus Sūtra, Avataṃsaka Sūtra, Laṅkāvatāra Sūtra, Larger Sukhāvatīvyūha (Pure Land). **Sources:** Watson *Lotus Sutra* (Columbia 1993, T1), Cleary *Flower Ornament Scripture* (Shambhala 1993, T1), Suzuki *Laṅkāvatāra* (1932 PD, T1), Gómez *Land of Bliss* (U Hawaii 1996, T1).
- [ ] **Bhagavad Gītā as standalone + Bhāgavata Purāṇa** — currently folded into Mahābhārata; the most-cited Hindu text in the West deserves its own node. Bhāgavata defines a thousand years of bhakti Vaishnavism. **Sources:** Patton *Bhagavad Gita* (Penguin 2008, T2); Zaehner *The Bhagavad-Gītā* (Oxford 1969, T1); Bryant *Krishna* (Penguin 2003, T1).
- [ ] **Josephus** — *Jewish Antiquities*, *Jewish War*, *Against Apion*, *Vita*. Irreplaceable primary for Second Temple period. **Sources:** Loeb Classical Library (T1); Mason *Josephus and the New Testament* (Hendrickson 2003, T2).
- [ ] **Memphite Theology / Shabaka Stone** — creation-by-word doctrine. The single most important Egyptian theological document for the Logos chain. **Sources:** Allen *Genesis in Egypt: The Philosophy of Ancient Egyptian Creation Accounts* (Yale Egyptological Studies 1988, T1); Hornung *Conceptions of God in Ancient Egypt* (Cornell UP 1982, T1).
- [ ] **Karbala (680 CE) event + Twelver Shia structural nodes** — Husayn is dead-linked from `tradition-shia-islam`. **Sources:** Mahmoud Ayoub *Redemptive Suffering in Islam* (Mouton 1978, T1); Madelung *The Succession to Muhammad* (Cambridge UP 1997, T1).
- [ ] **Linear B decipherment + Mycenaean Greek pantheon** — Bronze-Age pushback for the entire Greek timeline. Names Zeus, Hera, Poseidon, Hermes, Athena, Artemis on tablets c. 1200 BCE. **Sources:** Ventris & Chadwick *Documents in Mycenaean Greek* (Cambridge UP 1956, T1); Palaima *The Mycenaean Religion* (in Davies & Schofield eds. *The Mycenaean Greek*, 1991, T2).

## Tier-2 wedges (substantial returns)

- [ ] **Targums + early rabbinic Aramaic** — Memra theology, the Logos-equivalent that's the bridge between Hellenistic Judaism and John's Logos. Onqelos, Pseudo-Jonathan, Neofiti.
- [ ] **Mishnah + Tosefta** — currently a structural gap between Tanakh and Talmud. Tractates that anchor early rabbinic worldview.
- [ ] **Greek Magical Papyri (PGM)** — Hellenistic syncretism in primary form. Bilingual incantations, divine-name catalogs, theurgic recipes.
- [ ] **Chaldean Oracles** + theurgy texts (Iamblichus *De Mysteriis*) — Neoplatonic ritual theology; the bridge from philosophy to ritual.
- [ ] **Pistis Sophia + Books of Jeu** — late Gnostic Coptic corpus; not in Nag Hammadi.
- [ ] **Manichaean primary sources** — Kephalaia, Cologne Mani-Codex, Iranian Manichaean fragments. The world's first deliberately global religion.
- [ ] **Mandaean texts** — Ginza Rabba, Book of John. The only surviving Gnostic tradition.
- [ ] **Tantric Hindu corpus** — selected Tantras (Kularnava, Mahanirvana), Vijñāna Bhairava, Spanda Karikas.
- [ ] **Pali Abhidhamma + key suttas** — beyond the obvious (Dhammapada is in vault); Visuddhimagga, key Majjhima Nikāya suttas.
- [ ] **Tibetan tantric core** — Guhyasamāja, Hevajra, Kālacakra root tantras; *Bardo Thödol* (Tibetan Book of the Dead).
- [ ] **Mesoamerican primary texts** — Popol Vuh (Maya), Florentine Codex (Aztec), Chilam Balam books, Codex Borgia.
- [ ] **Andean primary texts** — Huarochirí Manuscript (only surviving pre-conquest Quechua corpus), Guaman Poma *Nueva Corónica*.
- [ ] **African cosmological corpora** — Yoruba Ifá literary corpus (256 Odù), Dogon mythology (per Griaule, with critical apparatus), Kuba origin myths.
