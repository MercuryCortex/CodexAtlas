## Quality bar reminder

Exemplars to match (per the brief): `demiurge`, `chaoskampf`, `tzimtzum`. The bar:

1. Full YAML frontmatter including `category`, `appearances`, `deity-instances`, and `refs` (at least 2, ideally Tier 1 + Tier 2).
2. A `traditions-where-attested` list (or equivalent named-tradition coverage in the body — currently most older themes use the body-bullet form rather than the YAML key from `tzimtzum`'s shape).
3. A definition paragraph plus a bulleted **Manifestations** / **Earliest attestations** / **Variants** section linking out to documents, deities, traditions.
4. A `Disputes` section where relevant.

## Tier C — Near-duplicates and consolidation candidates

These are not "bugs" per se — they often capture genuine sub-distinctions — but agents are creating links to the wrong slug. **Recommended action: add explicit cross-references and disambiguation paragraphs rather than merge, except where flagged MERGE.**

- **`final-judgment` ↔ `end-times-judgment`** — MERGE. `end-times-judgment` is more developed (57 lines, 3 refs, full disputes section). `final-judgment` lists the same documents with fewer refs. **Recommendation:** keep `end-times-judgment` as canonical (it's longer and richer), redirect `final-judgment` to it via stub-redirect note, update incoming links. Both should explicitly distinguish from `afterlife-judgment`.
- **`divine-kingship` ↔ `sacred-king`** — KEEP BOTH, sharpen. `sacred-king` (Cambridge-ritualist-flavored, dying-rising king ritual) and `divine-kingship` (institutional king-as-god / king-as-elected) capture different scholarly traditions. Currently both are too short to display the distinction. Both should explicitly link to the other in their opening paragraph.
- **`predestination` ↔ `predestination-protestant`** — KEEP BOTH (one general, one Reformation-specific). Make `predestination-protestant` declare itself a child of `predestination`.
- **`gentile-inclusion` ↔ `gentile-mission`** — KEEP BOTH (inclusion is theological status, mission is practice). Cross-link.
- **`logos-cosmic-reason` / `logos-philonic` / `logos-johannine`** — KEEP all three (Stoic, Philonic, Johannine are genuinely distinct usages). Add a parent `logos` index or strengthen the cross-references at top of each.
- **`dualism-cosmic` / `dualism-spirit-matter` / `ethical-dualism` / `light-and-darkness-dualism`** — KEEP all four (taxonomically distinct: Zoroastrian-cosmic, Gnostic-spirit-matter, Qumran-ethical, Manichaean/Iranian-light-dark). Add a parent `dualism-typology` cross-reference matrix.
- **`apocalyptic-revelation` / `apocalyptic-tradition` (deadlink) / `eschatological-imminence` / `realized-eschatology`** — `apocalyptic-tradition` is a *dead link* (2 refs incoming from Phase 8). Should resolve to `apocalyptic-revelation` either by stubbing or by adding it as an alias. Consider: `apocalyptic-revelation` is currently the closest fit.
- **`mystery-initiation` ↔ `initiation`** — KEEP BOTH (the first specifically the Greco-Roman mystery cults; the second a broader category).
- **`messianism` ↔ `messianic-secret`** — KEEP BOTH (different beasts; messianic-secret is specifically Wrede's reading of Mark).
