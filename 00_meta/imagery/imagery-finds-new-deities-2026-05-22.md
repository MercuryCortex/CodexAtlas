# New deities encountered during 2026-05-22 imagery sweep

These deities appeared in Wikidata search results / Wikipedia sitelinks /
Met-Museum object metadata while I was hunting for images for vault entries.
They are NOT currently in the vault. Listed here for future content batches
to consider.

Format: name — tradition — source surfaced from — one-line description.

## High-confidence "should be added" (multi-language coverage, museum hits)

- **Ame-no-Hiwashi (天日鷲神)** — Shinto — Wikidata Q10939697 — Japanese
  kami of weaving/feathers; collided with my Ame-no-Hohi search because
  the romanizations are similar. Has zh + ko sitelinks.

- **Munakata Sanjojin (宗像三女神)** — Shinto — Wikidata Q10948069 —
  the three-sister kami collective (Tagori-hime, Tagitsu-hime,
  Ichikishima-hime as a unit). Articles in ja/zh/ko. The vault has the
  three individuals separately but no collective node.

- **Takiribime / Tagori-hime alt-name** — Wikidata uses 多紀理毘売命
  romanized as Takiribime, not Tagori-hime. Worth a name-alias in the
  Tagori-hime node.

## Inca/Andean candidates worth investigating

- **Pachacámac (deity not place)** — surfaced when searching Viracocha;
  Q-id distinct from Pachacámac the archaeological site. Sometimes
  conflated with Viracocha but distinct creator-deity in coastal Andean.
  The vault has Pachacámac the site, not the deity. Verify if we
  conflate.

## Pre-Islamic Arabian pantheon (more antediluvian-list deities)

The Quranic Sūrat Nūḥ 71:23 lists FIVE antediluvian idols: Wadd,
Suwāʿ, Yaghūth, Yaʿūq, Nasr. The vault has Suwāʿ, Yaghūth, Yaʿūq.
Missing:

- **Wadd (وَدّ)** — Pre-Islamic Arabian (Banu Kalb tribe) — Q1142989 —
  god of love and friendship; serpent iconography.
- **Nasr (نسر)** — Pre-Islamic Arabian (Himyarite South Arabia) —
  Q3338762 — vulture/eagle deity. South-Arabian sites have actual
  Nasr-vulture reliefs.

## Etruscan pantheon gaps

While searching Nethuns I noticed the vault has Menvra, Nethuns but
NOT:

- **Usil** — Etruscan sun deity (paired with Nethuns in the bronze
  mirror engraving I used) — Wikipedia article exists in eswiki, dewiki,
  itwiki.
- **Thesan** — Etruscan dawn goddess (third of the triad) — same mirror.
- **Tinia** — chief Etruscan sky god (Zeus-equivalent).
- **Voltumna / Veltha** — Etruscan supreme god of the federation.
