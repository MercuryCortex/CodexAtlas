> **`opus-forge-pack-scale-fix` — root cause of "tiny dots on load + after resize" found and fixed — FINISHED 2026-05-19.** The bug class that survived Option B, the convention-flip, and the cache-bust diagnostics turned out to be a **camScale-at-pack-time invariant violation**. `packNodes` (in `src/js/engine/graph/node.js:126-131`) bakes the world radius using the camera scale at pack time so the on-screen-px clamp can be evaluated. Two places were violating this: (1) `rebuildForMode` called `packNodes` BEFORE `fitToExtent`, so the first pack used the camera's default scale=1.0 while the camera then shrank to the actual fit scale (~0.4 for a 663-node deities wheel in a wide viewport) — every disk drew at the wrong on-screen size; (2) `resizeAndFit` (sizeChanged branch) re-fit the camera and rebaked edges + focus state, but never called `rebakeNodes()` — so after every resize the cached wrong-scale radii survived. The `camera.onChange` listener has a 5%-drift guard that fires `rebakeNodes()` automatically, which is why John saw "snap to correct after first cursor move" — coincidence of his gesture crossing the threshold, not by design. Three states explained: **State 1 (load)** = first pack at scale=1.0, drawn at scale=0.4 → tiny; **State 2 (cursor move)** = some user gesture eventually crosses 5% drift → corrective `rebakeNodes()` fires → correct; **State 3 (resize)** = re-fit changes scale again but rebake hook may not trip → wrong-scale radii survive → tiny again. His "snaps when I resize smaller" observation = small resizes that *also* happen to cross 5% drift get the corrective rebake by coincidence. Two surgical edits in `src/js/views/forge.js`: move the `ext` computation + `camera.fitToExtent` call to BEFORE `packNodes` in `rebuildForMode`; add `rebakeNodes()` to the sizeChanged branch of `resizeAndFit`.
