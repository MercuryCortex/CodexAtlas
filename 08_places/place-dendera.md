# Dendera

Upper Egyptian city + principal cult-centre of Hathor; the Greco-Roman-period Temple of Hathor (begun under Ptolemy XII, continued through Augustus and into the early imperial era) is one of the best-preserved late-Egyptian temples, famous for its astronomical ceiling-reliefs (the "Dendera zodiac," now in the Louvre).
