# Sarnath

Site near Varanasi, India where the Buddha gave his First Sermon ("Setting the Wheel of Dharma in Motion"). Principal early Buddhist pilgrimage site; the [[ashoka|Ashokan]] Lion Capital (now national emblem of India) was found here.
