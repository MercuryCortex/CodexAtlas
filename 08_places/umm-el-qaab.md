# Umm el-Qaab

The royal necropolis of the **First Dynasty (and Predynastic) Egyptian kings** at [[place-abydos]] — the burial place of Narmer, Aha, Djer, Den, and the other early-dynastic founders of unified Egypt, plus the late-Predynastic tomb U-j (c. -3250, source of the world's earliest preserved Egyptian writing). The site name ("Mother of Pots") comes from the millions of votive pottery offerings later pilgrims deposited at the (re-identified) tomb of Djer, which the Late Period identified as the tomb of Osiris himself.
