# Philae

Nile island near Aswan at the Egypt–Nubia border, principal cult-centre of Isis from the Late Period through the closing of the temple by Justinian I in +537. Relocated in 1980 to nearby Agilkia Island to escape the Aswan-High-Dam flooding. The temple complex itself is treated in [[philae-temple-isis]].
