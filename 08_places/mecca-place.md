# Mecca Place

Mecca — city in the Hejaz region of western Saudi Arabia; birthplace of [[muhammad-prophet|Muhammad]]; the holiest city of Islam, the direction of Muslim prayer (qibla = [[kaaba|the Kaaba]]); annual Hajj pilgrimage destination.
