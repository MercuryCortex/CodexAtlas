# Coptos (Qift)

Ancient Egyptian Upper-Egyptian city + principal cult-centre of the ithyphallic fertility god [[min-egyptian|Min]]. The terminus of the Wadi Hammamat caravan route from the Red Sea, making it a key node in the Egyptian–Punt + later Egyptian–Indian Ocean trade networks. Site of the famous Min colossal statues (Petrie 1896, now Ashmolean).
