# Memphis (Egypt)

Ancient capital of unified Egypt from the Early Dynastic period (Narmer, c. -3100) into the New Kingdom — the political-religious-administrative hub of pharaonic Egypt and principal cult-centre of the creator god [[ptah]]. Saqqara serves as Memphis's necropolis; the Step Pyramid of Djoser + the Memphite-Saqqara royal pyramid field is the burial landscape of Memphite kingship.
