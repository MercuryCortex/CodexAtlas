# Bindu

## Identity
The **Bindu** (Sanskrit: बिन्दु — 'drop,' 'dot,' 'point') is the **primordial zero-dimensional point** — the absolute origin from which all creation emanates in Hindu cosmological and Tantric thought. It is at once:

1. **The cosmological source**: the undivided unity of Shiva (consciousness) and Shakti (energy) before their separation into the manifest, polarized universe — the pre-creative state that contains all potentiality
2. **The geometric foundation**: the dimensionless point that is the origin of all extension, all form, all number — a mathematical limit-point that generates space by expanding into line, plane, and solid
3. **The ritual locus**: the central point of every yantra (sacred geometric diagram), including the Sri Yantra, where it is the seat of the goddess and the most sacred location in the diagram
4. **The sonic mark**: the *anusvāra* dot above Sanskrit letters, representing the nasal resonance (*nāda*) that vibrates after a sound — the written form of cosmic vibration
5. **The body-mark**: the *tilaka* or *bindi* worn between the eyebrows — the physical inscription of the cosmic point on the human face at the Ajna chakra location

The Bindu is paradoxical: it is nothing (zero dimensions, no extension) and everything (the source of all that exists). It corresponds precisely to what the Neoplatonists call the 'One' and what the Kabbalists call the 'primordial point' (*nekudah ha-rishonah*) of the divine emanation.

## Earliest attestation
- **c. −400 BCE** — Pāṇini's *Aṣṭādhyāyī* uses *bindu* as a grammatical term for the *anusvāra* nasal resonance mark — the earliest systematic attestation of the term in Sanskrit.
- **c. 6th-9th c. CE** — The Bindu as a cosmological-metaphysical concept is attested in the Kaula and Kashmir Shaiva Tantric texts: *Kaulajñānanirṇaya* (c. 9th-10th c.), *Spanda-kārikā* (9th c.), Abhinavagupta's *Tantrāloka* (c. 1000 CE).
- **c. 16th c. CE** — Pūrṇānanda's *Ṣaṭcakranirūpaṇa* synthesizes the chakra + Bindu in yogic physiology. *Saundarya-laharī* (attributed to Śaṅkarācārya, c. 820 CE in its written form) gives the Bindu a central role in Śrīvidyā worship.

## Meaning(s) attested

**In Tantric cosmology** (Kashmir Shaivism, Śrīvidyā Tantrism):
- The Bindu is the **moment before the first movement** — the state of *anuttara* (the Absolute) at the instant before it stirs into creative vibration. It is described as *śiva-śakti-sāmarasya* — the 'equal taste' or non-dual bliss of Shiva and Shakti undivided.
- The Bindu's first movement (*spanda* — the 'throb' or 'vibration' of consciousness) produces **Nāda** (cosmic sound), which in turn differentiates into **Kalā** (creative energy / form). This Bindu → Nāda → Kalā sequence is the Tantric account of cosmic emanation.
- In the body, the Bindu is also the **seminal / vital drop** (*śukra-bindu* or *ama-kalā*) — the vital fluid that the yogi seeks to conserve and sublimate upward through the chakra system, transforming gross physical energy into refined spiritual energy. This is the Tantric 'physiology of the Bindu.'

**In Yantra worship (Śrīvidyā)**:
- The central Bindu of the Sri Yantra is the **dwelling place of the goddess Lalitā Tripurasundarī** — the supreme Shakti. To meditate on the Sri Yantra is to converge through all nine layers of triangles into the Bindu and dissolve into the goddess.
- The Bindu as **laya-point**: all the yantra's geometry radiates from the Bindu and all meditation returns to it — it is the point of *laya* (dissolution) into pure consciousness.

**In Sanskrit grammar**:
- The *anusvāra* (*bindu*) is the dot that marks the **resonance after a sacred syllable** — the vibration of OM that lingers when the voiced sound stops. The grammar's Bindu IS the cosmic vibration rendered as a written mark: the visible form of the Nāda.

## Cross-family appearance

**The Kabbalistic Primordial Point**: In Lurianic Kabbalah (Isaac Luria, Safed, c. 1570 CE), before creation God performs *tzimtzum* — divine self-contraction — withdrawing into himself and leaving a void (*ḥalal*). He then emits a thin ray of divine light (*qav*) into the void, which forms the first point (*nekudah ha-rishonah*) from which the *sefirot* emanate. This is the **exact structural form of the Bindu**: a dimensionless point of divine self-manifestation from which all creation flows. The sequence *Ein Sof → Tzimtzum → Nekudah → Sefirot* maps with remarkable precision onto *Anuttara → Spanda → Bindu → Nāda → Kalā → Manifest Universe*. Independent development in two distinct cultural lineages; the structural parallel-form is among the most precise cross-tradition mathematical-cosmological convergences in the vault.

**The Neoplatonic One**: Plotinus's 'One' (τὸ Ἕν, *Enneads* III-IV, 3rd c. CE) is the ultimate source prior to all differentiation — it has no attributes (it is beyond Being), no parts (it is simple), and yet it is the source of everything. The One → Intellect (*Nous*) → Soul (*Psychē*) → Matter emanation sequence maps onto Bindu → Nāda → Kalā → manifest universe. Both systems posit a dimensionless origin that generates all extension by overflowing into lower levels of reality. Plotinus uses mathematical point-imagery explicitly: the One is like the geometric point from which all lines and planes emanate.

**John Dee's Monas**: The *Monas Hieroglyphica* (1564) opens with Theorem I: the point is the origin of all geometric extension. Dee draws on Proclus's commentary on Euclid. The Monas-glyph is explicitly constructed from the point outward — a Bindu-logic embedded in Renaissance Hermetic mathematics.

**The Cosmological Singularity (structural parallel)**: The Big Bang singularity at t=0 — the mathematical point of infinite density and zero volume from which all space, time, and matter emerged — is the most precise modern scientific formal parallel to the Bindu concept. Both are dimensionless mathematical limit-points from which all physical extension emanates. Documented as a structural parallel, not as an identity claim: the physics (General Relativity / quantum gravity) is distinct from the metaphysics (Tantric consciousness-cosmology).

**Zen Ensō (structural)**: The Zen *ensō* ([[enso]]) begins with a single point of brush-pressure — the moment before the circle begins. The Bindu and the *ensō*'s initiating point share the same structure: the primordial creative instant before manifestation, the punctual origin of the circular form. The Zen practice of *ensō* painting instantiates the Bindu-principle in calligraphic action.

## Contested interpretations

- **The Bindu in Tantric physiology as literal seminal fluid vs. subtle energy**: Some Tantric texts treat the Bindu as literally the *śukra* (semen / vital fluid), which the male yogi conserves through *vajrolī mudrā* and other retention techniques. This physiological/literal reading is documented in the older Kaula texts. Later commentators and modern interpreters tend to sublimate this into a purely energetic-cosmological reading.
- **The anusvāra-as-bindu grammatical term**: Whether the grammatical *bindu* and the cosmological Bindu are related etymologically or through a parallel metaphorical extension is a question in Sanskrit intellectual history. Most scholars treat them as related through the shared 'dot/drop' meaning.
- **The Big Bang parallel**: The Bindu-singularity parallel is a powerful structural observation but requires careful tier-disciplined handling — the physics does not validate the metaphysics, and the metaphysics does not predict the physics.
