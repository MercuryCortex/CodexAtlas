# Khvarenah

The Iranian theological concept of the **divine kingly glory** or **royal fortune** — a luminous numinous power that legitimates kingship + accompanies the righteous. The principal Avestan source is the *Zamyād Yašt* (Yašt 19), which narrates the Khvarenah's flight from the apostate king Yima + its successive embodiment in various legitimate sovereigns. Continuous Iranian concept from Avesta through Sasanian royal-iconography (the Khvarenah-halo) into Islamic-Persian kingship-theory (*farr*). One of the principal templates for the eschatological + royal-legitimation theology of Persian-influenced regions.
