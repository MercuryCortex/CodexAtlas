# Dendera Zodiac

Carved ceiling-relief from a chapel of the Greco-Roman Temple of Hathor at Dendera, c. -50 to -30 (the Late Ptolemaic to early Roman period). Now displayed in the Louvre (removed in 1820 by Sébastien Saulnier). Depicts the full **Babylonian-Hellenistic 12-sign zodiac** + the **Egyptian 36 decans** in a single integrated cosmological diagram — the canonical visual document of the Late-Ptolemaic Egyptian-Babylonian-Greek astronomical synthesis.
