# Pyramid / Triangle

## The form

The **triangle** and the **pyramid** are the same cosmological symbol in two and three dimensions: a base on the earth and an apex pointing toward the sky. This is not an Egyptian invention — it is a universal geometric fact given theological meaning in every tradition that encountered it — but Egypt gave it its most imposing physical form, and the Western tradition received the symbol primarily through the Egyptian channel.

## Egyptian theology of the pyramid form

The **pyramidion** (*benbenet*) — the small pyramid at the apex of the monument — was the part of the pyramid that caught the first light of sunrise. It was gilded or made of electrum (gold-silver alloy). The pyramid's entire form is a **petrified sunbeam**: the sun's rays spread downward from a point in the sky to the earth in exactly the triangular shape of the pyramid. The Great Pyramid's angle (~51°50') is close to the angle of the sun's rays at the latitude of Giza during key seasons.

The pyramid encodes the theological direction:
- **Upward** = ascending from earth to sky = the dead pharaoh's *ba* (soul) rising to rejoin Osiris/Orion or to merge with Re
- **The base** = the horizontal world of the living, the Nile, human time
- **The apex** = the fixed, eternal, solar-divine world above time

This is the spatial theology documented in the **[[theme-pyramid-as-resurrection-machine]]** framework.

## Pythagorean and Neoplatonic triangle

The **equilateral triangle** (all sides equal; all angles 60°) is in Pythagorean and Neoplatonic philosophy the **first perfect polygon** — generated from three points in space, embodying the triad. Trinitarian thinking organized around three-part cosmic structure (heaven/earth/underworld; fire/air/water; body/soul/spirit) finds its natural geometric emblem here.

**Plato's *Timaeus*** uses triangles as the building blocks of the four elements: fire = tetrahedron (four equilateral triangles), earth = cube, etc. The triangle is the **atom of cosmic geometry**.

## The Christian Trinity equilateral triangle

The equilateral triangle enters Christian iconography as the emblem of the **Holy Trinity** — three equal sides representing three equal Persons. Combined with the **Eye of Providence** (divine omniscience), the eye-in-triangle becomes the standard Western Christian depiction of God from approximately the 16th century onward:
- Pontormo's *Supper at Emmaus* (1525) — early attestation
- Widely standardized in Catholic and Protestant devotional art through the 17th century
- The "radiant triangle" (triangle surrounded by rays, sometimes with internal eye) is effectively the visual summary of Christian Trinitarian theology

This Christian iconography is the proximate source of the Masonic All-Seeing Eye — not a direct Egyptian connection but a chain through Christian art.

## Alchemical triangles

In Western alchemical symbolism (from medieval Arabic alchemy through Paracelsus):
- **Upward-pointing triangle** (△) = **Fire** / Spirit / the ascending, volatile, solar principle
- **Downward-pointing triangle** (▽) = **Water** / Matter / the descending, fixed, lunar principle
- **Superimposed** = the **hexagram** (Star of David, Seal of Solomon) — the union of opposites

This makes the pyramid/triangle the primary alchemical emblem of spiritual ascent — the upward triangle is the soul's movement toward the divine, toward the volatile, toward fire. Every alchemical transmutation is, in this geometry, a pyramid: base metal at the base, gold/spirit at the apex.

## The Masonic pyramid and the US Great Seal

The **reverse of the US Great Seal** (designed 1782 by Charles Thomson and William Barton; printed on dollar bills since 1935) shows an **unfinished pyramid** of 13 courses (representing the 13 original states) with a **radiant eye** above a disconnected capstone and the motto *Annuit Coeptis* ("He has favored our undertakings").

The **unfinished state** is theologically significant: the pyramid without its capstone represents **human work awaiting divine completion** — the republic still being built, the Temple (in Masonic reading) still under construction. The Masonic source for this reading draws on:
- The unfinished state of Solomon's Temple as a Masonic ritual touchstone
- 18th-century Egyptophilia (Freemasonry explicitly connected its origins to Egyptian mystery traditions via writers like William Stukeley and Athanasius Kircher)
- The Eye of Providence from the Christian Trinity tradition

Whether the Founding Fathers who approved the design intended Masonic symbolism is debated — but the iconographic chain (Egyptian pyramid form + Christian Providence eye + Masonic unfinished-work theology) is real regardless of individual intent.
