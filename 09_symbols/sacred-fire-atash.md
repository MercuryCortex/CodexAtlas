# Sacred Fire (Ataš / Atar)

## Identity
The **sacred fire** (Avestan *Ātar*, Pahlavi-Persian *Ātaš* or *Adur*) is **the central cult-object of Zoroastrian religion** — the **visible-divine-presence** at the heart of every Zoroastrian temple, the focus of every Zoroastrian prayer, and **the longest-continuously-maintained-religious-substance in the history of human religious practice**. The current oldest continuously-burning sacred fire is the **Atash Bahram of Udwada in India** (the principal Iranshah fire, brought to India by Parsi refugees fleeing the Arab Muslim conquest of Iran ~+716, traditionally never extinguished since). The **Atash Bahram of Yazd in Iran** is reported to have been continuously maintained since the late Sasanian period (~+470). For the Codex Atlas mission the sacred fire is the **iconographic-and-ritual-cult-object of an entire religion** — distinct from but theologically continuous with the personified deity [[atar]]. The fire **is not worshipped** (a persistent outsider mis-characterization); Zoroastrians **worship Ahura Mazda facing the sacred fire**, which is the **visible-divine-presence facilitating that worship**.

## Earliest attestation
- **Gathas** ([[phase-2-002-gathas-of-zarathustra]]): Yasna 31.3, 31.19, 34.4, 43.4, 43.9 — fire as cosmic-religious principle and ritual focus.
- **Younger Avesta** ([[phase-2-004-yasna-younger-avesta]]): extensive. **Yasna 62 (the Atash Niyāyišn)** — the principal liturgical hymn to the sacred fire, recited daily.
- **Achaemenid period (-550 to -330)**: rock-relief depictions of the king praying before sacred fire (Naqsh-e Rustam royal-tomb reliefs); fire-altars on Achaemenid coinage.
- **Parthian-Sasanian period**: institutionalization of the **three-grade-fire system** (Atash Bahram, Atash Adaran, Atash Dadgah); the **Atash Bahram of Iranshah** is traditionally consecrated.
- **Modern Parsi-Iranian community**: 9 operative Atash Bahrams (8 India + 1 Iran), plus many lower-grade temple-fires.

## Meaning(s) attested

- **Visible-divine-presence**. The fire is the *seen* aspect of the divine cosmic order; Zoroastrians pray *facing* the fire but *to* Ahura Mazda.
- **Cosmic purification**. Fire purifies; all major Zoroastrian ritual involves fire. The river-of-molten-metal at the end of time (see [[frashokereti-cosmic-renovation]]) is the cosmic-scale fire-purification.
- **The five sacred-fire elements in nature**: Zoroastrian theology identifies fire in **five forms** — the visible temple-fire; the lightning-fire; the digestive-fire of the body; the cosmic-fire (the sun); and the divine-spark of Ahura Mazda. The temple-fire is the principal cult-form of this broader cosmic-divine fire.
- **Indo-Iranian cult-continuity**: the sacred-fire cult is shared between Zoroastrian Atar and Vedic Agni — both inherited from Proto-Indo-Iranian period.

## Cross-family appearance

### Indo-Iranian: Atar ↔ Agni
The **single cleanest Indo-Iranian shared-cult parallel**: both Zoroastrian Atar and Vedic Agni are personified-divine-fire central to temple-and-household cult. The cognate-cult-practice is one of the most-load-bearing pieces of Proto-Indo-Iranian religious-reconstruction. (See [[atar]] for the deity-level treatment.)

### Eternal-light traditions in other religions
- **Jewish *ner tamid*** (eternal-light commandment, Exodus 27:20-21): the institutionalization of the perpetual sanctuary-lamp is **post-exilic, in the Persian period** — the period of maximum Zoroastrian religious-cultural influence on emerging Second-Temple Judaism (see [[event-cyrus-conquest-of-babylon-539-bce]], [[saoshyant]]). Whether the *ner tamid* is **directly influenced** by Zoroastrian sacred-fire-cult is debated; the structural parallel + chronological coincidence is real. Boyce 1991 leans yes-influence.
- **Christian altar-light + vigil lamp + sanctuary lamp**: structural parallels with Zoroastrian fire-temple practice; direct genealogical influence unprovable, structural resonance real.
- **Greek-Roman hearth-fire** (Vesta, Hestia, state-prytaneion fires): Indo-European broadly, not Iranian-specific transmission. Independent development from shared Indo-European hearth-cult substrate.
- **Olympic torch / Olympic-flame**: modern symbol with classical-Greek-prytaneion-fire roots; structural cognate.

### Modern Western reception
- **Eternal flames at memorials** (the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier in many countries; the Kennedy Eternal Flame at Arlington; the Atatürk Mausoleum eternal flame): modern Western secular-religious eternal-flame culture has multi-rooted ancestry including Persian-Zoroastrian, Greek-Roman, and broader Near Eastern fire-cult.

## Contested interpretations
- **Fire-worship mis-characterization**: persistent outsider mis-reading. Zoroastrians do **NOT** worship fire — they worship Ahura Mazda facing the fire. The mis-characterization persists in Greek, Roman, medieval Islamic, and modern Western outsider-views. Zoroastrian apologists have consistently rejected it.
- **Continuous-combustion claims**: the Atash Bahram of Udwada (India) reports continuous combustion since ~+716 (the arrival of Parsi refugees in India); the Yazd Atash Bahram reports continuous since ~+470. Some of the institutional-continuity claims are partly folkloric (record-keeping across centuries of priestly succession is imperfect), but the **practice of careful continuous maintenance** is real and the chains are mostly documentable.
- **Three-grade-fire system origin**: Sasanian-period institutionalization is documented; pre-Sasanian forms are less clear.

## Mystery / unknown variants
None — the sacred-fire cult is well-documented. `mystery-status: documented`.
