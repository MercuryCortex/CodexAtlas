# Sudarshana Chakra (Vishnu's Spinning Discus)

## Identity
The **Sudarshana Chakra** (Sanskrit: *su-darśana* — "auspicious vision"; *cakra* — wheel, disc, circle) is the **spinning discus-weapon** of **[[vishnu|Vishnu]]** — held perpetually on the tip of his upper right index finger, ready to be hurled at any enemy of cosmic order (*dharma*). It is the **most important of Vishnu's four primary attributes** (*āyudhas*): the Chakra (discus), the [[shankha-conch|Shankha]] (conch), the [[lotus|Padma]] (lotus), and the Gada (mace).

The Sudarshana Chakra encodes **Vishnu's central theological paradox**: the *preserving* god of the Trimurti preserves cosmic order by *destroying evil*. The most devastating spinning weapon in the Hindu cosmological arsenal belongs to the deity whose function is preservation.

## The Returning Divine Weapon: A Cross-Tradition Pattern

The Sudarshana Chakra belongs to a specific cross-tradition pattern: the **returning divine weapon** — a weapon of cosmic order that is hurled at enemies, destroys chaos-forces or monsters, and returns to the defender. This is one of the most robustly-documented **Proto-Indo-European (PIE) divine patterns**:

| Deity | Tradition | Weapon | Returns? | Function |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[[vishnu|Vishnu/Krishna]]** | Hindu / Vaishnava | Sudarshana Chakra (spinning discus) | Yes | Destroys *asuras* and *adharma* |
| **Thor** | Norse | **[[mjolnir|Mjolnir]]** (war hammer) | Yes | Destroys Jötnar (giants of chaos) |
| **Indra** | Vedic Hindu | **[[vajra-dorje|Vajra]]** (thunderbolt club) | Yes | Destroys Vritra (the cosmic obstruction) |
| **Perun** | Slavic | Axe / thunderbolt | Yes | Destroys Veles (the chthonic serpent) |
| **Zeus/Jupiter** | Greek/Roman | Thunderbolt (*keraunos*) | — | Destroys Titans and monsters |

The PIE *Donnergott* — the Thunder God with the returning divine weapon — is the most widely attested of all reconstructed PIE divine figures. Sudarshana + Mjolnir + Vajra are three points in the same PIE constellation.

## The Wheel of Ezekiel: The Jewish Cosmic Wheel

**Ezekiel 1** (the *Merkavah* vision — "the chariot") describes a vision of the divine throne-chariot with:

> *"...wheels within wheels... their rims were tall and awesome, and the rims of all four were full of eyes all around... a wheel on the earth beside each living creature..."* (Ezekiel 1:15-21)

These spinning wheels — the *ophanim* (אוֹפַנִּים — "wheels" in Hebrew) — are fire-beings, later elaborated in Jewish angelology as the **Ophanim** (the third-highest choir of angels: wheels of fire surrounding the divine throne). In Christian angelology they became the lowest choir of the nine angelic orders.

The **Sudarshana Chakra** (spinning cosmic wheel of divine order) and the **Ophanim/Wheels of Ezekiel** (spinning cosmic wheels of divine fire surrounding God's throne) are structural parallels: **spinning wheels of fire encoding cosmic divine order**, wielded or constituted by divine power. Both arise independently in Indic and Semitic contexts from the same deep intuition: the cosmic disc/wheel is the form of divine sovereignty in motion.

## Vishnu's Four Attributes as Theological Set
The four *āyudhas* of [[vishnu|Vishnu]] constitute a complete theological vocabulary of divine action:
- **Sudarshana Chakra** (spinning discus): *Destruction of evil* — the active protective force.
- **[[shankha-conch|Shankha]]** (conch shell): *Proclamation of dharma* — the cosmic sound that sustains sacred time; also primordial (*Om* arises from the conch's sound).
- **[[lotus|Padma]]** (lotus): *Liberation and purity* — the lotus emerging from muddy waters = the soul's emergence from *samsara*.
- **Gada** (mace/club): *Material force and authority* — the instrument of royal-temporal power, Vishnu as cosmic sovereign.

The four attributes together span the full range of divine function: proclamation + liberation + material authority + destructive protection.

## The Sudarshana Yantra
The **Sudarshana Yantra** is the geometric embodiment of the Chakra's power — used in South Indian Sri Vaishnava temples for the *Sudarshana Homam* (fire ritual of protection). Its concentric structure of flames, blades, lotus petals, and geometric figures converging on a central *bindu* makes it the Vaishnava equivalent of the [[sri-yantra|Sri Yantra]] — the supreme geometric encoding of a divine principle in material form.

## Cross-Symbol Connections
- ↔ [[dharmachakra]] — *parallel-form*. Weapon-form vs. law-form of the cosmic spinning wheel; both encode dharma through the *cakra* image.
- ↔ [[shankha-conch]] — *co-appears-with*. Paired primary attributes of Vishnu in the standard iconographic formula.
- ↔ [[sri-yantra]] — *parallel-form*. Sudarshana Yantra = Vaishnava equivalent of the Sri Yantra; both supreme geometric encodings of a divine principle.
- ↔ [[mjolnir]] — *parallel-form*. Proto-Indo-European returning divine weapon pattern: cosmic-order deity's weapon that destroys chaos and returns.
- ↔ [[vajra-dorje]] — *parallel-form*. Indra's Vajra and Vishnu's Chakra are both Vedic divine weapons of cosmic order in the PIE thunder-god pattern.
