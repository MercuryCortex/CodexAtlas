# Salabhanjika Yakshi

Indian sculptural motif of a tree-nymph (*yakshi*) embracing a tree; iconographic ancestor of the *tribhanga* female pose in Indian art. Canonical early example: the East Torana of [[sanchi-great-stupa|Sanchi]].
