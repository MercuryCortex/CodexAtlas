# Royal Arch

## Identity

The **Royal Arch** (formally the **Supreme Order of the Holy Royal Arch**) is the degree that, in the English Masonic system, completes the initiatic journey begun in the three Craft degrees. In 1813 the United Grand Lodge of England declared that "pure Ancient Masonry consists of three degrees, including the Supreme Order of the Holy Royal Arch" — establishing it as the philosophical capstone of the Blue Lodge. It is the degree in which the "True Word of a Master Mason" — the Ineffable Name (Tetragrammaton) that was lost at Hiram Abiff's murder — is at last revealed to the candidate through the discovery of a sealed underground vault beneath Solomon's Temple.

The Royal Arch is also the locus of the **JABULON controversy** — the compound divine name used in some jurisdictions that became the center of a major religious debate about Masonry's theological commitments in the 1980s.

## Earliest Attestation

- **1738**: first clear documentary evidence of Royal Arch Masonry in Irish records.
- **1744**: Dunckerley and the earliest English references; exact origin is disputed between Irish, Scottish, and English priority claims.
- **1813**: Union of Grand Lodge declaration making the Royal Arch explicitly part of "pure Ancient Masonry."
- **1984**: The Knight/Hannah controversy over JABULON in the British press; UGLE subsequently revised the ritual.

## Meaning(s) Attested

### The Lost Word Recovered
The Master Mason degree enacts the death of Hiram Abiff and the loss of the "Genuine Secrets" of a Master Mason. The Royal Arch is the sequel: Zerubbabel's workers, rebuilding the Temple after the Babylonian exile, discover a sealed underground vault. In the vault is found an ark bearing a golden triangle-plate on which the True Name — the Ineffable Name of God, the [[tetragrammaton]] — is inscribed. The discovery completes the Masonic initiatic narrative: what was lost is found; the mason who has passed through symbolic death and resurrection now stands in the presence of the divine name itself.

### The Triple Tau
The Triple Tau is the principal visual emblem of the Royal Arch:

**Form**: Three interlocked tau (T) shapes, forming a hexagonal or triangular compound figure.

**Interpretations** (multiple, coexistent within Masonry):
1. **TH** = *Templum Hierosolymae* (Temple of Jerusalem) — the three T's form the monogram of the Temple's Latin name
2. **Three Tau** = the three pillars of Masonic wisdom (Wisdom/Strength/Beauty) in their completion
3. **Tau (τ / תָּו)** as the last letter of the Hebrew alphabet — the tau as *telos* (completion/perfection); echoes Ezekiel 9:4 where God marks the righteous with the taw for preservation; the triple form = three-fold completeness of divine knowledge
4. **Geometric**: the compound form creates a figure geometrically related to the Star of David / [[hexagram]] — three interlocked completions suggesting the same divine-interpenetration theology

### The Underground Vault
The Royal Arch narrative centers on a vault beneath the Temple — the discovery of a space hidden since Solomon's time. This resonates with real archaeological history: Charles Warren's 1871 excavations under the Temple Mount (the "Warren's Shaft" system) genuinely found ancient chambers and tunnels, and the intersection of Masonic ritual geography with archaeological reality created rich mythology around the idea that the Royal Arch's fictional vault might correspond to an actual subterranean Temple structure.

## JABULON Controversy — Tier-Disciplined Documentation

**What the ritual contained** (Tier-1/2 documented fact): The Royal Arch degree, in its pre-1984 English and some American workings, used a compound divine name typically rendered **J-A-B-U-L-O-N** (or **Jahbulon**), parsed as a combination of three divine names:
- **JAH** = the Hebrew divine name (Yah, abbreviated Tetragrammaton)
- **BUL** = interpreted as Baal (the Canaanite/Phoenician deity)
- **ON** = interpreted as Osiris (Egyptian deity) or the city of Heliopolis (*On* in Hebrew)

Hannah (1952, Tier-2 with polemical framing) documented this name in *Darkness Visible* with the ritual text; the name's existence is not disputed by Masonic scholars.

**The interpretations** require careful tier-separation:

| Claim | Source | Tier | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| JABULON name appeared in Royal Arch ritual | Hannah 1952; confirmed by UGLE | Tier-2/documented | TRUE — the name existed |
| JABULON = deliberate pagan syncretism (Baal worship + Osiris worship) | Stephen Knight, *The Brotherhood* (1984) | Tier-4 | CONTESTED — anti-Masonic polemical claim |
| JABULON = corrupted or interpolated text, not theologically intentional | Most Masonic scholars | Tier-2 (internal) | MAJORITY MASONIC POSITION |
| UGLE revised the ritual post-1984 to remove JABULON | UGLE official statement | Tier-1 | TRUE — documented institutional change |

**The scholarly consensus** (Stevenson 1988; Katz 1970): the compound name is likely a late interpolation into an older ritual, possibly derived from the Noachide degree tradition and conflated vocabulary, rather than a deliberate syncretism. No Masonic constitutional document endorses Baal worship; the name appears only in a degree whose theological coherence was always partly contested.

## Cross-Tradition Context

### Temple of Solomon Theology
The Royal Arch is the fullest expression of Masonic Temple theology — the idea that the Craft degrees and the Royal Arch together constitute a symbolic pilgrimage from the outer courts of Solomon's Temple through the inner sanctuary to the Holy of Holies, where the divine Name dwells. See [[pillars-jachin-boaz]] for the threshold theology; [[ark-of-the-covenant-symbol]] for the portable divine-presence emblem; [[tetragrammaton]] for the Name itself.

### Kabbalistic Resonances
Jacob Katz (1970, Tier-1) traces how Masonic Temple symbolism incorporated Kabbalistic elements as the tradition developed — the Tree of Life mapped onto Lodge architecture, the sefirot onto pillars and degrees. The Royal Arch's recovery of the Divine Name echoes Kabbalistic theurgic practice: the Name is not merely a label but an operative instrument of divine power. See [[sefirot-tree-of-life]].

### The Tau Cross and Completion Theology
The tau cross (Hebrew *taw* = the final letter) carries a deep completion-theology in Jewish and Christian tradition: Ezekiel 9:4 uses it as the protective mark of the righteous; the Church Fathers identified it with the Cross (the tau-cross = the T-shaped cross); Revelation 1:8 places it in the Alpha-Omega completion dyad. The Royal Arch's Triple Tau inherits this theology and triples it — three-fold completion of divine knowledge through the recovery of the Name. See [[tau-cross]] and [[alpha-omega]].

## Contested Interpretations

- **Historical origin**: Irish vs. Scottish vs. English priority in the Royal Arch's development is unresolved.
- **JABULON**: deliberate syncretism (Knight/anti-Masonic position, Tier-4) vs. textual corruption (Masonic consensus, Tier-2) vs. meaningful divine-name compound (Wilmshurst Tier-2). The ritual revision post-1984 effectively removed the name from operative Masonry, making the debate more historical than practical.
- **"Pure Ancient Masonry"**: the 1813 declaration that the Royal Arch is part of the three degrees is paradoxical — it is structurally a fourth degree. The claim is a face-saving institutional compromise of the 1813 union, not a theological statement.
