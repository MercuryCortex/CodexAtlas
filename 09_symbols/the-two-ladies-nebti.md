# The Two Ladies (Nebti)

The pairing of [[nekhbet]] (vulture goddess of Upper Egypt) and [[wadjet]] (cobra goddess of Lower Egypt) as the **Two Ladies** — one of the five names of the Egyptian pharaoh from the First Dynasty onwards (the Nebti name). The pair iconographically represents the unification of the Two Lands and the pharaoh's protection by the tutelary goddesses of each region.
