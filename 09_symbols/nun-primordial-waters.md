# Nun (Primordial Waters)

## Identity
The **Nun** (Egyptian: *nwn*, written with the water-ripple hieroglyph — also *Nu*) is the dark, limitless, primordial waters of Egyptian cosmology existing **before creation**. The Nun is not a deity in the conventional sense — there are no temples to Nun, no major festivals, no priestly cult — but a **cosmological condition**: the undifferentiated, watery, dark, infinite substrate that preceded all creation and that continues to surround and underlie the created world. In Egyptian cosmology the world is a bubble of ordered creation (Maat) floating within the infinite undifferentiated Nun; the primordial waters press in on all sides and lap at the foundations of the earth. Every Egyptian **temple's sacred lake** ritually reproduced the Nun: the priest's morning purification by washing before entering the sanctuary was a daily re-enactment of emerging from the primordial waters into the ordered world of creation.

## Earliest attestation
- The Nun appears in the **Pyramid Texts** (c. -2350 BCE, Pyramid of Unas, Saqqara — [[phase-1-002-pyramid-texts]]) — the oldest surviving religious corpus in the world. This is simultaneously the earliest attestation of the Nun and the earliest documented instance of the cross-tradition concept of an undifferentiated pre-creation aquatic substrate.
- The **Coffin Texts** (c. -2100 BCE — [[phase-1-009-coffin-texts]]) and the **Book of the Dead** ([[phase-1-010-book-of-the-dead]]) develop the Nun's role in solar theology and funerary cosmology.
- The **Memphite Theology** (Shabaka Stone, c. -700 BCE recording an Old Kingdom text — [[phase-1-027-memphite-theology-shabaka-stone]]) and the **Amduat** ([[phase-1-036-amduat]]) give the fullest accounts of the nightly solar transit through the Nun.

## Meaning(s) attested
- **Cosmological substrate**: The Nun IS what existed before Atum self-created (*Atum* = "the Complete One / the Nothing") upon the primordial mound (Benben) rising from the Nun. All Egyptian creation accounts — Heliopolitan (Atum), Memphite (Ptah), Hermopolitan (Ogdoad), Theban (Amun) — have the Nun as the precondition. The Nun is not created; it simply IS the pre-creation condition.
- **The ritual sacred lake**: The Egyptian sacred lake (*iʿrt*) is the Nun materialized in the temple precinct. Priests purify themselves in it before performing rites — washing in the primordial waters as a ritual return to and re-emergence from the pre-creation state. The daily purification ritual IS the creation myth re-enacted. (Allen 1988; Assmann 2001.)
- **The nightly solar transit**: Each night Ra's solar bark descends through the Duat (underworld) to the Nun at its nadir — the darkest, deepest, most dangerous passage — before being reborn at dawn. The Nun is where the sun is dissolved and reconstituted. This gives the Nun its dual nature: the deadly chaos that destroys form AND the generative substrate from which form re-emerges.

## Cross-family appearance: the MASSIVE WIN — primordial waters across traditions

The Nun is the Egyptian instance of one of the most widely attested cosmological concepts in world religion: **water as the pre-creation substrate**. These are independent attestations of the same cosmological claim — documented by scholars of comparative religion (Eliade, Heidel, Assmann) as one of the most universal mythological patterns:

### Hebrew Tehom (תְּהֹום) and the Spirit over the Waters
Genesis 1:2: *"and the Spirit of God moved over the face of the **waters**"* — the first creative act is divine motion over pre-existing primordial waters called *Tehom* (the Deep). Alexander Heidel (1946, *The Babylonian Genesis*) documented the linguistic relationship between Hebrew *Tehom* and Akkadian *Tiamat* — both derive from a Proto-Semitic root for "sea / deep water." The primordial waters of Genesis ARE the Egyptian Nun's Semitic cognate. The **Spirit (Ruah) moving over the Waters** = Atum floating on the Nun before self-creation.

### Mesopotamian Tiamat and Apsu ([[tiamat]], [[apsu]])
In the *Enuma Elish* (c. -1200 BCE — [[phase-1-008-enuma-elish]]), **Tiamat** (salt-water ocean) and **Apsu** (fresh-water ocean) are the undifferentiated primordial pair before any gods exist: *"When on high heaven had not yet been named... Apsu, the first one, their begetter, and creator Tiamat, who bore them all, had mixed their waters together."* The mixing of Tiamat-and-Apsu IS the Nun: undifferentiated, watery, dark, pre-cosmic. Nun (Egyptian) and Tiamat/Apsu (Mesopotamian) are structural parallels attested independently; the Egyptian tradition predates the Babylonian by over a millennium.

### Hindu Primordial Ocean: Kshirasagara
Vishnu reclines on the cosmic serpent Shesha (Ananta) floating on the **Kshirasagara** (Ocean of Milk) — the primordial ocean that exists between cosmic cycles. When Brahma emerges from the lotus growing from Vishnu's navel and begins creation, the Ocean of Milk is the undifferentiated substrate: the Hindu Nun. The connection between the cosmic waters and the resting/sleeping deity (Vishnu on Shesha = Atum on the Nun) is one of the most striking cross-tradition visual and structural parallels in comparative religion.

### Norse Ginnungagap
Before the Norse creation (Eddas — *Gylfaginning*), **Ginnungagap** (the "mighty gap" or "yawning void") exists as the primordial empty space into which the icy rivers of Niflheim flow and freeze, meeting the heat of Muspelheim. The primordial condition before Ymir is created is void + ice + water: the Norse pre-creation substrate parallels the Nun as formless aquatic precondition. No documented genetic connection; structural parallel.

### Baptism and the Nun: the MASSIVE WIN in Christianity
Christian baptismal theology (submersion in water = death to the old order; emergence = new creation) is a ritual re-enactment of the primordial waters mythology. Paul's baptism theology (Romans 6:3–4): *"we were buried with him by baptism into death... raised from the dead."* The baptismal font IS the Nun: the waters of pre-creation into which the old self is dissolved and from which the new creation emerges. This is the Egyptian Nun's cosmological logic translated into Christian initiatory ritual — mediated via the Hebrew Tehom and the Genesis creation narrative.

## Variants
See YAML — the sacred-lake variant (ritual/cultic), the Ogdoad variant (theological-cosmological), and the nightly-solar-transit variant (astronomical/eschatological) each represent different registers in which the Nun functions within Egyptian religion.

## Contested interpretations
- **Is Nun a deity or a cosmological principle?** Egyptian religion does not make this distinction cleanly. Nun is personified (depicted as a bearded man with arms raised supporting the solar bark) but is not an object of cult. Hornung (1982) addresses this — Egyptian cosmological forces exist on a continuum between principle and deity.
- **Does the Tiamat-Tehom linguistic parallel imply direct transmission?** Heidel (1946) documented the philological connection; the scholarly debate is whether the Genesis account directly borrows from the *Enuma Elish* or whether both draw on a common ancient Near Eastern mythological stock. The Nun predates both and may represent the older substrate of this cosmological tradition.
