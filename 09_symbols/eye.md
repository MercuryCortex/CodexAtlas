# Eye (Single / All-Seeing)

> **Methodology discipline note (4-ref entry):** The "eye in a triangle on the dollar bill" Illuminati / New-World-Order conspiracy-theory framework is **Tier 4** reception-history. The actual iconographic sources are: (a) Egyptian Eye of Horus / Eye of Ra (well-attested); (b) Renaissance and post-Renaissance Christian Eye of Providence (c. 1525 onward, well-attested); (c) Masonic adoption (c. 1750 onward, well-documented Masonic-internal sources); (d) US Great Seal (1782, Charles Thomson and William Barton, well-documented archival record). The conspiracy-theory layer atop these is a 20th-c. construction without Tier-1 evidentiary support.

## Identity

The eye as iconographic symbol has multiple distinct attestations: the Egyptian Eye of Horus (*wedjat* / *udjat* — lunar-feminine, healing-protective) and Eye of Ra (solar-feminine, destructive-protective) are the foundational ancient attestations from c. -2700 onward; the Christian Eye of Providence (post-Renaissance, c. 1525 onward) is a fresh Christian iconographic development drawing on biblical "eye of the Lord" textual tradition; the Masonic adoption (c. 1750 onward) brings the Christian Eye of Providence into Masonic iconography; the US Great Seal (1782) adopts the Eye of Providence as civic emblem; and the modern 20th-21st-c. conspiracy-theory reception treats all of this as evidence of secret-society control of the United States (Tier 4, no Tier-1 evidentiary support). The Egyptian Eye of Horus / Eye of Ra is independent of and far older than any of the Christian / Masonic / civic adoptions.

## Earliest attestation

- **Egyptian Eye of Horus / *wedjat* (c. -2700 onward)** — Old Kingdom; *Pyramid Texts* spells reference the restoration of Horus's eye; apotropaic amulets continuous through Roman period. (Wilkinson 1994; Pinch 2002.) ([[phase-1-002-pyramid-texts]])
- **Egyptian Eye of Ra (Old Kingdom onward)** — solar-feminine destructive aspect; canonical in the *Book of the Heavenly Cow*. (Pinch 2002.)
- **Christian biblical eye-of-the-Lord (Hebrew Bible textual)** — Psalms 33:18 and other "eye of the Lord upon those who fear him" verses; biblical-textual basis but iconographic-emblem development is much later.
- **Christian Eye of Providence (c. 1525 onward)** — Pontormo's *Supper at Emmaus* (1525) includes an early example; Cesare Ripa's *Iconologia* (1593) systematizes; 17th-18th c. Catholic and Protestant iconography. The Eye-in-Triangle (Trinity) form crystallizes through the 17th-18th c.
- **Masonic adoption (c. 1750 onward)** — Anderson's *Constitutions* (1723) does not yet feature the eye; the iconographic adoption is post-1750 Masonic reception. Mackey 1873 documents Masonic-internal self-understanding. ([[phase-6-026-anderson-constitutions-free-masons]])
- **US Great Seal (1782)** — adopted 20 June 1782 by act of Congress; designed by Charles Thomson (Secretary of Continental Congress) and William Barton.
- **US one-dollar bill back (1935 onward)** — Franklin D. Roosevelt's Treasury (Henry Morgenthau Jr.) added the Great Seal reverse to the dollar bill in 1935.

## Meaning(s) attested

- **Egyptian *wedjat*** — restored eye of Horus after the Set conflict; healing, restoration, protection. Apotropaic. (Wilkinson 1994.)
- **Egyptian Eye of Ra** — solar-feminine destructive emanation; protective-but-fearsome. (Pinch 2002.)
- **Christian Eye of Providence** — divine omniscience; God's surveillance / care for creation. Often within Trinitarian triangle with radiating glory.
- **Masonic** — the Great Architect of the Universe's watchful presence over Masonic work. (Mackey 1873 — Masonic-internal.)
- **US Great Seal** — divine providence over the American national project; *Annuit Coeptis* ("He has favored our undertakings").
- **Modern conspiracy reception** — Tier 4: claimed evidence of Illuminati / secret-society control of the US. NO Tier-1 evidentiary support.

## Variants

The six attested variants documented in YAML span the major eye-iconography traditions. The Egyptian and Christian-civic-Masonic lines are independent in origin; the Christian-civic-Masonic line is internally continuous.

## Cross-family appearance

- **Egyptian Eye of Horus / Eye of Ra ↔ Christian Eye of Providence** — `parallel-form`, **NOT `ancestor-of`**. The Renaissance Christian Eye of Providence is best read as fresh iconographic development from biblical "eye of the Lord" textual tradition, NOT as a derivation from Egyptian Eye of Horus. Athanasius Kircher's *Oedipus Aegyptiacus* (1652) is too late to be source; the iconographic standardization of the Eye of Providence (c. 1525-1593 Ripa) is earlier than the Renaissance Egyptological reception.
- **Christian Eye of Providence → Masonic eye** — `ancestor-of`. The Masonic eye is the Christian-civic Eye of Providence adopted into Masonic iconographic vocabulary c. 1750. Well-documented.
- **Masonic / Christian Eye of Providence → US Great Seal** — `ancestor-of`. The Great Seal designers (Thomson, Barton) drew on the Christian Eye of Providence convention; whether they also drew on Masonic precedents is debated (Thomson was not a Mason; Franklin was, but Franklin's eye-and-pyramid design was rejected).
- **US Great Seal → modern conspiracy-theory reception** — `appropriated-by` (in a Tier-4 reception sense). The 20th-21st-c. conspiracy-theory framework appropriates the iconography for its narrative, with no Tier-1 evidentiary support.

## Contested interpretations

- Whether the Renaissance Christian Eye of Providence has any historical-causal influence from the Egyptian Eye of Horus / Eye of Ra. Mainstream art-history scholarship (Cesare Ripa, the Renaissance iconographic tradition) reads it as fresh development from biblical textual tradition; the Egyptological reception of Egyptian iconography post-dates the Eye of Providence's standardization.
- Whether the US Great Seal designers drew on Masonic iconography. Thomson was not a Mason; Franklin's earlier Mason-influenced design was rejected. The final design's relationship to Masonic precedents is partly indirect.
- The Illuminati / New-World-Order conspiracy-theory interpretation. Tier 4; no Tier-1 evidentiary support for the specific claims.
