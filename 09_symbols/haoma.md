# Haoma

## Identity
Haoma (Avestan *Haoma*; Pahlavi-Persian *Hōm*) is **the ritual plant-and-drink at the center of Zoroastrian liturgical practice** — the principal sacrificial-cult-substance of the *yasna* (Zoroastrian liturgy, the cognate of Vedic *yajña*). The plant is **pressed in a mortar with a metal pestle, mixed with water + milk + pomegranate-leaves, and consumed in tiny amounts by the officiating priest** as the central ritual act of the yasna. Haoma is **directly cognate with Vedic Soma** (Sanskrit *Soma*) — both are Indo-Iranian inheritances from the Proto-Indo-Iranian period, and both are similarly central to their respective religious-liturgical traditions. The botanical identity of the haoma-plant is contested: most current scholarship favors **Ephedra** (an alkaloid-containing stimulant); Flattery & Schwartz 1989 argue for **Peganum harmala** (Syrian rue, containing harmaline alkaloids); Wasson 1968 famously argued for **Amanita muscaria** (the fly-agaric mushroom) for Vedic Soma — the Wasson-identification is mostly rejected by current Vedic studies but remains influential in the ethnomycological-and-psychedelic-religion-studies literature. For the Codex Atlas mission Haoma is doubly load-bearing: (1) it is **the cleanest documentable Indo-Iranian shared-religious-substance** — Haoma-Soma is the strongest single piece of Proto-Indo-Iranian religious-cult reconstruction; (2) it provides the **principal pre-Christian Near Eastern ritual-drink** structurally parallel to Christian Eucharistic-cup liturgy.

## Earliest attestation
- **Gathas** ([[phase-2-002-gathas-of-zarathustra]]): Yasna 32.14, 48.10 — Zarathustra is **ambivalent** about haoma (some Gathic verses appear critical of haoma-ritual excesses; the haoma-cult continued in Younger Avesta period regardless). The Gathic ambivalence is one of the major Zoroastrian-religious-historical interpretive puzzles.
- **Younger Avesta**: extensively. **Yasna 9-11 (the Hōm Yasht)** — the principal liturgical-narrative text on haoma; the personified Haoma-yazata visits Zarathustra and recounts his cosmic-religious history.
- **Vedic cognate Soma**: Rigveda Book 9 is **entirely** dedicated to Soma (114 hymns, *Soma Pavamāna* — "Soma Self-Purifying" — the principal Vedic-Soma corpus). Soma is one of the most-extensively-attested Vedic cult-figures.
- **Achaemenid period** (-550 to -330): haoma-cult continues institutionally; Greek ethnographers (Herodotus, Strabo) note Persian sacrificial-ritual practices including plant-drink offerings.
- **Sasanian period**: haoma-cult continues as central yasna-component; Pahlavi texts (Bundahishn, Denkard) treat haoma extensively.
- **Modern Parsi-Iranian**: haoma-pressing remains the central ritual act of the yasna-ceremony to the present day; the plant currently used is **ephedra** (Iranian Zoroastrian practice) or **a substitute** when ephedra unavailable.

## Meaning(s) attested

- **Cosmic-divine plant**. Haoma is personified as yazata (the Hōm Yasht); the plant **descends from a cosmic-divine source** and is brought down to humans for ritual use.
- **Inspiration / strength / immortality-bringing**. The Hōm Yasht names benefits the haoma-drink bestows: physical strength, mental clarity, courage in battle, vision in dreams, and (at the eschatological level) **immortality** at the end of time (haoma will be the principal substance distributed at the Frashokereti universal-renovation).
- **Ritual liquid-of-immortality**. The Avestan tradition explicitly treats haoma as **immortality-bringing**: the Vedic-Hindu cognate doctrine of *amrita* (immortality-liquid) shares this theological structure.
- **Anti-druj substance**. Haoma is theologically opposed to *druj* (the Lie / cosmic untruth); haoma-consumption strengthens the cosmic order against [[angra-mainyu-ahriman]].

## Cross-family appearance

### Indo-Iranian: Haoma ↔ Soma
**The cleanest single Indo-Iranian shared-religious-substance.** The cognate-cult-practice runs back to Proto-Indo-Iranian (~-1500 or earlier). Both traditions:
- Press a stalked plant in mortar-and-pestle.
- Mix with water and milk.
- Offer to fire.
- Consume in ritual context.
- Personify the substance as divine being.
- Attribute immortality / cosmic-renewal benefits.

The Haoma-Soma cognate is one of the most-load-bearing single pieces of Proto-Indo-Iranian religious-cultural reconstruction.

### Christian Eucharistic-cup parallel
Haoma's ritual-cult role provides **the principal pre-Christian Near Eastern ritual-drink parallel** to Christian Eucharistic-cup liturgy:
- **Ritual-cult-drink as cosmic-divine substance** — Haoma is divine-cosmic; the Eucharistic wine is sanctified to become (in various Christian theological readings) the Body of Christ.
- **Priest-mediated ritual consumption** — Haoma is prepared and offered by the priest; the Eucharist is consecrated and distributed by the priest.
- **Immortality-bringing** — Haoma confers eschatological immortality; the Eucharist confers eternal life (John 6:54).
- **Cosmic-cult-substance** — Both ritualize a plant-extracted liquid as cosmic-divine cult-substance.

Whether this represents (a) direct genealogical influence (Zoroastrian haoma → Hellenistic-Roman religious-cultural context → Christian Eucharistic-cup theology, mediated through Hellenistic-Jewish liturgical development), (b) shared Indo-European-Near-Eastern ritual-drink vocabulary, or (c) accidental convergence is debated. **The structural parallel is uncontested.** The vault catalogs the structural parallel without forcing a transmission-direction claim.

### Other ritual-drink parallels
- **Greek-Roman vine-and-wine**: Dionysian-Bacchic + Eucharistic; see [[vine-grape]] + [[thyrsus]].
- **Eleusinian kykeon**: the Eleusinian-mystery ritual barley-drink; see [[eleusinian-mystery-revelation]].

## Botanical identification (the central scholarly puzzle)
The principal scholarly debate about haoma is **what plant**. Major positions:

1. **Ephedra** (the dominant current view). Modern Iranian-Zoroastrian practice uses ephedra; contains alkaloids (ephedrine, pseudoephedrine) that are mild stimulants. Compatible with Avestan plant-descriptions (mountain-growing, stalked).

2. **Peganum harmala (Syrian rue)** — Flattery & Schwartz 1989. Contains harmaline alkaloids (psychoactive at higher doses). Argued from textual-historical reconstruction.

3. **Amanita muscaria (fly-agaric)** — Wasson 1968 for Vedic Soma. Argued from textual and ethnomycological evidence. Mostly rejected by current Vedic-studies scholars; remains influential in ethnomycology.

4. **Composite-substance** — some scholars propose that "haoma" and "soma" refer to **different plants at different periods** or to **composite preparations** combining multiple plants.

The botanical question is genuinely open. The **religious-cultural significance** of haoma is independent of which plant is identified.

## Contested interpretations
- **Botanical identification**: see above. Genuinely open.
- **Gathic Zarathustra's relationship to haoma-cult**: did Zarathustra reject haoma (some Gathic verses appear critical of haoma-excesses), or only reject *abuse* of haoma while accepting the cult? Modern scholarship is split. Boyce 1975 argues Zarathustra reformed but did not reject the cult; some interpret the Gathic ambivalence as more critical.
- **Psychoactive vs. stimulant**: was haoma strongly psychoactive (Wasson 1968, Flattery & Schwartz 1989) or moderately stimulant (the Ephedra view)? Affects how one reads the Avestan visionary-religious claims.
- **Christian-Eucharist transmission**: whether Zoroastrian haoma-cult had any genealogical influence on Christian Eucharistic-cup theology (mediated through Hellenistic-Jewish liturgical development) is debated. Most scholarship treats the parallel as structural rather than directly genealogical.

## Mystery / unknown variants
The **botanical identity remains contested** despite extensive scholarship. `mystery-status: partially-deciphered` — the ritual practice is well-documented; the specific plant is genuinely open.
