# Sol Niger

## Identity
The **Sol Niger** (Latin: "Black Sun") is an alchemical symbol representing the **sun in its eclipsed, blackened, or death state** — the *nigredo*, the first and darkest stage of the **Great Work** (*Opus Magnum*). In European alchemical iconography (c. 13th–17th c.), the Sol Niger marks the moment of maximum dissolution of matter: the *prima materia* is putrefied, blackened, reduced to *caput mortuum* ("death's head"), and falls symbolically into darkness before its transformation into the purified *Sol Aureus* (Golden Sun) at the Work's completion. The symbol encodes the alchemical law that death precedes resurrection, that darkness is the necessary precondition for light, and that the destruction of existing form is the precondition of new form.

**Critical appropriation warning**: In the 20th century, a 12-spoke sunwheel mosaic installed at **Wewelsburg Castle** by Heinrich Himmler (1933–1935) was retrospectively designated the "Black Sun" by post-war neo-Nazi esotericists and has since been adopted as a primary hate symbol of neo-Nazi, white supremacist, and occult-nationalist movements worldwide. This appropriation is documented, modern, and must be clearly separated from the pre-Nazi alchemical tradition. The two usages share a name and superficial solar-darkness imagery; they share nothing else.

## Earliest attestation
- **Alchemical nigredo**: The *nigredo* stage (including the darkening of the sun as a stage-emblem) is attested in Latin alchemical texts from c. **1200–1300 CE** onward — in the *Rosarium Philosophorum* (c. 1550, but drawing on older material), in Paracelsian texts ([[phase-6-008-paracelsus-corpus]]), and in the *Aurora Consurgens* (c. 14th c., attributed in manuscript tradition to Thomas Aquinas).
- **The term "Sol Niger"** as an explicit label: attested in Latin alchemical texts c. 15th–16th c. CE; standardized in the Paracelsian tradition and crystallized in **Michael Maier's *Atalanta Fugiens*** (1617) and in **[[carl-gustav-jung|Jung's]] *Psychology and Alchemy*** (1944) as a named stage-symbol.
- **The Egyptian nocturnal sun**: The concept of the sun passing through the underworld in darkness during the twelve hours of night is attested in the **Amduat** and **Book of Gates** from c. 1500–1200 BCE — independently representing the "dark sun" principle without documented connection to the alchemical tradition.
- **The Wewelsburg mosaic**: designed and installed 1933–1935; **named "Black Sun" / "Schwarze Sonne" retroactively** by Wilhelm Landig in his novel *Götzen gegen Thule* (1971). The SS did not use this name at the time of installation.

## Meaning(s) attested

### In alchemical tradition (Tier 1)
- **Nigredo = the first stage** of the Great Work: the putrefaction, blackening, and death of the *prima materia* (the raw material, whether literal chemical substance or allegorical soul-state). The Sun (gold, the solar principle) goes dark before it can be regenerated.
- **Caput Corvi** ("Raven's Head") is an alternate emblem for the same stage — the black raven alights on the dead matter.
- **The psycho-spiritual reading** (Jung 1944): the Sol Niger represents the descent into the unconscious — the dissolution of the ego's certainties, the confrontation with the shadow and the dark material of the psyche — as the precondition for individuation. The Sun's blackening = the ego's dismemberment before transformation.
- **The Saturnian correspondence**: In alchemical planetary correspondence, Sol Niger is associated with **[[saturn-roman]]** (lead, the heaviest metal, the slowest planet, the principle of time, death, and melancholy). [[marsilio-ficino|Ficino's]] *De Vita Triplici* (1489) documents the Renaissance Hermetic reception of the Saturnian dark-genius principle — the melancholic artist born under Saturn who descends into darkness as the precondition of creative illumination.

### In Egyptian religion (Tier 1, independent parallel)
The **nocturnal sun** (Ra-as-Osiris passing through the *Duat*/underworld during the twelve hours of night) is the Egyptian form of the Sol Niger principle. In the *Amduat* and *Book of Gates* (documented in Piankoff 1954), the solar barque traverses the underworld each night; the sun is the "dead Ra" / "Ra in the body of Osiris" during this journey. At the nadir of the journey (the 6th hour, midnight), the sun is at its darkest and weakest — and then regenerates. This is **not** a documented historical source for the alchemical Sol Niger; it is an independent parallel of the same mythological logic.

**Cross-tradition finding — Kali and Sol Niger**: The Hindu Tantric goddess [[kali]] — black-skinned, dwelling in the cremation ground, wreathed in skulls, the destroyer of forms — is the most structurally exact independent parallel to the alchemical Sol Niger in world religion. Kali's black skin is not darkness as evil but darkness as the *pre-manifest state*, the formlessness that precedes and follows all forms. Kali destroys to enable new creation, exactly as the alchemical nigredo destroys the old form so the new form can emerge. The cremation ground (smashan) as Kali's domain = the *prima materia* blackened in the alchemical retort. This is an independent parallel (the Latin alchemical tradition did not draw on Kali); the structural identity is exact.

**Cross-tradition finding — Dark Night of the Soul**: John of the Cross (*Noche Oscura del Alma*, written 1578–1580, published 1618) describes the mystical *dark night of the soul* — the dissolution of all spiritual consolation, the stripping of all certainty, the experience of God's apparent absence, the "death" of the old spiritual self — as the necessary precondition for the highest mystical union. This is the Sol Niger in Christian mystical vocabulary: darkness before light, dissolution before transformation, the "night" that precedes the dawn of union. The structural identity with the alchemical nigredo is the most important within-Western-tradition parallel; Jung (1944) explicitly draws this connection.

## Appropriation and Misuse

**This section is mandatory for the Sol Niger node and must be read before any use of the symbol in the vault.**

### The Wewelsburg Mosaic and Nazi SS Appropriation

**Heinrich Himmler** (1900–1945), Reichsführer-SS, acquired Wewelsburg Castle in Paderborn (1933) and developed it as the symbolic and ceremonial center of the SS order. The north tower's ground floor ("Obergruppenführersaal") received a dark marble inlay: a **12-armed sunwheel** (12 "Sig" runes in some interpretations, or stylized lightning bolts). This was commissioned under Himmler's direction as part of his deliberate construction of the SS as a pseudo-religious order with Germanic-mythological-occult foundations.

**The SS did not call this the "Schwarze Sonne" (Black Sun)**. The name was applied retroactively. The primary post-war source establishing the connection between the Wewelsburg mosaic and the term "Schwarze Sonne" is **Wilhelm Landig**'s novel *Götzen gegen Thule* (1971) — a work of **Nazi esoteric fiction** by a former Waffen-SS member. Landig's novels (the "Thule Trilogy") established the mythological framework in which the "Black Sun" became a post-war neo-Nazi esoteric symbol.

**The subsequent neo-Nazi reception** — through **Miguel Serrano** (Chilean Nazi esotericist), **Savitri Devi**, and other figures documented by **Goodrick-Clarke (2002)** in *Black Sun: Aryan Cults, Esoteric Nazism, and the Politics of Identity* (Tier-1, NYU Press) — transformed the Wewelsburg design into the central symbol of esoteric Nazism, "Aryan" occultism, and the neo-Nazi "Black Sun" ideology.

**Current hate-symbol status**: The 12-spoke sunwheel in the Wewelsburg style is classified as a primary **hate symbol** by:
- The **Southern Poverty Law Center** (SPLC)
- The **Anti-Defamation League** (ADL)
- German domestic intelligence (Verfassungsschutz) — the symbol's display is potentially prosecutable under §86a StGB (use of symbols of unconstitutional organizations) in Germany
- Multiple European law-enforcement agencies

**The operational principle**: The modern neo-Nazi "Black Sun" has the same structural relationship to the pre-Nazi alchemical Sol Niger as the Nazi swastika ([[swastika]]) has to the pre-Nazi Hindu/Buddhist/Jain/Aztec swastika — a pre-existing symbol of depth and antiquity **deliberately appropriated** by a genocidal ideology and **permanently contaminated** for many contexts by that association. The pre-Nazi alchemical tradition is legitimate and Tier-1 documented; the Nazi and neo-Nazi appropriation is equally documented and must be identified as such with no ambiguity.

**Vault rule**: Any node linking to `sol-niger` must clearly distinguish which layer (alchemical / Egyptian / Hindu parallel / Nazi appropriation) is being referenced. The alchemical tradition is not responsible for the Nazi appropriation; the Nazi appropriation does not delegitimize the alchemical tradition.

## Contested interpretations
- **Continuity between alchemical Sol Niger and Nazi Black Sun**: There is **no documented historical transmission** from the medieval/Renaissance alchemical Sol Niger tradition to the Wewelsburg mosaic or to Himmler's SS symbolism. The design was drawn from Germanic sunwheel motifs and possibly from runic scholarship; the connection to the alchemical Sol Niger was made *by* post-war neo-Nazi esotericists projecting backwards, not by historical transmission forward.
- **Egyptian nocturnal sun as source**: The claim that the alchemical Sol Niger derives from the Egyptian *Amduat* nocturnal-sun tradition is an attractive structural parallel but lacks documented historical transmission evidence. The alchemical tradition's Egyptian heritage is documented at the general level (Zosimos of Panopolis, c. 300 CE; the Hermetic corpus); specific solar iconography is harder to trace.
- **The cymatic / astronomical eclipse as universal source**: The solar eclipse as a universal natural phenomenon generates "black sun" mythological imagery independently across all cultures (Chinese dragon swallowing the sun; Norse Hati wolf; Aztec solar-darkness mythology). Whether this constitutes a "tradition" or a convergent natural-phenomenon response is a methodological question.
