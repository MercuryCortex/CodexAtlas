# Sun Disk / Solar Wheel

> **Methodology discipline note (4-ref entry):** Solar iconography is one of the most universally attested sacred-emblem patterns, which makes it both rich and prone to naïve diffusionism. Independent invention is the default for cross-continental attestations; specific intra-Mediterranean transmissions (Sol Invictus → Christian halo) are documented and treated as `appropriated-by`. The Buddhist Dharma-wheel is `parallel-form`, NOT a derived solar emblem despite 19th-20th-c. comparativist claims.

## Identity

The sun disk / solar wheel is one of the most universally attested cosmological-divine emblems in the human record. The iconographic form — circle with radiating lines / spokes / rays — recurs independently across Egypt (Aten, Ra), Mesopotamia (Shamash), Greek-Roman (Helios, Apollo, Sol, Sol Invictus), Vedic India (Surya), Japan (Amaterasu's mirror), Inca (Inti's golden disk), Aztec (Tonatiuh's solar calendar), and other contexts. The Akhenaten Aten of 14th-c.-BCE Egypt ([[phase-1-011-great-hymn-to-aten]]) is the first attested programmatic solar-monotheism; the Roman Sol Invictus (3rd c. CE) is the late-antique solar cult immediately preceding the Christian halo-iconography; the Christian Christ-with-radiating-cross-halo is iconographic continuation of the Sol Invictus vocabulary (the *nimbus crucifer*). The Buddhist Dharma-wheel is iconographically cognate but theologically distinct (Eightfold Path, the Buddha's first sermon's "first turning of the wheel" — not a solar emblem).

## Earliest attestation

- **Egyptian Ra and the solar disk (c. -2700 onward)** — Ra-as-sun-disk iconography is canonical Egyptian from Old Kingdom forward; the solar disk surmounts deity-heads, royal cartouches, temple iconography.
- **Aten and Akhenaten's programmatic solar-monotheism (c. -1353 to -1336)** — the Amarna period; *Great Hymn to Aten*; the distinctive solar-disk-with-rays-terminating-in-hands iconography. (Hornung 1999.)
- **Vedic Surya (c. -1500 onward textual)** — Rig Veda hymns to Surya, Savitr.
- **Mesopotamian Shamash (`[[utu-shamash]]` exists)** — solar deity with continuous Mesopotamian attestation; Code of Hammurabi stela depicts Hammurabi receiving the law from Shamash.
- **Roman Sol Invictus (270-275 CE Aurelian)** — official cult adopted 274 CE; December 25 festival. (Hijmans 2003.)
- **Christian Christ-with-cross-halo (c. 4th c. CE onward)** — Byzantine icon tradition crystallizes; the *nimbus crucifer*. (Jensen 2017.)
- **Japanese Amaterasu / mirror-emblem (textual c. 712 CE; tradition older)** — *Kojiki*, *Nihon Shoki*.
- **Inca Inti (c. 1200 CE onward Inca period; Coricancha c. 1438 CE)** — Inca royal-solar lineage and Coricancha temple in Cusco.

## Meaning(s) attested

- **Egyptian Aten** — sole divine principle; cosmic creator-and-sustainer through solar light. The Amarna programmatic monotheism. (Hornung 1999.)
- **Egyptian Ra (pre-Aten)** — solar deity in the Egyptian pantheon; daily journey of the solar barque defended against Apophis. (Pinch 2002.)
- **Roman Sol Invictus** — unconquered solar deity; imperial-political emblem.
- **Christian Christ-halo** — divine glory; cross-inscribed solar nimbus identifying Christ specifically.
- **Vedic Surya** — solar deity; eye of the cosmos; addressed in *Gayatri Mantra*.
- **Inca Inti** — divine ancestor of the Inca royal line; cosmic-political emblem.
- **Japanese Amaterasu** — divine ancestor of the Imperial line; central Shinto deity.
- **Buddhist Dharma-wheel** — the Eightfold Path; the Buddha's first sermon; the dharma in motion. **NOT a solar emblem** in original Buddhist theology. (Snellgrove 1987.)

## Cross-family appearance

- **Sol Invictus → Christian Christ-with-cross-halo** — `appropriated-by` (at the iconographic-vocabulary level). **THE LOAD-BEARING TRANSITION.** Hijmans 2003 contests the specific Sol-Invictus→Christmas-date link but does not deny the iconographic vocabulary continuity. Jensen 2017 documents the broader Christian iconographic adoption of Hellenistic-Roman solar-emblem vocabulary.
- **Aten / Akhenaten → broader monotheism** — `parallel-form`. The Aten program ended with Akhenaten's death and successors' restoration of traditional polytheism (Tutankhamun, Horemheb); whether the Aten program had any continuous historical influence on later monotheisms (Hebrew, Christian, Islamic) is the famous Freud thesis (*Moses and Monotheism* 1939) — **Tier 4** for the Freudian-causal claim. The Aten program is real and attested; its historical-causal effect on later monotheism is contested.
- **Surya → Buddhist Dharma-wheel** — `parallel-form` only. NOT `ancestor-of`. The Dharma-wheel emerges from the Buddhist tradition's own theology (Eightfold Path, first sermon at Sarnath) rather than being derived from solar iconography. Some 19th-20th-c. comparativists linked them but the linkage is not documented at the textual-iconographic level.
- **Multi-continental independent attestations** — Inca, Aztec, Japanese, Mesopotamian solar iconographies are each best read as independent attestations of a natural cosmographic emblem.

## Contested interpretations

- The Sol Invictus → Christmas-date question. Hijmans 2003 contests the straightforward "history-of-religions" Christianizing-of-the-pagan-festival reading; the iconographic-vocabulary continuity is not in dispute even where specific holiday-derivation is contested. Mainstream scholarly consensus: the iconographic vocabulary IS partly inherited; the specific December-25 question is more complex than older accounts allowed.
- The Aten programmatic-monotheism's historical-causal influence on Hebrew / Christian / Islamic monotheism. Freud's *Moses and Monotheism* (1939) thesis is Tier 4; mainstream Egyptology and biblical studies are skeptical. The Aten program is well-attested; its later-influence is contested.
- Whether the Buddhist Dharma-wheel has any genealogical connection to solar emblems. Mainstream Buddhist scholarship: no. Comparativist claims: yes. Flag as parallel-form per methodology.
