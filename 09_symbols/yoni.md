# Yoni

## Identity

The **yoni** (Sanskrit: *yonī* — womb, vagina, source, origin, birthplace; from Proto-Indo-European *\*yeu-ni-* — "joining") is the sacred symbol of the **feminine creative principle** in Hindu and Tantric tradition. As the base in which the Shiva lingam stands, it represents **Śakti** — the divine creative energy that manifests the world. Its geometric form is the downward-pointing triangle or the opening disc; its most sacred physical form is the natural rock cleft at Kāmākhyā (Assam) through which a spring flows, revered as the goddess's yoni without representational image. The yoni is the **womb of the cosmos**: the undifferentiated creative potential from which all manifest existence emerges, and the receptive ground to which it returns.

## Earliest attestation

- **If Venus figurines counted**: The **Venus of Willendorf** (c. 25,000 BCE, Austria), the **Venus of Laussel** (c. 23,000 BCE, France), and dozens of Paleolithic female figurines with pronounced sexual characteristics inaugurate a tradition of sacred female body as creative power. These are parallel-form proto-attestations of the yoni's semantic field — not textually connected to the Hindu tradition, but representing the same deep-time vocabulary of the female body as sacred generative source.
- **Neolithic**: The **Çatalhöyük figurines** (Turkey, c. 7500–5700 BCE) — female figures, some in birthing postures, associated with ritual spaces. The celebrated "Seated Goddess" figure flanked by two felines has been interpreted as a birth goddess / mother goddess.
- **Indus Valley**: The **Indus Valley cylindrical and ring stones** (Mohenjo-Daro, c. 2600–1900 BCE) — including ring-stones interpreted as yoni bases — appear alongside the proto-lingam cylinders in the same archaeological contexts.
- **Vedic textual**: The *Rig Veda* ([[phase-2-001-rig-veda-family-books]], c. 1500–1200 BCE) uses *yonī* in the sense of "source" and "womb" in cosmological contexts, including the primordial waters as the yoni of existence.
- **Explicit Tantric**: The *Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra* ([[phase-5-006-vijnana-bhairava-tantra]], c. 8th–9th century CE) and Abhinavagupta's *Tantrāloka* ([[phase-5-014-abhinavagupta-tantraloka]], c. 1000 CE) develop the yoni as a specific object of meditative focus — the aperture through which consciousness enters and exits the body, identical with the supreme Śakti.

## Meaning(s) attested

- **Creative source**: *Yonī* in Sanskrit means both physical vulva/womb and "source/origin" in the abstract sense. These are not separated in the tradition — the physical and metaphysical are the same reality.
- **Śakti as dynamic principle**: In Shaiva and Tantric theology, the yoni = Śakti (divine creative energy). Śiva (pure consciousness) is inert without Śakti's dynamism. Śakti as yoni IS the movement of creation. Without the yoni, the lingam cannot be: consciousness without creative energy is powerless.
- **Sāṃkhya encoding**: Yoni = Prakṛti (primal creative nature, the feminine principle of the Sāṃkhya system). Lingam = Puruṣa (pure consciousness, the masculine principle). Their union = saṃsāra (the manifest world). The lingam-yoni compound in every Śiva temple is **Sāṃkhya cosmology made sculptural**.
- **Gateway of birth and death**: The yoni as the aperture through which souls enter the world at birth and (in some Tantric readings) through which consciousness is released at death. The yoni-pitha shrines are locations of maximal sacred power because they mark where the divine Śakti manifested most intensely.
- **Absent image at Kāmākhyā**: The inner sanctum of the Kāmākhyā temple (Assam) contains **no representational image** — only a natural cleft in the rock through which a spring flows, draped with red cloth (red = menstrual blood = Śakti's power). The absence of image IS the image: the formless creative source cannot be represented; it can only be approached.

## Variants

**Yoni-pīṭha (sacred seat / yoni-shrine)**: The 51 Śakta Pīṭhas mark spots where Satī's body fell when Viṣṇu dismembered her. Each piece of Satī's body fell to earth and sanctified a place; her yoni fell at Kāmākhyā. The 51 pīṭhas form a **body-map of the goddess across the subcontinent** — each shrine venerates a specific body part, and pilgrimage through the pīṭhas reconstitutes the complete divine body. This is the inverse of the Osiris myth: rather than the body being reassembled (Osiris), it is scattered and each piece becomes a sacred site.

**Argha (the lingam base)**: The flat disc-shaped pedestal with a drainage spout (*praṇāla*) from which the lingam rises. The argha/yoni channels ritual water (poured over the lingam as abhiṣeka) outward. The drainage channel points north — the direction of liberation in Hindu cosmology.

**Downward-pointing triangle (▽)**: In Tantric yantra geometry, the downward-pointing triangle encodes the yoni/Śakti principle. In the **Sri Yantra** ([[sri-yantra]]), the five downward-pointing triangles (Śakti's triangles) and four upward-pointing triangles (Śiva's triangles) interlock to produce the central bindu — the point of absolute consciousness. The Sri Yantra IS the yoni-lingam cosmology as sacred geometry.

**Kāmākhyā's menstrual mystery**: During the annual *Ambubachi Mela* festival, the Kāmākhyā temple closes for three days. The belief is that the goddess is menstruating — the spring at the sanctum runs red (from iron-rich mineral content). This is celebrated as the most sacred time: the goddess's fertility-power at its peak. The menstrual cycle of the goddess = the renewal of the earth's creative power.

## Cross-family appearance

The sacred female creative void/womb as the generative source of all existence has cross-tradition parallels that constitute one of the most widespread cosmological patterns in world religion:

- **Mesopotamian Tiamat**: The primordial saltwater ocean — female, maternal, the source from which all gods emerge in the *Enuma Elish* ([[phase-1-008-enuma-elish]]). Tiamat's body, dismembered by Marduk, becomes the raw material of the cosmos — the cosmic female body as both source and substance of the world.
- **Egyptian Nut**: The sky-goddess Nut (whose body arches over the earth as the celestial vault) swallows the sun each evening and gives birth to it each morning. Nut's body IS the cosmic womb through which solar life cycles. Her yoni gives birth to Ra daily.
- **Hebrew Rūaḥ**: The *rūaḥ ʾĕlōhîm* ("spirit/breath of God") moving over the face of the primordial waters in Genesis 1:2. *Rūaḥ* is grammatically feminine in Hebrew. The primordial creative void — the breath/spirit moving over the undifferentiated waters — has a feminine grammatical and conceptual face in the Hebrew creation account.
- **Greek Gaia**: The primordial earth-goddess who generates the universe from herself without a male partner (in Hesiod's *Theogony* she produces Ouranos without copulation). Gaia as the self-generating cosmic female womb is the Greek parallel to the yoni-as-creative-source.
- **Geometric reception (Gothic architecture)**: Art historian Otto von Simson (*The Gothic Cathedral*, Princeton UP, 1956 — Tier 1) observes that the pointed Gothic arch and the **mandorla** (vesica piscis) surrounding Christ Pantokrator are geometrically identical to the yoni-triangle form. He does not use the term *yoni* but documents the structural parallel: the sacred opening through which the divine enters the world is geometrically the same pointed-oval form across Hindu and Gothic architectural traditions. No documented transmission; parallel-form.

## Contested interpretations

- **Whether the Venus figurines represent a "Great Goddess" tradition**: The reading of Paleolithic Venus figurines as representing a unified prehistoric goddess religion (Gimbutas *The Goddess and Gods of Old Europe*, 1974 — Tier 2/3) has been substantially challenged by archaeologists who note the figurines' diversity of form and context. The connection between Paleolithic female figurines and the Hindu yoni tradition is thus a **parallel-form** observation, not a historical continuity claim.
- **The Gothic-arch-as-yoni claim**: Simson's (1956) art-historical reading of the Gothic pointed arch as a vesica piscis / sacred feminine opening is an interpretive frame, not a claim about medieval intentionality. Medieval builders and theologians did not call the arch a "yoni." The connection is structural-geometric, not documented.

## Mystery / unknown variants

The **Indus Valley connection** remains the primary open question: the ring-stones and cylindrical stones from Mohenjo-Daro that appear to be proto-lingam and proto-yoni forms cannot be definitively interpreted because the Indus Valley script is undeciphered and the religion they served is unknown. The morphological similarity to later Hindu forms is striking; the religious-historical continuity is unproven.

## Cross-symbol edges (provisional)

- **→ [[shiva-lingam]]** (syncretic-fusion): The inseparable pair. Yoni (Śakti / Prakṛti / dynamic creative nature) + Lingam (Śiva / Puruṣa / static consciousness) = manifest cosmos. The compound image encodes the complete Hindu cosmological process.
- **→ [[sri-yantra]]** (syncretic-fusion): The Sri Yantra's five downward-pointing (yoni) and four upward-pointing (lingam) triangles IS the yoni-lingam cosmology as two-dimensional sacred geometry.
- **→ [[mandorla]]** (parallel-form): The mandorla / vesica piscis surrounding Christ Pantokrator and the Gothic pointed arch share the yoni's pointed-oval geometric form as "the sacred opening through which divinity enters the world."
- **→ [[vesica-piscis]]** (parallel-form): The vesica piscis IS the yoni-triangle's geometric equivalent — the pointed oval generated by the intersection of two equal circles.
- **→ [[sheela-na-gig]]** (parallel-form): Medieval Irish displayed-vulva apotropaic figure and the Hindu cosmic yoni are parallel attestations — across 7,000 miles and unconnected traditions — of the sacred-vulva vocabulary as creative-and-protective power.
- **→ [[cosmic-egg]]** (parallel-form): Both the yoni and the cosmic egg (Hiranyagarbha / golden womb) represent the container of undifferentiated creative potential from which manifest existence emerges. The yoni is the bodily form of what the egg encodes geometrically.
