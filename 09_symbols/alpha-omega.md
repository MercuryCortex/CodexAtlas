# Alpha and Omega (Α–Ω)

The first and last letters of the Greek alphabet as a divine title claiming totality — Christ as the beginning and end of all things, expressed through the structure of language itself.

## Core Attestations

**Revelation 1:8** (c. 95 CE): "I am the Alpha and the Omega, says the Lord God, who is and who was and who is to come, the Almighty." Repeated at 21:6 and 22:13 with "the First and the Last, the Beginning and the End." This triple epithet frames the entirety of the Apocalypse.

**Visual attestation:** From the 4th century onward, alpha and omega are carved flanking the Chi-Rho on sarcophagi, mosaics, and baptismal fonts throughout the Mediterranean church. The Catacombs of Rome preserve the earliest instances. The Chi-Rho + Alpha-Omega became the standard Christological monogram of the Constantinian empire.

## Pre-Christian Substrate: Aleph-Tav

The Hebrew analogue — *aleph-tav*, first and last letters of the Hebrew alphabet — appears as the direct object marker in biblical Hebrew (invisible in translation). The mystical reading (attested in rabbinic literature and Kabbalistic commentary) treats aleph-tav as encoding divine totality in the grammatical fabric of the Torah itself.

**Talmudic emet (truth):** Babylonian Talmud Shabbat 55a: "God's seal is truth (*emet*)." The word *emet* (אמת) spans aleph (first) + mem (middle) + tav (last) = a letter from each end and the center of the Hebrew alphabet = divine truth encompasses all language.

## Cross-Tradition Parallel: AUM

The Mandukya Upanishad opens: "All this is, indeed, Brahman. This Atman is Brahman." AUM is then analyzed as encompassing all states of consciousness and all of reality. Like alpha-omega, AUM is simultaneously a sound, a divine name, and a claim that this specific phoneme IS the totality of what exists. The structural logic is identical; the traditions are fully independent.

## Hermetic Qabalah

In the Hermetic Qabalah (Sefer Yetzirah; systematized by Isaac Luria in Safed, 1570s), the 22 Hebrew letters are the building blocks of creation — the 22 paths on the Kabbalistic Tree of Life linking the ten sefirot. The aleph-tav totality maps directly onto the structural claim that the alphabet IS the universe's architecture.
