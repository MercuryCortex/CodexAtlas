# Green Lion (Viridis Leo)

## Identity

The **Viridis Leo** — the Green Lion — is one of the most enigmatic and visually arresting emblems in Western alchemical iconography: **a green lion devouring the sun**. The solar disk (the great gold disc with a human face, representing the solar metal gold and the perfected alchemical state) is consumed by a rampant lion whose unnatural *green* color marks it as something outside the natural order — the **corrosive prima materia in its hungry, pre-transformed state**. The Green Lion represents the raw operative force that **dissolves gold** (the most perfect metal, the end-goal of the alchemical Work) as the *first act* of the Great Work: before gold can be recreated at a higher level, it must first be stripped, dissolved, devoured.

The canonical form is found in **[[michael-maier|Michael Maier]]'s *Atalanta Fugiens* (1617; [[phase-6-035-maier-atalanta-fugiens]])**, Emblem XXIV, and in the **[[phase-6-032-rosarium-philosophorum|Rosarium Philosophorum]]**. The symbol became one of the most discussed and debated images in alchemical literature — its paradox (why would you dissolve the very gold you are trying to create?) is the paradox of the entire Great Work: the *solve* must precede the *coagula*.

## Earliest attestation

- **c. 13th–14th c. CE** — Earliest appearances of the green lion and lion-devouring-sun imagery in Latin alchemical manuscripts. The color-coding (green = prima-materia stage, unripe, corrosive) is established in this period.
- **c. 1470** — [[phase-6-032-rosarium-philosophorum|Rosarium Philosophorum]] (manuscript; printed 1550) — the lion-and-sun imagery is prominent.
- **c. 1532** — *Splendor Solis* (attributed to Salomon Trismosin) — the green lion and green dragon appear in the iconic miniature painting tradition.
- **1591** — George Ripley, *Compound of Alchemy* ([[phase-6-046-ripley-compound-of-alchemy]]) — Green Lion prominent.
- **1617** — [[phase-6-035-maier-atalanta-fugiens|Michael Maier, *Atalanta Fugiens*]] Emblem XXIV — the most influential single depiction; the copperplate engraving by Matthäus Merian defines the image's canonical form.

## Meaning(s) attested

- **Operative-chemical** — Historians of chemistry identify the Green Lion operatively with **sulfuric acid** (oil of vitriol, *oleum vitriolum* — the corrosive green-blue liquid that dissolves metals) or with **aqua regia** (the royal water of mixed acids that dissolves gold). The "devouring of the sun" is the *actual laboratory operation* of dissolving gold in aqua regia. The green color directly references *green vitriol* (iron(II) sulfate, FeSO₄·7H₂O) and *blue vitriol* (copper sulfate, CuSO₄·5H₂O) — the vivid green-blue corrosives that were the alchemical laboratory's most powerful solvents. Principe 2013 establishes this operative reading as primary.
- **Alchemical-cosmological** — The Green Lion represents the **prima materia** in its first active stage: the undifferentiated base-matter in its corrosive, hungry, destructive manifestation. It must devour the gold (the apparent goal) in order for the gold to be reborn at a higher level through the subsequent operations of the Great Work.
- **Color-symbolic** — Green in alchemical color theory = **Venus** (copper/verdigris), **the unripe** (green fruit before golden maturity), **the beginning** (spring, growth, raw potential). The lion that *should* be golden (solar, regal) is instead green (Venusian, corrosive, hungry) — the inversion signals its prima-materia status.
- **Jungian-psychological** — [[carl-gustav-jung]] (*Psychology and Alchemy*, 1944) reads the Green Lion as the **devouring aspect of the unconscious** — the instinctual, shadowy, destructive-creative force that must consume the inflated solar ego (the persona's "gold") before genuine individuation can proceed. The Green Lion is the *nigredo-agent*, the psychological dissolution-force.

## Variants

See YAML. The principal variants are: (1) the canonical lion-devouring-sun (Maier's Emblem XXIV); (2) the Green Dragon variant (same substance, chthonic-serpentine form); (3) the operative chemical identification (vitriol / aqua regia); (4) the Venus-green symbolic strand.

## Cross-family appearance

The Green Lion as a *named alchemical emblem* is Western-European. But the underlying structure — **a solar force turned against itself in an act of destructive regeneration, embodied in a theriomorphic lion-figure** — appears across traditions:

### Massive Win 1: Sekhmet and the Eye of Ra (Egypt)
In Egyptian mythology, the **Eye of Ra** — the solar force personified — takes the form of a **lioness** ([[sekhmet|Sekhmet]]) when it is dispatched to destroy humanity. Sekhmet goes berserk, nearly annihilating all humans, until Ra halts her by flooding the fields with beer dyed red to look like blood. The myth's structure is: **the solar force (Ra's Eye) becomes its own destructive principle (Sekhmet the Lioness) and threatens to destroy the very humanity the sun sustains** — exactly the paradox of the Green Lion devouring the Sun. In both cases, a lion-form embodies the solar energy in its destructive, self-consuming manifestation. The difference: in the Egyptian myth, the lion-eye is a *solar emanation* turned destructive; in the alchemical Green Lion, the lion is the *prima-materia corrosive* that destroys the solar gold. The functional parallel is remarkable. [[sekhmet]] and [[tradition-egyptian-religion]] carry this.

### Massive Win 2: The Leontocephalus of Mithraism
The most mysterious figure in Mithraic iconography is the **Leontocephalus** — "lion-headed" — a male figure (often with wings) coiled by a serpent, with a lion's head where a human head should be, holding keys and a thunderbolt. The Leontocephalus has been identified with Aion (infinite time), Zurvan (Zoroastrian boundless time), and Phanes (the Orphic first-born light-being). The lion-headed Time-god is the principle that devours the solar cycles, consuming each aeon as it passes. The Green Lion devouring the Sun and the Leontocephalus devouring-time-by-being-time are structurally related as images of a lion-force consuming the solar principle. [[mithras-roman]] and [[tradition-mithraic-mysteries]] carry this; [[tauroctony]] is the better-known Mithraic symbol node.

### Massive Win 3: Dionysus and the Theriomorphic Lion-Force
**[[dionysus|Dionysus]]** is accompanied by lions in his *thiasos* (divine entourage); he is himself described in Euripides' *Bacchae* as appearing in lion-form. The Dionysiac lion represents the ecstatic, boundary-dissolving, intoxicating force of Dionysus — the force that dissolves civic order (as the Green Lion dissolves gold) in preparation for a higher form of life. The Dionysiac-lion-as-dissolver is functionally parallel to the Green Lion-as-prima-materia-solvent. [[dionysus]] and [[tradition-dionysian-mysteries]] are the nodes.

### Massive Win 4: Lion of Judah and Masonic Lion's Paw
The **Lion of Judah** (Genesis 49:9; Revelation 5:5) — the royal tribal emblem of Judah and the Messianic title — is the lion that *overcomes* the solar enemy. In **Freemasonry**, the "lion's grip" and "lion's paw" are ritual vocabulary in the **Master Mason degree (3rd degree)** concerning the resurrection of Hiram Abiff. The "lion's paw" is the grip by which the Master Mason is raised from the "dead" (the symbolic grave of the nigredo) — the same dissolution-and-raising structure as the Green Lion devouring the Sun and then (in the subsequent alchemical operations) releasing the purified solar gold. [[tradition-freemasonry]] and [[tradition-scottish-rite-freemasonry]] are the institutional vehicles for this Masonic lion-symbolism.

## Contested interpretations

- **Is the Green Lion primarily operative-chemical or primarily symbolic-spiritual?** Principe 2013 argues decisively for the operative reading (the green vitriol / aqua regia identification) as primary, with the symbolic layer *real but secondary*. Against purely Jungian readings that dematerialize the symbol entirely.
- **What is the Green Lion's relationship to the Green Dragon?** Most alchemical commentators treat them as interchangeable names for the same prima-materia substance. Some (notably Paracelsian-influenced texts) distinguish them: the Lion = the fixed corrosive (vitriol); the Dragon = the volatile corrosive (acid vapor). The distinction is not consistent across the tradition.
- **Operative identity question** — The Green Lion has been identified as: (a) sulfuric acid, (b) hydrochloric acid, (c) aqua regia, (d) concentrated nitric acid, (e) a mercury-sulfur amalgam, depending on the text and period. No single operative identification is universally accepted; Principe 2013 surveys the competing identifications.

## Mystery / unknown variants

The *precise* alchemical identity of the Green Lion — which specific corrosive substance or mixture it referred to in which alchemical manuscript context — is an active area of historical-chemistry research. Different textual traditions use the term for different substances. The symbol is "documented" in its iconographic attestation but "contested" in its operative-chemical identity.
