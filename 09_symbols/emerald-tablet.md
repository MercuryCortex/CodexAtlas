# Emerald Tablet

## Identity

The **Emerald Tablet** (*Tabula Smaragdina*) is the most influential single text in the history of Western esotericism — a 13-sentence statement attributed to **Hermes Trismegistus** that encodes the principle of macrocosm-microcosm correspondence and the alchemical program in the most compressed and memorable form ever achieved. It is the source of the dictum universally known as **"As above, so below."**

The Tablet is **not ancient Egyptian**. It is an **Arabic-Islamic construction** of the 8th–9th century CE that *claims* ancient Hermetic authority — part of the systematic Arabization of Greek Hermetic and Neoplatonic lore during the Islamic Golden Age. Its earliest surviving text appears in *Kitāb Sirr al-Khalīqa* (*Book of the Secret of Creation*) attributed to **'Balīnās'** (pseudo-Apollonius of Tyana). There is no Greek original. This is crucial: the Tablet is a product of **[[tradition-hermeticism|the Arabic Hermes tradition]]**, not of late-antique Greek Hermeticism (the [[phase-4-011-corpus-hermeticum-i|Corpus Hermeticum]]). The Corpus Hermeticum contains nothing like the Tablet.

## The Text (13 sentences)

The core Latin version (*Ruska 1926*; standard Scholastic-alchemical text c. 12th–13th c.):

1. *Verum, sine mendacio, certum et verissimum.* — True, without falsehood, certain and most true.
2. *Quod est inferius est sicut quod est superius, et quod est superius est sicut quod est inferius, ad perpetranda miracula rei unius.* — **That which is below is like that which is above, and that which is above is like that which is below, to accomplish the miracles of the one thing.**
3. *Et sicut res omnes fuerunt ab uno, meditatione unius: sic omnes res natae ab hac una re, adaptatione.* — And as all things have been and arose from one by the mediation of one, so all things have their birth from this one thing by adaptation.
4. *Pater eius est Sol, mater eius est Luna.* — The sun is its father, the moon its mother.
5. *Portavit illud Ventus in ventre suo.* — The wind carried it in its belly.
6. *Nutrix eius Terra est.* — The earth is its nurse.
7. *Pater omnis telesmi totius mundi est hic.* — The father of all perfection in the whole world is here.
8. *Virtus eius integra est, si versa fuerit in terram.* — Its power is integral if it be turned into earth.
9. *Separabis terram ab igne, subtile a spisso, suaviter, cum magno ingenio.* — Separate thou the earth from the fire, the subtle from the gross, sweetly with great industry.
10. *Ascendit a terra in coelum, iterumque descendit in terram, et recipit vim superiorum et inferiorum.* — It ascends from the earth to the heaven and again it descends to the earth and receives the force of things superior and inferior.
11. *Sic habebis gloriam totius mundi. Ideo fugiet a te omnis obscuritas.* — By this means you shall have the glory of the whole world and thereby all obscurity shall fly from you.
12. *Haec est totius fortitudinis fortitudo fortis, quia vincet omnem rem subtilem, omnemque solidam penetrabit.* — Its force is above all force, for it vanquishes every subtle thing and penetrates every solid thing.
13. *Sic mundus creatus est. Hinc erunt adaptationes mirabiles, quarum modus est hic. Itaque vocatus sum Hermes Trismegistus, habens tres partes philosophiae totius mundi. Completum est quod dixi de operatione Solis.* — So was the world created. From this are and do come admirable adaptations of which the means (or process) is here in this. Therefore am I called Hermes Trismegistus, having the three parts of the philosophy of the whole world. That which I have said of the operation of the Sun is accomplished and ended.

## Textual History

### The Arabic Origin (c. 800 CE)

The text embedded in *Kitāb Sirr al-Khalīqa* is presented via a literary **discovery frame**: Balīnās descends into an underground vault, finds an old man seated on a golden throne holding a tablet of emerald (*lauḥ zabarjad*), and copies the inscription. The vault-discovery frame is the *Vorlage* (source-template) for later esoteric vault-discovery narratives — including the Rosicrucian *Fama*'s vault of Christian Rosenkreutz (1614).

The **Arabic Hermes tradition** (al-Kindi, Jābir ibn Ḥayyān, the Brethren of Purity / Ikhwān al-Ṣafā) was the crucial transmitter of the macrocosm-microcosm correspondence doctrine from Greek Neoplatonism into Islamic philosophical and alchemical thought. The Tablet is the most compact Arabic-Hermetic statement of that doctrine.

### The Latin Reception (c. 1140 CE onward)

**Hugo of Santalla** (Tarazona, c. 1140–1150) produced the first Latin translation as part of the Toledo translation movement that transferred Arabic scientific and philosophical learning to Latin Europe. From this point the Tablet becomes foundational to all Latin alchemy.

**Gerard of Cremona** (c. 1175) produced an independent translation. Both translations circulate widely in alchemical compilations. By the 13th century the Tablet appears in Roger Bacon's citations, Albert the Great's *Liber Mineralium*, and virtually every major alchemical compilation.

### Newton's Translation (c. 1680)

**Isaac Newton** (1642–1727) translated the Tablet in English (Keynes MS 28, King's College Library Cambridge) as part of his extensive private alchemical research. Newton spent an estimated one million words on alchemy over several decades. The Tablet's program — identify the one substance, separate and recombine it through operations — maps directly onto Newton's matter-theory and his interest in active principles in nature. The Newton connection is the most decisive single demonstration that the early modern scientific program was not sharply separated from the Hermetic tradition the Tablet represents.

## "As Above, So Below" — The MASSIVE WIN

The Tablet's second sentence — *quod est inferius est sicut quod est superius* — is the most memorable formulation of the **macrocosm-microcosm correspondence** principle in world literature. The same principle was reached independently in at least five major traditions:

### 1. Hermetic-Alchemical
The Tablet's own statement. Planets above govern metals below; the soul's operations mirror material operations; the *opus* (the alchemical work) is simultaneously a physical and spiritual transformation because above and below correspond. The [[philosophers-stone|Philosopher's Stone]] is what the principle commands to be made.

### 2. Kabbalistic
*Zohar* (R. Moses de León, c. 1280 CE): *"There is no grass below that has not a mazal above who strikes it and says: Grow."* The divine emanations (**[[sefirot-tree-of-life|Sefirot]]**) above are reflected in earthly structures and the human soul below. The Tree of Life is a map of the above-below correspondence. The Kabbalistic and Hermetic traditions **merged** in the Renaissance (Pico della Mirandola, Reuchlin, Agrippa) to produce Christian Kabbalah — which reached its visual peak in Dee's [[monas-hieroglyphica|Monas Hieroglyphica]].

### 3. Vedantic-Hindu
*Chāndogya Upaniṣad* (c. 700 BCE): **'Tat tvam asi'** — "That thou art" — Brahman (cosmic absolute) = Ātman (inner self). The macrocosm and the microcosm are not merely *analogous* but *identical*. The Vedantic solution is more radical than the Tablet's: not just correspondence but identity. Yet both traditions share the fundamental move of treating the human being as a map of cosmic reality.

### 4. Stoic Cosmology
The *logos* (rational fire, divine reason) pervades all levels of existence. The rational fire above (in the aether, the *pneuma* of the cosmos) corresponds to the rational fire within the human soul (*hegemonikon*). Marcus Aurelius: the universe is a single living being with a single soul; to know the part is to know the whole. The Stoic tradition entered Arabic philosophy through the Neoplatonic synthesis and is one of the indirect sources of the Arabic Hermes tradition.

### 5. Chinese Correlative Cosmology
Heaven/Earth/Humanity (*tiān/dì/rén*) correspondence: the triad of levels through which *qì* flows, carrying the patterns of one level into the others. The 64 hexagrams of the **I Ching** encode correspondences between cosmic, social, and personal states — the same macrocosm-microcosm mapping the Tablet performs. Chinese correlative cosmology operates via **resonance** (*gǎnyìng*): things of the same category at different levels mutually activate each other, exactly as the Tablet's metals below respond to planets above.

**All five arrived at structurally identical conclusions via independent paths.** The Emerald Tablet is the most *memorable* and most *compressed* formulation of a philosophical insight that is genuinely cross-cultural. This is the grade-A MASSIVE WIN in the Tablet's dossier.

## The Alchemical Program

Beyond "as above, so below," the Tablet encodes a complete operational program:

1. **The one thing** (*res una*) — a single primal substance underlies all material reality (the *prima materia*)
2. **Solar-lunar parenting** — the one thing is animated by solar (sulfur/active) and lunar (mercury/passive) principles
3. **Wind and Earth as vehicle and nurse** — the one thing is carried by the volatile (*spiritus*, air) and grounded by the fixed (body, earth)
4. **Separation and conjunction** — "separate earth from fire, subtle from gross" = the alchemical operations of *separatio* and *coniunctio*
5. **The ascending-descending cycle** — the *opus circulatorium*: volatilize, elevate, fix; the alchemical equivalent of the spiritual descent-and-return
6. **"All obscurity will flee"** — gnosis as the by-product of successful operation

This is the blueprint for [[phase-4-074-zosimos-of-panopolis-corpus|Zosimos]]'s visions, [[phase-5-039-jabir-corpus-arabic-alchemy|Jabir]]'s balance theory, [[phase-6-008-paracelsus-corpus|Paracelsus]]'s tria prima, and [[phase-6-046-ripley-compound-of-alchemy|Ripley]]'s Compound of Alchemy — a single 13-sentence text generating 1,000 years of operational elaboration.

## Cross-Tradition Reception

The Tablet's influence is traceable in:

- **Islamic alchemy** ([[phase-5-039-jabir-corpus-arabic-alchemy|Jabir corpus]]): the Tablet's one-thing doctrine underlies Jabir's *mīzān* (balance) theory and his serial derivation of substances from a single base
- **Paracelsian medicine** ([[phase-6-008-paracelsus-corpus|Paracelsus]]): the tria prima (sulfur/mercury/salt) is a reformulation of the Tablet's one-thing with solar-lunar-elemental structure
- **Rosicrucianism** ([[rose-cross-rosicrucian|Rose Cross]]): the *Fama*'s vault-of-Rosenkreutz structurally mirrors the Tablet's discovery frame; the Rosicrucian program is Hermetic-alchemical in explicit self-identification
- **English Renaissance alchemy** ([[phase-6-046-ripley-compound-of-alchemy|Ripley]]): the Tablet is quoted as foundational authority in virtually all English alchemical texts
- **Newton** (Keynes MS 28): the Tablet is the theoretical statement that Newton took seriously enough to translate and annotate — his alchemical work and his physics share the one-substance assumption
