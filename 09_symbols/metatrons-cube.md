# Metatron's Cube

## Identity
**Metatron's Cube** is a sacred geometric figure derived from the **[[flower-of-life]]**: extract the 13-circle **Fruit of Life** subset (one central circle, six touching it, six alternating in the second ring), then draw straight lines connecting the center of every circle to the center of every other circle. The resulting figure contains the **two-dimensional projections of all five Platonic solids** — the tetrahedron, cube (hexahedron), octahedron, dodecahedron, and icosahedron — the five perfect geometric forms identified by [[plato]] in the *Timaeus* ([[phase-3-002-plato-dialogues]], c. 360 BCE) as the geometric building blocks of the four elements and the cosmos.

**Critical attribution note**: The name "Metatron's Cube" and the attribution of this figure to the angel [[metatron]] is a construction of the **modern New Age sacred-geometry movement** (primarily Drunvalo Melchizedek's *Flower of Life* workshop series, 1985 onward, formalized in print 1990). There is **no classical Jewish or Kabbalistic text** that calls this figure "Metatron's Cube" or associates it with the angel Metatron. The figure itself, as a geometric construction, is modern. The angel Metatron is ancient (attested in *3 Enoch* / *Sefer Hekhalot*, c. 5th–6th c. CE, and in Babylonian Talmudic material). The Platonic solid substrate is ancient (Plato, -360). These are three separate historical layers that the modern name conflates.

## Earliest attestation
- **Platonic solid substrate**: Plato, *Timaeus* c. 360 BCE ([[phase-3-002-plato-dialogues]]) — five geometric solids assigned to four elements + cosmos/ether. This is the foundational claim that all five Platonic solids together constitute the structure of reality.
- **The angel Metatron**: *3 Enoch* (*Sefer Hekhalot*, c. 400–600 CE) identifies Metatron as the transformed patriarch Enoch, now the highest angel, "Prince of the Presence," the "Lesser YHWH." The *1 Enoch* tradition ([[phase-3-004-1-enoch]]) provides the Enoch substrate; the full Metatron theology is in the late antique/early medieval hekhalot literature (Scholem 1960).
- **The Flower of Life substrate**: possibly Abydos, Egypt, c. 1290 BCE (attribution contested — see [[flower-of-life]]).
- **The modern figure "Metatron's Cube"**: Drunvalo Melchizedek, *The Ancient Secret of the Flower of Life* workshops (1985 onward), published 1990. The figure in this exact form with this name is modern.
- **Johannes Kepler's parallel**: Kepler's *Mysterium Cosmographicum* (1596) — the documented historical use of all five Platonic solids as cosmic structure (nested between the planetary orbits). Kepler's diagram is the most important pre-Melchizedek historical precedent, even though Kepler did not produce the same geometric figure.

## Meaning(s) attested
- **In Platonic cosmology**: Each of the five Platonic solids corresponds to an element — tetrahedron (fire), cube (earth), octahedron (air), icosahedron (water), dodecahedron (the cosmos / ether / quintessence). The cosmos is built from geometric forms, not material atoms. Cornford (1937) gives the foundational critical analysis.
- **In modern sacred-geometry reception**: Metatron's Cube is held to be the geometric "blueprint" from which all material structures are derived — the master template of physical reality. The 13 circles represent the 13 informational systems in the human body (a claim without scientific basis — document as Tier-3). The figure is used as a meditation object for accessing "the building blocks of creation."
- **Kabbalistic reading (modern)**: The angel Metatron's role as the divine intermediary and recorder (*sofer*, scribe of all human deeds) is mapped to the figure's role as the geometric record of all possible forms. Metatron as the "keeper of the blueprint" is a natural symbolic fit, even if the connection is modern.
- **Hermetic reading**: The synthesis of all geometric forms in one figure exemplifies the Hermetic principle *omnia in unum* ("all in one") — the same principle underlying John Dee's [[monas-hieroglyphica]] and the Rosicrucian alchemical synthesis emblem.

## Cross-family appearance
The figure itself is modern (1980s–1990s). Its components have ancient cross-tradition parallels:

**The Platonic-solid-as-cosmic-structure claim across traditions:**

| Tradition | Claim | Period | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greek Pythagorean/Platonic | Five solids = four elements + cosmos | c. -360 | Plato, *Timaeus*; Cornford 1937 |
| Platonic-Islamic | Five solids transmitted through Islamic Neoplatonism (Ikhwan al-Safa, *Rasa'il*, 10th c.) | c. 950 CE | Nasr 1993 |
| Renaissance Hermetic | Kepler's *Mysterium Cosmographicum* (all 5 solids nested as planetary orbits) | 1596 | Kepler 1596/1981 |
| Modern sacred geometry | Metatron's Cube contains all 5 Platonic solids = geometric blueprint of reality | 1985–present | Melchizedek 1990 |
| Molecular chemistry | C60 Buckminsterfullerene = truncated icosahedron; carbon structures encode Platonic geometries | 1985 | Kroto et al. 1985 |

**Cross-tradition finding — Kepler's Mysterium Cosmographicum**: In 1596, Johannes Kepler proposed that the six planetary orbits then known (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn) were separated by the five Platonic solids nested within each other — cube inside octahedron inside icosahedron inside dodecahedron inside tetrahedron, with the sun at the center. This is a documented historical attempt to find ALL FIVE Platonic solids as a single system structuring cosmic architecture — exactly the same claim as Metatron's Cube, though expressed as a 3D nested-solid model rather than a 2D circle-line diagram. Kepler's Mysterium Cosmographicum is the most important historical precedent for the "all Platonic solids as one cosmic structure" claim.

**Cross-tradition finding — C60 and molecular geometry**: The Buckminsterfullerene molecule (C60, discovered 1985, Nobel Prize 1996) is a truncated icosahedron — one of the Archimedean solids derived from the Platonic icosahedron. Sacred geometry appears in molecular chemistry: the icosahedron (one of Metatron's Cube's five Platonic solids) is a naturally occurring geometric principle in carbon bonding, viral capsid structure, and quasicrystal formation.

## Contested interpretations
- **The modern origin of the figure**: The most important contested claim is the implicit historical antiquity suggested by the name "Metatron's Cube" and by the attribution to an ancient angel. The figure is modern; the name is modern; the connection to Metatron is modern. This is thoroughly documented and must be stated clearly.
- **The Platonic solid encoding claim**: That Metatron's Cube contains all five Platonic solids as 2D projections is mathematically verifiable (and correct). The Platonic-solid encoding is real geometry; the cosmic-significance claim is an interpretive layer.
- **Kepler's system as validation**: Some sacred-geometry enthusiasts cite Kepler's *Mysterium Cosmographicum* as "proof" that Metatron's Cube is cosmologically valid. Kepler's own nested-solid model was his pre-discovery framework; after his discovery of elliptical planetary orbits (1609), he knew the Mysterium model was wrong. Its historical significance is as a document of Renaissance Platonic-geometric cosmology, not as a validated cosmic model.
