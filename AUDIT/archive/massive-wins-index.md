### 2.1 Isis Lactans → Madonna and Child `[TRANSMISSION]`
→ [`divine-child`](../09_symbols/divine-child.md)

The compositional scheme — divine mother enthroned, holding an infant god at breast or lap — passes from Egyptian into Coptic Christian and then into Western Christian iconography. One of the best-documented cases of religious iconographic transmission in art history. The Madonna and Child is an Egyptian theological image wearing Christian clothes.
