## Tier 1 — Highest graph leverage (touches many existing nodes)

### Foundational religious events
- **Karbala — Martyrdom of Husayn ibn Ali — 680 CE**
  - Foundational event of Shīʿīsm. Touches: tradition-shia-islam, tradition-islam, husayn-ibn-ali (currently dead-link from tradition-shia-islam).
  - Source: Mahmoud Ayoub, *Redemptive Suffering in Islam* (Mouton, 1978).

- **Council of Carthage 397 CE**
  - Fixes the NT canon for the Latin West (revisiting Hippo 393). Touches: tradition-christianity-canonical, augustine-of-hippo (likely attended), the gospel/epistle docs.
  - Source: Bruce M. Metzger, *The Canon of the New Testament* (Oxford UP, 1987).

- **Council of Hippo 393 CE** — precursor to Carthage.

- **Council of Laodicea ~363 CE** — early canon list (excludes Revelation).

- **Second Council of Nicaea 787 CE** — already a dead-link target in your vault. Iconoclast resolution.
  - Source: Leslie Brubaker & John Haldon, *Byzantium in the Iconoclast Era* (Cambridge UP, 2011).

- **Synod of Whitby 664 CE** — Roman vs. Celtic Christianity (Easter dating); Latin victory; turning point for Insular Christianity.
  - Source: Henry Mayr-Harting, *The Coming of Christianity to Anglo-Saxon England* (Penn State UP, 1991).

- **Fourth Lateran Council 1215** — annual confession, transubstantiation, distinguishing dress for Jews and Muslims. Major medieval policy synthesis.
  - Source: Brenda Bolton, *The Medieval Reformation* (Edward Arnold, 1983); R.I. Moore, *The Formation of a Persecuting Society* (Blackwell, 1987).

### Decipherment / discovery events (transformative for the timeline)

- **Decipherment of Linear B — Michael Ventris, 1952**
  - **HIGHEST PRIORITY**. Pinned the Olympian pantheon to Bronze Age Mycenaean Greek tablets — Zeus, Hera, Poseidon, Athena, Artemis, Dionysus, Hermes are attested in Linear B from ~1300 BCE. *Direct evidence* that the Olympian pantheon predates Homer by ~700 years. Massive comparative-religion datum that reshapes Phase 1–2 dating of Greek religion.
  - Source: John Chadwick, *The Decipherment of Linear B* (Cambridge UP, 1958); Stephanie Lynn Budin, *The Ancient Greeks* (ABC-CLIO, 2009).

- **Boğazköy / Ḫattuša archive discovery — Hugo Winckler, 1906**
  - Made the Kumarbi-Hesiod transmission demonstrable. Massive Phase 1 leverage.
  - Source: Trevor Bryce, *The Kingdom of the Hittites* (Oxford UP, 2nd ed. 2005).

- **Behistun decipherment — Henry Rawlinson, 1830s–1850s**
  - Old Persian / Elamite / Akkadian Rosetta-equivalent. Without this, no Mesopotamian study.
  - Source: Maurice Pope, *The Story of Decipherment* (Thames & Hudson, 1975, rev. 1999).

- **Mari archive discovery — André Parrot, 1933 onward**
  - Old Babylonian religious + administrative life. ~25,000 tablets.
  - Source: Jean-Marie Durand, *Documents Épistolaires du Palais de Mari* (Cerf, multiple vols.); Jack M. Sasson, *From the Mari Archives* (Eisenbrauns, 2015).

### Reformation / printing events

- **Gutenberg Bible — 1455**
  - First major printed book; the technical-economic basis for the Reformation a generation later.

- **Erasmus — *Novum Instrumentum Omne* — 1516**
  - First printed Greek NT; the textual basis for Luther's German translation and the entire Reformation-era Bible movement.
  - Source: Erika Rummel, *Erasmus' Annotations on the New Testament* (U Toronto Press, 1986).

- **Lorenzo Valla exposes the Donation of Constantine — 1440** (published 1517 in Hutten's edition)
  - Birth of modern philology. Demonstrated a foundational papal document was an 8th-c. forgery, using internal linguistic-historical evidence. Direct precursor to Renaissance humanist textual criticism and to all subsequent biblical higher criticism.
  - Source: Riccardo Fubini, *Humanism and Secularization* (Duke UP, 2003).

- **Diet of Worms 1521** — Luther's "Here I stand"; formal Imperial condemnation.

- **Council of Florence 1438–45**
  - Failed Greek-Latin reunion; but Plethon's attendance led to the transmission of Plato to Ficino — directly catalysed Renaissance hermeticism and Phase 6 Renaissance-Hermeticism tradition.
  - Source: Joseph Gill, *The Council of Florence* (Cambridge UP, 1959).

### Major persecutions / wars that affect the textual record

- **Tang Huichang persecution of Buddhism — 845 CE**
  - Paradigmatic East Asian state-vs-saṅgha event. ~4,600 monasteries destroyed, ~260,000 monks/nuns forced to laity. Reshaped Chinese Buddhism toward Chan/Pure Land away from text-heavy Huayan/Tiantai.
  - Source: Stanley Weinstein, *Buddhism Under the T'ang* (Cambridge UP, 1987).

- **Taiping Rebellion — 1850–1864**
  - Christian-millenarian rebellion led by Hong Xiuquan (self-identified as Jesus's younger brother). ~20–30 million dead. Largest religious war in human history.
  - Source: Jonathan Spence, *God's Chinese Son* (Norton, 1996).

- **Buddhist transmission to Japan — 552 CE** (or 538 per Nihon Shoki; date disputed).

- **Saichō and Kūkai bring Tendai and Shingon from China — 805–806 CE**.

## Suggested event-template additions

The methodology's edge taxonomy is good for documents but events could use additional edge types:

- `precondition-for` — Event A enables Event B (e.g., Linear B decipherment → revised Phase 1–2 Greek religion dating).
- `triggered-by` — Event A directly causes Event B (e.g., Council of Trent triggered by Lutheran reformation).
- `enabled-by-discovery` — Modern scholarly understanding of tradition X is contingent on discovery event Y.

This last edge type is especially valuable because half the value of an events folder is showing how *our knowledge* of religious history was constructed.
