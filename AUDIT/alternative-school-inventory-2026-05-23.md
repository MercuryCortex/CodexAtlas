> **Purpose.** Canonical inventory of the alternative-school and popular-rejected authors and their claims that the Codex Atlas should include — under the 2026-05-23 four-tier source-tier system. Each row lists author + work + claim + tier + mainstream rebuttal + INCLUDE-recommendation, so the next bulk content batch can be sequenced without re-deriving the research.

## 1. Methodology — the four tiers in operational form

The 2026-05-23 tier system collapses fuzziness across previous practice into four bins:

| Tier | Operational test | Vault posture |
|---|---|---|
| **T1** | Mainstream peer-reviewed; the consensus position of the discipline | Cite as primary rebuttal source. `tier: 1` in `refs:` |
| **T2** | Tenured academic / credentialed dissent from disciplinary consensus, published through normal academic venues, engaged-by rather than dismissed by mainstream colleagues | Include as node; flag dissent-from-consensus in body; cite BOTH the T2 claim AND the T1 rebuttal |
| **T3** | Alternative-school: sustained published corpus, major trade-press publishers, large readership, **rejected by mainstream but with academic critics engaging the claims** (not pseudoarchaeology in the diagnostic sense; the work is engaged-with even when refuted) | Include as node. Body must state mainstream rejection clearly and cite at least one peer-reviewed rebuttal in `refs:`. Carries `controversial-figure` tag |
| **T4** | Popular-claim-rejected: heavily promoted, near-universally rejected by relevant disciplines, no credentialed defenders, often shows the diagnostic features of pseudoarchaeology / pseudoscience (Stiebing 1984; Feder 2020) | Include only when circulation is high enough that omission would let misinformation circulate unrebutted (Sitchin, von Däniken, Cayce, Icke-Babylonian-Brotherhood, Atwill, Acharya S). Body LEADS with rebuttal. Carries `controversial-figure` + `pseudoarchaeology` (or appropriate `pseudo-*`) tag |

**Excluded entirely as out of scope:** authors whose claims attach to no specific deity / site / event / theme in the vault graph (pure science-conspiracy without religious-historical content); authors with negligible circulation; living social-media-only figures without a published corpus that scholars have engaged.

**Boundary cases (resolved in §7):** Carl Jung (T1 in psychology, T2 when read on mythology); Joseph Campbell (T2 academic figure with serious critique); Alain Daniélou (T2 — credentialed Indologist); Anne Baring (T2/T3 — outside formal academic appointment but engaged-with).

## 8. Edge cases / open questions

1. **Charles Hapgood** — already in vault at `04_persons/charles-hapgood.md`. He is a special case: a credentialed academic (Keene State College) whose crustal-displacement was a failed hypothesis but advanced through normal scientific channels — already correctly flagged in `pseudoarchaeology.md` as "not pseudoarchaeology." No new work needed.

2. **Joseph Campbell** — T2 by training/position; T3 by some methodological standards (cherry-picking, Frazer-style universalism). The vault already has the node — refine rather than recreate.

3. **Mauro Biglino** — Tiered position is genuinely ambiguous. He IS a credentialed translator (worked for Edizioni San Paolo, the Catholic publishing house) but his published-with-major-press-and-engaged-by-scholars status is mostly within Italian-language reception. The Brill *Handbook of UFO Religions* ch. 11 grants him case-study status; that justifies T3-not-T4. **Recommended: T3 with Italian-language flag in the body.**

4. **David Icke** — He is unambiguously T4 (no academic defenders, antisemitism documented, conspiracy-theory categorization by Barkun and the ADL). The question is whether to include him at all. **Recommendation: include**, because the "Marduk = reptilian" claim propagates on the open web and any Marduk-curious reader will hit Icke material; the vault's Marduk node needs an in-graph rebuttal. **Strict caveat:** wire Icke ONLY via `polemic` edges, never as a "religious thinker"; lead the node body with the ADL/Barkun rebuttal; tag explicitly `antisemitism-vector`.

5. **YDIH (Younger Dryas Impact Hypothesis)** — This is NOT T3. It is contested-but-published-in-PNAS-and-Earth-Science-Reviews science. Hancock + Carlson borrow it; but YDIH itself is **T2 contested science**. The theme node must distinguish "(a) the scientific YDIH (Firestone–Wittke–Sweatman–West) vs. (b) the Hancock cataclysmic-civilization-destruction reading of YDIH (which does not follow even if YDIH is correct)." This is the exact pattern the Hancock node already articulates — replicate.

6. **Drunvalo Melchizedek rebuttal gap** — No peer-reviewed scholar has engaged Drunvalo's mathematical-geometric claims because they fall too far outside any discipline's standards. The vault rebuttal must cite Hanegraaff 2013 for the cultural-phenomenon framing and acknowledge the peer-review-gap. This is a methodologically interesting case — a different kind of T4 than Sitchin (whom Assyriologists DO engage).

7. **Manly P. Hall** — Borderline T3/T4. Hall is an *encyclopedist* not a claim-originator — he compiled the perennial-philosophy story rather than inventing it. **Recommendation: T4 with framing as "primary source for 1920s American esotericism, not reliable historical source."** Hanegraaff 2013 treats Hall this way.

8. **Bauval** — T2 on the 1989 *Discussions in Egyptology* paper (peer-reviewed) and on the King's Chamber shaft alignments (mainstream-Egyptology-accepted). T3 on the 10,500 BCE precessional extension with Hancock and on *Black Genesis*. The existing node needs to make this split explicit.

9. **The "academic-aliens" Italian-language Biglino + the Italian Traditionalists (Evola, current heirs)** — There is a distinctive Italian-language esoteric current the English-language alt-archaeology literature has barely registered. Worth flagging that the vault's coverage is currently Anglo-centric and that future audits should sweep Italian + French + German alternative literatures (Charroux, Robert Bauer, etc.).

10. **Plant-of-renown / soma / kykeon ancient-drug literature** (R. Gordon Wasson, Carl Ruck, Terence McKenna) — Not in this inventory's scope but flagged as the NEXT alternative-school cluster to audit. McKenna's "stoned ape" hypothesis is T4 + Wasson's *Soma* is T2 — a similar tier-spread within one cluster.

11. **Ralph Ellis, Christopher Knight, Robert Lomas** ("Hiram Key" / Templar-Egyptian-Jesus genre) — T4 cluster; very high circulation; not covered in this inventory but flagged for future batch.
