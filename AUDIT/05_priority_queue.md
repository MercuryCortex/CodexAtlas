# Priority Queue — Top 10 Highest-Leverage Adds

If the agent only does ten things from this audit, do these. Ordered by ROI: highest first.

Each item lists the *first concrete step* an implementing agent should take, with the sources they need to cite to clear the existing source-integrity bar on first write.

---

## 1. The Hittite / Hurrian layer

**Why first.** Missing the structural hinge between Phase 1 (Mesopotamia) and Phase 2 (Greek). The Kumarbi Cycle is the direct source for Hesiod's Theogony succession-myth (Anu → Kumarbi → Teshub = Ouranos → Kronos → Zeus). Without it the entire Mediterranean diffusion story is missing its keystone.

**First concrete step.** Create six deity stubs (Teshub, Hebat, Sun Goddess of Arinna, Telipinu, Kumarbi, Illuyanka) and three document stubs (Kumarbi Cycle, Telipinu Myth, Illuyanka Myth) in Phase 1. Add `event-bogazkoy-archive-discovery-1906`. Add edges from existing Greek/Hesiodic nodes.

**Sources.** Harry A. Hoffner Jr., *Hittite Myths* (SBL Writings from the Ancient World, 2nd ed. 1998) — Tier 1; Mary R. Bachvarova, *From Hittite to Homer* (Cambridge UP, 2016) — Tier 1, explicit on the transmission; Trevor Bryce, *The Kingdom of the Hittites* (Oxford UP, 2nd ed. 2005) — Tier 1.

**Expected edge gain.** ~30–50 new high-quality edges across Hesiod, Theogony, chaoskampf theme, succession-myth theme.

## 2. The Apostolic Fathers corpus

**Why second.** The 90-year textual void between Paul (~60 CE) and Justin Martyr (~150 CE) is *the* structurally weakest point in Phase 3/4. Without these texts, Justin Martyr appears to spring from nowhere; you cannot explain the formation of orthodoxy without them.

**First concrete step.** Create eight Phase 3 or Phase 4 document nodes: Didache, 1 Clement, Ignatius's seven letters (could be one node with seven sub-sections), Shepherd of Hermas, Epistle of Barnabas, Polycarp's *Letter to the Philippians*, Martyrdom of Polycarp, 2 Clement. Each gets full YAML + context paragraph + 3 refs.

**Sources.** Bart D. Ehrman, *The Apostolic Fathers* (Loeb Classical Library, 2 vols., 2003) — single Tier 1 source covers the whole corpus with facing-page Greek/English; Michael Holmes, *The Apostolic Fathers in English* (Baker Academic, 2007).

**Expected edge gain.** ~40+ edges. These texts cite the NT, are cited by Justin/Irenaeus/Eusebius, share themes with the Pastoral Epistles, and pre-articulate doctrines (monepiscopacy, Real Presence, Trinitarian liturgy) that become orthodox in Phase 4. They bridge Paul to Catholic Christianity better than any other corpus.

## 3. Chinese textual canon (Phase 2 expansion)

**Why third.** East Asia is currently a stub at the global-atlas level: Daodejing + Analects + nothing. This contradicts the project's mission of equal weight across traditions.

**First concrete step.** Create eight Phase 1 / Phase 2 document nodes:

- **Yi Jing (I Ching)** — Phase 1 (Zhou layer) + Phase 2 (Ten Wings).
- **Zhuangzi** — Phase 2 (Inner Chapters ~4th c. BCE).
- **Mengzi (Mencius)** — Phase 2.
- **Xunzi** — Phase 2.
- **Mozi** — Phase 2.
- **Han Feizi** — Phase 2.
- **Shijing (Book of Songs)** — Phase 1.
- **Shujing (Book of Documents)** — Phase 1.

**Sources.**
- Yi Jing — Richard J. Smith, *Fathoming the Cosmos and Ordering the World* (UVA Press, 2008); James Legge, *The I Ching* (1882, public domain).
- Zhuangzi — A.C. Graham, *Chuang-Tzu: The Inner Chapters* (Hackett, 1981); Brook Ziporyn, *Zhuangzi: The Essential Writings* (Hackett, 2009).
- Mengzi — Bryan W. Van Norden, *Mengzi* (Hackett, 2008).
- Xunzi — Eric L. Hutton, *Xunzi: The Complete Text* (Princeton UP, 2014).
- Mozi — Ian Johnston, *The Mozi: A Complete Translation* (Columbia UP, 2010).
- Han Feizi — Burton Watson, *Han Fei Tzu: Basic Writings* (Columbia UP, 1964).
- Five Classics generally — Michael Nylan, *The Five "Confucian" Classics* (Yale UP, 2001).

**Expected edge gain.** ~50+ edges (Chinese intra-canon density is high; Yi Jing connects to divinatory-omen-reading theme; Han Feizi to legalism/civic-religion theme; etc.). And the optics fix is enormous.

## 4. East Asian Mahāyāna sutras

**Why fourth.** Amitabha is already a deity; the Pure Land sutras that constitute him are missing. Same for Avataṃsaka (foundational for the Indra's-net theme), Laṅkāvatāra (foundational for Chan/Zen), and the Lotus Sutra (most-influential Mahayana text in East Asia).

**First concrete step.** Four Phase 4 document nodes: Lotus Sūtra, Avataṃsaka Sūtra, Laṅkāvatāra Sūtra, Larger Sukhāvatīvyūha Sūtra (Pure Land).

**Sources.** Burton Watson, *The Lotus Sutra* (Columbia UP, 1993); Thomas Cleary, *The Flower Ornament Scripture* (Shambhala, 1993); D.T. Suzuki, *The Laṅkāvatāra Sūtra* (Routledge, 1932 — public domain); Luis O. Gómez, *The Land of Bliss* (U Hawaii Press, 1996).

## 5. Bhagavad Gītā as own node + Bhāgavata Purāṇa

**Why fifth.** The single most-cited Hindu text in the West (Gītā) is currently folded into the Mahabharata's "oral layers" node — that's undersized. And the Bhāgavata Purāṇa defines a thousand years of bhakti Vaishnavism and is just absent.

**First concrete step.** Two Phase 2 / Phase 4 document nodes.

**Sources.** Gītā — Patton, *The Bhagavad Gita* (Penguin Classics, 2008); Zaehner, *The Bhagavad-Gītā* (Oxford UP, 1969). Bhāgavata — Edwin Bryant, *Krishna: The Beautiful Legend of God: Srimad Bhagavata Purana Book X* (Penguin Classics, 2003).

## 6. Josephus

**Why sixth.** Irreplaceable primary for Second Temple history. Cited by every Phase 3 + Phase 4 scholar. Its absence is shocking.

**First concrete step.** Three Phase 4 document nodes (composition ~75–95 CE): *Antiquities of the Jews*, *Jewish War*, *Against Apion*. Plus *Vita* if completeness desired.

**Sources.** William Whiston translation (public domain via sacred-texts.com); H.St.J. Thackeray et al., Loeb Classical Library, 9 vols. (Tier 1); Steve Mason, *Josephus and the New Testament* (Hendrickson, 2003) — Tier 2.

## 8. Karbala (680) + Twelver Shia structural nodes

**Why eighth.** Husayn ibn Ali is currently a dead-link target from `tradition-shia-islam`. The foundational event of the Shīʿī world is missing. For a vault that has Council of Nicaea, Edict of Milan, and the Decian persecution, the absence of Karbala is striking.

**First concrete step.** One event node + person stubs for the Twelve Imams (Ali is already there; Husayn dead-linked; the rest absent).

**Sources.** Mahmoud Ayoub, *Redemptive Suffering in Islam* (Mouton, 1978) — Tier 1; Heinz Halm, *Shi'a Islam: From Religion to Revolution* (Markus Wiener, 1997) — Tier 1; Vali Nasr, *The Shia Revival* (Norton, 2006) — Tier 2.

## 9. Linear B decipherment event + Mycenaean Greek deities

**Why ninth.** Pinning the Olympian pantheon to Bronze Age tablets is a Phase 1 datum that reshapes the entire Greek-religion timeline in the vault. Zeus, Hera, Poseidon, Athena, Artemis, Hermes, Dionysus (yes, Dionysus too!) are attested in Linear B from ~1300 BCE — this is the single largest "compose date pushback" available across the entire vault.

**First concrete step.** One event node (`event-linear-b-decipherment-1952`) + amendments to existing Greek deity nodes' `period-active-earliest` fields. Add a new theme node: `bronze-age-substrate-of-greek-religion`.

**Sources.** John Chadwick, *The Decipherment of Linear B* (Cambridge UP, 1958) — Tier 1; Stephanie Lynn Budin, *The Ancient Greeks* (ABC-CLIO, 2009) — Tier 2; for the deities specifically, Walter Burkert, *Greek Religion* (Harvard UP, 1985), §1.3 — Tier 1.

## 10. Slug-normalisation sweep + tradition-string canonicalisation

**Why tenth (but actually high-leverage).** Single afternoon's work. Fixes ~150 dead-link occurrences and fixes the Pantheon clustering.

**First concrete step.** Two scripts:

1. `slug-drift-fixer.py`:
   - Read all filenames under `02_documents/_phase-*/`.
   - For each dead-link target in [dead-links.md](../00_meta/dead-links.md) matching `phase-N-NNN-*`, find closest filename match via Levenshtein on slug stem.
   - Emit CSV with proposed rewrites for human review.
   - On approval, run a wikilink find/replace across the vault.

2. `tradition-string-normalizer.py`:
   - Read all `tradition:` strings in YAML across `03_deities/`, `04_persons/`, etc.
   - Cluster by suffix similarity ("Sumerian / X" → all map to `mesopotamian`).
   - Emit YAML patch CSV for human review.
   - On approval, rewrite YAML uniformly.

**Expected gain.** ~150 dead-links fixed in a single batch; Pantheon clustering becomes accurate; build_data.py works as designed.
